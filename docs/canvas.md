# The 2D canvas

Exact 2D shapes drawn after tone mapping, for pcb's schematic and board, and the triangulator under its fills.

## Modules

### `canvas.c/h`, `canvas_edit.c`, `canvas_record.h`

Exact 2D shapes after tone mapping, at display resolution, kept on the GPU and edited in place: segments, arcs, circles, rounded boxes, polylines, filled polygons with holes, and text. An app owns the `Canvas` and draws it from an engine overlay with `canvas_draw(canvas, &view)`, once per VIEW -- a plain value with a rectangle, a pan, a zoom, a mirror, the layers it shows and its active layer, drawn after the others but under any layer kept `above_active`. Several views share one target the canvas owns, one framebuffer switch each. Nine things about it are easy to get backwards. **Coordinates are int64 units (pcb's nanometres) and never become floats until they are a distance from the view**: a record holds int32 offsets from its tile's origin (tiles of 2^22 units), the tile table adds the tile's int32 offset from the view's integer centre, and only then is anything a float, so a sheet 2^40 units from the origin draws to the pixel what it draws at it (`canvas-translation`). A segment or arc reaching past a tile, and a fill triangle crossing one, is cut at the tile grid, every crossing computed from the original ends in one order so neighbours share their corners to the unit; the cut pieces overlap by half a pixel each. **Coverage is clamp(0.5 - d)** with d the exact signed distance in framebuffer pixels from the known scale, never `fwidth`, so a shape's coverage summed is its area (`canvas-areas`); a line is never narrower than a pixel. **A layer resolves its coverage before it paints** (`canvas_pass.glsl`): the largest coverage any shape gives a pixel goes into the target's alpha, times the layer's opacity, then colour passes paint by alpha and zero it -- raised, solid, faint, and outline rings last -- so a translucent layer is translucent as a whole and its overlaps draw once. Max and not the union, because a cut shape and a polygon's corners cover a pixel twice and max is idempotent there. Every pixel a layer covers is painted by one of its passes, so each layer hands the next an alpha of zero and none is cleared. Where shapes of a layer overlap a raised one's colour shows, a raised shape's fringe being a fringe like any other; otherwise which colour shows is NOT defined -- fills draw before shapes and a tile's range moves as it grows -- so overlapping shapes want one colour. **Rings are drawn last for a reason**: a ring is not a distance that falls away, so where it crosses another shape of its layer -- the next segment of a picked trace at its joint -- that shape must win, and drawn raised the rings crossed every joint. **A fill is triangulated on the CPU** (`triangulate.h`) and each triangle measures its inner ramp against the six outline edges at its corners, not its own edges: an earcut ear thinner than half a pixel otherwise left its band to the triangle beside it. EDGE records draw the outer half. A fill triangle is one instance, as a shape record is, and an edit settles every arc counter-clockwise and every edge with its polygon on its left, so the shaders hold one orientation. **A shape refused part way is taken out whole** (an edit's mark and rollback): a polygon whose triangles cross too many tiles once kept the ones added before and its whole outline. **Every shape carries two tags, a net and an object**, indices into a `CanvasTags` table a canvas may share with another, so one write highlights a net in the schematic and the layout: colour, a pick outline, dim, hidden, raised. A tag or style write uploads no geometry. **Edits are built on any thread** (`CanvasEdit` holds no GL; layout, splitting, triangulating happen there) and `canvas_apply` merges one on the main thread; a tile is a RANGE of its layer's buffer, uploaded alone, not a buffer of its own, since a buffer per tile was a draw per tile per layer at about 2 µs each on Apple's GL. **Text is a glyph record each, placed by its pen in ems** from the string's anchor, so its size and turn ride the record; a canvas draws with at most four fonts, a texture unit each, and cuts at the font's true edge (`Font.sdf_edge`), which is half a code under stb's stated value because stb truncates each field texel -- cut at the stated value every glyph was 0.65% thin against stb's own rasterizer. The tables are buffer textures, the first in cetra. `canvas_generation` adds the tag table's writes to the canvas's own, for idle mode

### `triangulate.c/h`

An outline with holes into triangles, a port of Mapbox's earcut v2.2 with its ISC notice (spec 14.0). Holes in either winding, bridged at their leftmost points; every triangle's corners are points given, n - 2 + 2h of them for n points and h holes. Pure CPU and safe on any thread, so the canvas's edits call it off the main thread. Public, for an app with polygons of its own
