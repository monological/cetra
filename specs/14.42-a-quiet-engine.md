# cetra 14.42: a quiet engine

## Context

pcb 9.2 gives an agent a `make` tool that runs pcb's commands that write files as a child pcb and
answers with what the child printed. `pcb render -o FILE` makes an engine to draw the board, and an
engine always opens by printing cetra's banner and "Initializing Cetra Graphics Engine..." on
stdout, and logs at every level on stderr -- about fifty INFO lines: the GL version and renderer,
the sampler budget, the IBL precomputation, each embedded texture loaded, the screenshot saved. The
one line the agent needs, `pcb render: exit 0` or why it failed, comes back buried in them, and an
answer longer than a page hands the agent a first page of engine log. A person running `pcb render`
in a terminal sees the same.

pcb cannot quiet the banner, a `printf` in `engine.c`. The log it could: `ext/log.h` is on the
public allowlist, `cetra/ext/log.h`, and `log_set_level` with it, which this spec first said it was
not -- the thermo-nuclear review found it. Nothing sets the level today, so every message from TRACE
up is printed.

Asked, the user chose a cetra setting over filtering the child's output in pcb, which would match
on the log's format and could hide a real error (pcb 9.2's look, 2026-11-20).

Branch and spec: `14.42-a-quiet-engine`, off cetra's master (`bbbe69f0`).

## The design

In `EngineConfig`, among the switches an engine is made with:

```c
bool quiet; // true = no banner, and the log says only warnings and errors from here on
```

- **Both are decided where the engine is made**: `create_engine` prints the banner unless quiet,
  and for a quiet engine sets the log's level to warnings before anything is made, so the window,
  the context and the programs made after say nothing that went right. The banner leaves
  `_engine_init`, so how loud an engine is stands in one place.
- **The level is the process's**, as the log is, so every engine made writes it: warnings for a
  quiet one, everything for one that is not. The latest engine decides, and an engine made loud
  after a quiet one is loud whole, banner and log. A tool that makes one engine and exits, as `pcb
  render` does, is the use.
- **A switch, though the log's level is public**: told the premise above was wrong, the user kept
  the switch, which quiets the banner and the log as one, over the banner made a log line for an
  app to quiet through the level itself. A finer level, or the log sent elsewhere, is
  `cetra/ext/log.h`'s already.
- **Off by default**: every app, gate and golden runs as it does, banner and log unchanged.
- **Not quieted**: what the GL driver prints itself -- macOS's "UNSUPPORTED (log once): POSSIBLE
  ISSUE: unit 0 ..." in a headless 3D draw -- is not cetra's log.

## Phases

0. **The spec**, this file, committed alone.
1. **The switch**: `EngineConfig.quiet`; the banner and the level in `create_engine`;
   `docs/architecture.md` where an engine is made; 14.42 in `docs/eda-roadmap.md`, a need of pcb's
   found by its 9.2. Verified by building, and by an engine made quiet and not, the banner and the
   INFO lines gone with it and there without. No gate or golden reads the banner or sets the switch,
   so none runs. As built.
2. **Reviews**: `/simplify`'s four, the thermo-nuclear and a correctness review, background agents at
   effort high, applied once all are back. As built.

**Then**, at the user's word given with the request, master fast-forwards, the branch is deleted,
master is pushed, and pcb's cetra is bumped to it, `pcb render` making its engine quiet.

## As built

### Phase 1: the switch (`1cf96a67`)

- **`EngineConfig.quiet`**, its contract on the field. `create_engine` decides how loud the engine
  is before `_engine_alloc`: quiet, `log_set_level(LOG_WARN)`; otherwise the banner, which left
  `_engine_init`.
- **Docs**: `docs/cli-reference.md`'s Headless Mode, beside `.headless` from code, rather than
  `docs/architecture.md`, whose Engine paragraph lists what the engine holds and not how it is
  made; 14.42 in `docs/eda-roadmap.md`, among the smaller changes pcb asked for. `docs/modules.md`'s
  row for `engine.c/h` needed nothing.
- **Verified**: built; `shapes -x -f 3` prints the banner and its INFO lines as before. Quiet, by
  its first user: pcb's `pcb render -o` built on this commit printed one line, macOS's
  "UNSUPPORTED (log once)" from its GL driver, where it had printed the banner and some fifty INFO
  lines; its own refusal still came through, and pcb's board3d group, which runs `pcb render`,
  passed. No gate or golden sets the switch or reads the banner, so none ran.

### Phase 2: the reviews (`ac01b0da`)

`/simplify`'s four, the thermo-nuclear and a correctness review over `bbbe69f0..63a60997`,
background agents at effort high, applied once all were back. Reuse and efficiency found nothing;
correctness found no defect.

- **The premise was wrong** (thermo-nuclear): `cetra/ext/log.h` is public, so an app could set the
  level already, and only the banner was beyond it. The user was told and kept the switch, which
  quiets both as one, over the banner made a log line (Context and Design corrected).
- **Applied:**
  - **Every engine writes the level** (altitude, thermo-nuclear, correctness): quiet, warnings; not,
    everything. A loud engine made after a quiet one had printed the banner and kept the quiet
    log.
  - **An exact background not shown exactly is a warning** (correctness): the two lines saying a
    view's exact background has a stage over it the engine cannot undo, or is no colour its tone
    curve can give, were INFO, so a quiet engine -- pcb's 3D render asks for exact backgrounds --
    would hide the one sign its picture is not what was asked.
  - The comment in `create_engine` says only why the level is set first; the field's contract says
    the latest engine decides (simplification, thermo-nuclear).
- **Skipped:**
  - The four banner `printf`s as one (simplification, efficiency): moved as they were, cosmetic.
  - The import's bone-mapping table and the cook's site lines, which print on stdout past the log
    (altitude, correctness): neither reaches `pcb render`, and moving them is the log's own change,
    not this switch's.
  - Other refusals said at INFO -- `engine_set_window_mode` with no window, an animation not
    registered (correctness): neither reaches a quiet tool today.
- **Verified**: built; `shapes -x -f 3` prints the banner and its INFO lines as before; the
  `background-log` and `background-exact` arms passed, the first counting the raised line by its
  words; pcb's `pcb render -o` on this commit printed only the GL driver's line, and its board3d
  group passed.
