## What the engine gives, and what it does not (`cetra/src/game/audio.h`)
**Gives:**
- held Sounds from WAV, MP3 or FLAC, fully decoded, so a loop is seamless;
- `audio_sound_set_looping`, `audio_sound_set_position` and `audio_sound_set_volume`;
- the camera as the listener, updated after `on_pre_render`;
- `audio_system_read_pcm`, which renders offline when headless.

**Does not give:**
- **Occlusion:** walls do not block sound.
- **Tunable falloff:** it is a fixed inverse distance from 1 m.
- **A 2D held voice:** every held Sound is spatialised.

The plan works round all three in the app:
- **A bed follows the listener**, repositioned each frame, so it is heard everywhere at its set level.
- **Walls are faked by an INSIDE factor** (below), which crossfades the outdoor beds between a full layer and a muffled one, and dims the indoor sources from the street.

## Design

### A. One sound tool: `tools/fetch_sounds.py`, replacing `fetch_tick.py`
- **A `SOUNDS` table, like `fetch_textures.py`'s `SOURCES`:** every entry names its Freesound page, its licence, its preview URL, and a RECIPE. Provenance is one table for all of silent's audio.
- **Kept as they are:** the existing recipes for the tick and tock, the cleanest pair and the light case. `tick.wav` and `tock.wav` must come out byte-identical.
- **A new LOOP recipe:**
  - the steadiest window of N seconds (the least RMS variance), or a start given by hand;
  - an equal-power crossfade of about 0.5-1 s of the tail into the head, so it repeats with no seam;
  - levelled to a common target loudness, so the gains in C mean something;
  - mono for positional sources, stereo kept for the beds.
- **A MUFFLE recipe** derives an indoor layer from an outdoor one: a low-pass at about 600 Hz plus a level drop. That is how `wind_inside` and `rain_inside` are made.
- **Format:** loops are written as FLAC, which is gapless and about half a WAV's size, under `assets/audio/silent/`.
- **Audition:** `--audition DIR` renders every CANDIDATE as a loop repeated over 30 s, so the seam is audible, for the user to pick.
- It needs ffmpeg, as `fetch_tick.py` already does.

### B. Where each sound lives in the code
- **`lights.c` owns the tube buzz**, since it owns the tubes (`TUBES`) and the flicker (`flicker_level`).
  - A new `lights_start_audio(lights, audio)` puts a looping buzz at each tube's centre.
  - `lights_update` sets the failing tube's buzz volume from the same flicker level that sets its emissive strength. Silent when it drops out, half when half-lit.
  - If the chosen tube recording carries a starter's clicks, one is cut and played when the flicker catches (low to high).
- **`kitchen.c` answers where the fridge's motor is,** with `kitchen_fridge_motor(vec3 out)`, from the same frame and constants it builds the fridge with: at the back, near the floor. The hum loops there.
- **A new `sounds.c/.h`** holds the beds and the inside factor:
  - Wind outside and inside, and rain outside and inside, all following the listener.
  - Rain on the kitchen window: positional, at the pane's outside face.
  - **The INSIDE factor:**
    - from the listener's signed distance to the house's footprint, using `HOUSE_X0/X1` and `HOUSE_FRONT_Z/BACK_Z` from `layout.h`;
    - blended over about 1 m across the walls, and smoothed over about 0.4 s.
    - The front doorway is an open hole, so standing in it counts as half outside.
  - **Mixes:**
    - outdoor bed = full layer x (1 - inside) + muffled layer x inside;
    - the fridge, tubes and clock are dimmed to about 0.3 from the street, through `sounds_indoor_gain()`, read by `lights.c` and `clock.c`.
  - **Rain loudness from the rate:**
    - `gain = clamp(log1p(rate) / log1p(50), 0, 1)`, so drizzle is faint and a downpour is full;
    - zero, and the voices stopped, under `--no-rain`;
    - read from `scene->rain->rate_mmh`.
- **`silent.c`** creates them after the clock, and updates them in `on_pre_render` with the eye.

### C. Listening without a window: `--audio-dump PATH`
- Headless only.
- Each frame, `on_pre_render` pulls exactly one frame of audio through `audio_system_read_pcm`: 48000 / 60 = 800 stereo frames. Audio time is then game time.
- At exit it writes a WAV.
- Paired with `--cam-eye`/`--cam-target`, it renders what a pinned spot SOUNDS like, for the user to hear and for me to measure the levels.
- This was 13.6 phase 7's `--audio-probe`, as a file rather than numbers.

## As built (everything but the rain)

### 0. Setup
- A worktree at `.claude/worktrees/13.11-silent-ambience`, cut from local master `6defcccc`.
- The base was the user's choice, and it was offered three ways. Rain's sound waits for 13.9 to merge.

### 1. The tool and the picks
- **`fetch_sounds.py`** is `fetch_tick.py` renamed with `git mv`, and generalised:
  - one `RECORDINGS` table holds every CC0 source;
  - `JOBS` gives each loop kind its length and channels;
  - `CANDIDATES` is what `--audition` renders;
  - `LOOPS` and `MUFFLES` are what the game plays.
- **The tick and tock come out byte-identical.**
- **A loop is built in four steps:**
  - take the steadiest stretch, the least loudness wavering in 50 ms steps;
  - crossfade its tail into its head at equal power over 0.75 s;
  - high-pass it at 30 Hz, round its own period;
  - level it to an RMS of 0.1.
- **The levelling is capped at a peak of 0.9.** At the RMS alone two winds and the clunking fridge clipped, so a gusty recording comes out quieter rather than clipped.
- **Chosen by ear from 30 s audition renders:**
  - kyles' old fridge heard down a hall (635565);
  - Sauron974's ballast buzz with room tone (273625);
  - Clubadub's breezy night (386823).
  - `wind_inside` is the wind low-passed at 600 Hz at half level.
- **The loops are FLAC**, 5.4 MB together, and a second run reproduces each by hash.

### 2. The code
- **`sounds.c` holds the fridge hum, both wind layers and INSIDE.**
  - INSIDE comes from the listener's distance outside the house's footprint, blended over 1 m across the walls and lagging 0.4 s.
  - The wind layers ride half a metre over the listener's head: inside the falloff's first metre, and panned to neither ear.
  - `sounds_indoor_gain` takes the house's own sounds to 0.3 on the street.
- **`kitchen_fridge_motor`** answers from the same frame the fridge is built in, now the file-level `STOVE_WALL`.
- **`lights.c` buzzes each tube.**
  - The volume scales with its nits.
  - The failing tube's buzz is scaled by the same `flicker_level` its light uses.
  - The three start 1.9 s apart, so one loop never plays in step with itself.
- **`clock.c`** scales its beat by `hearing`.
- **`--audio-dump`** pulls what the sim clock has run since the last pull, through `audio_system_read_pcm`, and writes a 16-bit stereo WAV at exit. It is refused without `--headless`.

### 3. Listen
- Ten-second dumps at three spots:
  - by the fridge: -34.8 dBFS RMS, peak 0.14;
  - in the hall by the clock: -35.6, with the beats showing once a second;
  - on the street: -31.8, with gusts to -24.
- Nothing clips.
- **The user turned the fridge down**, from 0.35 to 0.15.
