# Spec 11.73 — Clustered decals (roadmap D1)

## Context

D1 is the roadmap's largest remaining environment-art gap: box decals culled into the existing
16x8x24 cluster grid, sampled in `pbr_frag` after the surface converges, modifying
albedo/normal/roughness in place. 11.68's roads already own the "made of one of the material's
own layers" ground slice; what D1 owns is **detail on walls and props** (which have no world-XZ
splat to override), **marks whose appearance is a picture** — a poster, a scorch, a logo — and
density past a handful of marks. It is the first booked feature that genuinely wants an image.

**The roadmap's texture premise is stale and this plan replaces it.** It books D1 on "a flat 2D
atlas takes the unit-6 alias, idle in the opaque pass" — but 11.41's cloud-shadow map holds
unit 6 for the *whole* opaque pass (`render.c:472-487` binds it unconditionally per program
switch under `SUBMIT_PASS_SHADE && !alpha_pass && !capturing`; the comment there refuses
scene-content predicates as a shipped-and-reverted bug). The roadmap's D1 and D2 entries were
written against the same free slot; only one spent it, and it shipped.

## The texture story — the decision everything hangs on

**Decal images become tenants of `materialArray` (unit 2, the `sampler2DArray` bound on every
draw).** This is 11.60's tenancy escape — the one CLAUDE.md's Wall 1 text calls "the cheapest of
the five and the one to reach for first" — used a sixth time. It costs **no unit, no declaration,
no pass exclusivity**: decals work in the opaque pass, the late pass, and probe/GI captures.
`material_texture_layer_for` dedups by GL id and knows nothing about what a layer means; the
array's contract fits (RGBA8 LINEAR, sRGB codes decoded post-blend — exactly the 11.60
layered-albedo pattern at `pbr_frag.glsl:1042`; a picture averages meaningfully under mips).
The optional decal **surface map** reuses the 11.60 packed format (normal.xy + roughness + AO,
decoded by `layerTangentNormal`, `layers.glsl:89-96`) — same pool, same decode, no new format.

Rejected alternatives, in one paragraph each candidate: a **unit-6 alias** is dead (above), and
would also inherit the capture exclusion — deleting decals from exactly the probe captures where
a poster should appear. A **GI-atlas region on unit 14** (the 11.70 escape) has no mips —
minification shimmer on exactly poster-like content, and the octahedral gutter reason mips are
refused does not go away — and is RGBA16F, 2x memory for 8-bit content. **Spending D0** to free
unit 11 makes a whole second roadmap item a dependency of this one.

Three array consequences, each with a policy:
- **Canonical-size coupling** (`material_texture_array.c:139-158`): a decal source larger than
  everything else promotes every layer. Policy (user-settled): **warn by name and proceed** —
  the cost is VRAM, reported at build; revisit as a refusal if a real scene hurts.
- **In-layer `GL_REPEAT` wrap**: a tap at UV 0.99 bilinears against the image's own opposite
  edge. Policy: shader-side half-texel inset clamp of the projected UV (canonical size is
  already a uniform).
- **Alpha-edge darkening under mips**: the transparent-texel RGB dilate in `texture.c` is gated
  on `is_srgb` and decal images load LINEAR. Policy: CPU-dilate RGB into fully transparent
  texels once at decal-apply time (the one place that knows an image is a decal), before the
  pool upload.

## Caps and layout (settled with the user)

- **`SCENE_MAX_DECALS 16`**, stated in the header with the roads-style reasoning: a room of
  posters and scorches is a dozen marks; a scene wanting fifty wants the paged content era
  (decals drawn into VT pages), not fifty per-fragment box tests. 16 keeps the compiled loop
  every decal-free scene pays at 2x the probe loop (11.70 measured that class of cost at
  +3%→+7% on a probe-free fixture), leaves the descriptor room to grow past 6 rows later, and
  raising it later is cheap where lowering it breaks scenes. 64 is structurally refused: its
  mask alone overflows the 16384 B guaranteed block.
- **Mask: 16 bits per froxel**, two froxels per uint32 — 6144 B, decoded by the `lightIndexAt`
  halfword idiom (`lights_ubo.glsl:176-179`).
- **UBO binding 10** (`UBO_BINDING_DECALS`), next free in `ubo.h:28-37`'s registry. This is
  pbr_frag's **tenth** fragment uniform block of GL 4.1's guaranteed twelve; update the
  `ubo.h:182-186` budget note.

**Descriptor: 5 vec4 rows (80 B), std140, every member a 16-byte row** (`light_cluster.h:35-37`
discipline, `_Static_assert(sizeof == SIZE)` + driver-size check at link via `ubo_wire_blocks`):

```c
typedef struct GpuDecalDesc {
    float row0[4];    // world->decal-local: rotation row i scaled by 1/half_extent[i],
    float row1[4];    // translation folded into .w — local coords land in [-1,1]^3
    float row2[4];
    float params0[4]; // x albedo layer (-1 none), y surface layer (-1 none),
                      // z opacity, w cos(angle fade)
    float params1[4]; // x edge feather (local units), y normal strength, zw reserved
} GpuDecalDesc;

typedef struct GpuDecalBlock {
    int32_t  info[4];                              // x = count             16 B
    GpuDecalDesc descs[SCENE_MAX_DECALS];          // 16 * 80 =           1280 B
    uint32_t cluster_masks[LC_CLUSTER_COUNT / 2];  // 16-bit/froxel       6144 B
} GpuDecalBlock;                                   // UBO_DECALS_BLOCK_SIZE 7440
```

Local convention: local +Z is the projection direction (`direction` through
`light_emission_frame`, `light.h:144-153` — the one existing convention for an authored oriented
frame; optional `up` is the V axis). UV = `local.xy * 0.5 + 0.5`. Authored order is paint order:
the loop composites index-ascending, later decals draw over earlier ones.

### Phase 4 — Shader
Files: new `cetra/shaders/include/decals_ubo.glsl`, `cetra/shaders/pbr_frag.glsl`.

**renderMode-6 question, settled: the early loop.** Painted-byte reads through mode 6 with
pinned exposure are the house's strongest instrument, and `pbr_frag.glsl:1077-1088` records
exactly the failure class (a broken decode shipping green) that a mode-6-invisible decal would

- `decals_ubo.glsl`: the `DecalBlock` UBO, `decalMaskAt(ci)` (halfword idiom), and
  `decalAccumulate(...)` returning accumulated albedo **codes** + alpha + tangent-space normal +

  `SCENE_MAX_DECALS`, dynamically-uniform `break` on count, `continue` on mask bit, then the
  cheap analytic rejects **before any fetch**: box test `all(lessThanEqual(abs(local), 1))`,
  angle fade `dot(Ng, -decalAxis) < cosFade` on the **geometric** normal (the wall-smear
  rejection), edge feather. Taps are `textureGrad(materialArray, ...)` with projected-UV

  implicit-LOD fetch in divergent flow (the `layers.glsl:32-39` rule). Half-texel inset clamp
  per the wrap policy, sized from `textureSize(materialArray, 0).xy` — no new uniform;
  `textureSize` is precedented in this corpus at `layers.glsl:415` (`sampleCachedSurface`).
  No silent per-pixel cap (11.56's argument): the trip bound is the cap and it is the
  authored one.
- `pbr_frag.glsl`, three edits at named seams:
  1. **Hoist `ddxWorld`/`ddyWorld` from :1330-1331 to just above the early decal loop** (i.e.
     between the alpha-cutoff discard and the mode-6 return) — one definition serving both the
     decal taps and the punctual-shadow use. The move is for **availability, not legality**:
     the :1330 comment is about the light loop's non-uniform flow, and the file already takes
     derivatives after the discard (specular AA's `dFdx(N)` at :1240), so no new
     derivative-discipline claim is being made.
  2. **Early loop between the discard (:1075) and the mode-6 return (:1089)**, guarded
     `if (decalInfo.x > 0 && passMode != 2 && passMode != 3)` — dynamically uniform, so
     decal-free scenes pay one branch and the moment/prepass exits never pay the loop (decals
     change no coverage and no depth). Apply the **albedo half** immediately: blend accumulated
     codes in code space, decode once (`sRGBToLinear`, the 11.60 decode-after-blend pattern),
     `mix` into `albedoMap` by accumulated alpha — mode 6 shows the decal.
  3. **N/roughness half at the wet-sand seam** (after :1324, where `N` and `roughnessMap`

# AS BUILT

Departures from the plan above, each found by building, by mutating, or by the review round that followed. The first eleven are the build; twelve onward are the review, and three of those are defects no arm could see.

**1. The cap moved to `decal.h` as `DECAL_MAX`, not `SCENE_MAX_DECALS` in `scene.h`.**
`light_cluster.h` sizes its mask array from it and cannot include `scene.h` — the same
relationship `probe_set.h` and `roads.h` already have with their own ceilings.

**2. `CSceneDecal` is a MIRROR, and the plan left that open.** The roads trick (the authored
type IS the runtime type) is conditioned on "the same five fields in the same units"; a decal
carries image PATHS where `Decal` carries `Texture*`, which is `CSceneMaterialLayer`'s
relationship to `MaterialLayer` and forces a mirror.

**3. `light_emission_frame` could not be called.** It takes a `const Light*`, so the plan's
"extract its vector core" became a real extraction: `orientation_frame(dir, ref, axis, up)` in
`light.c`, with the light function as a two-line adapter over it.

**4. The alpha dilate needed a new pool entry point.** `texture.c` frees its pixel data after
upload, so an apply-time dilate is impossible; and the existing gate is `is_srgb`, which a
decal image is not (it loads LINEAR for the array). `load_texture_path_into_pool_ex` takes the
decision as a parameter, with the plain form defaulting it to `is_srgb` exactly as before.

**5. `decal_is_live` was not in the plan and is load-bearing.** The descriptor pack and the
froxel mask loop both walk the scene's array and both index the same slot; a predicate that
differed between them would give mask bit *i* and descriptor *i* to different decals, and every
mark in the scene would be projected by its neighbour's box. One function, two callers.

**6. The `decals-edge` arm was written, measured, and REMOVED.** Deleting the half-texel UV
inset moves **0 px** on this fixture: its images carry a transparent margin, and a wrapped
blend between two transparent texels is still transparent. Making it observable needs an image
opaque to its own border, where the affected band is half a texel — 0.016 world units at the
canonical 64-texel array size over a two-unit box, sub-pixel at this framing. The inset stays,
being correct and free; the arm went, an arm that cannot fail being worse than the gap.

**7. The facing early-out is not the rejector, and the first mutation round proved it.**
`if (facing <= p0.w) continue;` was deleted and `decals-angle-fade` still passed — the
`smoothstep` under it returns 0 for any facing at or under the cutoff, so the compare saves the
texture fetches and decides nothing. The real mutation is removing the angle weight, which
smears the poster onto the oblique plate. Both facts are now written beside the code.

**8. `decals-mask` could not see over-marking, and gained a bound.** Marking every froxel
unconditionally took the bit count from 130 to 6144 and the arm passed: it watched the digest
and the off-screen case, and neither moves when culling stops working, because the shader's
exact box test makes over-marking a pure cost regression with no pixel to see it by. The bound
is the whole grid rather than a fit around 130, so a camera or a box moving a few cells does
not fail it. Note the frustum and depth rejects are ALSO invisible to pixels — deleting both
still reports 0 bits behind the camera, because `_sphere_touches_cluster` catches it anyway.
They are cost, defended in depth.

**9. The fixture's oblique plate covered the poster's own read point.** It stands in FRONT of
the wall, so the albedo arm sampled the plate and reported `(123,177,224)` — a decal that
landed, reported as a decal that did not. Read points are generator constants now, asserted
clear of the plate and inside the image's opaque region. The generator's edge-read assert then
fired immediately on the next attempt (0.94 of the box is inside the transparent margin), which
is the assert earning its place on the day it was written.

**10. A decal's colour was multiplied by its coverage TWICE, and every arm was green.** The
accumulation is premultiplied -- `mix(acc, tex, a)` IS `tex*a + acc*(1-a)` -- and the consumer
blended that premultiplied value by `alpha` again, so a mark darkened toward its own feathered
edge instead of fading to the surface under it. A decal at `opacity: 0.5` was wrong everywhere
it was drawn. Fixed by dividing the coverage back out at the seam (`decalResolvedAlbedo` and
its two siblings), and covered by a new arm, `decals-opacity`.

**And the first diagnosis of it was wrong, which is the more useful half.** It was recorded
here, and in a code comment, as "the accumulation is not premultiplied" -- so the mutation run
to falsify the new arm rewrote the accumulate line back to `mix(...)` and **the arm passed**,
because that edit changes nothing at all. The two spellings are one expression. What was
missing was never the premultiply; it was the DIVIDE, and the mutation that actually falsifies
`decals-opacity` is deleting `decalResolvedAlbedo` from the consumer (reads 182 against 223 in
red). A mutation that passes is not always a vacuous arm -- sometimes it is a wrong story about
what the code does, and only running it says which.

**The general lesson is worth more than the fix**: six arms over a feature whose whole job is
compositing, and not one of them read a partial alpha. An arm that only ever samples `a == 1`
is blind to how a mark MEETS the surface under it, which is most of what a decal is.

**11. Hoisting the derivatives cost 11% of the opaque pass on scenes with NO decals**, and
that is five times what the whole rest of the feature costs. The plan called for moving
`ddxWorld`/`ddyWorld` up to serve both the decal taps and the punctual shadow lookup, on the
reasoning that one definition beats two. Measured interleaved A/B/A/B against master on
`overdraw_tiles` at 800x600: **master 4.681 ms, branch 5.430 ms, +16%**, with the two
distributions not overlapping.

Bisected by building three variants. Dead-coding the decal CALL while keeping the include, the
UBO block and the hoist left it at 5.20 (+11%) -- so the loop was not the cost. Un-hoisting the
derivatives while leaving the call dead returned it to **4.67, master's own number**. Two vec3s
live across the whole shader body is register pressure every fragment pays whether or not it
takes the branch; recomputing them inside the guarded block is two instructions.

Final, interleaved: **master 4.690 ms, shipped 4.837 ms, +3.1%** -- in line with 11.70's
precedent for adding a per-fragment loop to this file, and `prepass-crossover` passes.

**The lesson is about the instrument as much as the code.** `prepass-crossover` reads the
prepass as a RATIO of the opaque pass, so a uniformly heavier `pbr_frag` shrinks it -- which is
how a 16% regression announced itself as a gate failure about depth prepasses. It also failed
on MASTER during this session at a different noise floor, so the first read had to be
attributed before it could be acted on: measure the baseline, do not assume the branch is the
cause.

Also: the fixture's first `environment` block authored `"sky": true`, which parses, warns, and
renders a scene whose probes would be refused — the `cornell_rooms` / `beach_fixture` trap a
third time. The key is `"mode": "sky"`.

# REVIEW ROUND

Seven agents over the branch: three quality/correctness, four cleanup. What they found, and
what changed. **Five real defects, three of which the eight shipped arms could not see.**

**12. Every mark was projected 180 degrees round.** `right = cross(up, axis)` puts +u at the
VIEWER'S left, and `v` runs down an image from row 0 where `local.y` runs up the world -- a
mirror and a flip, which compose into a rotation. Derived by review, then confirmed by painting
the poster in four quadrants and reading which world corner each reached: the PNG's top-left
arrived at the world's lower-right. **Every image in the fixture had been invariant under
mirror, flip, transpose AND rotation**, so eight arms were green over a feature whose entire
pitch is "marks whose appearance is a picture". A single-colour patch cannot test a projector.
`decals-orient` is the arm; the generator asserts the four codes separate in every channel, so
each wrong transform gives a different wrong answer.

**13. A probe captured at load contained no decal.** `apply_cscene_probes` captures every cube
about a hundred lines BEFORE `apply_cscene_decals` puts a decal on the Scene -- so a poster was
missing from its own reflection, permanently, in a frame that otherwise looked right. This is
the property the `materialArray` tenancy was chosen FOR over the unit-6 alias, so the design
argument was true about the shader and false about the load order. Found on the first run of
`decals-capture` -- the arm the plan specced, that was never written and whose absence was
never recorded while the docstring claimed ONE deliberate absence.

**14. The facing test read the raw varying.** This file flips the geometric normal at both
other places it asks the same question. On a double-sided material every visible back face
refused the mark, and the mirror case handed the surface half a normal opposing `N`, whose
midpoint is zero and whose `normalize` is a NaN into the normals target.

**15. `opacity` was unclamped, and `angleFade: 0` was undefined.** The first ends up as the mix
weight, so an authored 2.0 does not double a mark, it extrapolates past it into
`2*decal - substrate` -- negative albedo wherever the mark is darker than half the surface,
feeding the diffuse term, SSGI, bloom and the meter. The second makes `smoothstep`'s two edges
meet. Both reachable from a `.cscn` and from a restored snapshot; the GUI slider stops at 1.

**16. The edge feather was inert, by the UV inset's own mechanism.** The transparent margin
retired the image's alpha at `|local| 0.75` while the ramp did not begin until 0.88 -- disjoint
bands, so deleting `edgeWeight` moved 0 px. That is the failure AS BUILT 6 diagnosed for the
inset, in a second guard, not looked for again, while the docstring called the inset the only
absence. FEATHER is 0.35 now and the generator asserts the bands overlap.

**17. `decals-surface` was a pixel count claiming to be a normal test.** Its twin popped the
surface map, which drops relief AND roughness AND occlusion, so the `normalStrength: 0` it
advertised was contributing nothing. And the map's normal was a flat 128/128 -- which encodes
0.0039, not 0, tilting the surface a third of a degree toward its own normal on a surface
driven to roughness 0.98 -- so the entire tangent-frame construction was unobservable and
swapping its two basis vectors was a literal no-op. Split into a renderMode-8 byte read against
the painted code with a floor control, plus `decals-normal` for the isolated relief.

**18. Nine config rows were dead to the whole suite.** `CONFIG_FIXTURE` is `cornell_rooms`,
which authors no decal, so `_decal_at` returns NULL at every index and the section is empty in
every config arm -- `config-perturb` included, the one arm that can see a row whose apply does
nothing. And `config-coverage` matches on the TRAILING member name, where `position`, `opacity`
and `enabled` are already satisfied by Light and Probe rows: the decal rows would have passed
that census if deleted. `decals-config` closes it through the snapshot path.

**19. The gate transcribed the fixture's constants under a comment saying that was the failure
mode.** Nine hand-copied literals, two of them derived by hand from the `.gltf`'s
`baseColorFactor`s, under a paragraph explaining why transcribing them is silent-failure-prone.
The mechanism it described already existed (`_import_fixture_gen`) and was in use by the layers
gate. Three of the nine were dead, including the scorch's own colour, which two documents
quoted as a measured reading and no arm asserted.

**20. Structure, where all seven agents or a clear majority agreed.** The resolve moved inside
`decalAccumulate`, which is `probeSetSpecular`'s shape and `LayerSurface`'s -- handing back
premultiplied data needed a warning comment telling every consumer to divide, and the branch
shipped the exact bug that comment described. `decal_fill_descriptors` hands back the bounding
sphere per slot, so the mask loop stops re-deriving an enumeration that had to agree across two
files on three separate things, only one of which was written down. The canonical-size warning
generalised to an argmax of a scan that already ran (25 lines out, 4 in). `Decal` moved to
`decal.h`. FNV-1a hoisted to `util.h`; the digest is computed when asked rather than 6 KB per
build, seven times over on a capture frame. `TextureAlpha` replaces a fourth bool whose call
sites read `(false, true)` and `(false, false)`.

**Declined, with reasons.** Extracting the froxel-mark walk: the agents split 2-1 and the
dissent is stronger -- lights use a bitset plus a prefix sum, so this is consumer TWO of the
mask shape, and the GLSL twins cannot share a token either way. The full `TextureKind` enum
across ~30 call sites: it should ride a change with an independent reason. FNV-1a's five
word-wise copies: gate-asserted, out of blast radius. `_decal_run`/`_probe_run` unification and
the `gen_*.py` helper triplication: real, but they reach into other gates' fixtures.
