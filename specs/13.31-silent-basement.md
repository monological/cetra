# Spec 13.31: the basement under the player's house (silent)

## Context

The player's house in silent has a basement door in the hall but nothing behind it.

- **The door today:** `door_shut` in `home.c:261` builds a fixed leaf and a plug collider.
- **Below it:** nothing. The house's floors are solid boxes from y 0 to `FLOOR_Y` 0.30, and the street's dirt box (y −0.4 to 0) runs under the whole house.

**The request:** "build a creepy looking basement with a stair case going downstairs and a single bulb hanging when you first open the door."

**What the user chose:**
- **The bulb:**
  - It is already lit.
  - The first opening of the door sends a draft down the stair and sets it swinging. It settles to a faint sway.
  - Now and then the filament dims for a moment.
- **The size:** a basement under the whole house, with a low joisted ceiling and a beam on posts. The bulb lights only the stair's foot; the rest is for the flashlight.
- **The dressing, all four kinds:**
  - **Utility:** a coal "octopus" furnace with its ducts, a water heater, a laundry tub with a slow drip, a floor drain.
  - **Storage:** sagging shelves of old preserves, boxes, a workbench.
  - **Cobwebs and damp.**
  - **Something wrong:** a chair facing a corner, and a padlocked crawlspace door.

**What's already taken:** 13.29 (shader hooks) and 13.30 (tv static) are other branches, so this is **13.31**.

**How it is checked:** no gate arms, goldens or fixtures (silent is a demo app). Every phase ends with screenshots opened in Preview. The user walks the colliders themselves.

## Lighting

### GI (`build_home_gi`, `silent.c:353`)
- `lo.y` goes from 0 to `BASEMENT_Y`. That gives 5 layers with centres at −1.5, −0.5, 0.5, 1.5 and 2.5, so the ground floor's layers are unchanged.
- That makes 11×5×12 = 660 probes, up from 396. Its 60 rows are under the mansion's 77, so the atlas slot does not grow.
- The opening sweep, one frame at load, is about 67% longer. Check the profiler's "gi capture" row.
- **Rule for big solids:** each must either enclose a probe centre or stay 0.15 m clear of it. The furnace body at (−3, 13), r 0.55, is placed to enclose the probes at (−3, −1.5, 12.5/13.5); its duct arms stay off the due-±z lines.

### Reflection probes (`silent.c:421-513`)
`PROBE_ROOM_COUNT` goes from 9 to **11**, and the comment's "nine" is fixed.
- **Stairwell:** probe at (−3.3, 1.5, 18.85), box (`HOUSE_X0+KIT_PANE_HALF`, `JOIST_Y0`, `STAIRWELL_Z0−0.5·INT_WALL`) to (`HALL_X0`, `CEIL_Y`, `HOUSE_BACK_Z−KIT_PANE_HALF`).
- **Basement:** probe at (−1.2, −0.75, 15.3), box from the foundation's inner faces at `BASEMENT_Y` to `SUBFLOOR_Y0`.

### The haze below grade (`silent.c:539-542`)
- `fog_floor_y` goes to `BASEMENT_Y`.
- The night density is scaled by `expf(−BASEMENT_Y / falloff)` (×1.034), so every height above grade is unchanged.
- Without this, the flashlight's beam stops dead at the floor line looking down the stairwell.
- **Look item:** the basement takes the same faint ambient-lit haze as the house's rooms. If it greys the dark out, revisit.

### The bulb (`basement_start` / `basement_update`)
- **Fixed parts:** a ceramic rose on the stairwell ceiling at (−2.75, `CEIL_Y`, 18.85), in the world kit.
- **The swinging part: its own kit and node**, following the `clock.c:277-311` pattern, about a pivot at (−2.75, 2.87, 18.85):
  - a cord to −0.86;
  - a socket;
  - a pull chain with a bead;
  - an A19 glass in its kit's private copy of `MAT_BULB`, centred 0.99 m down.
  
  That puts the light at y 1.88: eye level from the hall, framed in the doorway, with the stair falling away under it.
- **It casts nothing:**
  - new `Kit.casts_nothing`;
  - `kit_finish` (`kit.c:1656`, `1677`) then builds no shadow cells and sets every mesh `MESH_SHADOW_NONE`;
  - `node->capture_hidden` is set.

  So the swinging meshes are never movers in the cached tiles (whose store pool is full) and never enter a capture.
- **The light:** `basement_bulb`, a point light.
  - Colour {1, 0.78, 0.52}, 30 cd, range 6, `cast_shadows` and `shadow_cache` on.
  - **No body**, so it takes 6 tile cells, not 48, and casts hard shadows.
  - `shadow_near` 0.05.
  - Each frame, `light_set_position` puts it at the glass's centre.
- **The draft, latched:** `basement_update` runs from `on_pre_render` after `clock_update` (`silent.c:872`), given the basement door. The first time `door->travel >= 0.25` it records the time. Sim time drives everything, so a headless run repeats.
- **The pendulum:**
  - ω = √(g/L), about 3.15 rad/s, a period of about 2 s.
  - About z: −0.16·e^(−u/3.5)·sin(ωu). It pushes west first.
  - About x: −0.05·e^(−u/3.5)·sin(ωu + 0.4).
  - A faint sway of 0.012 rad fades in over 3 s and stays.
  - Before the first opening the bulb hangs still.
- **Moving shadows: a new per-light setting, `Light.shadow_refresh`** (under SETTINGS in `light.h`): "a cached light's kept faces are drawn again every frame, from where it is."
  - It is one more term in `render_shadow_tiles`' invalidation test (`shadow.c:2261`), beside the global `ss->tile_refresh`.
  - It is needed because a cached light WITHOUT a body reads its faces from where they were drawn, with no shift (`punctual_tiles.glsl:179-198`), and redraws only past `tile_tolerance` 0.05 m (`shadow.c:1505`). Left alone, the swing's shadows would step every 5 cm.
  - The bulb sets it while the swing's amplitude is over 1.5 cm, about the first 8 s. That is 6 faces a frame, then nothing.
  - The residual sway (≤ 2.7 cm) stays under the tolerance and never redraws.
  - This replaces the plan agent's suggestion of nudging `shadow_near` to force a redraw.
  - It is per-frame state, not configuration, so it stays out of the config snapshot.
  - Run `gates.py --only tiles` and `--only config` once, for the engine change.
- **Brownouts:** `lights_brownout(t, seed)` in `lights.c` beside `flicker_level`, reusing `hash01`. A pure function of time:
  - an 11 s cycle, 35% of cycles without a dip;
  - a dip lasts 0.35–1.45 s and is 0.35–0.70 deep, with a smoothstep fall and recovery;
  - the level never goes below 0.30, because a cached light at 0 stalls `shadow_tiles_cover`.
  
  Each frame the dip drives together:
  - `light->intensity`;
  - the glass's `emissive_strength`;
  - the colour, warmer as it dims, on both the light and the glass.

## As built

### Phase 1: the hole, the shell, the dirt cut and the GI reach
- **The floors are boards on framing.** Each room keeps a collider through the floor's whole depth, but what is drawn is 2.5 cm of boards. Under them, in `basement.c`, are a subfloor, 4.5 cm joists at 0.4 m lapped over the beam, rims on sill plates, a doubled trimmer under the partition and a header at the landing's edge carrying the cut joists. Only faces seen from below are drawn.
- **The posts stand at z 12, 15 and 18**, not 12.0 / 14.8 / 17.6: the plan's 17.6 put a post 5 cm from the probe column at (0, y, 17.5). Whole-number z is midway between the half-metre probe rows.
- **The yard** is now four boxes round the dig. `ground()` takes an x range, and `ground_strip()` is the street-long case.
- **The world kit grew from 400k to 481k vertices**, mostly the joists' and subfloor's grime grids.
- **GI:** 660 probes, converged at load.
- **Ground floor unchanged by eye** in all five Phase 0 frames.

### Phase 2: the stair, the rails and the door
- **The flight** is as planned: 11 risers of 0.209, 10 treads of 0.23 going. Treads are 3.5 cm boards with a 2 cm nosing; each has a collider one rise deep. Stringers are housed, cut plumb at the last riser (x -4.30) and level on the floor.
- **The stringers ride only 1.5 cm over the nosings**, not the usual 4-5 cm. Any higher and the open leaf, whose bottom is at 0.32, cuts into the south stringer at the landing (0.34).
- **No rail colliders.** On the open south side, the floor's own bodies keep a capsule on the flight above the last tread: its top reaches above y = 0 on every tread but the tenth. The two posts under the open side (x -3.30, -4.03, slab to trimmer) collide. The lower one stands a tread short of the foot, so the turn south at the bottom is 0.84 m clear.
- **A handrail on the back wall** on three iron brackets, and a rail between the two posts below the floor line.
- **The door** swings in, hinged on its front jamb, as planned. It has a threshold like the front door's. The prompt reads from the hall, shut and open.
