# cetra 14.20: exact rows, fillers read back and scrolls counted

## Context

pcb 6.5's phase 13 built its compared pane on 14.19: two editors side by side, each change padded
with filler rows so its lines stand level with their counterparts, the two held together by
copying the leader's row from the text's start. Five reviews of that phase found four things pcb
works round because cetra does not give them, each checked against the code:

- **A line's rows are wrapped again on every ask.** `ui_editor_line_rows` says a line is "wrapped
  now when it was a guess", but it calls `_wrap_line` every time (`ui_editor.c:1617-1622`), which
  keeps the starts of one line only (`wrapped_line`, `:305`). pcb asks for every changed line on
  both sides each frame, so a file it compares whole is wrapped whole every frame. The count the
  editor keeps for each line (`at.rows`) is already put back to a guess when the columns change
  (`_rows_guessed` from `_place`, `:1188`) and when a change rewrites the line (`_shift_lines`,
  `:892`); a change of the text's tab width puts back nothing, as the draw wraps what it shows again
  and the sums keep their counts.
- **The fillers cannot be read back.** Folds can (`ui_editor_folds`); fillers cannot, so pcb keeps
  its own copy of what it gave. The editor's copy moves through every change, and a change that
  rewrites a filler's line drops it (`_shift_fillers`, `:866`); pcb's does not, so a line pasted over
  with the same text leaves the two sides a filler apart while pcb thinks them level. pcb also
  clears fillers every frame on every editor not compared, as it cannot see whether one holds any.
- **Two editors count the rows above a line apart while one guesses** (14.19's known limit). pcb
  saw it: a thousand lines that each wrap to 10 rows, guessed at 7, above the view when its side
  showed a new text, and the side stood 37 lines off. pcb now copies the row again every frame the
  two differ, so they meet once the budget has wrapped those lines; until then the side stands
  apart for a few frames.
- **Nothing says an editor was scrolled.** pcb works out which side the person moved from each
  side's top line and how far into it, compared with the last frame, and gives a side two frames
  after it is shown on a text in which it cannot lead, as it is laid out a frame late and its top
  moves then with no one scrolling.

Branch and spec: `14.20-exact-rows-fillers-read-back-and-scrolls-counted`, off cetra's master
(`ee6f84c8`).

## The design

In `ui_editor.h`, beside the calls each concerns:

- **`ui_editor_line_rows` answers from the count it keeps**: laid out, a line whose rows are known
  gives them; one that is a guess is wrapped now, as before. Its contract is unchanged; its
  implementation now keeps it. So the counts stay right, `_place` treats a tab width other than the
  one the rows were wrapped at as it treats other columns: every row a guess again. The tab width
  is kept beside the columns, in the place and where a place is parked.
- **`size_t ui_editor_fillers(const UIElement* el, const UIEditorFiller** out)`**: the fillers as
  they stand, in order of their lines, one to a line and none of no rows, those under the text last;
  valid until they or the text change. 0, and NULL, for none and for anything not an editor showing
  a text. The same shape as `ui_editor_folds`.
- **`ui_editor_top_row` and `ui_editor_scroll_row` count exact rows**: every line that shows above
  the place counts the rows it wraps to, a guessed one wrapped first, rather than a guess from its
  length. For `ui_editor_top_row` that is the lines above the top; for `ui_editor_scroll_row`, the
  lines from the start until the sums reach the row asked, at the ask or when a pending one is
  taken. A line a closed fold hides shows none and is left a guess. A mark below which no line is a
  guess, lowered by a change and put back to the start by new columns, means a later ask wraps only
  the lines past it. The bar and the wheel still count guesses: a drag through a large text wraps
  none of it. The cost is the first ask after a jump into a large text, which wraps what is above
  the place at once rather than over the frames the budget would take. Two editors on texts that
  stand level then agree on every row at once, which removes 14.19's known limit.
- **`uint64_t ui_editor_scrolls(const UIElement* el)`**: the scrolls asked of the view so far. One
  for each `ui_editor_reveal`, `ui_editor_scroll_place`, `ui_editor_scroll_line` and
  `ui_editor_scroll_row`, and for each the editor makes itself: a wheel's, the bar's, a mode's
  scroll by rows, the reveal after an action, a drag past the view's edge. Counted when asked,
  whether or not the view moves, and not again when one asked before the editor was laid out on its
  text is taken. An edit, a fold, rows settling or a layout moves the view by none. The editor's,
  kept across the texts it shows, as its look is. Two editors held together lead by whichever count
  changed.

## Tests

Probe cases in `apps/gametest/src/ui_editor_probe.c`, each read by an arm in the `ui` group with
`doc_flags` and its docstring in `run_ui_gate`'s list, built as 14.19's are (`editor_fixture`,
`editor_pass`). The count of lines wrapped is read through a new hook in `ui_editor_hooks.h`,
`ui_editor_wraps`, beside `ui_editor_settled` and `ui_editor_columns`.

- **`editor-rows-kept`** -> `ui-editor-rows-kept`. A text of 200 lines, some wrapping at 300 points:
  - `kept`: laid out and settled, `ui_editor_line_rows` on every line, twice, wraps none.
  - `guess`: on a text just shown, the first ask of a line wraps it and the second does not.
  - `columns`: at a narrower width, an asked line is wrapped again and gives its new count.
  - `tab`: the tab width changed, the next layout makes the rows guesses again, and a line with
    tabs gives its count at the new width.
- **`editor-fillers-read`** -> `ui-editor-fillers-read`:
  - `given`: fillers given out of order, a line named twice and one of no rows read back sorted,
    the last given for the line, the empty one left out.
  - `moved`: a line put in above them, they read back moved with their lines.
  - `rewritten`: a change rewriting a filler's line, it no longer reads back.
  - `under`: one on the line count reads back last.
  - `none`: no fillers, and an element not an editor, read back 0.
- **`editor-exact-rows`** -> `ui-editor-exact-rows`. Two editors on one text of 600 lines, each of
  words that each take a row, so the guess from a line's length counts fewer rows than its wrap; one
  settled, one on the text just shown:
  - `top`: both put at the same line, the fresh one's `ui_editor_top_row` on its first ask is the
    settled one's.
  - `scroll`: `ui_editor_scroll_row` on the fresh one at the settled one's row puts its top on the
    same line and row.
  - `bar`: a drag of a fresh editor's bar to its middle leaves the lines above guesses.
  - `folded`: the lines a closed fold hides are left guesses.
  - `again`: a second ask at the same place wraps none.
- **`editor-scrolls`** -> `ui-editor-scrolls`:
  - `asked`: each of `ui_editor_reveal`, `ui_editor_scroll_place`, `ui_editor_scroll_line` and
    `ui_editor_scroll_row` adds one.
  - `own`: a wheel's step, a drag of the bar and a key moving the caret each add one.
  - `pending`: an ask before the editor is laid out adds one when asked and none when taken.
  - `still`: an edit above the view, a fold closed and opened, a layout at another width and the
    budget settling add none.

## Phases

0. **Spec**: this plan into `specs/` on the branch, committed alone; a task list.
1. **The four**: the rows kept, the fillers read back, the exact rows and the scrolls counted; the
   hook, the probe cases and the arms. Build with no warnings; `scripts/gates.py --only
   ui,gate-docs`, nothing else rendering. **Falsified**, each committed first:
   `ui_editor_line_rows` wrapping on every ask, and `kept` fails; the fillers read back as given,
   and `given` fails; `ui_editor_top_row` counting guesses, and `top` fails; a pending ask counted
   when taken, and `pending` fails. **Look**: `apps/canvas --editor` headless, rendered twice first
   for the noise floor, byte-identical to master's, as nothing it draws asks for any of it. As built,
   committed.
2. **Docs**: `docs/ui.md`'s editor section (the rows kept, the fillers read back, the exact rows and
   what they cost, the scrolls counted; 14.19's known limit gone); `docs/eda-roadmap.md`, a 14.20
   bullet under "Not among the nine" and the smaller specs' line to 14.20. Committed.
3. **Reviews**: a correctness pass and `/thermo-nuclear-code-quality-review`, in background agents
   at effort high, applied once both are back; the review as built.

**Then, with the user's say-so**: cetra's master fast-forwards and the branch is deleted. pcb moves
`extern/cetra` to it, in 6.5's phase 13 review fixes:
- its fillers are compared with what the editor holds (`ui_editor_fillers`), its own copy and the
  flag that goes with it dropped, and an editor not compared cleared only when it holds some; the
  stale filler reproduced first in a window case, a padded line pasted over with the same text;
- its sides lead by `ui_editor_scrolls`, the remembered top lines and the settling frames dropped;
- the row copied every frame the two differ stays, as the invariant it holds, and `compare-jump`
  stands level at once.
