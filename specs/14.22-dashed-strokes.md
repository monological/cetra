# cetra 14.22: dashed strokes

## Context

pcb 6.5 draws what a version had and now lacks, or had elsewhere, as a dashed ghost of it. The
canvas draws no dashes, so pcb cuts its ghost's lines into dashes itself, and a review of that
phase found the dash belongs in the canvas: a pattern every stroked shape honours. Each gap,
checked against the code at cetra `d94032f3`:

- **No shape has a dash.** `CanvasPaint` is a layer, a style, a net and an object
  (`canvas.h:158-163`); the shapes have widths and strokes and nothing along them (`:165-227`). Each
  kind's distance in the fragment stage measures across the shape only (`canvas_frag.glsl:43-152`),
  and nothing there knows how far along a shape a fragment is.
- **Nothing carries a place along a path from piece to piece.** A polyline is its segments added
  one by one with nothing passed between them (`canvas_edit.c:237-244`), as is each ring of a
  stroked polygon (`:750-760`). A segment or an arc cut at the tile grid gives each piece its own
  ends and nothing of where on the shape it began (`:185-217`, `:333-357`).
- **The record has no room for one.** A record is 48 bytes (`canvas_record.h:46-52`). An arc's
  measures are full, its half width, its centre's offset and its radius (`:18-20`), and a box's are
  full too (`:23`). A segment has three floats spare and a circle four, but a cut arc, the one shape
  that most needs its place along, has none. Growing every record to 64 bytes would add a third to
  every shape of a board, and to every upload (the `canvas-uploads` arm counts 48 bytes a record,
  `scripts/gates.py:31483`), to dash a few ghosts.
- **A store is generic over the record it holds.** `CANVAS_STORES` and its size and `u`-offset
  tables (`canvas.c:182-185`) are what a tile's runs, the upload (`:817-874`), the removal by object
  (`:541-568`) and the clear (`:573-600`) loop over. A third kind of record costs a table entry, a
  vertex array (`_store_gl`, `:747-775`) and a draw in each pass (`_draw_layer`, `:1023-1060`).
- **A record's flags have room**: ten bits, five used (`canvas_constants.glsl:20-24`,
  `canvas_record.h:54-56`).
- **Detail by zoom has its precedents**: a line is never drawn under a pixel
  (`canvas_vert.glsl:77`), and `text_min_px` and `shape_min_px` are settings (`canvas.h:130-132`,
  read at `canvas_vert.glsl:157` and `:180`).
- **pcb dashes by hand** (`apps/pcb/src/window/schematic_shapes.c`):
  - `Pen.dash` (`:39-44`), from the drawing's dash for lines and text, 0 for fills (`:57`);
  - `segment` cuts a line into flat-ended segments, a dash and a gap each `GHOST_DASH` (`:85-101`,
    `:389`), each line starting the pattern afresh;
  - `polyline` gives up the polyline for those segments (`:103-113`);
  - `unit_draw` turns a dashed rectangle into four of them (`:158-163`) and a polygon into its
    outline (`:171-175`), so a polygon with no width, which is filled, loses its fill and draws as
    dashes a pixel wide, a segment of no width being drawn a pixel wide (`canvas_vert.glsl:77`);
  - circles and arcs stay solid (`:179-189`);
  - a ghost costs a shape a dash, not a shape a line.
- **A style is the wrong home in pcb too.** `SSTYLE_MOVED` is "a moved thing's ghost, and the ring
  where it is now" (`schematic_shapes.h:32`): the ghost is dashed and the ring round the unit where
  it now stands is not (`schematic_shapes.c:424-427`). The ghost's text takes the ghost's style
  (`:52`), and is drawn translucent, not dashed.
- **Dashes are on the roadmap's list left for later**, "for schematic buses and courtyards"
  (`docs/eda-roadmap.md:126`, `specs/14.0-the-2d-canvas.md:351`).

Branch and spec: `14.22-dashed-strokes`, off cetra's master (`d94032f3`). 14.21, for the code
editor, is planned at the same time.

## The design

### The dash is the shape's (`canvas.h`)

```c
// A stroke drawn in dashes, in units: `length` drawn, then `gap` left, over
// and over, the shape starting `phase` into that pattern. Solid when length
// or gap is 0.
typedef struct CanvasDash {
    int64_t length;
    int64_t gap;
    int64_t phase;
} CanvasDash;

typedef struct CanvasPaint {
    CanvasLayer* layer;
    uint16_t style;
    uint32_t net;    // 0 = none
    uint32_t object; // 0 = none; what canvas_edit_remove_object removes by
    CanvasDash dash; // {0} = solid; a fill and text are never dashed
} CanvasPaint;
```

- **On the paint, not the style.** A style-level dash could be turned on after shapes were added in
  it, so every record would have to carry its place along its path in case: 64 bytes for every
  shape, or a style dashed or not for life. And pcb draws a dashed ghost and a solid ring in one
  style. A paint's dash is known to the edit, which bakes the pattern, the shape's place in it and
  any stretch into the record in double precision, so only dashed shapes pay. The cost: changing a
  pattern is an edit, not a table write. pcb clears and refills its ghost layers with every
  comparison anyway (`schematic_shapes.c:477-480`), and its buses would take a dash from the pen of
  their route kind.
- **A new field at the end of the paint.** A paint given by designators or by position, pcb's
  `pen()` among them, leaves it zero, which is solid.
- **Refused by name**, beside the widths and strokes (the list at `canvas.h:277-280`): a dash whose
  length or gap is negative, or `2^30` units or more. A refused dash refuses its shape, whole.
- **The units are the app's**, the lengths it states, never scaled by anything.

**What honours it**, and where its pattern starts:

| Shape | Dashed | The pattern starts |
|---|---|---|
| Segment | always | at `a`, running to `b` |
| Polyline | always | at its first point, carried through every point; a repeated point adds nothing |
| Arc | always | at `a`, running to `b`, whichever way round the arc goes |
| Circle | when stroked | at its rightmost point, counter-clockwise |
| Box | when stroked | at the middle of its bottom side in its own frame, counter-clockwise |
| Polygon | when stroked | each ring at its first point, in the order given |
| Polygon, circle, box filled | never: a fill is solid | |
| Text | never: a glyph has no length along | |

- **A closed shape's pattern is stretched to a whole number of periods round it**, the nearest, and
  at least one: a circle, a stroked box, a closed polyline and each ring of a stroked polygon. Its
  dashes then meet themselves where it closes, with no seam of a part dash. The phase is stretched
  with the pattern. An open shape's dashes are their stated lengths.
- **A dash's ends are flat**, square across the stroke; on an arc or a circle, along its radius.
- **The pattern runs on past a shape's ends** as though it went straight on, so a round end shows
  as far as a dash reaches it. A shape at phase 0 starts with a dash's flat end; a dash that reaches
  a round end draws it whole. Two segments of a polyline meeting inside a dash join round.
- **A segment from a point to itself is drawn solid**, its dot being no length to dash.

**`Canvas`, under SETTINGS:**

```c
float dash_min_px; // a shape whose dash or gap spans fewer pixels is drawn solid
```

2 when made, so a pattern on screen is never finer than two pixels on and two off. Below it the
dashes beat against the pixel grid and crawl as the view pans. Drawn solid, a dashed line zoomed out
keeps its weight, as a line thinner than a pixel is kept a pixel wide.

### How it reaches the GPU

- **A third store a layer**, `CANVAS_DASHES`, beside `CANVAS_SHAPES` and `CANVAS_FILLS`, of 64-byte
  records:

  ```c
  // A dashed shape's record: a shape record and its pattern, in units.
  typedef struct CanvasDashRecord {
      CanvasRecord record;
      float dash[4]; // length, gap, its place in the pattern at the record's a, unused
  } CanvasDashRecord;
  ```

  The pattern's lengths are the stretched ones on a closed shape, and the place is reduced by the
  period in double on the edit's thread, so it is a float under one period however long the path.
- **Its head says so**: two flags in `canvas_constants.glsl`, `CANVAS_FLAG_DASHED` (32u, the record
  carries a pattern) and `CANVAS_FLAG_DASH_BACK` (64u, the pattern runs from `b`: a clockwise arc,
  whose ends the edit swapped). The vertex stage reads the pattern only under the first, so the
  shape store's vertex array leaves attribute 3 disabled and nothing reads it.
- **The edit**: `CanvasPiece` gains the pattern its record takes (`float dash[4]`), zero for a solid
  piece. `_segment` and `_polyline` take the distance along the path at their first point; a piece
  of a cut segment adds its share of the length, a piece of a cut arc its share of the arc's, and a
  closed ring sums its length before its first segment so its pattern can be stretched. Fills and
  edges never take it.
- **`canvas_apply`** puts a piece flagged dashed into the dashed store and the rest where they go
  now. A dashed record's `u` is where a shape record's is (`CANVAS_ITEM_U`), so the tile bound, the
  object index, removal by object, the clear and the tags work unchanged: a dashed shape is coloured,
  dimmed, hidden, raised and ringed as any other.
- **The uploads are a tile's three runs**, each a range of its layer's store, uploaded alone. A tile
  holding solid and dashed shapes uploads two ranges when both change.
- **A draw a pass for a layer that holds dashes**, with the shape program and the dashed store's
  vertex array. The order within a layer is not defined (`canvas.h:93-98`), so the dashed draw
  coming after the solid one breaks no contract.
- **The vertex stage** turns the pattern into pixels with `uPixelsPerUnit`, as it does every length,
  and hands the fragment stage `flat out vec4 vDash`: the dash, the period and the place at `a`, in
  pixels, and a period of 0 for solid, which a solid record and a pattern under `uDashMinPx` both
  give. `uDashMinPx` is set beside `uShapeMinPx` (`canvas.c:1074`).
- **The fragment stage** measures how far along the shape the fragment is, and takes the larger of
  the shape's distance and the dash's, how far outside its dash the place lies along the path:
  - a segment: its own `along` (`vLocal.x`), unclamped past either end;
  - an arc: the radius times its turn from `a` within the sector, and past an end the arc's length
    plus the distance along the tangent there; the dash's distance scaled to the fragment's own
    radius, so its ends lie along the radius;
  - a circle: the radius times its turn counter-clockwise from the rightmost point, likewise;
  - a box: the perimeter of its rounded centreline from the middle of its bottom side, its sides then
    its quarter-circle corners;
  - a cut end: the place runs on through the half pixel of overlap, so both pieces give a pixel
    there the same coverage, which the layer's max takes once.
- **What it costs**: 16 bytes a dashed shape on the GPU and in each upload, nothing for a solid one;
  16 bytes a piece in an edit, solid pieces too; a draw a pass for each layer holding dashes; one
  flat varying and one uniform in the shape program; no sampler, no table and no new program. The
  fill program is untouched. Attribute 3 is the canvas's own, on its own vertex arrays
  (`docs/shaders.md`, Vertex Attributes, is the meshes' table).
- **A solid shape draws as it does now**: the same record, and a distance the dash's branch leaves
  as it was. If the compiler lowers the changed shader so a solid pixel moves, the difference is
  shown, not worked round with a variant.

### Rings

A tag's outline ring is drawn round each dash, as it is round any shape, since the distance it is
measured from is the dashed one: nothing in the ring pass changes. pcb's ghosts are untagged, so
pcb never shows one.

## Tests

Probe lines from `apps/canvas` under `--canvas-probe`, read by arms in the `canvas` gate group
(`run_canvas_gate`, its docstring listing each), built as 14.0's are: `_canvas_run`,
`_canvas_coverage`, `_canvas_cell`, `compare` and `_rgb_near`; `probe_cell`, `probe_pixel` and
`white_paint` in the app.

**The app gains:**
- **`--sheet dashes`**, white on black, an 8 mm view: a cell a case, each printing its box and its
  closed form (`canvas dashes NAME box x0 y0 x1 y1 ...`) or a pixel and its colour (`canvas
  dashes-at NAME x y want RRGGBB`);
- **`--dash LENGTH,GAP`**, in millimetres: every paint a sheet makes is dashed, its fills and text
  among them, which must ignore it. `--sheet symbol --dash 0.5,0.5` draws the symbol as pcb's ghost
  would;
- **`--dash-min-px PX`**, written to `Canvas.dash_min_px`.

**Arms**:

- **`canvas-dash-areas`**: each cell's coverage summed against its closed form, within 0.03 px per
  pixel of edge, as `canvas-areas`:
  - `bar`: a flat-ended segment whose length is a whole number of periods less a gap, so it ends on
    a dash's end;
  - `carried`: an open polyline of two legs at a right angle, the corner in the middle of a gap, a
    round end at the far end inside a dash: its dashes and that one round end. The legs' lengths
    are chosen so the pattern restarted at the corner covers more than the tolerance more;
  - `ring`: a stroked circle: its ring times the dash's share of the period, which stretching keeps;
  - `arc`: a flat-ended arc whose length is a whole number of periods less a gap;
  - `box`: a stroked box whose sides are whole periods with its corners in the middle of gaps,
    which stretching keeps;
  - `polygon`: a stroked square polygon of the box's size, the same area;
  - `fill-circle`, `fill-box` and `fill-polygon`: filled shapes in a dashed paint, each its solid
    closed form.
- **`canvas-dash-places`**: pixels at stated places, each within 2 codes of white or black: inside
  the first dash and the first gap of the bar, of the arc, of a clockwise arc from its own `a`, of
  the circle from its rightmost point, of the box from the middle of its bottom side, and of the
  polygon from its first point.
- **`canvas-dash-world`**: the dashes sheet at 8 mm and at 4 mm across: along the bar's middle row,
  the same count of dashes, each run within a pixel of its length times the pixels a unit. Dashes
  are lengths in units, not pixels.
- **`canvas-dash-twins`**: the dashes sheet 2^40 units from the origin against it at the origin,
  0 px; mirrored against it flipped, within a code; with 65 um tiles against the default, within a
  code, every segment and arc cut at the grid carrying its place across the cuts. And a string in a
  dashed paint against the same string in a plain one a whole number of pixels along, 0 px.
- **`canvas-dash-solid`**: the symbol sheet under `--dash 0.5,0.5` against it without, 200 mm
  across, where every dash and gap spans under two pixels: 0 px. At 48 mm across, and at 200 mm
  under `--dash-min-px 0`, the two differ, so the solid picture is the setting's doing.
- **`canvas-dash-tags`**: a dashed segment whose object's tag colours it, its first dash that
  colour within 2 codes; and one added and removed by its object in one edit, its cell black.
- **`canvas-dash-refused`**: a segment whose gap is negative and an arc whose dash is 2^30 units,
  each refused by name in the log (`_canvas_shot`'s `log`) and its cell black.

Every arm of 14.0's stays as it is: the sheets it draws hold no dash. `canvas-uploads` holding 48
bytes a record is what says a solid shape still costs what it did.

## Phases

0. **Spec**: this plan into `specs/14.22-dashed-strokes.md` on the branch, committed alone; a task
   list.
1. **Dashes**, in this order:
   - `CanvasDash`, the paint's dash and its refusals, `dash_min_px`; the edit's places along, the
     stretch and the pieces' patterns; the dashed store, its vertex array and draw; the two flags,
     the vertex stage's pattern and the fragment stage's distance for each kind. The app's sheet
     and flags. Build with no warnings.
   - **Look**, before any arm: `apps/canvas --sheet dashes -x -f 2 -S` rendered twice for the
     noise floor; then the dashes sheet and `--sheet symbol --dash 0.5,0.5` at 48, 120 and 200 mm
     across, zoomed onto a round end and a joint, opened in Preview. You judge the flat dash ends,
     the stretch round closed shapes and `dash_min_px`'s 2 before anything is gated; the command to
     run the branch's build in a window comes with them, to zoom through the threshold. Then the
     shapes, symbol, board, areas and glyphs sheets against master's, byte for byte, or the
     difference shown.
   - The seven arms and their docstrings.
   - **Falsified**, each committed first:
     - a polyline's segments each starting the pattern afresh, and `carried` fails;
     - a cut piece starting the pattern afresh, and `canvas-dash-twins`' tiles fail;
     - a clockwise arc's pattern from the record's `a`, and its place in `canvas-dash-places` fails;
     - a closed shape not stretched, and `ring` fails;
     - a fill taking the dash, and the `fill-` cells fail;
     - the pattern taken as pixels, and `canvas-dash-world` fails;
     - `dash_min_px` not read, and `canvas-dash-solid` fails.
   - `scripts/gates.py --only canvas,gate-docs`, nothing else rendering.
   - As built, committed.
2. **Docs**, committed:
   - `docs/canvas.md`: a section on dashes (on the paint, where each kind's pattern starts, carried
     through a polyline, stretched round a closed shape, flat dash ends and the pattern run on past
     an end, solid under `dash_min_px`, never a fill or text), and among the things easy to get
     backwards, that a pattern is baked into its records, so changing one is an edit;
   - `docs/shaders.md`'s 2D canvas entry: the dashed store's records and their pattern;
   - `docs/cli-reference.md` and `apps/canvas/README.md`: the dashes sheet, `--dash` and
     `--dash-min-px`;
   - `docs/eda-roadmap.md`: item 1's row and its built paragraph name 14.22, "Dashed lines" leaves
     its list left for later, and "What pcb has to know" gains that a dash is the paint's. The line
     on the smaller specs (`:69`) is left alone, 14.22 being item 1's, which keeps it apart from
     14.21's edit there.
3. **Reviews**: a correctness pass over the CPU half (the edit's places, the stretch, the stores)
   and one over the GPU half (the shaders' distances, the cut ends, the passes), and
   `/thermo-nuclear-code-quality-review`, in background agents at effort high, applied once all are
   back; the review as built.

**Then, with the user's say-so**: cetra's master fast-forwards and the branch is deleted. pcb moves
`extern/cetra` to it, and in `src/window/schematic_shapes.c`:
- `SchematicDraw.dash` becomes a `CanvasDash`, `{GHOST_DASH, GHOST_DASH, 0}` for a ghost, and
  `pen()` puts it on the paint; `Pen.dash` goes, as fills and text ignore it;
- `segment`'s loop of dashes (`:90-100`) goes, leaving `canvas_edit_segment`;
- `polyline`'s dashed branch (`:107-112`) goes, leaving `canvas_edit_polyline`;
- `unit_draw`'s dashed rectangle (`:158-163`) and polygon (`:171`) go, leaving `canvas_edit_box`
  and `canvas_edit_polygon`;
- its circles and arcs are dashed with nothing more, a filled polygon's ghost keeps its fill, and a
  ghost costs the shapes the solid drawing does.

What you see change in pcb's comparison, to be looked at before it is committed: a round-ended wire
keeps its round end where a dash reaches it, a ghost rectangle's pattern runs round it rather than
restarting at each corner, and circles and arcs are dashed.
