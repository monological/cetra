# Spec 13.8 -- silent's kitchen, lived in

## Context
The user compared silent's kitchen against a reference render and wants its density and grime:
- **Clutter.** Knives, a pot, plates, a cutting board, a towel by the sink, a light under the fume hood, soap by the sink, a metal can of spatula and wooden spoons, bowls. Shelves of dark preserves (jams) in glass, and things on top of the wall cupboards.
- **Grime in the crevices, done procedurally.** The reference's cupboards are dirty AROUND THEIR EDGES and in the gaps, and that is most of why they read as real.
- **Photographs on the fridge,** and a paper held by a magnet that looks like text.
- **The table:** a mug, papers and a plate.
- **The chair:** a foam cushion.

App-only work in `apps/silent/`, no engine change. Branch `13.8-silent-kitchen-detail`; spec `specs/13.8-silent-kitchen-detail.md`.

Still uncommitted on master: the fridge and stove on `MAT_APPLIANCE` (the dirty-white scan), the table on `PaintedMetal001`, the fade from black, and night exposure at 0.014. That goes on the branch as its own first commit, before the spec.

## Design

### A. Crevice grime, baked into vertex colours
**The approach, and why it needs no engine change.**
- The kit already batches every surface into one `MeshBuilder` per material.
- `pbr_frag.glsl` multiplies the albedo by the vertex colour (`vertexColorExists`, line ~1258), treating the colour as sRGB.
- So grime can be baked per vertex, which is also how the PS2-era games this app imitates did their shading.

**Changes:**
- **`mats.c`:** `MatSpec` gains `grime`, a strength from 0 to 1; 0 means none. Starting values: the cabinets (`MAT_ENAMEL`), the appliances, the counters and frames (`MAT_TRIM`), wood furniture and the table top. Walls get none (next item says why).
- **`kit.c`:** `kit_material(kit, material, repeat, grime)`. A slot with grime inits its builder with colours, and every vertex of that slot carries one. Prisms and loose quads carry white. `mb_vertex` copies the colour unconditionally, so NULL is not an option there.
- **The gridded face:** in `kit_box`, a grimed slot's faces are emitted as a grid, not one quad.
  - The grid lines are the face's edges, a band a few centimetres in from each edge, and interior lines about every 12 cm.
  - Each vertex's grime is `strength × edge_falloff(distance to the nearest face edge) × noise(world position)`, with bottom edges boosted and top edges eased, because dirt settles.
  - The colour is `mix(1, GRIME_TINT, amount)`, with the tint a warm dark brown.
  - The noise is a hash of the vertex's world position, so the result is deterministic and seed-stable.
- **Walls are excluded on purpose.** `kit_wall` splits a wall into slabs around openings, so its slab edges are not real edges, and grime there would draw false seams down the middle of a wall.
- **Watch the cost.** The GI opening sweep renders the scene about 2,000 times in one frame, so extra vertices lengthen the start-up pause. Keep the grid modest, and measure the vertex count and the sweep time (the log's GI timestamps) before and after.

### B. Props, from the kit's existing vocabulary
**Existing builders to reuse** (`kitchen.c`): `plates`, `mug`, `bottle`, `jar`, `pot`, `clutter`, and the `kit_frame_box` / `kit_frame_prism` / `kit_frame_bar` primitives.

**New prop builders:**
- a knife block with handles, and knives on the board;
- a cutting board;
- a towel folded over the counter edge by the sink;
- a soap bottle with a pump, and a soap bar on a dish;
- a steel utensil can holding a spatula and wooden spoons;
- bowls.

**Placement:** authored for the sink run, the stove run and the tops of the uppers. The seeded `clutter` stays for the remainder.

**The hood light:** a new entry in `lights.c`'s `TUBES` table. It is a small strip under the canopy facing down, dimmer than the ceiling tubes, with its own mesh so the engine derives a panel for it, like the other tubes.

**The tops of the cupboards:** cardboard boxes, a pot, bottles, jars and a tin. Cardboard is a new CC0 set (ambientCG `Cardboard00x`) added to `tools/fetch_textures.py`.

**The shelves:** mostly jars with lids, dark contents and paper labels.
- New contents materials: dark red jam, plum and near-black brown. They are glossy, on a flat colour or `Smear008`.
- The labels are a `MAT_PAPER` band around some jars.

### C. Photographs, a note, papers and the cushion
**`apps/silent/tools/make_cards.py`** writes one atlas, `assets/textures/silent/cards_albedo.png`, with these cards:
- **About five snapshots,** cropped 4:3 from CC0 Poly Haven HDRI thumbnails of ordinary places (a farm road, a lakeside, a park, an autumn field). Faded and yellowed, with a white border.
- **A handwritten note,** rendered with an OFL handwriting font the tool downloads and caches. Only the rendered image is committed, never the font.
- **A typed sheet** for the table, rendered with Roboto, which is already vendored.

The tool is re-runnable and deterministic, following `fetch_textures.py`'s cache and flip conventions.

**Kit:**
- `kit_frame_card(kit, f, mat, a0, a1, y0, y1, d, uv_rect)` is one outward quad with EXPLICIT atlas UVs. Every other kit face is UV-mapped world-planar, which a picture cannot be.
- One `MAT_CARDS` material serves every card.

**Placement:**
- **Fridge:** the photos, plus the note under a small coloured magnet. They replace the blank paper boxes.
- **Table:** the plate and mug stay. Add loose sheets (`MAT_PAPER` boxes at a few yaws) and the typed sheet.
- **Chair:** a foam pad on the pulled-out chair's seat. It is a new CC0 foam set (ambientCG `Foam00x`), yellowed, in a new `MAT_CUSHION`.

### 1. Crevice grime
- **In the kit, as planned.** `kit_material` takes a grime strength, and a grimed slot's builder carries colours. `kit_box` cuts each face of a grimed slot into a grid, and the lit shader's existing vertex-colour multiply does the rest.
- **Strengths live in a `GRIME` table in `mats.c`,** the `GLASS`/`GLOWS` pattern, rather than an eighth positional `MatSpec` field: cabinets 0.75, appliances 0.6, counters and frames 0.55, wood 0.45, table 0.5. Walls stay clean for the slab-seam reason.
- **The first cut read as "a grime box round the perimeter",** the user's words, so three things break it up:
  - a smooth patch field over the room (value noise on a 0.45 m lattice), deciding how dirty each stretch of edge is;
  - a band reach jittered per vertex, 0.4 to 2.0 times the 6 cm band;
  - edges that ADD where they meet, so corners collect the most, with an exponential falloff and an extra cut a third of the way into the ring, giving a hard dark line at the edge with a soft tail.
- **Cost:** 32,274 vertices become 197,042 (6.1 times; the plan's 1.5 times was a guess and is not what binds). The 10-frame headless run, which is mostly the GI's opening sweep, measures 4.11 and 4.19 s against 4.12 to 4.27 s before, so the sweep is traversal-bound and the vertices do not show.
- **Kept on silent's start-up line:** `silent: 77 colliders, 197042 vertices`.
