# cetra 14.49: an element no shorter than a length

## Context

pcb's go list (the finder, the palette and every pick list) is a popup 640 points wide, its search
and its rows stretching across it, a strip of keys at its foot. pcb 10.8's parts picker says more in
its strip -- a key more, `a to add it`, and the libraries' mark, "libraries cannot be reached" --
which runs past 640, so pcb 10.8 made a list with a key more 720 points wide, chosen by hand
(`GO_KEYED`, `src/window/go.c`). pcb's AGENTS.md gives sizing to cetra's layout and says to ask
where it cannot: it cannot, here.

The popup wants to be as wide as its content and never under 640. cetra's UI has `max_size`, the
most an element measures, and nothing for the least: a panel sized FIT is as wide as its content
and no wider, and its stretching children have no width of their own to hold it at 640. Tried in
pcb 10.8: the panel fitting, the search FIXED at 640 -- the strip widened the panel, and the search
stopped short of the rows. Decided with the user: this spec, after pcb 10.8 landed; pcb 10.8.1 then
uses it.

Branch and spec: `14.49-an-element-no-shorter-than-a-length`, off cetra's master (`111c50ee`), in
the main checkout.

## The design

```c
// SETTINGS, beside max_size:
// The least a FIT, GROW or SHARE element measures along each axis; 0 = no least. Content shorter
// than it is placed by the alignment; a parent with less room than it, the element draws past.
float min_size[2];
```

- **One clamp for both**: `_at_most` (`cetra/src/ui.c`) becomes `_held`, the length held to the
  element's least and its most. Every place a length is held to its most holds it to its least
  too, and nowhere else:
  - **the measure** (`_measure`'s last line): a FIT or GROW element measures at least its least,
    so a parent fitting its children is at least that long round it -- a pane of rows and a
    growing pane down the axis it scrolls among them;
  - **a share** (`_share_len`): its share raised to its least;
  - **across the flow** (`_cross_len`): a stretched child stretched to at least its least.
  - The growing round (`_arrange`) needs nothing: a GROW child starts from what it measured, its
    least among it, and grows from there; `_still_growing` keeps reading the most alone.
- **A FIXED axis ignores it**, as it ignores its most: its length is its own.

### Decided from the code (said, for the user to overturn)

- **The least wins over the most** where both are set and the least is longer, as CSS's
  `min-width` wins over `max-width`.
- **The least is a floor on what the element measures**, so a growing child with a least shares
  the room as one with that much content does: in a 600-point row of three empty growing
  children, one with a least of 400 is 400 and takes its share of the 200 left, 466.7, the others
  66.7. This is cetra's growing rule as it is (a child grows from what it measures), not CSS's,
  where it would be 400 and 100 and 100; changing the rule is no part of this spec.
- **A parent with less room than a child's least**: the child keeps its least and draws past, as
  content past an element's most does now. Nothing is squeezed.
- **No look phase in cetra**: nothing drawn changes until an element sets a least. The look is pcb
  10.8.1's, its parts picker pictured.
- **No other setter**: a plain write, as `max_size` is (SETTINGS); `ui_set_size` touches neither.
