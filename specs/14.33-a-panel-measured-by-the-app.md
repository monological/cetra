# cetra 14.33: a panel measured by the app

## Context

pcb 7.4.3 lays out its board modal's graph of Z and loss against frequency with cetra's UI. The
titles, `Z, ohm` and `loss, dB/in`, are labels, and the legend is a row of labels with a stroke
before each. Each plot is a panel with a draw hook.

Each plot's values, `110`, `100`, ... `70`, stand in a column at its left. The hook paints them, and
the grid line each one names, from one sum, so the two cannot drift apart. The title belongs over the
plot, starting at its left edge, which is right of the values. So the layout has to know how wide
the values are, and it cannot: a panel with a draw hook measures as its children, and the values are
not children.

pcb's AGENTS.md forbids the workaround, which is pcb measuring the values and setting a width by
hand. Its first try at 7.4.3 put the titles at the graph's left edge, over the values, and the user
turned that down: the title belongs over the plot.

Two answers were weighed with the user:
- **Taken: the panel measured by the app.** The values' column is a panel whose draw hook paints
  them. The layout asks the app what the panel's content measures, as it asks a label's text, so
  the column is as wide as the widest value, and the titles and plots stand right of it. Yoga, the
  layout under React Native, has the same: a leaf node's measure function.
- **Turned down: the values as labels**, spread down a column by a new alignment that spaces
  children evenly, as CSS's `space-between`. The layout would then place each value and the hook
  each grid line, two owners for where a tick is, which agree only while the column's ends match
  the plot's to the pixel. pcb would also have to keep a column built to match the plots', title
  row for title row, and a pool of labels for a count of ticks that changes. The frequencies along
  the foot are not evenly spaced, so they would stay painted anyway.

Branch and spec: `14.33-a-panel-measured-by-the-app`, off cetra's master (`20888036`).

## The design

In `ui.h`, beside `ui_set_draw`:

```c
// What a panel's content measures along `axis` (0 across, 1 down), in points inside its padding.
// Across, `room` is 0; down, it is the width the panel was given, as a wrapping label's height is
// taken from its width.
typedef float (*UIMeasureFn)(const UIElement* el, int axis, float room, void* user);

// A panel's content measured by the app as well as by its children: a FIT axis hugs the larger,
// and a GROW axis takes it as its least. NULL for none. Refused by name on any other kind, which
// measures its own content.
void ui_set_measure(UIElement* el, UIMeasureFn measure, void* user);
```

`UIElement` gains `measure` and `measure_user` under BY FUNCTION.

- **Where it is asked.** In `_intrinsic`, where a label's text and a table's rows are measured, the
  answer is the content's measure. The padding is added to it as it is to any content. A FIXED or
  SHARE axis asks nothing, as it measures nothing now.
- **With children**, the panel measures as the larger of its answer and its children, as an element
  with text and children does now.
- **Down, `room` is the content's width**: the panel's width less its padding, set by the width
  pass, which runs before every height (`docs/ui.md`, Layout, one axis at a time). A drawing that
  scales to its width can say how tall that makes it.
- **An answer below 0, or not a number, counts as 0.** The measure is the app's, and a bad one would
  otherwise reach every rect after it.
- **Asked at every layout**, once per axis, as text is measured. An app with a dear measure keeps
  its own answer. A layout run again in a frame, for a pane's rows, asks again.
- **A panel only.** A label, a control, a field, an editor, a table and a scrolling pane each
  measure their own content, and two measures would leave it unsaid which wins. The refusal is
  logged by name, and the element is left as it was.
- **Not an escape hatch.** The element list stays closed. The measure is an input to the layout,
  beside `max_size`, and draws nothing.

## Tests

**`ui-measure`**, a new arm in the `ui` group, from a new probe case `measure` in
`apps/gametest/src/ui_text_probe.c`, each check an exact rect:
- **Across**: a FIT row holding a measured panel, 6 points of padding each side, whose measure
  answers 37 across, then a label. The panel is 49 wide, and the label starts the row's spacing
  past it.
- **Down from its width**: that panel's measure answers 3000 over `room` down, so a panel given
  37 points of room is 3000/37 plus its padding tall. A second panel, FIXED at 112 wide (100 of
  room), is 30 plus its padding.
- **Children**: a panel answering 10 across, holding a child FIXED at 80, is 80 plus its padding.
- **GROW**: a FIT row holding only a GROW measured panel is as wide as its answer.
- **FIXED asks nothing**: the 112-wide panel's measure is asked down and never across. Each panel's
  calls are counted.
- **`max_size` holds**: an answer of 500 under a most of 200 is 200.
- **Bad answers**: -5 and NaN each measure as no content, the padding alone.
- **Refused**: `ui_set_measure` on a label leaves its measure NULL, and it is its text's width.
  NULL on the measured panel clears it, and it measures its padding alone.

**Falsified**:
- `_intrinsic` not asking the measure: the arm fails on every rect.
- `room` passed as 0 down: the height-from-width checks fail.
- The guard against bad answers taken out: the bad answers fail.
- The refusal taken out: the label fails.

## As built

### Phase 1: the measure

- **As designed.** `UIMeasureFn` and `ui_set_measure` are in `ui.h` beside `ui_set_draw`, and
  `measure` and `measure_user` are under BY FUNCTION. In `_intrinsic`, the answer is the content's
  measure, with the padding added; down, `room` is the width less the padding, never below 0.
  `_measure` already takes the larger of `_intrinsic` and the children, and asks nothing on a FIXED
  or SHARE axis, so neither needed a change.
- **The arm's one correction**: the GROW panel has the probe's 6 points of padding each side, so
  its fitting row is 67 wide, its answer and padding, not the 55 the plan said.
- **Measured**: the first panel 49 by 93.081 (37 + 12 by 3000/37 + 12), the label after it 8 on;
  FIXED at 112, 42 tall, asked down once and never across; each measured panel asked once a way in
  one layout.
- **Falsified**, each failing `ui-measure`:
  - `_intrinsic` not asking: every measured panel its padding alone, 12 by 12, and none asked;
  - `room` 0 down: 3012 tall for 42 and 93.081;
  - no guard: the -5 panel 7 by 7, and the NaN reaching every rect laid out after it, the label's
    and the cleared panel's included;
  - no refusal: the label 300 by 308 beside its twin's 45.5 by 30, its measure set and asked.
- **Gates**: `ui-measure`, `ui-elements-closed`, `ui-share`, `ui-max-size`, `ui-wrap-label` and
  `ui-baseline` pass, and so does `gate-docs`, from `out/bin`'s build.
