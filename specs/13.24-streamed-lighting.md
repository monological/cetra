# Spec 13.24 -- lighting data that streams

## Context
**What the user wants in silent.**
- The Gothic house doesn't fit a kitchen with a fridge and tube lights. Keep the kitchen, the
  grandfather clock and the hallway as the PLAYER'S house, and make that a real house in the style
  of P.T. (the user's three reference screenshots).
- Move the rest of the Gothic house to the END OF THE STREET, up a winding road through creepy bare
  trees, with:
  - iron gates on stone piers,
  - a rusted wrought-iron fence,
  - a small graveyard,
  - dead street lamps, one of them flickering.
- In the mansion, the kitchen becomes a Gothic DINING ROOM.
- The CAT lives in the mansion.
- The player's house is walkable: hall, kitchen, living room and bathroom; its other doors are shut.

**Why the engine has to change first.** The engine holds ONE GI volume per scene and at most 8
reflection probes, and the cached shadow pool is first-come, first-served.
- Two buildings about 100 m apart cannot share one 1.2 m grid.
- They need 9 probes.
- They carry more candles than the shadow pool holds.

The user also wants a large open world with many locations and houses. So the fix is not "two
volumes" but **lighting data that STREAMS**:
- the world holds any number of GI volumes and reflection probes, and the nearest few are resident;
- each is captured once and kept on the CPU, and re-uploaded when it comes back into range;
- a never-captured one is captured a little at a time as the camera approaches;
- cached shadow tiles go to the nearest lights.

**This is two specs,** each on its own branch, because they change for different reasons. 13.22 is
booked: the local-exposure fringe, which lives on silent's stair, so the stair must stay reachable
in the mansion.
- **13.24-streamed-lighting** (engine, gated) lands first. This document.
- **13.25-silent-mansion-and-home** (demo app: screenshots, not gates) uses it, and gets its own
  spec when its branch starts.

## The rule
- The world holds any number of GI volumes and reflection probes, and the nearest few are RESIDENT.
- Each is captured ONCE and its texels are kept on the CPU. When it comes back into range it is
  re-uploaded, never re-captured.
- A never-captured one is captured a little at a time as the camera approaches.
- Cached shadow tiles go to the nearest lights.
- A scene with one location renders exactly as today.

## A. Residency (`cetra/src/stream.c/h`; no GL, no clock)
- A small pure module. Given each item's box (a light's sphere as a box), the camera position, a
  capacity K and a hysteresis margin, it returns the resident set with STABLE slots.
- Ranking: distance from the camera to the box, 0 inside. Ties go by registration order, so a run
  repeats itself.
- A resident item leaves only when a non-resident one is nearer by more than the margin, so items
  don't trade places at equal distance.
- The ranking is computed ONCE per frame from the main camera and reused by every capture pass in
  that frame. Ranking per pass would thrash.
- GI (K = 8) and probes (K = 16) use it as is. The tiles use its ranking with their own budget.

## B. GI volumes become a world list
**Registration.**
- `Scene.gi_volume` becomes a list owned by the scene (`scene_add_gi_volume`).
- `gi.enabled`, `gi.rate` and `gi.debug_atlas` become world settings, so the config rows keep their
  meaning.
- The GUI lists the volumes; its Recapture button marks every volume dirty.
- New `gi_volume_fit_spacing(gi, min, max, spacing)`: a grid from BOUNDS and a cell size, so placing
  one is "here is the building".

**The shader** (`include/gi_volume.glsl`).
- Uniform arrays for 8 resident slots: grid min, spacing, counts, and the region's atlas origin,
  irradiance rows and far clip. Plain uniforms, since pbr_frag has 10 of 12 blocks.
- A fragment uses the READY slot whose box contains its biased point. A containing volume that is
  not ready yet gives the environment answer, as a lone pending volume does today. Outside every box
  it uses the NEAREST ready volume's clamped edge, which is today's behaviour.
- `giTileUV` adds the region's origin. Nothing else in the sampler changes.
- The arrays are uncached in `uniform.c` and the bind runs on every program switch, so they upload
  once per frame per program generation.

**Capture.**
- Volumes resident at frame 0 run their opening sweep in one frame, as today. That keeps the
  profiler's `gpu-gated` premise.
- A volume first captured LATER sweeps at `gi.stream_rate` probes per frame.
- At convergence its region is read back once (`glReadPixels` through `tile_fbo`, as `shadow.c:3111`
  reads cells) and kept as half floats.
- Evicted means the slot is freed and the CPU copy kept. Readmitted means `glTexSubImage2D` into the
  new slot, with `captures_total` unchanged.
- `mark_dirty` (sun, environment, GUI) discards the CPU copies. Resident volumes reconverge; the rest
  recapture when readmitted.
- An origin shift moves every volume's grid and keeps every copy, since a translation leaves them
  valid.

**Probes inside walls switch themselves off** (DDGI's classification).
- Each GI probe's capture adds a 16² depth-only cube with culling OFF that marks back-facing texels.
- A probe that sees more than about 25% back faces is inside geometry.
- Its irradiance tile's alpha (written 1 today) becomes 0, and the sampler multiplies the weight by
  it.
- This is what lets a grid be placed from bounds with no hand-tuned corner, unlike silent's.

## C. One lighting atlas, owned by the scene
**Today.** The probe set allocates the unit-14 texture and GI adopts it once, side by side. GI
REFUSES a second adoption.

**After.**
- A scene-owned atlas holds GI regions plus probe columns.
- Each list's capacity is min(K, world count), so the 2-probe and 1-volume goldens keep their exact
  texture size and UVs.
- It is sized when the lists are first captured, and regrown with a blit of the resident regions if
  the world grows.
- Probe columns pack two high where GI's height leaves room. The free `GpuProbeDesc.column.y` carries
  each column's v0.
- Too large for `GL_MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE` is refused by name.

## D. Reflection probes become a world list (16 resident)
**The cap.**
- `PROBE_SET_MAX` is now the RESIDENT cap, 16. `ProbeBlock` goes to `probeDesc[16*4]` and a 16-bit
  froxel mask, in the decals' packing (`decals_ubo.glsl:32`). That is 7344 B, a block that already
  exists.
- Slots are filled like `decal_fill_descriptors`: slot, column and mask bit are one index.
- The test at `pbr_frag.glsl:2434`, `probeInfo.x >= 2`, becomes "the set is armed". A world with
  exactly ONE probe keeps the single-probe path unchanged; a world of two or more uses the atlas at
  any resident count.

**Capture.**
- A resident, never-captured probe is captured when it is READY (section E), one probe per frame.
- Its column is read back, and the probe's prefilter cube and raw cube are both freed. Today the
  prefilter cube is kept: 12.6 MB a probe, never read again.
- Readmission uploads the kept column.
- `capture_all`'s all-at-once sweep goes. That also ends today's transient peak of every raw cube
  alive at once (about 315 MB for 5).

**Elsewhere.**
- `CSCENE_MAX_PROBES` rises to 64, with its static assert against the resident cap removed.
- `probe_set.c`'s print gains the resident list and per-probe captures, for the gates.

## E. When a capture may run, and what it sees
- **GI first.** A probe captures only when no READY-pending GI volume overlaps its box: the 13.13
  rule, per volume.
- **Shadows first.** A GI or probe capture waits until every cached light whose range reaches its box
  holds a WHOLE block. Otherwise it photographs light through walls, permanently.
- **Moving things stay out.**
  - Captures skip skinned meshes, and any node with the new `SceneNode.capture_hidden`.
  - A capture now happens while the game runs, so a cat or a character would be frozen into a
    reflection forever.
  - silent's "attach the cat after the probes are ready" workaround becomes unnecessary.

## F. Cached shadow tiles by distance
**Ranking.**
- `tiles_reconcile` takes the lights in A's ranking (nearest first) instead of `scene->lights` order.
- When the budget is full, a nearer light takes the block of the farthest holder outside the margin.

**Rate limit.** At most `tile_new_blocks_per_frame` (default 2) blocks are placed per frame, nearest
first.
- Regaining a candle draws all 48 faces at once, and the CPU cost of each face scales with the draw
  list.
- A light waiting for a block is unshadowed. It is far, in fog, and section E keeps captures from
  seeing it.

**What stays.** A freed block is reused next frame, as now. `tiles-budget` (`gates.py:28662`) is
rewritten for the ranking.

## G. The render app, a fixture, and the instruments
**The render app.**
- `.cscn` gains `giVolumes: [{boxMin, boxMax, spacing}]`, parsed like `parse_probes`.
- `--cam-at` becomes REPEATABLE, a schedule that walks one run through several places.
- `--stream-probe` prints, per frame, the resident GI and probe sets, each item's capture count, and
  the tile holders.

**The fixture.** A new generator, `assets/generators/gen_stream_rooms.py`, writes
`stream_rooms.cscn`.
- About ten closed rooms in a row, about 60 m apart.
- Each room has its own wall colour, GI volume, reflection probe and bodied light.
- One room's grid is deliberately misaligned so probes sit in its walls.
- There are more bodied lights than the pool holds.

**Not used:** silent, in any gate, golden or fixture.

## As built

### Phase 1 -- residency, the scene's atlas, the GI world list
- **`stream.c/h`:** `stream_box_distance` and `stream_assign`. Stable slots; a holder is evicted
  only by a candidate more than `margin` nearer; candidates are taken nearest first; ties go to the
  lower index. No GL, no clock.
- **`probe_atlas.c/h` became `lighting_atlas.c/h`** (`git mv`), and the atlas moved from the probe
  set to the SCENE (`Scene.lighting_atlas`, engine-owned).
  - `lighting_atlas_sync` sizes it from what the GI world and the probe set need: `min(K, count)`
    GI slots, each as large as the largest volume, then one column per probe of a set of two or
    more.
  - It only grows, copying every slot and column across by blit.
  - `gi_volume_adopt_atlas` and the per-volume atlas, `owns_atlas` and `tile_fbo` are deleted;
    projections render through the atlas's one FBO at the slot's offset.
  - The probe set BORROWS the scene's atlas (`set->atlas`), so its call sites kept their shape.
- **`GIWorld`** (gi_volume.h) is the world list, owned by the scene (`Scene.gi`, created by
  `scene_add_gi_volume`).
  - `enabled`, `rate` and `debug_atlas` moved from the volume to it, and the `gi.*` config rows now
    resolve to it.
  - **Ranking is its own call**, `gi_world_rank`, run in the frame loop before the capture
    scope opens. The scope is gated on `gi_world_dirty`, which only means anything once slots are
    assigned: folded into the update, frame 0's sweep ran with no "gi capture" row and the
    profiler's `gpu-gated` arm lost its premise.
- **The shader** (`include/gi_volume.glsl`).
  - The grid uniforms became one `vec4 giSlot[8 * 4]` table, a single `glUniform4fv` per program
    switch, uncached (the cache slot is 16 floats).
  - `giSlotAt` picks the containing slot, -1 inside one still sweeping, and the nearest swept one
    outside them all. `giIrradiance` returns false for the environment's answer, and pbr_frag
    takes `envIrradiance`, or `envAmbient` with no IBL, which is what a lone pending volume gave.
- **Measured:**
  - Goldens: `cornell_box` (GI) and `cornell_rooms` (probe set) are 0 px.
  - Gates: `emissive`, `probe-set`, `profiler`, `config`, `ao` and `gate-docs` are green.
  - `gpu-frame` failed once against a 54% run-to-run floor and passed alone at a 0% floor. That
    is load, and that arm does not run GI.

### Phases 2 and 3 -- GI streaming, classification, probes as a world list
One commit, since the probe columns and the GI slots share the atlas helpers both phases needed.

**GI.**
- **Kept copies.** A volume's slot is read back at each convergence (`lighting_atlas_read_rect`, RGBA half floats), and restored by `lighting_atlas_write_rect` on readmission, with no capture.
- **Rate-limited first sweeps.** An opening sweep begun after load runs at `stream_rate` probes a frame (default 32, `--gi-stream-rate`), except when the camera is already inside the volume.
- **Re-arming.** `mark_dirty` drops every copy.

**Classification** (`gi_volume.classify`).
- A second capture per probe, with `engine->capturing_back_faces` culling FRONT faces.
- `gi_project_frag`'s mode 2 compares back-face depth against front-face depth over the sphere and writes 0 into the irradiance tile's alpha past a quarter. The sampler multiplies the weight by it.
- `create_gi_volume_spaced` (box + cell size, centred, classify on) is what the spec called `gi_volume_fit_spacing`: counts are fixed at creation, so a grid from bounds is a constructor.
- Measured on the fixture's twin rooms, mean grey: aligned 115.6, misaligned classified 116.5, misaligned unclassified 113.2.

**Probes.**
- `ReflectionProbeSet` holds any number, 16 resident.
- The `ProbeBlock` is 7344 B, with 16-bit froxel masks in the decals' packing.
- Descriptors are published for loaded resident probes only, in PROBE order, and `pbr_frag` tests `probeInfo.x >= 1`.
- At load every resident probe captures in one frame, as before; later admissions capture one a frame.
- After projection the column is read back to the CPU and BOTH cubes are freed. The prefilter cube used to be kept, at 12.6 MB a probe.
- Probe columns stack in the GI slots' height when it holds more than one (`atlas_columns_per_stack`), and each descriptor carries its column's corner.

**Home slots** (`stream_assign`'s `home`).
- Without them a probe readmitted beside another took whichever column its eviction freed, and room 0's frame after A -> B -> A differed by 24 px: its UVs round differently in a new column. With them it is 0 px.
- Two runs with different histories still place a probe in different columns, so room 9 walked into against room 9 resident from frame 0 differs by at most 1 code, on 124 px (0.026%). `stream-equal` asserts that bound, not 0 px.

**The render app and the fixture**, pulled forward from phase 5 because nothing could be verified without them:
- `.cscn` `giVolumes[]`;
- `CSCENE_MAX_PROBES` 64 and `CSCENE_MAX_LIGHTS` 64;
- `--cam-at` repeatable;
- `--stream-probe N`;
- `--gi-stream-rate`;
- `assets/generators/gen_stream_rooms.py`, which writes `stream_rooms.gltf` and `stream_rooms.cscn`: ten rooms 40 m apart, 10 volumes, 20 probes, 20 bodied cached lights.

**Measured:**
- Goldens: `cornell_box` and `cornell_rooms` are 0 px.
- Gates: `probe-set`, `emissive`, `config`, `ao`, `decals` and `origin` are green.
- `gpu-frame` failed again against a 67% noise floor; it is to be re-run alone at the end.

### Phase 4 -- what a capture may see, and tiles by distance
**Readiness.**
- `gi_world_ready_in(box)`: every GI volume touching the box is resident and swept.
- `shadow_tiles_cover(box)`: every cached light reaching the box holds a whole block. In the frame the tiles open, it asks instead whether the light falls within the budget that frame places, nearest first, since the capture's own depth pass is what places them.
- A GI volume's sweep and a probe's capture both wait on these.
- The profiler scopes are gated on `gi_world_capture_due` / `probe_set_capture_due`, which apply the same tests, so a far building waiting for its lights files no 0 ms rows.
- **The pacing of an opening sweep is decided when it STARTS, not when the volume is admitted.** silent's mansion will be admitted at load and wait, possibly for minutes, until its candles are shadowed; decided at admission, that sweep would have run whole in one frame mid-walk.

**Out of every capture.** `SceneNode.capture_hidden` (inherited) and every skinned mesh get `DRAW_CAPTURE_HIDDEN`, and a `CullView` built while `capturing` OR inside a burst (`engine->capture_burst`, set by `scene_capture_begin`) leaves those items out. Hiding the surface was not enough:
- The first measurement had the props' SHADOWS still in the probes, through the cached tiles.
- So a kept face no longer holds such a caster (`tile_keeps`): it is drawn over the copy every frame like a mover.
- The movers pass draws every dynamic face inside a burst, in view or not: a face out of view keeps the copy the last frame drew, movers and all.
- Measured on the fixture's prop room: the GI volume's and both probes' digests with the props hidden equal those with the props removed (958a66ab, 298aa1e8, b9e03420), and differ with `hidden_prop` photographed.

**Tiles by distance.**
- `tiles_rank` orders the cached lights by distance from the camera to their reach, once a frame. It is shared by every depth pass of the frame, a capture's included, since those run before the camera is swapped.
- `tiles_reconcile` walks that ranking. A light without a block takes a free one, or evicts the farthest holder more than 2 m farther with room enough.
- Once the tiles have opened, at most `tile_new_blocks_per_frame` (2) blocks are placed a frame.
- The full warning now names the nearest light left out.
- On the fixture the nearest 16 bodied lights hold whole blocks in room 0 and again in room 9, and none farther does.
- `tiles-budget` passes unchanged: its three 64-view lights still overflow, and the log line still begins "Cached shadow tiles full".

**Instruments.**
- `--stream-probe` prints the tile ranking.
- The GI and probe lines carry an FNV digest of their kept texels.
- The render app gains `--capture-hide NAME` and `--remove-node NAME`.
- The fixture gains `hidden_prop` and `skinned_prop` (a one-joint skin) in room 3.

**Measured:**
- All 32 goldens are 0 px.
- Gates: `shadow-tiles`, `probe-set`, `emissive`, `cull`, `shadow-lag` and `contact` are green, before the capture-burst change. Re-run below.

### Phase 5 -- the gates, falsified
**The `lighting-stream` group**, seven arms on `stream_rooms.cscn`. Named so because `--only` matches by substring and "stream" is inside "terrain-stream".

**Falsified by hand**, each mutation committed-against and reverted with `git checkout`:

| Mutation | Arm that went red | Other effects |
|---|---|---|
| The 8-bit mask: bits only for descriptors below 8 | `stream-probes-16` | coverage magenta on room 8's floor |
| The classification flag ignored in the sampler | `stream-classify` | all three greys equal |
| The capture skip dropped from `draw_item_visible` | `stream-hidden` | digests differ |
| Re-capture on readmission (kept copies ignored) | `stream-once` | probe captures 20 -> 24; the frame stayed exact, so the counters are what see it |
| Ranking by list index | `stream-nearest` | the cascade into equal, once, probes-16 and classify, since room 9 is never resident |
| Tiles in list order | `stream-tiles` | see below |
| `shadow_tiles_cover` always true | `stream-equal` | see below |

**Two of these first came back GREEN, and both were the arm's fault.**
- **Tiles in list order.** `stream-tiles` checked the first sixteen of the engine's own printed ranking, which the mutation had left in list order. It now sorts the printed distances itself.
- **`shadow_tiles_cover` always true.**
  - Room 9's two lights were placed by the capture's own depth pass in the very frame the capture needed them, at the default two blocks a frame.
  - Inside a closed room an unshadowed light lights what a shadowed one does, so there was nothing to see either way.
  - The fixture gained `Room9Behind`, a light 0.4 m behind room 9's back wall whose reach crosses into the room.
  - The walk now runs at `--tile-blocks-per-frame 1` (a new render flag).
  - Under the mutation `stream-equal` reads 479,717 px, peak 14 codes.
