# Spec 13.15 -- brass candlesticks with lit candles round silent's house

## Context

13.14 gave the engine fire; 13.13 gave silent its Gothic house. Nothing in silent burns yet: the
study's two brass candlesticks stand unlit (`study.c:246-259`, "a flame is 13.14's"), and the
great hall, the stair and the gallery have no light of their own -- 13.13 records them as very
dark at night. The user wants brass candle holders with lit candles placed round the house where
they look deliberate, not scattered.

The engine already does the burning: a `FIRE_FLAME` per candle steps its own spine, draws itself
in the late draw, and drives a borrowed point light in candela, with no per-frame app code
(`engine.c:3160-3167`). What is missing is the holders, the placement, and two engine limits.

## Placement (the aesthetic plan)

Candles go where a Gothic house would put them -- framing its focal points, marking the route,
and in pairs -- and only in the panelled, stone rooms. Thirteen flames:

| Where | Holder | Flames | Why there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Great hall, flanking the hearth, (-2.90, floor, 19.1) and (1.40, floor, 19.1) | tall floor pricket stands, ~1.5 m, thick pillar candle | 2 | frame the hearth on the hall's axis; seen through the arch from the hall, symmetric about the hearth's centre x -0.75 (east stand moved 5 cm to clear the GI probe column at x 1.625) |
| Hearth cornice, the outermost crenels either side of the hood (y 2.82) | short chambersticks | 2 | the chimneypiece's own candles; they light the carved hood and its arms from below |
| Stair foot newel cap (3.95, 1.72, 18.425) and stair head newel cap (3.95, 4.38, 15.0) | table candlesticks on the caps (base radius < the cap's 0.104) | 2 | mark the climb from the hall to the gallery, the route 13.13 lays out |
| Gallery wall, flanking the study doorway (x -3.0..-2.1) | single-arm wall sconces with a backplate, above the panelling (~4.8) | 2 | frame the one door on the gallery that opens, so the eye goes to it |
| Study desk, the existing pair | the existing candlesticks, now lit | 2 | 13.13 built them for this |
| Study, the two end bays' arched niches of the east bookcase (4.97, z 10.55 and 13.29) | short candlesticks in the niches | 2 | candles in arched niches are the most Gothic thing the room can do, and they bracket the case |
| Kitchen table (3.15, 1.08, 13.22) | a stubby chamberstick, the candle half burnt | 1 | the spawn room's one human touch, someone sat here; the tubes stay the room's light |

Kept out: the hall (the bulb and the clock already own it), the porch (rain), the lancet sills
(6 cm deep), the hearth stone (the jamb plinths cover it). Every holder is checked against the GI
probe centres (`silent.c:276-288`), the rule 13.13 followed for furniture.

## Engine changes (small, first)

- **`FIRE_MAX` 8 -> 32** (`fire_constants.glsl:6`). No shader array is sized by it; it costs
  about 77 KB per `FireSystem`, a few KB of readback and the `CSceneFire` desc. Thirteen candles
  plus the hearth's fire later.
- **Candles flicker independently.** The height "breathing" hash ignores the fire's index
  (`fire.c` `_flame_step`: `_hash_signed(fire->steps, 977u)`), so candles lit on the same frame
  stretch in unison. Fold the index in, as the sideways impulses already do.

## Silent: one candle module

New `apps/silent/src/candles.c/h`, built from `on_init` after `lights_build` (so the flashlight
stays the first spot) and before `kit_finish`:

- **Four holders, all lathes and pipes from the kit** (`kit_frame_lathe`, `kit_frame_pipe`,
  profiles in {radius, height} metres, 2-32 points): the table candlestick (study's existing
  profile, moved here and given a height), a chamberstick (wide drip pan, short stem, a pipe
  ring for a handle), a floor pricket stand (stepped round base, knopped stem, wide pan), and a
  wall sconce (lathe backplate on the wall, a curved pipe arm, pan). `study.c`'s
  `candlestick()` goes, and the study calls the module, so the shape is stated once.
- **Brass that reads as brass**: a new `MAT_BRASS_BRIGHT` (`mats.h`/`mats.c`) beside the dull
  `MAT_BRASS` the clock and stair rods keep -- surface-only on the `Smear008` set like it,
  tint near polished brass's linear F0 (0.91, 0.78, 0.42) pulled down a little for age,
  roughness about 0.35, light grime gathering at the foot. `MAT_WAX` for the candles, a
  few heights so they read as burnt to different lengths; tapers of radius 0.011, pillars
  about 0.035.
- **One table, one statement of every candle**: holder kind, world position (or a kit frame and
  local point), candle height. From it the module builds the brass, the wax and the wick, and
  for each candle one `FIRE_FLAME` at the wick's tip (world space, `node` NULL -- silent never
  shifts its origin) with `params.wind_response = 0` (silent's street wind would otherwise
  lean every indoor flame 2-4 cm), pillars a slightly larger flame than tapers, and one point
  light: unshadowed (a point light takes 6 of the 8 shadow layers and 3 are in use), range
  about 2.5 m (the default ~17 m would flood the cluster pool 13.13 already overflowed once),
  colour and candela driven by the fire.
- **The scene's fire system**: `g_scene->fire = create_fire_system()`, freed by the scene.
- **`--no-candles`**, beside `--no-rain` and `--no-wind`, for A/B and frame-time checks.

## As built

### Phase 1 -- the engine

- `FIRE_MAX` is 32. Nothing in a shader is sized by it: it sizes `FireSystem.fires`, the
  renderer's per-fire GRID targets (GL names, made only for a GRID fire), the readback row
  (2 x 32 RGBA32F texels), the late draw's stack list (192 drawables) and the parsed `.cscn`
  fire block, which is heap-allocated with the rest of the desc.
- A FLAME's height breathing is seeded by its index, at slot 0 of the row `index * 131 + i`
  its sideways impulses already used from slot 1 up, so two candles lit on one frame no longer
  stretch together. Both fire fixtures run; the fixture's one candle flickers on a new seed.

### Phase 2 -- the holders, the flames and the lights

- **Where a candle is placed moved from one table to the builder that owns its surface**, and
  that departs from the plan on purpose. A candle stands on the cornice, a newel's cap, a shelf,
  a desk, and the dimensions of each are private to its builder (`CORNICE_Y` and the merlon
  pitch in `hearth.c`, the newel height in `interior.c`, `DESK_TOP` and `SPRING` in `study.c`),
  so a central table would have restated them all and floated a candle the day a table moved.
  The SHAPE is still stated once, in `candles.c` (`candle_build`), and a holder leaves its wick
  on the kit the way a gutter leaves its drip line; `candles_light` burns a FLAME on every wick
  after `kit_finish`. The kit holds `FIRE_MAX` wicks.
- **Four holders**, each a lathe with what is not round added: the table candlestick (the
  study's old profile at one height, 0.24 m, with a cup the candle stands in), a chamberstick
  (a rimmed pan, a column, a ring for a finger), a floor stand (1.3 m, a stepped foot, two knops,
  a wide pan, a pillar) and a sconce (a lancet-shaped plate, a boss, an arm on a cubic out to a
  pan). Stands and sconces collide. All in `MAT_BRASS_BRIGHT`.
- **The cornice takes candlesticks, not chambersticks**: a chamberstick's ring does not fit
  the 0.19 m crenel beside its pan, and a candlestick standing in the crenel reads from the floor
  where a pan 2.8 m up shows only its underside.
- **The candle's top is a melted pool** and the wick stands 9 mm out of it, its flame's foot
  2 mm above the wax, so the wick is inside the flame's dim base. Pillars burn a flame 1.35
  times a taper's.
- Thirteen flames, placed as planned. Every wick's point light is unshadowed, ranged 2.5 m,
  `wind_response` 0. `--no-candles` leaves the holders and candles standing unlit: no fire
  system and no lights, so the geometry is the same either way.
- **Measured: a FLAME casts 1.24 cd**, a physical candle, and at the night's pinned exposure
  (middle grey near 13 nits) that lights a wall to mid-grey only within about 10 cm. The first
  captures show the flames as white points and almost nothing lit round them. How far past
  physical to take them is phase 3's question.
- The cluster pool overflows once in a GI capture, naming the street lamp: 140 slots dropped
  without candles and 235 with them. It predates this spec (13.13's street lamps).

### Phase 3 -- the look, first pass

- **Two placements were wrong in the house, and the user caught both by walking it.** A
  chamberstick in a bookcase's end bay burned 7 cm under a wooden arch, beside the books, and a
  candlestick on a stair newel stood where a passing hand knocks it over. The bookcase pair went
  to the great hall's west wall, a sconce on the stone under each truss where its post comes
  down, so the wall reads lancet, sconce, lancet, sconce; the newel pair became two sconces on
  the stone either side of the lancet over the stair, centred 2.1 m over the line of the
  nosings so a head clears them, the upper one far enough down the flight to keep off the near
  truss's corbel. The count stays thirteen.
- **Warmer, and the light scaled apart from the flame.** `brightness` scales what is drawn as
  well as what is cast, and the flame is past white already, so scaling it only swelled the white
  and fed the bloom. The engine gained `Fire.light_scale` (and `lightScale` in a `.cscn`): the
  driven light's intensity over what the fire casts, applied once in `_drive_light`, so
  `intensity`, the vigour and `--fire-probe` stay the drawn flame's candela. The candles cast
  20 times a real one's and are white-balanced at 0.55 instead of 0.85, which reads as a
  candle's orange; adaptation keeps luminance, so that moved colour only.
- **The review pass** (reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude, and one held to the
  thermo-nuclear standard), taken:
  - the holder enum and its dispatcher are gone: four functions, each standing its own candle
    in its own cup, the cup read off the profile's last point rather than restated;
  - a sconce takes a `Facade`, so it hangs on a wall facing either way, the gallery's passing
    its own facade instead of a hand-built frame, and the hall's the stone lining's face
    (`LINING`, named once, which `lining()` builds from);
  - `silent.c` creates the fire system and `candles_light` only adds to it, so the hearth's
    fire, when it comes, shares it;
  - `battlements()` returns its outermost crenel and the cornice is `CORNICE_HALF` /
    `CORNICE_FRONT`, stated once; the newel height is named for all three posts;
  - `glm_bezier` for the sconce's arm, the flame's seed row named once, `CANDLE_RANGE`
    documented as a cluster budget, stale and caller-naming comments fixed.
  Skipped: a hoop helper for the third hand-built closed pipe (it is new kit API reaching
  `door.c`); caching each FLAME's 288-sample light integral, which repeats the same ring sums
  every frame (13.14's code; the frame-time measurement in phase 4 says whether it matters);
  deriving each light's range from its intensity (it would set the pools' edges by a lux floor
  rather than by the pool budget, a look change); a frustum test for the march.
- New colliders for walking: the two floor stands, and six sconces (two up the stair, two on
  the west wall, two on the gallery). The newel caps are bare again.
- The cluster pool still overflows in a GI capture: 258 slots in the first starved capture now.
  It warns only the first time, so that is a floor on what is dropped, not the total.

## Open at merge

- **The candle lights pass through walls.** They are unshadowed point lights, and in this engine
  only a shadow map tells a light where the geometry is (`cornell_leak`: 0.41 of the lit half
  through the partition unshadowed, 0 shadowed). The gallery sconces light the study's bookcase
  behind their wall, and every candle near an outside wall lights the outside of the house; the
  hall bulb (13.10) has always done the same. The punctual pool cannot hold them: 8 layers, all
  re-rendered every frame, a point light taking 6 and silent already using 3. The fix is its own
  spec: shadows kept between frames for every candle, redrawn every frame from the flame's
  current position for the candles nearest the camera so nearby shadows dance, edges softened to
  the flame's size, and silent's per-material world meshes split so a candle's shadow draws only
  what is near it. A cached shadow read from the flame's moved position does NOT fake the dance:
  it is wrong for exactly the occluders nearest the flame, whose shadows dance most.
- Phase 4 (frame time against `--no-candles`, the AGENTS.md silent row) was not done.
