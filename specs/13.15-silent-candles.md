# Spec 13.15 -- brass candlesticks with lit candles round silent's house

## Context

13.14 gave the engine fire; 13.13 gave silent its Gothic house. Nothing in silent burns yet: the
study's two brass candlesticks stand unlit (`study.c:246-259`, "a flame is 13.14's"), and the
great hall, the stair and the gallery have no light of their own -- 13.13 records them as very
dark at night. The user wants brass candle holders with lit candles placed round the house where
they look deliberate, not scattered.

The engine already does the burning: a `FIRE_FLAME` per candle steps its own spine, draws itself
in the late draw, and drives a borrowed point light in candela, with no per-frame app code
(`engine.c:3160-3167`). What is missing is the holders, the placement, and two engine limits.

## Placement (the aesthetic plan)

Candles go where a Gothic house would put them -- framing its focal points, marking the route,
and in pairs -- and only in the panelled, stone rooms. Thirteen flames:

| Where | Holder | Flames | Why there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Great hall, flanking the hearth, (-2.90, floor, 19.1) and (1.40, floor, 19.1) | tall floor pricket stands, ~1.5 m, thick pillar candle | 2 | frame the hearth on the hall's axis; seen through the arch from the hall, symmetric about the hearth's centre x -0.75 (east stand moved 5 cm to clear the GI probe column at x 1.625) |
| Hearth cornice, the outermost crenels either side of the hood (y 2.82) | short chambersticks | 2 | the chimneypiece's own candles; they light the carved hood and its arms from below |
| Stair foot newel cap (3.95, 1.72, 18.425) and stair head newel cap (3.95, 4.38, 15.0) | table candlesticks on the caps (base radius < the cap's 0.104) | 2 | mark the climb from the hall to the gallery, the route 13.13 lays out |
| Gallery wall, flanking the study doorway (x -3.0..-2.1) | single-arm wall sconces with a backplate, above the panelling (~4.8) | 2 | frame the one door on the gallery that opens, so the eye goes to it |
| Study desk, the existing pair | the existing candlesticks, now lit | 2 | 13.13 built them for this |
| Study, the two end bays' arched niches of the east bookcase (4.97, z 10.55 and 13.29) | short candlesticks in the niches | 2 | candles in arched niches are the most Gothic thing the room can do, and they bracket the case |
| Kitchen table (3.15, 1.08, 13.22) | a stubby chamberstick, the candle half burnt | 1 | the spawn room's one human touch, someone sat here; the tubes stay the room's light |

Kept out: the hall (the bulb and the clock already own it), the porch (rain), the lancet sills
(6 cm deep), the hearth stone (the jamb plinths cover it). Every holder is checked against the GI
probe centres (`silent.c:276-288`), the rule 13.13 followed for furniture.

## Engine changes (small, first)

- **`FIRE_MAX` 8 -> 32** (`fire_constants.glsl:6`). No shader array is sized by it; it costs
  about 77 KB per `FireSystem`, a few KB of readback and the `CSceneFire` desc. Thirteen candles
  plus the hearth's fire later.
- **Candles flicker independently.** The height "breathing" hash ignores the fire's index
  (`fire.c` `_flame_step`: `_hash_signed(fire->steps, 977u)`), so candles lit on the same frame
  stretch in unison. Fold the index in, as the sideways impulses already do.

## Silent: one candle module

New `apps/silent/src/candles.c/h`, built from `on_init` after `lights_build` (so the flashlight
stays the first spot) and before `kit_finish`:

- **Four holders, all lathes and pipes from the kit** (`kit_frame_lathe`, `kit_frame_pipe`,
  profiles in {radius, height} metres, 2-32 points): the table candlestick (study's existing
  profile, moved here and given a height), a chamberstick (wide drip pan, short stem, a pipe
  ring for a handle), a floor pricket stand (stepped round base, knopped stem, wide pan), and a
  wall sconce (lathe backplate on the wall, a curved pipe arm, pan). `study.c`'s
  `candlestick()` goes, and the study calls the module, so the shape is stated once.
- **Brass that reads as brass**: a new `MAT_BRASS_BRIGHT` (`mats.h`/`mats.c`) beside the dull
  `MAT_BRASS` the clock and stair rods keep -- surface-only on the `Smear008` set like it,
  tint near polished brass's linear F0 (0.91, 0.78, 0.42) pulled down a little for age,
  roughness about 0.35, light grime gathering at the foot. `MAT_WAX` for the candles, a
  few heights so they read as burnt to different lengths; tapers of radius 0.011, pillars
  about 0.035.
- **One table, one statement of every candle**: holder kind, world position (or a kit frame and
  local point), candle height. From it the module builds the brass, the wax and the wick, and
  for each candle one `FIRE_FLAME` at the wick's tip (world space, `node` NULL -- silent never
  shifts its origin) with `params.wind_response = 0` (silent's street wind would otherwise
  lean every indoor flame 2-4 cm), pillars a slightly larger flame than tapers, and one point
  light: unshadowed (a point light takes 6 of the 8 shadow layers and 3 are in use), range
  about 2.5 m (the default ~17 m would flood the cluster pool 13.13 already overflowed once),
  colour and candela driven by the fire.
- **The scene's fire system**: `g_scene->fire = create_fire_system()`, freed by the scene.
- **`--no-candles`**, beside `--no-rain` and `--no-wind`, for A/B and frame-time checks.

## Phases (each ends with a build, a signed commit and its as-built note in the spec)

0. EnterWorktree `13.15-silent-candles` off master; copy this plan to
   `specs/13.15-silent-candles.md`, commit it alone; task list.
1. Engine: `FIRE_MAX` and the per-fire flicker. Check: both fire fixtures still run.
2. Silent: material, holders, the candle table, the flames and lights, `--no-candles`, the study
   moved onto the module.
3. **Look, with the user**: pinned headless captures (`-x -f N -S`, `--cam-eye`/`--cam-target`)
   of each placement -- the hearth through the arch from mid-hall, the hearth close, the stair
   foot looking up, the gallery toward the study door, the study desk and niches, the kitchen
   table from the spawn -- opened in Preview. Iterate on positions, sizes and brightness here
   until the user is happy. No gate work while the look is open.
4. Frame time with and without candles (`--no-candles`), same build and framing, measured twice
   for the floor first. Docs: the silent row in `AGENTS.md`, the spec's as-built note.
