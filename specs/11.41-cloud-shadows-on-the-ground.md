# Spec 11.41 — cloud shadows on the ground

## Context

Spec 11.39 shipped cloud shadows into the froxel volume and measured what that bought:
**sky/air RMSE 0.0353, ground 0.0013**. The froxel half shadows in-scattered light, so it puts
weather in the haze and essentially nothing underfoot. The moving dappled light on terrain — the
thing D2 was written to deliver — is unbuilt, and was recorded as hard-blocked on D0.

**It is not blocked.** `pbr_frag` has no program-variant machinery; unit sharing is one declaration,
a `#define` alias, a structural exclusivity argument and a per-pass bind (`pbr_frag.glsl:248`,
`render.c:388-406`). And unit 6 is set unavailable at the top of the opaque pass and stays unbound
and unread for the whole of it (`render.c:1123`, resolve not until `:1360`). A consumer read only in
the opaque pass takes it by alias at no cost. The surface half needed a free *pass*, not a free unit.
That is committed to the roadmap as Wall 1's third escape (`83f5263`).

**Outcome intended:** moving dappled sunlight on terrain, the catcher and the water, at zero cost to
the sampler ledger and with no golden re-bake of the IBL corpus.

---

## What the three surfaces actually need — they are three different mechanisms

Reading the shaders changed the shape of this from "the same lookup in three places" to one real
consumer and two small ones.

**`pbr_frag` — the feature.** The light loop already has the sun's CSM slot in the UBO:
`dirShadow.w` is `float(CSM slot)` (`lights_ubo.glsl:21`), which is the **same number** `sky.c`
publishes as `cloud_shadow_light` (`sky.c:368`). So the sun is identified by index, exactly as the
froxel side does it, with no direction comparison — the thing `sky.c:365-367` explicitly warns
against. Multiply into `shadow` at `pbr_frag.glsl:1606-1616`, after the CSM/punctual branch and
alongside the POM self-shadow, which is the existing precedent for a second occluder folded in.

**`catcher_frag` — not a lit surface at all.** It writes `vec4(0,0,0,alpha)` where alpha is
`Σ shadowLightWeight[i] * occlusion_from(i)` (`catcher_frag.glsl:48-61`) — a darkening plane, not
shading. So its cloud shadow is a modification of the occlusion, not of a light term: sun reaching
the ground is `(1 - occlusion) * cloudSun`, so `occlusion → 1 - (1 - occlusion) * cloudSun`. It
declares **zero** samplers of its own (one tap via `csm.glsl`), so it takes a real unit, not the
alias.

**`water_frag` — one line, and much smaller than expected.** Its only sun-dependent code is the
caustics block (`:353-369`); there is **no analytic sun specular** anywhere in the file. The
reflection is a split-sum environment lookup, which already carries clouds through the sky bake, and
the bed arrives through `sceneColorTex` already shaded by `pbr_frag` and therefore already dappled.
So water's whole participation is attenuating caustics by the cloud transmittance — correct, cheap,
and FFT-only by construction since caustics already are. 4 own samplers + 9 from `ocean.glsl`, so it
takes a real unit too.

## Approach

### Phase 1 — the shared lookup and the bind

`cetra/shaders/include/cloud_shadow.glsl`, holding the shear lookup that
`froxel_inject_frag.glsl` currently inlines:

```glsl
vec2 hit = P.xz + cloudShadowShear * (cloudShadowShellY - P.y);
cloudSun = texture(cloudShadowTex, hit / cloudShadowTile).r;
```

Four consumers must state the shell geometry identically, which raises this from convenience to
requirement — the same reason `include/mboit.glsl` exists (11.17) and `include/ocean.glsl` owns both
current and previous position (11.33). Port `froxel_inject_frag` onto it in the same commit so the
include has two callers from the start and cannot be written to fit only the new one.

Bind side: `sky.c` already derives the shear at `:360-364` for the postfx publish. Add a
`sky_bind_cloud_shadow(sky, program, unit)` sibling to `bind_ltc_tables` (`ltc.c:47`) and
`bind_reflection_probe`, so the three scene programs each get it the house way. Sky owns the
"is there a map" test — `shadow_tex` being non-zero is already that statement (`sky.c:348-350`).

### Phase 2 — `pbr_frag`

`#define cloudShadowTex sceneColorTex`, mirroring `oitMomentTex` and carrying the same kind of
comment: why the alias is forced rather than clever, and why the tenants cannot collide. Bind the map
to unit 6 in the opaque pass by extending the two-way tenant choice at `render.c:397-406` to three.
Skip the read under `SUBMIT_PASS_DEPTH_ONLY`, which shades nothing.

### Phase 3 — the catcher and the water

Both take real samplers. Catcher folds into `occlusion_from`; water attenuates the caustic `focus`.
Note the two are **mutually exclusive** — the catcher is suppressed whenever water is active — so
they never share a frame and the combination needs no test.

### Phase 4 — an arm that can fail

11.39 shipped a shadow map saturated to zero at every texel with both its arms passing, and 11.40's
whole subject was green results that could not have gone red. So:

- Leave the three existing `cloudshadow-*` arms alone; they measure the froxel half.
- New arm on the same `aerial_fixture.gltf`, reading a **ground band**. `CLOUDSHADOW_BAND`
  (`gates.py:2839`) covers y 0.05-0.33, the sky; the ground band is a new constant at the terrain's
  y range. Success is the 0.0013 figure rising substantially.
- **The arm must be seen to fail** against a stubbed lookup before it is trusted.
- `--cloud-coverage 0.2`, not the 0.45 default: 11.40 measured map mean 0.023 at 0.45 (near-overcast,
  no dapple) against 0.397 at 0.2.
- Check `--sky-debug`'s shadow tile first. It is the only instrument that separates a bad map from a
  bad lookup, and it is what caught 11.39's dead map.
- Assert 0 px with `--no-cloud-shadows`, and 0 px with clouds off.

## Verification

- **The defining measurement:** ground-band RMSE on `aerial_fixture` with `--clouds --fog`, currently
  0.0013 against the air's 0.0353. Report both bands before and after — the air band must not move.
- Determinism floor measured at the chosen framing first, per house rule.
- `--no-auto-exposure -E 1.0` on every comparison.
- Full suite: baseline **160/160 gate arms, 24/24 goldens 0 px**. No IBL golden should move — that is
  the point of avoiding D0. Identify any golden combining `--clouds` with lit ground before running.
- No GPU work alongside the gates.
