# Spec 13.53: the locked house

Branch and spec `13.53-silent-locked-house`, in a worktree off `master` (13.52 is merged).

## Context

13.52 built the haunting and bound it to K. This spec gives it its place in the story. The
player's front door is locked from the inside, and the way out is a key in the basement:

- **Getting the key needs the backpack.** The flashlight is in the backpack, and the basement is
  barred to a player without a light.
- **Trying the front door with the backpack begins the haunting.**
- **The basement can't be reached before the haunting.** The cellar door is stuck until then.
- **Opening the cellar door ends the haunting first.** Then the door flings open onto an ordinary,
  lit stair, with no red bulb.
- **Leaving needs the key and the map.** With the key but not the map, the player remarks on the
  map in the kitchen and the door stays shut.

## Behaviour (as decided with the user)

**The front door, on E** (the door is locked while the story is short of `STORY_OPEN`):

| State | What happens |
|---|---|
| No backpack | A locked try: knob and rattle, the leaf jolting toward you. Thought: "It's locked. From the inside? I need a key... and my bag." No haunting. |
| Backpack, haunting never begun | The same locked try. Thought: "It's locked from the inside. I need to find a key." Then the haunting begins (`haunt_begin`) and the story moves to `STORY_HAUNTED`. |
| Haunted, or haunting over, no key | A locked try. Thought: "I need to find the key." |
| Key, no map | No try. Thought: "I think I saw a map in the kitchen. I might need it." The door stays shut. |
| Key and map | Thought: "It fits." The door unlocks and opens with its normal swing, and the story moves to `STORY_OPEN`. From then on it is an ordinary door. |

The prompt stays "E   Open door". The thought text is a first draft; the user may change it.

**The cellar door, on E:**
- **Before the haunting** (`STORY_LOCKED` and the haunting not active): it is stuck. A small try of
  the player's own: the knob take, the leaf jolting toward the hall. Thought: "It won't open.
  It's stuck."
- **During the haunting** (whether begun by the front door or by K):
  - `haunt_end` comes first, so the lights, the power and the colour all come back, and the cellar
    bulb is normal.
  - Then `door_slam(cellar, 1)` flings the door open in 0.3 s, and the slam sound plays as it
    lands. The bulb swings in the door's own draft.
  - The story moves to `STORY_OVER`.
  - This replaces 13.52's fling with the red bulb.
- **After that:** an ordinary door.

**The key:**
- A heavy brass bit key lies on the basement workbench, in its clear stretch at about (4.6, -1.5,
  16.4).
- "E   Take the key" puts it in the backpack (`ITEM_KEY`, footprint 2x1), with the thought "A key.
  It might fit the front door." The game autosaves.
- The key on the hall console is removed, so this is the only key in the house.

**Saving:**
- A new save section, `story`, holds the stage. The key rides the items section automatically.
- **A load in `STORY_HAUNTED`** starts the haunting over: the house goes red again and the chain
  waits to be watched.
- **The front door** is forced shut on load while the story is short of `STORY_OPEN`.
- **A player whose feet are outside the house** (`room_at` gives `ROOM_WORLD`) after a load or at
  start counts as `STORY_OPEN`. This covers old saves made outside, and `--player-at` out in the
  town.
- **Old saves** have no `story` section and read as the start's `STORY_LOCKED`. `save_game_load`
  gathers the live start state before reading, so missing rows keep it and no migration is
  needed.
- **When it autosaves:** on the haunting beginning, on the key being taken, and on the door
  unlocking (`save_game_due`).
- **Quit to Title** already calls `haunt_end`, and the start state resets the story.

**K stays a debug toggle.** It begins and ends the haunting anywhere, without changing the story's
stage, so a haunting begun by K can still be ended at the cellar door. `--haunt` and `--haunt-at`
stay too.

## Design

**New module `apps/silent/src/story.c/h`** (the house's story) owns:
- the stage (`StoryStage { STORY_LOCKED, STORY_HAUNTED, STORY_OVER, STORY_OPEN }`);
- the placed key node, built the way `fridge_map.c` builds the map: `mats_kit`, `kit_finish`,
  `capture_hidden`, and `kit_node_shown` to put it on and off the bench;
- the door-try sounds, loaded from `door_takes.h`'s `DOOR_KNOB[]`/`DOOR_RATTLE[]` files and played
  as voices (`audio_play_voice`) at the door's leaf;
- the try in progress, which jolts the door's leaf on its take's hits through `door_jolt`, per
  fixed step;
- the slam sound for the cellar door's fling.

API, roughly:
- `story_start`, `story_free`;
- `story_front_door(...)`, the E handler, returning whether it took the press;
- `story_cellar_door(...)`;
- `story_step(time)`;
- `story_settle(feet)`, which applies the outside-means-open rule;
- `story_restore` for loads.

**Shared with `haunt.c`:** `hit_pull`/`take_pull` move to `door.c/h` as `door_take_pull(take, at,
u)`, so the cellar tries and the player's tries share one rule.

**`haunt.c` changes:**
- The cellar-door fling detection in `haunt_step` (`cellar->want > 0 ...`) is removed. The story
  handles E at the cellar door instead, ending the haunting first.
- The red-bulb code, `CELLAR_GAIN` and `CELLAR_SWING`, is deleted, along with what only it used
  in `basement.c/h`: `Basement.tint`, `gain`, `gust` and `basement_swing`. `basement_settle`'s gust
  reset goes with them.
- `haunt_end` must still let a slam that is under way land. The story plays the slam sound when
  `cellar->slam` clears.

**`silent.c`:**
- In the interact branch (`if (reach.take) ... else if (reach.door)`), the front door and the
  cellar door go to the story first, and `door_toggle` runs only if the story did not take the
  press.
- The key is offered with `reach_offer(&reach, story key at, NULL, take_key, "E   Take the key")`
  while it is on the bench.
- `story_step` runs in `on_update` after the doors move.
- A load in `STORY_HAUNTED` begins the haunting on the first playing frame.
- New flags:
  - `--key` gives the backpack and the key.
  - `--story locked|haunted|over|open` sets the stage, for headless testing.
  - Both join `starts_elsewhere`, and the usage text lists them.
- `haunt_start` is given the story-free house as now.

**Other files:**
- **`backpack.c/h`:** `ITEM_KEY` before `ITEM_COUNT`, and an `ITEMS[]` row: id "key", "Key", a
  line, `{2,1}`, and `key_model`. The model is the key along +z: a ring bow (`kit_frame_pipe`
  loop), a shank (`kit_frame_lathe_on`) and a bit (boxes), in `MAT_BRASS`. The same builder makes
  the bench's key.
- **`save_game.c/h`:** a `story` table (stage int) through `add_table`. `apply()` puts the key
  node on or off the bench from `backpack_holds(ITEM_KEY)`, forces the front door shut below
  `STORY_OPEN`, and calls `story_settle`. `SaveWorld` gets the story.
- **`home.c`:** the console's keys are removed.

a demo app.

     master.

     sound.

  - with no backpack: no haunting begins;

  - with `--key`: the map remark and the door still shut;
  - with `--key --map`: the door opens.

## As built

### Phase 1: the locked front door and the stuck cellar door

- **`door_try.c/h`:** `door_take_pull` (from `haunt.c`'s `hit_pull`/`take_pull`, so the
  haunting's tries and the player's share one rule) and a by-hand `DoorTry`. A try is the knob's
  take heard at the leaf, and the rattle 0.3 s later when it shakes, both as voices. The leaf is
  jolted on the takes' hits through `door_jolt` each fixed step and put back on its stop when the
  try is over. `haunt.c` takes `door_take_pull` from it. `door_takes.h`'s generator now says it is
  included by both.
- **`story.c/h`:** the stage, and `story_door` taking the action key on the front door below
  `STORY_OPEN`, and on the cellar door.
  - **The front door:** a locked try, knob and rattle, the leaf pulled 1 cm at its latch.
    - Without the bag, the "and my bag" thought.
    - With it, the first try begins the haunting, moves to `STORY_HAUNTED` and is saved.
    - After that, "I need to find the key".
  - **The cellar door:** stuck while `STORY_LOCKED` and nothing haunts the house. A knob-only try,
    4 mm, and "It won't open. It's stuck."
- **`silent.c`:** `story_door` before `door_toggle` in the interact branch, and `story_step` after
  `haunt_step`. `--trace-haunt` prints the story's turns too.
- **`home.c`:** the console's ring of keys is gone.
- **Checked headless**, standing at (-0.72, 11.3) facing the front door and pressing E at 4 s:
  - without the bag: "front door locked, no bag", no haunting;
  - with `--backpack`: "haunt begin" and "the haunting begun";
  - at the cellar door with no haunting: "cellar door stuck".

### Phase 2: the cellar ending

- **E at the cellar door while the house is haunted:** `story_door` ends the haunting
  (`haunt_end`) and then `door_slam(cellar, 1)`, and plays `door_slam.wav` at the leaf the step the
  slam lands. The stage becomes `STORY_OVER` and the game autosaves. The stair is lit by its
  ordinary bulb, swinging in the door's own draft.
- **`haunt.c`:** the fling detection in `haunt_step` is gone, and with it the `quiet` flag (nothing
  set it any more), `CELLAR_GAIN` and `CELLAR_SWING`. `haunt_end` catches the pendant
  (`PENDANT_CAUGHT`, 0.4 s), so it stops at the cellar door as asked in 13.52, and at K too.
- **`basement.c/h`:** `tint`, `gain`, `gust` and `basement_swing` are gone. The header is as it was
  before 13.52's red bulb, and the code differs from that only by the taller ground storey.
- **Checked headless** at the cellar door, 8 s into the haunting, E at 2 s: "haunt end", "flung
  open, the haunting over" at 1.95 s, and "slammed" at 2.23 s. Frames: red with the door shut
  before; ordinary colours with the door open on a warmly lit stair after.

### Phase 3: the key

- **The model:** `story_key_model` builds the key, 10 cm from bow to bit, in `MAT_BRASS`:
  - a bow, a ring of 3.5 mm wire 3 cm across (`kit_frame_pipe`);
  - a round shank with a collar where it leaves the bow (`kit_frame_lathe_on`);
  - a bit of three plates, with a ward cut into its middle.
  It is built either lying flat on a height or standing on its edge. The bench uses the first, and
  the backpack's model (`ITEM_KEY`, 2x1 cells, "KEY") the second.
- **The bench key:**
  - **Where:** the workbench's frame is `basement.h`'s `BASEMENT_BENCH`, with its top at
    `BASEMENT_BENCH_TOP`, and the bench is built from the same two. The key lies 0.85 m along it
    and 0.3 m out, turned 1.2 rad off square, in the clear stretch between the vise and the jar.
  - **How it is made:** a kit of its own (`mats_kit`, `kit_finish`) that `story_start` builds and
    `story_free` frees. It is `capture_hidden`, and is put on and off the bench with
    `kit_node_shown` (`story_key_on_bench`).
- **Taking it:** "E   Take the key" is offered at it while it is on the bench. Taking it:
  - adds `ITEM_KEY` to the bag;
  - takes the key off the bench;
  - thinks "A key. It might fit the front door.";
  - autosaves.
- **The front door's key rows:**
  - With the key and no map: no try. Thought: "I think I saw a map in the kitchen. I might need
    it." The door stays shut.
  - With both: "It fits." The story moves to `STORY_OPEN`, the game autosaves, and `story_door`
    gives the press back so `door_toggle` opens the door. From then on it is an ordinary door.
- **`--key`** starts with the bag and the key in it, added beside `--map`'s so that
  `--open-backpack key`, which implies it, finds the key there.
- **Checked headless:**
  - At the front door with `--key`: "the key but no map".
  - With `--key --map`: "front door unlocked", and the door stands open on the street.
  - A still of the key on the bench in the torch's pool. E pressed there takes it off the bench,
    with the thought.
  - `--open-backpack key` shows it beside the flashlight, turning, with its line.

### Phase 4: saving

- **The `story` section:** one row, `stage`, written as its name (`STORY_STAGE_NAMES`: locked,
  haunted, over, open) through a `SAVE_ENUM`, from `Kept.story_stage`. `SaveWorld` gained the
  story. The key needs nothing of its own: it rides the items section, and the bench follows from
  it.
- **`story_restore(stage, feet)`** runs in `apply()` after the doors and the bag. It:
  - sets the stage, unknown values reading as `locked`;
  - makes it `STORY_OPEN` when the feet are anywhere but the home's rooms (`ROOM_HOME`, `_LIVING`,
    `_KITCHEN`, `_BASEMENT`);
  - drops a try under way, putting its leaf back on its stop;
  - shuts the front door below `STORY_OPEN`;
  - puts the key on or off the bench from `backpack_holds(ITEM_KEY)`;
  - at `STORY_HAUNTED`, marks the haunting to begin again.
- **Not `ROOM_WORLD` alone, as the plan said:** a player in the mansion or the cabin is out of the
  house too, and would otherwise have met the front door locked from the inside on the way back.
- **`story_play`** begins that haunting on the first frame the game is played, beside `--haunt`'s
  begin. A haunted load comes back black, then red, with the chain waiting to be watched.
- **Old saves** have no `story` section. `save_game_load` gathers the live story before reading,
  so they keep the start's `locked` (then the outside rule).
- **At start**, `story_restore` is called with `--story`'s stage, or `locked`, and the start's feet.
  So `--player-at` out in the town starts open. `--story` joins `starts_elsewhere`.
- **Quit to Title** puts the start back through the same `apply()`, so the story is `locked` again
  there.
- **Checked headless**, with `CETRA_SETTINGS_DIR` pointed at a scratch directory:
  - The haunting begun at the front door under `--slot` autosaves `"stage": "haunted"`. Loading it
    traces "haunted again" and frames go red.
  - Started on the porch with `--story locked` and saved at quit: `"open"`. E there opens the door
    on the hall with no story turn.
  - A haunted save edited to hold the front door open loads with it shut ("E   Open door").
  - The haunted save with its `story` section cut out loads locked: E at the front door begins the
    haunting.
