# Spec 13.16 -- cached point-light shadows: candles that respect walls, and dance

## Context

13.15 put 13 candles round silent's house, each a FIRE_FLAME driving an unshadowed point light
(range 2.5 m, `light_scale` 20). Unshadowed, a light ignores geometry: in this engine only a
shadow map tells a light where walls are (`cornell_leak`: 0.41 of the lit half through the
partition unshadowed, 0 shadowed). The user found the gallery sconces lighting the study's
bookcase behind their wall; every candle near an outside wall lights the facade, and the hall
bulb (13.10, unshadowed, 7 m) has always done the same.

Why they have no shadows (verified): the punctual pool is one depth array on unit 15, capped at
8 layers that are ALL re-rendered every frame (`shadow.h:14-21`); a point light takes 6, silent
uses 3. Nothing in the shadow path is cached, faces use the scene's 0.1-200 m near/far rather
than the light's range, point shadows have only a fixed 3x3 PCF, and silent's world is one mesh
per material across the whole street, so any shadow face culls nothing. Spec 9.8 already named
"cube-face culling and caching maps for static lights" as the answer for many shadowed points.

Outcome: every candle and the hall bulb blocked by walls (surfaces and haze); the 2 candles
nearest the camera redraw their shadow every frame from the flame's current position, so near
shadows dance; soft, flame-sized, contact-hardening edges; per-frame cost bounded; scenes that do
not opt in byte-identical.

User decisions: 2 hero candles; soft edges in this spec; the hall bulb opts in; fog shadowed.

## Design

**Storage: tiles in the existing unit-15 array, beside the rain tenant.** A new sampler is
impossible (the uber `pbr` declares all 16), so cached faces are 256x256 tiles carved from extra
layers after the per-frame pool and the rain layer (the rain map already lives in a corner of a
layer). One fixed tile size, so a tile's contents never depend on the array edge. At silent's
2048 edge a layer holds 64 tiles: 13 candles x 6 + 2 hero blocks + the bulb = 96 faces = 2
layers, 32 MiB, under a separate 64 MiB tile budget (refusal named once past it). Texel at the
candle's 2.5 m reach ~2 cm, at 0.4 m ~3 mm. Tile size is one constant, so 512 is a rebuild A/B at
the look step. The array edge stays sized by the per-frame lights only.

**API (`light.h`, AGENTS.md roles).** SETTINGS: `bool shadow_cache` (with `cast_shadows`: the
light and its casters are static; faces drawn once and kept), `float emitter_size` (metres
across the emitting body; >0 gives soft contact-hardening shadows, 0 a hard 3x3 PCF),
`float shadow_near` (0 = a ratio of range; the bulb sits inside its own glass). ENGINE-OWNED:
`int shadow_tile`, `vec3 shadow_origin`. `LightDesc` and `.cscn` keys to match. A tiled light
requires `range > 0` (its far plane), else warned once and left on the per-frame pool.
`ShadowSystem` SETTINGS: `tile_heroes` (default 0), `tile_fill_budget`, `tile_tolerance`
(set from the measured sway, >= 3x it), `tile_refresh` (instrument). Functions:
`shadow_live_tile`, `shadow_light_mapped`, `shadow_tiles_invalidate(_box)`, `shadow_tiles_probe`,
`shadow_tiles_map` (dump a light's six faces). Shared numbers in a new both-language
`include/shadow_tile_constants.glsl` (`rain_constants.glsl` rules).

**Shader.** Lights UBO gains a 7th vec4 `shadowTile` (xyz = origin the faces were drawn from,
w = first tile, -1 none); 12512 -> 14560 B, under 16 KB. A tiled light packs
`shadowMisc.y = SHADOW_TILE_MARK` (8): every existing "has a map" test stays true, so contact
shadows skip it with no edit, and the legacy lookup returns lit before indexing the 8-matrix
array (static-asserted). Emitter in `attenCutoff.z`, near in `upArea.w`, far = `posRange.w`
(= range). New `include/punctual_tiles.glsl` projects a cube face analytically from origin,
near and far (a per-face table matching `compute_perspective_light_space` + `light_space_up`,
with an 8-texel guard band, fov 93.7 deg) and addresses the tile from `textureSize`; a C mirror
`shadow_tile_project` is checked against the matrices. Gated by a new bit `PBR_FEAT_SHADOW_TILES`
(256; ALL becomes 511) that declares no sampler and no block, so the `pbr_frag` text a non-opting
variant compiles is unchanged. Receiver-plane bias reprojected onto the same face, clamped in
metres; taps clamped inside the tile. Soft edges: a PCSS path for tiles whenever
`emitter_size > 0` (independent of the directional `pcss_enabled`), reusing `POISSON16`,
`ign()` and the stochastic rotation; radius capped at the guard band.

**Depth pass (`render_shadow_depth_pass`).** Tiled lights skip the per-frame pool. After the
pool and before the rain pass: reconcile blocks (generation, drift past tolerance, near/far
change, refresh), pick heroes, fill dirty rest faces nearest-first within the fill budget, draw
hero faces, publish `shadow_tile`/`shadow_origin`. Rest faces: drawn once a flame has cast
(not from the wick tip), static caster set (no skinned, no wind), LOD 0, far plane = range.
Heroes: the K moving lights nearest the camera (a static bulb never qualifies) with hysteresis,
a second block each, redrawn every frame from the light's current position with the full
opaque set, faces whose pyramid misses the camera frustum skipped. Per-tile draws scissor the
clear. Profiler scopes "shadow tiles" and "shadow heroes". Captures force `tile_heroes` 0 and
restore it (beside `cascade_count`/`msm_enabled`). Invalidation: moved items invalidate the
faces containing their old or new bounds (the clock pendulum refreshes a face or two of the
bulb each frame, which is its real shadow); a graph change invalidates all at first, refined
to per-face signatures only if the probe shows churn; origin shift moves origins; array
reallocation blits valid tiles across (silent reallocates at frame 2 and on the first
flashlight toggle). Contact shadows' mapless count uses `shadow_light_mapped`.

**Fog.** The froxel inject's clustered point loop takes a single-tap tile visibility for tiled
lights (rest or hero), averaged by the fog's own accumulator; bias in metres.

**Shadow-only geometry (engine), added after phase 1's first attempt.** A mesh gains a shadow
role, a SETTINGS field on `Mesh`: `MESH_SHADOW_CASTS` (0, the default: drawn and casts, today's
behaviour), `MESH_SHADOW_NONE` (drawn, casts nothing), `MESH_SHADOW_ONLY` (casts, never drawn).
The draw list carries it as an item flag; camera passes and captures skip shadow-only items,
every shadow pass (the per-frame pool, the tiles, the cascades, the rain's occlusion map) skips
items that cast nothing. This is the standard feature under other names: Unity's
`ShadowCastingMode.ShadowsOnly`, Godot's `cast_shadow` "Shadows Only", Unreal's hidden mesh with
"Cast Hidden Shadow". A scene that sets no role is byte-identical.

**Silent.** `--profiler` (forest's pattern). What the camera draws stays as it is, one mesh
per material, marked `MESH_SHADOW_NONE`. `kit_finish` also builds a shape-only copy of the same
triangles, cut into spatial cells by triangle centroid with every material merged into one mesh
per cell (oversized triangles to a large cell, stable order, vertex remap), marked
`MESH_SHADOW_ONLY`, so a shadow face draws a few cells near it as a few draws. It carries what
casts today: the triangles of materials the opaque caster set takes, never the alpha-masked
ones. The cell size, and whether the rain's occlusion map reads the cells or the camera's
meshes, are set against the profiler in phase 1. Candles: `cast_shadows`, `shadow_cache`,
`emitter_size = flame width` (1.2 cm taper, 1.62 cm pillar). `tile_heroes = 2`,
`--tile-heroes N`, `--no-candle-shadows`. Hall bulb opts in (`shadow_near` 0.045,
`emitter_size` 0.06).

## Phases (run the app at the end of each that changes what it draws)

0. Branch `13.16-cached-point-shadows` off master in the main checkout (no worktree); spec
   copied in and committed alone. Silent `--profiler`; profiler rows at three pinned framings
   (study, great hall, street); candle sway from `fire_fixture --fire-probe` across frames,
   which sets the tolerance.
1. Shadow-only geometry: the engine's mesh shadow role, then silent's shape-only cells beside
   its per-material meshes. Checkpoint: goldens 0 px (no scene sets a role). Done when, at the
   three pinned framings against phase 0, the frame time does not regress, the camera's draws
   are unchanged and the tubes' shadow draws and triangles fall; pixel diff (expect ~0), look.
2. Plumbing with zero behaviour: fields, keys, constants, the 7th vec4 and packing.
   Checkpoint: goldens 0 px. If any moves, plan B: lights UBO text unchanged, tile slots in a
   feature-gated uniform array.
3. Tiles drawn, not shaded: layout, blocks, scissored draws, probe, face dumps; look at the
   dumps from cornell_point and fire_fixture.
4. Shading with hard PCF: the bit, `punctual_tiles.glsl`, `pbr_frag` dispatch, mapless count;
   look at cornell_leak with a cached point light (leak gone) and cornell_point cached vs not.
5. Soft edges: emitter 0 / 0.012 / 0.05 / 0.2, face seams.
6. Heroes: selection, hysteresis, face culling, capture clause; fire_fixture candle as a hero.
7. Invalidation and reallocation migration.
8. Fog tap. Checkpoint: `froxel_fog` and `water_submerged` goldens 0 px, else a
   `FROXEL_SHADOW_TILES` define variant of the inject.
9. Silent opt-in: candles, bulb, 2 heroes; profiler against `--no-candle-shadows`.
10. **Look with the user, before any gate**: the study bookcase behind the gallery sconces
    (dark now), the desk candlesticks up close (dancing; vs `--tile-heroes 0`), the west-wall and
    stair sconces (softness, acne), a floor-stand pillar, the hall bulb and the clock's shadow,
    the haze, the outside walls, ceilings above candles (no face seams), hero swaps while
    walking, 256 vs 512. Record what they say.
11. Gates, goldens, docs: AGENTS.md (shadows bullet, `shadow.c` row, silent row),
    `docs/shader-subsystems.md`, `docs/cli-reference.md`, spec as-built.

## Verification

- New gate group `shadow-tiles` (fixtures mutated in memory, as `run_contact_gate` does; silent
  gets none): off-identical (0 px when not opting in; `pbr-variant` ALL 511), leak (cornell_leak
  with a cached point light: far side <= 0.01 of lit, with the unshadowed falsifier >= 0.2),
  match (cached vs per-frame map; CPU projection vs matrices), fresh (frame 30 cached vs
  `--tiles-refresh` 0 px), draws (6n fills at frame 1, then 0; range culls), invalidate
  (explicit, light move past/within tolerance, node move, pool growth with migration), hero
  (current-origin redraws, static light never a hero, hysteresis), capture (no heroes in
  captures), pcss (penumbra grows with emitter and with distance from contact), contact (a cached
  light counts as mapped), rain (rain layer unchanged), fog (far-side in-scatter cut), seam,
  grazing, budget (named refusal past 64 MiB).
- `--only` the touched groups between phases (shadow, contact, rain, froxel, pbr-variant); the
  full suite and goldens once at the end, never while rendering.
- Silent: screenshots and the user's own walk, no arms; frame time from `--profiler` with and
  without candle shadows.

## Risks (each measured where named)

- A shader edit moving non-opting pixels (AGENTS.md's 130 px untaken-`if`): `pbr_frag` gated by
  the preprocessor; the UBO stride change checked alone (phase 2, plan B); the inject checked at
  phase 8 with a define fallback.
- Depth precision per light near/far; receiver-plane bias in non-linear tile depth; face-edge
  seams; scissored clears and blits; frozen LOD/excluded casters in rest maps; hero draw cost
  (the cells in reach; fallback K = 1); hero-swap pop; reallocation hitch; stochastic PCSS
  needing TAA.
- Shadow-only cells drifting from what is drawn: they are built from the same builders in the
  same `kit_finish`, never authored separately, so a change to the house changes both.

## As built

### Phase 0 -- measurements

- **Candle sway** (`fire_fixture --fire-probe`, six samples at frames 40-240, wick at
  (0.70, 1.40, -1.00)): the flame's centroid ranges 3.4 mm in x, 4.1 mm in z and 17.7 mm in y --
  the flame breathes far more than it sways, so the dance is mostly vertical. The largest
  excursion is about a centimetre; `tile_tolerance` 0.05 m is past 3x it. Intensity ran
  1.19-1.85 cd over the same samples.
- **Silent before any change** (`--profiler`, 960x540, frame 150, pinned framings):

  | framing | frame (wall) | opaque draws / tris | punctual shadow draws / tris |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | study | 19.7 ms | 38 / 348k | 109 / 925k |
  | great hall | 17.4 ms | 21 / 293k | 109 / 925k |
  | street | 18.6 ms | 68 / 439k | 109 / 925k |

  The two shadowed tubes draw 925k triangles a frame between them: the world is 472k (the
  rain occlusion pass draws all of it, 75 meshes), so each tube layer draws nearly the whole
  house and street, because a per-material mesh spans all of it. Six candle faces at that rate
  would be 2.8M triangles a candle. The chunking (phase 1) is what makes any of this affordable.

### Phase 1, first attempt -- chunking what the camera draws, measured and refused

Splitting each material's mesh into 3 m x 3.5 m cells, one mesh per material per cell, culled
as intended and made the frame slower. 801 meshes instead of about 64; the picture moved 101
and 83 px of 2M (draw-order ties on coplanar faces).

| framing | frame before | frame after | shadow draws / tris | camera draws | rain map draws |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| study | 19.7 ms | 24.3 ms | 109 / 925k -> 448 / 431k | 38 -> 274 | 75 -> 849 |
| great hall | 17.4 ms | 22.8 ms | the same | 21 -> 81 | the same |
| street | 18.6 ms | 27.3 ms | the same | 68 -> 480 | the same |

Half the shadow triangles, but every draw costs CPU -- most in the camera's pass, which binds a
material per draw (about 10 us in this debug build), least in the depth-only passes -- and a
cell cut per material multiplies the draws by the materials in it. A candle face seeing three
cells would still be 30-60 draws, and two heroes 360-720 a frame. The shadows want small
pieces and the camera wants few, so the two get different geometry: the camera's meshes stay
whole and the shadows get shape-only cells with every material merged (see "Shadow-only
geometry" above). The per-material chunking code was not committed.
