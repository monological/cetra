# Spec 13.16 -- cached point-light shadows: candles that respect walls, and dance

## Context

13.15 put 13 candles round silent's house, each a FIRE_FLAME driving an unshadowed point light
(range 2.5 m, `light_scale` 20). Unshadowed, a light ignores geometry: in this engine only a
shadow map tells a light where walls are (`cornell_leak`: 0.41 of the lit half through the
partition unshadowed, 0 shadowed). The user found the gallery sconces lighting the study's
bookcase behind their wall; every candle near an outside wall lights the facade, and the hall
bulb (13.10, unshadowed, 7 m) has always done the same.

Why they have no shadows (verified): the punctual pool is one depth array on unit 15, capped at
8 layers that are ALL re-rendered every frame (`shadow.h:14-21`); a point light takes 6, silent
uses 3. Nothing in the shadow path is cached, faces use the scene's 0.1-200 m near/far rather
than the light's range, point shadows have only a fixed 3x3 PCF, and silent's world is one mesh
per material across the whole street, so any shadow face culls nothing. Spec 9.8 already named
"cube-face culling and caching maps for static lights" as the answer for many shadowed points.

Outcome: every candle and the hall bulb blocked by walls (surfaces and haze); the 2 candles
nearest the camera redraw their shadow every frame from the flame's current position, so near
shadows dance; soft, flame-sized, contact-hardening edges; per-frame cost bounded; scenes that do
not opt in byte-identical.

User decisions: 2 hero candles; soft edges in this spec; the hall bulb opts in; fog shadowed.

## Design

**Storage: tiles in the existing unit-15 array, beside the rain tenant.** A new sampler is
impossible (the uber `pbr` declares all 16), so cached faces are 256x256 tiles carved from extra
layers after the per-frame pool and the rain layer (the rain map already lives in a corner of a
layer). One fixed tile size, so a tile's contents never depend on the array edge. At silent's
2048 edge a layer holds 64 tiles: 13 candles x 6 + 2 hero blocks + the bulb = 96 faces = 2
layers, 32 MiB, under a separate 64 MiB tile budget (refusal named once past it). Texel at the
candle's 2.5 m reach ~2 cm, at 0.4 m ~3 mm. Tile size is one constant, so 512 is a rebuild A/B at
the look step. The array edge stays sized by the per-frame lights only.

**API (`light.h`, AGENTS.md roles).** SETTINGS: `bool shadow_cache` (with `cast_shadows`: the
light and its casters are static; faces drawn once and kept), `float emitter_size` (metres
across the emitting body; >0 gives soft contact-hardening shadows, 0 a hard 3x3 PCF),
`float shadow_near` (0 = a ratio of range; the bulb sits inside its own glass). ENGINE-OWNED:
`int shadow_tile`, `vec3 shadow_origin`. `LightDesc` and `.cscn` keys to match. A tiled light
requires `range > 0` (its far plane), else warned once and left on the per-frame pool.
`ShadowSystem` SETTINGS: `tile_heroes` (default 0), `tile_fill_budget`, `tile_tolerance`
(set from the measured sway, >= 3x it), `tile_refresh` (instrument). Functions:
`shadow_live_tile`, `shadow_light_mapped`, `shadow_tiles_invalidate(_box)`, `shadow_tiles_probe`,
`shadow_tiles_map` (dump a light's six faces). Shared numbers in a new both-language
`include/shadow_tile_constants.glsl` (`rain_constants.glsl` rules).

**Shader.** Lights UBO gains a 7th vec4 `shadowTile` (xyz = origin the faces were drawn from,
w = first tile, -1 none); 12512 -> 14560 B, under 16 KB. A tiled light packs
`shadowMisc.y = SHADOW_TILE_MARK` (8): every existing "has a map" test stays true, so contact
shadows skip it with no edit, and the legacy lookup returns lit before indexing the 8-matrix
array (static-asserted). Emitter in `attenCutoff.z`, near in `upArea.w`, far = `posRange.w`
(= range). New `include/punctual_tiles.glsl` projects a cube face analytically from origin,
near and far (a per-face table matching `compute_perspective_light_space` + `light_space_up`,
with an 8-texel guard band, fov 93.7 deg) and addresses the tile from `textureSize`; a C mirror
`shadow_tile_project` is checked against the matrices. Gated by a new bit `PBR_FEAT_SHADOW_TILES`
(256; ALL becomes 511) that declares no sampler and no block, so the `pbr_frag` text a non-opting
variant compiles is unchanged. Receiver-plane bias reprojected onto the same face, clamped in
metres; taps clamped inside the tile. Soft edges: a PCSS path for tiles whenever
`emitter_size > 0` (independent of the directional `pcss_enabled`), reusing `POISSON16`,
`ign()` and the stochastic rotation; radius capped at the guard band.

**Depth pass (`render_shadow_depth_pass`).** Tiled lights skip the per-frame pool. After the
pool and before the rain pass: reconcile blocks (generation, drift past tolerance, near/far
change, refresh), pick heroes, fill dirty rest faces nearest-first within the fill budget, draw
hero faces, publish `shadow_tile`/`shadow_origin`. Rest faces: drawn once a flame has cast
(not from the wick tip), static caster set (no skinned, no wind), LOD 0, far plane = range.
Heroes: the K moving lights nearest the camera (a static bulb never qualifies) with hysteresis,
a second block each, redrawn every frame from the light's current position with the full
opaque set, faces whose pyramid misses the camera frustum skipped. Per-tile draws scissor the
clear. Profiler scopes "shadow tiles" and "shadow heroes". Captures force `tile_heroes` 0 and
restore it (beside `cascade_count`/`msm_enabled`). Invalidation: moved items invalidate the
faces containing their old or new bounds (the clock pendulum refreshes a face or two of the
bulb each frame, which is its real shadow); a graph change invalidates all at first, refined
to per-face signatures only if the probe shows churn; origin shift moves origins; array
reallocation blits valid tiles across (silent reallocates at frame 2 and on the first
flashlight toggle). Contact shadows' mapless count uses `shadow_light_mapped`.

**Fog.** The froxel inject's clustered point loop takes a single-tap tile visibility for tiled
lights (rest or hero), averaged by the fog's own accumulator; bias in metres.

**Shadow-only geometry (engine), added after phase 1's first attempt.** A mesh gains a shadow
role, a SETTINGS field on `Mesh`: `MESH_SHADOW_CASTS` (0, the default: drawn and casts, today's
behaviour), `MESH_SHADOW_NONE` (drawn, casts nothing), `MESH_SHADOW_ONLY` (casts, never drawn).
The draw list carries it as an item flag; camera passes and captures skip shadow-only items,
every shadow pass (the per-frame pool, the tiles, the cascades, the rain's occlusion map) skips
items that cast nothing. This is the standard feature under other names: Unity's
`ShadowCastingMode.ShadowsOnly`, Godot's `cast_shadow` "Shadows Only", Unreal's hidden mesh with
"Cast Hidden Shadow". A scene that sets no role is byte-identical.

**Silent.** `--profiler` (forest's pattern). What the camera draws stays as it is, one mesh
per material, marked `MESH_SHADOW_NONE`. `kit_finish` also builds a shape-only copy of the same
triangles, cut into spatial cells by triangle centroid with every material merged into one mesh
per cell (oversized triangles to a large cell, stable order, vertex remap), marked
`MESH_SHADOW_ONLY`, so a shadow face draws a few cells near it as a few draws. It carries what
casts today: the triangles of materials the opaque caster set takes, never the alpha-masked
ones. The cell size, and whether the rain's occlusion map reads the cells or the camera's
meshes, are set against the profiler in phase 1. Candles: `cast_shadows`, `shadow_cache`,
`emitter_size = flame width` (1.2 cm taper, 1.62 cm pillar). `tile_heroes = 2`,
`--tile-heroes N`, `--no-candle-shadows`. Hall bulb opts in (`shadow_near` 0.045,
`emitter_size` 0.06).

## Risks (each measured where named)

- A shader edit moving non-opting pixels (AGENTS.md's 130 px untaken-`if`): `pbr_frag` gated by
  the preprocessor; the UBO stride change checked alone (phase 2, plan B); the inject checked at
  phase 8 with a define fallback.
- Depth precision per light near/far; receiver-plane bias in non-linear tile depth; face-edge
  seams; scissored clears and blits; frozen LOD/excluded casters in rest maps; hero draw cost
  (the cells in reach; fallback K = 1); hero-swap pop; reallocation hitch; stochastic PCSS
  needing TAA.
- Shadow-only cells drifting from what is drawn: they are built from the same builders in the
  same `kit_finish`, never authored separately, so a change to the house changes both.

## As built

### Phase 0 -- measurements

- **Candle sway** (`fire_fixture --fire-probe`, six samples at frames 40-240, wick at
  (0.70, 1.40, -1.00)): the flame's centroid ranges 3.4 mm in x, 4.1 mm in z and 17.7 mm in y --
  the flame breathes far more than it sways, so the dance is mostly vertical. The largest
  excursion is about a centimetre; `tile_tolerance` 0.05 m is past 3x it. Intensity ran
  1.19-1.85 cd over the same samples.
- **Silent before any change** (`--profiler`, 960x540, frame 150, pinned framings):

  | framing | frame (wall) | opaque draws / tris | punctual shadow draws / tris |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | study | 19.7 ms | 38 / 348k | 109 / 925k |
  | great hall | 17.4 ms | 21 / 293k | 109 / 925k |
  | street | 18.6 ms | 68 / 439k | 109 / 925k |

  The two shadowed tubes draw 925k triangles a frame between them: the world is 472k (the
  rain occlusion pass draws all of it, 75 meshes), so each tube layer draws nearly the whole
  house and street, because a per-material mesh spans all of it. Six candle faces at that rate
  would be 2.8M triangles a candle. The chunking (phase 1) is what makes any of this affordable.

### Phase 1, first attempt -- chunking what the camera draws, measured and refused

Splitting each material's mesh into 3 m x 3.5 m cells, one mesh per material per cell, culled
as intended and made the frame slower. 801 meshes instead of about 64; the picture moved 101
and 83 px of 2M (draw-order ties on coplanar faces).

| framing | frame before | frame after | shadow draws / tris | camera draws | rain map draws |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| study | 19.7 ms | 24.3 ms | 109 / 925k -> 448 / 431k | 38 -> 274 | 75 -> 849 |
| great hall | 17.4 ms | 22.8 ms | the same | 21 -> 81 | the same |
| street | 18.6 ms | 27.3 ms | the same | 68 -> 480 | the same |

Half the shadow triangles, but every draw costs CPU -- most in the camera's pass, which binds a
material per draw (about 10 us in this debug build), least in the depth-only passes -- and a
cell cut per material multiplies the draws by the materials in it. A candle face seeing three
cells would still be 30-60 draws, and two heroes 360-720 a frame. The shadows want small
pieces and the camera wants few, so the two get different geometry: the camera's meshes stay
whole and the shadows get shape-only cells with every material merged (see "Shadow-only
geometry" above). The per-material chunking code was not committed.

### Phase 1 -- shadow-only geometry, as built

- **Correction to phase 0's frame times**: those runs, and every timing up to here, ran three
  at once (batched tool calls run concurrently), so they shared the GPU. Run one at a time, two
  identical study runs read 20.5 and 18.5 ms: the profiler reports its last half-second window,
  which at frame 150 can still hold the GI volume's warm-up captures. Frame time is therefore
  not the measure for changes this size here; the per-pass submission counts are exact.
- **Engine**: `Mesh.shadow_role` (`MESH_SHADOW_CASTS` 0 / `MESH_SHADOW_NONE` /
  `MESH_SHADOW_ONLY`). A shadow-only mesh gets a lane of its own, `DRAW_LANE_SHADOW_ONLY`, which
  every camera pass and capture already skips by naming the lanes it draws, so none of them was
  edited; a cast-nothing mesh carries `DRAW_NO_CAST`, which `caster_set_wants` refuses. The
  translucency test there now names BLEND and TRANSMISSIVE instead of "not OPAQUE", identical
  for the three old lanes. **The rain's cover reads what is DRAWN** (`SHADOW_CASTERS_RAIN`):
  pointed at the cells it went 75 -> 253 draws and 0.45 -> 0.9 ms of GPU, since its map spans
  the street and culls nothing, and the rain lands on the surfaces the camera sees anyway.
- **Silent**: `kit_finish` builds the cells first (they read the builders handing a mesh over
  empties), from the materials that cast plainly -- opaque, single-sided, still -- merged per
  3 m x 3.5 m cell, triangles over 6 m to one large cell. Glass and cutouts keep casting from
  their own meshes as before. 63 meshes and 224 shadow cells.
- **Measured**: the camera's draws unchanged at every framing (38 / 21 / 68); the rain map back
  to 75 draws; the tubes' shadows 109 draws, 925k triangles and 109 material switches -> 90,
  506k and 15. Pixels: 0 at all three pinned framings. Goldens: 31 of 31 at 0 px.

### Phase 3 -- tiles drawn, not shaded, as built

- **Layout.** Cells count from layer 0 across the whole array, row-major in a layer (16, 64 or
  256 a layer at edges 1024 / 2048 / 4096), so a light's first cell is the one number its lookup
  needs and the edge decides only how many fit in a layer. The region starts at
  `tile_base_layer` and a block is six consecutive cells; blocks sit in a fixed table of
  `SHADOW_TILE_MAX_BLOCKS` (42, the 64 MB budget at 256^2 x 4 bytes over six), kept by light
  identity frame to frame. `tile_layers` is derived each frame from the block high-water mark
  and the edge, so it never carries a count sized for one edge into another.
- **Correction to the design: the rain's layer is reserved only once it has rained.** The base
  is the high-water mark of the per-frame layers plus the rain's when it rains. Reserving it
  always cost `fire_fixture` 64 MB for a rain that never falls: a lone area light builds the
  array at 4096, where a layer is 64 MB. With the fix that fixture's array is 2 layers
  (128 MB: the hearth's layer and one tile layer holding 256 cells for 6 tiles). The tile layer
  at 4096 is the whole tile budget whatever the tile count -- within the budget, and the price of
  one edge for every layer.
- **Invalidation so far**: a block holds nothing when the region's `tile_generation` moved (an
  array rebuild, the base rising), when its planes changed, when its light is past
  `tile_tolerance` from where the faces were drawn, or under `tile_refresh`. A block is drawn
  only once its light emits (`intensity > 0`), so a candle's faces come from the flame and not
  the wick tip, and is published (`shadow_tile`, `shadow_origin`) only when all six faces are in.
- **Drawing**: after the per-frame lights, under the same program and depth policy; nearest the
  camera first within `tile_fill_budget` (0 = all); a scissored clear per tile
  (`begin_depth_tile`), viewport on the tile, fov `2 atan(256/240)` = 93.7 degrees, far = range,
  near = `shadow_near` or 0.002 x range. Caster set `SHADOW_CASTERS_KEPT`: opaque, minus anything
  whose surface moves under its node, at level 0. "Moves" is a new draw flag, `DRAW_STILL`
  (unskinned, unswayed, unmorphed), settled at build, which the occluder test now reads too
  instead of its own copy of the three terms. The plan's "no skinned, no wind" was the same idea
  short of the morph term.
- A cached point light with no range warns once and is drawn into the pool every frame. The
  array's log line now says how many of its layers are redrawn each frame rather than calling
  every layer a traversal.
- **Instruments**: `--tiles-probe` (the region, every block, each face's layer:x:y) and
  `--tile-map <light> <path>` (the six faces, +X -X +Y over -Y +Z -Z, grey by metres along the
  face's axis, white where nothing was drawn).
- **Looked at**: `cornell_point` with its lamp cached draws six faces at frame 1 and none after
  (probe at frame 3: `faces_drawn=0`, `valid=0x3f`); the dump shows the box from inside, white
  only past the open front, with the guard band's 8 texels showing the opening at the side
  faces' edges. `fire_fixture` with the candle cached: drawn from the flame (origin 20 mm above
  the wick, 3 mm from where it burned at frame 60), the -Y face holding the candle's own body
  below the flame, -Z the wall behind. Shading is unchanged (the packing still writes -1), so
  goldens: 31 of 31 at 0 px.

### Phase 4 -- shading with hard PCF, as built

- **Bit**: `PBR_FEAT_SHADOW_TILES` 256, `PBR_FEAT_ALL` 511, a scene bit set by
  `shadow_light_takes_tiles` over the light list (the same predicate the depth pass routes
  by, now exported). It declares no sampler: a tiled variant is `pbr-256` at 12 samplers.
- **`include/punctual_tiles.glsl`**: `tileShadow`, the per-frame lookup's 3x3 PCF and
  receiver plane over an analytic face projection -- three const tables (axis, right, up per
  face, written from glm_lookat over light_space_up), `TILE_INNER` = 240/256, the depth from
  the near (`upArea.w`) and far (`posRange.w`, the range). The plane's two neighbours are
  projected onto the point's own face, never re-chosen. Taps clamp to the tile, not the face.
  The cell is addressed from `textureSize`, so no uniform was added.
- **Dispatch**: the cluster branch keeps the light index under the bit; the shadow site takes
  `tileShadow` when the layer is at or past `SHADOW_TILE_MARK`. Without the bit the marker
  reaches `punctualShadow`, which reads it as lit before indexing anything.
- **Packing**: `_pack_cluster_light` takes the shadow system and asks `shadow_live_tile`; a
  live tile packs the marker, the origin and first cell, and the near. The contact-shadow
  mapless count asks `shadow_light_mapped` (a layer or a tile).
- **Looked at**: `cornell_leak` with its lamp a cached point light -- the far room black, as
  with a per-frame map, where unshadowed it is lit through the partition. Against the
  per-frame map, 1510 px past 30 codes, nearly all at the edge of the ceiling fixture's shadow
  on the ceiling 10 cm above the lamp, which a tile sees at grazing: traced analytically, the
  per-frame edge is about a pixel dark of the truth and the cached one 6 px light of it (2 cm
  on the ceiling, a texel or two). At 512 tiles the cached edge is 1-2 px off. That is the
  resolution question the look step asks; 512 would need the tile budget raised to 96 MB for
  silent. `cornell_point` cached against per-frame: 5322 px of 1M past 8 codes, the boxes'
  shadow edges displaced 1-2 px and one lit sliver along a silhouette, both texel-scale.
- Goldens: 31 of 31 at 0 px.

### Phase 5 -- soft edges, as built

- `tileSoft` in `punctual_tiles.glsl`, taken whenever the light packs an emitter size
  (`attenCutoff.z`, which was reserved; written only for a live tile): a 16-tap blocker search
  over the receiver-to-emitter cone at its widest (the near plane), then a 16-tap filter whose
  half-width is the penumbra by similar triangles, `w/2 (dR - dB) / dB`, in uv at the
  receiver. Physical rather than scaled: the cascades' `PCSS_PENUMBRA_SCALE` 4 is not used.
  Both radii stop at the guard band (8 texels), and taps clamp to the tile. Blocker
  separation 0.99 of the receiver's distance, the cascades' reason. The include moved below
  the cascades' PCSS in `pbr_frag` so it reads `POISSON16`; the rotation is theirs, under
  `pcssStochastic`, which with `pcssFrameIndex` now uploads ahead of the binder's
  no-directional early return (a cached light needs it in a scene with no sun).
- **Looked at**, `cornell_point` with the lamp cached at emitter 0 / 0.012 / 0.05 / 0.2 m:
  the edge widens with the emitter and stays hard where the front box meets the floor,
  widening away from it. Headless without TAA the unrotated disk steps visibly at 0.05 and
  0.2 (the stepped ghosts the cascades' POISSON16 comment records); with `--taa
  --headless-jitter` over 40 frames the rotated kernel converges to smooth penumbrae. 0.2
  saturates at the guard band. No face seams on the floor or walls at any size.
- Goldens: 31 of 31 at 0 px.

### Phase 6 -- heroes, as built

- `ShadowSystem.tile_heroes` (SETTINGS, 0 by default): the moving, emitting cached lights
  nearest the camera each get a second block, `hero` in `ShadowTileBlock`, redrawn every frame
  from where the light is now, with the full opaque caster set at the camera's level. A kept
  block records where its light was last frame (`seen`) and whether it has moved since
  (`moving`), so a light that never moves -- a bulb -- never becomes a hero.
- **Selection** (`tiles_pick_heroes`, every frame, at a count of 0 too, which is how a hero's
  block is let go): nearest first, a current hero ranked at `TILE_HERO_HOLD` 0.8 of its
  distance so two candles about equally far from a walking camera do not trade places. A
  released hero block stays a placeholder until a kept light or a new hero takes it.
- **Faces out of view are skipped**: a face's pyramid -- the light out to its far plane, with
  the guard band -- against the camera frustum built from `projection_matrix * view_matrix`
  (current at shadow time, where `view_proj` is last frame's); all five corners outside one
  plane is out. A skipped face keeps whatever it last held, which nothing on screen reads.
- **Publishing** is one pass after both draws: the hero block when a light has one, else the
  kept block once whole. A hero's kept block is left as it was, so a light demoted mid-walk
  goes back to faces at most `tile_tolerance` from its flame.
- **Captures** force `tile_heroes` 0 and restore it, beside the cascade count and MSM: a
  capture outlives its frame, so it must hold the kept shadow and not one instant of a
  flicker, and a hero's faces are culled to a view the capture does not share.

### Phase 7 -- invalidation and migration, as built

- **Moved casters** (`tiles_note_changes`, every frame the tiles are drawn): a KEPT-set item
  whose node's global transform differs from last frame's -- the transform walk's own `prev`,
  so this is bit-exact -- clears every kept face whose volume (the face's own matrix as a
  frustum) reaches its world box where it was or where it is. Skinned, swaying and morphing
  meshes are not in the KEPT set, so they redraw nothing. Hero blocks are never cleared: they
  are redrawn every frame anyway.
- **A graph change** (`scene_graph_epoch` moved) clears every kept block: a node added or freed
  has no previous frame for the draw list to show. The plan's refinement to per-face
  signatures waits on churn being seen; `fire_fixture` at frame 60 draws no kept face.
- `shadow_tiles_invalidate` / `shadow_tiles_invalidate_box` for what neither can see
  (geometry edited in place).
- **Origin shift** moves each block's origin and `seen` by the delta; the faces are drawn about
  their origin, so nothing is redrawn.
- **Migration**: `init_punctual_shadow_array`, on a rebuild, blits every whole kept face from
  the old array to the new one under the new layout (`tile_held_base` and the old edge to the
  region's new base and the new edge), then frees the old one; a block whose cells no longer
  fit is dropped. A base that rises WITHOUT a rebuild loses the tiles (a generation step),
  which is rare: the base rises with the per-frame layers, which grows the array. The
  generation step on every rebuild is gone. Exercised by silent's flashlight in phase 9.
- Goldens: 31 of 31 at 0 px.

### Phase 8 -- the fog's tap, as built

- `include/tile_lookup.glsl` is the projection and addressing split out of
  `punctual_tiles.glsl` (which now includes it and keeps the surface's PCF and soft edge),
  plus `tileVisibility`: ONE tap, biased in metres along the face rather than in depth, for a
  point that is not a surface. It declares no sampler; the includer's `punctualShadowMaps` is
  read. `punctualCubeFace` moved to `include/cube_face.glsl` so the fog, which declares its own
  punctual sampler and cannot include `punctual_shadow.glsl`, chooses faces by the same rule.
- `froxel_inject_frag`'s clustered point walk multiplies a light's attenuation by the tap when
  it carries `SHADOW_TILE_MARK`, bias `FOG_TILE_BIAS` 0.02 m; the fog's own accumulator averages
  the binary answers, as it does the cascades'. `shadow_publish_to_postfx` binds the punctual
  array for the fog whenever a light has a live tile, asked of `shadow_live_tile`, the
  packing's own question, so the array is bound exactly when a light carries the marker.
- **Checkpoint**: `froxel_fog` and `water_submerged` at 0 px (all 31 goldens at 0), so the
  define variant of the inject is not needed: the untaken branch costs this driver nothing.
- **Looked at**: `cornell_leak` with the lamp a point light, `--fog --fog-density 0.3`, mean

### Phase 9 -- silent opts in, as built

- **Candles** (`candles_light`, which now takes whether they shadow): `cast_shadows`,

  `tile_heroes` 2 by default (`DEFAULT_TILE_HEROES`); `--tile-heroes N`, and
  `--no-candle-shadows`, which turns the candles' off and leaves the bulb's.

  and pendulum went dark under the bulb: the punctual depth pass draws glass solid (it has no
  transmittance map), and a clear pane at transmission 0.97 standing in front of the dial put
  it in full shadow. The two cached sets now leave the blend and transmissive lanes out --
  KEPT, and a new HERO set, which is OPAQUE without glass -- so the dial reads from the doorway
  again, and a candle behind a window lights through it. The per-frame pool keeps its policy.
