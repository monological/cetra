# A3 Subplan — Screen-Space Contact Shadows

## Context

Third feature of the AAA rendering roadmap (`specs/9.0-aaa-rendering-roadmap.md`, Tier 1 item 3),
after A1 (clustered forward, 9.1) and A2 (LTC area lights, 9.2), both merged. Shadow maps
physically cannot resolve the last few centimeters of contact — a CSM texel at scene scale is
larger than the gap under a foot, a collar, a hand on armor — so grounded objects float. The
Uncharted 4-style fix: an 8-step per-pixel ray march through the depth buffer along the key
light, darkening receivers with nearby depth occluders. Post-only: zero pbr_frag changes, zero
material texture units, no frame latency (same-frame composite in tonemap beside AO).

**Decisions locked (from session direction):**
- **ImGui controls ARE in scope** (reversal of A2's no-GUI stance, per the area-lights-checkbox
  exchange): written to the existing panel idioms, behavior verified headless, placement
  verified interactively by the user before merge. Its own commit.
- **Default OFF** — opt-in `--contact-shadows`; keeps every baseline byte-identical.
- Comments follow AGENTS.md `## Comments` (contract at declarations, mechanism at
  implementation site, no caller-naming, no self-praise).

## Verified integration facts (explored on current master)

- **Aux G-buffer** (`fx->aux_texture`, RGBA32F, full internal res): `.xy` motion (UV units),
  `.z` linear view-space Z (negative in front, **0 = sky sentinel — and the shadow-catcher
  floor writes it too** (`catcher_frag.glsl:67`), so contact shadows neither land on nor cast
  from the virtual floor; same as GTAO). Resolved once at `postfx.c:1558-1561`.
- **Key light already published**: `shadow_publish_to_postfx` (`shadow.c:713-775`, called
  `engine.c:1759-1760`) fills `fx->fog_light_count` + `fx->fog_light_dir[0]` (normalized
  world-space TRAVEL direction of the slot-0 shadow caster). `postfx_run` already receives
  `mat4 view` (`postfx.c:1513-1515`), so toward-light view dir =
  `glm_mat4_mulv3(view, -fog_light_dir[0], 0.0f)` C-side. **The roadmap's predicted
  "shadow_publish_to_postfx extension" is unnecessary — zero shadow.c changes.**
  Consequence to document: `--no-shadows` (count 0) silently disables contact shadows.
- **Reused machinery**: `viewPosFromLinZ` (`include/depth.glsl`); R8 targets at AO res
  (`ssao_width/height` = internal/2); the generic `ssao_blur` program (its static `texelSize`
  is already correct for AO res; `edgeAware`/textures are per-draw — no leak between
  consumers); `run_temporal_accum` (`postfx.c:1143-1160`) + caller-owned `PingPong`; the IGN
  hash + `frameIndex` rotation gated on `temporal` (SSR idiom `ssr_frag.glsl:119-126` —
  frozen when TAA is off, which is what keeps headless goldens deterministic). **No noise
  texture needed.**
- **Tonemap**: `aoFactor = mix(1.0, aoVisibility(), aoStrength)` at `tonemap_frag.glsl:243-245`
  multiplies HDR in `sceneToToned` (`:186`); sharpen taps reuse the centre `aoFactor`, so
  folding csFactor into that variable after the mix gets sharpen-consistency free and stays
  independent of specular occlusion AND of `aoEnabled`. Sampler units 0-9 taken; unit 10 free.
- **Predicates/patterns**: `postfx_wants_aux_gbuffer` (`postfx.c:1098-1109`) gains
  `|| contact_shadows_enabled`; lazy targets on the `postfx_ensure_fog_targets` template
  (`postfx.c:655-666`); debug enum ends at `POSTFX_DEBUG_SPEC_OCC = 7` (`postfx.h:52`) with
  dispatch `tonemap_frag.glsl:201-239` + suppression table `postfx.c:1862-1877`; history
  invalidation via the AO `ao_accum_ran` single-site flag pattern (`postfx.c:1643,1701-1702`);
  programs via `create_post_program(name, frag_str)` (`program.c:807-814`).
- **CLI**: `render_args.h` + parse (`--fog` at `render.c:486-490`) + apply block
  (`render.c:1385-1434`) + scene-scaled defaults block (`render.c:2054-2071`).
  **GUI**: effect-group helpers in `_engine_gui_panel` (`engine.c` ~1555, SSR distance slider
  precedent for log-scale sliders).

## Design (validated by the design agent; corrections folded in)

**Shader `contact_shadow_frag.glsl`** (new; AO res; R8; `.r` visibility, 1 = lit):
- Inputs: `linDepthTex` (unit 0 = aux), `normalsTex` (unit 1, opportunistic), `useNormalsTex`,
  `projection`, `lightDirVS` (unit vector TOWARD light, view space), `csDistance`,
  `csThickness`, `temporal`, `frameIndex`. Includes `depth.glsl` only. `invFocal` and the
  near-plane distance derive from `projection` in-shader (GTAO/SSR idioms).
- Flow: sample `linZ` + `fwidth(linZ)` BEFORE any divergent branch; sky/catcher sentinel
  (`linZ >= -1e-4`) writes 1.0. Reconstruct P. Opportunistic N·L early-out when
  `useNormalsTex` (back-facing surfaces are already dark; kills terminator acne; zero-normal
  hair marker falls through). Near-plane clamp: `tMax = min(csDistance,
  (-nearV - P.z)/lightDirVS.z)` when the ray heads toward the camera.
- **March is 2 MADs/step, not a mat4 multiply**: projection is linear in homogeneous coords,
  so precompute `clipP = projection*vec4(P,1)` and `clipL = projection*vec4(lightDirVS,0)`;
  each of 8 steps evaluates `clipS = clipP + t*clipL`, breaks on `clipS.w <= 1e-4` or UV
  off-screen, fetches scene `linZ` at the marched UV (skipping sky samples), and accepts an
  occluder when `diff = sceneZ - rayZ` (positive = ray point behind surface, RH-verified,
  same test as SSR) lands in `(bias, csThickness)`.
- Screen-reach clamp before marching (GTAO `MAX_SCREEN_RADIUS` idiom, ~0.15 UV) so
  near-camera pixels don't stride across the frame.
- **Bias**: `0.02*csDistance + 2.0*fwidth(linZ)` — the step-quantization term scales with
  march length, the slope term with per-texel depth gradient (which grows with |z| under
  perspective automatically). No absolute constants: scene scales here span meters to
  hundreds of units.
- Jitter: IGN hash of `gl_FragCoord.xy`, `+ frameIndex*5.588238` ONLY when `temporal==1`.
- On hit: `occ = (1 - t/csDistance) * screen-edge smoothstep` (contact-hardening fade over
  the FULL distance so strength decays continuously to 0 as csDistance -> 0), then
  `visibility = 1 - occ`, break.

**C-side (postfx.h/c)**:
- Fields: `contact_shadows_enabled` (false), `cs_strength` (0.6), `cs_distance` (0.3),
  `cs_thickness` (0.15), `cs_ready`, `cs_fbo[2]/cs_texture[2]` (R8, AO res, raw+blurred),
  `cs_history` PingPong (**R16F** — 0.9-feedback accumulation bands in 8 bits, ao_history
  precedent), `contact_shadow_program`. `postfx_ensure_contact_targets` on the fog template;
  unconditional teardown in `free_postfx`. New `POSTFX_DEBUG_CONTACT = 8`.
- Run site: after the normals resolve (`postfx.c:1608-1613`), before `ao_result_tex`
  (`:1636`). Gate: `contact_shadows_enabled && aux_written && fog_light_count > 0 &&
  cs_distance > 0.0f && ensure_targets`. **The `cs_distance > 0` gate is load-bearing**: it
  makes `--cs-distance 0` take the identical off code path (hard 0-px guarantee instead of
  trusting GPU `mix` exactness).
- Per frame when active: march pass (units 0=aux, 1=normals-or-0) -> `ssao_blur` program
  into `cs_texture[1]` with **`edgeAware` hard-coded 1** (silhouette bleed is worse for a
  directional shadow than for AO) -> `run_temporal_accum` into `cs_history` when
  `taa_resolving`. One invalidation site after the block:
  `if (!cs_accum_ran) fx->cs_history.valid = false;` — structurally covers all six skip
  branches.
- Tonemap composite: `csTex` unit 10; after the aoFactor mix:
  `if (csEnabled == 1) aoFactor *= 1.0 - csStrength * (1.0 - texture(csTex, TexCoords).r);`
  (exact identity at cs==1). Debug view 8 shows the raw term; suppression-table entry when
  inactive.

**App (apps/render)**: `--contact-shadows`; `--cs-distance <f>` (implies enable; 0 = off via
the C gate); `--cs-debug` (implies enable); help text; scene-scaled defaults in the existing
`scene_radius` block: distance `max(0.3, scene_radius*0.02)` when unset, thickness
`0.5*distance` when unset.

**GUI (engine.c)**: effect group "Contact Shadows" (checkbox) + log-scale distance slider
(SSR Distance precedent) + strength slider, beside the AO group. Behavior verified headless
(flag == C1's pixels); placement verified interactively by the user.

**Out of scope (stated)**: spot/point contact shadows; `.cscn` post key (later batch);
default-on flip; contact shadows on the shadow-catcher virtual floor (sentinel-limited, same
as GTAO).

## Risks (ranked)

1. **Acne at grazing sun elevations** (high) — the bias law + N·L early-out are the
   mitigations; tune `BIAS_FRAC` on the fixture at `--sun-elevation 10-20` with the debug
   view.
2. **Triple-darkening** where CSM + AO + contact stack (high likelihood, low severity —
   artistic): modest 0.6 default strength.
3. **Off-gate drift from the tonemap edit** (medium): uniform-guarded branch + exact-identity
   multiply; spec-occ/edgeAware precedents held byte-identical; exposure pinning per
   CLAUDE.md is mandatory in the compare.
4. **Peter-panning** (medium): bias too large detaches the shadow — keep the fraction at
   0.02, let `fwidth` carry slope.
5. **Thickness mistuning** (medium): too small leaks light through thin geometry; too large
   streaks foreground shadows onto background. 0.5x distance default, uniform exposed.
6. **Step banding without TAA** (medium): 8 steps + frozen IGN + bilateral blur is the proven
   non-temporal recipe.
7. **Silent no-op** with `--no-shadows` / no directional caster / catcher-only receivers
   (low, by design): documented in help text + spec.
