# 11.70 — a mirror in every room (roadmap C4: clustered specular probes)

## Context

`scene->probe` is singular: ONE parallax-corrected reflection probe per scene, bound by
STEALING sampler unit 12 from the global IBL prefilter (`bind_reflection_probe`,
probe.c:138-156) — a hard replace, decided per scene rather than per fragment. A character
walking from a lit hall into a side room keeps the hall's reflections on every shiny
surface. A4 gave diffuse GI a spatial structure (spec 9.7) and specular never got one.

C4 makes it N probes with **per-fragment selection and blending**: each fragment gathers the
probes whose influence boxes reach it through a per-froxel mask on A1's cluster grid, blends
them by box weight, and lets the remainder fall to the global environment. That is the model
UE's reflection captures use and the one the roadmap prescribed ("A1's cluster grid for
probe→cluster assignment ... A4's octahedral atlas + `include/octahedral.glsl` for storage,
so N probes cost one sampler, not N").

**Chosen over per-draw selection** — the cheaper Unity-style answer, where the CPU picks one
probe per draw item and binds it into unit 12 exactly as today. That costs no shader change
at all and was seriously considered. It fails on the motivating geometry: a floor mesh
running from the hall through the doorway into the side room is ONE draw, so it keeps one
probe across its whole surface and the artifact survives at mesh granularity. The fixture
this spec ships is built around exactly that mesh, so the choice is falsifiable rather than
asserted.

**Chosen over a `samplerCubeArray` on unit 12** — killed on three independent grounds, each
verified against source. Array layers share one size, and the three live sizes disagree (env
1024/9 mips, ibl.h:12-13; env 256/7 under `--sky`, sky.h:31-33; probes 512/8, probe.h:18-20)
— unifying up costs ~600 MB at cap 8, unifying down moves every IBL golden. `samplerCubeArray`
needs GLSL 400 against a shader corpus that is uniformly `#version 330 core` (96 files, zero
exceptions), which `punctual_shadow.glsl:11` and `:127` already refused twice in writing and
which `mboit.glsl:12` and `msm.glsl:12` depend on for their float-arithmetic contracts; and
the bump cannot be scoped to one shader, because water_frag declares the same unit-12
sampler and GL 4.1 has no `glTextureView` to re-expose a layer as a plain cube for SSR. And
one declaration per unit means even the single-probe path must sample `vec4(dir, layer)`,
which destroys the verbatim-branch identity below.

Probe ARMING has two sites, not one: the `--probe` flag family AND `environment.probe_scene`
in a `.cscn` (`apps/render/src/cscene_apply.c:79-82`). The second is live in the gate suite —
`abandoned_window_shadowed.cscn` runs a full six-face scene capture inside the submission
group's shadowcull-live arm — so that group joins the must-stay-green list and a `.cscn` can
arm a probe with no flag on the command line.

## The core decisions

**Two shader modes, not one.** `probeCount <= 1` keeps today's expressions VERBATIM: the
stolen unit 12, the scalar `probePos` / `probeBoxMin` / `probeBoxMax` / `probeIntensity` /
`probeMaxLOD` / `probeBoxFade` uniforms, and the exact parallax-and-feather block at
pbr_frag.glsl:2009-2035. The multi path arms only at `probeCount >= 2`, as a third branch
beside the existing pair. This is the file's own byte-identity idiom, stated twenty lines
above the code it protects (pbr_frag.glsl:1949-1954, the `splitAmbientSpec` precedent: *"The
inline branches keep the original expressions verbatim: they are the byte-identity gate for
every non-split mode"*). No committed golden passes `--probe`, so **every existing golden is
0 px by construction rather than by tolerance**, and the two probe consumers that DO exist
in the suite — emissive-spec and shadowcull-live — both ride the verbatim branch at N=1.

**Storage: an octahedral 2D atlas with roughness ROWS, riding the `giAtlasTex` declaration
on unit 14.** pbr_frag declares exactly sixteen samplers and the driver counts DECLARATIONS,
not distinct units (pbr_frag.glsl:314-318), so a seventeenth cannot link and N probes must
ride something already declared. Unit 14's declaration is already a 2D atlas of octahedral
probe tiles (`gi_volume.glsl:26`) — the same shape of thing, addressed by the same
`octahedral.glsl` mapping. Roughness is ROWS rather than mips because `glGenerateMipmap`
would bleed across the tile gutters that make an octahedral tile filterable at all.

When the GI volume and the probe set are both live they are **tenants of one physical
texture**: GI keeps rows `[0, gi_h)` at its exact current coordinates (`gi_tile_origin`,
gi_volume.c:30-39 — and `giAtlasSize` is already a uniform, so GI's UVs stay texel-exact
under a taller texture), the specular region appends below. With GI absent the probe set
owns the texture alone. The tenancy carries one ordering constraint that is asserted rather
than assumed: `gi_ensure_targets` allocates LAZILY on the first `gi_volume_update` and
early-returns once `gi->atlas` is set, so the probe atlas must exist before that frame or
the two silently split into separate textures. The probe sweep runs at app load, before the
engine loop's first frame, so the constraint holds by construction today.

**Storage stays swappable.** Every fact about where a probe's radiance lives is inside
`probe_atlas.c/h` and `include/probe_specular.glsl`. Nothing else in the engine knows.

**Prefiltering reuses the shipped GGX kernel.** Per probe the capture pipeline is today's,
unchanged: `scene_capture_faces` supersampled at 1024 with `SCENE_CAPTURE_RADIANCE` (a
mirror must keep seeing the lamp — render.h:52-59 is where that flag's whole purpose is
written down), then `ibl_prefilter_cubemap` to a 512/8-mip cube. A NEW pass
(`probe_project_frag.glsl`) then resamples each mip into one atlas roughness row:
`octahedral.glsl`'s `octDirFromTexel` for the direction, a one-texel gutter wrap in the
style of `gi_project_frag.glsl:65-75`, and a plain `textureLod(srcCube, dir, row)` body.
There is deliberately no second importance sampler — the tested BRDF kernel keeps sole
custody, and nothing exists for it to drift against.

The capture and prefilter cubes are SHARED SCRATCH across the sweep and freed after
projection. Eight probes resident cost ~34 MB, where the single probe today retains ~63 MB
(both cubes are `GL_RGB16F`, 6 B/texel: a ~50.3 MB raw 1024 mip chain plus a ~12.6 MB 512/8
prefilter, kept for the lifetime of the scene).

**Selection: a cluster-grid MASK, not an index pool.** At a cap of 8 probes a per-froxel
8-bit mask is 3072 bytes flat — no offsets, no prefix sum, no truncation states, and none of
the two-pass touched-bitset machinery the light path needs at 128 lights and 6144 indices.
Assignment bounds each probe's box by its sphere and reuses `_sphere_touches_cluster` and
`_tile_range_for_sphere` (light_cluster.c:84-146) unchanged; over-coverage is harmless
because the fragment computes the exact box weight anyway, which is zero outside the box.
Armed at count >= 2; at count <= 1 no mask is ever built.

**Capture cadence: a load-time full sweep. Relight is deferred, and said out loud.** The set
captures sequentially at load and attaches only after ALL probes succeed, so no probe ever
photographs another's reflections and a headless run sees a converged result from frame 0 —
the `first_pass` reasoning from gi_volume.c:269-282, which itself cites the reflection probe
as the precedent it follows. `probe_set_mark_dirty` exists as the re-arm seam and nothing
calls it this spec; a budgeted re-capture belongs in the `gi_volume_update` slot
(engine.c:2552-2564) when relight arrives. `captures_total` makes the converge-then-idle
claim checkable from outside the process.

## Format — the UBO block, the atlas, the weights

**One new block at binding 9** (0-8 are taken, ubo.h:28-36). The fragment stage matters more
than the binding here: pbr_frag's include closure already declares 8 uniform blocks
(LightsBlock, ClusterBlock, ClusterIndexBlock, IesBlock, ViewParams, ShoreFilmBlock,
VtPageBlock, RoadsBlock) against GL 4.1's guaranteed 12, so this is the ninth and the plan
is to stop. Descriptors are static and masks are view-dependent, but the whole block is
3.6 KB — splitting it would spend a tenth declaration to save an upload the frame's three
12 KB light uploads already dwarf.

The block re-uploads whole, per `render_current_scene` invocation, while armed. That is the
light-grid cadence and NOT the IES revision cadence, deliberately: masks are view-space and
a capture face re-enters with its own view, so a per-frame-once upload would leave every
capture face reading another view's masks. A whole-block write also has no undefined tail,
which is the trap the IES table was placed to avoid (ubo.h:100-105).

```c
// ubo.h
#define UBO_BINDING_PROBES 9
// probe_set.h
#define PROBE_SET_MAX 8
typedef struct GpuProbeDesc {                     // 64 B
    float pos_intensity[4];   // xyz capture position (world), w intensity
    float box_min_fade[4];    // xyz parallax box min,         w box_fade
    float box_max_rows[4];    // xyz parallax box max,         w max row index (7.0)
    float atlas_rect[4];      // x u0, y v0 (texels), z row-0 tile size, w gutter
} GpuProbeDesc;
typedef struct GpuProbeBlock {
    int32_t probe_counts[4];  // x count (0 disarms), y mask bits set (diagnostic), zw pad
    float atlas_params[4];    // x 1/atlas_w, y 1/atlas_h, z atlas_w, w atlas_h
    GpuProbeDesc descs[PROBE_SET_MAX];            //  512 B
    uint32_t cluster_masks[LC_CLUSTER_COUNT / 4]; // 3072 B, one 8-bit mask per froxel
} GpuProbeBlock;                                  // 3616 B
```

The GLSL mirror packs the masks as `uvec4 probeClusterMasks[192]` and decodes with the
`clusterWord` idiom — std140 gives a scalar array a vec4 stride, so a raw `uint[3072]` would
silently quadruple the block (lights_ubo.glsl:48-49):

```glsl
uint probeMaskAt(uint ci) {
    return (probeClusterMasks[ci >> 4u][(ci >> 2u) & 3u] >> ((ci & 3u) * 8u)) & 0xFFu;
}
```

**The atlas** is `GL_RGBA16F`, no mips, sampled with `textureLod(..., 0.0)`. Each probe owns
a column of 8 roughness rows at 512, 256, 128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 8, each with a **1-texel
gutter** — GI's own `GI_TILE_BORDER`, and the reason is mechanical rather than aesthetic:
`wrapToInterior` tests the outermost ring and steps exactly one texel in, so a 2-texel gutter
would leave its outer ring unmirrored unless the wrap is generalized. A column is
514 × 1040 ≈ 4.28 MB, so 34.2 MB at cap 8. Row r carries roughness r/7, the same
`mip / (mips - 1)` convention `ibl_prefilter_cubemap` uses (ibl.c:477), so the lookup is two
hardware-bilinear fetches and one `mix`. `--probe-set-res 1024` is the near-mirror sharpness
dial at ~17 MB/probe. Tenanted with GI the texture is
`max(spec_w, gi_w) × (gi_h + spec_h)`.

**The weights** are Lagarde-normalized with the remainder falling to the environment:

```
w_i  = clamp((1 - maxComp(|P - c_i| / h_i)) / max(fade_i, 1e-3), 0, 1)   // 1 inside, 0 at the face
W    = sum of w_i over the fragment's cluster mask
spec = sum((w_i / max(W, 1)) * L_i(P, R, rough))  +  max(1 - W, 0) * [the verbatim env expression]
```

`L_i` is the per-probe parallax AABB intersect and direction feather — the same arithmetic
the single path runs, factored so the two cannot diverge. Every term is continuous in P;
W > 1 renormalizes; W < 1 falls to the environment expression the `probeEnabled == 0` branch
already uses.

**What the emissive-spec bar actually measures**, since this spec has to keep it green: a
probe photographs an emissive quad that an 11.49 LTC panel also integrates analytically, and
`EMISSIVE_SPEC_MAX = 1.10` caps the worst derived-over-authored RATIO of that double count
— recorded at 1.0386, which is a ratio and not a lift (the measured max LIFT is 2.6464,
floored separately by `EMISSIVE_SPEC_LIFT_MIN = 1.5`). The multi path's probe term is a
convex combination of per-probe values, so the double-counted contribution cannot exceed the
worst single probe's and the bound transfers. `probe-set-spec-count` measures it anyway.

## Mechanism

**New files**

- `cetra/src/probe_set.c/h` — `ReflectionProbeSet` (probes, count, ready, atlas ids,
  `captures_total`, `mask_digest`); create/free; `probe_set_primary()` returning probes[0] or
  NULL, which is what keeps every single-probe consumer a one-line change;
  `probe_set_capture_all` (drain the async loader once, then per probe the existing capture
  plus projection against shared scratch, `ready` only if all succeed);
  `probe_set_shift_origin` looping the existing per-probe subtract (the atlas is
  texture-space and needs nothing); the PostFX publish; `probe_set_mark_dirty` as the
  relight seam; the debug atlas blit on the `gi_volume_debug_blit` pattern.
- `cetra/src/probe_atlas.c/h` — the swappable storage module: allocation including the GI
  tenant region, the per-(probe, row) rect, the projection pass, the `atlas_rect` fill.
- `cetra/shaders/probe_project_frag.glsl` — `octDirFromTexel` front matter, gutter wrap,
  cube resample.
- `cetra/shaders/include/probe_specular.glsl` — the ProbeBlock declaration, `probeMaskAt`,
  `probeBoxWeight`, and `probeSetSpecular(sampler2D atlas, uint mask, vec3 P, vec3 R,
  float rough)` returning `vec4(rgb, W)`. The sampler is a FUNCTION PARAMETER, which GLSL
  330 allows: pbr_frag passes `giAtlasTex` and ssr_frag passes its own `probeAtlasTex`, so
  two programs share one implementation and there is no second copy of the weight math.

**Changed files**

- `ubo.h/.c` — binding 9, the size constant, the asserts, one `ubo_wire_program_block` line.
- `light_cluster.h/.c` — `probes_ubo` on the context beside `ies_ubo`; in
  `light_cluster_build_and_upload`, strictly AFTER `_fill_index_pool` and touching none of
  the three light arrays: per probe a view-space sphere, the slice and tile ranges,
  `_sphere_touches_cluster`, OR the bits; then pack, digest, count, upload. Disarming
  uploads one zero block so a scene switch cannot leave stale masks.
- `scene.h/.c` — `probe` becomes `probe_set`; the free path; the origin-shift site.
- `render.c` — at the probe bind, count <= 1 calls `bind_reflection_probe(primary)`
  verbatim; count >= 2 sets `probeEnabled = 0`, leaves unit 12 holding the environment, and
  the probe set binds the shared atlas on unit 14 (with GI live, `gi_volume_bind` binds the
  same texture and the two agree). The debug background stays single-mode.
- `pbr_frag.glsl` — a third branch at `probeCount >= 2`, with both existing branches
  byte-untouched. Clearcoat keeps its current behaviour in single mode, which means it keeps
  the pre-existing LOD mismatch (it samples the bound probe with `maxReflectionLOD` 8 against
  the probe chain's 7) — byte-identity outranks fixing that here, and it is named rather
  than silently inherited. In multi mode clearcoat reads the global environment, which is
  what its own comment has always claimed it does.
- `engine.c` + `postfx.h/.c` + `ssr_frag.glsl` — count <= 1 publishes today's scalar fields
  verbatim, so SSR is bit-identical; count >= 2 publishes the atlas and a multi flag, and
  ssr_frag calls the shared include at the catcher's world position with the same per-pixel
  blend, keeping its `* preExposure` conversion (captures render at unity pre-exposure).
- `gi_volume.c` — `gi_ensure_targets` gains an adopt-external-texture path behind an
  `owns_atlas` flag, warning once if GI has already allocated when a probe atlas arrives.
  Every GI coordinate is unchanged.
- `cetra/src/cscene.h/.c` — `CSceneProbe`; a top-level `"probes": [...]` array on the
  `parse_fog_volumes` idiom (required-key skip-and-warn, cap-overflow warn) PLUS a closed key
  list through `warn_unknown_keys`, which fogVolumes itself lacks and the roads parser has.
  Collision policy, said out loud: a `.cscn` carrying both `environment.probe_scene` and a
  `probes` array takes the array and warns that the flag was ignored.
- `apps/render/src/render.c` — authored probes build the set and capture it; absent, today's
  auto-placement path runs verbatim and becomes set[0] at count 1. `--probe` beside an
  authored array warns and the array wins.
- `gui.c` — verbatim controls at count 1, per-probe groups above it.

## Flags and the diagnostic

`--probe`, `--probe-pos`, `--probe-scene`, `--probe-debug` are unchanged. New:
`--probe-set-res N` (row-0 tile size, default 512), `--probe-set-probe N` (print every N
frames), `--probe-set-debug` (atlas blit). One printf shape, one gate regex:

```
probe-set frame= count= mode= atlas= captures= mask_bits= digest=
probe-set probe idx= pos= box= rect= rows=        (final block, one per probe)
```

`digest` is FNV-1a over the cluster masks, so determinism is one comparison; `mode` is
none / single / multi; `captures` is `captures_total`, which is what makes converge-then-idle
checkable rather than merely claimed.

## Gates — the new `probe-set` group

`assets/gen_cornell_rooms.py` gains a fourth variant, **cornell_rooms**. It generates three
today (cornell_box, cornell_leak, cornell_point) and writes `.gltf` ONLY — the `.cscn`
siblings are hand-authored, and cornell_rooms follows that rather than teaching the generator
to write scene files. The generator edit lands under the fixture-gen gate, which asserts
every `gen_*.py` reproduces its committed assets, so all four must emit byte-for-byte.

The variant: two rooms joined by a doorway, with ONE polished dielectric floor mesh spanning
both — that shared mesh is the anti-vacuity of the whole design, since it is precisely the
geometry per-draw selection cannot light correctly — and a spot in room A only. Roughness
0.08 for the emissive-spec stability reason (a metal collapses every lift denominator toward
black). The hand-authored `.cscn` carries `probes: [A, B]` with boxes overlapping at the
doorway. Arms ride `--sky --sun-elevation -10`: probes require a precomputed IBL to exist at
all, and the sky path keeps the repo HDR-free while putting the confound below the horizon.

| arm | asserts | anti-vacuity | mutation |
|---|---|---|---|
| probe-set-single | mode=single, mask_bits=0; frame byte-identical twice; emissive-spec numbers unmoved | count=1 printed; companion lift over the floor | force multi at N=1: pixels move, so the verbatim branch is the live one |
| probe-set-rooms | dark-room floor patch under the bar; a probes:[A]-only variant over a higher bar; the ratio is the signal | lit-room floor bright in both | selection pinned to probe 0: the dark read matches the truncated variant |
| probe-set-seam | max adjacent-column delta across the doorway band under the bar | both probes' weights nonzero in the band | normalization dropped for a hard max select: the step explodes |
| probe-set-fallback | a patch outside both boxes against a probes:[] variant, floor measured twice first | mode=multi in the armed run | the remainder term dropped: the patch darkens |
| probe-set-converge | captures constant across probe rows and equal to count; digest identical twice | captures == 2, not 0 | re-arm every frame: captures grows |
| probe-set-ssr | the catcher's miss region under [A,B] against [A]-only | SSR fallback nonzero in both | SSR pinned to probe 0: the ratio collapses |
| probe-set-spec-count | the single-probe ratio unmoved at 1.0386; the two-probe variant under 1.10 | lift over EMISSIVE_SPEC_LIFT_MIN | normalization removed: the ratio exceeds the bar |
| probe-set-schema | a missing box skipped by name; 9 probes truncation-warned; an unknown key warned | probe count printed equals authored | the required-key check removed: the warn line disappears |

`probe-set-fallback` asks for equality with its floor measured first rather than promising
0 px: the two runs execute DIFFERENT branches of one shader, and 11.28 measured that a
shader edit alone reschedules float math into 1/255 differences. A recorded last-bit residual
is acceptable there and a structural darkening is not.

**Must-stay-green, by real selector** (checked against `GATE_GROUPS` — there is no `gi`,
`ssr` or `lights` group, and `--only gi` substring-matches origin and region while
`--only cluster` runs the 11.63 meshoptimizer cluster gate, so those selectors must never be
written here): **emissive** (both recorded numbers), **submission** (shadowcull-live runs the
suite's other scene-probe capture), **fixture-gen**, **gate-docs**. Goldens: **cornell_box is
the SOLE unit-14 tenancy watchdog**, being the only recipe that passes `--gi-volume`;
cornell_leak is a golden but carries no GI flag. Every existing recipe stays 0 px and
cornell_rooms is new.

Worth stating because it shapes where the mask build goes: **no gate anywhere watches
`light_cluster.c` directly** — every lit golden exercises it indirectly — so the probe mask
build sits strictly after `_fill_index_pool`, touches none of the light arrays, and the
probe-set arms are its only direct coverage.

## Risks, bounded

1. **Emissive-spec headroom is ~6%** (bar 1.10, recorded 1.0386) and every probe added is
   another camera re-photographing derived panels. Bounded structurally by the convex blend
   and measured by `probe-set-spec-count`; the single-probe number must not move at all.
2. **Octahedral sharpness at row-0 512** is roughly a 209-cube's angular density against
   today's 512 cube, in multi mode only. The phase-5 A/B decides whether that ships as the
   default; `--probe-set-res 1024` is the dial.
3. **Unit-14 tenancy** cannot regress GI when probes are off (the own-texture path is
   unchanged), and its only existing watchdog is the cornell_box golden. The lazy-allocation
   ordering constraint is asserted, not assumed. A future third tenant argues at the module.
4. **Fragment cost** is at most 8 box tests and 16 atlas fetches, only in mask-covered
   froxels, and `probeCount >= 2` is dynamically uniform so probe-less scenes pay one branch.
   Measured A/B/A/B with `--profiler`.
5. **Load time** is N times today's `--probe` cost, at load only, reported per probe in the
   diagnostic. A runtime budget is deferred with relight.
6. **The fragment stage reaches 9 uniform blocks of a guaranteed 12.** Static-asserted and
   named for whoever wants the tenth.
7. **Capture ordering**: probes never see each other. GI captures do see converged probes,
   exactly as they see today's single probe — stated, not changed.

## AS BUILT

Eleven things differ from the plan above, or were learned building it.

**1. The box fade runs OUTWARD from the proxy faces, not inward, and the plan had
it wrong.** An inward fade gives a fragment weight 1 at the box centre falling to
0 at the faces, which reads as the obvious shape and is unusable: a floor lies
exactly ON the bottom face of the box that box-projects it — `ssr_frag.glsl` has
said so in a comment since 11.4 — so every floor in every scene weighed exactly
zero and the one surface this feature exists for fell entirely back to the global
environment. It shipped that way for a day and the frame looked plausible,
because a floor reflecting nothing and a floor reflecting a dim room are the same
picture. Weight is now 1 anywhere inside the box, falling to 0 over the fade
measured outward, so the blend happens where boxes overlap. Found by rendering
the fixture, not by reading the formula.

**2. `GpuProbeBlock` lives in `light_cluster.h`, not `probe_set.h`.** Half of it
is the froxel masks, which that file's grid sizes and its build pass fills, and
putting it in `probe_set.h` created an include cycle (`probe_set.h` →
`light_cluster.h` → `ubo.h` → ... → `probe_set.h`, whose guard was already set,
leaving `PROBE_SET_MAX` undefined).

**3. `UBO_PROBES_BLOCK_SIZE` is the literal 3616, not an expression.** `ubo.h`
cannot reach `LC_CLUSTER_COUNT` — `light_cluster.h` includes it — which is the
same reason the cluster block sizes above it are literals. The `sizeof` assert
lives beside the struct, where both numbers are visible, and is what catches
either drifting.

**4. The GI volume is created BEFORE the probes in `apps/render`.** The atlas has
to reserve the volume's columns at allocation, and can only measure a volume that
exists. Reversed, each allocates its own texture and whichever binds second wins
unit 14. The block moved rather than the constraint being documented.

**5. The two atlas regions sit SIDE BY SIDE, not stacked.** Stacking pads the
narrow region out to the wide one's width: at eight probes beside an 8x4x8
volume that is ~28 MB of nothing.

**6. The gutter is one texel, GI's own, not the two the plan invented.**
`wrapToInterior` mirrors exactly the outermost ring and steps exactly one column
in, so a two-texel gutter leaves its outer ring unwritten. The wrap moved into
`octahedral.glsl` as `octWrapToInterior` and `gi_project_frag` now shares it —
measured 0 px on the cornell_box golden, which is what says the two copies really
were one.

**7. The froxel mask is a measured 0 px identity on this content.** Replacing it
with `PROBE_MASK_ALL` in `pbr_frag` changes nothing, which is the correct result
and worth stating: the mask is an OPTIMIZATION, and what decides a probe's
contribution is its box weight. That is also why SSR passes `PROBE_MASK_ALL`
rather than pulling four light blocks into a post pass to save at most seven box
tests.

**8. There is no seam arm.** One was built: sweep the floor across the doorway,
compare peak curvature in the handover band against a leg whose boxes abut. It
discriminated at 1.68x, and both blend mutations drove it to exactly 1.00. It
stopped discriminating when the fixture moved to spots — the two probes now agree
closely where they hand over, in luma and in hue, which is what a doorway between
two similarly-lit rooms means. An arm that cannot fail is worse than an absent
one. The blend is still held by `probe-set-rooms`, which both blend mutations
also failed.

**9. There is no SSR arm either.** SSR shades only what the shadow catcher
marked, the catcher is installed only on scenes authoring no lights of their own,
and this fixture authors two. The multi-probe SSR path is implemented and
ungated; the suite has no SSR gate of any kind to add it to.

**10. The fixture needed two corrections a gate could not have caught.** Opening
`cornell_rooms.cscn` by hand rendered a scene with its probes REFUSED, because
only the arms were passing `--sky` for the IBL a probe needs — the trap
`beach_fixture` is already on record for. The file authors its own environment
now. And its lights are spots rather than shadow-casting area panels, for
`cornell_leak`'s stated reason plus a scalloped shadow blob the panels printed at
the partition base; the partition also has its own thickness rather than the leak
room's 6 cm, which is thinner than a shadow texel at this scale.

**11. Known open, and not caused by this feature: a stair-stepped region on the
right room's floor.** Present with `--no-shadows` off, so it is shadow-related
rather than a probe artifact, and it survives both the partition thickening and a
cone change (which made it worse and was reverted). Not diagnosed. It is in the
committed golden, which means the golden pins it and a fix will move that frame
deliberately.
