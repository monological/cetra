# Spec 13.54: the haunting begins at the key

This goes on branch and spec `13.54-silent-key-haunting`, in a worktree off `master` (13.53 is merged
at 9627874a).

## Context

In 13.53 the haunting fires in the first minutes of the game:
- Trying the front door with the backpack begins it 5 s later.
- The cellar door is stuck until it begins.
- Opening the cellar door ends it.

That makes the haunting a lock and the cellar door its key. It also spends the whole haunting before
the player has been to the basement.

The user's new order:
1. The player goes down to the basement. The flashlight in the backpack is already what lets them
   down, so the cellar door no longer needs to be stuck.
2. **Taking the key begins the haunting, and the cellar door shuts at that moment.** The player hears
   it slam above them in the dark.
3. They climb back up, open the cellar door themselves, and cross the haunted house.
4. **Opening the front door with the key and the map ends the haunting.**

**The swing question is answered by this order.** The cellar door opens into the stairwell, and
shutting it sweeps about 0.8 m across the top of the flight. The player takes the key at the
workbench, about 6 m from that sweep, and the door is shut long before they could climb into it. So
the door needs no guard.

## Behaviour

**The front door, on E:**

| State | What happens |
|---|---|
| No bag | Unchanged. A locked try (knob and rattle), and "It's locked. From the inside? I need a key... and my bag." |
| Bag, no key | A locked try, and "It's locked from the inside. I need to find a key." Nothing begins: the 13.53 dread timer and "That's weird..." go. |
| Key, no map | Unchanged. "I think I saw a map in the kitchen. I might need it." The door stays shut. |
| Key and map | "It fits." `haunt_end`: the power, the lights and the colour come back. The stage becomes `STORY_OPEN` and the door opens with its normal swing. |

**The key** (E at the workbench): it goes in the bag with "A key. It might fit the front door.", as now.
- From `STORY_LOCKED`: `haunt_begin(time, 0)` runs at once. It plays the recording, cuts the power
  (the cellar bulb over the flight dies) and starts the drone, and the hall comes back red 1.5 s on.
- `door_slam(cellar, 0)` runs at the same moment, heard from where the door lands through the
  existing slam path (`door_update` returning a hit, then `door_hear`). If the player shut the door
  behind them on the way down, nothing slams.
- The stage becomes `STORY_HAUNTED`, which autosaves.
- If K has the haunting on already, the door still slams and the stage still moves.

**The cellar door:** an ordinary door again. It is not stuck before the haunting and does not end it.
Its 13.53 stuck branch and its fling-open ending are deleted.

**The rest of the haunting's chain is unchanged.** The first time the player stands at the front of
the hall looking down it, an open cellar door slams. The bathroom creaks 3 s on, and from 6 s the
cellar door is tried from the stair.

One fix is needed, because opening the cellar door no longer ends anything. If the player opens it
while it is being tried, the tries stop for good, since nothing is there. Otherwise the knob would
keep turning and the leaf keep jolting on an open door. This is a flag on `Haunt`, set when the
cellar door is found not shut after `RATTLE_AT`. It calls `hush_door`, and `haunt_begin` resets it.

**Stages:** `STORY_LOCKED`, `STORY_HAUNTED` and `STORY_OPEN`. `STORY_OVER` and its name "over" go,
because the haunting and the lock now end together.
- A save made under 13.53 in "over" is an unknown name on load. Its row is dropped and the stage
  stays at the start's `STORY_LOCKED`. Phase 1 must check that `SAVE_ENUM` behaves this way.
- With the key already held, such a save opens at the front door with the map as usual. No haunting
  begins, since the key is never taken again.
- `story_settle`'s rule that a player outside the house counts as open is unchanged.
- A `STORY_HAUNTED` load still begins the haunting in `start_play`.

## After: the door beats rearranged for the climb back up

The user asked for three changes.

**The tries come from the hall, as the player climbs back up.** They are on the haunting's own
clock now: from `RATTLE_AT`, 4 s after it began, rather than 6 s after the slam was watched for.
- They begin as soon as the cellar door stands shut, and end for good when it is opened on them.
  That is a three-state `HauntTries`.
- A haunting begun by K in the hall with the cellar door open waits for the watched slam to shut
  it.
- From the bench, the 4 s puts the first try as the player turns back toward the stair, and the
  second, the first with the rattle, 1.9 s later.
- The sounds were already held at the leaf's hall side. On the stair they come through the shut
  door's link, at 0.4.
- The leaf jolts toward the stair, the way a door that opens into the stairwell gives when pushed
  from the hall.

**The bathroom creaks a second after the player first stands in the hall** (`CREAK_AT` 1.0). In
the story that is the moment they step out of the cellar door. It no longer waits for the look back
down the hall.

**The slam alone still waits to be watched for.** `haunt_step` is now two functions, `door_beats`
(the slam and the creak, which wait for the player) and `cellar_tries` (on the clock). One `stepped`
time replaces the per-clock `last_u`. `--haunt-at` puts the slam and the creak past and the tries
on their clock.

Checked headless with `--trace-haunt`:
- `--haunt`, standing at the cellar door in the hall: the creak at 1.000 and tries at 4.017 and
  5.900. With E at 8 s, "tries over at 7.950" and no try at 12.2.
- `--haunt`, standing in the cellar stairwell for 8 s: tries at 4.017 and 5.900, and no creak.

## Review

Two background reviews of the branch ran: the thermo-nuclear standard and a simplify pass (reuse,
simplification, efficiency, altitude). Neither found a structural regression. Everything they
raised was applied.

- **The creak no longer depends on the cellar door.** `haunt_step` returned early with no cellar
  door, and `haunt_begin`'s part-way path covered the bathroom door only when there was one. An
  unhung cellar door silenced the creak, against `HauntHouse`'s "any may be NULL" contract. Now
  only the slam and the tries check for the cellar door.
- **Late tries start at their first try.** Their clock (`tries_at`) now starts when they get under
  way, not at `RATTLE_AT`. Before, tries that waited for the door to shut began part way through
  one try, with no knob and already near full force. That happened with K in the hall with the
  cellar door open, or on a haunted load saved with it open. A part-way begin past `RATTLE_AT`
  starts them under way, as before.
- **Dead code went.**
  - `door_hand_try_begin`'s `shake`: its only `false` caller was the stuck cellar door.
  - Its `play` argument and field are now `HAND_PLAY` in `door_try.c`.
  - `story_door`'s one-case dispatcher: `front_door` is folded into it.
- **Stale and caller-naming comments were fixed:**
  - haunt.c's constants narrated the story; that choreography now sits on `story_key`;
  - haunt.h's "pure function of the seconds" and "wait for nobody";
  - the `stepped` field;
  - silent.c's "door beats wait for where the player looks";
  - the `--haunt-at` usage text.
- **The docs describe the tries once**, in the haunting's own paragraph. The story's paragraph
  points to it.

Left as they are:
- `in_hall` stays; there is no hall room to ask `room_at` about.
- `ReachTake`'s time argument is unused by two takes.
- `StoryHouse.cellar` exists for the one slam.
- `rattle_try` walks every try from the first each step. Its cost grows with how long the tries
  run, but it is a few hashes per try.

Re-checked after the fixes:
- the hall run: creak at 1.000, tries at 4.017 and 5.917, over at 7.950;
- `--haunt-at 8`: the next try at 12.200, as before, over at 15.950;
- the front door with the key and the map: "haunt end", unlocked;
- the front door with only the bag: locked.
