# Spec 13.54: the haunting begins at the key

This goes on branch and spec `13.54-silent-key-haunting`, in a worktree off `master` (13.53 is merged
at 9627874a).

## Context

In 13.53 the haunting fires in the first minutes of the game:
- Trying the front door with the backpack begins it 5 s later.
- The cellar door is stuck until it begins.
- Opening the cellar door ends it.

That makes the haunting a lock and the cellar door its key. It also spends the whole haunting before
the player has been to the basement.

The user's new order:
1. The player goes down to the basement. The flashlight in the backpack is already what lets them
   down, so the cellar door no longer needs to be stuck.
2. **Taking the key begins the haunting, and the cellar door shuts at that moment.** The player hears
   it slam above them in the dark.
3. They climb back up, open the cellar door themselves, and cross the haunted house.
4. **Opening the front door with the key and the map ends the haunting.**

**The swing question is answered by this order.** The cellar door opens into the stairwell, and
shutting it sweeps about 0.8 m across the top of the flight. The player takes the key at the
workbench, about 6 m from that sweep, and the door is shut long before they could climb into it. So
the door needs no guard.

## Behaviour

**The front door, on E:**

| State | What happens |
|---|---|
| No bag | Unchanged. A locked try (knob and rattle), and "It's locked. From the inside? I need a key... and my bag." |
| Bag, no key | A locked try, and "It's locked from the inside. I need to find a key." Nothing begins: the 13.53 dread timer and "That's weird..." go. |
| Key, no map | Unchanged. "I think I saw a map in the kitchen. I might need it." The door stays shut. |
| Key and map | "It fits." `haunt_end`: the power, the lights and the colour come back. The stage becomes `STORY_OPEN` and the door opens with its normal swing. |

**The key** (E at the workbench): it goes in the bag with "A key. It might fit the front door.", as now.
- From `STORY_LOCKED`: `haunt_begin(time, 0)` runs at once. It plays the recording, cuts the power
  (the cellar bulb over the flight dies) and starts the drone, and the hall comes back red 1.5 s on.
- `door_slam(cellar, 0)` runs at the same moment, heard from where the door lands through the
  existing slam path (`door_update` returning a hit, then `door_hear`). If the player shut the door
  behind them on the way down, nothing slams.
- The stage becomes `STORY_HAUNTED`, which autosaves.
- If K has the haunting on already, the door still slams and the stage still moves.

**The cellar door:** an ordinary door again. It is not stuck before the haunting and does not end it.
Its 13.53 stuck branch and its fling-open ending are deleted.

**The rest of the haunting's chain is unchanged.** The first time the player stands at the front of
the hall looking down it, an open cellar door slams. The bathroom creaks 3 s on, and from 6 s the
cellar door is tried from the stair.

One fix is needed, because opening the cellar door no longer ends anything. If the player opens it
while it is being tried, the tries stop for good, since nothing is there. Otherwise the knob would
keep turning and the leaf keep jolting on an open door. This is a flag on `Haunt`, set when the
cellar door is found not shut after `RATTLE_AT`. It calls `hush_door`, and `haunt_begin` resets it.

**Stages:** `STORY_LOCKED`, `STORY_HAUNTED` and `STORY_OPEN`. `STORY_OVER` and its name "over" go,
because the haunting and the lock now end together.
- A save made under 13.53 in "over" is an unknown name on load. Its row is dropped and the stage
  stays at the start's `STORY_LOCKED`. Phase 1 must check that `SAVE_ENUM` behaves this way.
- With the key already held, such a save opens at the front door with the map as usual. No haunting
  begins, since the key is never taken again.
- `story_settle`'s rule that a player outside the house counts as open is unchanged.
- A `STORY_HAUNTED` load still begins the haunting in `start_play`.

## Changes

**`apps/silent/src/story.c/h`:**
- Delete `dread`, `felt`, `dread_step`, `DREAD_FELT`/`DREAD_BEGINS`, `cellar_door`, `CELLAR_PLAY`,
  `STORY_OVER` and its name.
- `story_door` handles only the front door.
- New `void story_key(Story* s, double time)`, the key's turn: it begins the haunting unless it is
  already on, slams the cellar door, and moves the stage to `STORY_HAUNTED` from `STORY_LOCKED`. It
  prints the turn under trace.
- `front_door`'s key-and-map row calls `haunt_end` before going to `STORY_OPEN`.
- `story_step` only steps the try.
- Rewrite the header comment and the stage comments.

**`apps/silent/src/silent.c`:**
- `take_key` calls `story_key(&g_story, game time)`. It needs the time passed through, or it reads
  the game's time the way the other reach callbacks do.
- The usage text for `--story` becomes `locked|haunted|open`, and its "(ended at the cellar door)"
  goes.
- `start_play` is unchanged.

**`apps/silent/src/haunt.c/h`:** the tries-stop flag described above, and the tries' comment
("until the player opens it").

**Docs:**
- In `AGENTS.md`, rewrite the silent row's "Since 13.53" passage to the new order: the key begins the
  haunting and slams the cellar door, and the front door with the key and the map ends it. Remove the
  stuck cellar door, the 5 s dread and the cellar ending, and keep the rest.
- In `docs/cli-reference.md`, the story section and `--story`.
- The spec's as-built notes.

## Verification

All checks run headless with `--trace-haunt`. Each pad script is a single press of X (E) from a
pinned `--player-at`, not a walk.
- **The key.** Use `--backpack` at the workbench, facing it, and press X. Expect "story key taken"
  then "haunt begin", and "door basement_door slammed" when the cellar door was open. A run with the
  door shut first shows no slam.
- **The front door.** Run at the front door with the cases below and press X.
  - `--story haunted --key --map`: "front door unlocked" and "haunt end".
  - `--key` without the map: the remark, with the door still shut.
  - `--backpack`: the locked try, and no haunting.
- **The tries.** Run at the cellar door with `--haunt-at 8` and press X to open it. The tries stop
  and no further "haunt try" lines appear.
- **Saves.**
  - Saved haunted with `--slot`, then reloaded with `--load`: it comes back haunted.
  - A slot hand-edited to `"over"` loads as locked with a warning, not a refusal of the file.
- **One still** from the stair after the take: the flashlight only, and the shut door at the top.
- **In the window:** the user plays the bag, the stairs, the key, the climb, the hall and the front
  door.
