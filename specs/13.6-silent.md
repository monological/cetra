# Spec 13.6 -- silent: a grimy kitchen and its foggy street

## Context

The user wants a new app inspired by (not a copy of) a screenshot of a grimy horror-game kitchen:
- a grimy kitchen lit by fluorescent tubes;
- a first-person player;
- a window onto a foggy street of houses, not a cityscape.

Answers already given:
- **The look is PS2 era**: low-poly boxy models, low-res procedural textures, smooth filtering, heavy fog, grain.
- **The street is walkable**: a door leads out onto it.
- **Extras**: a flashlight, a flickering tube, and ambient audio.

Everything is procedural (meshes and textures) and built in C, like tree, forest and gametest. The reference screenshot is not itself low-poly; it is a dense, photoscan-like capture. The PS2 look comes from four things:
1. few polygons, flat-shaded;
2. a low texel density (about 128 px/m, textures 128-256 px);
3. baked-looking dirt (edge grime, drips, stains in the albedo rather than detail in the geometry);
4. fog and darkness hiding the draw distance.

**No engine change is required for any of that.** Engine additions are only an optional later phase, if the look review asks for them.

## Decisions (mine, stated)

- **App name** `apps/silent`, branch `13.6-silent`, spec `specs/13.6-silent.md`.
- **Night by default**, to match the reference: lit house windows, street lamps in fog, flashlight. `--day` gives a white overcast fog world.
- **Textures and props are app-local** (`apps/silent/src/`), not in `cetra/src/procedural/`: nothing else consumes them, and that keeps the public API unchanged.
- **Every prop is described once**: a list of boxes drives both the mesh and the static Jolt collider. This is gametest's `grotto_box` pattern (`gametest.c:843`), so what you see and what you bump into cannot drift apart.

## Reused

- **Player**: the game framework, Jolt `CharacterVirtual`, input actions, mouse capture, and a `CameraRig` at distance 0, all copied from forest's structure:
  - action table `forest.c:276-289`; cursor capture `forest.c:301`;
  - physics and character init `forest.c:2524-2622`;
  - move `forest.c:2780-2840`; look `forest.c:2919-2964`.
- **Meshes**:
  - `mesh_builder.h` (`mb_init`/`mb_vertex`/`mb_tri`/`mb_transfer`), with a normal per face vertex for flat shading;
  - `mesh_generate_box` (already flat, 24 vertices).
  - Never rely on `mesh_compute_normals`, which smooths across shared vertices.
- **Textures**:
  - `veg_fbm2_tiled` / `veg_worley2_tiled` (seed right before each bake, on one thread);
  - `noise_perlin3_tiled` with its own `NoisePerm`;
  - `texture_load_memory_owned` with `texture_desc(is_srgb)` and `TEXTURE_USE_NORMAL`;
  - the packed layout of `terrain_layer_maps`: albedo+height, and normal.xy / roughness / AO.
- **Lights**:
  - `LIGHT_AREA` tubes (LTC, with shadows), following gametest's worked example `gametest.c:1850-1882`;
  - a spot light for the flashlight, with shadows;
  - spot lights for the street lamps.
  - Budget: at most 8 punctual shadow layers.
- **Fog**: global froxel fog at a low density for indoor haze, plus up to 8 dense feathered `FogVolume`s over the street (`scene_add_fog_volume`), and `postfx_set_fog_ambient` for the colour. Volumes only add density, so the house interior stays clear.
- **Glass**: `transmission` about 0.9, `thickness` 0, with a grime opacity/albedo map.
- **Post**: grain, vignette, dither, and optionally the film look (`postfx_apply_film_look`) and render scale 0.5.
- **Audio**: `AUDIO_SOURCE` components carrying procedural tones; `audio_system_read_pcm` for headless runs.
- **Headless args**: the hand-rolled parse in `tree.c:937-1070`: `-x -f -S -W -H --screenshot-every`, plus gametest-style `--cam-eye/--cam-target` so a framing can be photographed twice.

## Layout

```
 street (x along road, ~70 m, houses both sides, lamps every ~15 m, fog volumes)
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   sidewalk | front yard + porch
            | [front door]--hallway--[kitchen 4.6 x 3.6 x 2.6 m]
            |                         window on the street-facing side wall
```

The player starts in the kitchen. The hallway turns the view, so the street is seen first through the window. The house the kitchen sits in is one of the procedural houses, with a real interior.

## Phases

Each phase ends with:
- `./build.sh`;
- a headless screenshot at pinned framings, **shown to the user** (the app draws, so it gets looked at every phase);
- a commit, and the spec's as-built section updated.

### 0. Setup
- Branch off master and copy this plan into specs. Commit it on its own.

### 1. Skeleton: walk a room
- `apps/silent/CMakeLists.txt` (`add_cetra_app(silent)`) and `src/silent.c`: args, `main`, the game hooks.
- `src/player.c/.h`: forest's walker, trimmed down to WASD, mouse look, sprint, crouch-less.
- `src/kit.c/.h`, the prop kit:
  - `KitBox {centre, half, rot_y, material id, collide}`;
  - `kit_build(...)` emits the flat-shaded mesh and the static box body.
  - **UVs are world-scaled** (u,v = metres × texels-per-metre / size), so texel density is the same on every surface. This is the thing that makes it read as PS2.
- The kitchen shell: floor, ceiling, walls with a window opening and a door opening, and one placeholder light.
- **Check**: walk into a wall and stop.

### 2. Procedural textures (`src/tex.c/.h`)
- One height/grime field per kind, and all maps derived from it (the bark pattern). 256² unless stated. Kinds:
  - **Floor tile**: 30 cm ceramic, grout, cracked tiles, a wet sheen held in the roughness.
  - **Backsplash tile**: 15 cm.
  - **Plaster wall**: stains, damp, peeling patches.
  - **Ceiling**: water stains.
  - **Cabinet enamel**: off-white, with chips down to rust.
  - **Metals**: stainless steel (sink, hood), brushed steel.
  - **Wood**: table, chair, doors.
  - **Lino/rug**.
  - **Outside**: asphalt with a centre line, concrete sidewalk, clapboard siding (3 tints), shingle roof, brick, fence wood.
  - **Window grime**: opacity.
- **A shared grime pass**: an AO darkening toward the bottom edge, vertical drip streaks from the seeded noise, and blotchy stains. The dirt is authored here, not in the geometry.
- **Shown**: a swatch sheet PNG, plus the kitchen with textures on.

### 3. Kitchen props (`src/kitchen.c`)
- An L of lower cabinets with a counter, doors and handles.
- Upper cabinets.
- Stove and oven with knobs, and a range hood with a duct.
- Fridge with magnets and paper notes.
- Sink with a faucet.
- Table and two chairs.
- An open shelf with jars and bottles (6-8 sided cylinders, tinted glass-ish).
- Plates, pots, a towel on the oven handle, a floor mat.
- Tube fixtures.
- Seeded clutter placement; `--seed` changes it.
- Everything walkable collides; small clutter doesn't.

### 4. Lights and the flashlight
- **Two ceiling/window tubes and the hood strip** as `LIGHT_AREA` with emissive tube meshes. The colour is a cool green-white, about 4100 K tinted toward green.
- **Flicker**: one tube follows a seeded pattern (mostly on, bursts of stutter). Light intensity and `emissive_strength` are driven from one value, so they cannot disagree. `--no-flicker` turns it off.
  - The pattern is a function of the frame index (sim time), so a headless run is deterministic.
- **Flashlight**:
  - a spot light with shadows, at the camera with a small offset, lagging the view slightly;
  - `F` and pad Y toggle it;
  - `--no-flashlight`, and `--flashlight` to start with it on (it starts off indoors).
- **Exposure is pinned to a stated EV** in the app, so the dark reads as dark and the meter doesn't open it up.
- **Shown**: the reference-like framing, the flashlight on and off, and flicker frames.

### 5. The street (`src/street.c`)
- **Road**: asphalt, curbs, sidewalks, and grass-less dirt yards.
- **House generator** (seeded): 1-2 storeys, a box body with a gable roof prism and eaves, a porch with posts, door and windows.
  - Windows are frames plus glass. About 25% are lit: emissive, warm, with a curtain-shaded albedo, and no light object (cost).
  - Siding tint and variation.
- **Also**: fences, street lamps (spot lights, the nearest 3-4 with shadows by budget, the rest emissive only), telephone poles with low-poly wires, a boxy parked car, a mailbox.
- **Colliders**: house boxes, fences, poles and the car. The street ends in fog and an invisible wall, where the fog is thick enough to hide it.
- **The player's own house**: the hallway and front door connect the kitchen to the porch.
- **Fog**:
  - global haze density about 0.01;
  - 4-6 street volumes at density about 0.25, feathered 3 m, not overlapping the house;
  - ambient grey-blue at night, white by day.
  - The distance to full fog is about 25 m by day and 15 m at night. That is judgment, to tune with the user.
- **`--day`**: an overcast sky or a dim sun, white fog ambient, no lamps, windows unlit.
- **Shown**: from the window, from the porch, and mid-street, at night and by day.

### 6. Glass and the look pass
- The window pane and house windows: a transmissive grime pane.
- **Post**: grain about 0.06, vignette, dither on, a mild green-grey grade (a small generated `.cube`, or the film look), and bloom kept low.
- **Try `render_scale` 0.5** for the PS2 softness, under TAA in a window.
- **Tuning**: fog and exposure together with the user, from A/B crops.

### 7. Ambient audio
- **Fridge hum**: about 60 Hz plus a harmonic, positional at the fridge.
- **Fluorescent buzz**: about 120 Hz, quiet, at each tube; the flickering tube's buzz follows its intensity.
- **Outside**: faint wind noise, if the procedural source supports noise; otherwise skipped and said so.
- `--mute`, and `--audio-probe` for headless offline renders.

### 8. Optional engine additions (only if the phase 6 review asks)
- `TextureDesc.filter`: `LINEAR` (today's trilinear + 8x aniso, the default, so nothing moves) or `NEAREST` / `BILINEAR` (no aniso). It is honoured in `texture_set_default_sampler_state` (`texture.c:100`).
- A `render_scale` floor of 0.25 with a nearest upscale on the non-TAA blit (`postfx.c:3593`).
- Each addition gets its own commit, and goldens must stay at 0 px because the defaults are unchanged.

### 9. Wrap-up
- **No gate arms and no golden for this demo** (the user's call). Verification is by looking: headless screenshots at pinned framings, plus a windowed run.
- **Docs**: AGENTS.md's app table and headless list, and `docs/cli-reference.md` (silent flags).
- **Reviews**: thermo-nuclear and /simplify, in the background. Wait for all of them before applying anything.
- **Suites**: none for the app itself. Only if phase 8 touches the engine, run `goldens.py` once to confirm the defaults didn't move.

## Risks
- **Punctual shadow budget (8)**: the flashlight, 3 tubes and 3-4 lamps is at the limit. Nearer lamps take shadows; the rest go without.
- **Fog volume cap (8)**: the street has to fit in 4-6 boxes.
- **LTC area tubes at 5 cm wide** can alias in speculars. The hood strip can fall back to a spot if it does.
- **Game-framework apps and TAA headless**: headless screenshots run with TAA off and render scale 1, so an A/B crop compares like with like.
- **The look is judgment**: every phase shows crops before moving on.
