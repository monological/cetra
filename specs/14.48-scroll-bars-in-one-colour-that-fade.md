# cetra 14.48: scroll bars in one colour that fade

## Context

pcb's window draws two scroll bars. Its go list's, a scrolling pane (`ui_scroll`), has a faint
thumb, white at 35%; its code panes' (`ui_editor`) and its tables' (`ui_table`) a solid one, nearly
white. The user, looking at pcb 10.6: "it should be like it is in the modal throughout the app."

The difference is cetra's. A pane's thumb is its scroll style's `fg` (`ui.h`: "`fg` its bar's
thumb"), which pcb never sets, so cetra's default. An editor's and a table's thumb is its own `fg`,
its text colour (`ui.c`, `_emit`), which pcb sets to its code's and its window's text. No theme can
make the bars one colour without making the text that colour too.

With phase 1 built, the user asked for bars that show when first seen and then fade away, as
macOS's do, an option that can be turned on or off. Asked, they chose: everything brings a bar
back -- scrolling by any means, the pointer over it, its element appearing or taking the keys --
"same as macos" for the timings, "hard coded since we don't want people fiddling with that..too
low level"; a faded bar taking no press, "like macos"; pcb shipping with it on. Phase 2.

Branch and spec: `14.48-scroll-bars-in-one-colour-that-fade`, off cetra's master (`109d3758`),
named `14.48-every-scroll-bar-in-one-colour` until phase 2. pcb 10.6.1 follows it: its theme
file's name for the bar written into `theme.scroll`, and the fade turned on.

## The design

- **Every bar's thumb is the scroll style's `fg`**: a pane's its own, as now; an editor's and a
  table's the theme's `scroll` entry in the state the element is drawn in, over cetra's default for
  a pane, white at 35%. An app colours every bar with `theme.scroll[state].fg`, the field `ui.h`
  already names for a bar's thumb. No new field.
- **One function draws a bar's thumb** for the pane's emit and the widget's, where two drew one.
- What changes on screen: an editor's and a table's thumb, from its text colour to a pane's.
  Nothing else.
- Turned down: a new `UITheme` colour for the bar, a second field for what `scroll`'s `fg` already
  is; an element's own style colouring an editor's thumb, which keeps its text and its bar tied.

### Bars that fade (phase 2)

- **`UITheme.scroll_fades`**, false as made, so every bar shows as now.
- **A bar wakes** where, at a build that draws it: it appeared -- the build before drew no bar of
  it: shown first, unhidden, sized from nothing, its content outgrowing its view; it moved -- its
  place or its content changed: the wheel, the bar, a key, a reveal, a find, an editor's own
  scroll; its element came to hold the keys (a pane's FOCUS look, the focus anywhere inside it);
  the pointer is over its grip, the bar a press there would take; or the pointer holds it.
- **It shows for 0.5 s after it last woke, then fades over 0.25 s, eased in and out, and comes
  back at once**: the timings Chromium imitates macOS's overlay scrollers with
  (`ui/native_theme/scrollbar_animator_mac.h`, `kFadeOutDelay` 500 ms and `kAnimationDuration`
  250 ms, on an ease-in-out curve; its thumb "never fades in"), neither documented by Apple. Fixed
  in `ui.c`, not the theme's. The curve is a smoothstep, near the cubic Bézier's.
- **A faded bar takes no press**: one the last build drew none of leaves the press to what is
  under it, so a click at a code pane's edge places the caret. The pointer over the grip brings it
  back, and then it takes one. Chromium's imitation does not bring a hidden bar back under the
  pointer; the user chose that it does, and it is how a faded bar is taken hold of.
- **On the UI's clock, `now`**: the wall clock in a window, the frames times the fixed step
  headless, set by a test through `ui_system_set_now` (`ui_hooks.h`). Not a sum of the frames'
  deltas: idle mode keeps its waits out of the engine's delta, so a fade counted that way would
  stall across the wait for its own start.
- **It asks for its frames**: a wake when its fade begins and every frame through it, through the
  system's wake, which only the caret asked for until now and so is renamed from `caret_wake` to
  `wake`; `ui_system_wake` reads it for a test.
- Turned down: the timings in the theme; a fade-in, which macOS has not.

## As built

**Phase 1, one colour, the arm, the look.**
- `_emit_bar` (`ui.c`, before `_emit`) draws an element's thumb where `_bar` finds one: a pane's in
  the style it is drawn in, as before; an editor's or a table's in `theme.scroll` at the state
  `_state_of` gives, over `_default_style(UI_SCROLL, ...)`. The widget's case in `_emit` and
  `_emit_pane` call it, where each drew a thumb of its own.
- `--ui-probe bar-colour` (gametest's editor probe): a 200 by 150 pane of twenty 30-point panels,
  an editor over 200 lines and a table of 100 rows, in a row; each thumb is the flat quad a bar wide
  in its element's right 20 points. In cetra's own theme all three are (1, 1, 1, 0.35); under
  scroll red with the editor's and table's text green, all three red; the pane under its own style
  of blue, blue. Labels take `_` because the probe's reader allows no `-` in one.
- `ui-bar-colour`, `ui-scroll-bar`, `ui-editor-bar-room`, `ui-theme-identity`, `canvas-panes`,
  `canvas-ui-table`, `canvas-ui-table-pane` and `gate-arm-docs` pass. No golden draws an editor or
  a table.
- Falsified: the editor's and table's thumb left on its own style; `ui-bar-colour` failed with the
  editor's at (0.86, 0.87, 0.84, 1) and the table's at (0.9, 0.9, 0.9, 1), each green under the loud
  theme. Put back.
- Looked at: `canvas --editor cetra/src/ui.c` and `canvas --ui-table`, headless, each thumb now the
  pane's faint white over the dark.
- `ui.h`'s `scroll`, `docs/ui.md`'s scrolling panes and the roadmap's line and date say it.

**Phase 2, bars that fade.**
- `UITheme.scroll_fades` (`ui.h`, after `scroll_step`). `UIElement.bar`, engine-owned: the clock
  when it last woke, how much showed, its place's `at` and `total`, whether its element held the
  keys, and the build that drew it. `UISystem` counts its builds and finds, as each begins, the
  element whose bar a press at the pointer would take (`_scrolling_at`, as the press does).
- `_bar_wake` (`ui.c`) runs for every bar drawn, fading or not, so turning the fade on finds each
  bar's state current: woken where its `built` is not the build before, its `at` or `total` moved,
  its focus look rose, the pointer is over the grip of the bar a press would take, or the pointer
  holds it. `UI_BAR_HOLD` 0.5 and `UI_BAR_FADE` 0.25; a smoothstep from the hold's end; the alpha
  multiplied into the thumb's `fg`, none drawn at 0. Fading, it asks for a wake at the hold's end
  and at once through the fade.
- The press: a bar takes one where the theme does not fade or the last build drew some of it.
- `caret_wake` is `wake`; `ui_system_set_now` and `ui_system_wake` (`ui_hooks.h`).
- `--ui-probe bar-fade` (gametest's text probe) on the pane fixture, 300 by 200 over 20 rows, the
  fade on: (alpha, wake) are (0.35, 0.5) at 0, (0.35, 0.1) at 0.4, (0.175, 0) at 0.625, (0, none)
  at 0.8; a press on the track with the pointer coming there pages 0 and draws it at (0.35, 0.5);
  a press then pages 180; gone at 3, the wheel at 3; gone at 5, the pane given a key hook and the
  focus at 5; gone at 7, hidden, shown at 7.5; the fade off at 20, (0.35, none). The pane takes a
  key hook only for the focus, so the pointer passing over it earlier moves no focus to it.
- `ui-bar-fade`, the ui and idle groups whole (144 arms, `idle-type` and `idle-editor` among them,
  reading the wake), `canvas-panes`, `canvas-ui-table`, `canvas-ui-table-pane` and `gate-arm-docs`
  pass.
- Falsified: a faded bar taking a press; `ui-bar-fade` failed, "a press on the faded bar paged
  180.0 (want 0)". Put back.
- Looked at: `canvas --editor cetra/src/ui.c --fade-bars`, headless at frames 10, 39 and 60 (the
  thumb shown, half faded, gone), and by the user in a window: "looks good".
- `ui.h`'s theme, `docs/ui.md`'s scrolling panes, apps/canvas's README and the roadmap's line say
  it.
