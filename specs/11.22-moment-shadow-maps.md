**And one more the item must answer rather than rediscover.** `fog_esm_frag.glsl:23-25`:

> This buffer is the FOG's alone. Surface shading keeps the exact depth array: ESM's
> failure mode is light leaking near a blocker, tolerable in a medium (the paper says
> as much) and not on a contact shadow.

A filterable shadow representation has already been evaluated for surface shading
here and confined to fog. That does not kill A6 — four moments leak far less than one
exponential, which is exactly why Peters wrote MSM after VSM — but it makes the
gate's `hole` arm the number that decides the item, not merely a check.

**Scope is the directional cascades, not the area/punctual path.** 10.4 wanted VSM for
the area panel's staircase and that is still the sharper problem, but it is also where
the no-spare-unit reason still bites and where `area-penumbra-centre` leaves an
unresolved positional bias. The machinery is identical wherever it is later pointed;
build it where it is provable.

## The three decisions

### 1. Resolve moments from the finished depth array; never touch the depth pass

`esm_build_layer` (`postfx.c:2185`) already has this exact shape: read a depth cascade
layer, transform it into a filterable float array, separably blur it through a
scratch, three draws per layer, ending in `dst` so there is no handle swap. **MSM is
that with four channels instead of one.**

Peters writes moments in the depth pass. His way here means the shadow FBO grows a
colour target — and both depth FBOs are created deliberately colour-less
(`glDrawBuffer(GL_NONE); glReadBuffer(GL_NONE)`, `shadow.c:43-44`) — plus every
depth-pass path including punctual has to learn about it. Resolving instead leaves the
depth pass, its polygon offset and its front-face culling **byte-identical**, which is
what makes `--no-msm` a provable 0-px no-op rather than a hoped-for one. The only
thing his way buys is MSAA inside the map, which a larger map buys too.

`fog_esm_frag.glsl` also settles the transform pass's shape: a **2x2 box in the
source**, because the target is a fraction of the source size and a single tap would
alias in exactly the frequencies the pass exists to remove — and each tap must be
transformed *before* averaging, since averaging depths and then comparing is not the
average of the comparisons. MSM inherits both.

### 2. No new sampler — MSM replaces unit 10 rather than adding to it

`csm.glsl:24` declares `uniform sampler2DArray shadowMaps` and every consumer reads
`.r`. An RGBA16F array binds to that same declaration; `.r` becomes the first moment
instead of a depth. B6's hard-won lesson — *"there is no seventeenth sampler in
`pbr_frag` for any unit, free or not, because the driver counts declarations"* — does
not bite A6. This is what makes the item an M, not an L.

The consumer set is closed and small — three shaders, four programs:

| shader | `CSM_OUTERMOST_PCF` | programs |
|---|---|---|
| `pbr_frag.glsl:146` | no | `pbr`, `pbr_skinned` |
| `catcher_frag.glsl:27` | yes, half-kernel 2 | `shadow_catcher` |
| `particle_frag.glsl:48` | yes, half-kernel 1 | `particle` |

So the branch belongs in `csm.glsl` — already the declared "one text to rename"
contract file for this uniform block. `froxel_inject_frag.glsl:33` declares its own
`shadowMaps` under private names and is deliberately outside the include; not a
consumer, does not change.

Two consequences, accepted rather than worked around:

- **PCSS is forced off under `--msm`.** Its blocker search (`pbr_frag.glsl:664`) reads
  `.r` as a depth, which under MSM is a mean depth. The roadmap predicted this cost.
- **The catcher and particles get moments too**, via `csmOutermostOcclusion`
  (`csm.glsl:75`). Same one-place branch.

### 3. Storage: RGBA16F with Peters' quantisation matrix — not RGBA16

The paper specifies 16-bit UNORM plus a quantisation matrix, because the fourth moment
concentrates near zero and needs the conditioning. But **`GL_RGBA16` would be the first
normalized 16-bit format anywhere in this codebase** — no `GL_RGBA16`/`GL_RG16`/`GL_R16`
in `src/`, `specs/` or `docs/`, and no record of one being tried. This project has
already hit a colour-renderability wall on this target: `specs/10.7:73` records
`GL_RG16F` -> `GL_RGBA16F` because "RGB16F is not reliably color-renderable."

So: **apply the quantisation matrix from the start** (it improves conditioning
regardless of storage, one 4x4 multiply) and store in RGBA16F, the proven path —
`GL_R32F` and `GL_RGBA32F` arrays already ship here filtered bilinear. RGBA16 becomes a
pure storage swap later *if the gate says fp16 is not enough*: measured escalation, not
speculative hardening. Memory is identical either way at 8 bytes/texel.

`create_texture_2d_array_float` (`texture.c:149`) takes the internal format as an
argument and already gives `GL_LINEAR` — which MSM wants — so it allocates this with no
source change. **It also hardcodes `GL_CLAMP_TO_EDGE`, with no border control**, where
the depth array uses `CLAMP_TO_BORDER` with a white border (`shadow.c:33-38`). Accept
the edge clamp: `calculateShadow` and `csmOutermostOcclusion` both bounds-check in the
shader *before* tapping, so the border is belt-and-braces for kernel taps near the edge,
and clamping moments outward extends the last known occluder rather than inventing one.
State it in the code; do not silently inherit it.

**Size: 1024^2, half the depth map**, with a `--msm-size` knob. Follows the ESM precedent
(`POSTFX_FOG_ESM_SIZE` is 512 against a 2048 scene map, "deliberately far below") — a
representation whose whole claim is filterability does not need the depth array's texel
density.

```
depth cascades   2048^2 x 9  DEPTH24    151 MB   (exists, stays: it is the source)
moment array     1024^2 x 9  RGBA16F     75 MB
blur scratch     1024^2 x 9  RGBA16F     75 MB
                                        ------
                              added     151 MB
```

`--msm-size 2048` makes that 604 MB added. Log the figure at allocation, as
`--oit-moments` does.

## The safety invariant

**The moment array is a NEW field on `ShadowSystem`. `shadow_map_array` is never
repointed.** Named because the failure mode is silent: `shadow_publish_to_postfx` reads
`ss->shadow_map_array` directly (`shadow.c:925`), so repointing it would hand the fog a
moment array that `esm_build_layer` and `froxel_inject_frag` would both read as `.r`
depth — no error, just wrong fog. Binding a different handle to unit 10 in
`bind_shadow_maps_to_program` does **not** touch the fog, which is what makes this design
safe; the invariant keeps it that way.

## Phase 1 — the resolve, the lookup, `--msm`. Ends at a GO/NO-GO.

**New shaders**
- `msm_resolve_frag.glsl` — modelled on `fog_esm_frag.glsl`: mode 0 downsamples a depth
  layer (2x2 box) into quantised moments, mode 1 is one separable blur axis.
- `include/msm.glsl` — Hamburger 4MSM reconstruction plus the quantisation matrix and
  its inverse. Default to a **separate file from `mboit.glsl`**: both do
  Cholesky-of-Hankel plus a quadratic root solve, but they carry different
  regularisation constants and end differently (Peters' `Switch` case analysis vs an
  expectation under the moments). Factor the shared kernel out only if it comes out
  genuinely identical with the code in front of me — two 12-line blocks differing in
  their constants is the thin abstraction the review bar rejects. Cross-reference either
  way.

**`shadow.c` / `shadow.h`** — `msm_array`, `msm_scratch`, `msm_fbo`, `msm_quad_vao`,
`msm_program`, `msm_enabled`, `msm_blur_texels`, `msm_size`. Lazily allocated, dropped
on a cascade-count or size change. `msm_enabled` defaults **false in the library**, app
opts in — the `pcss_enabled` precedent at `shadow.c:89`.

- **Insertion point: after `shadow.c:846`**, where `render_shadow_depth_pass` has
  restored viewport, program and polygon offset and left the FBO at 0. All cascade
  layers are complete at `:813`; the punctual loop after it touches only its own array.
  The fully-restored slot also sidesteps the pass's pre-existing un-restored
  `glCullFace(GL_BACK)` (`:791`, never saved) rather than inheriting it.
- **`shadow.c` has no quad VAO and no state save/restore of its own**, so the body takes
  `esm_build_layer`'s three-step shape with `mask_array.c:158-197`'s discipline: explicit
  save/restore of FBO, viewport and the blend/cull/depth enables, its own FBO,
  `create_fullscreen_quad_vao` / `draw_fullscreen_quad` (`util.c:56`, `util.c:78`) cached
  on the struct. Completeness checked once after attaching layer 0, logging and falling
  back to the depth path — the ESM idiom at `postfx.c:2242-2249`.
- **Skip the resolve under `scene_capture_begin`** (`render.c:1090`, reached from
  `gi_volume.c:288` and `probe.c:101`). Probe and GI bakes run the depth pass with
  `cascade_count` forced to 1 and do not need moments; resolving per cube face is pure
  cost.
- `bind_shadow_maps_to_program` binds the moment array to unit 10 when it built, **the
  depth array otherwise, and never 0** — the unconditional bind at `shadow.c:360-362`
  exists because "a sampler2DArray must resolve to something for the program to be
  complete."
- **`msmEnabled` must upload BEFORE the early return at `shadow.c:383`.** That return
  fires when no directional light casts and skips `shadowTexelSize`, `shadowBias`, all
  four PCSS uniforms and the cascade uploads; putting `msmEnabled` after it leaves a
  stale value in exactly the scenes with no cascades. Add it to `uniform_cache_shadows`
  (`uniform.c:137-158`) — not required, since `uniform_location` resolves lazily through
  a uthash cache, but it is the house pattern for this block.

**`csm.glsl`** — one `csmOcclusion(layer, uv, z)` branching on `msmEnabled`, so all three
consumers change in one text.

**`--msm` CLI**, following `--no-pcss` through its eight sites: field
`render_args.h:45` -> help `render.c:82` -> `else if` `render.c:482` -> assignment inside
the `render.c:2359` block near `:2367` (forcing `pcss_enabled = false` and logging it) ->
`bool` in `ShadowSystem` near `shadow.h:98` -> default `shadow.c:89` -> uniform upload
`shadow.c:394` -> GUI toggle `gui.c:522`.

**Then stop and produce A/B crops** on `dir_shadow_fixture` and the raiden recipe: MSM vs
PCSS vs plain PCF, at 1:1. Cetra has backed penumbra work out on look twice
(`540e477`/`13fe550`, `ce795a4`/`47a1a68`) and declined to ship a third (`3f1b32b`, spec
10.4 Phase 4 — *"built, measured, not shipped"*). MSM trades contact-hardening for a
uniform blur, so on a scene with contacts it may simply look worse. If it does: revert
Phase 1 and record it as a measured rejection, the way 10.4 Phase 1 and B8's fibre lobes
are. That is a legitimate outcome of this plan.

## Phase 2 — controls

`--msm-size <n>`, `--msm-blur <texels>`, and the GUI sliders under the existing
`_begin_effect_group` / `_end_effect_group` idiom at `gui.c:522`.

**No `.cscn` keys.** Verified: the scene format expresses *no* shadow-system settings at
all — PCSS, softness, cascade count and csm debug are every one of them CLI-only, applied
at `render.c:2359-2390`, and `cscene.h`'s `has_*` block is post-processing. Adding a
shadow block for MSM would invent a category rather than follow one.

## Phase 3 — gate arms, no new fixture

`run_dir_shadow_gate` (`gates.py:1733`) already measures the four properties that decide
MSM, each against an analytic prediction rather than a stored image:

| arm | measures | tolerance | why it is the right MSM instrument |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hole** | worst umbra term over ~30 visible samples, both ellipses, eroded to 0.75 | `<= 0.02` | light leaking IS MSM's signature failure, and the number that answers `fog_esm_frag.glsl:23-25` |
| **acne-40deg / -10deg** | worst `abs(term - 1)` on bare ground, 11x11 | `<= 0.05` | MSM's promise is that this holds *without* bias tuning |
| **edge-cc3 / -cc1** | 50% crossing vs the computed ellipse edge, 500-sample scan | `<= 0.05` world units | blur must *soften* an edge, not *move* it — sets the blur budget |
| **churn** | AE px between frames 90 and 120 under TAA vs a `--no-shadows` floor | `<= 2x floor + 100` | a single prefiltered tap should beat 32 stochastic ones |

**The gate already runs every arm under `--no-pcss`**, so its baseline is plain 3x3 PCF.
That makes the MSM comparison apples-to-apples and means the "MSM gives up PCSS" cost
does not distort a single measurement.

`_dir_render(workdir, scene, tag, extra)` takes an argv list, so each arm is a one-line
append. The `--no-shadows` references are algorithm-independent and reusable, so the cost
is **+4 renders (7 -> 11)**: one each for `r40_msm`, `r10_msm`, `r40_cc1_msm`, and one TAA
sequence. Per `run_oit_gate`'s recorded lesson, **every arm names its configuration
explicitly** — never let an arm inherit whichever default is live.

Do **not** write an "off is off" arm. `run_flare_gate:844-864` records that exact arm
failing to falsify. Instead, per B7's rule that a gate arm which cannot fail is not
coverage: show the `hole` arm FAILS against a deliberately broken reconstruction —
dropping to two moments (VSM's Chebyshev bound) should leak on the floating sphere and
trip it — before trusting any of it.

**Known gap, verified.** The fixture has **no thin caster**: two closed spheres and one
closed 0.3x0.3x3.0 box pillar over a ground quad that is the receiver, not a caster. A
thin occluder's moments are near-degenerate and that is MSM's other weak spot. Adding one
to the existing `.cscn` would move every current dir-shadow measurement, so if the `hole`
arm proves unable to discriminate, it gets its own scene rather than an edit.
