# 11.9 — Review cleanup, and closing the spec-occ retirement question

## Context

The 11.8 audits converged on five structural follow-ups, none of them
defects — the branch shipped gated and merged. They are recorded rather
than fixed, and recorded follow-ups rot. This clears them while the
reasoning is still fresh.

Separately: LEGACY and BENT spec-occ modes have been carried as
"retire once SPLIT has soaked" since 11.4, referenced as an open item in
three places across two specs. **They are staying** — they are the only way
to A/B the split composite against what it replaced, and that comparison is
worth more than the deletion. This spec closes that question rather than
leaving it open for a fourth spec to rediscover.

Exploration corrected two things worth stating up front, both of which
argue the retirement was never as clean as it read:

- **The bent normal is not BENT-mode's.** `specOccSplitAt` (the shared
  include) reads the AO buffer's `.gba` bent normal, so SPLIT is its
  primary consumer — retiring BENT would have removed one of five reads and
  freed no storage. The `--bent-debug` view is the only inspection tool for
  a buffer SPLIT depends on.
- **A live claim in 11.5 is wrong** (`:66-68`, `:146`): retiring the modes
  would NOT collapse `pbr_frag`'s inline arms. `render.c` also drives
  `splitAmbientSpec` to 0 for the OIT alpha pass and for probe captures, so
  those arms stay live regardless — and so does the recorded
  `aoMap`-on-specular residual on those paths, which needs its own fix.

## Settled design

Five cleanups. Exploration confirmed all five and corrected three of them;
the corrections are the interesting part.

**C1 — Split `postfx_free_targets` in TWO, not three.** It currently does GL
teardown, clears eight lazy `_ready` guards, and resets temporal state, and
`free_postfx` pays for the last two in dead stores into memory it is about
to `free()`. The review asked for a three-way split. That does not work as
stated: ping-pong validity is not written in the function at all — it is a
side effect of `free_pingpong`, reached from ten call sites — so lifting a
"temporal reset" group means either duplicating those writes or teaching
`free_pingpong` to stop doing its job.

So: `postfx_free_targets` (GL deletes only) and `postfx_invalidate_targets`
(the eight `_ready` flags, `fog_layer_failed`, `fog_layer_frame`).
`free_postfx` calls the first; `postfx_resize` calls both. That removes
every dead store the complaint was actually about, and leaves
`free_pingpong` alone.

**C2 — Hoist `del_fbo`/`del_tex` to `util.h`, and convert four modules, not
six.** `util.h` already includes GLEW and already hosts the GL helpers
(`create_fullscreen_quad_vao`, `draw_fullscreen_quad`), so it is the natural
home with no new dependency.

The review named six other modules writing the pattern longhand. Only three
of them actually delete-*and-zero* — `shadow.c` (which also carries a
redundant `if (*x)` guard, since `glDelete*` on 0 is a no-op), `engine.c`
(six sites), and `sky.c` (one). It missed a fourth, `sky_clouds.c`, which is
the strongest match after engine.c. The other three named modules
(`ibl.c`, `gi_volume.c`, `probe.c`) are `free_*` paths that never zero;
converting them would ADD a store that is dead by construction — precisely
what C1 removes elsewhere. **Leave those three alone**, and say why in the
commit so the next reader does not "finish the job."

`shadow.c` and `probe.c` do not include `util.h`; only `shadow.c` needs it
added.

**C3 — Extract `create_bloom_pyramid`.** 34 lines in a bare scope block
inside `postfx_alloc_targets`, whose only reason to exist is scoping four
locals — a leftover from when it lived in the much larger `create_postfx`.
`create_ssr_buffers` in the same file is the model (same mip-count loop,
`glTexImage2D` chain, `MAX_LEVEL` pin, `fx->*_mips` out-param, `return
false` on failure). Not a perfect twin: the bloom block also attaches and
validates its FBO, so it keeps an unbind-on-error path the SSR one lacks.

**C4 — Move the diagnostic render-scale schedule into the render app.**
`ENGINE_RENDER_SCALE_SCHEDULE_MAX`, three `Engine` fields (516 bytes on
every engine), a public API, and a per-frame loop call, all serving one CLI
flag in one app. `engine_run`'s `update` hook runs after the frame-top
resolution sync and before any scene GL work, and `engine_set_user_data` is
free for the render app (it is reserved for `run_game`'s `Game*`, and the
render app is not a game app). `game_frame_update` is the model.

**Two consequences to accept explicitly, not discover.** The switch lands
one frame later: `update` writes `engine->render_scale`, and the rebuild
happens at the *next* frame's sync. That renumbers the equivalence gate and
nothing else. More subtly, `update` is not called on a non-renderable frame
— the loop `continue`s above it — so a run that suspends on a failed
rebuild can no longer recover when a later scheduled scale would have
succeeded. Today the schedule fires above that check and can un-suspend.
For a diagnostic that is the better behaviour anyway: a failed rebuild
should stay reported, not be silently walked out of, and the frame-advance
fix from 11.8 means the run still terminates at `--frames`. Record it.

**C5 — Parse `--render-scale-at` at parse time**, so a malformed schedule
fails before the window and GL context exist (today it aborts 26 lines
after `create_engine`, leaking the engine). Rides with C4, since C4 moves
the storage into the app anyway — doing them separately means doing C5
twice. `--gi-probes` is the model for the arity-check + `sscanf` +
message-and-`return -1` shape, but this flag is variable-length where every
existing model is fixed-arity, so `RenderArgs` gains a small parallel-array
+ count block. The cross-flag headless precondition (`headless &&
(!force_taa || !headless_jitter)`) must STAY in `main` — those three flags
can appear in any argv order, so it cannot be evaluated mid-loop.

**C6 — Close the retirement question in the specs.** `specs/11.4` (`:87`,
`:135`, `:211`) and `specs/11.5` (`:128`, `:146`) carry it as an open,
user-gated follow-up. Replace those with the decision and its reason: the
modes stay as the A/B instrument for the composite that replaced them.
While there, correct the wrong `pbr_frag` claim at `specs/11.5:66-68` and
`:146`, and re-record the `aoMap`-on-specular residual for the OIT and
capture paths as a follow-up that outlives it.

committed first. Every phase: build, 15-golden sweep 0 px, `gates.py`

**P1 — postfx internals (C1, C2, C3).** No behaviour change anywhere; the

**PR — reviews.** Background audits, wait for ALL, apply as one commit,
re-gate. This is a cleanup spec, so a finding that the cleanup made

## As built

All six items landed. Three of the five cleanups were corrected during
execution, and the most interesting result is that **one of them was wrong
as designed** and the review caught it.

**C1 shipped as ONE function, not two.** The plan's two-way split
(`postfx_free_targets` deletes, `postfx_invalidate_targets` forgets) removed
the dead stores it was after, and created a pairing hazard in exchange: two
functions that must both be called, enforced by nothing, where forgetting
the second leaves a `_ready` flag true beside a deleted handle. The
`ensure_` guard then returns early and the pass binds framebuffer 0 and
samples texture 0 — it draws into the default framebuffer rather than
failing, which is the silent class 11.8 already documented.

That was not theoretical. Between the phase commit and the review I had
myself dropped the second call from the resize failure path, reasoning that
nothing in between sets a `_ready` flag. True at the time, and precisely the
sort of cross-function invariant that stops holding the moment someone makes
a lazy group eager. `postfx_invalidate_targets` now calls
`postfx_free_targets` itself: `postfx_resize` makes one call, `free_postfx`
makes the other, no pairing survives, and every dead store is still gone.
`free_shadow_map_array` was the existing precedent and the branch had moved
away from it.

**C2 converted four modules, not six, and its header rule had to be
rewritten.** Of the six the review named, only three genuinely
delete-and-zero (`shadow.c`, `engine.c`, `sky.c`); it missed `sky_clouds.c`
and misdiagnosed the rest. `ibl.c` and `probe.c` are not "free paths" — they
are delete-then-immediately-regenerate, where the zero is pointless because
the next line overwrites the handle. Only `gi_volume.c` matches the original
description. The first version of `util.h`'s comment forbade the helpers in
`free_*` paths and then the same branch used them in `free_engine` and
`free_postfx`; the prohibition is gone and the accurate criterion is in its
place.

**C4 was nearly declined on a claim I had not checked.** I argued the move
would break the frame-top guarantee the equivalence gate depends on.
Verifying the loop settled it: the sync is at `engine.c:2566` and `update`
at `:2624`, so a schedule driven from `update` still lands at a frame top —
the next one. The scene pass never sees a half-applied state. The move
shipped; the switch lands one frame later and the gate measured 65.2 dB
against 66.2 before, which is the one frame of convergence, not a
regression.

### Measured

Master gate at every phase: 15 goldens exact 0, `gates.py` green, raiden
byte-identical to the 11.7 Phase-0 baseline, determinism x2 at 0 px. The
11.8 rebuild gates re-run after the schedule move and again after the C1
restructure: 65.2 dB switch-vs-straight with DoF, SSGI, fog, motion blur and
contact shadows all live across the switch; zero GL errors over 30 switches;
peak RSS +1.5 MB. The correctness audit independently diffed the delete and
flag sets against master symbol-by-symbol and exercised four live resizes
with every lazy group active.

### Behaviour changes the plan did not record

All confined to `--render-scale-at`, all found by review:

- Duplicate flags now accumulate; before, the last occurrence won.
- An over-long schedule aborts at parse time; before, it warned and
  truncated to 64.
- An entry landing on a non-renderable frame (zero-area window as well as a
  suspended rebuild) is dropped rather than deferred, because `update` is
  not reached on a skipped frame.
- An empty `--render-scale-at ""` no longer trips the headless precondition,
  since it lexes to zero entries.

### Follow-ups

- The `aoMap`-on-specular residual on the OIT and probe-capture paths.
  Recorded in 11.5 as something LEGACY/BENT retirement would resolve; it
  would not, and the modes are staying. It needs its own fix.
- `render_args.h` churns ~100 lines of comment realignment whenever the
  longest declaration in the struct changes. Putting field comments on their
  own line would stop it; this branch happened to reverse 11.8's instance.
- `create_pingpong` returns on its first failure leaving slot 1 untouched,
  and several `postfx_ensure_*` helpers leak on a partial failure and retry
  every frame. Pre-existing, made more reachable by the resize path.
