# 11.5 — Spec-occ follow-ups: aoMap to diffuse-only, SSGI split-sum gather, SSR closure

## Context

The three follow-ups recorded in 11.4's as-built, sized and reshaped by
exploration:

- **aoMap → diffuse-only** (the 4.2.1 divergence): the baked material AO dims
  both ambient shares; baked AO models diffuse hemispherical occlusion and
  should not dim the specular lobe. The 11.4 split made the fix a three-line
  deletion. **Discovery that reshapes it:** `aoMap` is identically 1.0 in
  every asset in the repo — no fixture or model carries an occlusion texture,
  and the reason is an import bug: assimp delivers glTF `occlusionTexture` as
  `aiTextureType_LIGHTMAP`, which has no row in `texture_mappings[]`
  (import.c:218-234), so glTF AO textures are **silently dropped** (while
  their `strength` IS read at import.c:317-323 — half a feature). Without the
  import fix the aoMap change is dead code; scope approved to include it plus
  a purpose-built fixture + golden.
- **SSGI gather reading the split sum**: under split, GTAO's SSGI gather
  samples `hdrTex` before the composite folds ambient specular back, so
  bounced light loses that energy. Real; small; unit 4 is free in gtao_frag
  and `fx->spec_texture` is resolved ~150 lines before the GTAO draw.
- **SSR occluded by the same cone** — **structurally empty, close with
  evidence.** SSR produces non-zero output ONLY on shadow-catcher pixels
  (ssr_frag.glsl:150-166 early-outs on `floorFade < 0.004`; model surfaces
  never trace), and the catcher is deliberately excluded from the AO chain
  (catcher_frag writes `AuxOut = 0`, the sky sentinel, so GTAO's early-out
  gives ao = 1 and a view-facing bent normal there; the edge-aware AO blur's
  depth tolerance rejects bleed across the z=0 cliff). There is no pixel
  where SSR fires AND the cone term has data — the item, and the SSR half of
  "SSGI/SSR escaping AO under SPLIT" (escaped factor = mix(1, ao≈1, s) ≈ 1),
  both close as recorded no-ops. No code.

## Settled design

**D1 — glTF occlusion import (import.c).** A guarded fallback AFTER the
mapping-table loop, exactly the metallicRoughness-UNKNOWN precedent at
process_ai_material (import.c:594-607): if `!material->ambient_occlusion_tex`
and `aiGetMaterialTexture(ai_mat, aiTextureType_LIGHTMAP, 0, ...)` succeeds,
`load_material_texture(..., set_material_ambient_occlusion_tex,
"Occlusion(lightmap)")`, linear (is_srgb false). Guarded so an FBX that maps
a real `AMBIENT_OCCLUSION` row never gets overwritten; in practice the two
types never coexist (glTF → LIGHTMAP only, FBX → AMBIENT_OCCLUSION only).
The strength read at :317-323 already works and needs no change. The AO
texture then flows through the existing mask-array path automatically
(mask_array.c:97 → `ao_layer` → pbr_frag :878-889).

**D2 — the fixture.** New `assets/gen_ao_fixture.py` →
`assets/ao_fixture.gltf`: a sphere on a ground plane, one material carrying a
synthetic occlusion texture (embedded, like parallax_fixture's embedded
textures) with an unmistakable pattern — a dark ring on the ground and a
gradient on the sphere — plus `occlusionTexture.strength` authored ≠ 1 to
pin the strength path too. Goldens must stay HDR-free (HDRs are gitignored),
so the fixture authors its own lighting the way the area/cornell fixtures
do; the golden pins the DIFFUSE dimming and the import path, and the
spec-share verification (Phase 2's A/B) uses raiden + studio HDR
interactively. Golden: `assets/ao_fixture_golden.png`, recipe documented in
the spec, added to the sweep battery.

**D3 — aoMap to diffuse-only (pbr_frag.glsl), split arms only.** Delete
`* aoMap` from exactly three lines: the split base arm (:1513
`ambSpec = specular * aoMap * envScale`), the split clearcoat lobe (:1542),
and the split sheen lobe (:1560). The inline arms (:1515, :1544, :1563) stay
textually intact: they are the byte-identity anchors for legacy/bent modes
and the capture/OIT/late paths — changing them would spend the anchors to
"fix" modes that exist as historical references.
Recorded residual: captures/OIT keep aoMap on their (inline) specular.
**Correction (spec 11.9):** this paragraph originally said the residual
would disappear when LEGACY/BENT were retired, "once retirement collapses
the arms." It would not have, and the modes are now staying anyway (see
11.9). `render.c` drives `splitAmbientSpec` to 0 for the OIT alpha pass and
for probe captures independently of the mode, so the inline arms are live
on those paths regardless — the residual is theirs, and needs its own fix.
The `sssDiffuse` tap (:1522) and
the no-IBL else-arm (:1579) are diffuse and keep aoMap. The "dims BOTH
shares" comments at :1538-1539/:1555-1556 describe the coat/sheen dims and
stay correct; add one line at the accumulator declaration noting aoMap is
diffuse-share-only under split.

**D4 — SSGI gather split sum (gtao_frag.glsl + postfx.c).** gtao_frag gains
`uniform sampler2D specTex` (unit 4) and `uniform int gatherSpec`; the
gather's source line becomes

    vec3 rad = texture(hdrTex, sUV).rgb;
    if (gatherSpec == 1)
        rad += texture(specTex, sUV).rgb;
    rad = min(rad, vec3(WS_BOUNCE_MAX));

— clamping the SUM once, not each term, so the existing +6-stop bound and
the auto-exposure meter's fixed point are preserved (view.glsl:60-88 records
why these ceilings must not move). postfx.c: unit 4 in the set-once sampler
block (:601-607), and at the GTAO draw bind `fx->spec_texture` with
`gatherSpec = split_live && ssgi_active` (split_live is computed at the spec
resolve, before the GTAO draw — in scope). Recorded, accepted: the gathered
spec is pre-occlusion (the composite hasn't run), a slight bounce
overestimate in crevices — smaller than the loss being recovered.

**D5 — SSR closure (spec text only).** The as-built records the structural
proof from Context and closes both "SSR occluded by the same cone" and the
SSR half of "SSGI/SSR escaping AO under SPLIT". If SSR ever learns to trace
model surfaces, the cone belongs at its composite (multiply rgb only, not
alpha — coverage must not change), with `ao_result_tex` threaded into
postfx_run_ssr; noted as the design for that future, not built now.

## As built

All three items landed or closed as planned; the discovered import bug was
the load-bearing piece.

- **Import (Phase 1):** the guarded LIGHTMAP fallback landed after the
  mapping-table loop; the log line `Occlusion(lightmap) texture loaded` on
  ao_fixture is the assertion. 13 goldens 0 px, raiden byte-identical.
- **aoMap diffuse-only (Phase 2):** the three split-arm deletions. The 13
  existing goldens held at exact 0 — aoMap is 1.0 there and multiply-by-one
  is an IEEE identity, so even the recompile was byte-clean. Under the
  studio HDR, split vs legacy differ on 494,363 px with the diff footprint
  exactly the AO-textured ring and band. `ao_fixture_golden.png` baked
  after 1:1 inspection; the battery is 14 goldens now. Recorded residual:
  captures/OIT (the inline arm) keep aoMap on their specular. Written here
  as "until LEGACY/BENT retirement collapses the arms" — **wrong, corrected
  in 11.9**: `splitAmbientSpec` is forced to 0 for the OIT alpha pass and
  for probe captures regardless of the spec-occ mode, so those arms never
  collapse and the residual is independent of the modes.
- **SSGI split-sum gather (Phase 3):** specTex on unit 4, gated on
  split-live AND SSGI-active, summed before the single WS_BOUNCE_MAX clamp.
  Measured on ao_fixture + studio HDR with --ssgi --taa: 13,732 px of 1.6M
  at RMSE 0.0017, strictly additive; determinism ×2 at 0 px. Recorded,
  accepted: the gathered spec is pre-occlusion — a slight bounce
  overestimate in crevices, smaller than the loss recovered.

**SSR closure (D5), the structural proof.** "SSR occluded by the same cone"
is empty in this engine and closes without code:

1. SSR produces non-zero output only on shadow-catcher pixels — the march
   early-outs on `floorFade < 0.004` (ssr_frag), and only the catcher writes
   a negative marker; model surfaces never trace (they rely on IBL, by
   design — screen-space rays off curved geometry graze their own
   silhouettes and sparkle).
2. The catcher is deliberately excluded from the AO chain: it writes
   `AuxOut = 0`, the sky sentinel, so GTAO's early-out yields ao = 1 and a
   view-facing bent normal on every catcher pixel, and the edge-aware AO
   blur's depth tolerance rejects bleed across the z = 0 cliff.

There is no pixel where SSR fires AND the cone term has data. The same
argument closes the SSR half of 11.4's "SSGI/SSR escaping AO under SPLIT":
the AO factor the reflection escapes is mix(1, ao ≈ 1, strength) ≈ 1. If
SSR ever learns to trace model surfaces, the cone belongs at its composite
— multiply rgb only, never alpha (coverage must not change) — with
`ao_result_tex` threaded into postfx_run_ssr.
