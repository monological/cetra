# 11.13 — B3 Pre-integrated skin shading (Penner 2011)

## Context

Roadmap Tier 2 item 12, the last unfinished item in that tier and the start of
the character tier (`docs/aaa-rendering-roadmap.md:257-267`, billed "Effort S,
zero infra"). The idea: for skin, replace the clamped Lambert `NdotL` falloff
with a curvature-aware RGB falloff, so the terminator wraps and reddens instead
of clipping to black — the difference between a head that reads as skin and one
that reads as painted plastic.

Exploration says **"Effort S, zero infra" is wrong and the sketch is wrong in
four independent ways**, three of them load-bearing enough that building to the
sketch ships a feature that does almost nothing. The roadmap sketch has already
proven wrong on four counts on a previous item (`:133-142`); the pattern is that
sketches written before the code mis-state where a term lives.

This spec is written so it **can end in rejection**. The precedent is
`specs/10.4:86-129` — built, measured, thrown away, recorded. Phase 0 exists to
make that outcome reachable on evidence rather than argument, and §K states the
numeric bar in advance.

Numbering: 11.11 and 11.12 are shipped froxel-fog work, so this is **11.13**.

## What the roadmap sketch gets wrong

**S1 — "extend `include/skin.glsl`" is a build break.** That file is linear-blend
*bone* skinning (`MAX_BONES`, `skinMatrix`), `#include`d by two **vertex** shaders
(`pbr_skinned_vert.glsl:43`, `shadow_depth_vert.glsl:16`). `fwidth`/`dFdx` do not
compile in a vertex stage, so following the sketch literally breaks the shadow
depth pass for every skinned mesh. New file
`cetra/shaders/include/preintegrated_skin.glsl`; `include/sheen.glsl` (included at
`pbr_frag.glsl:399`) is the structural model.

**S2 — "replace the Lambert term feeding att4 DiffuseOut" delivers a no-op.**
The shipped invariant is that blur is linear and `D = s·D_l`, so
`composite = S + (1−s)·D_l + s·blur(D_l)` — a convex blend of two non-negative
signals, which is the contract `pbr_frag.glsl:1714-1717` protects. Replace only
the att4 tap and you get

    composite = S + D_l + s·(blur(D_p) − D_p)

`hdr` still carries Lambert at full strength, and the Penner response enters
*only* as a signed second difference. Using the heat-kernel identity
`blur(D_p) − D_p ≈ (σ_b²/2)·F_θθ`, on the face at the terminator the intended
wrap is `D_p = 0.128` and the delivered value is
`F(90°, 0.336) − F(90°, 0.32) = 0.006` — **5% of what was asked for**. It is a
no-op, not a broken sign.

*(Negativity is real but secondary, and not by the obvious argument: integrating
the tail exactly gives `F''(t) = g(t) + g(t+π) − F(t) > 0` across the whole dark
side, so `blur(D_p) > D_p` there. The genuine negative cases are spatial, where
the second-order expansion fails — the limb, where a 48-px kernel spans 60°→180°
of surface; cast-shadow edges, where a wrap-lit pixel's neighbours have `D_p = 0`;
and albedo/normal detail, where a wrap-lit pixel adjoins a dark nostril or lip
line. That last one is the general case on a face, not an edge case.)*

**S3 — `length(fwidth(N))` measures the normal map, not the mesh.** At the point
the sketch samples it, `N` is post-normal-mapping (`:836-857`), and the shader
already computes that quantity at `:928-936` to widen roughness for **specular
anti-aliasing** — i.e. it is a texture-detail metric. Because σ scales *linearly*
in curvature, millimetre normal detail gives `curv ~ 10³/m`, hence `σ >> 1`
everywhere, hence the flat `1/π` response: **the terminator disappears entirely
and skin becomes a uniform waxy field**, mip- and distance-dependent. Curvature
must come from `normalize(Normal)`, the interpolated geometric normal.

Penner's own split is the right one: **curvature from the geometric normal,
`NdotL` from the shading normal** — surface detail belongs in the cosine.

**S4 — gating on `subsurface > 0` silently reshades the tree canopy.**
`engine->sss_enabled` defaults true (`engine.c:217`), and `apps/tree/src/tree.c`
sets `subsurface = 0.6` on leaves (`:1531`) and `0.45` on grass (`:1567`) — thin
foliage cards in an app CLAUDE.md calls baseline-grade deterministic.

## The premise: the clamp, and why this is not simply redundant

Penner pre-integration and the shipped Jimenez blur are the same physical
phenomenon in two coordinate systems, so where both run at full width they
genuinely double-count and variances add. The question is whether the shipped
system is ever in that regime.

`sss_blur_frag.glsl:78` clamps `radPx` to `MAX_PX = 48` (`:30`), with
`proj_scale = 0.5·projection[1][1]·fx->height` (`postfx.c:1335`). On the one head
in the tree (`my_models/face/face.cscn`, authored `radius 0.08`, head radius
0.25 m, camera 1.40 m, fov 22.9):

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| authored σ_ang (red) | `0.08/0.25` = 0.320 rad = **18.3°** |
| `radPx` requested | `0.08 · 2667 / 1.40` = 152 px |
| after the clamp | 48 px |
| delivered σ_ang | `0.0252/0.25` = 0.101 rad = **5.8°** — **31.5%** |

    both at authored 0.32:              √(0.32² + 0.32²)  = 0.453   (+41%, wrong)
    Penner 0.32 + clamped blur 0.101:   √(0.32² + 0.101²) = 0.336   (+5%)

**In the clamped regime the double-count objection is worth 5%, not 41%.** It
bites only past the knee (`d ≥ 0.08·2667/48 = 4.45 m` for the face), where the
blur runs unclamped.

The clamp also carries a shipped bug: unclamped, `radPx ∝ height` so the *world*
width is resolution-invariant; once clamped it is `MAX_PX·depth/projScale ∝
1/height`, so at `--render-scale 0.67` the delivered σ_ang rises to 0.150 —
**lower resolution scatters 50% more**. That is a 4.12 defect independent of B3,
and no gate covers it (`gates.py` has no render-scale entry).

## The other limitation: the cast-shadow terminator

`shadow` multiplies **outside** the diffuse (`:1380`, `:1386`), so at a cast-shadow
edge the falloff is roughly constant in `NdotL` and B3 contributes nothing. On a
face under one key, the nose, brow and neck terminators *are* cast-shadow edges.
**B3 softens only the attached terminator.**

Not fatal, and the complementarity is real: the attached terminator is the
large-area feature (the whole shaded side of the face) and the one that reads as
plastic; cast edges are already softened twice, by PCSS and by the screen-space
blur, which *does* cross them because it blurs `D` and `D` contains `shadow`. So
the blur owns cast edges and spatial albedo bleed; B3 owns the attached
terminator, which the blur under-delivers 3× because of the clamp. Penner's
second LUT (shadow-penumbra scattering) is the textbook fix and is **B3.1**, not
this spec — it changes `calculateShadow`'s signature, which `catcher_frag` shares.

## Settled design

**D1 — New include** `cetra/shaders/include/preintegrated_skin.glsl` (never
`skin.glsl`, S1). Also `#include`d by `sss_blur_frag.glsl` if the generator
supports it there, so the `MAX_PX` clamp cannot drift between the two consumers;
if not, mirror the constant with a comment, as that file already does for
`MAX_SSS_PROFILES`.

**D2 — Opt-in via `Material.curvature_scale`, default `0.0f` = off.** Keep the
roadmap's name; document `0 = off, 1.0 = physically correct` the way
`parallax_scale` documents its units. It is degenerate with `sssRadius` as a
continuous knob, but the distinguished off value carries a bit nothing else does,
and it makes `apps/tree`, the existing fixture, all 15 goldens and raiden
byte-identical **by construction**. Gate:

    bool skinPreint = sss && skinPreintEnabled > 0 && curvatureScale > 0.0
                      && sssProfileIndex >= 0;

All four are uniforms ⇒ uniform control flow ⇒ `dFdx` inside is well-defined
(precedent `if (specularAAStrength > 0.0)` at `:928`). The `sssProfileIndex >= 0`
term is load-bearing: `DiffuseOut.a = max(idx+1,0) = 0` for `idx = -1`, which the
blur reads as non-skin and rejects (`:98`) — such a material already gets no
screen-space SSS and must get no pre-integration either.

**D3 — `skinPreintEnabled` folds in `!engine->capturing`** at the upload
(`cetra/src/render.c:348`, precedent `:356`). Load-bearing, not an optimisation:
B3 modifies `Lo` and therefore `FragColor`, which *does* land in probe/DDGI
radiance, and the deficit term (D5) depends on the **capture** camera's
projection and depth. Baking a camera-dependent wrap into an irradiance probe is
wrong in a way that shows up as slightly-wrong GI that changes when you move.

**D4 — The falloff replaces `NdotL` in BOTH `Lo` and the att4 tap, with the same
value.** Target semantics `composite = S + (1−s)·D_p + s·blur(D_p)`, preserving
the cancellation on one signal. Additions only — the two existing statements stay
byte-identical, no re-parenthesising. The in-loop guarded `Lo +=` is shipped
precedent: `:1391-1394` does exactly this for `subsurfaceTransmission` with zero
measured drift in 4.12.

    if (skinPreint) {
        vec3 dSkin = kD * albedoMap / PI *
                     (preintDiffuse(dot(N, L), sigmaAng) - vec3(NdotL)) *
                     radiance * shadow;
        Lo += dSkin;
        sssDiffuse += dSkin;
    }

`dot(N, L)` is recomputed **unclamped** — the whole wrap band lives at
`NdotL < 0` and `:1291` clamps. Deliberately raw (no `sheenScale`, `coatAtten` or
firefly factor), matching the existing tap, so the pre-existing `Lo`-vs-tap
mismatch applies only to the Lambert part and does not grow. Fallback if drift
appears: accumulate a local `skinLo` and add once after the loop at `:1396`.

*Mismatch quantified: `sheenScale` = 1.0 and `coatAtten` = 0.0 on every shipped
SSS material, and `clampFactor` = 1.0 exactly (`BRDF_MAX = 1000` vs a diffuse
peak near 1/π). **Cost today: zero.** Two latent negative-value modes exist for
materials nothing authors yet — clearcoated skin at grazing (`coatAtten → 1`) and
gloss-lip skin at roughness 0.02 (`clampFactor ≈ 5e-4`). Record as a separate
4.12 correctness item; B3 must not inherit them, which the raw delta ensures.*

**D5 — Composition: the deficit form.** `pbr_frag` already knows curvature,
depth and the authored radius, so it computes the blur's shortfall itself rather
than the blur being retuned:

    float want    = prof.w;
    float got     = min(want, sssMaxScatterPerDepth * (-ViewPos.z));
    float k       = got / max(want, 1e-6);
    float deficit = sqrt(max(1.0 - k*k, 0.0));
    sigmaBase     = want * curv * curvatureScale * deficit;

with `sssMaxScatterPerDepth = MAX_PX / (0.5·proj[1][1]·fx->height)` as one
uniform bundling `MAX_PX` and `projScale`. Six ALU, no new G-buffer traffic, blur
untouched. Measured against the authored 0.320 rad:

| config | got | k | deficit | σ_preint | σ_blur | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| face 1.40 m, scale 1.0 | 0.0252 | 0.315 | 0.949 | 0.304 | 0.101 | **0.320** |
| face 1.40 m, scale 0.67 | 0.0376 | 0.470 | 0.883 | 0.283 | 0.150 | **0.320** |
| face 4.45 m (knee) | 0.080 | 1.0 | 0 | 0 | 0.320 | **0.320** |
| face 8 m | 0.080 | 1.0 | 0 | 0 | 0.320 | **0.320** |

Constant across distance and render scale, continuous through the knee. It fixes
the resolution bug for opted-in materials as a side effect and makes total
scatter view-independent — *more* stable than today, where scatter shrinks as you
walk toward a face. It also makes `curvature_scale` non-degenerate: `sssRadius`
widens both terms and the deficit, `curvature_scale` widens only the angular one.

Rejected: retuning the blur to high-frequency-only (throws away the one thing
pre-integration cannot do — bleed across albedo/normal detail — and re-authors
every SSS asset); plain additive (correct near, 41% over-soft past the knee).
Documented fallback if the crossfade reads as a seam: `deficit ≡ 1.0`, one line.

**Consequence for fixtures: B3 is a no-op wherever the blur runs unclamped.** The
A/B must be shot in the clamped regime (close-up, or a large authored radius).

**D6 — σ_ang, and the curvature estimator.** The reduction is *exact*, not
small-angle: substituting the true chord `2r·sin(x/2)` into the profile makes `r`
cancel identically, leaving one dimensionless parameter per channel,
`σ = sssColor_c · sssRadius · curv`. So the fit domain is exactly 2-D. Use the
exact chord in the ground truth — the face's broad sub-Gaussian is already
`2.2 × 0.32 = 0.70` rad, whose ±2σ sits where chord/arc is 0.92, so the true
kernel has fatter angular tails than a Gaussian of width σ.

Estimator — deviate from the roadmap's mixed-norm `fwidth` ratio, reusing the
world derivatives already at top-level flow (`:942-943`):

    float skinCurvature(vec3 Ng, vec3 ddxW, vec3 ddyW) {
        vec3 dNx = dFdx(Ng), dNy = dFdy(Ng);
        float dP2 = dot(ddxW, ddxW) + dot(ddyW, ddyW);
        return sqrt((dot(dNx, dNx) + dot(dNy, dNy)) / max(dP2, 1e-12));
    }

Exactly `1/R` on a sphere at any screen orientation, two fewer derivative ops,
no anisotropy bias (the roadmap form is up to ±40% off edge-on).

- Flat surfaces → `dFdx(Ng) = 0` → σ = 0 → delta exactly `+0.0` (see D7).
- **Hard-edged meshes are benign by the GL quad rule, not by luck**: derivatives
  are computed per 2×2 quad of the *same* primitive, and helper invocations
  extrapolate that triangle's varyings, so a flat-shaded triangle gives
  `dFdx(Ng) = 0` in the interior *and* in every partially-covered edge quad.
  Silhouettes are not garbage either — `|dP/dx|` and `|dNg/dx|` blow up together
  and the ratio stays `1/R`.
- **The real noise source is tessellation**, not silhouettes: the interpolated
  normal is only C⁰ across triangles, so a quad straddling a boundary sees a large
  ΔN over a small ΔP. Worst exactly where the deficit makes σ_preint largest.

**Clamp σ, not curvature** — curvature has units of 1/length and any bound on it
is a scene-scale magic number; σ is dimensionless. Clamp the base scalar so the
red-widest hue relation survives (house precedent `:1376-1379`):

    base = min(base, SKIN_SIGMA_MAX / max(maxComp(prof.rgb), 1e-3));

`SKIN_SIGMA_MAX ≈ 2.0`. Be honest that this is a **look** parameter, not just a
fit guard: nose tip, ear rim and lip edge run `curv ~ 200/m` → σ ~16 before
clamping, and the mixture never fully flattens (the 0.30σ core keeps the cosine
mode alive), so this value visibly controls how much thin features glow.

**D7 — The fit, and the constraint that makes it affordable.** Ground truth is
the exact integral over the engine's own mixture. Two corrections to how it must
be computed: it is a **mixture**, and the sub-Gaussians are **peak-1, not
area-normalised** (`profileWeight` returns `0.35g₁+0.40g₂+0.25g₃` and the blur
normalises by `sumW`), so effective area weights are `(0.0995, 0.379, 0.521)` —
**52% broad tail, 10% core**. An integrator that misses this fits systematically
too sharp.

**σ does not depend on `L`.** The fit must factor into σ-only coefficients
computed once per fragment × a cheap shape function evaluated per light, or the
per-light cost is ~50 ALU and the roadmap's "~10 ALU" is fiction. Recommended
family — constrained wrap + cubic shaper, all `vec3`:

    per fragment: u = σ²/(1+σ²);  w = u·(W1+u·(W2+u·W3));
                  a1 = 1+u·(A1+u·A2);  a2 = u·(B1+u·B2);  a3 = u·(C1+u·C2);
    per light:    t = max((NdotLraw + w)/(1+w), 0.0);  D = t·(a1 + t·(a2 + t·a3));

**σ = 0 is bit-exact**: `u = +0.0` ⇒ `w = 0, a1 = 1, a2 = a3 = 0` ⇒
`t = max((x+0)/1, 0) = max(x,0)` by IEEE ⇒ `D = t`. So `D − NdotL` is exactly
`+0.0` and flat geometry and hard edges are *provably* byte-identical. Write it in
this algebraic order and record why. **No `pow`** — `pow(x,1.0)` is not
ulp-exact and `pow(0,1)` is undefined in GLSL, which rules out the obvious
wrapped-diffuse-with-exponent family.

Nine constants fitted by least squares with two hard constraints: **energy**
(`∫D dθ = 2` exactly for every σ, since convolution with a normalised kernel
preserves it — a systematic energy error is a global brightness shift on all
skin) and **monotonicity in `NdotL`** (non-monotone reads as a ring at the
terminator). Note the energy constraint alone kills both obvious wrap families.
Bars: max abs ≤ 0.005, RMS ≤ 0.0015, max relative ≤ 2% where `D > 0.05`,
`|∫D dθ − 2| ≤ 0.005` — reported separately for σ ≤ 0.6 (the shipped band) and
the full domain σ ∈ [0,2], with behaviour shown out to σ = 4.

Honest cost: **~60 scalar per skin fragment + ~15 per light**, not ~10.

`tools/gen_skin_preint_fit.py`, `gen_ltc_lut.py`'s house pattern. Prints nine
constants for paste into the include (too small to justify a generated header),
and — load-bearing — **is re-runnable as a verifier**: given the pasted constants,
print their error against a fresh integration. That is the only thing that catches
drift if anyone edits `sss_blur_frag.glsl:38-46`, a coupling with no compiler
enforcement. It also prints three spec numbers: exact-chord vs small-angle
divergence, the matched-pair vs full-mixture convolution error (the measured cost
of D5's variance budget — my 43% estimate is arithmetic, not measurement), and
the shipped-parameter table.

**D8 — Uniforms.** `_update_program_material_uniforms` (`render.c:107`) has no
`engine`, so the per-material vec4 route is blocked and changing that signature
is a wider blast radius. Upload `vec4 sssProfiles[MAX_SSS_PROFILES]` at the
per-program-switch block (`render.c:340-356`, `engine->postfx` reachable) as one
ranged `glUniform4fv` — the idiom already at `postfx.c:1350-1352`. Dynamic
indexing of a uniform array is legal in GLSL 3.30 (only *sampler* arrays need
constant expressions). Re-adding `Material.subsurface_radius` is rejected: it
would be a second source of truth that can silently disagree with the profile the
blur uses, which under D5 breaks the variance budget rather than being merely
write-only.

| uniform | scope |
|---|---|
| `int skinPreintEnabled` | program switch (`&& !capturing`) |
| `float curvatureScale` | material |
| `vec4 sssProfiles[8]` | program switch |
| `float sssMaxScatterPerDepth` | program switch |

**D9 — A new fixture, not an edit to the existing one.** Scaling
`gen_sss_fixture.py:66-69`'s nodes would silently empty the repo's only SSS gate:
in `sss_scale_fixture.cscn` the camera is eye `(0,0.25,1.5)`, fov 40 vertical, and
the sphere centres at `y=1.0` already sit ~36° above the view axis against a 20°
half-fov — only the lower limbs are in shot, and a 0.35-scaled node leaves the
frustum entirely. Exactly the silent-cap failure `gates.py:569-573` exists to
prevent. New generator (precedent `specs/10.4:75-84`, `specs/11.6:124-140`).

`assets/gen_skin_curvature_fixture.py` → `skin_curvature_fixture.gltf`: one sphere
mesh (48×96, POS+NRM, winding per `7ae16d0`), one material `skin_curv`, three
nodes at uniform scale 0.5/1.0/2.0 → curvature 2.0/1.0/0.5 m⁻¹, a 4× spread in 2×
steps giving two independent ordering inequalities, authored purely by node scale
so the ladder is exactly `1/R`. Sibling `.cscn`: one **directional** key (identical
`L` on all three spheres, which is what makes the control assertion exact),
grazing/front, no environment, ambient 0, neutral tonemap, exposure 1. Camera and
authored radius chosen so the spheres sit in the **clamped** regime (per D5).

**D10 — One golden, baked with `--no-sss --no-shadows --no-bloom`.** SSS has no
pixel reference today. Baking without the blur pins the analytic falloff alone,
so the blur follow-up cannot invalidate it. Battery 15 → 16.

## Phases

Branch `skin-preint` off master. Spec committed alone first, then the task list.
Every phase: `./build.sh`; golden sweep exact 0; `gates.py` green; determinism ×2;
raiden byte-identical to `tmp/taau_p0_raiden.ppm`; commit.

**Phase 0 — measurement that can kill the feature. No shippable code.**
- **M1 (the clamp):** `face.cscn` with/without `--no-sss` at framebuffer heights
  200/500/1000 (requested `radPx` 28/71/141 = unclamped/clamped/clamped). Measure
  `w(H)`, the shift of the 5%-of-lit terminator crossing in units of image height;
  `ρ = w(1000)/w(200)` predicts ≈1.0 unclamped, ≈0.34 fully clamped. Throwaway
  `MAX_PX = 512` edit to confirm the clamp is load-bearing and inspect for tap

- **M2 (the shadow term):** with/without `--no-shadows`, divided. On scanlines

- **M3 (redundancy):** same division on a throwaway front-lit copy of the SSS

- **M4 (D5's two invariances), once the fit tool exists:** resolution — same frame

## Kill criterion (§K), written before implementation

Rejected, shipping Phase 1 only, if any holds when measured:

- **K1 — the curvature term is dead.** On the fixture with `--no-sss
  --no-shadows`, linear luma at an analytic 95° differs by < **15%** between the
  R=0.5 and R=2.0 spheres. A falloff that ignores a 4× curvature change is a wrap
  term, and `saturate((NdotL+w)/(1+w))` is two lines and wins on cost.
- **K2 — invisible where it was bought.** On `face.cscn` with shadows on, fewer
  than **25%** of terminator-band pixels change by more than 2/255 vs
  `--no-skin-preint`.
- **K3 — the blur already does it.** M1 returns ρ ≥ 0.90 *and* M3 shows the blur
  already delivering ≥ 80% of the angular softening B3 would add.
- **K4 — it boils.** TAA churn AE on the fixture > **2×** the `--no-skin-preint`
  run, not brought under 1.3× by `SKIN_SIGMA_MAX`.
- **K5 — no domain left.** M2's surviving wrap support < **4°**. Re-open only
  with B3.1 in scope.
- **K6 — the fit misses.** D7's bars unreachable with energy and monotonicity
  active, and the Φ-based escalation costs > 4× the ALU budget.

A kill is recorded as an ATTEMPTED/REJECTED section in `specs/10.4:86-129`'s form,
Phase 1 still ships, and the roadmap's B3 row is closed with the reason.

## Verification

Master gate every phase: 16 goldens (15 until Phase 4) exact 0; `gates.py` green;
determinism ×2; raiden byte-identical. Three new gates:

1. **Off-path byte identity** — `cornell_point`, `sss_scale_fixture`,
   `contact_fixture` rendered twice each (default vs `--no-skin-preint`), assert
   AE == 0; plus, from Phase 3, AE > 0 on the curvature fixture. The zeros prove
   the guarded block is inert; the fourth stops a trivial pass if the flag is dead.
2. **Total-scatter invariance** (D5's payoff, and the shipped resolution bug) —
   the fixture at two render scales, terminator width in surface radians, assert
   within 5%.
3. **Curvature ordering** — `--no-sss --no-shadows --no-bloom`, sampling
   analytically-known surface points via `_projector`. Assert wrap ordering follows
   radius (≥1.15× per 2× curvature step), a floor on delivered wrap, reddening
   alive vs 45°, and **no free lunch** (fully-lit luma unchanged within 3%).
   Controls on the OFF frame prove the rig sees a real terminator and equal
   illumination. A minimum-screen-radius guard ERRORs rather than passing silently.

Not covered, deliberately: absolute agreement with Penner's LUT (ordering and sign
only); behaviour with shadows (reported, not asserted); faceted and skinned
geometry; hue accuracy beyond "redder than at 45°".

## Recorded, not fixed

- **On an IBL-lit face, B3 does nothing** — the ambient tap (`:1526`) has no `L`.
  Correct ambient pre-integration needs a curvature-widened irradiance prefilter
  the engine does not have.
- **Skin under an area/LTC panel gets no SSS at all today** (panels `continue` at
  `:1212`). Self-consistent, not broken — but a softbox portrait is *the* canonical
  skin setup, so this is where a user hits it first. Own roadmap line.
- Penner's wrap and `subsurfaceTransmission` both brighten just past 90°; a
  material carrying both doubles the rim. Fixture check.
- Two latent negative-value modes in the shipped `Lo`-vs-tap mismatch (D4).
- The blur's *authored* width deficit (as opposed to its resolution dependence,
  which D5 fixes for opted-in materials) needs a downsampled chain — own spec.

## Open risks

1. **Crossfade seamlessness.** D5 makes the total width invariant, but the two
   terms have different shapes; the total can be right while the character reads
   as changing on a dolly. Most likely reason to fall back to `deficit ≡ 1.0`.
2. **Tessellation noise on the real face mesh**, worst exactly where the deficit
   makes σ_preint largest. Could demand a curvature smoothing pass — a much larger
   feature.
3. **ALU budget** — ~60 + ~15/light believed achievable; the Φ escalation is 3-4×.
4. **Whether nine parameters suffice** for a 2-D surface with a moving kink under
   two hard constraints.
5. **Byte-identity of the `Lo +=`** — the `:1391-1394` precedent is strong but
   4.12's zero drift was measured, not proven.

## Effort

**M, not S.** The shader math is small; everything around it is not — a fit tool
with a verifier mode, one fixture, three gates, one golden, a `.cscn` schema key,
a material scalar, four uniforms, a capture gate.
