# 10.8.1 — HDR environment aliasing: mip the equirect before conversion

## Context

Under the aquarium 8k HDR the whole backdrop shimmers while TAA runs — the
user's live report, confirmed by the churn harness: 339,501 px frame-to-
frame at the wide framing, with the dilated diff map showing the four
spheres as clean islands and the ENTIRE environment boiling. Root cause,
verified in code: `load_hdr_environment` (ibl.c:272-279) uploads the
equirect as GL_RGB16F with plain GL_LINEAR and NO mips, and the
equirect-to-cubemap conversion then samples it at 2048-face resolution — a
~4:1 minification with raw bilinear taps. That bakes 8k-frequency aliasing
into the environment cube's base mip; the skybox's own trilinear sampling
(skybox_frag.glsl:56, cube mipped at ibl.c:661) cannot repair content that
is aliased at mip 0. TAA's subpixel jitter then re-samples the jaggies at a
different offset each frame — aliasing animated into shimmer. The studio
HDR never showed it because its content is smooth; the sheen/SSR work is
NOT implicated (the spheres are the stable part of the frame).

The conversion shader (ibl_equirect_frag.glsl:20) samples with `texture()`
— implicit derivatives — so mipping the source engages filtering with no
shader change.

## Design

In `load_hdr_environment` (ibl.c, after the glTexImage2D upload): set
`GL_TEXTURE_MIN_FILTER` to `GL_LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR` and call
`glGenerateMipmap(GL_TEXTURE_2D)`, with a comment recording the why (the
conversion is a ~4:1 minification for an 8k source; unmipped sampling
bakes aliasing into the env cube that TAA jitter animates). Wrap modes
stay untouched (the horizontal CLAMP_TO_EDGE seam behavior is
pre-existing and out of scope).

**Known risk, checked by measurement:** implicit derivatives explode at
the equirect's atan seam (uv.x wraps 1 -> 0), which with mips picks the
coarsest level for a thin texel column on the face crossing the seam. At
2048 faces this is usually invisible; verify with a camera aimed at the
seam azimuth. If visible, the recorded remedy is a manual LOD in the
conversion shader computed from the direction derivative (seam-free)
rather than uv — a follow-up, not taken preemptively.

**Residual option, also by measurement:** even clean band-limited content
can sizzle slightly at near-1:1 frequencies; if the churn number stays
high after the fix, a small positive LOD bias on the skybox sample is the
lever — measured, not preemptive.

**Cost:** +1/3 mip memory on the retained hdr_texture (~67 MB for an 8k
RGB16F) and a one-time glGenerateMipmap at load. A follow-up candidate
(recorded, not done): free hdr_texture after conversion — nothing samples
it afterward.

## Blast radius, stated honestly

Every HDR-environment render changes BY DESIGN (the old cube content was
aliased). That includes the raiden determinism baseline — the 0-px
cross-build chain re-baselines at this commit, recorded in the spec.
Everything else is untouched: procedural-sky and no-env scenes never call
load_hdr_environment, so all committed goldens (aerial, froxel, sheen,
specular, cornell family) and the whole gate suite must stay byte-identical
— proven, not assumed.

## As built

The plan's design evolved twice by measurement; the shipped change is
three mechanisms, not one. All churn numbers are frame-120-vs-122 AE at
1280x720, aquarium 8k HDR, pinned exposure; the pre-change baseline was
339,501 px (RMSE 61.4) wide-framing.

1. **Equirect mips before conversion** (the planned fix): correct but
   minor -- 339,501 -> 316,969 alone (~-7%). The comment's original "~4:1"
   minification figure was reviewed and corrected: ~1.3:1 at an equator
   face centre, severe only toward the poles.
2. **The spec's LOD-bias lever: measured and REJECTED.** Bias 1.0 on the
   skybox sample gave -37% (316,969 -> 200,908) at a real, visible
   sharpness cost. Not shipped.
3. **Motion-adaptive TAA resolve window (promoted lever, not in the
   plan):** feedback mix(0.97 rest, 0.9 moving) on the resolve's own
   velocity -- the 10.7.2 SSR accumulator's split applied to TAA. Alone:
   316,969 -> 127,762 (RMSE -> 32.8), the studio close-up 123,826 ->
   83,046, at zero sharpness cost. THE BLAST-RADIUS SECTION OF THE PLAN IS
   SUPERSEDED: this alters convergence in every TAA-live frame regardless
   of environment (goldens survive because they are all no-TAA); both
   TAA-live churn gates dropped in lockstep with their no-shadow floors
   (dir 9245 -> 7965 over 6698 -> 5882; catcher 104,138 -> 89,892 over
   101,316 -> 90,641). The recorded cost: lighting changes on static
   geometry converge ~3x slower (bounded by the neighborhood clamp;
   documented at the constants in taa_resolve_frag).
4. **Anisotropic filtering (8x) on the environment cubemap** (added after
   the user's live report): the interactive render app AUTO-ORBITS in
   every windowed session (set_mouse_drag_auto_orbit(!headless)), so the
   camera is never truly at rest and the at-rest window never fully
   engages live. The visible artifact at the user's close-up was the
   ground-projected grate -- fine periodic content at grazing angle --
   whose moire REORGANIZES under continuous micro-motion. That is a
   prefiltering gap, not a temporal one: isotropic trilinear at grazing
   must pick between blur and moire; aniso resolves the footprint. The
   cube sets its own parameter because texture.c's aniso helper targets
   GL_TEXTURE_2D. Verified visually at the user's exact camera: the grate
   resolves as a mesh instead of crawling mush. Static AE churn rises to
   154,042 (RMSE 35.4) BECAUSE the backdrop now resolves real detail that
   trilinear had blurred away -- the static count metric penalizes
   sharpness; the motion artifact it fixes is what the user reported.

**Seam check (the plan's recorded risk): PASS.** A render aimed at the
equirect wrap azimuth (-X) shows no blurred column -- the conversion bakes
face-by-face, so the uv-wrap derivative spike affects at most a
fragment-wide strip, invisibly at 2048.

**Proofs:** all committed goldens 0 px (no-TAA by construction); gates
pass; the raiden determinism chain re-baselines at this commit as planned
(all HDR-env renders change by design -- the aliased cube content was the
bug).

**Review outcomes** (combined thermo + simplify agent): the redundant
velocity fetch now reuses the reprojection's own vector (the identity that
makes the motion measure sound); the motion-window comment moved off the
inverse-luma essay's tail to the constants and the measure site; the
feedback pair became named constants with the frames-window arithmetic and
the honest lighting-lag cost; the dead-band comment records that its units
(internal-resolution px) differ from ssr_accum's (effect-resolution px);
the minification ratio corrected. Recorded, not done: freeing hdr_texture
after conversion (nothing samples it after the cube bake; the mip chain
strengthens the case).

**Recorded follow-ups:** an interactive "auto-orbit off" toggle would let
a live session actually reach the at-rest window (today only headless
does); if backdrop sizzle at rest ever needs more, the measured next
levers are a higher rest feedback or the rejected LOD bias.
