# 10.8.1 — HDR environment aliasing: mip the equirect before conversion

## Context

Under the aquarium 8k HDR the whole backdrop shimmers while TAA runs — the
user's live report, confirmed by the churn harness: 339,501 px frame-to-
frame at the wide framing, with the dilated diff map showing the four
spheres as clean islands and the ENTIRE environment boiling. Root cause,
verified in code: `load_hdr_environment` (ibl.c:272-279) uploads the
equirect as GL_RGB16F with plain GL_LINEAR and NO mips, and the
equirect-to-cubemap conversion then samples it at 2048-face resolution — a
~4:1 minification with raw bilinear taps. That bakes 8k-frequency aliasing
into the environment cube's base mip; the skybox's own trilinear sampling
(skybox_frag.glsl:56, cube mipped at ibl.c:661) cannot repair content that
is aliased at mip 0. TAA's subpixel jitter then re-samples the jaggies at a
different offset each frame — aliasing animated into shimmer. The studio
HDR never showed it because its content is smooth; the sheen/SSR work is
NOT implicated (the spheres are the stable part of the frame).

The conversion shader (ibl_equirect_frag.glsl:20) samples with `texture()`
— implicit derivatives — so mipping the source engages filtering with no
shader change.

## Design

In `load_hdr_environment` (ibl.c, after the glTexImage2D upload): set
`GL_TEXTURE_MIN_FILTER` to `GL_LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR` and call
`glGenerateMipmap(GL_TEXTURE_2D)`, with a comment recording the why (the
conversion is a ~4:1 minification for an 8k source; unmipped sampling
bakes aliasing into the env cube that TAA jitter animates). Wrap modes
stay untouched (the horizontal CLAMP_TO_EDGE seam behavior is
pre-existing and out of scope).

**Known risk, checked by measurement:** implicit derivatives explode at
the equirect's atan seam (uv.x wraps 1 -> 0), which with mips picks the
coarsest level for a thin texel column on the face crossing the seam. At
2048 faces this is usually invisible; verify with a camera aimed at the
seam azimuth. If visible, the recorded remedy is a manual LOD in the
conversion shader computed from the direction derivative (seam-free)
rather than uv — a follow-up, not taken preemptively.

**Residual option, also by measurement:** even clean band-limited content
can sizzle slightly at near-1:1 frequencies; if the churn number stays
high after the fix, a small positive LOD bias on the skybox sample is the
lever — measured, not preemptive.

**Cost:** +1/3 mip memory on the retained hdr_texture (~67 MB for an 8k
RGB16F) and a one-time glGenerateMipmap at load. A follow-up candidate
(recorded, not done): free hdr_texture after conversion — nothing samples
it afterward.

## Blast radius, stated honestly

Every HDR-environment render changes BY DESIGN (the old cube content was
aliased). That includes the raiden determinism baseline — the 0-px
cross-build chain re-baselines at this commit, recorded in the spec.
Everything else is untouched: procedural-sky and no-env scenes never call
load_hdr_environment, so all committed goldens (aerial, froxel, sheen,
specular, cornell family) and the whole gate suite must stay byte-identical
— proven, not assumed.
