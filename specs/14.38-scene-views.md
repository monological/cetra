# cetra 14.38: scene views

## Context

**What 14.38 builds.** cetra stops assuming one camera filling the window. A frame draws any number
of **scene views**, each a camera, a rectangle, its own post settings and its own buffers and
history, drawn off-screen and copied into its rectangle before the overlays. A frame with no view
draws no scene and no post chain. A still view averages jittered frames, stops drawing, and is
copied again on frames that only redraw the UI. It is items 3 and 4 of `docs/eda-roadmap.md`.

**Why now.** pcb's spec 8.1, the 3D view, is planned in its worktree
(`apps/pcb/.claude/worktrees/8.1-the-3d-view`, branch `8.1-the-3d-view`). Its phase 7 needs this
spec on cetra's master: any number of 3D panes beside the layout, each its own camera, resting
when still, the window idle while you type. Its phase 8 needs 14.39 (tags in 3D), which builds on
the per-view state made here. The brief is in pcb's spec, "What cetra builds".

**The shape.** The same split Unreal, Godot and Blender use: one shared scene; per view a camera,
a rectangle and settings the app writes, plus state the engine owns (buffers, histories, exposure,
last frame's matrices); shader programs compiled once; the window composites finished pictures.

### Decided with the user (2026-11-16)

1. **Views are explicit everywhere.** Nothing reads "the current camera": every pass that draws a
   view is handed it, world work takes one named focus view, and a tool takes the view it steers.
   Probe and GI captures become views. Every feature draws in every view; world caches and the rain
   and water windows follow the focus view (7). **Phases 1-3 are a pure refactor**: every golden
   byte-identical. Per-camera state a subsystem keeps for itself moves into the view's state, and
   every history's "last frame" is its own view's.
2. **Shadows.** The sun's cascades and the per-frame lamp shadows are drawn per view, just before
   that view, reusing the one shadow texture, fitted to its camera and leaving out what it hides.
   Kept (cached) lamp shadows stay shared; the engine logs once when a view hides a node within a
   kept lamp's reach.
3. **A still view finishes by accumulation.** TAA while it moves; once still, an equal-weight average
   of jittered frames at TAA's place in the chain, then it stops. A view's count is 16-64, 32 when
   an app turns it on. The last TAA picture shows until the average holds 4 frames.
4. **Knowing a view changed.** The engine notices its camera, rectangle and settings (byte for
   byte), meshes added or uploaded, and nodes moved. The app declares plain writes to materials,
   lights and scene settings with one call on the scene. A gate holds that a still view rests.
5. **`PostFX` splits four ways**: per-view plain settings (Exposure's settings with them) and
   per-view state (buffers, histories, the meter's readings); shader programs shared; the window's
   own parts (the CRT, the picture overlays draw on, the final pass). Settings that had setters
   because the engine must react become plain fields the engine notices changing. What owns GPU
   memory (a loaded LUT, an app's passes) stays out of the plain struct. The 2D preset splits into
   a view half and a scene half. The skin profiles move to the scene.
6. **No default view.** Every app makes its own. **Every view shows the engine's current scene**:
   the world's once-a-frame work (the walk, the hooks, fires, lighting caches, uploads) stays on that
   one scene, and the mask (11) lets views show different parts of it; a scene per view is left for
   later. Rectangles are in the UI's units, or "fills its window". `engine->camera` and
   `engine_set_camera` go; the rig attaches to a view. The window has its own clear colour. Views
   never draw into the window directly, so a second window can come later; no Window object is
   built now.
7. **One focus view** (the first added, or the one the app names) ranks and centres all world work:
   GI and reflection-probe residency, kept lamp shadows, terrain pages and their votes, the rain's
   cover map, water's caustic window and ripples, the origin shift, the audio listener. The engine
   logs once when another view draws rain or water. Rules that would serve every view equally are
   left for later (below).
8. **Samples per view**, default 4. Changing it rebuilds that view's buffers.
9. **An exact background.** A view's background is either "lit" (today's: cleared into the scene and
   carried by the chain) or "exact": the app gives the colour wanted on screen, and the engine
   inverts the view's tone curve and display encode on the CPU to the value it clears to. The engine
   logs once when an exact background meets a stage it cannot invert.
10. **A hidden view keeps its buffers and finished picture.** Shown again, it reuses the picture if
    nothing it shows changed, else it starts as a cut.
11. **A 32-bit mask on each node**, inherited down the tree, default all bits; a view draws a node
    when they share a bit. Its shadows honour it.
12. **Render scale and supersampling per view**, as 8.
13. **Every view that needs a draw draws every frame**, in list order.

### My calls, for the user to overturn

- **Accumulation is off unless a view turns it on** (`accumulate_frames` 0). Every existing app, gate
  and golden keeps TAA's behaviour; pcb turns it on. A view with accumulation off draws every frame,
  as a scene does today.
- **The name is `SceneView`** (`scene_view_*`): `CanvasView` already exists (`canvas.h:376`).
- **A view holds its camera by value**; a pane split copies the view. `free_engine` freeing a camera
  its header calls borrowed (`engine.c:447`) goes with `engine->camera`.
- **Debug render modes and overlays are per-view settings** (albedo/normal modes, wireframe, light
  lines, bones, axes, the camera HUD). Capture views have none, which fixes light lines being baked
  into probes and GI (`render.c:2039` is not skipped under a capture).
- **The debug GUI and the session file act on the focus view.** The debug blits (sky LUTs, the
  lighting atlas, the fire slice) draw over the focus view.
- **The engine owns an added view**, as `engine_add_scene` owns a scene; `engine_remove_view` hands
  it back.
- **Overlapping views composite in list order**, later on top.
- **The picking rays become view functions** taking a point inside the view
  (`engine.c:4188-4231`).
- **`load_hdr_environment_memory`** reads an HDR from bytes, for `cetra_embed_files`.
- **With zero views there is no focus**: caches keep their last ranking, captures still run (each is
  its own view), the origin does not shift, the listener stays where it was.
- **A rectangle is in the UI's units**: float window points, top-left origin, +Y down, as `ui.h`
  and `CanvasView` (`canvas.h:377`) lay out. The engine turns it into framebuffer pixels by the
  content scale, each edge rounded to the nearest pixel, so two panes sharing an edge share a
  pixel boundary with no gap. The rays take window points relative to the view.
- **A view's settings start from the engine's defaults at its typical size**:
  `engine_init_view_settings(engine, &settings)`, then the app's own values, then
  `create_scene_view`, keeping the rule "set the size first, your own values after".
  `engine_set_typical_size` rewrites every added view's engine-chosen lengths and its rig, as it
  rewrites `PostFX`'s today, and the compare restarts it.
- **The mask hides a node's meshes and their shadows**, nothing else: lights, particles, fire,
  decals, fog volumes and water are not filtered. A moved node restarts only the views sharing its
  bit when all it carries is masked meshes; a node carrying anything the mask does not hide (a
  light) restarts every view.
- **Accumulation needs render scale 1.** A view asking for both logs once and keeps TAAU. At
  supersampling 2 the average runs at the post size, where TAA does.
- **Engine-wide settings that change the picture** (the material-feature toggles, LOD bias, the
  culling and draw levers, alpha jitter, `engine.h:308-436`) are declared by whoever writes them:
  `engine_mark_changed`, which restarts every view, called by the app, the GUI and the session file.
- **Readers the inventory missed** take the focus view: the IBL bake's fog settings
  (`ibl.c:625-635`) and the game settings' field of view (`game/settings.c:342-345`).
- **Headless, one view at its size** is a window made at that size with one view filling it, as
  `pcb render` will; no new capture path.
- **The canvas app's 3D pane is a small scene built in code** (lit spheres and a floor under an
  environment from the corpus), so it needs none of the render app's `.cscn` code. The corpus scenes
  for the multi-view arms and the look go through `apps/render`.

## Found while planning

- **About 90 camera reads in 13 files** (`engine.c`, `render.c`, `shadow.c`, `water.c`,
  `rain_render.c`, `fire_render.c`, `gi_volume.c`, `probe_set.c`, `layers_vt.c`, `app.c`, `gui.c`,
  `config_snapshot.c`, `game/game.c`), plus the apps. Sorted into the three kinds by an inventory:
  draws a view; world work ranked by an eye; a tool steering a camera.
- **`PostFX` mixes four things** (`postfx.h:232-872`): settings, ~60 size-dependent targets and
  histories, ~40 programs compiled from source per instance (`postfx.c:851-897`,
  `program.c:1484`), and the window's CRT, picture and present pass. Exposure mixes its settings
  with the meter's readings (`exposure.h:43`).
- **The session file binds post fields through `engine->postfx`** and exposure and camera through
  the engine (`config_snapshot.c:999-1010`).
- **`engine_set_2d_preset` writes view and scene settings in one call** (`engine.c:2860`).
- **The background is a working-space value**: `clear_color` clears attachment 0 (`engine.c:154`)
  unscaled and runs through the tone curve and the display encode. Exposure is applied at the scene
  passes, never in the tone map (`tonemap_frag.glsl:414`), so it never reaches the background. The
  encode is a 2.2 power, not sRGB (`include/display.glsl:6-15`). cetra's Neutral curve has a toe
  (`tonemap_frag.glsl:145`): a navy `#1A1B26` comes out about `#060A1E`. `Scene.render_skybox`
  already lets an environment light without being seen.
- **The window has its own 4x multisampling** (`engine.c:590`), which the canvas and UI draw into;
  the scene's sample count sizes only the scene buffers. A multisampled window takes no blit, so
  a view's picture is copied in by a draw. The screenshot reads the window's back buffer
  (`engine.c:2955`).
- **Per-camera state kept outside the camera's readers**, which the compiler will not find:
  - the clouds' march history and previous camera, and the drift advanced inside the march, so a
    second view would drift twice (`sky_clouds.c:350-406`);
  - every history keyed to `total_frames` parity and adjacency (`sky_clouds.c:350`,
    `postfx.c:1445, 2689, 3053, 3073, 3220`);
  - the froxel volume's previous camera in `PostFX`, which the origin shift reaches into
    (`engine.c:3740`);
  - LOD and the morph eye chosen once a frame and reused by the shadow pass (`render.c:942`,
    `engine.c:3653`, `shadow.c:1161`);
  - late draws reading the engine's matrices from inside the post chain (`render.c:2775-2796`,
    `fire_render.c:166-197`, `rain_render.c:139-165`);
  - the cached tiles' mover faces culled by the camera's frustum (`shadow.c:2416-2451`);
  - water's caustic window and ripples, and the rain's cover map, centred on the camera;
  - `ShadowSystem.cascade_count`, a setting a capture overwrites (`render.c:2156`) and the depth
    pass forces to 1 with no camera (`shadow.c:3237`).
- **A capture borrows the one camera by saving and restoring about 15 pieces of state**
  (`render.c:2182-2402`, `2105-2180`), and leaves `draw_projection`, ViewParams, the cluster UBOs and
  `camera_travel` as its last face drew them. It refuses to run without `engine->camera`
  (`probe.c:41`, `render.c:2186`).
- **Nothing states what animates forever.** `_engine_scene_draws` (`engine.c:950-956`) answers
  "is there anything to draw" (any item, IBL or sky), so it cannot be the test: a still board would
  never rest. The list is new.
- **The world's once-a-frame work reads the one current scene** (`engine.c:3870-4140`), and the
  graph epoch is process-wide (`draw_list.c:17-25`).
- **Terrain page prediction mixes last frame's view-projection with this frame's eye**
  (`layers_vt.c:459`), its comment giving a reason from before spec 11.96.
- **No shader depends on the sample count**; the resolve is a blit. Alpha-to-coverage and the
  shore's coverage read it on the CPU (`render.c:499`, `water.c:3141`).
- **TAA writes alpha 1** (`taa_resolve_frag.glsl:130`): the chain carries no coverage.
- **The baseline is not green**: 25 arms fail on master `8c33d44b` (AO, glare, local exposure,
  Purkinje, SSS, water, rain, origin, layers-VT, occlusion and prepass timing, coverage, quadtree).
  They are being fixed in a session of their own; 14.38 branches once that lands.

## Design

### The view (`scene_view.h`, public)

```c
typedef struct SceneViewSettings {   // plain values only; compared byte for byte each frame
    PostFXSettings post;             // the chain's knobs, PostFX's SETTINGS today
    ExposureSettings exposure;       // Exposure's SETTINGS today
    int samples;                     // scene target sample count; 4
    int ss_scale;                    // 1 or 2
    float render_scale;              // 0.5..1
    int accumulate_frames;           // 0 = TAA only; 16..64 = accumulate when still
    vec3 background;                 // lit: a working-space value; exact: the display colour
    bool background_exact;
    RenderMode render_mode;          // and the debug overlays' switches
    ...
} SceneViewSettings;

typedef struct SceneView {
    // SETTINGS
    Camera camera;                   // by value; the view shows the engine's current scene
    float x, y, width, height;       // window points, top-left, as the UI; ignored while fills_window
    bool fills_window;
    uint32_t mask;                   // draws nodes sharing a bit; all bits
    bool hidden;                     // kept, neither drawn nor composited
    SceneViewSettings settings;
    // BY FUNCTION: the rig (scene_view_set_rig), the LUT (scene_view_load_lut /
    // scene_view_clear_lut), app passes (scene_view_add_pass)
    // ENGINE-OWNED: the derived matrices, finished, frames accumulated, the state
    struct SceneViewState* state;
} SceneView;
```

- **`create_scene_view(const SceneViewDesc*)`** takes a `CameraDesc`, the rectangle and the
  settings, filled first by `engine_init_view_settings`; `free_scene_view` frees it.
  `engine_add_view` takes ownership and adds it at the end of the list; `engine_remove_view` hands
  it back. `engine_set_focus_view` names the focus; the first added is the default, and removing it
  passes focus to the new first.
- **Compared settings** are a `memcpy` copy against `memcmp`: padding can only make a false
  restart, never a missed one, and the struct is zeroed at creation.

### The view's state (`SceneViewState`, engine-owned)

Moved here, each from where it lives today:
- **From `Engine`:** the scene MRT, every G-buffer attachment and depth, the OIT and moment targets,
  the opaque-colour and depth resolves and their sizes; the view, projection, view-projection and
  jittered projection; last frame's view-projection, eye and validity; the camera's travel; the
  sample count actually allocated.
- **From `PostFX`:** every size-dependent target and history (`PostFXState`), the froxel volume and
  its previous camera, the fog layer, `ssr_prev`, the TAAU canvas and jitter, `pre_exposure`, the
  DoF autofocus.
- **From `Exposure`:** the meter's readings and the adaptation.
- **From `Sky`:** the cloud march's targets, history and previous camera. The drift moves out of the
  march to the world step, once a frame.
- **New:** the view's last drawn frame, for every history's adjacency and parity; the accumulation
  buffer and count; the last TAA picture; the finished picture; the change record (the settings
  copy, the matrices, the rectangle, the mask, the scene's change count and the graph epoch seen).

### Shared and window parts

- **`PostFXPrograms`**: the ~40 post programs, the noise texture and the quad, made once per engine.
- **On the engine, for the one window:** `window_color` (a display colour, cleared exactly), the CRT
  and the window picture, the present pass, the overlays, the GUI, the list of views, the focus.
- **A view's picture**: RGBA8, dithered by its tone map, when nothing follows the tone map; RGBA16F,
  undithered, when the view has passes after its tone map or the window has a CRT, and then the
  window's copy dithers it with the present pass's maths. So the dither stays on the last write,
  and one view filling the window writes today's bytes.

### The frame

1. Events and the window's size; a `fills_window` view takes it.
2. The app's update hook.
3. World, once, on the current scene: the origin shift from the focus view's eye, the sky cycle, the
   cloud drift.
4. The latch, the pre-render hook, the walk. The walk computes each node's effective mask and ORs
   the masks of nodes whose global transform or pose changed into the scene's moved mask. Each
   view's rig onto its camera; each view's matrices derived.
5. World, once, on the current scene: the fires, the lighting update (GI, probes, kept tiles, ranked
   from the focus eye), the rain's cover map and water's windows (focus), async uploads, the material
   array, roads, VT residency, POM heights, the draw list.
6. **Per view**, in list order, each shown view that needs a draw: LOD for its eye, occlusion, its
   shadows, the clear to its background, the app's render hook `(engine, scene, view)` or
   `engine_render_scene(engine, scene, view)`, the VT votes (focus only), the post chain into its
   picture, accumulation at the seam.
7. **The window:** clear to `window_color`, copy each shown view's picture into its rectangle, the
   overlays, the CRT or present, the GUI.
8. A frame is asked for while any shown view is unfinished; then the screenshot, the swap, the wait.

**Zero views** run steps 1-5 (uploads, captures and world work go on) and 7. **A capture** is an
internal view per face: its camera at the probe, a 90 degree frustum, no post chain, no TAA, no
debug overlays, refraction off, shadows from the outermost scene-fit map alone. It draws through the
same per-view path into the face's target, and the save-and-restore block goes.

### Still views

- **A view needs a draw** when it is shown and any of: its change record differs (camera matrices,
  rectangle, mask, settings), the graph epoch moved, the scene's change count moved
  (`scene_mark_changed`, `engine_mark_changed`, `engine_set_typical_size`), the moved mask shares a
  bit with its mask, the scene animates forever (sky cycle, cloud drift, water, rain, fire,
  particles, wind, an animator playing, a program or hook reading time), the engine's own work is
  unfinished (GI or probe captures, kept tiles opening, a mover's hold, exposure adapting, uploads
  out), or it is not finished.
- **A view with accumulation off is never finished**, so it draws every frame as today.
- **With accumulation on**, the first still frame restarts the count. Each frame takes the next
  jitter of a 64-long Halton (2,3) sequence, jitter on even where TAA is off (headless too), and TAA's
  resolve runs as an average: weight 1/(n+1), no reprojection, no clamp. The chain after the seam
  runs each frame; the composite shows the last TAA picture until n reaches 4. At `accumulate_frames`
  the view is finished: no draw, its picture copied each frame. A pass's own history that has not
  converged by then is what the average holds.
- **Idle mode** waits when every shown view is finished, or none is shown. Headless, a run ends at
  the frame after which that holds, so `pcb render` gets the finished picture.
- **Hidden then shown**: finished and its record unchanged reuses the picture; else a cut (histories
  reset, the count from 0).

### The exact background

Each frame for an exact view, on the CPU: the display colour through the 2.2 decode
(`displayDecode`'s maths), then the inverse of the view's tone curve, to the value it clears to.
Exposure never reaches a cleared texel, so it takes no part.
- **Linear**: the 2.2 decode alone. **Passthrough**: the colour itself, since a passthrough frame is
  blitted and never tone-mapped (`tonemap_frag.glsl:58-59`). **Neutral**: closed form (the toe's
  `m = sqrt(out/6.25)`, then the offset added back). **ACES**: the quadratic. **AgX**: the outset
  matrix inverted, the sigmoid solved by bisection, the log encode and the inset inverted.
- **A colour the curve cannot produce** is clamped, logged once.
- **Logged once** when an exact view has a stage that changes colour by position or is not
  invertible on: vignette, grade, LUT, local exposure, grain, Purkinje, fog or aerial over the
  background. Bloom near bright things glows onto it, as a camera would; the dither moves a pixel at
  most one code, and a view can turn it off.

### The mask

`SceneNode.view_mask` (`uint32_t`, all bits). The walk ANDs it down the tree into each draw item.
`draw_item_visible` takes the view's mask, so the scene passes and the view's shadows honour it.
Kept lamp shadows draw every node; the engine logs once when a view's mask hides a node inside a
kept lamp's reach.

### Apps and the game layer

Every in-tree app makes its view where it calls `engine_set_camera` today, and its settings writes go
to the view. `EngineConfig.msaa_samples`, `taa`, `ss_scale` and `render_scale` move to
`SceneViewDesc`. `game_listen` takes the focus view's camera; the rig is applied per view by the
engine. The 2D apps (canvas, sprites, shapes, network) take `scene_view_set_2d_preset` and
`scene_set_2d_preset`. In phase 4 the canvas app's 2D-only frames become zero views.

## Phases

**Every phase** records its "As built" in the spec, keeps `docs/eda-roadmap.md` true where it
decided or changed anything, falsifies at least one rule with the work committed first, runs what it
can reach by name, announces its boundary, and commits signed. Phases 1-3 end with the goldens at
0 px against phase 0's baseline and the full suite at the baseline, the full suite with your
go-ahead each time. Every phase that changes what is drawn runs the apps in a window for you.

0. **Baseline, branch and spec.** Once the baseline's fixes land on master: the goldens and the full
   suite recorded as the baseline. Branch `14.38-scene-views` off master; this plan copied to
   `specs/14.38-scene-views.md` and committed alone; the roadmap's items 3 and 4 marked started; the
   task list.
1. **Shared programs and the window's parts.** `PostFXPrograms` made once per engine; the CRT, the
   window picture, the present pass and the choice of the last write move to the engine's window
   part. Byte-identical.
2. **Settings and state.** `PostFXSettings` and `PostFXState`; `ExposureSettings` and the meter's
   readings; the skin profiles to the scene; `postfx_set_ssr_full_res`, `postfx_set_fog_ambient`
   and the froxel grid's rebuild driven by the settings compare; the session file's rows and the GUI
   re-pointed. Still one of each, on the engine. Byte-identical.
3. **The view, one per app.** `SceneView` and its state; every camera reader re-pointed by kind;
   the scene targets, matrices, travel and jitter into the state; the per-camera state found above
   moved (clouds' history and drift, the froxel camera, late draws handed the view, LOD per view,
   history adjacency per view); captures as views; `engine->camera`, `engine_set_camera`,
   `engine->postfx`, `engine->exposure`, the sample and scale setters and `engine_set_2d_preset`
   removed; every app and the game layer make their view; the render hook takes the view; debug
   modes per view; the GUI and the session file on the focus view; the picking rays as view
   functions. Byte-identical, except two commits measured on their own: capture-overlay (light
   lines kept out of captures), and terrain pages predicted from last frame's eye with last frame's
   view (the forest arms, measured).
4. **Several views.** Rectangles and `fills_window`; `window_color`; the copy into the window; zero
   views; hidden views; the mask; samples and scales per view; the focus view and its logs; the kept
   lamps' log; order; headless at a view's size. `apps/render`: `--views`, `--view-mask`,
   `--view-hide-at`, `--view-samples`; `apps/canvas`: `--view3d`, a 3D pane among its 2D panes, its
   2D-only frames now zero views. The views-* arms. **Look:** `apps/render --views 2x2` and the
   canvas app's 3D pane in a window.
5. **Still views.** Accumulation, the change record, `scene_mark_changed`, `engine_mark_changed`, the
   moved mask, what animates forever and what is unfinished, idle resting on finished views, the
   headless end, the finished picture copied on UI-only frames. `apps/render`: `--accumulate`,

### Phase 10: a panel's shadow

**What is wrong.** The board's key light is a 20 x 15 cm panel about 32 cm from the board. Its
per-frame shadow is a cube of five 90-degree faces from the panel's centre (spec 13.27), and the
punctual budget (96 MB) holds the panel's five layers and the lamp's six only at 1024 texels a
face. A face is 64 cm across at the board, so a texel is about 0.63 mm, and the 3x3 filter of
`punctualShadow` shows every one: a staircase under each package and along the board's edge on the
table, several pixels a step in a close view, with filter noise along it.

**What was tried, and why it was not enough.**
- **Spec 10.4** (the same staircase, on `cornell_box`): fitting the panel's map to its casters was
  rejected, since a room's walls reach the panel's own plane and the cone they demand is 180
  degrees; the VRAM budget for the map's edge and the receiver-plane bias shipped; a penumbra from
  the panel's size was built and held back, its band 0.07 too far out.
- **`c15c1323`, on the branch `area-penumbra-centre`** (August 2026), finished that penumbra --
  a blocker search, linear depths, similar triangles, 32 Vogel taps rotated per pixel and per
  frame -- and passed the gate's centre at 0.7587 against 0.7500 +/- 0.01. It was never merged:
  it still looked bad. Spec 13.27 then made the panel's map a cube.
- **Why it still looked bad.** A package 1.5 mm tall under that panel casts a penumbra about
  0.2 x 0.0015 / 0.32 = 0.9 mm wide: about one and a half texels. A correct penumbra over a
  0.63 mm grid is barely wider than the 3x3 already was, so the staircase stays. The shape was
  right; the density was the problem, and the penumbra cannot fix density.

**The fix: density first, then the penumbra.**
1. **Fit a panel's per-frame shadow to the view drawing it**, as the cascades are fitted. Not the
   fit 10.4 refused: that one bounded the casters for every receiver. This one bounds the
   receivers this view can see -- its frustum's corners, cut at the panel's range and the camera's
   far plane, in front of the panel -- with a cone from the panel's centre, widened by the panel's
   own angular size so the blocker search never reads off the map. Every caster that can shadow a
   receiver in the cone lies between the receiver and the panel's centre, so it is inside the cone
   too. The cone is the panel's first layer, with its own matrix; the panel keeps its five layers
   allocated, so the array's layout and a capture's cube are unchanged. A cone wider than about
   100 degrees -- a view inside the panel's room -- falls back to the cube, as does a capture,
   which has no view. In the board's three-quarter view the cone is about 30 cm across at the board:
   about 0.3 mm a texel; in a close-up, about 0.1 mm.
2. **The penumbra by the panel's size, on that layer**, ported from `c15c1323`: the blocker search
   over the panel's footprint in the map, axial distances from the layer's own near and far, a
   filter radius by similar triangles, 32 Vogel taps rotated per pixel and per frame where TAA or a
   still view's average turns them, and the old hard 3x3 for a point or spot, byte for byte. In a
   close-up the package's penumbra is then about nine texels: a ramp, not steps.
3. **Arms:** the `penumbra` fixture's width held to the model as well as its centre (the 3x3 gives
   about 0.01 against a predicted 0.21); a staircase arm on the board close-up, the shadow edge's
   crossing measured along the package's side, whose wobble the texel grid sets; a fitted cone's
   fallback to the cube inside a room, the `cornell_*` panels unchanged where they must be. The
   goldens with a shadowing panel (`board_fixture`, `cornell_box`) re-baked, each looked at first.
4. **Look**: the board's three views and a close-up, in a window, before the merge.

  level every frame: correct, but an O(items) pass per view, about 0.1-0.5 ms per view at 10k+

  choice free again. Left because every pass reads the list, and a board has few LOD meshes.

  punctual layers; drawing them once per distinct mask a frame saves up to six traversals per
  caster per extra view, about 1-3 ms in a silent-class town. Left because casters draw at the
  drawing view's levels, so a second view would take the first's and `views-alone`'s 0 px would
  no longer hold; a board has one or two lamps.
- **The picture's format decided per view** (phase 9). One view with a pass after its tone map,
  or the CRT, makes every view's picture RGBA16F and the window one dither, the focus view's; and
  `record.through` restarts every still view when one pane adds or removes such a pass. Per view,
  a view with such passes would run them on a 16F scratch and end with its own dithered 8-bit
  write, and only the CRT would draw the window through a picture. Left because it changes which
  dither a view gets and where overlays land, so it moves pixels, and it reworks the composite the
  direct-write fix changes; pcb uses no such passes.

### Phase 5: still views

- **`SceneViewSettings.accumulate_frames`**: 0, the default, draws every frame under TAA as before;
  16..64 (clamped) averages that many jittered frames once nothing the view shows has changed for a
  frame, then rests. `SceneView.frames_accumulated` and `finished` are engine-owned.
- **The decision, once a frame per view** (`_engine_decide_view`, after the rigs and the matrices,
  before any view draws): a TAA frame when anything changed, the next frame of its average when
  nothing did, nothing once the average is complete. A change is any of: the record differing --
  the settings byte for byte, the view and projection matrices, the rectangle in pixels, the mask,
  the scene's and the engine's change counts summed, the graph epoch, whether the window is drawn
  through a picture -- the scene's moved mask sharing a bit with the view's, the scene animating
  of its own accord, the engine's own work out, or the view's meter adapting.
- **Change counts**: `scene_mark_changed` (`Scene.change_count`) for a plain write the engine
  cannot see -- a material, a light, a scene setting; `engine_mark_changed` (`Engine.change_count`)
  for an engine-wide setting; `engine_set_typical_size` says it itself. The **moved mask**
  (`Scene.moved_mask`): the transform walk ORs in the effective mask of each node whose global
  transform changed since the latch, every bit for one carrying a light, a camera or particles;
  the latch clears it.
- **What animates forever** (`_scene_animates`): water, rain, fire, particles, wind, the sky's
  clock, the clouds' drift (wind above 0), a pose, a shader hook marked animated. A pose counts
  whether or not an animator runs it, since the engine cannot see an animator: a skinned scene
  never rests. An app pass reading `time` is not seen; an app with one says so with
  `scene_mark_changed` each frame.
- **The engine's work out** (`_engine_world_busy`): textures loading, GI volumes or reflection
  probes owed a capture (`gi_world_pending`, the new `probe_set_pending`), a kept lamp's faces not
  all drawn or a mover's held.
- **The average** sits in TAA's place (`taa_resolve_frag`'s `accumulate`): the history is the mean
  so far, and the frame joins it at weight 1/(n+1), no reprojection, no clamp, whether TAA is on
  or not. Its jitter is the n-th of a 64-long Halton (2,3), headless too; PCSS's rotation and the
  alpha jitter ride the same flag and are averaged with it. Every other temporal pass runs as it
  does under the view's TAA setting.
- **The view's own frame count** (`SceneViewState.draw_index`), started at the engine's when the
  view is made and counting its draws: the chain's `frame_index` (grain, history parity), TAA's
  jitter phase and PCSS's seed. For a view drawn every frame it is the engine's, so nothing moved.
- **Shown while young**: the first averaged frame keeps the view's last TAA picture
  (`SceneViewState.held`), which the window shows until the average holds 4 frames.
- **Resting**: a finished view is not drawn and asks for no frame; its picture is copied each
  frame. A view that may rest never takes the direct path, keeping its picture. Under idle a

- **Hidden then shown**: unchanged, the kept picture is reused with no draw; changed, a cut

- **Below render scale 1** an asked average is said once and the view keeps TAA's upscale.
- **apps/render**: `--accumulate N`, `--change-at F:KIND[:ARG]` (camera:V, material,

### Phase 6: the exact background and HDR from memory

- **`SceneViewSettings.background_exact`**: `background` is then the display colour wanted on
  screen. Each frame the view draws, the engine turns it back through the view's tone curve and the
  display encode on the CPU (`background.c`, `background_working`) to the working value it clears
  to (`SceneViewState.exact_background`, which the G-buffer table clears attachment 0 to). A lit
  background clears to `background` as before.
- **The inverses**, each landing near the answer by the curve's own structure and then polished by
  Newton steps on a C mirror of the curve, in single precision as tonemap_frag runs it:
  passthrough the colour itself (a passthrough frame is copied, never tone-mapped, as is a debug
  view's); linear the 2.2 decode; ACES the quadratic's positive root per channel; Neutral, below
  its knee, the toe's `x = sqrt(m / 6.25)` (or `m + 0.04` above it) and the offset added back,
  Newton above the knee; AgX the 2.2 power, the outset matrix, the sigmoid by bisection, the log
  encode and the inset matrix, each undone.
- **Said once**: a colour the curve cannot give (the nearest shown), and an exact background with
  a stage over it the engine cannot undo -- vignette, grade, LUT, local exposure, grain, Purkinje,
  sharpen, fog or aerial perspective. Bloom near something bright glows onto it, as a camera's
  would, and is not said: measured, AgX's near-white working value sits above the bloom threshold,
  and a flat field of it blooms one code brighter. The dither moves it a code at most.
- **`load_hdr_environment_memory(ibl, bytes, size)`**: an HDR from bytes, decoded by
  `stbi_loadf_from_memory`, sharing the path loader's upload (`_ibl_adopt_hdr`). Its cook key is
  its content, `memory:<fnv1a>:<size>`, where a file's is its path.

#### Bugs

1. **A use-after-free every frame** (broad, CONFIRMED). `_scene_animates` walks last frame's draw
   list before this frame's build, dereferencing each item's mesh and material; a hook freeing a
   mesh, node or material leaves it reading freed memory, accumulation on or off.
2. **Still views that never rest.**
   - `_engine_world_busy` reads the kept tiles' bitsets and stays true forever in three ways
     (still views, CONFIRMED): a block with more cells than its light's faces (a block is reused
     or grown, never shrunk); a kept lamp at intensity 0, given a block but never drawn; an item
     not `DRAW_KEPT_STILL` that does not move -- a morph mesh at fixed weights, a
     `capture_hidden` node, a hooked caster -- which keeps its block's `dynamic` set.
   - The meter's fixed 0.01-stop tolerance against a target metered from jittered frames may
     restart the average for good on a scene of small bright features (still views, per-camera;
     PLAUSIBLE).
3. **Changes a resting view misses** (CONFIRMED unless marked).
   - The debug GUI and `config_snapshot_apply_file` call neither mark: light, IBL, probe, shadow,
     sky and decal rows, the material window and the engine's toggles change nothing a view's
     record compares (broad, still views, per-camera).
   - A hidden view returns before its record is compared, and the moved mask and the busy test
     live one frame: a node moved, a texture landed or a capture taken while a view is hidden is
     never seen when it is shown again, nor across `engine_remove_view` and a second
     `engine_add_view` (broad, several views, still views, per-camera).
   - A switch of the current scene is not a change when the two scenes' counts match, as two
     fresh ones' do (broad, still views).
   - `scene_view_load_lut`, `scene_view_clear_lut`, `scene_view_add_pass`, a pass's `enabled`,
     `scene_add_light`, `scene_remove_light`, `node_set_light` and `node_set_camera` restart
     nothing; nor a write to a node's `view_mask`, which the docs do not list among the writes to
     say (broad, several views, still views).
   - A node moved in a render hook, after the views are decided, is cleared by the next latch and
     never seen; `scene_mark_changed` in a render hook on the frame a view finishes asks for no
     frame, so idle waits with it undrawn (still views).
   - The sky's sliced re-bake (`slicer.item >= 0`, `cycle_dirty`) is not work out: a view rests
     before the swap and, idle, stops the slicer (broad). Nor are VT pages still baking, the
     composite cache a frame behind, or POM heights resolving after the drain (broad, per-camera;
     PLAUSIBLE).
   - Neither mark wakes a waiting loop, and `change_count` is a plain integer read on the main
     thread: a worker thread's mark is a data race and is drawn only when something else asks
     (still views).
   - The graph epoch is process-wide, so a mesh uploaded in any other scene restarts every still
     view (still views; low).
4. **The mask leaks** (CONFIRMED).
   - `render_occlusion_pass` rasterises every occluder whatever its mask, so an occluder a view
     hides still culls what is behind it there (broad, several views).
   - `render_late_items` draws the late lane with no mask test (several views).
   - `_engine_pick` picks from the whole graph, so a click drags a node the view hides (broad).
   - A view mask of 0 is "every item" to `draw_item_visible` and nothing to `_view_changed`'s
     moved test, so such a view never restarts on a move (broad).
5. **Several views.**
   - The composite copies a picture at the rectangle as it stands at present time but at the size
     it was drawn, so a rectangle changed after its view drew (a render hook, an overlay) overflows
     its neighbours or leaves a strip for a frame. The canvas app lays out its 3D pane in an
     overlay, so on every resize the pane is a frame late (altitude, several views; CONFIRMED).
   - A hidden focus view starves the terrain pages: `vt_pages_update` predicts from the focus's
     `view_proj`, written only in a scene pass and identity until then, and the feedback votes come
     only from the focus's draw. The render app's `--views` keeps its hidden driver view as the
     focus (several views, per-camera; CONFIRMED).
   - The render app's `--views` grid copies the driver's settings but not its LUT or its scene
     file's post passes, which stay on the hidden driver (broad, per-camera; CONFIRMED).
   - The rain's cover is drawn once per view, identically, each time rebuilding the focus's LOD

#### Declined, and why

- **Water's world passes stay at the first draw of the surface**, with their frame stamp: a probe
  capture can draw water before any view does, so "the first draw this frame, whatever draws it"
  is the honest condition, and the stamp is its cheapest form.
- **The chain's per-run inputs stay fields** set by `_engine_run_view_chain` each frame: gathering
  them into one argument moves the same assignments without deleting any state. With the direct
  write gone, `window_through_picture` is the chain's one output flag, and no impossible state is
  left.
- **The still view's phase stays three fields** (`draws_this_frame`, `average_n`, `away` and
  `missed`), with one restart and one cut, rather than an enum: each is read where it means
  something, and the proxies the review found are gone.
- **The draw list keeps its own inherited mask**: a node added after the walk is drawn by the build
  before any walk reaches it, which `walked_mask` would not know.
- **"Said once" stays a flag per message**, the engine's idiom (`lightless_warned`), as the
  altitude review advised.
- **Kept tiles and movers stay per view**: movers are culled by the drawing view's frustum, so the
  faces drawn differ by view.
- **A shared points-to-pixels rectangle with the canvas, the tone curves' constants in a shared
  header, the grid-cell helper and the arms' shared runs** are left: the first would move the
  canvas's pixels for a seam no arm has seen at the integral scales the suite runs, the second
  touches every program including `view.glsl`, and the last two are local tidiness.
- **A dedicated arm for an occluder masked out** needs a fixture whose occluders are nodes; the
  corpus's occluders are authored boxes, which no mask reaches.
- **The graph epoch stays process-wide**: a mesh uploaded in another scene restarts every still
  view, which costs a redraw, never a stale picture.
- **The kept-lamp check still scans each frame until it is said**: a masked view hiding nothing
  near a kept lamp pays a walk of its hidden items against the blocks every frame. Keying it on a
  change misses the blocks that stream in after the mask is set, which `views-kept-log` relies
  on. Left for later.
- **The exact background's inverse is taken every frame**: 32 Newton steps of a 3x3 system, below
  any frame's noise.
- **A hidden view never shown still builds its targets at creation**, which `create_scene_view`
  does so a grade or a pass can be added before the first frame; the render app shrinks its
  hidden driver to a point instead.
