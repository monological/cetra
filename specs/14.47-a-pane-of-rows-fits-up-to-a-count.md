# cetra 14.47: a pane of rows fits up to a count

## Context

pcb 10.3.1 made its go list (the finder, the palette and every pick list) a scrolling pane of
virtual rows (`ui_scroll_set_rows`), so it scrolls past the twelve rows it shows. To show twelve,
pcb sets the pane's height itself from a row's height it counts by hand: the font's line, the
label padding cetra's theme leaves at its own default and so cannot be read, and pcb's own row
padding. That went wrong once (eight rows, not twelve), and a palette row whose summary wraps is
taller than the count says. A pane of rows sized FIT is as tall as every row, which a list of
hundreds cannot be.

pcb's AGENTS.md: where cetra's UI cannot do something, ask. Asked; the user chose this over pcb
keeping its count or the list becoming a table: "Cetra change first".

Branch and spec: `14.47-a-pane-of-rows-fits-up-to-a-count`, off cetra's master (`04949129`).

## The design

```c
// The most rows a pane of rows sized FIT is as tall as: its first `most`, as built and measured or
// at the estimate until then, and the gaps between them; past them it scrolls. 0, as made, is
// every row.
void ui_scroll_set_rows_most(UIElement* pane, int most);
```

- **Kept with the rows** (`struct UIRows`), so `ui_scroll_set_rows` starting the rows over keeps
  it; refused by name on anything not a pane of rows, and for a count below 0.
- **The FIT height** (`ui.c`, where a pane of rows measures its height) is the top of row `most`
  with its gap taken off, where it has more rows than that; all of them otherwise, as now. The
  rows in view are built and measured at the first layout, as now, so the height is their own at
  once.
- A FIXED, SHARE or GROW pane ignores it; `max_size` still caps a FIT one.
- Nothing drawn changes for a pane that sets no count.

## Phases

0. **The spec**, this file, committed alone.
1. **The count and the arm**: `ui_scroll_set_rows_most`; `docs/ui.md`'s virtual rows; the arm
   `ui-scroll-rows-most` reading gametest's `--ui-probe scroll-rows-most` -- a FIT pane of 30
   one-line rows given 12 is as tall as twelve rows and their eleven gaps; given 12 with 5 rows,
   as five; a two-line row among the first twelve makes it taller by a line; 0 gives all 30
   (the probe measures, it does not draw); row 20 revealed lies inside the pane. Falsified by the
   count left out of the height. The roadmap's line.

## As built

**Phase 1, the count and the arm.**
- `struct UIRows` holds `most`, 0 as `calloc` leaves it; `ui_scroll_set_rows` reuses the struct and
  does not touch it. `ui_scroll_set_rows_most` (`ui.h`, after `ui_scroll_set_row_count`) refuses a
  pane with no rows and a count under 0 by name.
- `_measure` takes `top[most] - gap` where `0 < most < count`, `top[count]` otherwise. The height is
  summed on the same tops the next settle finds the view on, so the view at the top ends at row
  `most - 1` and the settle builds rows 0 to `most`, whatever the estimate; the layout after
  measures them, inside the first `ui_layout`.
- `--ui-probe scroll-rows-most`: a FIT pane 300 wide, no padding, 8-point gaps, of 30 label rows at
  an estimate of 50, given 12, measured after one `ui_layout`. Rows measure 30 (a 22-point line and
  the label's padding): 448 given 12; -1 refused, still 448; row 7 built again with a second line,
  470; row 20 revealed at 405 to 435 in the pane's 125 to 595; started over with 5 rows, the count
  kept, 182; given 0, 1132. `ui-scroll-rows-most` wants each from the measured row and line.
- It, `ui-rows-built`, `ui-rows-measure`, `ui-rows-anchor`, `ui-rows-focus`, `ui-max-size`,
  `ui-field-append` and `gate-arm-docs` pass.
- Falsified: the count left out of the height; `ui-scroll-rows-most` failed on 1132 given 12. Put
  back.
- `docs/ui.md`'s virtual rows and the roadmap's line and date say it.
