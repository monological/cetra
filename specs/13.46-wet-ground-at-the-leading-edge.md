# Spec 13.46: wet ground at the leading edge of a turn

## Context

Turning in silent at night shows a bright vertical band at the leading edge of the screen, gone when
the camera stops. Spec 13.45 fixed the fog's share of that band. While measuring it, the larger
share turned out to be wet ground's reflection.

**Measured in silent, before this spec.** Headless on the street (`--player-at -0.8,4,200`), a
full-stick turn, compared with the same turn made 100 frames earlier and held:
- the band reads 0.62 codes in the last 5 columns at 1, 2 and 4 fog samples alike;
- it sits on the ground: +3.4 codes at rows 720-779 and about +2 across rows 900-1079 of 1080,
  with the air above at the floor.

**The cause, in `cetra/shaders/spec_occ_composite_frag.glsl` `wetReflection` (spec 13.21).** Wet
ground's environment reflection share is REPLACED by the SSR trace, before TAA, using the PREVIOUS
frame's trace (`PostFX.ssr_prev`), read at the pixel less its velocity. Where that previous
position is off-screen, the function returns the environment's share unreplaced: "what --no-ssr
would show for that pixel". On a turn that is the newly revealed strip at the leading edge, for
exactly one frame. On a wet night street the environment's share is much brighter than the dark
street the SSR sees, so the strip flashes.

13.21 stated this behaviour without weighing an alternative (`specs/13.21-wet-reflections-before-
taa.md:44`), so this changes a default rather than reversing a refusal.

**The fix:** clamp the previous position onto the screen, borrowing the nearest of the frame
before's reflections, instead of falling back to the bare environment share.
- **Evidence:** as a throwaway experiment in 13.45, since reverted, it took silent's band from 0.62
  codes to 0.08 against a floor of 0.05, and the last 30 columns from 0.19 to 0.05.
- **Cost:** nothing; it is one `clamp` in place of a branch.
- **Wrap mode:** the trace textures are already `GL_CLAMP_TO_EDGE` (`util.c:171`,
  `postfx.c:167`), so the clamp states explicitly what the sampler would do.

**Intended outcome:**
- the wet strip at the leading edge of a turn is gone or no longer visible, in the render app and
  in silent;
- every still frame is unchanged to the pixel, since a still pixel's previous position is always
  on screen;
- the user looks at silent with 13.45 and 13.46 together.

- **Class mixing at the edge.** The borrowed pair can be a catcher's or an unmarked pixel's.

  coverage, so it degrades to the environment share.

## As built

### Phase 1: the instrument, and the arm red

**`rain_fixture` cannot show silent's strip, so the arm measures the mechanism instead** (the
user's choice). Four framings were tried, each against the clamp as a scratch experiment:

| Framing | What the wet replacement changes at the leading edge |
|---|---|
| the `fog-miss` turn, no sky | ±0.06 codes: the environment there is as dark as the scene |
| a turn onto the open ground, with a sky | the clamp moves 2,365 px by at most 3 codes |
| the `fog-miss` turn onto the sealed ground, with a sky | the clamp moves 1,607 px by at most 2 |
| a bright ambient in place of a sky | SSR against none differs by at most 0.13 codes |

Silent's strip is +2 to +3.4 codes over whole rows of street. It takes a bright environment share
(silent's reflections are baked through the fog, `reflect_fog`) against a dark street, under
smooth film with high SSR coverage. The yard has none of that: its replacement is worth 1 to 3
codes wherever it is looked at.

**The arm, `rain-ssr-edge`, is exact.** Wet ground that falls back leaves its pixel exactly what
`--no-ssr` shows, and one that takes a reflection does not, so the arm needs no contrast:
- four runs of the shared turn under the sky: the turn and its held twin, each with SSR and
  without;
- the **wet pixels** are those in the leading columns that SSR changes when held;
- the arm counts the share of them the turn leaves at exactly their `--no-ssr` value.

Nothing that reads the whole frame may run (the meter, bloom), or SSR's effect elsewhere would
reach the edge and no pixel could equal its twin.

**Red today:** 1,221 wet pixels in the 38 leading columns, and 1,215 of them, 99.5%, at exactly
their `--no-ssr` value. The pixel minimum is 500; the bare share's maximum is provisional at 25%
until the fix is measured.

**The shared turn.**
- 13.45's capture is now `TURN_*` with `_turn_pose`, `_turn_yaws`, `_turn_missed` and
  `_turn_run`, used by both groups.
- `_turn_missed(w, h)` is the closed form at any grid: the fog volume's for `fog-miss`, the
  frame's pixels for `rain-ssr-edge`.
- What only the fog needs, the trilinear spread and the edge rows it implies, stays in
  `run_fog_miss_gate`.
- `fog-miss` reads the same figures to the third decimal.

**Silent before the fix**, from 13.45: 0.62 codes over the last 5 fog columns at 1, 2 and 4 fog
samples alike, +3.4 codes at rows 720-779 and about +2 across rows 900-1079.

### Phase 2: the fix

**Why the yard showed so little, and the probe that makes it show.** `ssr_frag` fades a hit by
how near the HIT is to the screen's edge, over the last tenth (`edgeFade`). Wet ground reflects
within its own column, so at the frame's edge its hits fade, and whatever fills the rest of the
weight is what wet ground shows there: the reflection probe (`probeSample`, at full weight).
- With no probe, `probeSample` is exactly 0, the pair at the edge is nearly empty, and wet ground
  replaces almost nothing there, fallback or not. That was Phase 1's yard, and the clamp alone
  moved it from 99.5% to 76.8%, mostly pixels whose borrowed pair was empty.
- Silent has probes, so its edge replaces the bright environment share with the probe's darker
  reflection, and that is the strip a fallback skips.
- `rain-ssr-edge` therefore runs with `--probe`.

With the probe, on today's code:
- 4,130 wet pixels in the 38 leading columns;
- **98.0%** of them exactly at their `--no-ssr` value;
- on average 2.65 codes from the held frame, at most 20.4.

**The fix, `wetReflection` in `spec_occ_composite_frag.glsl`.** The previous position is clamped
onto the frame, so wet ground at the leading edge takes the nearest of the frame before's
reflections.
- On the arm: 1.8% left at the `--no-ssr` value, 0.37 codes from the held frame on average, at
  most 7.8.
- In silent's turn: 0.08 codes against a floor of 0.05, from 0.62. Rows 720-779 went from +3.4
  to +0.2. Rows 900-1019 keep +1.3 and -0.6 where the borrowed reflection is not quite the true
  one, against +1.9 and +2.0 before.

**The cut, and the reach that bounds the borrow.** On a 90° cut (the yard with the probe, the cut
frame against the same pose held, ground rows only), the bare clamp lent one edge column to the
whole wet ground:
- clamp alone: mean 1.24 codes from the held frame, 99th percentile 12.2;
- today's environment share: 0.46 and 2.1.

So the borrow fades out between `WET_EDGE_REACH` (0.1 of the frame off its edge) and twice that.
- **The gate's turn (3% a frame) and silent's are untouched by the bound:** 0 px in silent
  against the bare clamp.
- **The cut is back to today's:** 0.44 codes and 2.1, 777 px from today's frame.
- **Still frames and the inside of a turn cannot move:** an on-frame position gives a weight of
  exactly 1 and a clamp that is the identity.

**Thresholds:** `RAIN_EDGE_MIN_PX` 2000 (measured 4130), `RAIN_EDGE_BARE_MAX` 10% (98.0% before,
1.8% after).

**Measured:**
- `rainfall`, `fog-miss`, `water` and `gate-docs` pass: 5 groups, 97 arms.
- `rain-ssr`, `rain-ssr-taa` and `rain-ssr-taau` read exactly what they did before. Every term
  of the composite is still non-negative.
- All 32 goldens are 0 px.

### Review

A thermo-nuclear review of the branch. It kept the fade and its reach (the cut measurement
justifies them), the explicit clamp, and the four renders the arm takes. What it changed:
- **One shared per-column fact.** `_turn_columns(n)` gives each column None where it missed, else
  its growth. The column-rows, the edge rows and the reach are `fog-miss`'s own and moved into its
  gate; they were split across the helper's boundary. `rain-ssr-edge` lost its dummy grid height
  of 1, its rebuilt mask and an unused local. So Phase 1's "`_turn_missed(w, h)`" is now
  `_turn_columns(w)`, and what only the fog needs is all in `run_fog_miss_gate`.
- **The poses are built where they are drawn.** `_turn_runs(workdir, prefix, args, variants)`
  takes `(tag, extra, held)` rows and returns the runs or the first failure's tail. `_turn_yaws`,
  both variant comprehensions, both failure scans and a doubled file-name prefix are gone.
- **The shader names the frame's nearest point once** and measures how far off the frame the
  previous position was from it, where it had stated the edge twice. On the frame the distance is
  exactly 0 in either form.
- **`TURN_FIXTURE = RAIN_FIXTURE`.** The turn's distance and yaw are that file's, and the rain
  gate's existence check now covers it by name rather than by two equal strings. The turn's
  constants and helpers are one block.
- **The arm is `_rain_ssr_edge(workdir) -> (ok, detail)`,** printed by `run_rain_gate`, which is
  where `gate-arm-docs` reads its name. Its docstring entry names `RAIN_EDGE_BARE_MAX` rather than
  claiming every wet pixel takes a reflection.
- **The shader comment** states the bare environment share as the alternative and its failure,
  and drops a sentence restating the early return.

**What the plan said and the build does not:**
- the plan's "one `clamp` in place of a branch" became a clamp and a fade, for the cut;
- the plan's second half of the arm (the same turn with `--no-ssr` shows no strip) was not built.
  The exact test against the `--no-ssr` twin, with `RAIN_EDGE_MIN_PX` wet pixels, does that work;
- the plan's "Reused" listed `quad_moved` and `ground_quad`, which the arm does not use.

**Measured:**
- `rainfall`, `fog-miss` and `gate-docs` pass with every figure as before: `rain-ssr-edge` 4,130
  and 1.8%, `fog-miss` 0.132 codes, 34,048 cells, 0 px and 0 of 74;
- the 90° cut is 0 px against the build before the shader's restatement;
- all 32 goldens are 0 px.
