# Spec 13.27 -- area lights shadowed all round, and kept

## Context
An area light's shadow is ONE perspective map down the panel normal with a fixed 120-degree cone
(`AREA_SHADOW_FOV`, `shadow.c:1236`; spec 9.8, `a28cbfe6`). `punctualShadow`
(`include/punctual_shadow.glsl:64-79`) reads anything off that map as lit, by design ("the safe
direction to be wrong"). So every receiver more than 60 degrees off the normal is unshadowed, walls
included, and a panel lights the rooms around it. silent shows it since spec 13.25 put the home's
hall beside the kitchen: `tube_ceiling` (2.30, 2.83, 12.0) lights the hall through the shared wall,
and the frustum's side plane is the diagonal across the hall's front wall and door (predicted
y 0.66/1.48 at its ends, measured 0.62/1.51; widening the cone to 170 degrees removed it, as a
throwaway test). master renders it the same to the pixel.

The user asked for both halves, as two parallel paths -- the split point lights already have:
1. **The fix: a per-frame cube for area lights**, so a panel's shadow covers every direction it
   emits into. Unreal shadows rect lights the same way, from the centre, as a point light.
2. **The perf: area lights may opt into the cached tiles** (spec 13.16, `shadow_cache`), so a panel
   that never moves -- silent's tubes -- is drawn once and kept.

Spec 10.4 already refused fitting the cone (a room's walls reach the panel's own plane, so the
conservative cone is 180 degrees). Soft shadows from a panel's extent are not this spec: both paths
shade from the panel's centre with the hard 3x3 PCF the single map has today.

**Workflow.** Branch `13.27-area-lights-shadowed-all-round` off master; copy this plan to
`specs/13.27-area-lights-shadowed-all-round.md` and commit it alone; task list; commit and update the
spec's as-built notes after each phase; announce phase boundaries.

- **The uniform budget, measured, not estimated.** Raising `MAX_PUNCTUAL_SHADOW_LAYERS` adds a mat4
  per layer to `pbr_frag` (`punctualShadowMatrix[]`, the only program that declares it). A hand count
  puts `pbr_frag` at about 935 of GL 4.1's guaranteed 1024 fragment uniform components today and
  about 1063 at 16. Measure with a throwaway probe after link: `GL_MAX_FRAGMENT_UNIFORM_COMPONENTS`,
  and the sum of the active uniforms' components (`glGetProgramiv(GL_ACTIVE_UNIFORMS)` +
  `glGetActiveUniform`, sizes times type widths) for the uber variant and `pbr-0`. Rule: the cap is
  the largest of 16, 14 or 12 whose count stays within 1024 on the uber variant; record the numbers
  in the spec. (A cube is 5 layers, so 12 holds a point and an area light with a spot spare.)

## Phase 1 -- the per-frame cube (the fix)
1. **A hemicube in the panel's own frame.** `light_emission_frame` (light.c:217) gives the axis `n`
   and the orthonormal up `u`; `r = cross(u, n)` is the width axis `ltc.glsl:138` uses. Five faces,
   +r -r +u -u +n -- the cube-face order with (r, u, n) as (X, Y, Z) -- and the -n face skipped: every
   direction whose dominant frame axis is -n is behind the panel's plane, where `ltcPanel` is already
   0 (`ltc.glsl:135`). In a world-axis cube that skip is wrong for a tilted panel (the face opposite
   the normal still covers front directions); in the panel's frame it is exact.
   - `punctual_layers_for` (shadow.c:1240) returns 5 for `LIGHT_AREA`.
   - `compute_punctual_matrices`' area case builds the five 90-degree faces from the light's centre
     over the system's near/far, as the point case does.
   - **The roll is a frame axis.** `compute_perspective_light_space` (1208) derives its up from
     world Y, which would turn a face's square frustum off the region the shader assigns it. It takes
     an explicit up (a sibling that the existing one calls with `light_space_up(dir)`); each panel face
     is rolled to another frame axis (+-r faces up `u`, +-u faces up `n`, +n face up `u`).
   - `AREA_SHADOW_FOV` and its comment go.
2. **The lookup.** In `pbr_frag`'s area branch (~2039-2044): the fragment's direction from the panel
   in the frame, `vec3(dot(d, r), dot(d, u), dot(d, n))` from `dirType.xyz` / `upArea.xyz` (already
   packed for every type), through `punctualCubeFace` (`include/cube_face.glsl`) to the face; face 5
   reads lit; `aLayer + face` into `punctualShadow`, unchanged otherwise (receiver-plane bias, PCF,
   face-edge clamp and grazing fade all already per layer). The frame convention is one GLSL helper
   beside `punctualCubeFace` in `cube_face.glsl`, `panelCubeFace(d, axis, up)`; the C face order in
   `compute_punctual_matrices` names it, as the point's names `punctualCubeFace`.
3. **The pool.** `MAX_PUNCTUAL_SHADOW_LAYERS` to the cap phase 0 measured, in `shadow.h` and
   `punctual_shadow.glsl:18` together; `SHADOW_TILE_MARK` (shadow_tile_constants.glsl:44) to match
   (the static assert holds it). The edge table (`punctual_size_for`) is unchanged: one cube takes 5
   layers at 2048, about 13% less density at a face's centre than the 120-degree map at 4096. The
   pool-full warning says what the loop does: a later light needing fewer layers may still fit.
4. **Not touched, checked:** contact shadows skip panels and lights with a map; the froxel fog does
   not scatter panels; water, rain and fire read only `shadowMisc.zw` (the size, unchanged); captures
   re-run the depth pass and pick the cube up, at 4 more traversals per panel per capture burst.
   The pool rule is unchanged (a light takes all its layers or none, first come), so until phase 2
   caches them, silent's two shadowed tubes and the flashlight ask 11 layers.
5. **Look -- the user's, before any gate work.** The hall from the leak framing (the diagonal gone,
   the wall dark where the kitchen wall stands), the kitchen under its tubes, before/after crops in
   Preview, and the windowed run command. Iterate on the picture only until it is signed off.
6. **Gate arms**, in the `penumbra` group, which owns area shadows (`run_penumbra_gate`,
   gates.py:1763):
   - `area-reach`: `cornell_leak` through `cscn_copy` with an authored area light (`lights[]`,
     `"type": "area"`, `cast_shadows`) facing down by the partition, the far room more than 60
     degrees off its normal. The far room dark against the near one (`tiles-leak`'s ratio), and the
     same panel unshadowed lights it. Falsified by restoring the single 120-degree map.
   - `area-tilt`: the panel tilted off every world axis, an occluder beside it: the receiver behind
     the occluder in shadow. Falsified by skipping the world-axis face opposite the dominant normal
     instead of the frame's -n face.
   - The existing arms re-run: `penumbra` (centre within 0.01; the edge seen from the centre is on
     the -Y face either way, texels 2.54 -> 2.93 mm), `emissive-match`/`-occluded`/`-gi`/`-spec`
     (both sides through the same shadow code; `emissive-occluded`'s dark band corner sat on the old
     frustum's edge, so it can only darken), `shadow-tiles` (the fire fixture's `hearth_light` casts:
     its per-frame layers go 1 -> 5 and the edge 4096 -> 2048, which `tiles-dance` cancels).
   - Stale gate comments go: gates.py:441-444 (area goldens as references for 10.3's bug) and
     20083-20090 / 20375-20379 (a panel's shadow unmeasurable on cornell_box, partly the old frustum).
7. **Goldens:** `cornell_box` re-baked after the look is signed off, every other golden 0 px.

## Phase 2 -- cached area lights (the perf)
1. **Admit panels.** `shadow_light_takes_tiles` (shadow.c:1341) takes `LIGHT_AREA` too; the
   cached-with-no-range warning (2771) says "cached light" rather than testing `LIGHT_POINT`. Ranking,
   budget, stores, movers, the capture wait (`shadow_tiles_cover`) and the variant bit
   (`render.c:880`) already go through that predicate.
2. **A panel is cached from its centre, one view, six world-axis faces** -- the point light's
   tiles as they are, 6 cells. Its `source_radius`/`source_length` are 0, so `light_has_body` is
   false and `tile_body_now` gives no segment; a panel carrying either is refused a body by name
   (the capsule runs along `direction`, which on a panel is its normal -- a rectangle body is booked).
3. **Packing without losing the panel's size.** A cached point light parks its radius and near
   plane in `shadowMisc.zw`, which on a panel is the size that LTC, rain and fire read
   (`light_cluster.c:239,271-272`). The near plane moves to `upArea.w` for every cached light --
   packed 0 today and read by nothing -- and a panel's radius is 0 by type in the tile decode
   (`include/tile_lookup.glsl:50-62`). Its segment slots are already free on a panel. The lights UBO
   has no room for an eighth vec4 (14560 of 16384 B, `ubo.h:43-45`), which rules out adding one.
4. **The lookup.** The area branch, under `#if CETRA_HAS(PBR_FEAT_SHADOW_TILES)`, calls
   `tileShadow` when `aLayer >= SHADOW_TILE_MARK`, as the point branch does (pbr_frag:2204-2210).
5. **A scene file can cache a derived panel.** `light_overrides` (`cscene.c:433-450`) parses
   `cast_shadows` and not `shadow_cache`, and drops unknown keys silently; it takes `shadow_cache`
   too. `lights[]` already carries both (`cscene.c:374-375`).
6. **silent's tubes cache.** `lights_update` (`lights.c:299-310`) sets `cast_shadows` and `range`
   on the derived panels each frame from the TUBES table (51-84); a column says each tube caches:
   `tube_ceiling` and `tube_window`, and `tube_hood`, unshadowed today though the hall's floor is in
   front of its plane through the wall, given a cached shadow too (6 cells each). A panel is drawn
   while it emits; the flicker floor keeps it above zero. They leave the per-frame pool, which goes
   back to the flashlight alone.
7. **Look**: the hall and kitchen framings against phase 1's, which should be the same picture; any
   difference is shown, not assumed.
8. **Gate arms** in `run_shadow_tiles_gate`:
   - `tiles-area`: `area-reach`'s panel cached against the same panel per frame, within
     `tiles-match`'s tolerance; its block 6 cells, 1 view. Falsified by the area branch skipping
     `tileShadow` (the cached panel lights everything).
   - `tiles-area-size`: the cached panel lights a region nothing occludes exactly as the per-frame
     one does, 0 px there -- what packing the near plane over the size would break, the LTC
     rectangle shrunk to the near plane. Falsified by packing near into `shadowMisc.w` again.
9. **Profile** silent: "shadow punctual" against phase 1's, the tubes' blocks in the tile probe.

- **The uniform budget, measured.** On this Apple M1 Max (OpenGL 4.1 Metal) the uber `pbr` program's
  default-block uniforms come to 1029 components packed tight and 1552 padded to vec4, against a
  `GL_MAX_FRAGMENT_UNIFORM_COMPONENTS` of 4096. The count is the whole program's -- GL 4.1 cannot ask
  which stage references a uniform -- so the fragment stage's is somewhat lower, but the plan's
  premise, that the engine sits under GL 4.1's guaranteed 1024 today, is false: it already relies on
  more, and by the plan's rule no cap would qualify, 8 included. So the rule changes to the one the
  engine already lives by, the driver's limit: **the cap is 16**, adding 8 mat4s, 128 components,
  for about 1157 tight / 1680 padded of 4096. Desktop drivers report 4096 or more.
