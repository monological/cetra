# 13.13 Silent: a Gothic house

## Context

Silent's house today is a one-storey box with a plain gable and siding, 10 x 6 m. Inside are
the kitchen, a short hall with the clock, and two unfurnished rooms. The user wants it rebuilt
as a two-storey GOTHIC house, after four reference photos. Photo 2 leads: a weathered charcoal
Carpenter Gothic house in fog, with a steep scalloped bargeboard, lancet windows, a stone base,
and an arched porch under a pent roof.

What the user asked for:
- **Kept:** the kitchen stays at street level, so the spawn still looks out at the street.
- **Two storeys, kept simple.**
- **Finishes:** hardwood floors, and mahogany panelling with ornate carving.
- **The hall leads into the living room**, which has a large, ornate castle fireplace.
- **Rugs:** runners down the halls, and a large Persian rug in the living room.
- **Upstairs, a tower that is the study:** a lot of books, stained glass, and gargoyles.
- **The main house first.** The neighbours are unchanged in this spec.

Decided with the user:
- **Palette:** weathered charcoal (photo 2).
- **Layout:** a double-height great hall, with the stair up its east wall to a railed gallery.
- **Gargoyles:** built in code from kit pieces.
- **The fire:** lit.
- **Order:** this spec builds the house. A **stand-in fire** fills the hearth: emissive flame
  cards, embers, a flickering light and a crackle. **Engine fire is spec 13.14**, which replaces
  the stand-in.

This spec is app-only, in `apps/silent/` and its asset tools. It makes no engine change; if a
look step shows one is needed, it is raised with the user first. The demo-app rules hold: no
gate arms, no goldens, no review fan-outs. It is verified by renders shown to the user after
every phase that changes the picture, and a windowed walk with sound at the end.

## What exploration found (and what it forces)

- **The kit cannot draw Gothic shapes yet.**
  - No polygon or extrude primitive, so no pointed arches, cusps, scallops or tracery.
  - Walls are axis-aligned only, with at most 6 rectangular openings, and an octagonal tower
    needs walls at other angles.
  - Colliders are yaw-only boxes.
  - 64 material slots, 50 of them used.
- **Stairs work as boxes.** Jolt WalkStairs is on with `step_height` 0.25 (`character.c:345`),
  so boxes with 19 cm risers climb with no ramp.
- **A slope bug.** `player.c:49` sets `max_slope_angle = 45.0f`, but the field is in radians
  (`character.h:28`).
- **The shadow pool is 8 layers.** A shadowed point light takes 6 of them; a spot or area light
  takes 1. So the firelight is an AREA light across the firebox mouth, which takes 1 layer.
  Area panels do not scatter in fog, so the fire does not glow through the walls into the
  street, which a point light would.
- **Fog scatters only the scene's first spot.** The flashlight must stay registered first:
  every new light is added after `lights_build`.
- **Stained glass works as it is.** Thin transmission tints what is behind it by the albedo
  picture (`pbr_frag.glsl:2524`). A lit study therefore shows its colours to the street.
  - The colour has to live in the albedo, since attenuation does nothing at thickness 0.
  - Glass casts solid shadows, and is opaque in captures.
- **GI is one volume, swept in a single frame.** Roughly 800-1300 probes against today's 324
  lengthens the opening hitch about 3x, behind the existing fade-in. Probe centres must miss
  every wall and slab. Reflection probes are capped at 8, each with an axis-aligned box.
- **No culling inside a kit.** `kit_finish` makes one mesh per material, so the house and the
  street cull as a whole, and every shadow layer and GI face redraws all of it. This is
  measured, not assumed (Phase 2).
- **The picture atlas.** `make_cards.py`'s atlas is 80% full, with a flat normal map. A new atlas
  needs a normal map derived from a height field. Its roughness stays at 256²: the material
  array promotes every roughness layer to the largest, currently 512x256, about 18 MB.

## Design

### The plan of the house (`layout.h`, rewritten)

**Fixed points:**
- The kitchen, the hall, the clock, the spawn and every `KITCHEN_*` constant stay where they are.
- The house deepens from 6 m to 9.5 m: `HOUSE_BACK_Z` goes from 16 to 19.5.

**Ground floor:**
- **The front band** (z 10 to 13.8): the parlour at the tower's base (x -5 to -1.5, door shut),
  the hall with its runner and clock (x -1.5 to 0), and the kitchen as it is.
- **The great hall** behind it: x -5 to 5, z 13.8 to 19.5, two storeys high and open to the
  trusses.
- **Into the great hall:** a Tudor arch from the hall, sprung at 2.3 m with its apex at 2.8, under
  the gallery.
- **The fireplace** is centred on the back wall at x -0.75, the hall's axis. Walking down the
  hall shows it framed in the arch.
- **The Persian rug** lies in front of the fireplace, about 3 x 4 m.
- **The stair** climbs the east wall, about 1 m wide: 15 risers of 0.19 m and 14 treads of
  0.28 m, rising toward the front.

**Upper floor:**
- `FLOOR2_Y` is 3.15 and `CEIL2_Y` is 5.75. The eave is at 5.9.
- **The gallery** is 1.2 m deep along the great hall's front wall, with a runner, a balustrade
  and a newel at the stair head.
- **Off the gallery:**
  - the study, through a pointed doorway;
  - the box room over the hall, door shut;
  - the bedroom over the kitchen, door shut.

**The tower:**
- An octagon of 2.3 m apothem, centred on the front-west corner at (-5, 10).
- The house's walls butt into the middles of two of its faces. Its exterior is five whole faces
  and two half faces.
- **Inside**, it merges with the parlour below and the study above. The rectangle's walls inside
  the octagon are not built.
- **Above `CEIL2_Y`**, the octagon's three inner faces close the tower. The study's tower bay
  rises to a ribbed octagonal vault at about 9 m.
- **The walls** rise to 9.6 m: a corbel band, four gargoyles at the outer corners, then an
  octagonal spire to about 15.5 m with an iron finial.

**Roofs:**
- The main roof is ridged along z (front and back gables) at Gothic pitch, about 50 degrees, with
  its ridge near 11.9 m.
- Its west slope and the front gable's west rake are CUT round the tower.
- A pent roof crosses the front at the floor line, from the tower's east face to the east corner.
  It covers the porch and the kitchen window.
  - Its overhang is chosen so the kitchen window still catches the wind-driven rain about as
    it does today. That is checked at the look step.

**The street side:**
- The fog's house gap widens from 6 m to 8 m for the tower.
- `sounds.c`'s inside test asks `house_inside_distance()`, a signed distance to the rectangle
  union the octagon, so the layout lives in one file.

### The kit's Gothic vocabulary (`kit.c/h`)

- **`kit_polygon_facing`:** a coplanar world-space polygon, ear-clipped, with the kit's world UVs.
  - **`kit_frame_extrude`** is built on it: an (a, y) outline extruded from d0 to d1, with caps
    and flat sides.
  - **`kit_slab`:** a horizontal polygon slab with a bounding-box collider, for the octagon's
    floors.
- **Arch outlines:** `kit_arch_outline(KitArch shape, a0, a1, spring, ...)`, for POINTED (lancet or
  equilateral, by a radius factor) and TUDOR (four-centred) arches. The openings, the window
  frames, the carved panels' blind arches and the bargeboard cusps all draw from it.
- **Arched openings:** `KitOpening` gains `KitArch arch`. Above the springing line, the wall
  fills the two spandrels with extrusions, and the slab over the opening starts at the apex.
- **Walls at any angle:** `kit_wall` turns into a frame, and `kit_frame_wall` takes any yaw. Every
  slab goes through `kit_frame_box`. Axis-aligned walls build exactly as before.
- **Stairs:** `kit_frame_stair`, with treads, risers and one box collider per step.
- **Caps raised:** `KIT_MAX_MATERIALS` to 96, `KIT_MAX_OPENINGS` to 8.
- **Folded in:**
  - `clock.c`'s convex `plate` fan becomes `kit_polygon_facing`.
  - `player.c:49` becomes `glm_rad(45.0f)`.

### Materials and textures

- **Photo scans** (`fetch_textures.py`, CC0 from Poly Haven or ambientCG, 256 px), with
  candidates shown on a contact sheet for the user to pick:
  - hardwood floor;
  - weathered dark planks for board-and-batten (rotated vertical);
  - castle ashlar for the fireplace, the great hall above the panelling, and the gargoyles;
  - rough foundation stone;
  - fish-scale slate, generated if no scan fits;
  - leather.
- **Reused scans:**
  - mahogany is the clock's `lacquered_cherry_wood` at a panel repeat;
  - iron is a dark painted metal;
  - roof slate is the existing `roof_slates_02`.
- **Map names:** any new displacement map is saved as `_disp`, never `_height` (13.12's
  parallax trap).
- **A new picture atlas, `tools/make_gothic.py`:**
  - A sibling of `make_cards.py`, importing its packer, header writer, `fetch` and veneer
    helpers. It generates `src/gothic.h`.
  - Albedo and normal are 2048², the normal taken from a drawn height field; roughness is 256².
  - **The cards it holds:**
    - linenfold and blind-tracery panel bays and a quatrefoil frieze, carved mahogany;
    - the Persian rug, plus runner end and middle pieces that tile along a run;
    - three stained-glass lancet designs and a leaded diamond-quarry piece;
    - four book-spine strips, gilt bands and titles in IM Fell, which is OFL;
    - a carved coat of arms and a cast-iron fireback;
    - stand-in flame shapes.
- **New MatIds:**
  - floorboards, mahogany, carved, rug, stained glass and leaded glass (thin and transmissive,
    colour in the albedo), book;
  - leather;
  - stone, carved stone, foundation;
  - dark siding, fish-scale slate, iron;
  - embers and flame (emissive, `emissive_light = 1`), soot, wax.
- **Rain tables:** porosity and beads get rows for every new exterior material. The leaded and
  stained glass bead by themselves, being transmissive.

### Lights, sound, GI

- **Lights:**
  - The **firelight** is an area light at the firebox mouth. It casts shadows, takes 1 layer,
    and flickers as a pure function of the sim clock, as `flicker_level` does, so headless runs
    repeat.
  - The **study lamp** is a downward spot with no shadows, and a gallery sconce is dim. Both are
    registered after the flashlight.
  - **The shadow budget:** flashlight 1, two tubes 2, fire 1, which leaves 4 of the 8 layers
    free.
- **Sound:** `fetch_sounds.py` gains a `fire` loop job, with CC0 Freesound candidates auditioned
  by ear. It is placed at the hearth and scaled by `hearing`.
- **GI:** the volume is refitted over both storeys and the tower. A probe-placement check prints
  any centre within 0.15 m of a wall line or slab, and the grid is tuned until there are none.
- **Reflection probes:** kitchen, hall, great hall, gallery and study, 5 of 8. The clock's
  comment about probe rows is updated to match the new grid.
- **Drip lines:**
  - the pent-roof gutter: its joints, the overflow past the kitchen window, the downpipe shoe;
  - four gargoyle spouts, at a stream's rate, onto the yard;
  - the bare side eaves.
  - The total is printed against `RAIN_DRIP_MAX` (48).

  - `tower.c/h`: the octagon, the vault, the corbels, the spire, the gargoyles' placement.

## Phases

Each phase ends with a commit (signed), its as-built note in the spec, and a one-line phase
announcement. Phases 2 to 7 each end with pinned headless screenshots shown to the user, and
the next phase does not start until the user has seen them.

0. **Branch and spec.** Branch `13.13-silent-gothic-house` from master. Copy this plan into
   `specs/13.13-silent-gothic-house.md` and commit it alone. Create the task list.
1. **The kit's Gothic vocabulary.** Polygon, extrude, slab, arch outlines, arched openings, frame
   walls, stairs, the raised caps, the `plate` reuse and the slope fix. Before and after renders
   at spawn must match by eye: axis-aligned walls are unchanged by construction.
2. **The shell.**
   - `layout.h` rewritten. Walls with lancet, Tudor and door openings. The slabs, the gallery,
     the stair, and the tower's walls, floors and vault.
   - The main roof cut round the tower, the pent roof and the spire, all in existing materials.
   - Colliders and the fog gap. The GI grid with its placement check, and the probe set. The
     inside test. The gutter and drips re-hung.
   - **Checks:**
     - climb the stair and walk the gallery into the study with `--pad-script` and
       `--trace-player`, which shows y reaching `FLOOR2_Y`;
     - frame time at the spawn and in the great hall, before and after the rebuild. If the
       house costs more than about 25% at the spawn, the kit gains regions, with one mesh per
       (region, material), so it can cull.
   - **Look:** from the street, down the hall into the great hall, up the stair, the gallery, the
     study.
3. **The exterior's dress.**
   - Pick textures from the contact sheet.
   - Board-and-batten with battens that skip the openings. The stone base and water table, the
     slate.
   - Scalloped bargeboards with pendant finials. Iron cresting and finials. The porch's arched
     brackets and balustrade. Lancet tracery and glass. Label mouldings, the kitchen window's
     included. The chimney stack.
   - The tower's corbels and spire, and the gargoyles with their spouts.
   - **Look:** the street by night and by `--day`, in the rain, and the gargoyles from the yard.

## As built

### Phase 1 -- the kit's Gothic vocabulary

- **New kit pieces:**
  - `kit_polygon_facing`: ear-clipped, with the normal found by Newell's method, so a concave outline's first corners never decide it.
  - `kit_frame_polygon` and `kit_frame_extrude`, both over one world-space `extrude`.
  - `kit_slab`: its body is one yawed box per edge, reaching to the centroid. That covers a convex outline exactly and nothing outside it, so the octagon's ground slab leaves no invisible step round the tower.
  - `kit_arch_outline`: pointed arches, and Tudor arches that fall back to pointed past a rise of about 0.39 of the span.
  - Arched openings: the spandrels are extruded per layer, and the collider treats the head as solid from the springing line.
  - `kit_frame_wall`: `kit_wall` is now a quarter-turn frame over the same path.
  - `kit_frame_stair`.
- **Raised caps:** 96 materials, 8 openings, and 128 corners in an outline.
- **The clock's `plate` fan** is now `kit_frame_polygon`. The slope limit is `glm_rad(45)`.
- **Check:** the spawn and the clock were each rendered before and after.
  - The spawn moved 3,118 px with a largest channel difference of 7. The clock view moved 34,078 px with a largest difference of 5.
  - Both differences are float rounding at the z-walls' corners under their quarter-turn frame, plus the re-cut hands.
  - The frames are identical by eye, and the run-to-run floor is 0 px.
