# 11.44 — A beach with a real surf, and an ocean that knows how big a metre is

## Context

`apps/tree`'s shoreline reads as a hard line with foam glued to one side of it, not as
surf. Three causes were established by measurement today, not guessed:

1. **The surf zone is 0.38 m wide.** Its width is the shoal depth window divided by the
   bed's slope. The window (`OCEAN_SHOAL_MIN/FULL`, `ocean.glsl:153-154`) is 2.56 world
   units, and `ground.h:46` states tree's scale as **22 units per metre** — so the window
   is 12 cm of depth. `ground.h:32` pins the beach slope at 0.31 deliberately, to make the
   island "fit the frame". 2.56 / 0.31 = 8.4 units = 0.38 m. There is no surf zone to see.
2. **The ocean has no idea how big a world unit is.** `OCEAN_SHOAL_MIN/FULL`,
   `WATER_SHORT_NEAR/FAR`, `WATER_MAX_BEND` and the caustic depth window are all physical
   quantities written in world units. AGENTS.md already records this as an open gap. The
   engine has the number — `Sky.world_units_per_km` — and **tree never sets it**: it stays
   at the default 1000 while tree is 22000, so tree's atmosphere is mis-scaled too.
   `tree.c:608` exposes a GUI slider for it and nothing writes it.
3. **The bed is a dome.** A paraboloid meets the water steeply. Even with the window fixed,
   a proper beach wants a gentle shelf through the waterline.

And a corpus gap found while committing the foam fixes: **both water goldens are Gerstner
with no bed**, so crest foam does not exist in them by construction and shore foam is
identically zero. All three foam fixes moved **0 golden pixels**. Anything about surf needs
a fixture that can actually see surf.

**Outcome:** a beach that shoals gently into turquoise shallows, with a continuous soft
surf line, procedural sand that goes wet near the waterline, and rocks in the shallows —
plus an ocean whose physical constants mean what they say at any world scale.

## Phase 1 — the ocean learns the world scale

Everything else rests on this; without it a flatter beach still gets a 12 cm surf band.

- `water.c` already reaches `scene->sky` (`:1181`, `:1225`). Upload
  `sky->world_units_per_km` to the water program and to the spectral and foam passes — all
  three include `ocean.glsl`. Name it `unitsPerKm` to match `sky.c:319`.
- `ocean.glsl` and `water_frag.glsl`: restate the unit-bearing constants **in metres** and
  convert at use. `OCEAN_SHOAL_MIN/FULL` (0.14, 2.7 → the depths a wave actually shoals
  over), `WATER_SHORT_NEAR/FAR`, `WATER_MAX_BEND`, the caustic depth window.
- `tree.c` sets `sky->world_units_per_km = GROUND_UNITS_PER_METRE * 1000.0f`.

**Bar:** `assets/water_fixture.cscn` is authored at 1 unit = 1 metre, so its factor is
exactly 1.0 and it must render **0 px**. That is the entire safety argument for touching
constants this load-bearing.

**The atmosphere moves with it, and that is accepted.** `world_units_per_km` also drives
aerial perspective and cloud scale, so every tree frame changes — the sky has been
mis-scaled by 22x all along and this corrects it. Delivered as an A/B with the change
quantified, not slipped in: one source of truth for one physical fact is the whole point,
and giving the ocean a private scale uniform would re-create exactly the bug being fixed.

## Phase 2 — the beach shelf

- `ground_height_at` (`ground.c:12`) gains a shelf: the paraboloid crown eased into a
  gentle ramp through the waterline. Keep a **closed-form shoreline** — `ground.h:14-17`
  chose the paraboloid precisely so `TREE_WATER_LEVEL` stays derived from the shape rather
  than tuned by eye, and that property must survive.
- **`ground_build_mesh` must be switched to call `ground_height_at`.** It re-derives the
  paraboloid inline today (`ground.c:85` for height, `:100` for the slope/normal) while
  `ground.h:74` claims one authority. `ground_build_seabed` already reads the function
  (`:191`). Left alone, the profile change would move the bed, the seabed, the grass and
  the leaf litter while the island mesh kept the old shape.
- Re-check two derived quantities the shelf invalidates: `TREE_WATER_EXTENT` 400 must still
  contain the waterline (at 310 today), and `tree.c:312` records the bed at 3.1 units per
  texel against an 8.4-unit ramp — a wider ramp is fine, a moved shore may not be.
  `ground_sphere_fit` is a crown fit and should be confirmed still valid for the collider.

## Phase 3 — procedural sand

- New `cetra/src/procedural/sand.c/h`, beside `vegetation_tex.c` and following the bark
  generators' shape (`veg_bark_albedo` / `_normal` / `_roughness`, `vegetation_tex.h:39-42`)
  and reusing `veg_fbm2` / `veg_worley2`: fBm dunes, Worley grain, directional ripples.
- **Large-scale colour comes from vertex colour, not a second texture.** `pbr_frag` is at
  16/16 samplers, so a sand/upland blend cannot add one. The island mesh gets a vertex
  colour driven by height above the waterline (sand at the shore, earth and grass upland);
  `generate_island_albedo` (`tree.c:107`) becomes a tiled neutral detail map. The mesh's UVs
  are planar and tiled (`ground.c:116`), so a baked radial gradient would repeat — vertex
  colour is the mechanism that works.
- **Wet sand**: a darker, glossier band just above the waterline on the same channel. Cheap,
  and it is much of what sells the reference.

## Phase 4 — rocks in the shallows

Reuse `rock_default_params` / `rock_build_mesh` (`procedural/rock.h`), scattered through the
surf zone the way `apps/forest`'s `build_rocks` does (`forest.c:501-516`).

## Phase 5 — a fixture that can see surf

- `assets/gen_beach_fixture.py` + `beach_fixture.cscn`: a shoaling bed, a shoreline, and
  shallow water in one frame, following `gen_water_fixture.py`'s shape (it authors every
  key `parse_water` reads, and `water-fixture-roundtrip` now asserts that).
- Gate arms on it: surf-zone width, the turquoise gradient (R/B falling with depth), and the
  foam band present and bounded. This is what closes the gap where three foam fixes moved no
  golden at all.


- Phase 1 must leave `water_fixture` and `water_submerged` at **0 px** — the fixture's scale
  factor is 1.0, so any movement is a bug in the conversion.
- Phases 2-4 move tree only; the water goldens should not move, and any that do is a finding.
- A/B crops at the reported framing before committing any look change, per the standing rule:
  `--fov 31.5 --cam-eye -181.460,-7.849,-240.435 --cam-target -180.919,-8.513,-240.951`,
  and at `--sun-elevation 8`, which is the framing where tree is legible at all.
- Re-bake only what the geometry change explains, and say what moved and why.
