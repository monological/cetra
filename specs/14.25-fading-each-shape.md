# 14.25 Fading each shape

A correction to 14.24 (dimming by light), found in pcb 7.3.1: a selection box drawn over a dimmed
part was faded with it, and under the mask erased.

## What is wrong

14.24 fades after the layers are drawn: the lit pass records, per pixel, the largest coverage of
any tagged shape and of any lit one, by max over every layer, and the composite fades a pixel by
them. Which shape is on top is never asked, so:

- **An untagged shape over a dimmed one is faded.** pcb's selection box, drawn on its own layer over
  the part above the chosen one, fades where it crosses that part's silkscreen, and under the mask
  is erased there. 14.24 promised an untagged shape never fades.
- **A hidden shape still covers what is lit.** Under the mask a dimmed shape over a lit one fades
  the pixel to the paper, the lit one with it, where hiding should show what lies beneath.
- **A lit shape spares a dimmed one over it**, where they overlap.

## The change

**Each shape fades as it is drawn, not the picture after.**

- **A shape left out** -- tagged, neither tag lit -- fades in its own colour passes, by the view's
  `fade`, less within `fade_room` of a lit shape as before.
  - Below a fade of 1 its colour is mixed toward `fade_to`, and it still covers what lies under it,
    in the layers' order.
  - At a fade of 1, the mask, its coverage goes instead, so it is gone and what lies under it
    shows; within the room it comes back as the fade eases.
- **An untagged shape never fades**, whatever it is drawn over; **a lit one never fades** either.
- **The room** is read as before, from the lit pass's target with rings of taps, but by the shape's
  fragment, so the lit pass draws before the layers, and only while the view fades with room. Its
  target keeps the lit coverage alone, one channel.
- **The composite copies the view's target**, as before 14.24.
- **Order is unchanged**: a dimmed shape over a lit one draws faded over it, as layers order them.

**To decide (the user's):** whether a lit shape should draw over every dimmed one instead, so
nothing dimmed ever covers what is lit. Not in this spec.

## Phases

1. **Fading each shape**: `canvas_pass.glsl` fades by a flag the vertex stages pass from
   `canvasColour`; the room's taps move there from the composite; the lit pass first, and only with
   room, into an R8 target; the composite a copy.
2. **The fade sheet and its gate**: the sheet gains an untagged blue box on a layer over a dimmed
   red one, and a lit green box under a dimmed red one; `canvas-fade` checks them at half and, run
   again at a fade of 1, that the blue shows, the green shows through, and what is left out is
   gone. Looked at.
3. **Docs**: `docs/canvas.md`'s dimming, `docs/shaders.md`, the roadmap if it names the fade.
4. **The reviews**, then the merge, and pcb brought up to it.

## As built

### Phase 1: fading each shape

- `canvasColour` (`canvas_paint.glsl`) says whether a shape fades -- tagged, and neither tag lit --
  and both vertex stages pass it on flat (`vFades`). In the lit pass it draws lit shapes alone.
- `canvasOutput` (`canvas_pass.glsl`) fades a shape left out by `uFade`, less by `canvasLight`, the
  room's rings of taps moved there from the composite: below 1 its colour toward `uFadeTo`, at 1
  its coverage, in every pass, so the layer's alpha and its colours agree.
- `canvas_draw` draws the lit pass first, and only while the view fades with room, into an R8
  target on a unit of its own (`CANVAS_UNIT_LIT`); the fade's uniforms go with the view's. With no
  room the shapes read nothing from it.
- The composite is a copy again, as before 14.24.

### Phase 2: the fade sheet and its gate

- The fade sheet gains a layer over the first: an untagged blue box on a red one left out, and a red
  one left out on a lit green one. Under `--fade` at 1 the far and near boxes are paper and the
  green shows.
- `canvas-fade` checks the six points at half and again at 1; the room's near point read 20 against
  128 with no room. The `canvas` group and `gate-docs` passed.
- Looked at in renders at half, at 1 and with 20 pt of room.

### Phase 4: the reviews

The thermo-nuclear review and `/simplify`'s four angles in one agent, both at effort high. Applied:
- **Fixed: text vanished in a view fading with room.** The lit pass, drawn first now, bound its
  target on the last font's unit and unbound it there. It now binds on `CANVAS_UNIT_LIT` and leaves
  it bound for the layers.
- **Fixed: with room, a light under a shape left out spared it**, so under the mask with room it
  still covered the lit shape: the lit target holds coverage alone, so a light under a shape and
  one beside it read alike. `canvasLight` gives no room where a light covers the pixel itself, so
  the overlap is as with no room.
- **The fade amount travels, not a flag**: `canvasColour` gives `uFade` for a shape left out, so the
  fragment stage tests it alone; `uFade` lives with the vertex stage.
- **The taps after the discards**: the mask scales coverage, then the coverage and rank discards,
  and only the pass that paints mixes the colour.
- **The mask with no room drops a shape left out in the vertex stage**, as a hidden tag does.
- The lit pass's dead colour write, the room passed once, `canvas.h`'s `fade` saying 1 hides, and
  the constants' and the unit enum's stale comments.
- **The gate**: the overlap checked with room too, the mask run with room, and a lit H whose ink
  each run with room must draw. The `canvas` group and `gate-docs` passed.

Falsified: the lit pass binding on the last font's unit took the H's ink to 0 in both runs with
room; the room counting a light under the pixel left the faded box over the lit one red with room,
and covering it under the mask with room. `docs/canvas.md` says a shape over a lit one fades wholly.

Not applied: working the room out once a pixel in a pass of its own, so a shape's fragment reads one
texel rather than tapping; it costs a target and a program, and matters only with room on.

### Phase 3: docs

`docs/canvas.md`'s dimming says each shape fades as it is drawn and the mask hides; `docs/shaders.md`
gives the fade to `canvas_pass.glsl`; the EDA roadmap has 14.25's entry; the canvas app's README the
sheet's new boxes and a STRENGTH of 1.
