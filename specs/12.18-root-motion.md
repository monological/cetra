# Spec 12.18 — Root motion

## Context

Root motion is named in **17 places across 7 files** — `ik.h:94`, `docs/foot-locking.md` (4),
`docs/verification.md:673`, `docs/game-engine-roadmap.md` (4), and specs 12.1, 12.9, 12.10 — always
as the road not taken: *"the other answer to the same problem, and still not in this engine."*
There is no code, no flag, no probe case, no gate arm and no API surface for it anywhere.

Spec 12.10 took the other road: `animation_stride_speed` measures the ground speed a clip's feet
imply, the game divides its wanted speed by that and writes the quotient to `animator->speed`. The
character drives the clip. Root motion inverts it — **the clip drives the character** — and it is
what an authored lunge, dodge, vault or turn-in-place needs, because those are choreographed
distances rather than steered ones, and rate-scaling a fixed distance is exactly wrong.

**Outcome: what a clip states about its own displacement is what the character does, everywhere a
clip states anything; the stick steers and the animation travels.**

---

## What "everything root-motion driven" means here, stated honestly

The scope chosen is the broadest of the three: the character's speed comes from the clip, with
stride matching as the fallback. Exploration turned up the fact that decides how that reads:

> **No committed clip in this repository carries root motion.** `puppet.gltf`'s hips track is a
> vertical bob only (`gen_puppet_fixture.py:192-195`); the eight shared FBX clips each carry
> exactly one translation curve on the hips, and `anim-stride` passing at all is proof they are
> **in-place** — a clip with forward root motion has a near-stationary stance toe in model space
> and `animation_stride_speed` would refuse it.

So "everything root-motion driven" cannot mean "switch the existing clips over"; there is nothing
to switch. It means:

| clip | drive |
|---|---|
| carries a root curve (the new puppet clips) | **root motion**, at playback rate 1 — travel is exactly what the clip lays down |
| in place (every committed FBX, so every `--puppet` rig) | stride matching, unchanged |

The choice is made per playing source from a measured property of its clips, not by a flag. On the
generated puppet — the demo's default — locomotion, the lunge and the turn are all root-motion
driven end to end. On `--puppet assets/models/t_pose.fbx` nothing changes, because those clips say
nothing about their own displacement. Making a RETARGETED clip carry translation is a second
feature and is out of scope below; it is the only reason the second row exists.

## Design

### The measurement: `animation_root_travel`, in `animation.c`

```c
// animation.h, beside animation_stride_speed
// What this clip's ROOT states about its own displacement over its whole length: the
// translation and the yaw from its first tick to its last, in MODEL units and radians.
// False when the root is not driven or states no motion -- an in-place clip, which is a
// real answer about the clip and not a failure to measure.
bool animation_root_travel(const Animation* clip, const Skeleton* skeleton, int root_bone,
                           vec3 out_travel, float* out_yaw);
```

Two samples, not sixty: `animation_sample_pose` at tick 0 and at `duration`, each accumulated to
model space through `pose_local` + `accumulate_global` — the same machinery
`animation_stride_speed` uses (`animation.c:1043-1053`), which is the function to read before
writing this one. It is the *statement* where 12.10's is an *inference*, so it needs no refusal
tests, no stance windows and no median.

**Which bone is the root** is asked of the rig, not assumed: `skeleton_resolve_bone` for the hips
(ragdoll's precedent, `ragdoll.c:152`), falling back to the first bone with `parent_index == -1`.
Resolved once by the animator at create, logged once.

### The extraction lives in `animator.c`, because only it can be right

12.10's argument transfers exactly (`animator.h:176-182`): only the animator knows each source's
clock, its wrap, its weights and the fade between two sources. The whole of it is four things:

1. **Read the root from the final blended pose.** `Animator.base_pose` (`animator.h:114`) is the
   pose about to be applied, after the space blend, the crossfade and the masked layer. Its root
   bone's local position and yaw are what the frame will draw, so the delta taken from it is the
   blend's own — `pose_blend` has already done the weighting and nothing needs re-deriving.
2. **Correct the wrap.** A looping source jumps back at its wrap, which is not travel. The space
   is PHASE-SYNCED — one clock, so every entry wraps together and `s->wrapped` is the whole
   signal — and the correction is `sum(w_i * loop_travel_i)`, each entry's per-loop travel
   measured once at `switch_source` by the function above. Algebraically exact:
   `(end - prev) + (now - start) == (now - prev) + loop_travel`.
3. **Suppress a switch.** A cut, a `play_once`, a resume and a `stop` all move the pose without
   the character having gone anywhere. Each rebases `prev_root` instead of reading a delta.
4. **Zero it in the pose, into `base_pose`, before `animation_state_apply_pose`.** The root's
   position takes its bind XZ and its yaw is divided out, or the character moves twice — once as
   a body and once as a mesh sliding off its own capsule. Y and every other channel are untouched.

   Note the retargeted path **already does this**: `animation.c:923` and `:950` overwrite a matched
   bone's position with the bind pose. That is why an in-place retargeted clip yields a zero delta
   and falls back with nothing to configure, and why this spec touches neither line — 12.11's
   behaviour and the `anim-retarget` arm stay bit-identical.

New state on the animator: `vec3 prev_root`, `float prev_root_yaw`, an accumulator pair, and the
resolved root bone index. The authored values are captured **before** the zeroing, or next frame's
delta reads against a pose that was already neutralised.

### The consumer: a drain, not a velocity

The two clocks do not line up, and the exploration pinned it: the animator ticks **once per
rendered frame** from `game_pre_render` (`game.c:290-291`) with `sim_clock.delta`, which is
`N * fixed_timestep` for the N fixed steps that frame — after the accumulator loop has already
run. The character controller takes a **velocity** per fixed step (`character.c:350`), and
`character_controller_set_position` is a teleport with no sweep, so velocity is the only
collision-respecting channel in.

```c
// animator.h
bool animator_root_motion(const Animator*);                      // does the playing source carry any
bool animator_take_root_motion(Animator*, vec3 out, float* yaw); // drains what has accumulated
```

A **drain** rather than a published velocity, because it conserves distance under any frame
pacing: whatever the animator laid down is handed over exactly once, and a frame that ran two
fixed steps or none neither doubles nor loses it. The caller divides by its own `dt` to get the
velocity the controller wants. One frame of latency is inherent (the delta is extracted after that
frame's steps have run) and goes in the header rather than being papered over.

### `gametest` — the stick steers, the clip travels

Root motion changes what the input MEANS, and the change is the demo:

- The camera-relative direction (12.17) becomes a **target facing**: `player_yaw` turns toward it
  at a rate, rather than being derived from the velocity after the fact (`gametest.c:3108-3113`).
- `animator->param` takes the stick's magnitude against the clips' own top speed; `animator->speed`
  stays **1.0**. Travel is then whatever the blend lays down, which is the point — no rate warping,
  so the feet and the ground cannot disagree by construction.
- The drained delta is scaled by `PLAYER_SCALE`, rotated by `player_yaw`, divided by `dt` and
  written into `vel[0]`/`vel[2]`. Gravity, jump and swim keep the Y channel exactly as they are.
- `--no-root-motion` restores today's path, the spelling `--no-ik`, `--no-lock` and `--no-puppet`
  already use, and the A/B the acceptance test needs.

`build_locomotion` gains the root-travel case: where the clips state a travel, entries sit on the
m/s axis at `|travel| / seconds * PLAYER_SCALE` and `player_speed` is the fastest of them. Where
they do not, it is today's stride path, printed line and all.

### The asset: clips that actually travel

The generator already writes a Hips translation track for its height curve
(`gen_puppet_fixture.py:379-386`), so this is one field widened from a scalar `hips_y` to a curve
that also moves in XZ, plus a root yaw curve. Four clips **appended** — the generator's own comment
says appending is the cheap place, since every byteOffset and animation index ahead of it is
untouched and every consumer binds by name:

| clip | length | states |
|---|---|---|
| `travel_walk` | 1.0 s loop | `gait()` plus a linear +Z ramp: **1.20 m per loop**, so 1.20 m/s at rate 1 |
| `travel_run` | 0.5 s loop | the run's shape plus **1.60 m per loop**, so 3.20 m/s |
| `lunge` | 0.6 s one-shot | **1.20 m** forward, ending on the bind pose |
| `spin` | 1.0 s one-shot | **180 degrees** of yaw, zero travel — the yaw half on its own |

Every number is stated in the generator and asserted there, so a gate expects a number this file
declares rather than a frame somebody looked at. `idle`, `walk`, `run`, `jump`, `wave`, `hold90`,
`rest` and `swim` are **untouched** — `puppet_golden` plays `--anim-clip run` by name and must stay
0 px.

New actions: `lunge` on **Q** and `spin` on **C**. Both take a trigger axis on the pad rather than
a button, because the pad is genuinely full — every one of GLFW's fifteen buttons is already bound
and the two triggers are the only free inputs left.

### Decisions taken (do not relitigate)

- **Extract from the pose, not from the channel.** The channel is in the SOURCE rig's units for a
  retargeted clip and would need a proportion transfer this spec does not build; the pose is
  already in target-rig model space and already blended.
- **Rate stays 1 on the root-motion path.** Scaling playback to hit a requested speed exactly is
  stride matching wearing root motion's coat, and it re-introduces the coupling this removes.
- **The override layer contributes no root motion.** A wave does not walk; masked layers are the
  wrong shape to carry travel and pretending otherwise costs a weighting nobody can check.
- **Model units, and the caller owes the scale.** 12.10's rule, and 12.9's live bug — `PLAYER_SCALE`
  is applied at the one call site in the app.

## Phases

Branch `12.18-root-motion` off master; the spec commits first, alone, before any code. Each phase
ends with its arms green and the app **run** where the phase changed what it draws.

**Phase 0 — look at it, and build the arm that is red.** Walk the demo today and record what the
feet do. Confirm by measurement rather than inference which committed clips carry translation —
`print_animation` (`animation.c:1333`) already prints per-channel key counts and is declared,
implemented and **called nowhere**; it is the diagnostic this needs. Then write
`anim-root-travel` against `travel_walk`, which does not exist yet: it must be **red**.

**Phase 1 — the measurement.** `animation_root_travel`. Arms: exact agreement with the authored
1.20 m on `travel_walk`, and a **refusal** on `idle` and on `strut_walk.fbx`.

**Phase 2 — the asset.** The generator's hips curve widened, the four clips appended, the asserts
beside them. Re-run it and check `anim-clip-loads` still binds all 22 joints and the puppet golden
is 0 px before going further.

**Phase 3 — the extraction.** The animator's delta, wrap correction, switch suppression, pose
zeroing, and the two public calls. Arms: `anim-root-blend`, `anim-root-wrap`, `anim-root-switch`,
`anim-root-zeroed`.

**Phase 4 — the app, and the look.** Steering, the drain into the controller, the yaw,
`build_locomotion`'s new case, `--no-root-motion`, the two new actions. **Then run it** — walk,
turn, lunge into a wall, spin, and the same three against `--no-root-motion`.

**Phase 5 — the arms that read the app.** `anim-root-lunge` (the character travels the authored
1.20 m x `PLAYER_SCALE`, and under `--no-root-motion` it does not) and `anim-root-spin` (exactly
180 degrees). Two-sided, off `--trace-player`'s existing `pos` columns.

**Phase 6 — the docs.** All 17 mentions say the opposite thing now. `ik.h:94`,
`docs/foot-locking.md` (4 lines), `docs/verification.md`, `docs/game-engine-roadmap.md` (the row at
:268, Part 3's tail, the bottom line), `docs/cli-reference.md` for the flag and the two keys, and
`AGENTS.md`'s `animation.c/h`, `animator.c/h` and `gametest` rows.

## Verification

Scoped per phase:

```bash
python3 scripts/gates.py --only anim        # phases 1, 3, 5
python3 scripts/gates.py --only gamepad     # phase 4 -- see the warning below
python3 scripts/gates.py --only ik          # phase 4
```

**The arm most likely to need attention is `pad-camera-relative`.** It reads the trace's `vel`
columns, and under root motion those come from `player_yaw` turning toward the input rather than
from the input directly, so the heading now *settles* into the camera's frame instead of snapping
to it. It must stay a real two-sided test of the movement scheme; if it needs a longer run, say so
in its docstring rather than widening the tolerance.

End of job: `./build.sh`, the full `gates.py`, `goldens.py`. **No golden should move** — `run` is
untouched, the player is idle at startup in both menu goldens, and a clip appended to the fixture
changes no existing byteOffset. Assert 0 px rather than re-baking.

**The numbers that decide it**, not a green suite:

- `travel_walk` measured at exactly the authored 1.20 m, and `strut_walk.fbx` refused.
- The lunge's travel: 1.20 m x `PLAYER_SCALE` against `--no-root-motion`'s stick-driven distance.
- The spin's 180 degrees, and the delta across a loop wrap summing to one `loop_travel` with no
  spike at the seam.

**The watch**, which no arm replaces:

```bash
./out/bin/gametest                                    # walk, turn, lunge into a wall, spin
./out/bin/gametest --no-root-motion                   # the two told apart
./out/bin/gametest --puppet assets/models/t_pose.fbx  # the fallback: unchanged, and it says so
```

**What this spec does NOT get to claim.** The generated puppet's `gait()` is a straight-leg
pendulum with no stance phase (`docs/verification.md:717-720`), so its feet slide whatever the body
does, and the committed FBX clips carry no root motion to drive. **Root motion's effect on foot
slide is therefore not measurable on any asset in this tree**, and no arm or doc line should say it
is. What is measurable is that the character travels exactly the distance the clip states.

## Out of scope

- **Translation through the retarget.** `animation.c:923` and `:950` pin a matched bone's position
  to the bind pose, so a retargeted clip can never carry root motion. Fixing it needs a proportion
  transfer (hip height, leg length) that does not exist, and it would move 12.11's behaviour.
- **Vertical root motion.** Y stays with gravity and the jump; an authored vault wants the
  controller's ground handling to yield, which is its own piece of work.
- **An animation state machine.** `gametest`'s `player_medium` stays the hand-rolled one it is.
- **Speed warping** — scaling playback so root-motion travel hits an exact requested speed.
- **Saving the accumulator.** It is one frame of transient; `player_yaw` is already carried.
