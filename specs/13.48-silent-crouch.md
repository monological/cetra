# Spec 13.48: crouching in silent

## Context

The user wants to crouch in silent. Their choices:
- **Controls:** C toggles a crouch, Left Ctrl holds it, and pad B toggles.
- **Fits under things:** the capsule really shrinks, and standing up is refused while there is no room.
- **Slower and quieter.**
- **Smaller head bob.**
- **Sprint stands the player up**, as soon as there is room.

**What the code has today:**
- **The engine.** `cetra/src/game/character.c` builds one capsule at creation and has no way to change it. The capsule is shared between the CharacterVirtual and its inner body.
- **JoltC.** It binds `JPC_CharacterVirtual_SetInnerBodyShape`, but not `CharacterVirtual::SetShape` or `RotatedTranslatedShape`.
  - Jolt itself has both, and its own crouch sample shows the way (`Samples/Tests/Character/CharacterVirtualTest.cpp:185`, `CharacterBaseTest.cpp:103-106`):
    1. a RotatedTranslatedShape per stance;
    2. `SetShape(shape, 1.5 * mPenetrationSlop, ...)`, which refuses when the new shape would penetrate more than that;
    3. `SetInnerBodyShape` after a success.
  - `SetShape` already ignores the character's own inner body.
- **What every app assumes.** silent, forest and gametest all treat the controller's position as the capsule's centre. silent uses `CAPSULE_REST`, a constant, to put the feet under it.
- **No new routes (measured).** I checked every barrier, fence, door, the dock and the lake ring. Nothing a 1.15 m capsule can get under is closed to a 1.7 m one. The only low spots are:
  - the mansion gallery sconces, 1.47 m above the gallery floor, where a crouch passes under and standing is refused;
  - the mansion fireplace mouth, which a standing player already clears by 5 cm;
  - the deck over the dock, with 0.95 to 1.03 m of clearance, which a crouch still does not fit under.

## Design

**Keep the position where it is.** A crouched capsule is shorter and offset downward, so its foot stays where the standing one's is.
- The controller's position stays the centre of the capsule it was made with, so `CAPSULE_REST`, `player_feet`, the gait's displacements and every app's arithmetic are unchanged.
- A crouch therefore moves the position by nothing, and the gait sees no step. Only the eye moves.

### Engine (`cetra/src/game/character.c/.h`, and a new `cetra/src/character_jolt.cpp/.h`)

- **`CharacterController.half_height`:** the capsule's cylinder half-height now. It starts as the config's.
- **`bool character_controller_set_half_height(CharacterController* cc, float half_height)`:**
  - **The shape:** a capsule of the config's radius and `half_height`, translated by `(0, half_height - config.capsule_half_height, 0)`. Its foot stays put.
  - **Shrinking** always succeeds: the check is skipped (FLT_MAX), since the shorter capsule lies inside the taller.
  - **Growing** is checked at `1.5 * mPenetrationSlop`, Jolt's own sample value. If it would not fit, the call returns false and changes nothing, so the caller retries next step.
  - **On success** the inner body takes the same shape.
  - It returns bool per the naming rule: a refusal the caller can act on.
- **`character_jolt.cpp/.h`:** the fourth one-TU C++ escape, after `cluster_build`, `physics_cook` and `ragdoll_jolt`.
  - It sits at the `src/` root, because only that directory is globbed for C++.
  - Its header is internal and plain C, and redeclares the opaque JoltC types the way `ragdoll_jolt.h` does.
  - `jolt_character_set_capsule(JPC_CharacterVirtual*, JPC_PhysicsSystem*, JPC_TempAllocatorImpl*, int object_layer, float radius, float half_height, float offset_y, bool check)` uses the same layer filters as JoltC's `ExtendedUpdate`. The layer is passed in as `OBJ_LAYER_DYNAMIC`, not restated.
  - It casts handles with JoltC's `reinterpret_cast` idiom, as `ragdoll_jolt.cpp` does.
  - The JoltC submodule is not touched.

### silent

**`player.c/.h`:**
- **Constants:**
  - `CROUCH_HALF_HEIGHT` 0.295, which makes the capsule 1.15 m;
  - a crouched eye of 1.05 m, giving `look_lift` 0.20 against standing's 0.77;
  - `CROUCH_SPEED` 0.8 m/s, half the walk.
  - These are judgements. People crouch-walk at about 0.6 to 0.9 m/s with the eye at 1.0 to 1.2 m.
- **Fields:**
  - `crouched`: the collider's stance;
  - `crouch_latched`: the toggle;
  - `crouch`: 0..1, the eye's eased stance.
- **Fixed step (`player_update`):**
  - `want = crouch_latched || down("crouch")`.
  - When `want != crouched`, call `character_controller_set_half_height`. On success flip `crouched`; a refused stand retries every step.
  - The speed is `CROUCH_SPEED` whenever crouched, whatever sprint says.
- **Per frame (`player_pre_render`):**
  - A press of `crouch_toggle` flips the latch, and a press of `sprint` clears it. These are read per frame, because an edge read in a fixed step is doubled or dropped when a frame runs two steps or none.
  - `crouch` eases toward the stance: down in 0.2 s, up in 0.3 s, smoothstep-shaped.
- **Why 0.2 s down:** while the eye descends it can stand above the crouched capsule's top. The eye sits a radius (0.28 m) behind the capsule's front, and braking to crouch speed covers under that in 0.2 s, so the eye cannot reach a beam the capsule has just ducked under.
- **Per frame, the eye:**
  - `look_lift = lerp(stand, crouched, smoothstep(crouch))`.
  - `gait->lag_above` is recomputed each frame from the current capsule's top less the eye and the near plane. The init-time line becomes this per-frame one.
  - The bob offset is scaled by `lerp(1, CROUCH_BOB 0.5, crouch)` at the anchor. It is still lowest as a heel strikes.
- **`player_eye_height(p)`:** the body's feet-to-eye, `CAPSULE_REST + rig->look_lift`, for readers that sense the player.
- **Trace:** `--trace-gait` lines gain `crouched` on each step line and `crouch` on each frame line.
- **The header comment** gains crouch, and the action names "crouch" and "crouch_toggle".

**`silent.c`:**
- **`ACTIONS`:**
  - `{"crouch", {INPUT_KEY(LEFT_CONTROL, 1)}}`
  - `{"crouch_toggle", {INPUT_KEY(C, 1), INPUT_PAD(B, 1)}}`

  B is otherwise only `menu_back`, a UI action read while a screen holds the game's input, when the crouch reads zero.
- **`footfall()`** passes `g_player.crouched` to the footsteps.
- **A `--crouch` flag** starts crouched, so a headless run can photograph the crouched eye.
- **The `--help` usage** gains C/Ctrl.

**`footsteps.c/.h`:** `footsteps_fall(..., bool crouched)` multiplies the share by `CROUCH_SHARE` 0.5 (-6 dB), on top of the slower pace's own softening.

**`cat_brain.c`:** the gaze target and the line-of-sight chest use `player_eye_height(p)` rather than `PLAYER_EYE_HEIGHT`. A crouched player is then seen lower, and so is hidden by what is between. Hearing is by speed, which a crouch already lowers.

## As built

**Phase 1, the engine**, went in as planned:
- `character_controller_set_half_height` and `CharacterController.half_height`.
- `character_jolt.cpp/.h`, which build the capsule through `CapsuleShapeSettings` inside `RotatedTranslatedShapeSettings`. Going through the settings means a degenerate capsule is refused by name; `CapsuleShape`'s constructor would assert instead, and Jolt's default assert is a breakpoint.

**Phase 2, silent**, kept the constants as planned, and the user signed off on the feel:
- `CROUCH_HALF_HEIGHT` 0.295, so the capsule is 1.15 m;
- `CROUCH_EYE_HEIGHT` 1.05;
- `CROUCH_SPEED` 0.8;
- `CROUCH_DOWN` 0.2 s and `CROUCH_UP` 0.3 s;
- `CROUCH_BOB` 0.5;
- `CROUCH_SHARE` 0.5 in `footsteps.c`.

What the scripted walk measured (`--pad-script` with B, then LB, `--trace-gait`):
- The eye went 1.62 → 1.05 in 12 frames and back in 18.
- The feet held at 0.3000, the kitchen floor, through every stance change.
- Crouched footsteps played at 0.16 to 0.21 volume, against 0.48 to 0.50 standing.
- LB stood the player up.

Two changes from the plan:
- **Where the crouch share is applied.** It went on the footstep's share at the end of `footsteps_fall`, not its start, because the switch on the fall's kind assigns the share outright.
- **A held crouch while a screen is open.** While a screen holds the game's input the stance is kept. Otherwise a held Ctrl, which reads zero under suppression, would stand the player up behind the backpack's screen.

Screenshots were taken in the kitchen only. The crouched view shows the hood's light strip under the canopy's lip, which the standing eye never sees.

**Phase 3, the `character` group:**
- **The `floor` case** stands still for half a second before it counts steps off the ground. The capsule is put 2 cm above the floor and takes two steps to come down, and those were being counted.
- **The result:** all three arms green. Standing stops at z 1.7000, the face less the radius and the padding. Crouched reaches 4.0. Standing is refused under the beam and allowed past it. The centre and the ground underfoot move 0.0000 mm, with 0 steps off the ground.

## The review

Two thermo-nuclear reviews ran in the background, one on the engine half and one on silent's.

**Applied, silent:**
- **A bug: under a pinned camera the cat saw the player's eye at 0.85 m.** `player_eye_height` read the rig's `look_lift` back, and `camera_rig_set_pose` zeroes it. `--cat-cam` and `--cam-eye` pin every frame, and the cat reads the height in the fixed step that follows. The height is now derived from the stance, `glm_smoothinterp(PLAYER_EYE_HEIGHT, CROUCH_EYE_HEIGHT, crouch_eased)`, and `look_lift` is written from it, at init and every frame. One derivation replaces three, and the hand-written smoothstep and lerps go.
- **`Player.crouched` is gone.** It was a second record of the controller's `half_height`. `crouched(cc)` and `player_crouched(p)` read the controller, and the fixed step now asks for the stance it wants unconditionally, since the engine returns early on an unchanged height.
- **The crouch is gathered** into `crouch_step` (the fixed step) and `crouch_frame` (per frame: the keys, the ease, `look_lift` and `lag_above`). The fields are renamed `crouch_toggled` and `crouch_eased`. Before, one word, `crouch`, had four meanings in the file.
- **The footstep share** for a crouch now sits with the others, straight after the switch.
- **Comments and trace:**
  - the town claim leaves `player.c`, as a fact about the level that the spec already holds;
  - the cat's comment is rewritten so it reads;
  - the frame trace prints the bob the eye actually took.

**Applied, engine:**
- **One verdict helper at module scope, `_probe_verdicts`,** used by both the gait and the character groups. It runs each case once and turns a row the probe never printed into a FAIL naming it. Before, a missing row raised a KeyError and ended the whole run. The probe's `-1` sentinel and its branch are deleted. Re-running the no-op mutation shows the new path: `character-stand-refused FAIL  the probe printed no ('under', 'stood') row`, and the run went on to `feet-kept`.
- **`half_height` says it is engine-owned** and names its setter.
- **One capsule builder:**
  - `jolt_character_capsule` builds the shape: a plain capsule at offset 0 (default density 1000, the shape creation always made), and a translated one otherwise. Creation and resizing both go through it.
  - `jolt_character_set_shape` gives the shape to the character.
  - Creation now releases its own reference, which had leaked since JoltC was first wired in.
- **Jolt's handle casts are stated once,** in `jolt_handles.hpp`, with the single statement of why the C++ files exist. `physics_cook.cpp`, `ragdoll_jolt.cpp` and `character_jolt.cpp` include it; the per-file copies and the lineage sentences are gone.
- **The probe stands on `probe_floor_world`,** split from `ik_probe_world`, which now uses it too. The world is built once with the beam always present, the floor case walks clear of it at x = 15, and the duplicated exit blocks are gone.
- **The probe prints its fixture** (radius, padding, both heights, the beam's faces), so the gate restates none of it.
- **`CHARACTER_LAYER` names the controller's layer once.** Its comment says it must match JoltC's update, and the setter's header says that "fits" counts every body round it, a door the controller passes through included.

**Declined:** saving the capsule's height in the save table. No app saves while crouched. A row in a versioned section is a format change that wants its own migration and arm, which is a save spec's work, not this one's.

**After the review:**
- `--only character,gait,gamepad,ik,ragdoll,gate-docs` passes: 6 of 6 groups, 53 arms.
- The scripted crouch walk matches the run before the review, to four decimals, in eye height, stance, speed and footstep volume. Only the trace's bob column moved, because it now prints the bob the eye took.
