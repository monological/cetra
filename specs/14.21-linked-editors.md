# cetra 14.21: linked editors

## Context

pcb 6.5 shows a compared file as GitHub's split diff: two editors side by side, the version's text
read only on the left and now's on the right, each change padded with filler rows (14.16, 14.19)
so the two show the same rows in the same order, and each run of unchanged lines folded alike on
both. pcb holds the two together itself, by polling every frame in `src/window/sides.c`: it
compares each editor's `ui_editor_scrolls` with the last frame's to guess which was scrolled, the
one with the keys when both were (`lockstep`, `sides.c:277-292`); copies the leader's
`ui_editor_top_row` to the other with `ui_editor_scroll_row` whenever the two differ, as a side
just shown is laid out a frame late; and diffs each side's fold open-states against a mirror of
its own to find which side turned a fold, then turns the fold of the same index on the other
(`folds_follow`, `sides.c:202-220`, the mirror `Sides.folds.open`, `:28-31`, rewritten by
`folds_make`, `:180-189`). A review found this the wrong depth: cetra should hold two editors
together itself, inside its own layout, and turn a fold on both at the moment it is turned. Each
gap, checked against the code:

- **A host can only copy a row between two layouts, and the rows change at a layout.** The layout
  gives each element its place alone and children first (`_place_widgets`, `ui.c:1640-1648`), and
  `ui_layout` ends there (`:1650-1665`). pcb copies in `compare_frame`, after `ui_update`'s layout
  (`ui.c:2413`) and before `ui_build`'s (`ui.c:2628`, then the draw, `:2637`; pcb's order is
  `window.c:102-108`). But an editor's rows above its top change in `_place` itself: new columns or
  tab stops make every row a guess (`ui_editor.c:1207-1211`), the top is held to its line while
  the rows above it are summed again (`:1212`), and a place asked before the layout is taken there
  (`:1215`). So whatever changes the columns -- the side shown, which halves the code editor's
  width; the window resized; a gutter widened -- is drawn for a frame with the two apart, the
  follower given the leader's row from before the layout that moved it. In pcb the side stands
  before the code in the tree (`panes.c:254-255`), so even a follower that took the row in its own
  `place` would read the leader's before the leader is placed.
- **Who leads is guessed from counts compared across frames.** 14.20 counts every scroll asked of a
  view where it is asked (`_pend`, `ui_editor.c:1467`; `_scroll_rows`, `:650`; `_scroll_to`,
  `:1280`), so the editor knows at that moment that it was moved. pcb learns it a frame later by
  keeping each side's count (`Sides.scroll.asked`, `sides.c:24-27`), starting both over when the
  side shows another text (`sides_of`, `:54-60`), and ties two scrolled in one frame by the focus.
- **A fold turned on one is seen on the other a poll later, through a mirror the host keeps.** Every
  turn of an existing fold goes through `_fold_open` (`ui_editor.c:1576-1583`), from four places:
  `ui_editor_set_fold_open` (`:1585-1591`, which vim's `zR` and `zM` call for every fold,
  `ui_editor_vim.c:5040-5041`, and insert mode's Up over a fold, `:3387-3388`); `_fold_carets`
  (`ui_editor.c:1595-1604`: the `open-fold`, `close-fold` and `toggle-fold` commands, vim's `zo`,
  `zc`, `za` through `ui_editor_fold_carets`, `ui_editor_vim.c:5043`, and its `'foldopen'` after a
  motion and an undo, `:5066-5067`, `:2225`, `:3230`); a press on a closed fold's row
  (`ui_editor.c:1764-1766`); and an edit at a caret in one (`_open_for_edit`, `:1608-1611`). None
  tells anything outside the editor. pcb finds the turn by comparing both sides' open-states with
  its mirror, so the mirror has to be made again with every `ui_editor_set_folds` it gives.

What pcb keeps doing is not in question: it makes the folds from the hunks, with their labels and
which are open (`folds_of`, `folds_make`, `sides.c:95-197`); it pads each change with fillers at the
rows its lines wrap to (`fillers_follow`, `:245-261`); it gives both sides the larger text's gutter
digits (`:298-302`); and it says which side has the keys.

Branch and spec: `14.21-linked-editors`, off cetra's master (`d94032f3`).

## The design

In `ui_editor.h`, after `ui_editor_scrolls`:

- **`void ui_editor_link(UIElement* el, UIElement* other)`**: `el` held to `other` row for row and
  fold for fold, `other` leading until a scroll is asked of `el`; NULL lets `el` go. Linking an
  editor already linked lets its partner go first, so a link is a pair and each editor is in one at
  most. An element not an editor, or an editor linked to itself, is refused and logged, and the
  links stay as they were. A mutator, so void: every refusal is a bad argument.
  - **The editor's, not the text's**, kept across the texts it shows, as its look and
    `ui_editor_scrolls` are. What holds two editors together is where they stand on screen; a link
    parked with a place would come back with a text, which no host asks for, and go with the text
    when it is freed. The folds it pairs are each place's own, so a link pairs whichever folds the
    two hold now.
  - **A pair, not an object**: no `create_`/`free_` for the host to keep in step with two elements
    either of which may be freed first. Each `UIEditor` holds its partner and whether it leads.
  - **Two at most.** A split diff has two sides; three in a ring is not asked for.
- **Which leads**: the one a scroll was last asked of, counted where 14.20 counts one -- `_pend`
  (`ui_editor_reveal`, `_scroll_place`, `_scroll_line`, `_scroll_row`, and the reveal after an
  action through `_follow`), `_scroll_rows` (the wheel, the bar's track, a mode's
  `ui_editor_scroll_rows`, a drag past the view's edge) and `_scroll_to` (the bar's thumb). One
  helper, `_asked`, counts the scroll and takes the lead, so the two cannot drift. Taken when asked,
  never when a pending one is taken at the layout (`_take_pending`), so a place asked before an
  editor is laid out leads from the ask, and nothing the layout does makes an editor lead. Two
  scrolled between layouts: the later leads, which in one input pass is the keys' reveal, as
  `_update` takes the bar and the wheel (`ui.c:2436-2470`) before the keys (`:2537`) -- the tie pcb
  breaks by the focus. A no-op reveal leads as well, at the row both already stand at.
- **An editor shown another text follows** (`_show`): the other leads until a scroll is asked of
  the one shown. A side shown the version's text comes to where the code is, as pcb's
  `sides_of` sets by hand. A host showing both of a pair new texts between two layouts sees only
  the last order, since nothing moves until the layout. Showing NULL leaves the link and holds
  nothing, as an editor showing no text is never laid out.
- **The row taken at the layout, by the second of the pair placed.** `_place` stamps the editor with
  the layout that placed it (`ui_system_layouts`, below) and, at its end, when its partner carries
  the same stamp and the same `UISystem`, holds the pair: both are placed, their columns, gutters
  and pending places taken, so the follower is put at the leader's exact row -- `_exact` and
  `_top_row` on the leader as `ui_editor_top_row` reads it, `_exact` and `_scroll_row` on the
  follower as a `PENDING_ROW` is taken -- whenever the two differ. In the tree's order either way:
  the side before the code, as pcb has it, or after. Not counted as a scroll, and never a lead.
  - **Every layout, not only after a scroll**, since that is where the rows change: new columns,
    fillers set, a fold turned, a line wrapped by the budget. The invariant pcb holds by copying
    every frame the two differ is the editor's.
  - **Only at the layout, not also when the leader is scrolled.** Every row change lands by a
    layout, and the draw follows the layout in `ui_build`; a host may rearrange texts, folds and
    fillers between two layouts and see the pair settle once. The cost: a press on the follower in
    the same input pass as a wheel on the leader lands on the rows of the last layout, a frame's
    input, as pcb's has it today.
  - **No widget op for it.** A pass after `_place_widgets` would need a `placed` call in
    `UIWidgetOps` and a second walk of the tree; the second of the pair placed knows the pair is
    whole, which needs only the stamp.
  - **Held to the follower's own rows**, as `ui_editor_scroll_row` holds them: a follower with fewer
    rows stands at its last, and one asked a scroll then leads from there.
- **Hidden, removed, freed.** A hidden editor (`ui_set_hidden`, 14.18) is not placed
  (`ui.c:1641`), nor is one taken out and waiting to be freed (`_leave`, `ui.c:1403-1411`), so it
  carries no stamp of the layout, its partner is not held to it, and it is not held to its partner.
  Its lead is kept: shown again, the next layout holds the pair from whichever was last asked. An
  editor freed (`ui_editor_free`) lets its partner go. Two editors on different screens are never
  placed by one layout, so their rows stay their own; their folds still pair, which harms nothing,
  and a screen test at the link would need an accessor for little.
- **Folds turned on both at once.** `_fold_open` gains the editor (`_fold_turn(ed, f, open)`) and
  its four callers above go through it; having turned the fold, it turns the fold of the same index
  on the partner through `_fold_open` itself, so the turn goes no further. Whatever turned it -- a
  command, a `z` key, a click, a host's `ui_editor_set_fold_open`, an edit opening the fold at a
  caret -- and whether or not the partner is shown, as a fold is state, not layout.
  - **By index, while the two hold as many folds**: pcb sets them in pairs, the i-th of each over
    counterpart lines. With counts apart -- an edit has taken a fold's lines out on one side, and
    the host has not set them again -- a turn turns none on the other, silently: it is a moment
    between the host's sets, not a bad argument. Pairing by lines was rejected: the two texts'
    lines are the host's to map, through its hunks.
  - **`ui_editor_set_folds` touches only its own editor**: the host sets both, each from its own
    text, and the open-states it gives are its own reading of the pair.
  - A turn moves the partner's view only as a fold turned in it would (`_top_onto_fold`); the next
    layout holds the rows.
- **What stays the host's**: making the folds, their labels and which are open; the fillers; the
  gutter digits; which side has the keys; when to link and when to let go. The link reads none of
  them.
- **`ui_editor_scrolls` stays public.** pcb no longer needs it, but its contract is general -- a host
  that holds views together its own way, a status line, a probe -- and `ui-editor-scrolls` holds
  it. Its comment stands; `docs/ui.md` says the link leads by the same counting. Whether to take it
  out of the public header now that pcb, its one caller, drops it is the user's call.

Beside them, private:

- **`uint64_t ui_system_layouts(const UISystem* ui)`** in `ui_internal.h`, beside
  `ui_system_engine`: the layouts run so far, so two elements stamped with the same count were
  placed by one layout. `UISystem.layouts`, counted at the start of `ui_layout`.
- **`UIElement* ui_editor_linked(const UIElement* el)`** in `ui_editor_hooks.h`: the editor `el`
  is linked to, NULL for none and for anything not an editor. For the probe's `freed` and
  `refused`; no host needs it.

## Tests

Probe cases in `apps/gametest/src/ui_editor_probe.c`, each read by an arm in the `ui` group with
`doc_flags` and its docstring in `run_ui_gate`'s list, built as 14.19's `top_row_level` is: two
editors 300 by 220 in a row on a screen of their own (`scene_alone`, `fixed_editor`), on two texts
of 200 lines, line 40 wrapping to three rows on the left where its counterpart takes one, the right
padded with two fillers above its line 41, so standing level is a count of rows and not a line.
Passes are `editor_pass`, or `layout_only` where one layout has to be told from the next.

- **`editor-link`** -> `ui-editor-link`, the left linked to the right:
  - `level`: the right scrolled by the wheel to line 100 and a part of a row, one pass leaves the
    left at the same `ui_editor_top_row`, and lines 101 and 102 drawn at the same height
    (`rows_level`).
  - `order`: a row asked of the leader before it is laid out on a text just shown (pending), one
    layout (`layout_only`) puts the follower at the row the leader took, with the follower first in
    the tree and again with it last.
  - `lead`: each of `ui_editor_scroll_row`, a wheel's step, a drag of the bar and a key moving the
    caret, on the left, makes it lead: the right stands at the left's row after the next pass.
  - `pending`: a line asked of the left before it is laid out, then a row asked of the right: the
    next layout leaves both at the right's row, the left's place taken and then held.
  - `uncounted`: the follower's `ui_editor_scrolls` is the same after it was moved, and it does not
    lead: a wheel on the leader after it still moves both.
  - `shown`: the left shown another text standing level with the right's, laid out and not drawn,
    stands at the right's row from its first layout, the right not moved, though the left was the
    last scrolled.
  - `hidden`: the right hidden, a row asked of it, the left stays where it was; shown again, the
    next pass puts the left at the right's row. The left hidden and the right scrolled, the left
    shown again takes the right's row at its first layout.
  - `freed`: the right taken out and freed, `ui_editor_linked` of the left is NULL and its wheel
    scrolls it alone; the left linked to a third, then to a fourth, the third is let go.
  - `refused`: linked to itself and to a panel, logged, `ui_editor_linked` unchanged; NULL lets go
    of both sides.
- **`editor-link-folds`** -> `ui-editor-link-folds`, both given three folds over counterpart lines,
  closed:
  - `command`: `close-fold` and `open-fold` run by name (`ui_editor_run`) at a caret in the left's
    second fold turn the right's second, and only it.
  - `call`: `ui_editor_set_fold_open` on the right's third turns the left's third.
  - `click`: a press on the left's closed first fold's row opens the right's first.
  - `edit`: typing at a caret in the right's closed fold opens the left's.
  - `vim`: in the vim mode, `zM` closes all three on both, `zo` opens the one at the cursor on both.
  - `counts`: the left given two folds, a turn on either turns none on the other.
  - `set`: `ui_editor_set_folds` on the left leaves the right's folds and open-states as they were.
  - `level`: a fold closed above the top on the follower, one pass leaves both at the same row,
    lines below drawn level.

## As built

### Phase 1: the link

- **The pair**: `UIEditor.partner`, `leads` and `placed`. `ui_editor_link` lets go of both editors'
  partners (`_unlink`, also what `ui_editor_free` does first) and pairs them, `other` leading.
  Refused and logged: an element not an editor, on either side, and an editor linked to itself.
- **The lead**: `_asked` counts the scroll and takes the lead, in place of 14.20's three counts in
  `_pend`, `_scroll_rows` and `_scroll_to`. `_show` hands the lead to the partner before the text is
  taken, so a text shown, NULL among them, follows.
- **The hold**: `_place` ends by stamping `placed` with `ui_system_layouts(ui)` and calling
  `_hold`, which returns unless the partner shows a text, was placed by the same system and carries
  the same stamp: so the second of the pair placed holds it, and a hidden, removed or other
  screen's partner, never placed by this layout, holds nothing. The follower is put at the leader's
  row when the two differ. `ui_editor_top_row`'s body is now `_exact_top_row`, and a
  `PENDING_ROW`'s taking `_exact_scroll_row`, which the hold calls on the two sides, so the hold and
  the public calls count rows one way.
- **`ui_system_layouts`** in `ui_internal.h`, `UISystem.layouts`, counted at the start of
  `ui_layout`, as the layout's virtual rows may lay a screen out three times in one call and every
  editor placed once at its end.
- **The folds**: `_fold_turn(ed, f, open)` turns the fold and the partner's of the same index
  through `_fold_open`, while the two hold as many folds and the partner shows a text.
  `ui_editor_set_fold_open`, `_fold_carets` (the commands, vim's `zo`, `zc`, `za` and its
  `'foldopen'`, and `_open_for_edit`) and the press go through it. A toggle hands the partner the
  state the fold took, not a toggle of its own.
- **Two cases**, after 14.20's in `ui_editor_probe.c`, on a pair built by `pair_scene` (`side_text`,
  the left's line 40 wrapping to 3 rows at 41 columns, 2 fillers above the right's line 41):
  - **`editor-link`**, 9 flags. `order` builds the scene twice, the right first in the tree the
    second time, as `scene_alone` gives each its own screen.
  - **`editor-link-folds`**, 8 flags. `edit` gives the focus a pass before typing: an element the
    app focused takes no keys in the pass it was focused in, so the first try typed nothing.
- **Falsified**, each committed first:
  - the follower taking the row in its own `_place`, the stamp not looked at: `order` fails (the
    left at 0, the right at 120), and `hidden` with it, as a hidden leader then holds its partner;
  - the lead taken in `_take_pending`: `pending` fails (both at 52, the left's line 50, against 80);
  - the follower moved through `_pend`: `uncounted` fails. `lead` does not, against the plan: every
    one of its steps asks a scroll of the side it means to lead, and the follower is only moved
    after the leader is placed, so a lead the hold takes is taken back by the next ask;
  - the partner's fold turned only from `ui_editor_set_fold_open`: `command`, `click`, `edit` and
    `vim` fail, `vim` as `zo` goes through `_fold_carets`;
  - `_show` leaving the lead as it was: `shown` fails (both pulled to the new text's 0).
- **Gates**: `--only ui,gate-docs` passes, 118 arms.
- **The look**: `apps/canvas --editor cetra/src/shore_runup.h -x -f 10` rendered twice is
  byte-identical (noise floor 0), and byte-identical to master's.
