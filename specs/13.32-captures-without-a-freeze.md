# Spec 13.32 -- light captures that do not freeze the game (then 13.34, a loading screen)

## Context
silent freezes at startup: macOS shows the beachball for about 16 s. Measured on the basement
branch (13.31):
- **GI capture.** The home's GI volume grew from 396 probes to 792 when the basement was dug, and
  the engine captures every probe of a volume's first sweep in ONE frame
  (`gi_volume.c:494-507`). That frame took about 7 s before the basement and 14 s after.
- **Reflection probes.** About 2 s more, also in one frame (`probe_set.c:205`).
- **Before the first frame.** About 6 s more: the scene build, texture loads, shader compiles,
  and the first frame's shadows.

A sample of the GI frame (5,094 main-thread samples, every other thread asleep):
- **The wall test.** Half the samples are the second capture each probe takes to test whether it
  sits inside a wall (`gi_volume.c:313`). That capture renders a fully lit, shaded image, but the
  projection reads only its DEPTH (`gi_volume.c:243-251`). Spec 13.24 planned "a 16² depth-only
  cube"; the build went to a full shaded capture.
- **The light grid.** 54% is the 16x8x24 = 3,072-cell light grid, rebuilt on the CPU for every
  16x16 cube face (`render.c:1441`). Spec 9.7 recorded this cost and left it.
- **Draw submission** is 34%.
- **Emissive panels** are re-fitted for every face, 4%.

The user's point: a game must never freeze its window. Unreal bakes static GI offline, and Lumen
spreads its updates over frames. DDGI in production (Majercik et al. 2021, as shipped in RTXGI,
UE4 and Unity) classifies probes from rays it already traces, and initializes over five frames.

The user chose two specs:
- **13.32:** captures that are cheap and paced, so no frame stalls.
- **13.34, after it:** a proper loading screen for the time before the first frame.

Nothing earlier is thrown away:
- The probes, the lighting atlas, streaming, kept copies, residency, the readiness waits and the
  25% back-face rule all stay.
- Phase 1 finishes 13.24's own plan.
- Phase 2 pays 9.7's recorded debt.
- Phase 3 reverses one decision, the one-frame first sweep, and does it for windowed runs only.

## Phase 1 -- the wall test captures depth only
- **The mode.** `scene_capture_faces`' `back_faces` bool becomes a mode: shaded, or back-face
  depth.
- **What depth mode draws.** `engine_render_scene` builds the frustum, the CullView and the face
  matrices, masks colour, and draws only what writes depth today:
  - The opaque lane, through the existing `_submit_lanes(..., SUBMIT_PASS_DEPTH_ONLY)`. Every item
    keeps its own program, so positions and discards (cutouts, offset hooks, the POM silhouette,
    fur programs) are identical by construction. A program with no depth-only pass (shape, app
    programs) shades with colour masked, so its depth is identical too.
  - The shadow catcher, whose depth comes from a shadow-dependent discard. Its whole block
    (`render.c:1733-1816`: binds, `surfaceMode`, polygon offset, culling off) moves into a
    function, and both paths call it unchanged.
- **What depth mode skips:** the emissive panels, the light build, the sky, the transparent lane
  and the shading.
- **Unchanged:**
  - culling (GL_FRONT, with the per-item double-sided toggle), GL_LESS, LOD and the
    capture-hidden rules;
  - the displacement and skinning uniforms.
- **The lean prepass program is NOT used here.** It is opt-in and silent's materials have never
  been through it. It is added only if `sample` shows submission still dominating.
- **Check.**
  - The `--stream-probe` GI digests are identical to phase 0: the classification alpha matches,
    and RGB is untouched.
  - Goldens at 0 px.
  - `--only stream`.
  - Time: about half the sweep is expected to go.

## Phase 2 -- a GI capture builds its lights once, not once per face
- **Once per burst.** `scene_capture_begin` runs `scene_build_emissive_lights` BEFORE its shadow
  pass (`render.c:2009-2036`), so a new panel gets its shadow layer.
- **Once per probe.** `scene_capture_faces` builds the light list before the face loop, through a
  capture form of the gather in `light_cluster.c`:
  - **The sphere test.** The frustum and depth culls (`light_cluster.c:324`, `:348`) become one
    test against the capture's sphere: the probe's position, radius `far * sqrt(3)`. The
    `radius == 0` skip and the always-included lights (radius < 0) stay.
  - **Past the 128-light cap.** Today's `break` drops lights in scene order (`:327-333`). The
    capture form keeps the nearest 128 to the probe, and warns once per capture.
  - **One shared range.** Every cluster word points at the same range, `(0 << 12) | N`
    (`light_cluster.h:86-90`), in scene order. `cluster_params` is the same for all six faces.
  - **Decals.** Their frustum and depth rejects (`:676-683`) are dropped. Every live decal
    reaching the sphere is marked everywhere, and the shader's exact box test makes that
    harmless.
- **Safe across six faces.** Every packed light field is world space (`light_cluster.c:196`,
  `208`, `257-259`, `276`). The shadow layers and tile lookups come from the burst's one shadow
  pass. The face loop's `engine_render_scene` skips the panel fit and the grid build while the
  capture holds them.
- **GI only.** Reflection-probe faces are large (1024² at 2x supersampling, `probe.h:18`). There,
  every fragment would loop over all the lights instead of its cell's few, trading CPU for GPU.
  They keep the per-face grid, and phase 0's numbers say whether that needs revisiting.
- **What changes on screen**, both corrections:
  - A light with no range keeps its faint tail past its cull radius, which cell edges cut off
    before.
  - Lights the overflowing capture pool dropped ("225 of 3072 clusters starved") are back.
- **Check.**
  - Measure the move against the noise floor: the digests, the GI goldens (`cornell_box`
    `--gi-volume`) and silent's rooms.
  - Show the crops, and re-bake only if a golden moves.
  - The capture overflow warning is gone.
  - `--only stream`, plus probe, decals and emissive.

## Phase 3 -- no frame stalls on a capture, in a window
- **The unit: time when windowed, no limit when headless.** A probe count cannot serve both
  sides:
  - The gates need fast convergence. `stream-once` needs about 10 probes a frame, and
    `cornell_box`'s 256 probes must converge in 30 frames.
  - A frame needs few. Silent at 16 ms would take about 4 probes a frame.
  So the split follows the fixed-step clock's (`engine.c:3079`): headless runs keep one-frame
  convergence (every golden, gate and headless silent run unchanged), and windowed runs spend at
  most `engine->capture_budget_ms` a frame on captures.
- **How the budget is spent.**
  - One budget a frame, shared by GI and the reflection probes.
  - The burst's own shadow pass and draw-list build count against it.
  - At least one unit always runs, so everything converges.
  - A setting an app may change at any time. Silent keeps it large while its screen is black and
    small once it fades in; 13.34's loading screen will do the same.
  - The `opened` / `streamed` and first-capture exceptions are deleted; the budget replaces them.
- **Reflection probes by face.** One probe is six large shaded faces plus a prefilter: about
  200-500 ms on silent. So a probe's capture spans frames, one face a unit, with the cube held
  between frames until it is projected.
- **A forcing flag for the paced path.**
  - `--capture-budget-ms` (render, silent) forces the windowed path headless.
  - A `stream-paced` arm in the `stream` group holds that a paced run converges to the SAME
    digests as the one-frame run. Captures run at render time 0 and openings write outright, so
    the frame a probe was taken in cannot matter.
  - This is the only test of the windowed path.
- **silent's fade waits for the probe set too** (`set->ready`), not only the GI at the eye
  (`silent.c:898`). Otherwise reflections pop in after the fade.
- **The user looks at it:** launch with no beachball, the time to a lit frame, walking up to the
  mansion while it captures (no hitch), and the rooms' lighting.

## Spec 13.34 -- a loading screen (after 13.32)
**Why.** After 13.32 the window still blocks about 6 s before its first frame:
- `create_engine` shows the window (`engine.c:599`);
- `game_run` runs the whole `on_init` synchronously (`game.c:336`);
- frame 0 compiles shader variants and places every cached shadow tile;
- nothing pumps events until frame 0's swap (`engine.c:3394`).

There is no loading phase or ready state today. GL 4.1 on one context rules out Unreal's loading
thread, so loading runs in short steps on the main thread, with a loading screen drawn and window
events pumped between them.

**Branch.** `13.34-loading-screen` off 13.32's branch, once the user signs 13.32 off. Its own
spec file is committed first.

**Phase 0 -- measure.** Silent's startup, step by step in milliseconds: window shown, each
`on_init` step, frame 0, settled. Forest's `startup_ms` is the pattern. The numbers decide which
steps need splitting further, and whether the cached tiles' first-frame burst (`shadow.c:1719`)
goes under the 13.32 budget too.

**Phase 1 -- load steps in the game framework (optional: an app without them is unchanged).**
- **The table.** `game_set_loading(game, &(GameLoading){steps, count, user})`: named steps, each
  `bool run(Game*, void*)`. A step returns true when it is done. A step may wait on textures only.
  Lighting advances only inside `engine_run`, so a step waiting on lighting would never finish.
- **The loop.** `game_run` runs the steps in a minimal loop before `engine_run`, as `apps/splash`
  does, each frame:
  - poll events;
  - run one step;
  - drain async textures;
  - bind framebuffer 0 at the framebuffer's size and clear;
  - `ui_build` with the loop's own dt, then `ui_draw_list_render`;
  - swap.
  The UI's overlay callback reads `delta_time` and `total_frames`, which do not advance here
  (`ui.c:1123`, `ui_draw.c:703`).
- **Hooks wait.** No `on_update` or `on_pre_render` runs until the steps are done: silent's hooks
  dereference what `on_init` builds.
- **Headless** runs the steps straight through, drawing nothing.

**Phase 2 -- settle behind the screen.**
- **Real engine frames** run after the steps, with the simulation held by a framework flag (not
  `game->paused`, which is the app's). The loading screen is opaque on top of them.
- **The overlay.** The screen takes the engine's single overlay slot. The app's own overlay
  (silent's prompt, gametest's UI) is saved and put back when the screen lifts.
- **Settled is one engine call**, over checks that mostly exist:
  - textures drained (`async_loader_is_busy`);
  - every shader variant the scene needs compiled. They are warmed through
    `scene_build_emissive_lights`, `engine_build_draw_list` and
    `engine_resolve_material_variants`, a few a frame, under a budget that applies only while
    settling.
  - the panels derived and ranked;
  - the cached lights at the camera holding their tiles (`shadow_tiles_cover`);
  - the GI at the camera swept (`gi_world_ready_in`);
  - the probe set ready.
- **`scene_capture_ready` gains two conditions:** the panels are derived, and the resolver is
  finished. A first capture is kept forever, so one taken on stale variants or before the panels
  exist would be wrong for good.
- **The budget is large while settling**, since nothing is on screen.
- **`-f N` counts frames after the screen lifts**, through an offset the framework sets when it
  lifts.

**Phase 3 -- silent loads through it.**
- **The steps.** `on_init` splits at its seams, with the kits held in statics between steps:
  - materials
  - the street kit
  - the mansion kit
  - `kit_finish`
  - candles and doors
  - the cat
  - audio
  - sky and post
  - player and physics optimize
- **The frame-2 install goes.** It waited for the emissive panels (`silent.c:884-889`).
  `scene_capture_ready` now asks that itself, so the GI volumes and probes are installed in a
  load step.
- **The cat** attaches before the warm-up, so its skinned variant compiles while loading.
- **The fade** from black starts when the screen lifts.
- **The look** is silent's own (black, its type face, a quiet bar). The user looks at it.

**Phase 4 -- docs.**
- AGENTS.md: the Game Framework section, and silent's row.
- `docs/cli-reference.md`: what `-f` counts.

### The review (thermo-nuclear and simplify, six agents)
Applied:
- **The budget is one call**, `capture_budget_take(budget, first)`, asked only with a unit in
  hand. Each kind takes its first unit a frame, so neither starves the other, and a frame with
  nothing to capture never syncs. The clock starts at the frame's first ask; GI asks before its
  burst opens, so the burst's shadow pass counts.
- **Reflection probes.** One burst a frame for every face the frame takes. One loop for a set and
  a world of one probe, with `faces_captured` the one record of a capture under way (the
  set's `capturing` is gone). A capture under way waits, keeping its faces, while its box may not
  be captured, and one whose probe leaves residency is dropped with its cube. The
  environment-only re-prefilter is its own function; the wrapper that drained the loader was
  unreachable and is deleted.
- **The light grid.** A capture gathers its lights once by its sphere, both kinds
  (`light_cluster_capture_begin`), in one pass into a sorted array of the nearest 128, no
  allocation; each face assigns its cells (`light_cluster_capture_face`), which decides the shared
  list for itself. The camera's build lost its `capture` flag. The dead "uncullable" branch and
  the second copy of the decal mask packing are gone.
- **`residency_loaded_where`** is shared by `gi_world_ready_in` and `probe_set_ready_in`, and
  `scene_lighting_ready_in` asks both; silent uses it, and sets its budget once at init and once
  as the view comes up rather than every frame.
- **The capture path.** A bad face range is refused, not clamped. The supersample target lives on
  `IBLResources` from a cube's first face to its last, rather than allocated per call.
  `_render_capture_depth` shares `_scene_pass_prepare` with the scene pass and unbinds after
  itself, and the variants are resolved once a burst, in `scene_capture_begin`.
- **Comments** that had gone stale or named their callers, and the reason given for the
  `glFinish`, which had claimed a capture is mostly GPU work against the phase 1 profile.
- **`--capture-budget-ms`** on render goes through `_ranged_arg`.
- **The gates.** The budget is 0.001 ms, which lets exactly one capture of each kind through a
  frame, so the pace is the same on every run. `stream-paced-walk` runs the teleport walk at that
  pace, leaving room 0 with a probe four faces in and a volume part swept, and holds the end's
  resident digests to the unbudgeted walk's. `stream-paced-single` paces a world of one probe.

Found on the way: **a volume evicted mid-sweep came back with another volume's texels in its
slot.** Its tiles do not cover its rectangle -- an irradiance tile is 10 px in an 18 px column --
and its last holder, room 9's 5x4x5 grid, had written between them. Nothing sampled them; the
slot's digest did, and `stream-paced-walk` failed on it. An opening sweep now clears its slot
first (`lighting_atlas_clear`). 13.24's eviction could reach this too.

Found by the user, running silent windowed: **"Cached shadow stores full" printed every frame of
the home's sweep.** The store pool's warning is an edge, said when a pass runs out and re-armed
when one does not. A capture's shadow pass draws every face a mover reaches, in view or not, and
runs out; the camera's then does not. With a capture burst every frame, the two spent and re-armed
the one latch every frame. Master took its captures in one frame, so it said so once.
- **The first fix was wrong, and measured so.** Keeping captures from taking store cells at all
  stopped the warning, but drew every off-screen face a mover reaches whole in each capture: GI
  capture 35.1-35.4 ms a frame against 24.4-25.1, the frame 78.4-78.6 ms against 66.2-68.9 (silent
  headless at 0.001 ms, 120 frames, two runs each). The cells were not changing hands between the
  two passes, as assumed: a face keeps the cell it holds, so the copies a capture takes stay good
  from frame to frame. Only the warning was flipping.
- **The fix:** the warning and its latch are the camera's pass's alone. 118 warnings in 120 frames

Not applied, and why:
- **GPU timer queries in place of the `glFinish`.** A frame-late estimate per kind would never
  sync, but a budget that is wrong for a frame after a change of scene is a hitch of its own; the
  sync happens only on frames that capture.
- **One burst shared by GI and the probes.** Nothing in a burst depends on its kind, but a probe
  waits on the volumes its box touches, so the two kinds rarely capture in one frame, and sharing
  would put one burst across two modules and merge the profiler rows `gpu-gated` reads.
- **A shadow array kept for captures**, and **re-uploading a GI probe's lights only when they
  change**: both real costs, smaller than a capture, and each a cache to keep right.
- **The panels built once a frame** and **`engine_render_scene`'s per-view setup split from its
  passes**: each a restructure of the scene pass beyond this spec.
- **The lean depth program for the wall test**: phase 1's condition for it is met (the depth
  capture is mostly submission), but it is opt-in per material and silent's have never been
  through it.
