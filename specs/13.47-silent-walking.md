# Spec 13.47: walking in silent

## Context

Silent's walker (`apps/silent/src/player.c`) is a Jolt capsule with the camera on its head at 1.62 m. The user wants walking that feels real, "as good as possible", and chose these five pieces first. A full body, or arms, may come later and is not part of this spec.

**What is wrong today:**
- **The eye moves in whole 60 Hz steps.**
  - `on_pre_render` drops `alpha` (`silent.c:1238`). The rig is anchored at the latest step's position (`player.c:124`).
  - The look turns at the frame rate but the body moves at 60 Hz. The user's display is a 120 Hz MacBook.
  - The rig and the flashlight beam are clocked by `sim_clock.delta`, which is 0 on every frame that ran no step. So the arrow-key look and the beam step at 60 Hz too.
- **Every stair snaps the eye.** Jolt's stair step (`character.c:361`) lifts the capsule a whole riser in one step.
- **Start and stop are instant.** `player.c:89` sets full speed in one step.
- **There is no head bob.**
- **There are no footsteps.** Silent has no footstep sound at all.

**Facts the design rests on:**
- **Step order.** The app's `on_update` runs before the controller moves the capsule. So in `on_update`, `entity->position` is the previous step's result.
- **Headless:** exactly one step per frame, and alpha stays about 0.
- **Velocity readback.** `character_controller_get_velocity` returns the velocity we commanded, not the one achieved.
- **Colliders carry no surface or material**, and raycast normals are zero.
- **Voices have no pitch control.**
- **Jolt on these stairs.** On 0.19/0.25 stairs a 0.28 m capsule is always carried by the nosings. It moves up by a mix of partial pops and continuous ride-ups, not one clean pop per riser. So nothing may depend on detecting risers.

## Phase 0: measure the stairs first

- **`--trace-gait` in silent:** one line per step with frame, feet, dy, dh, grounded and the commanded speed. Later phases add the gait's fields to the same line.
- **Scripted runs.** Using `--pad-script` and `--player-at`, walk the mansion's great-hall stair (15 risers of 0.19) and the home's cellar flight (12 of 0.225), up and down. Do each at 1.6 m/s and at about 0.46 m/s.
- **Report:**
  - how Jolt spreads each rise (pops against ride-ups);
  - the horizontal shove from `walk_stairs_min_step_forward`, which is 0.02 m and reachable through `CharacterControllerConfig`;
  - the real grade along each flight.

  The filter constants below are chosen from this.

## Phase 1: smooth motion (silent only)

- **Interpolation (`player.c`):**
  - `Player.step_from` is set to the spawn position in `player_init`. A windowed first frame can run no step.
  - It is latched as the first line of `player_update`, which is the state before this step moves the capsule.
  - `player_pre_render` anchors the rig at `lerp(step_from, entity->position, alpha)`.
  - `player_view_feet()` is that same position at the feet. It is used by per-frame code: `lake_update`'s wake, the map arrow, and the footstep surface lookup.
  - `player_feet` stays the raw simulation position for `on_update`'s users: the cat, `basement_hold` and the finds.
- **Render dt.** It is the change in `game->time + game->accumulator` from the last frame: 1/60 headless and smooth windowed. It goes to `camera_rig_update` (`player.c:125`) and `lights_update`'s beam lag. Nothing in the simulation changes.
- **Speed ramp (`player_update`):**
  - The speed ramps as a scalar: about 5 m/s² up and 8 down.
  - The heading turns fast, about 10 rad/s, so a mouse turn does not skid.
  - Reversing by more than about 100° brakes through zero.
  - In the air, a little control.
  - The commanded horizontal velocity is kept in `Player`, not read back from Jolt. The wrong "solved velocity" comment at `silent.c:1099` is fixed.
- **Flags:** `--no-interp` for an A/B.

**Look:** the user walks and turns windowed, with and without `--no-interp`.

## Phase 2: the gait, and stairs

**New pure engine module `cetra/src/gait.c/.h`.** It has no GL, no physics, no clock and no game type, which is the camera rig's charter. It is public through the CMake allowlist. It takes DISPLACEMENTS rather than positions, and `gait_reset` handles teleports and origin shifts. It has two halves, so the simulation never depends on the frame rate.

- **`gait_step(g, dpos, grounded, target_speed, dt)`**, from the top of `player_update` with the exact last step:
  - **grade:** |dy|/|dh|, filtered over horizontal distance (about 0.5 m, two treads), not over time;
  - **stair weight:** `w = smoothstep(0.25, 0.5, grade)`;
  - **pace:** `min(1, v_climb / (unscaled_target * grade))`, with a tunable floor (about 0.6 m/s). A person keeps a climb rate: about 0.35 m/s up and 0.45 down when walking, 0.8 when jogging. Pace is computed from the target before scaling, so nothing loops back.
  - airborne time and the impact speed of a landing.

  `player_update` scales its target speed by `pace`.
- **`gait_frame(g, dpos, grounded, yaw, dt)`**, per rendered frame on the interpolated displacement with the render dt:
  - **Phase:** `dphase = (1-w)·dh/L(speed) + w·|dy|/0.18`. The step length is `L = 0.40 + 0.25·speed` m, which gives about 120 steps a minute at 1.6 m/s and 163 at 3.4. On stairs, steps come from height climbed, about one a riser, however Jolt spreads the rise. It is linear in the increments, so splitting the path into frames does not change it.
  - **Eye smoothing:** a critically damped spring on all grounded vertical motion. It holds at most 0.25 m of lag below and about 0.10 m above, so the eye stays under the capsule's top under a low soffit. Airborne, it decays fast (about 0.02 s) rather than snapping. If phase 0 shows Jolt's forward shove, a small clamp on horizontal smoothing (about 3 cm) is added too.
  - **Landing:** a dip of about 0.03·v_impact, at most 8 cm, recovering in about 0.25 s, plus a landing footfall.
  - **Footfalls:** at most one per frame. They are emitted when the phase crosses the heel strike, which is the bob's trough. There is also a soft settle step when stopping mid-swing, after which the phase settles to rest.
  - **Shuffle steps when turning on the spot:** about one per 70° turned, at most one per 0.35 s.
  - **Outputs:** `smooth_offset`, `bob_offset` (phase 3) and this frame's footfall, if any.
- **Silent wires it in `player_pre_render`:**
  - The rig anchor is the interpolated position plus `smooth_offset` plus `bob_offset`.
  - The camera rig is not changed.
  - The reach cone uses the steady eye (interpolated position plus `look_lift`) kept in `Player`, so the E prompt never flickers at the edge.
  - The gait runs under a pin too (`--cat-cam`, `--cam-eye`); only the anchor write is skipped.
- **Flags:** `--no-gait`, and `--trace-gait` gains phase, offsets, pace and footfalls.

**Look:** the user walks both flights, the porches and the terrace flights. The pace on stairs and its floor are an A/B with them.

**Then the `gait` gate group.** It runs `gametest --gait-probe <case>`, pure, before the engine exists, like the rig's probe cases. Arms:
- split invariance (60 Hz, 120 Hz, irregular frames);
- cadence: 120 and 163 steps a minute;
- rest settles to exactly 0 with the phase at rest;
- the spring's step response, its asymmetric clamp and the airborne decay;
- a synthetic flight: footfalls = risers ±1, and the steady pace in closed form;
- landing: one footfall and the dip in closed form;
- at most one footfall per frame through a 0.25 s hitch.

## Phase 3: the head bob

- **In `gait_frame`:**
  - Vertical: `-A·(1-cos 2πφ)/2`, where 0 is standing height at mid-stance and the trough is at heel strike. A is about 2 cm walking and 3.5 cm jogging, roughly half the body's real 4 to 5 cm and 6 to 8 cm.
  - Sway: about 1 cm peak to peak toward the stance leg, once a stride, along the body's right.
  - The amplitude eases with speed (about 0.25 s) and fades out on stairs by `w`.
  - No roll and no pitch.
- **`bob_scale`:** zero is bit-identical to no bob. Silent writes `--bob X` (default 1) times the rig's `shake_player_scale`, so the engine's `reduce_motion` setting removes the bob as it removes the shake.
- **Look:** tune A and the sway with the user. Bob numbers are a judgement, and the real excursions are the stated reference.
- **Gaze fixation is the user's call after this look.** A translation-only bob is fixation at infinity. Against fixing on a wall it differs by under a degree, except within about 1.5 m of a wall.
  - If near walls are seen to swim, add the rig seam:
    - SETTINGS `eye_offset` and `gaze_distance`;
    - applied to the blend's target, not after it;
    - `base` kept without them;
    - `cam-eye-offset` and `cam-gaze` arms, with a probe line for `base`;
    - silent's distance from a ray masked to static and kinematic geometry, at least 0.6 m, smoothed in log space.
  - Otherwise it is noted as not done, with the measurement.
- **Then add these arms to the `gait` group:**
  - `bob_scale` 0 is bit-identical;
  - each footfall lands at the bob's trough;
  - the sway is toward the stance foot.

## As built

### Phase 0: the stairs, measured

`--trace-gait` prints one line a fixed step from the top of `player_update`: the feet, the rise and
run since the last step, Jolt's ground state and normal, and the commanded speed. The runs were
scripted pads from `--player-at` at the foot of each flight, up, round, and down.

**On its treads, the mansion's stair could not be climbed in a straight line.**
- **Up the middle (x 119.4):** the capsule rode up three treads and stopped on the fourth for
  good. It wedged on the second on x 119.6, and on x 119.15 at the first, against the balustrade's
  colliders.
- **Why it wedges.** Resting on a tread against the next riser, Jolt reads its ground as the
  nosing, steep at a normal of 0.37. `ExtendedUpdate` cancels the velocity into steep ground
  before it moves, so the capsule never pushes into the riser. Its stair walk only steps up over
  a contact that stopped a move (`CanWalkStairs`'s `mHadCollision`), so it never fires.
- **Where it did climb**, each tread was a ride up the nosing over about 11 steps (0.18 s), up to
  4.2 cm a step (2.5 m/s) at its start. It was a lump, not one snap.
- **At 0.18 m/s** each tread took four steps, each with a 2 cm shove forward (Jolt's
  `mWalkStairsMinStepForward`).
- **The cellar flight:**
  - down, it dropped in uneven bites, up to 12 cm in one step (7 m/s);
  - up, it rode at 0.1 to 0.8 m/s with 6 cm pops.

**Decided with the user: a flight collides as a ramp.**
- `kit_frame_ramp` is a faceless slab whose top runs along the line through the nosings, from one
  going before the first riser to the floor above. This is how most AAA games collide stairs.
- `kit_frame_stair` lays one under every flight it builds, the great hall's and the terrace
  flights', and its steps no longer collide. The cellar flight's riser boxes gave way to its
  ramp too.
- The cellar is pitched at 47 degrees, so the player's walkable slope went from 45 to 50 degrees.
- **On the ramps:**
  - the mansion's flight climbs at a constant 1.29 cm a step and 1.69 cm across (1.27 m/s along
    the slope), and goes down at 2.0 cm a step;
  - the cellar climbs at 1.33 cm a step and goes down at 2.9.

  Both are straight lines from end to end.

**What it changes in the plan:**
- **No risers to count.** On stairs the gait's cadence is Blondel's rule: a step is 2R + G = 0.63 m
  of rise counted twice plus run, `dphase = w·(2|dy| + dh)/0.63`. That is one step a tread on
  both flights (0.19/0.25 gives 1.00 and 0.225/0.21 gives 1.05). It stays linear in the
  increments.
- **The eye spring now has less to do:** the 25 cm single steps, the kerbs and porch steps, and
  where a ramp meets its floors.

### Phase 1: smooth motion

- **Drawn between steps.** `player_update` latches `step_from` first, before anything moves the
  capsule. It is set at spawn too, since a window's first frame may run no step.
  `player_pre_render` takes the game's `alpha` and anchors the rig at
  `lerp(step_from, position, alpha)`, kept as `Player.view`.
  - Headless, alpha is about 0, so a headless picture shows the body one step back, where the last
    step began.
  - `player_view_feet` is that position at the feet. The lake's wake and the map's arrow read it;
    the cat, the cellar's hold and the finds keep `player_feet`, the capsule's own.
  - `--no-interp` draws the body where the last step left it, for the A/B.
- **The render clock.** It is `game->time + game->accumulator`, and `Player.frame_dt` is its change
  since the last frame. Headless it is 1/60, as the sim delta was. The rig's stick and arrow-key
  look and the flashlight beam's lag take it, so neither steps at 60 Hz on a faster display.
- **The speed ramp (`ramp_move`):**
  - The speed rises at 5 m/s² and falls at 8 m/s², which reaches the walk in 19 steps (0.32 s,
    traced).
  - The way the body goes turns toward the way it is asked at 10 rad/s, so a turn of the head
    does not skid.
  - Asked round more than 1.75 radians, it stops before it goes back.
  - In the air it keeps a fifth of all three.
  - The ramp is kept in `Player.move` rather than read back from Jolt, which cancels what pushes
    into steep ground.

### Phase 2: the gait, and stairs

**`cetra/src/gait.c/.h`** is public, in the CMake allowlist. It has `create_gait`/`free_gait`,
`gait_reset`, `gait_step` and `gait_frame`, with the fields under the usual three roles. The
SETTINGS are last in the struct, which is what `gait_reset` keeps.
- **Stride:** level steps of `0.40 + 0.25 * speed`; Blondel's 0.63 m on a flight.
  - `stairs` is `smoothstep(0.25, 0.5, |grade|)`.
  - The grade is rise over run, each sum weighted by `exp(-run / 0.5 m)`.
- **Pace:** `min(1, climb / |grade|)`, with `climb` 0.22 up and 0.28 down, floored at 0.6 m/s
  of speed asked.
- **The eye's lag:** a critically damped spring at 20 rad/s on the ground and 60 in the air,
  clamped to 0.25 m below and 0.10 above.
- **A landing:** more than 0.25 s in the air dips the eye by 0.03 m per m/s of fall, at most
  8 cm, on a 12 rad/s spring.
- **Footfalls:**
  - at most one a frame, when the phase passes x.5;
  - a settle step when the body stops more than 0.2 of a step past a heel;
  - a shuffle step every 70 degrees turned standing, no closer than 0.35 s.

**Silent:**
- `player_update` feeds `gait_step` the last step's whole move, then scales the speed asked by
  `pace` before the ramp.
- `player_pre_render` feeds `gait_frame` the drawn body's move since the last frame, under a pin
  too, and adds `smooth_offset` to the rig's anchor.
- The reach cone works from `Player.reach_from`, the body's own eye, which no lag moves.
- `--no-gait` is the A/B, and `--trace-gait` gains the grade, stairs and pace, every frame's feet,
  eye, phase and speed, and every footfall.

**Measured on the mansion's flight:**
- **The floor is what paces it, not the climb.** The ramp is 37 degrees, so 0.22/0.76 asks
  0.46 m/s and the floor asks 0.6.
- **Jolt slides a capsule up a slope at the commanded speed times the cosine**, so the climb's
  horizontal speed is that times the cosine again: 0.38 m/s up, 1.52 treads a second.
- **Going down, Jolt keeps the horizontal**: 0.6 m/s, 2.4 treads a second.
- **Footfalls come one a tread both ways.** The phase follows Blondel, and frames 70 to 576 hold
  14 strikes up a 15-tread flight.
- **The 180-degree turn on the gallery shuffled twice.**

**The user found that climb far too slow, and it was, by two causes stacked:**
- the floor of 0.6 m/s was taking the pace;
- Jolt's slope then cut the run to 0.38 m/s, 1.5 treads a second, which is slow even for a person.

**Retuned:**
- `player_update` asks for `pace * (1 + grade^2)` going up, so the run is the pace's.
- `climb_up` is 0.29 and `climb_down` 0.36, which puts a walk of 1.6 m/s at 2.4 treads a second
  up this flight and 3 down.
- `pace_floor` is 0.3, a safety now rather than the pace.

**Traced:**
- up: 0.61 m/s, a footfall every 24.5 frames (2.45 a second), the flight in about 6 s against 10;
- down: 0.76 m/s, every 19.5 frames.
- `--climb X` scales both rates for trying by feel.

**The `gait` gate group:**
- It runs `gametest --gait-probe <case>`, before any engine exists, on moves the probe makes up:
  no window, no physics, no world and nothing of silent's.
- Ten arms, each a closed form or an equality: gait-split, gait-cadence, gait-rest, gait-shuffle,
  gait-bob-off, gait-trough, gait-spring, gait-flight, gait-land, gait-hitch.
- The defaults they hold are restated in `gates.py`'s GAIT_ constants.
- **Falsified:** with the strike moved from x.5 to the whole phase -- the footstep at the top of
  the bob, half a step out -- gait-trough alone went red, reading 0.0004 of the way down.

**On a 15 cm kerb from the road:**
- Jolt rides the capsule up its edge over 15 steps.
- The eye follows on the spring and settles about 0.25 s later.
- The level cadence is a strike every 30 frames at 1.6 m/s, which is 120 a minute.

### Phase 3: the head bob

**`gait_frame` writes `bob_offset`, a move only, with no pitch or roll:**
- **Height:** `0.0125 * speed`, at most 3.5 cm, top to bottom. That is 2 cm at a walk, as
  `-rise * (1 - cos 2*pi*phase) / 2`: 0 over the standing foot, lowest as a heel strikes.
- **Sway:** 1 cm side to side, all there by 1 m/s, as `sway/2 * cos(pi * phase)` along the body's
  right, toward the foot that struck last.
- **Fades:** both fade out by `stairs`, and `bob_scale` 0 makes it exactly zero.

**Silent:**
- adds the bob to the anchor with the lag;
- writes `--bob X` (default 1). Phase 3 multiplied it by the rig's `shake_player_scale` and said
  Reduce Motion took the bob with the shake, but silent never calls `settings_apply`, so that
  scale was always 1, and a value copied once at init would go stale anyway. The review dropped
  both the product and the claim.

**Traced walking east along the street at 1.6 m/s:**
- each footfall lands on the bob's bottom, at -2.00 cm at phases 3.5 and 4.5;
- the sway peaks at +0.5 cm (south, the body's right) at phase 4 after the right heel at 3.5, and
  at -0.5 cm after the left.

**Silent, `footsteps.c/h`:**

Each gives its six cleanest events as `step_<surface>_1` to `_6`, cut by the one-shot recipe: a
shoe's step at most 0.45 s, a wade or a step in mud at most 0.9.

### The review

Five reviews ran over the branch: thermo-nuclear, and simplify's reuse, simplification, efficiency
and altitude.

**Applied:**
- **Footstep surfaces come from what the feet stand on.** The plan-based cascade restated every
  building's footprint with its own margins and ended in a guess: anything above the land was
  boards. Now:
  - every body the kit makes goes through one `body()`, which notes its material in a
    `KitSurfaces` table that silent owns and every kit made beside the first shares;
  - floors drawn as boards over a separate body say what they are, through `kit_frame_floor`
    (the home's rooms, the mansion's ground and upper floors, the cabin and the dock);
  - a footfall casts one ray down, and the body it finds gives its material, which maps to a
    surface;
  - three things are not bodies of a material: a rug (its own tests, the mansion's now one
    world-space `mansion_cloth_at` that the cat's paws use too), the lake (the feet under its
    level), and the open ground, one mesh, where the drive and the track are ribbons drawn over
    it and everything else is the land's own facet, `land_mat_at`, which shares `land_build`'s
    choice of ground per cell.
  - Gone: `lake_on_dock`, the house, mansion, cabin and porch rectangles, the lift test.
  - Traced the same walks again: the street and yard, the kitchen and the mansion's flight answer
    as before (the flight is cloth, its runner); from the cabin out onto the dock, wood, gravel,
    one step of the bank's woods floor, wood; down the bank, woods floor, mud, water.
- **The stair hole.** A flight's step boxes stopped colliding when the ramp went in, so the
  mansion's stair was a 0.2 m slab, open from the side and from under the gallery. Each step
  collides again, cut down to `RAMP_THICK / cos` under the line through its nosing so it never
  meets the walking surface. The mansion's climb traces the same to the millimetre.
- **A frame clock in the game layer.** `Game.frame_clock` is the sim time a frame is drawn at
  (`time + accumulator`) and its advance. silent's player, beam, HUD, backpack, map and fade use it;
  the HUD, the bag and the map still moved in 60 Hz steps. gametest's and forest's rigs take it
  too: headless it is the fixed step exactly, so nothing a gate reads moves. `Player.frame_at`,
  `frame_dt` and the `alpha` parameter are gone, alpha being `accumulator / fixed_timestep`.
- **One "no footfall" kind.** `GAIT_FALL_NONE` and `GAIT_FALL_KINDS` replace the `fell` flag that
  said whether `footfall` was valid; one `offer` keeps the first foot of a frame;
  `gait_fall_name` names them for the trace.
- **Smaller pieces:**
  - `wrap_pi` for both angle wraps;
  - `smooth_offset` is a float, being only ever a height;
  - the ramp keeps a speed and a heading rather than a vector it decoded each step;
  - the footstep voices play where the foot landed rather than riding the body, which cost an
    entity scan a voice a frame;
  - `kit_xrnd` and `path_exists` replace a third xorshift and an `fopen` probe;
  - `pick` can now choose any take first;
  - a surface with no recordings says so at startup;
  - one surfaces table instead of two parallel arrays;
  - the tub's near edge is one constant;
  - the trace counters are the engine's own frame count and the game's time.
- **`lag_above` is set from the capsule** by silent: the capsule's top less the eye's height less
  the near plane, 3 cm. The gait cannot know the body's height.
- **The tests.**
  - The probe: each case owns its gait, one tick helper, no filler numbers, and the trough is
    measured against the lowest bob of each step instead of restating the bob's formula
    (0.9973).
  - The gate: one helper for the no-output case, the expected values owned by the gate,
    `GAIT_CLIMB_UP` restated, and one reader for the audio group's two brightness grammars.

**Not applied, and why:**
- **Interpolating entities in the game layer** (`prev_position` and a view position): the right
  fix before the gait has a second user, and the spec defers it. Only the clock half came in.
- **`keep_horizontal_speed` in the character controller**, Unreal's
  `bMaintainHorizontalGroundVelocity`. It would replace the climb's `1 + grade^2` compensation,
  which lags at a flight's ends. It changes how every flight walks, which the user tuned and
  approved, so it waits for its own pass.
- **The eye's spring only for what the grade does not explain:** also a change to the approved
  feel on flights.
- **A nested state struct for `gait_reset`:** the repo's struct roles lay settings out as plain
  fields last, the camera rig's included.
- **Merging the probe spawns into one `--gait-probe all`, and the pitch case into `decode`:** a
  saving of under a second against the group's one-case-one-spawn pattern.
- **The 50-degree slope limit:** relaxed for the cellar's 47-degree ramp, so a facet of the land
  between 45 and 50 degrees is now climbable. For the user to walk: the chasm's lip, the ridge
  and the hill's banks.
