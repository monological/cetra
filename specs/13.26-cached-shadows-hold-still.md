# Spec 13.26 -- cached shadows that hold still

## Context
**The problem.** silent runs at about 5 fps on the drive. Profiled headless at 1600x900 from the street's end
(`--cam-eye 30,1.7,1.5 --cam-target 60,2,0`), a frame takes 214 ms. The "shadow movers" pass of the cached
point-light tiles takes 166 ms of GPU and 168 ms of CPU of it, redrawing each frame:
- 408 tile faces;
- 920k meshes walked;
- 11.7k draws;
- 49 million triangles.

**What is and isn't the cause** (throwaway tests):
- With the dead trees' `wind_response` set to 0, the frame takes 46 ms, movers 8 ms, and 24 faces are redrawn.
- The cat makes no difference.

**Why.** It is an engine rule, not silent's content:
- **Any wind material is never `DRAW_STILL`** (`draw_list.c:120`). So `tiles_note_changes` (`shadow.c:1844`)
  marks every tile face its wind-padded box reaches as dynamic, on every frame, forever.
- **The rule ignores whether the scene's wind can move anything.** silent's wind keeps the default
  `strength` 0.02 (`wind.c:25`; `silent.c:663-670` never sets it). At the trees' node scale of 0.045-0.07 that is
  about 0.6 mm of world sway, 1% of the 5 cm drift the kept views already tolerate. The engine pays 166 ms to
  follow motion nobody can see.
- **Stores never fit.** Lights take the whole 768-cell budget first (16 bodied lights x 48 cells), so
  `tiles_take_stores` (`shadow.c:1888`) silently gets no store. Every dynamic face is then redrawn with every
  caster (`draw_tile_cell_all`), not with just the movers over a copy.
- **Captures pay too, unseen.** Every GI or probe capture redraws all dynamic faces as well, in view or not, and
  the profiler is paused during captures.

**Unreal's answer** is the per-primitive "Shadow Cache Invalidation Behavior: Rigid": material motion (wind) does
not invalidate cached shadows, but transform motion still does. This spec brings that, fixes the windless case,
and gives stores room of their own.

**Workflow.** Branch `13.26-cached-shadows-hold-still` off master; copy this plan to
`specs/13.26-cached-shadows-hold-still.md` and commit it alone; make the task list; commit and update the spec's
as-built notes after each phase. Booked 13.22 (local-exposure fringe) is unaffected.

## Phase 1 -- swaying casters hold still in cached faces (Unreal's Rigid)
1. **The windless rule.** `classify` (`draw_list.c:82`) takes `const Wind*` (from `append_node`, `:308`, passing
   `scene->wind`). A caster sways only when it can actually be displaced:
   ```c
   bool rigid = !mesh->is_skinned && mesh->morph_max_offset == 0.0f;
   bool sways = wind_max_offset(wind, mat->wind_response, mat->wind_mode,
                                mesh->wind_flex_max, mesh->wind_leaf_max) > 0.0f;
   bool still = rigid && mat->wind_response == 0.0f;                     // DRAW_OCCLUDER, unchanged
   bool kept_still = rigid && (!sways || mat->cached_shadow_wind == CACHED_SHADOW_WIND_REST);
   ```
   `DRAW_STILL` is renamed `DRAW_KEPT_STILL` so the compiler finds both readers (`shadow.c:876,1844`). The
   capture_hidden and skinned clear at `:312-313` stays, and the comment at `draw_list.h:51` is rewritten.
2. **The material row.** In `Material` (`material.h`, beside `occluder`), add `int cached_shadow_wind`:
   0 = sway (default), 1 = rest. It gets an enum-label row `cachedShadowWind` `{"sway","rest"}` in
   `MATERIAL_PARAMS` group "Shadows", directly after `foliageShadows` (`material.c:138`, consecutive rows). A
   charter paragraph goes in `material.h:443-491`: it moves no lane, and its only failure is a caster's own shadow
   held at rest, off by at most its sway. The GUI, the config snapshot and `.cscn` reach it through the row.
3. **Draw at rest only in kept faces.** In `draw_shadow_layer` (`shadow.c:1279`), when
   `caster_set_at_rest(set)` (`KEPT` or `KEPT_STILL`):
   - cull with `cull.wind = NULL`, so the box matches the pose drawn;
   - upload the wind strength at rest before `_draw_shadow_items`, and the scene's after.

   The upload goes through a new `wind_upload_strength(const Wind*, bool rest, UniformManager*)` in
   `wind.c/h`, which `wind_upload_to_program` (`wind.c:80-85`) also calls, so the uploaded strength is decided in
   one place. The restore is immediate because TSM's nearest-translucent step reuses `depth_program` without
   re-uploading (`shadow.c:2475-2479`); the comment names that. `KEPT_MOVERS`, the cascades, the per-frame
   layers, the rain and the camera pass keep the sway.
4. **The node-moved box.** In `tiles_note_changes` (`:1874-1879`), use `draw_item_bounds` for both the previous
   and current positions, not `mesh->aabb`: `KEPT_MOVERS` draws a moving node with its wind.
5. **Kept membership has a digest.** Before the epoch test (`:1824`), hash by list position whether each item is
   wanted by `KEPT` and is `DRAW_KEPT_STILL`. If the digest changed while the graph epoch did not, take the epoch
   branch (new tile generation, every face redrawn once). This closes stale faces after a runtime toggle of this
   row, `wind_response`, opacity or shadow role, which today leave a ghost at the rest position or a caster
   missing for good. It needs a `tile_kept_digest` field beside `tile_epoch` (`shadow.h:391`).
6. **The epoch branch's early `return`** (`:1825-1834`) is dropped, so a graph change no longer drops every
   non-still caster's shadow (the cat's) for one frame. 13.18 said to; `ccff7d45` put it back.
7. **A render flag for the toggle arm.** `apps/render`: `--material-at FRAME NAME KEY VALUE`, repeatable, applied
   in `pre_render_callback` before the node swing (`render.c:2979`). It goes through `scene_find_material` and
   `material_param_find`, and accepts a number or an enum label.
8. **silent's trees opt in.** `apps/silent/src/trees.c:47-48`: `m->cached_shadow_wind = CACHED_SHADOW_WIND_REST;`.
9. **Gate arms** (in `run_shadow_tiles_gate`, each falsified by hand after a commit):

   The wind fixture is `cornell_point` with its lamp cached, via `cscn_copy`. It adds a `wind` block (strength
   0.15) and makes `cornell_tall_box` `windResponse 1, windMode "cloth"` (it has no UV1). Variants: `rest`, `sway`
   (the control) and `rigid` (response 0).

   | Arm | Asserts | Falsified by |
   |---|---|---|
   | `tiles-rest` | `rest` settles to `mover_faces_drawn` 0, block `dynamic` 0, and a `--tile-map` byte-identical to `rigid`'s; `sway` draws mover faces | dropping the zeroing (held at the frame-1 pose) or the classification |
   | `tiles-windless` | wind materials with no wind block draw 0 mover faces | master's response-only rule |
   | `tiles-rest-toggle` | `--material-at 10 ... sway` then `--material-at 20 ... rest`: the frame-30 tile map equals `rest`'s | removing the digest |

10. **Look -- the user decides.**
    - Walk the drive in a window, with the run command given from the branch.
    - `--tile-map` of a home light and a mansion candle, before and after, show the casters are unchanged.
    - Show the trees at the current strength next to a strength that visibly sways. Whether silent's trees
      should sway visibly is the user's call. With the opt-in it costs nothing either way.
11. **Profile** both poses as in phase 0. Expected: about 46 ms at the street end, and about 46 ms under
    `--no-wind` even without the opt-in.

## Phase 2 -- stores get room of their own (a per-face pool)
1. **The pool.** One store block of `SHADOW_TILE_STORE_CELLS` 64 cells (16 MiB), with its free list a 64-bit
   mask. It is made lazily, past the lights' cells and on top of their 768. Carving it out of the 768 would break
   `lighting-stream`'s nearest-16-whole assertion. `shadow.h:61-75`:
   - `PUNCTUAL_TILE_MAX_LAYERS` grows by the pool, or `init_punctual_shadow_array` (`shadow.c:314`) refuses the
     whole array;
   - the block table gets one more slot;
   - `held[]` (`:1595`) is sized to the table;
   - `tile_block_take` (`:1490-1498`) caps lights at 768 and the pool beyond it.
2. **Per face, not per block.** A store is one cell per DYNAMIC face. A mover usually reaches 8-24 of a
   bodied light's 48 faces, so a block-sized store wastes most of its 48 cells.
   - `ShadowTileBlock.store` becomes `int8_t store_cell[6 * SHADOW_TILE_VIEWS]`; `stored` stays.
   - Free and drop work per face (`:1506-1519`); `tile_block_blank` (`:1474`) initialises the cells.
3. **Assignment.** `tiles_take_stores` (`:1888`) assigns cells nearest light first and keeps existing
   assignments. It takes a cell from another light only if that light is more than `TILE_STREAM_MARGIN` farther,
   the `tile_rank_to_evict` rule. When the pool is full it logs once by name: "Cached shadow stores full (N
   faces): '<light>' ...".
4. **Counters and flags.**
   - `render_shadow_movers` (`:2066-2084`) splits `mover_faces_drawn` into `mover_faces_copied` and
     `mover_faces_whole`.
   - `shadow_tiles_probe` (`:3171`) prints `store_cells`, `store_used` and per-block `stored_faces`.
   - A runtime setting `tile_store_cells` (default 64, 0 = no pool) reaches `--tile-stores N` in render and
     silent.
5. **Gate arm `tiles-store`.**
   - Setup: `stream_rooms.cscn` at room 3 (`--cam-eye 120,1.6,1.8 --cam-target 120,1,-2`), frame 30. Its 21
     bodied lights fill the 768 cells, and its `skinned_prop` moves every frame, which is silent's situation.
   - Asserts: 16 blocks of 48; `mover_faces_copied` > 0; `mover_faces_whole` 0; 0 px against `--tiles-refresh`.
   - Control: `--tile-stores 0` gives whole redraws and the "stores full" line naming room 3's light.
6. **Profile** both poses. Expected: shadow movers about 1 ms.

### Phase 1 -- swaying casters hold still in cached faces
- **Classification.** `classify` asks `wind_max_offset` under the scene's wind, so a response under no
  wind, or a wind of no strength, moves nothing. `DRAW_STILL` became `DRAW_KEPT_STILL`, set for a rigid
  caster that cannot sway or whose material says `rest`; `DRAW_OCCLUDER` keeps its own local `still`.
- **Rest in kept faces.** `draw_shadow_layer` culls `KEPT` and `KEPT_STILL` with no wind and uploads
  the strength at rest through `wind_upload_strength`, which `wind_upload_to_program` now also calls.
  It puts the scene's strength back straight after the walk, because the transmittance map's
  nearest-depth step draws through the same program without uploading again.
- **The digest** (`tiles_kept_digest`, FNV over each item's kept and kept-still bits by list position)
  takes the epoch branch when it changes. The draw list is rebuilt and re-classified every frame, since
  its stamp carries the frame count, so a material change reaches it the frame it happens.
- **The early return is gone.** On an epoch frame the swaying and posed casters mark their faces
  dynamic, so the movers pass draws them that frame instead of the faces being drawn whole without
  them.
- **The node-moved box** is `draw_item_bounds` for both positions, falling back to the import box.
- **`--material-at FRAME NAME KEY VALUE`** (render, up to 8) resolves an enum label at parse time and
  refuses an unknown key, label or non-scalar row by name.
- **silent's `dead_bark`** opts in.
- **Gates.** `tiles-rest`, `tiles-windless` and `tiles-rest-toggle` pass. All nine scoped groups pass:
  shadow-tiles, lighting-stream, cull, occlusion, translucent and its off-path twin, shadow-lag,
  config, gate-docs.
- **Falsified by hand:**
  - **Kept faces drawn with the wind on:** `tiles-rest` fails at 52,907 px, the faces held at the
    pose of the frame they were drawn on. `tiles-rest-toggle` fails with it, since it compares
    against the same faces.
  - **The material-only rule:** `tiles-windless` fails, 5 faces drawn over a copy under no wind.
  - **No digest:** `tiles-rest-toggle` fails at 110,655 px. Both of these were broken in one build,
    since each can reach only its own arm, and `tiles-rest` stayed green in it.
- **The look.** Built with and without the trees' opt-in, the frames differ by 0 px at the street end
  and from the drive, and by 4 and 8 px at one code in the study and dining room, of 5.76 M. Holding
  the trees at rest changes nothing a player can see. Their sway is too small to see at all at the
  default wind strength of 0.02. Whether they should sway visibly is a separate art call, and with
  the opt-in it costs nothing either way.
- **The profile** (240 frames, 1600x900, two runs each):

  | Pose | Frame | Shadow movers, GPU | Faces a frame | Whole run |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Street end | 46.1 / 47.2 ms | 8.3 / 8.5 ms | 24 | 26.6 / 27.0 s |
  | Mansion from the drive | 41.7 / 43.0 ms | 8.4 / 8.7 ms | 24 | 21.6 / 22.2 s |
  | Street end, `--no-wind`, opt-in removed | 47.3 ms | 8.7 ms | 24 | 25.5 s |

  From 227 and 288 ms: five and seven times faster. The windless rule alone recovers the frame under
  `--no-wind`. The 24 faces left belong to the blocks the probe still lists dynamic, four of the
  home's lights and two study candles, and every one is still drawn whole for want of a store,

### Phase 2 -- stores get room of their own
- **The pool** is one block of 64 cells, `is_store`, made the first time a face needs a store,
  appended past whatever the lights hold. `tile_region_room` lets the lights reach 768 cells and the
  pool 64 more, and the table one more block (`SHADOW_TILE_TABLE`), so no light loses its slot.
  `PUNCTUAL_TILE_MAX_LAYERS` grows by the pool's 16 MiB; silent's array goes from 15 layers at
  2048^2 to 16.
- **A cell a face.** `ShadowTileBlock.store` became `int8_t store_cell[48]`, and the pool's free
  cells are a 64-bit mask. `tiles_take_stores` gives back the cells of faces no longer dynamic (and
  of any past `tile_store_cells`), then serves the ranked lights nearest first: a face keeps its
  cell, takes a free one, or takes the cell of a light more than `TILE_STREAM_MARGIN` farther. A
  face left without one is drawn whole, and "Cached shadow stores full" names the nearest such
  light once, latched.
- **Instruments.** `mover_faces_copied` and `mover_faces_whole` split `mover_faces_drawn`, which
  stays as their sum, so `tiles-movers` and `tiles-rest` read it unchanged. The probe's region line
  gains `store_cells`, `store_used` and the two counts; a block's `store=` became `stored_faces=`,
  which no arm read. `--tile-stores N` in render and silent sets `tile_store_cells`.
- **cppcheck** refused the free mask's dead branch, `1ull << 64`, which is undefined even unrun. It
  is `~0ull >> (64 - SHADOW_TILE_STORE_CELLS)`.
- **Gates.** `tiles-store` passes: room 3 of `stream_rooms` with sixteen lights at 48 cells, the pool
  at 64, 24 faces copied and 0 whole; with no pool, 24 whole, the refusal naming `Room3A`, and the
  two pictures 0 px apart. shadow-tiles, lighting-stream (`stream-tiles` included) and gate-docs all
  pass.
- **Falsified:** the pool made to fit inside the lights' budget (`pooled` without `pool ||`) turns
  `tiles-store` red alone: pool 0 cells, 0 copied, 24 whole.
- **The profile** (240 frames, 1600x900, two runs each):

  | Pose | Frame | Shadow movers, GPU | Copied / whole | Pool used | Whole run |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Street end | 44.3 / 38.8 ms | 1.6 / 3.0 ms | 24 / 0 | 64 of 64 | 24.8 / 24.7 s |
  | Mansion from the drive | 33.8 / 34.8 ms | 3.0 / 3.3 ms | 24 / 0 | 24 of 64 | 19.9 / 19.9 s |
  | Street end, `--tile-stores 0` | 46.3 ms | 8.4 ms | 0 / 24 | none | 26.0 s |

  `--tile-stores 0` reproduces phase 1 (46.1 / 47.2 ms, 8.3 / 8.5 ms) and names `hall_bulb`. The
  pass walks 504 meshes a frame where it walked 54,120. **At the street end the pool is exactly
  full**: the sixteen nearest lights there have 64 dynamic faces between them. One more, and the
  farthest light's faces are drawn whole and said by name, which is a reason to raise
  `SHADOW_TILE_STORE_CELLS` (it is capped at 64 by its one-word mask) if a scene shows it.
- **Overall:** the street end from 227 ms to about 40, the mansion from the drive from 288 to
  about 34.

### Review fixes
Six background reviews (two thermo-nuclear: engine core, apps and gates; four /simplify: reuse,
simplification, efficiency, altitude), deduplicated and applied together.
- **The rest pose is per material, not per pass.** A kept set draws a `rest` material with a wind
  response of 0 (`caster_wind_response`), set per draw since the material block is uploaded once
  across every set of the depth pass. That deleted `wind_upload_strength`, the strength restored
  after the kept walk, and the transmittance step's hazard it guarded; and it puts a `rest` caster
  on a moving node at rest in the overlay too, where it used to sway until it stopped and then snap.
- **The digest became a per-item comparison** (`ShadowTileSeen`, `tiles_mark_changed_looks`): the
  list as the kept faces last saw it, each item's mesh, node, lane, flags and, for an alpha-tested
  caster, a hash of its cut-out. An item that changed is drawn again where it is and where it was;
  only a change to which items the list holds draws every face. The digest redrew every face of
  every cached light for one toggled caster -- about 300 ms in silent -- and an albedo or opacity
  map arriving from the async loader changes an item's flags with no graph change, so that was a
  hitch at load as well. It also missed `doubleSided` (a kept face is drawn with the caster's cull
  mode) and a foliage caster's cutoff, UV transform and albedo.
- **The pool left the block table.** It is `tile_store_first`, a cell range appended to the region
  like a block; `is_store`, `SHADOW_TILE_TABLE`, `tile_region_room`'s pool arm (which could never
  fail), `tile_store_pool_take` and `tile_block_drop_store` are gone. The free mask is a local of
  `tiles_take_stores`, built from the faces' cells, so the two can no longer disagree.
- **Cells go to faces drawn this frame** (`tile_mover_faces_drawn`, which `render_shadow_movers`
  now asks too), nearest light first, and a face keeps its cell while nothing moves in it. A face
  needing one takes a free cell, then an idle one (a face not drawn this frame, the farthest
  light's first), then one of a light more than the margin farther (`tile_rank_farther`, which
  `tile_rank_to_evict` shares). Distance alone let faces off screen hold cells the pool, full at
  the street end, needed on screen; and giving a cell back after one idle frame threw away a copy
  a returning mover would have used.
- **A block re-placed with fewer views drops the dynamic bits of the faces it lost**
  (`tile_block_place`); they were drawn by nothing and held store cells for good.
- **Smaller:** `mover_faces_drawn` is printed as copied plus whole rather than kept; the moved
  node's bound fails the way the deforming one's does; `wind_mesh_max_offset` is the one five-field
  call; `material_param_enum_value` resolves a label for `--material-at` and the `.cscn` loader;
  `--material-at` runs in `render_frame_update` with a `frame N:` line, sets every material of the
  name, and refuses a bad frame, an enum value past its labels and an unknown material.
- **Gates:** `tiles-rest-toggle` asserts the box was redrawn twice (`kept_changes` in the probe)
  and that no face of the region was lost, so a toggle that never happened cannot pass;
  `tiles-windless` carries its own sway control; `tiles-store` derives its counts; `_tiles_block`
  is shared; the redundant `rigid` render is gone, `tiles-rest` comparing against the cached
  lamp's own map.
- **Docs:** the rest pose's measured 4 and 8 px replace "0 px"; the store warning says it comes
  each time the pool runs out, which is what the latch does; the comments that narrated another
  file's mechanism state contracts.
- **Skipped:** deciding stillness from the sway's size against `tile_tolerance` (it reverses the
  opt-in this plan chose), sizing the pool by need (64 is the mask's word; booked as the lever the
  phase 2 profile names), and folding `--layer-blend-at` into `--material-at` (outside the diff).
