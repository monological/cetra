# Spec 13.21 -- wet ground's reflection before TAA

## Context
**Background.** Spec 13.20 found and half-fixed a source of negative light. Wet ground (rain, spec 13.9) gets its screen-space reflection from `ssr_fold_wet_frag`. That pass adds the traced reflection and subtracts the share of the environment's specular the trace replaces.
- **The occlusion half is fixed** (`4ce92e2f`, `fec008a0`): the fold now subtracts what the split composite actually put back.
- **The other half is open.** The fold runs AFTER TAA. It subtracts this frame's raw, render-res specular from a frame TAA has already resolved, and under silent's render scale 0.5 also upscaled. On a thin ripple or a wet edge the two disagree, and the fold takes out light that isn't there.

**Measured in 13.20** (with an instrument that was never committed):
- silent's porch keeps about 3,100 negative pixels (652 below -0.005).
- It has none with the subtraction off, and none with TAA off.
- `sceneLight` keeps them from turning white. They show as dark edge pixels and faintly warm ripple arcs.

**No gate or golden sees this.** Every rain arm runs without TAA, no golden has rain, and nothing counts negative light.

**Fix:** composite wet ground's reflection BEFORE TAA, in the split composite, from LAST frame's SSR result reprojected.
- The reflection and the specular it replaces then live in one image, and TAA resolves both.
- The output is `spec*SO*(1 - coverage) + reflection`. Every term is non-negative, so it cannot go below zero.
- Unreal also composites SSR before TAA. It traces this frame and samples last frame's colour at the hit. Moving our trace that way would re-encode the catcher's path and move six goldens; reusing last frame's whole result keeps the catcher and its goldens untouched.

## Design
**Last frame's result.**
- `postfx_run_ssr` records what it leaves, when it ran in replace mode: `fx->ssr_prev = {GLuint tex, int frame, float pre_exposure}`.
  - `tex` is the final texture: `ssr_atrous.tex[0]` with denoise on, otherwise the accumulator's written side or `ssr_texture`. Store the handle; never derive it from parity, since `frame_index` advances on undrawn frames.
  - `pre_exposure` is `fx->pre_exposure`.
- It is cleared in `create_ssr_buffers`/`destroy_ssr_buffers`. It is ENGINE-OWNED cross-frame state, like `fog_layer_frame`.
- The composite uses it only when all hold:
  - `fx->rain_wet`;
  - `ssr_prev.tex != 0`;
  - `ssr_prev.frame == frame_index - 1`, the fog layer's adjacency rule. This also covers frames where SSR was off or skipped (its `valid` flags are never cleared then).

**The composite** (`spec_occ_composite_frag`) handles wet pixels:
- Class 2 (`ssrMarkerClass` on this frame's normals) reads last frame's pair at `uv - aux.xy`. The velocity is in UV units; the SSR buffer is render res, or half with `--no-ssr-full-res`, and is bilinear-sampled.
- Its `.rgb` is scaled by `_history_scale(fx, ssr_prev.pre_exposure)`, the 13.20 rule; coverage `.a` is not scaled.
- It outputs `spec*SO*(1 - a) + rgb` in place of `spec*SO`. The alpha (AO factor) is unchanged.
- Off-screen reprojection, or no valid result, gives `spec*SO` alone: the `--no-ssr` answer for that frame.
- `split_occlusion.glsl` folds back into the composite (it has one reader again). `_bind_split_occlusion` gains the SSR input on a free unit, plus `ssrPrevActive` and `ssrPrevScale`.

**The post-TAA fold** (`ssr_fold_wet_frag`):
- Wet pixels take nothing; they were done before TAA.
- Catcher pixels keep their class-separated lerp, so wet pairs still never bleed into them.
- The subtraction and its include are deleted, and `postfx_run_ssr` no longer needs `SplitOcclusion`. It still traces in replace mode only when `rain_wet && split_live`, so next frame's composite gets replace-encoded pairs.

**One path with TAA on or off.** Without TAA it costs one frame of lag and buys nothing, but two paths would double the surface. In the gates' static frames last frame's trace equals this frame's, apart from the animated ripples.

**An instrument:** `--negative-probe` on render and silent. At the frame's end it reads the canvas just before the tonemap (`glGetTexImage`, float) and prints the count below -0.0005 and below -0.005, and the most negative value. It is the committed form of 13.20's diagnostic.

## Risks
- **Reflections arrive one frame late,** reprojected by SURFACE velocity, which is parallax-wrong for a reflection. The SSR accumulator already does the same at 0.97 feedback, so it is in character.
- **Reflections of reflections.** The trace reads the post-TAA canvas, which now holds last frame's wet reflections. This is bounded by Fresnel, but visible.
- **Edges.** A reprojected texel can come from a pixel that was a catcher last frame and be misread. A pixel that was unmarked degrades gracefully, since its pair is zero.
- **Rain starting,** or a skipped frame, gives one frame of wet ground with no SSR.
- **Codegen drift on dry frames** from the composite's new branch: shown, and re-baked if it is 1 code.
- **`rain-drips` asserts 0 px on open wet ground.** It holds only while the trace's source stays the post-TAA canvas, before the late draw, and the plan keeps it there.

## Phase 2 -- the change

- **As designed, with three simplifications.** `postfx_run_ssr` records `ssr_prev` (the final texture when it traced in replace mode, else 0; the frame; the pre-exposure) and takes `bool split_live` again. The composite's inputs are `SplitComposite` (was `SplitOcclusion`), which gains the previous trace and its `_history_scale`, settled in `postfx_run` beside the other inputs; `_bind_split_occlusion` is gone, since one program reads it, and its sampler units (0-5) are seeded once at init. `include/split_occlusion.glsl` is deleted and its body is back in `spec_occ_composite_frag`, plus `wetReflection`: on a wet-marked pixel, `spec * (1 - a) + rgb * scale` from the pair at `uv - velocity`, the specular unchanged off the edge. `ssr_fold_wet_frag` now folds the catcher alone (wet and unmarked take 0), and reads only `srcTex` and `normalsTex`.
- **Negative light, same runs as phase 1:**
  - `rain_fixture --sky` 800x600: 0 on every frame with TAA, with TAAU and with neither.
  - silent's porch by day: 0 on every frame (was 2,490-4,250). The night road: 0 (was 4-71).
  - silent's sill by day: 12-38 px on 38 of 120 frames, to -1.65. **Not this defect**: the same counts and depths are in the baseline on the same frames (frame 8: 35 px to -1.35 both), with a 16-frame period, the jitter sequence's; they stay with `--no-rain` and go with `--no-taa`. The TAAU resolve clamps the history to the neighbourhood's box in YCoCg (`taau_resolve_frag.glsl:140`), and a point inside that box can be an RGB with a negative channel where a very bright neighbour sits beside a dark saturated one -- the window's edge. Frame 0 is a third source: 125 px to -882, and still there with `--no-taa` (147 px, to -1064) and `--no-rain` (89, to -316). Both predate this branch.
- **The reflection survives the move**: `rain_fixture --sky` with TAA moves 4,727 px (0.25%) before against after, and 99,379 (5.2%) against `--no-ssr`. On the porch what changed is the baluster outlines, the dark red arc, and the dark ripple rings on the open pavement, which were the subtraction's mismatch -- each frame's sharp ring taken from a TAA-smoothed one. Under a foggy sky a correct reflection barely shows a ring.
- **`gates.py --only rainfall`**: all 34 arms pass, `rain-ssr` open 4,244 of 19,040 px, covered 0, wall 0.
