# Spec 13.7 -- an overcast sky, on a photometric scale

## Context
`apps/silent`'s day mode is parked (spec 13.6). What it needs is a bright, grey overcast world: a dome lighting everything evenly, no hard sun, and fog the same grey as the sky.

The engine's Hillaire sky cannot do that today, for two reasons:
- **It is always a clear sky.** It is a blue gradient and a hard sun. The volumetric clouds are the wrong tool here: they are lumpy and cost a march every frame.
- **It runs on a relative scale.** `SUN_ILLUMINANCE = 3` (`atmosphere.glsl:29`, mirrored in `sky.c:159`) sets the whole sky's level, and nothing ties it to the photometric lights (nits, cd, lux). silent's tubes, authored in nits, came out hundreds of times too bright against its daylight.
  - Spec 10.2 left this open: "scene radiance is not yet one space".
  - docs/shader-subsystems.md says the sky sits "four decades under" photometric.

One spec, by the user's call. It does two things:
- adds an overcast amount to the sky;
- gives the sky a photometric scale, so an app can author its lights in real units beside it.

It ends by finishing silent's day mode on both.

Branch `13.7-overcast-sky` off master (master is at a3691c42, silent merged). The spec file is `specs/13.7-overcast-sky.md`.

## Design

### A. Overcast: `SkyAtmosphere.overcast`, 0..1, default 0
- A baked field. It joins the "BY FUNCTION" list at `sky.h:148-153` and propagates exactly as `night_floor_brightness` does: `sky_update_sun`, then `scene_environment_changed`.
- **Dome model:** the CIE standard overcast sky, L(θ) = Lz (1 + 2 cos θ)/3, neutral grey.
  - Lz follows Krochmann: (8.6 sin h + 0.123) kcd/m² at sun elevation h, onto the sky's scale by `SKY_SUN_ILLUMINANCE / 127.5`. At h = 35° that is 0.119 relative, about 5 kcd/m² once photometric.
  - The sin h term is clamped at 0, and the constant fades with the night factor, so an overcast evening darkens with the cycle.
  - Computed in one C helper, `sky_overcast_zenith(sky, sun_y)`, from the sun the bake is FOR. The slicer bakes from `latched_sun_dir`, so this cannot read the live sun (`sky.c:684, 713, 1171`).
- **One blend weight everywhere:** the clear part × (1 − o), the overcast part × o. The direct sun fades by the same (1 − o), so at o = 1 there is no sun light and no shadow.

| Site | Change |
|---|---|
| `sky_view_frag.glsl:107` | before the night floor: sky rays mix toward the CIE curve (`mu = viewDir.y`); ground rays toward the lit floor `GROUND_ALBEDO·(7/9)·Lz`. Uniforms set in `sky_bake_view_lut_to` (`sky.c:684-701`) |
| `include/sky_ground.glsl:13-14` | direct term × (1−o), blending toward `groundSky` alone, so no dome term is added twice |
| `aerial_lut_frag.glsl:62-99` | `L = mix(L_clear, (1−T_lum)·Lz/3, o)` before its clamp, so a far ridge meets the horizon behind it with no seam. Uniforms at `sky.c:474` |
| `clouds.glsl:218` (env and march) | the cloud sun term × (1−o). Uniforms at `sky.c:743-753` and `sky_clouds.c` ~396 |
| `sky_render_background` | sun disc `20·(1−o)` (`sky.c:1397`); stars × (1−o) (1403); moon disc × (1−o) (1511) |
| `sky_apply_sun_to_light`, `sky_moon_light_scale` | × (1−o) in the scale (`sky.c:910, 973`). The cast floor then drops shadows by itself |
| `sky_zenith_radiance` (`sky.c:320`) | mix(clear, (7/9)·Lz, o). This is the dome's horizontal irradiance over π, which is what fog and water read it as |

- **Guarded:** each new term sits behind `if (overcast > 0.0)` on a uniform, the pattern `starIntensity` uses, so the default path is the old code.
- **The night floor is not dimmed by overcast.** A deck at night reflects the town's light. Stars and the moon are covered.
- **Plumbing, the night floor's route throughout:**
  - render CLI `--overcast <f>`, which implies `--sky` (`render.c` ~1355 parse, ~3640 apply);
  - `.cscn` `environment.overcast` (`cscene.c` `parse_environment` plus the known-keys list at 207);
  - a config row using the `_apply_sun_angle` hook (`config_snapshot.c:644`);
  - a GUI slider using the `sun_moved`/`sun_released` pattern (`gui.c:549-558`).
- **Known and left alone:** `scene_environment_changed` does not re-capture scene probes or probe sets (`scene.c:618-627`), so a live overcast change leaves them stale. Apps set overcast at startup.
- **A `--sky-probe` flag** (after `--moon-probe`, `render.c:4739`) prints Lz, the zenith radiance, the blend weight, and the sun and moon intensity with `cast_shadows`, so every number above can be checked as a number.

### B. Photometric scale: `SkyAtmosphere.radiance_scale` K, default 1
- **What it is:** nits per sky unit. A new constant, `SKY_PHOTOMETRIC_SCALE = 128000/3`, makes the unattenuated sun 128 klux, and the atmosphere's own transmittance brings it to about 100 klux at noon.
- **Baked:** K is in the captures, so it is BY FUNCTION like overcast.
- **Applied when things are BAKED, not when they are read.** The critique's findings:
  - Folding K into `ibl->intensity` would apply it twice to GI and to scene-captured probes. Those captures already see the scaled sky, and `envScale` multiplies them again (`pbr_frag.glsl:2330-2387`).
  - Relative storage with K applied at read time would push the night floor into fp16 subnormals.
- So everything the SUN drives is stored ABSOLUTE:
  - the sky-view LUT, the aerial LUT, and the cloud march and cloud env terms (× K at the `SUN_ILLUMINANCE` sites);
  - the ground's direct term;
  - hence the env cube and the IBL;
  - the sun disc intensity;
  - the sun light (`base × K`);
  - the clear part of `zenith_radiance`.
- **Stored clamps become `min(100·K, 60000)`.** That is exactly today's 100 at K = 1, and it cannot overflow RGBA16F at any K.
  - The circumsolar sky clips at 60,000 nits when K is large. That is documented and accepted: the analytic sun light carries the sun's energy, and the IBL is not supposed to (the "sun-disc firefly rule").
- **The background's `min(sky, 30000) * preExposure`** (both background shaders) becomes a clamp in working space after exposure (`WS_SCENE_MAX`; check that it fits fp16). This bounds captures, which render at pre-exposure 1, when the sun disc is 20·K. Neither clamp binds at defaults.
- **The night terms are authored ABSOLUTE and do not take K:** the floor (0.03), the stars, the moon disc, and the moon light (`sun_base × 0.04 × …`, with no K). At K = 1 nothing changes. At photometric K they already sit near real night levels (the floor about 0.03 nits, moonlight about 0.12 lux), so night stays night, and Purkinje sees real scotopic luminances.
- **Sun light policy:** intensity = `sun_base_intensity × K`.
  - At K = 1 every app is unchanged (3, 6 or 10).
  - A photometric app sets `sun_base_intensity = SKY_SUN_ILLUMINANCE` so its light agrees with its sky. The header states this beside both fields.
  - Fix the stale claim in `include/view.glsl:10-20`, which says the procedural sun is 10 lux and matches `sky.c`.
- **Plumbing:**
  - render CLI `--sky-scale <f|photometric>`;
  - `.cscn` `environment.sky_scale`;
  - a config row;
  - a GUI "Photometric" checkbox that switches K between 1 and the constant. An arbitrary K is not a meaningful slider.

### C. silent's day, on both
- **Always:** `radiance_scale = SKY_PHOTOMETRIC_SCALE`, `sun_base_intensity = SKY_SUN_ILLUMINANCE`, lights left in nits and candela.
- **By day:**
  - `overcast = 1`;
  - the fog ambient taken from the sky (drop the 400-nit override);
  - a daylight exposure: a physical camera, or metered within bounds;
  - no indoor haze (kept from 13.6).
- **At night:** unchanged in character, since the night terms do not take K. The first check is how far the night frame moves.
- **Delete** 13.6's parked constants and comments (`EXPOSURE_DAY` and the relative-scale note).

## As built

### 1. Overcast core
- `SkyAtmosphere.overcast` (clamped by `sky_overcast_amount`), and the dome in one C helper, `sky_overcast_zenith_at(sun_y)`, which the LUT and aerial bakes are handed from the sun they bake for.
- **One weight, `sky_clear_fraction`,** is what the sun light, the moon light, the sun disc, the stars and the moon disc fade by. The LUT, the ground, the aerial volume and the zenith mix with the same weight.
- The cloud march took a `sunScale` parameter, so both its callers pass the clear fraction.
- `--overcast <f>` and `--sky-probe` on the render app.
- **Identity:** the aerial fixture renders 0 px run to run (the floor), 0 px between `--overcast 0` and no flag, and 0 px against the committed aerial and cloud goldens, whose shaders this phase touched.
- **Numbers:** the probe matches a Python twin of Krochmann and CIE to float precision:
  - Lz = 0.118959 at 35 degrees, 0.0380323 at 10, and 0.00086976 at -4 (the twilight constant alone);
  - zenith = (7/9) Lz at full overcast;
  - the sun light is 0 and casts nothing at full overcast, and is halved and still casting at 0.5.

### 1b. Review (thermo-nuclear and /simplify, both in the background)
- **The deck became one struct.** `sky_clear_fraction` and the per-program overcast uniforms are gone. `SkyDeck` = {clear, sun_scale, zenith, floor} is computed once in `sky_deck_at`, and `sky_bind_deck` is its one upload, to every program that includes `include/sky_deck.glsl`.
  - The dome terms are premultiplied by the amount, so a clear sky adds exact zeros and multiplies by exactly 1.
  - `sun_scale` is split from `clear` now so that phase 4's K has one place to land.
- **A bug the review found:** the virtual ground applied the overcast weight twice, so it was not linear in the amount. It is now `(1 - a)·a·deckFloor`.
- `--overcast` is range-checked (0..1).
- **Skipped:** the plan's `if (overcast > 0.0)` guards. The premultiplied terms already make the default path exact without a branch, and a branch around unchanged arithmetic is not byte-identical on this driver.
- **Measured:** all 31 goldens are 0 px. The o=1 and o=0.5 frames moved by at most 1 LSB, in the sky only; that is the dome's arithmetic re-associated.

### 2. LOOK
- **Shown:** the sweep, a low sun, dusk, and clouds with overcast, under auto exposure and under a fixed one.
- **Overcast reads darker under auto exposure.** The render app's sun (10) is 3.3 times the sky's own (3), and auto exposure only darkens. Phases 4 and 6 are what address that.
- **Defaults stated, with no objection:**
  - the dome stays neutral grey;
  - no extra shadow softening, because the sun light's fade already drops the shadows;
  - silent's street is judged in phase 6.

### 3. Overcast plumbing and arms
- **Plumbing:**
  - `.cscn` `environment.overcast`, which the CLI beats;
  - config row `sky.overcast` on the `_apply_sun_angle` hook;
  - a GUI "Overcast" slider on the `sun_moved`/`sun_released` chain.
- **The `overcast` gate group has ten arms, all green.**
  - The three frame levels sit on the probe's Lz: the sky at 26 and 9 degrees within 0.5% of E·Lz(1+2 sin h)/3; the foreground's IBL lift 0.1109 against albedo·(7/9)·Lz·E = 0.1107; dense fog 0.9983 of the zenith radiance.
  - At o=1 the frame is 0 px between sun azimuths 40 and 220, against 478,551 px at o=0.5.
- **Falsified:** pointing the aerial dome term at the zenith (`deckDome(1.0)`) fails `overcast-aerial` at 1.366 against a bar of 1, and moves no other arm.
- **Where the arms differ from the plan:**
  - **`overcast-seam` became `overcast-aerial`.** This fixture's world edge never reaches T = 0, and the clear sky has the same step: at o=0 the far ground is 1.77 times the sky over it. So "no step at the horizon" is not true here at any overcast. With the IBL off, the edge shows the aerial dome term alone, (1 - T)·Lz/3, which must lie above 0 and at or below Lz/3. It measures 0.455.
  - **`overcast-ground` and `overcast-fog` are LEVELS against the probe,** not the plan's "the path is live". A liveness check passes on a dome with the wrong constant.
  - **`overcast-azimuth` is new.** It is the frame-wide statement that nothing sun-driven survives the deck.
  - **No separate config pin.** `config-perturb` reproduces `sky.overcast` (it is not among its 13 exceptions), and `config-coverage` holds the slider to its row.
- **`overcast-slice` cannot see a slicer that reads the live sun instead of the latched one.** Under a still sun the two agree. The docstring says so.

### 4. Scale mechanism (with phase 5's plumbing folded in)
- **`SkyAtmosphere.radiance_scale` K, BY FUNCTION, default 1.** It rides the deck rather than a second upload:
  - `sun_scale` = clear·K; `zenith` and `floor` take K; and a new `store_max` = min(100·K, 60000) replaces the literal 100 in the sky-view and aerial LUTs.
  - The cloud march's return is held under `store_max` too. Both of its callers store fp16, and its ambient alone is 1.5 times the sky it samples. It cannot bind at K = 1, where cloud radiance is a few units.
- **The background's clamp moved into working space,** `min(sky * preExposure, WS_SCENE_MAX)` in both background shaders. A photometric disc is 850,000 nits, which an exposure brings back to range and an absolute 30,000 would have flattened first.
- **What does not take K:** the night floor, the stars, the moon disc and the moon light.
- **The sun light is `sun_base_intensity × K`.** The body function is unchanged; the sun passes K as its scale.
- **Constants:** `SKY_SUN_ILLUMINANCE`, `SKY_SUN_KLUX` and `SKY_PHOTOMETRIC_SCALE` = 127500/3 = 42,500 moved to `sky.h`, because an app matching its sun to the sky needs them. The plan's 128000/3 was a rounding of the same number.
- **`include/view.glsl`'s "ONE EXCEPTION" paragraph is rewritten.** It is the note spec 10.2 left open, and it now says what the scale does.
- **Plumbing, done here rather than in phase 5 because the scale arm needed the scene key:**
  - `--sky-scale <f|photometric>`;
  - `.cscn` `environment.sky_scale` (a positive number or "photometric", refused by name otherwise);
  - config row `sky.radiance_scale` on `_apply_sun_angle`;
  - a GUI "Photometric" checkbox (a click is a move and a release);
  - `scale=` on `--sky-probe`'s sun row.
- **The render app keeps its sun base of 10 at any K.** That makes its photometric session 3.3 times sun-heavy against its own sky, exactly as its relative one is, which is what invariance asks. Matching the sun to the sky is a photometric app's choice (`SKY_SUN_ILLUMINANCE`), and silent makes it in phase 6.
- **Identity:** all 31 goldens are 0 px at K = 1, and the overcast and config groups are green.
- **Invariance:** the `scale` group's new `sky` entry is aerial_fixture at x1000 with fog on and the sun turned into frame, so the disc and the zenith march (which reaches a frame only through the fog ambient) are read.
  - It measures peak 1 LSB (12,466 px round differently) against the group's 1 LSB bar.
  - `_scale_emitters` scales a sky scene's `sky_scale` and NOT its `environment.intensity`, which the sky already carries.
- **Falsified:** dropping K from the zenith march's clear part fails `sky` at 245,841 px, peak 2 LSB. That is a narrow catch, because this fog is mostly sun-lit.

### 5. LOOK (render app, aerial_fixture)
- **Day.**
  - Relative at -E 1 and photometric under a Sunny-16 camera (f/16, 1/125 s, ISO 100: EV 15) read alike. Photometric is 8% brighter, which is the gap between 1/(1.2·2^15) and 1/K.
  - Under auto exposure the two are the same picture.
  - At full overcast, relative with auto exposure is nearly black, because the gain only darkens. Photometric meters to a grey overcast day. That difference is the reason for the scale.
- **Dusk.**
  - At +10 the frames are identical, and at -14 they are identical again: the floor, the stars and the moon light carry no K, so a night frame is the same on either scale.
  - In between, photometric keeps a lit twilight (purple at -4, blue with the stars out at -8) where relative has already gone black. The twilight scatter now sits K above the absolute floor, and the meter can open for it.
- Sheets: `day_sheet.png` and `dusk_sheet.png` in the session scratchpad's `overcast/look5/`.

### 6. silent's day (awaiting the user's look)
- **The sky is photometric always.**
  - By day: `overcast = 1` and `sun_base_intensity = SKY_SUN_ILLUMINANCE`, so the sun agrees with the sky and delivers nothing through the deck.
  - At night the base stays 0, as 13.6 shipped it. The moon's light rides that base, so the street is lit by its lamps and the night floor alone. The zero dates from the app's first commit and was never a decision, but the night was approved with it.
- **The night is unchanged:** 639 px (kitchen) and 107 px (street), peak 1 LSB, against a 0 px floor measured on both poses. It is the -18 degree twilight scatter now carrying K, which is invisible.
- **By day the fog ambient comes from the sky:** the dome's (7/9)·Lz, about 3,950 nits at 35 degrees. The 400-nit override is gone. The night keeps its authored 5-nit veil.
- **The day exposure is metered, within a bound.** The camera is 1, the key 0.45, and the metered luminance is floored at log2(key/0.02), so a room never opens past the night's pin. The kitchen sits exactly on that bound: by day it is exposed as at night, plus the window's daylight.
- **Deleted:** `EXPOSURE_DAY`, the relative-scale note, and `--day`'s "unfinished" in the usage.
- **A trap met on the way:** the meter maps the ABSOLUTE metered luminance to the key and then multiplies the camera. A camera of 0.02 under a meter therefore made the day 50 times too dark (the street went navy black). The cap has to be the meter's floor, not the camera.
- **Key 0.18 against 0.45:** 0.18 puts the fog world at middle grey. 0.45 (+1.3 stops, the usual fog and snow compensation) reads light grey. The kitchen does not move between them, since it sits on the bound. Which to keep is taste.
- Sheets in the session scratchpad's `overcast/look6/`:
  - `silent_final_sheet.png`: 13.6 against now for the kitchen by day, the street by day, and the kitchen at night;
  - `day_v3_sheet.png`: key 0.18 against 0.45.

- **The full gate suite** on phase 6's build: 85 of 86 groups green. `ladder-fire` failed with no probe row for one texture while five review agents shared the machine, and passed alone on the same binary: a load flake, not a sky change.
- **Five reviews** (thermo-nuclear, plus /simplify's reuse, simplification, efficiency and altitude), all in the background and all waited for. Taken:
  - **K in the sun light's FULL level, not its fade.** The cast floor is a thousandth of the full level, so with K as a fade a photometric sun kept its cascades down to 2.4e-8 of its light. `overcast-sun` now asserts a photometric sun at o = 0.9995 is lit (212.5 lux) and not casting. Putting K back in the fade fails it: `casts 1.0`.
  - **`SkyDeck` renamed `SkyEmission`,** with `sky*` uniforms and `include/sky_emission.glsl`. "Deck" is the volumetric cloud layer's word in `clouds.glsl`, and the struct carries the scale as well as the overcast. The body function no longer computes the emission itself; each caller passes its full level, its own factors and the clear fraction.
  - **`include/sky_constants.glsl`:** `SKY_SUN_ILLUMINANCE`, `SKY_SUN_KLUX` and `SKY_STORE_FP16_MAX`, included by `sky.h` and `atmosphere.glsl`. The GLSL hand mirror is deleted, since apps now compute with the constant.
  - **Every sky target held under fp16 at its WRITE:** view LUT, aerial volume, both env programs and the cloud march. The one clamp on the scatter term never bounded the stored sum, because the dome, the floor and a cloud composite are added after it. The scatter keeps its own K-relative ceiling of 100·K, the historical 100 at K = 1.
  - **`sky_radiance_scale`,** an accessor clamping to `SKY_RADIANCE_SCALE_MIN..MAX`. The flag and the file refuse outside the same range. `environment.overcast` is range-checked too; a -0.2 used to reach the sky as a silent clear.
  - **K in the config snapshot's `source` block,** applied before the load. It changes the sky's units, and a scene-captured probe is shot once. The GUI checkbox stays: a live flip leaves such probes as shot and logs it by probe, as an overcast drag does.
  - **silent drops its moon Light.** Its only effect was reading zero off the night's zeroed sun base. The base is now `SKY_SUN_ILLUMINANCE` in both modes, and the exposure setup is one if/else.
  - **`gates.py`:**
    - the row lookup goes through `_cscn_camera` and `_projector`;
