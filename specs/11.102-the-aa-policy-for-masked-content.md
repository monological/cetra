# Spec 11.102 — The AA policy for masked content (roadmap row 64, G3)

## Context

Spec 11.101 measured where the live-path flicker on masked (alpha-tested) content actually comes
from: the render app's interactive session runs **TAA at MSAA 1**, and at one sample a binary
alpha test under a clamped TAA history cannot hold fractional coverage — the ladder fixture reads
0.16–0.22 of the stored coverage and churns, where **MSAA 4 + alpha-to-coverage keeps 0.89 at 8
flips a frame**. 11.101 shipped the jitter as a palliative for the one-sample path and booked the
policy question as G3: *should a masked material in the scene make the interactive default
MSAA 4 + TAA, what does that cost on forest, and does the one-sample path keep the palliative?*

Exploration settled more of it than the row expected, and corrected the row twice:

- **The row is wrong about `apps/tree`.** It says tree "runs MSAA 4 + TAA and never had the
  problem". Tree runs **MSAA 1 + TAA unconditionally** (`apps/tree/src/tree.c:1187`, commit
  6ff9082b, 2026-08-27, during 11.88) — chosen for cost AND because A2C on near-black leaves
  against a sunset produces a *correct* partial-coverage fringe that reads as an orange outline
  (2,616 rim px, none brighter than a straight blend). `docs/cli-reference.md:374-376` carries the
  same stale claim ("this app runs 4x MSAA *and* TAA").
- **Forest's cost is already measured.** Spec 11.34 (1600x900/60f, opaque GPU row, 0.23% floor):
  **121.6 ms at 1 sample, 353.2 at 2 (A2C on), 539.8 at 4 (A2C on)** — "A2C alone costs 202 ms;
  the fragment set explodes when foliage stops discarding." Promotion is unaffordable there; the
  phase-1 A/B re-confirms at head (forest is a different world since 11.63/11.98) rather than
  discovers.
- **11.31's "genuinely looks better" on the raiden groom does not transfer.** It was measured at
  the accidental 2-sample target AND under the 0.02 threshold 11.87 later called a bug ("the 0.02
  is in the wrong space") — strands came back partly because of the defect. Re-measure with crops.
- **The one-sample request is genuinely one sample since 11.34** (`engine.c:581-584`), so
  "A2C for free on the accidental second sample" is not an option.
- **The render app loads the scene before its AA policy runs** (`render.c:3158/3164` vs `:4191`),
  has no runtime scene reload, and the config snapshot applies AFTER the policy (`:4231`), so a
  restored `msaa_samples` still wins. A load-time content check is sufficient and safe.

So the answer to (b) is a number already in the tree, the answer to (c) follows from it (forest
and tree stay on the one-sample path, so the palliative keeps its consumers), and (a) is a
change to ONE app: the render app, whose scenes are single models and fixtures where four samples
are affordable and where the defect was surfaced.

## The design

**One engine fact, one app policy, headless made explicit.**

1. **`scene_has_masked_material(const Scene*)`** in `cetra/src/scene.c/h` — one loop over
   `scene->materials[i]->alpha_mode == ALPHA_MASK`. The material list is complete at that point
   (assimp import and `.cscn` material blocks set `alpha_mode` synchronously, `import.c:461-470`;
   only textures stream). Precedent for "a scene fact gates a default": OIT on `oit_mesh_count`,
   SSS inert without `subsurface > 0`.

2. **The render app's TAA policy chooses the sample count from content.** `render.c:4191`:
   ```c
   if (!args.headless || args.force_taa) {
       const bool masked = scene_has_masked_material(scene);
       set_engine_msaa_samples(engine, masked ? MASKED_MSAA_SAMPLES : 1);
       set_engine_taa_enabled(engine, true);
       // one log line naming the decision and the override
   }
   if (args.msaa > 0) set_engine_msaa_samples(engine, args.msaa);   // unchanged, still after
   ```
   `MASKED_MSAA_SAMPLES` is 4 unless phase 1 shows 2 clears the ladder's A2C bars (rule below).
   **The promotion rides the TAA policy, not `!headless` alone**, deliberately: a headless
   `--taa` run then reproduces the window's AA (the whole point of `--taa` as a diagnostic), and
   the decision is testable headless through `--config-dump`. The cost is that every gate arm
   that renders a masked fixture under `--taa` without `--msaa` currently gets the one-sample
   path by accident and must now **say `--msaa 1`** — which `mask-samples`' own comment already
   argues is the honest form. Headless without `--taa` (all 29 goldens) is untouched: MSAA 4,
   TAA off.
   `--msaa N` overrides either way; the GUI "MSAA 4x" checkbox and the snapshot's `msaa_samples`
   row remain the runtime levers (`set_engine_msaa_samples` rebuilds in place, `engine.c:794`).

3. **Forest and tree: no behaviour change.** Forest's comment at `forest.c:3222` says it
   "matches render's policy" — it no longer does; rewrite it to cite 11.34's number as the reason
   it stays at one sample. Tree's comment already states its two reasons.

4. **The palliative stays as shipped.** Its consumers are forest, tree, `--msaa 1`, and any
   unmasked-then-masked edge the policy cannot see; its gate (`alphaToCoverage == 0`) already
   turns it off on the promoted path. No shader change.

5. **A gate arm that asserts the policy** — `ladder-policy`, in the `ladder` group (the group
   about the live path for masked content): three cheap headless `--taa --config-dump` runs at
   400x300 / few frames, reading `engine.msaa_samples` from the JSON: the ladder fixture → the
   promoted count; `GPU_FIXTURE` (unmasked) → 1; the ladder fixture with `--msaa 1` → 1 (the
   override still wins). Docstring column added (gate-docs enforces both).

## Decision rules, pre-committed (phase 1 fills the numbers)

| question | instrument | rule |
|---|---|---|
| forest promotion | forest `--profiler`, 1600x900/60f, default vs `--msaa 4`, interleaved A/B/A/B/A/B, floor from adjacent A samples (`_timing_delta`'s shape) | refused if the opaque row more than doubles (11.34: 4.4x). Recorded, not decided by this spec's taste |
| 2 vs 4 samples for the render app | ladder `_ladder_pair` at `--taa --headless-jitter --msaa 2`: ratio (ladder-a2c's) and churn (ladder-still's) | ship the SMALLEST count clearing both `LADDER_A2C_MIN_RATIO 0.6` and `LADDER_A2C_CHURN_MAX 200`; expected 4 |
| render-app cost | raiden recipe + `ivy_arcade` (the user's scene) at 1600x1200 (the HiDPI window), `--taa --headless-jitter --profiler`, `--msaa 1/2/4`, interleaved, floor first | no bar — a viewer's frame; the numbers go in the row so the policy is priced |
| the fringe | 300% point-filtered crops, 1 vs 4 samples + TAA, ivy leaf edge and raiden strand against the brightest background | **hold point: the user's eye**, before phase 2. Tree's orange outline is the precedent |

## Phases (one commit each; scoped gates between; full suite + goldens at the end)

1. **Spec** — `specs/11.102-the-aa-policy-for-masked-content.md`, committed alone on the branch.
2. **Measure** (no code): the four rows above. Forest re-price at head; ladder at 2 samples;
   raiden + ivy cost at 1/2/4; the crops. **Stop and show the crops** before going on. Fill the
   spec's ledger and fix `MASKED_MSAA_SAMPLES`.
3. **The fact + the policy** — `scene_has_masked_material`, the render.c site, the log line, the
   help text at `render.c:312`, forest's comment. Falsifiable by hand: `--config-dump` headless
   with `--taa` reads 4 on the ladder fixture and 1 on `dir_shadow_fixture`.
4. **The gates** — enumerate every arm passing `--taa` on a fixture with a MASK material
   (`alpha_ladder`, `alphacov`, `mask`, `translucent_shadow`, `sss_masked`, and any `.cscn`
   importing a masked model) and pin `--msaa 1` where the arm's claim is the one-sample path
   (ladder-churn is the known one); each pinned arm's printed number must be unchanged. Add
   `ladder-policy`. Run `--only ladder`, `--only mask`, `--only alphacov` and whichever groups
   the enumeration touches.
5. **Docs + closeout** — roadmap row 64 (correct the tree claim, DONE with the numbers) and the
   tail; `docs/cli-reference.md` (render `--msaa`/`--taa` gain the policy; tree's entry loses
   "runs 4x MSAA *and* TAA"); AGENTS.md's TAA paragraph gains the sample-count sentence;
   `docs/verification.md`'s ladder row (ladder-churn now pins `--msaa 1`); `specs/11.101` limits
   note pointing here. Full suite + 29 goldens.

## Risks

- **A masked material that is not foliage** (raiden's `Hair_1`, `translucent_shadow_fixture`'s
  one MASK material among BLEND) promotes the whole scene to four samples for one mesh. That is
  the rule working as stated; the cost row prices it on raiden, and `--msaa 1` is one flag away.
- **The fringe.** A2C's partial coverage against extreme contrast is correct AA that tree refused
  on look. The crops are the hold point; if the user refuses it on ivy, the escape is to keep the
  policy at 1 and record G3 as "documented, not promoted" — a legitimate closing of the row.
- **Gate enumeration misses one `--taa` masked arm** → it silently starts measuring MSAA 4. The
  full suite at the end catches a moved number; the enumeration is by grep, not memory.
- **Driver rounds `--msaa 2` up** (`engine.h:93-96` says above 1 the count is the driver's
  answer): the ladder measurement at 2 reads `msaa_samples_actual` from the log before trusting
  the row.
