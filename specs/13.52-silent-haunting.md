# Spec 13.52: the haunting

Branch and spec file `13.52-silent-haunting`, cut from `master`.

## Context

The user wants a scripted event in silent's player house, after P.T., that they can trigger
later from the story. For now it is bound to a key. In order:

1. The hall light turns **red and flickers**, and the TV static turns **red**.
2. The living-room lamp **flickers**.
3. Then the **basement door moves**, as if someone on the stair side were pushing and pulling
   the knob, with sound.

Extras the user chose:
- **The house goes quiet:** the clock stops, the fridge cuts out, and a drone fades in.
- **Footsteps** climb the cellar stair before the rattle.
- **The bathroom door creaks ajar.**
- **A red, crushed picture.**

Behaviour the user chose:
- If the basement door is open when the haunting starts, it **slams shut**.
- The rattle **stops dead** when the player comes within reach.
- After that, E opens the door normally onto the empty, lit stair.

It is one module with a begin/end API, so a story trigger can later replace the key.

## The module: `apps/silent/src/haunt.c/h`

```c
void haunt_start(Haunt*, const HauntHouse*, unsigned seed); // borrow lights/materials/doors/clock/tv/fridge; load sounds
void haunt_begin(Haunt*, double time, double into);          // `into` = seconds of timeline already past
void haunt_end(Haunt*);                                      // put back everything it drove
bool haunt_active(const Haunt*);
void haunt_step(Haunt*, double time, const vec3 feet);        // fixed step, after the doors
void haunt_frame(Haunt*, double time);                        // per frame
void haunt_rest(Haunt*, bool rest);                           // captures see the normal house
```

**Initial state.** `normal` holds the authored values, read from the objects at begin. `live`
holds what the last frame applied.

**Randomness.** Everything is a pure function of (seconds since onset, seed), like
`flicker_level` and `lights_brownout`, so a headless run repeats itself. `lights.c`'s `hash01`
moves to `kit.h` as `kit_hash01` so both modules share it.

**Flicker levels.**
- `hall_level`: a failing circuit. Sags, dropouts with one catch, stutters in 35 ms cells.
- `lamp_level`: a loose filament, out of step with the hall.
- Both have a floor of 0.03 and never reach 0. All three lights have cached shadows, and a
  cached light at intensity 0 leaves its shadow undrawn (`basement.c:722`).

**Rattle sync.** The door moves when the sound clacks. `fetch_sounds.py` writes
`src/door_takes.h`, a generated header in the same family as `cat_clips.h` and `cards.h`. It
carries each rattle and knob take's length and the times of its transients. The leaf's jolt
pulses are placed on those times. Each try plays a knob take, and a rattle take a beat later
under the leaf's pulls.

## Changes to existing files

**`door.c/h`**
- New fields:
  - `jolt`: radians past the swing; never saved.
  - `pace`: seconds for a full swing on the current move.
  - `slam`: driven linearly, with no ease.
- `pose()` subtracts `jolt`, and uses linear travel while slamming.
- New calls:
  - `door_jolt(door, angle)` places the leaf immediately, because `door_update` returns early
    when `travel == want`.
  - `door_drift(door, want, seconds)`.
  - `door_slam(door)`: re-maps travel so the pose doesn't jump, then shuts in 0.3 s.
- `door_toggle` and `door_set` clear `pace` and `slam`.
- **The jolt is set in the fixed step.** Kinematic bodies and nodes sync inside `game.c`'s
  step, so a jolt set there is drawn the same frame.

**`tv.c/h`**: `tv_tint(tv, filter)` owns all three places the TV's colour lives:
- `tvWhite` on the picture,
- the glass's `emissive`,
- the `tv_glow` panel's colour.

`tv_rest` applies white, so the TV rests itself.

**`mats.c/h`**: `mats_shade_slot(shade, at)` and `mats_shade_candela(shade, slot, cd)`. The
floor lamp and the bedside lamp share one lampshade material; the floor lamp is slot 0, found
by position.

**`clock.c/h`**: `clock_stop(clock, bool)`. It keeps the time it lost, so on restart the clock
runs late rather than jumping, and no beat fires while it is stopped.

**`kitchen.c/h`**: `kitchen_start_audio` returns the hum (today it is dropped).
`kitchen_hum(hum, running)` keeps the volume constant in `kitchen.c`.

**`basement.c/h`**: `basement_footfall(k, side, out)`, 13 placements. k = 0 is the cellar floor
at the flight's foot, then the 11 treads, then the head. The flight's numbers stay in
`basement.c`.

**`silent.c`**:
- `static Haunt g_haunt`, and keep the fridge's `Sound*`.
- Action `"haunt"`: key **K** plus pad right-stick click, so a `--pad-script` can toggle it
  headless.
- Flags, all in the usage text:
  - `--haunt`, which skips the title, since it changes doors a save keeps.
  - `--haunt-at S`: begin S seconds into the timeline.
  - `--trace-haunt`.
- Wiring:
  - `haunt_start` after `scene_set_capture_rest`.
  - `haunt_rest` in `rest_lights`.
  - `haunt_step` after the door loop in `on_update`.
  - K and `haunt_frame` in `on_pre_render`, after `basement_update`.

**The red picture**: an app post pass after the tone map (spec 13.29: `postfx_add_pass` with
`create_post_pass_program`). It draws colour toward luma × red by a weight. `grade_gamma`,
`grade_lift`, `vignette_*`, `grain_*` and `lut_strength` are blended alongside it, each lerped
from `normal`.
- PostFX has no saturation field, which is why the pass is needed.
- `grade_gain` is not touched: silent's fade owns it.

**`tools/fetch_sounds.py`**
- `events()` takes a per-job `join` gap (today a fixed 60 ms), so a rattle stays one burst.
- New jobs: `rattle` (02), `knob` (03), `slam` (11), plus `clunk` (573936, to audition).
- A `CUTS` table for fixed windows: `door_creak` is 426228 from 1.0 s to 4.0 s, faded,
  high-passed and levelled like every one-shot.
- New `RECORDINGS` rows for all four.
- It generates `src/door_takes.h`.

## Timeline (seconds from onset; one table in `haunt.c`)

| u | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 | The hall's two lanterns and their shared frosted glass go red `{1,.08,.04}` at about 2x intensity, flickering. The lamp flickers, staying warm. The TV goes red. The clock stops, the fridge clunks off, and a drone of 55, 55.6 and 110 Hz tones fades in over 6 s. The red picture fades in over 2.5 s. If the basement door is open, it slams. |
| 3.0 to ~15 | 13 footfalls climb the flight, about 0.95 s apart and slowing to 1.2 s near the top. They are `step_wood` voices at pitch 0.88, heavy, alternating sides, heard through the shut door's link. |
| 16.5 | The bathroom door, if shut, creaks a hand's width ajar over about 2 s. |
| 19.0 | The rattle starts: tries every 0.8 to 2.5 s, each a knob take and a rattle take with the leaf pulled up to 1 cm at the latch, and some tries shoves. It grows over 20 s. It only runs while the basement door is shut. |

**The quiet.** From the climb on, if the player's feet come within 2.2 m in plan of the door:
- The held sounds stop, the jolt goes to 0 and the drone cuts.
- Nothing more is scheduled.
- The lights stay red and the clock stays stopped.

E reaches only 1.9 m, so the quiet always comes before "Open door" can show.

**End (K again).** Everything is restored from `normal`: lights, glass, lamp, TV, clock, fridge,
drone, picture and jolt. The slammed door stays shut and the bathroom door stays ajar, since
those are real door states.

**Neither the haunting nor the jolt is saved.**

**A player behind the basement door at onset** gets the lights and the house going quiet, but
none of the door beats. A slam would sweep through them.

**Captures rest to the normal house**, so the GI cook key is unchanged and no red is baked in.

## As built

### Phase 1: the sounds

All picked by the user, by ear, from the audition's cuts of the four recordings:
- `door_rattle.wav`: event 1 of 444704, the 2.0 s try. It is the only rattle take, so every try
  plays it. The knob takes give the variety.
- `door_knob_1..4.wav`: events 0-3 of 727791, the four long tries (2.0, 1.05, 0.84 and 1.22 s).
  The game picks among them at random.
- `door_slam.wav`: event 0 of 571797.
- `fridge_clunk.wav`: event 0 of 573936. It plays as the fridge cuts out at onset.
- `door_creak.wav`: 426228 from 1.0 s to 4.0 s, through `CUTS`, a new table of hand-chosen
  windows. The event finder would trim a slow squeal's quiet ends.

Changes to `fetch_sounds.py`:
- A one-shot job's `join` gap (60 ms unless it says). The rattle, knob and clunk jobs use 0.2-0.3
  s, since a try at a locked door is a burst of clacks a tenth of a second or two apart.
- `door_takes.h` is GENERATED: each rattle and knob take's length, and its HITS (the peaks of a
  120 Hz envelope, at least 50 ms apart, standing a tenth of the loudest above their
  surroundings), at most 24 a take. The rattle take carries 15 hits; the knob takes 14, 8, 3 and
  10. The tool runs clang-format on it, so the commit hook leaves it alone.
- `take(name)` is the one way a `ONESHOTS` row becomes samples, shared by the writer and the
  header. The header's hits are therefore those of the file the game plays.

### Phase 2: the onset, and the look by eye

Built as planned:
- `haunt.c/h`, K / the right stick, `--haunt`, `--haunt-at`, `--trace-haunt`.
- The capture rest holds the normal house.
- `tv_tint` owns all three places the TV's colour lives.
- `mats_shade_slot` / `mats_shade_candela` drive the floor lamp's bulb in the shared shade.
- `kit_hash01` is the hash `lights.c` and `haunt.c` share.

Changed after the user looked at it:
- **The flicker is a dying bulb, not a strobe.** The first cut stuttered in 35 ms cells for most
  of every 2.2 s, and the user called it a strobe light, "not scary". Now each circuit has one
  episode a period, 5 s for the hall and 7 s for the lamp:
  - one or two blinks;
  - a sag to 0.3-0.6 that recovers slowly;
  - or a fall out of about a second that catches once.

  Every edge is eased over a few hundredths of a second, the way a filament cools.
- **More red.**
  - The hall is `{1, 0.02, 0.01}` at 2.5x. The glass keeps its own nits: at the light's gain it
    burned through the tone curve to pink.
  - The TV is `{1.8, 0.04, 0.03}`.
  - The red picture was brought forward from phase 3. `shaders/haunt_grade.glsl` is an app post
    pass after the tone map that drains the frame toward red (`hauntGrade`, 0.8 of the way). The
    vignette deepens to 0.85 and a 0.04 grain comes on, all faded in over 2.5 s once the hall is
    red. PostFX has no saturation field, so the pass is the only way to drain colour.
- **The night outside goes.** The user asked to stop light coming in through the windows:
  - The house's clear panes (`MAT_WINDOW_GLASS`) go black. A pane passes on what is behind it
    tinted by its albedo, so a black one shows nothing and keeps only its shine.
  - The street lamps (every light named `street_lamp`, and the `MAT_LAMP_GLOW` heads) go out.
    They cast no shadow, so their cold light reached the hall through the walls as well as the
    windows.
  - The panes and the lamps are part of `apply`, so captures rest them too.

### Phase 3: the house goes quiet

- At onset, the moment the lights die: `clock_stop` holds the pendulum mid-swing and keeps the
  time it lost, so on restart it runs late rather than jumping. `kitchen_fridge_run` silences the
  hum with `fridge_clunk.wav` at the motor.
- The drone is four looping tones, heard whole, rising over 6 s: 55 and 55.6 Hz (a 0.6 Hz throb),
  the tritone 77.8, and 110.9 for speakers that give nothing under 100 Hz.
- An audio dump from the hall: the clock's ticks stop at onset, and the room's RMS goes from
  about 0.013 to 0.025-0.033 as the drone comes up.

### Phase 4: the doors

Built as planned: the climb, the creak, the tries with the leaf jolted on each take's hits, the
slam of an open cellar door, and the quiet within 2.2 m in plan.

Changed after the user played it:
- **The door beats wait to be watched for, not timed from K.** Run from where the user stood,
  the timeline played out while nobody was in the hall to hear it. Now the lights, the quiet house
  and the drone still begin at K, but the door beats start the first time the player stands in the
  front of the hall (short of the pendant, `WATCH_Z1`) looking down it within about 30 degrees,
  1.5 s or more after K. That moment is the timeline's 0: the slam if the cellar door is open, the
  climb at 3 s, the creak at 16.5, the tries from 19. It fires once a haunting. `haunt_step` takes
  the player's forward for it. `--haunt-at S` above 0 still starts the beats S seconds in without
  waiting. Checked headless: at (-0.75, 11) facing down the hall it starts at 1.517 s with the
  first footfall at 3.017; facing the front door it never starts.
- **The bathroom door creaks either way.** It drifted ajar only if it was shut, so a second
  haunting, with the door left ajar by the first, had no creak. Now an open door drifts shut over
  3 s, the creak's length, under the same sound.
- **The mix.** The drone covered the door beats, so it was halved, and the climb (5.0), the knob
  and rattle (3.5), the creak (1.5) and the slam (2.0) were raised.
- K is a toggle: a second press ends the haunting and a third begins a new one, every beat armed
  again.
- **The onset is a power cut to black, then the red.** The user saw the lights change colour
  several times on the way to red: the hall went dark, came back red under the house's warm bounce
  light, and then the picture's red grade faded in over 2.5 s. Now at K everything electric goes
  out at once and stays out for 1.5 s:
  - the hall pair, the floor lamp and the TV (its hiss too);
  - the bathroom bulb, the bedside lamp, the kitchen tubes and the cellar bulb, which stay out for
    the whole haunting (`lights_set_mains`, `Basement.mains`);
  - the light that comes from no lamp: the GI bounce (a new engine switch, `GIWorld.hidden`: not
    sampled, still captured and kept), the reflection probes (intensity 0), the sky's light
    (`ibl->intensity` 0, or every glossy surface reflected the night sky whole once the bounce was
    gone) and the fog's glow (`fog_ambient` 0, which lit the rooms as a haze and filled the windows
    with the street's fog).

  The red grade comes in at full under the dark, and the hall, the lamp and the TV rise over
  1.2 s already red. The blackout frame measures a mean of 1.5 and a maximum of 12 of 255, the
  bedroom candle's glint at the hall's end. Every capture rests all of it to the house as it was,
  and K again puts it all back.
- **No climb, and the tries come sooner, harder and longer.** The user did not want the
  footsteps, and asked for the door to be rattled more aggressively and the knob tried for
  longer after the creak. The climb is gone, with `basement_footfall`. The creak moves from
  16.5 s to 3 s after the hall is watched, and the tries from 19 s to 6 s. A try now turns the
  knob 2 to 4 times back to back, never the same take twice running, and from the second try on
  shakes the door all through it, one rattle take after another. The leaf travels 3 cm at the
  latch, up from 1.2. The knob plays at 4.0 and the rattle at 5.0, up from 3.5 each. Both grow
  from 0.8 to 1.2 of that over 20 s, where they grew from 0.7 to 1. The gaps between tries are
  0.4 to 1.4 s, down from 0.8 to 2.5.
- **The quiet waits for the tries and for the player to come to the door.** It used to fire as
  soon as the climb began if the player was already near the cellar door. Now it fires once the
  tries have begun, on the step the player comes within 2.2 m. A player already standing there
  has the door tried in front of them until they step away and come back, or open it.
- **A brighter red.** With the bounce gone the hall is lit by its two lights alone, and the user
  asked for more. `HALL_GAIN` went from 2.5 to 6. At the same moment of the haunting, the frame's
  mean red was 19.6 of 255 with the bounce at 2.5, 10.4 without it, and is 15.9 at 6.
- **The door is tried until it is opened, and then flies open.** Coming near no longer stops
  anything: the tries go on until the player presses E at the cellar door. Then they and the
  drone stop dead, and the door is flung open in 0.3 s and slams against its stop. `door_slam`
  takes the way it is flung, shut or open. The stair's bulb comes on red at 4 times its light,
  swinging three times as wide as the door's draft swings it and dying away three times as
  slowly: `Basement.tint` and `gain`, and `basement_swing`, whose `gust` a load puts back to the
  door's own. Opening it before the tries begin, once the beats have, does the same.

## Phase 6 (plan): the hall's light, its fittings, and a taller ground floor

Asked for after the user walked phase 4 at night. With the bounce gone the hall was lit only where
its two lights fell, the wall lantern's wall burned and the cellar door could not be seen; and the
fittings, frosted boxes on iron, looked cheap.

1. **The bounce returns with the red.** The lit surface multiplies its whole ambient -- the GI
   bounce, the probes' reflections and the sky's -- by the environment's strength,
   `ibl->intensity` (`envScale` in `pbr_frag`). That is the haunting's one dial for the light from
   no lamp: 0 in the blackout, then the hall's own level, failing with it. `GIWorld.hidden` and
   zeroing each probe become redundant, and come out once a render shows the blackout as black
   without them; the engine is then untouched.
2. **The home's ground storey 0.5 m taller**, 3.1 m clear. `CEIL_Y` and the storey above it are
   shared with the mansion, whose GI grid is fitted to them, so the home takes heights of its own
   in `home.h` and every home use moves to them: its walls, slabs, roof, upper windows, cornice,
   sound rooms, GI box, probe boxes, the kitchen's hood and tubes, the bathroom's bulb, the cellar
   stair's bulb, and the hall's lights.
3. **The hall's fittings, remade.** One builder for the pendant over the clock and the wall lantern:
   an aged brass cage, metal and glossy so it reflects, clear glass panes of the jars' kind, which
   cast nothing for a cached light, and a filament bulb in a brass socket, the light's body the
   bulb's. The haunting drives the filament's glow where it drove the frosted glass.
4. **The pendant swings.** It becomes a node of its own about its ceiling rose, as the cellar
   stair's bulb is, its light following with `shadow_follow` while the swing is wide, held straight
   at rest. Still in the normal house; swinging once the haunting begins.

Each step is looked at before the next.
