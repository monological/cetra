# cetra 14.28: lines and convex shapes in the UI

## Context

pcb 7.4.2 (controlled impedance) shows, in its board modal, each impedance profile's impedance and
loss against frequency, one curve per copper layer, and a profile's trace drawn as Altium's picture
of it: a trapezoid, its top narrower than its foot by the fab's etch, with dimension marks and their
arrowheads. The modal is UI: a modal screen whose panels are drawn by draw hooks.

The UI draws only what `_push_quad` makes, a quad with its sides along the axes: fills, rounded
boxes, images and glyphs. A curve, a slanted side and an arrowhead cannot be drawn. The ways round it
each fail:

- **Many small quads along a curve** draw a staircase, and their coverage is a box's, not the
  curve's: a slope reads lumpy and heavier where it is steep.
- **The canvas** (spec 14.0) has polylines with caps, joins and dashes, but is an engine overlay of
  its own, which pcb orders under the UI's (`UI_OVERLAY_ORDER`, 100) so its screens cover its views:
  a modal's panel covers a canvas behind it, and one canvas over the UI would cover every screen.
- **An element's own program** (`ui_set_draw_program`), its curve in a texture: a shader and an
  upload for every graph, and a program change breaks the batch.
- **A picture drawn on the CPU** and shown with `ui_draw_textured_quad`: blurred at any other scale,
  and uploaded again whenever it changes.

Branch and spec: `14.28-lines-and-convex-shapes-in-the-ui`, off cetra's master (`89b10d88`).

## The design

Two primitives in the UI's draw list, in its one vertex format and its one program, batching with
the fills as they do, under the same edge rule as every UI shape (spec 14.23): a one-pixel ramp
centred on the edge, `clamp(0.5 - d / aa)`, `aa` a pixel's size in points.

### The API (`ui.h`, beside `ui_draw_rounded`)

```c
// A point in window points, top-left origin, +Y down.
typedef struct UIPoint {
    float x, y;
} UIPoint;

// A straight line from a to b, `width` points wide, its ends round: every point within width / 2 of
// the segment. A line thinner than a pixel is drawn a pixel wide at its width's share of its alpha.
void ui_draw_line(UIDrawList* dl, UIPoint a, UIPoint b, float width, vec4 color);
// Lines through `count` points, each to the next. The round ends join them with no notch; where two
// meet, a colour with alpha below 1 is laid twice over the join.
void ui_draw_polyline(UIDrawList* dl, const UIPoint* points, size_t count, float width, vec4 color);
// A filled convex shape of 3 or 4 corners, in order either way round. Any other count, a shape not
// convex or one of no area draws nothing and is logged.
void ui_draw_convex(UIDrawList* dl, const UIPoint* corners, size_t count, vec4 color);
```

`UIPoint` rather than cglm's `vec2`: a `vec2*` will not take a `const` array in C11 without a
warning, and the UI's other shapes are its own structs (`UIRect`).

### One place geometry enters

`_push_quad` takes a quad's sides along the axes. It becomes `_push`, taking four corners in any
position (a triangle's last repeated), with `_push_quad` the axis-aligned call to it. It stays the
only place geometry enters the list, so the clip, the offset, the alpha and the batch are applied to
a line and a convex shape exactly as to a fill. Every primitive stays four vertices, which
`ui_draw_list_quad_count` and `ui_draw_list_quad` count on. The fills, images and glyphs it pushes
are the vertices it pushes now, byte for byte.

### The vertex: no new attribute

The vertex keeps its 80 bytes and its six attributes; the two new modes use fields the fill's corner
rule does not, as the glyph's mode already does with `params.w`:

| Field | Fill, image, glyph | Line (mode 3) | Convex (mode 4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rect` | the box measured against | the line's bounds | the shape's bounds |
| `params.x` | corner radius | half the drawn width | unused |
| `params.z` | mode | 3 | 4 |
| `border` | the border's colour | the two ends, a.xy and b.xy | this corner's distance to each edge |

`rect` stays each shape's bounds for every mode, so `ui_draw_list_quad` reads back a line or a
convex shape as the rect it covers, with kind 3 or 4. `ui_draw_list_quad_border` returns false for
both: they have no border. The field's comment says what it holds in each mode.

### A line (`UI_MODE_LINE`)

- **Its geometry** is a quad along the segment, reaching past it on every side by half the width
  and one pixel, so its round ends and its ramp's outer half are drawn. The ends move with the draw
  list's offset as the rounded corner's rect does.
- **Its fragment**: `d` is the distance to the segment less half the width, the coverage the ramp's.
  A line of no length is a dot of its width.
- **A line thinner than a pixel** is drawn a pixel wide, its alpha multiplied by its width in
  pixels: the ramp alone would cover a pixel by half at any width below one, so a hairline would
  draw as heavy as a one-pixel line. Its coverage then sums to its area.

### A convex shape (`UI_MODE_CONVEX`)

- **Its geometry** is its bounding box a pixel past, as a fill's is, so the ramp's outer half is
  drawn.
- **Each vertex carries its signed distance to each edge**, positive inside, in points; a
  triangle's fourth is a large constant that never limits. A distance to a line is affine in the
  position, so interpolated across the box it is exact at every fragment.
- **Its coverage is the product of the edges' ramps**, `clamp(0.5 + d_i / aa)`: the box filter's
  coverage of a corner between edges at right angles, so a convex shape of four corners on whole
  pixels draws as `ui_draw_quad` of the same rect does.
- **Refused**: a count other than 3 or 4; corners turning both ways (a cross product of consecutive
  edges of each sign); a shape of no area. Logged, nothing drawn, as a mutator does.

### The shader (`ui_frag.glsl`)

Two branches before the fill's, `UI_MODE_LINE` 3 and `UI_MODE_CONVEX` 4, beside the C defines of
the same names, each ending in the fill's discard bound and straight colour. `aa` is the fill's,
`length(dFdx(vPos))`. No uniform, sampler or attribute is added, and a custom program draws what it
is given, as it does a fill.

## Tests

A case in `apps/canvas`, beside `--ui-seams` (spec 14.23), and an arm in the `canvas` group of
`gates.py`, its docstring in the list:

- **`--ui-lines`** -> `canvas-ui-lines`. On an opaque backdrop of one colour, shapes of another,
  each printed by the probe as its geometry in pixels. A pixel's coverage is how far it lies from the
  backdrop toward the shape's colour.
  - `level`: a line 2 pixels wide, level and centred on a pixel boundary: every pixel of its two
    rows, its round ends aside, is its colour within 2 codes, and the rows above and below are the
    backdrop's.
  - `slant`: a line 2 pixels wide and 100 long at 30 degrees: its coverage summed over the picture
    is its area, length times width plus its ends' disc, within 2%.
  - `hair`: a line 0.5 pixels wide at 30 degrees: its coverage sums to its area within 5%.
  - `joined`: a polyline of three segments turning 60 degrees at each join: the pixel at each join
    is its colour within 2 codes.
  - `trapezoid`: a convex shape of four corners, its sides slanted and its edges part way into
    pixels: its coverage sums to its area within 1%.
  - `square`: a convex shape on whole pixels and `ui_draw_quad` of the same rect, side by side: the
    two match pixel for pixel within 1 code.
  - `meeting`: two triangles sharing a diagonal edge, in one colour: no pixel along it shows more
    than a quarter of the backdrop, the limit `docs/ui.md` states for two shapes meeting part way
    into a pixel.
  - `refused`: a concave shape of four corners and one of five: the pixels where they would be are
    the backdrop's.
- `canvas-ui-seams`, `canvas-ui-borders`, `canvas-under-ui` and the `ui` group pass as they are:
  `_push` leaves every existing primitive's vertices as they were.

`extern/cetra` to it, and 7.4.2 draws its curves and its trace with them.

## Not in this spec

Dashes in the UI, and butt or square ends and mitre joins: the canvas has them (14.0, 14.22), and
pcb's graph needs none. A translucent polyline without its joins doubled. Concave shapes, shapes of
more than four corners, and curves drawn as curves rather than as polylines.

## As built

**Phase 0.** This plan, committed alone.

**Phase 1, lines.** `_push` takes four corners, each with its UV and its `border` field, and the
shape's bounds, which the offset moves with the corners; `_push_quad` computes a fill's corners a
pixel past its rect and calls it, so a fill's, an image's and a glyph's vertices are what they were.
The border colour's alpha is now multiplied by the list's alpha in `_push_quad`, since `_push` no
longer knows the field is a colour. `ui_draw_line` and `ui_draw_polyline` as planned;
`ui_draw_list_quad_border` returns false for a line.

- **Departure: a line's `border` holds its own frame, not its ends.** Each corner carries its place
  along the segment from `a` and across it, and the segment's length (`along`, `across`, `len`, 0).
  Both are affine in the position, so they interpolate exactly, and they hold no position: the
  offset has nothing in the field to move, which the ends would have needed. The fragment's distance
  is `length(vec2(along - clamp(along, 0, len), across)) - half_width`.
- `aa` is taken at the top of `ui_frag.glsl`'s `main`, before any branch, so every fragment of a
  pixel quad reaches the derivative; the fill's value is the same.
- A line's arguments not finite, or a width not above 0, are logged and nothing is drawn.
- `canvas-ui-lines`: the level line's rows 0 px wrong; the slanted line's coverage 0.2% off its
  area; the hairline's 0.6%; 0 px wrong at the joins. `canvas-ui-seams`, `canvas-ui-borders` and
  `canvas-under-ui` pass as they were; the `canvas`, `ui` and `gate-docs` groups pass.
- **Falsified**, together, since they reach different cases: the hairline's alpha left at 1, the
  hairline 101.3% off its area; the line's quad reaching only a pixel past its ends rather than half
  the width and a pixel, 2 px wrong at the joins, the pixels on their outer side that only the round
  ends cover. The plan's "half the width past its ends" leaves the outer-side pixel covered, since
  it lies a pixel and a half past the end at a width of 8; the pixel short of half the width is the
  version the test reaches.

**Phase 2, convex shapes.** `ui_draw_convex` as planned: its bounds a pixel past, each box corner's
signed distance to each edge in `border`, `UI_CONVEX_FAR` (1e6) for a triangle's fourth and for an
edge of no length, which a corner given twice makes; the fragment multiplies the four ramps. Refused
and logged: a count other than 3 or 4, a corner not finite, corners turning both ways, twice the
signed area not above 0. A refusal is logged at every call, so a shape refused in a draw hook logs
once a frame, as every UI mutator given bad arguments does.

- `--ui-lines` gains `trapezoid`, `square` beside `quad` (a `ui_draw_quad` of the same rect),
  `halves`, `meeting`, and for `refused` a concave dart (`concave`) and a pentagon (`five`).
- **`halves` is the case made to catch the minimum**: a square whose edges pass through pixel
  centres, each corner pixel covered by a quarter, the product of its edges' halves. On whole pixels
  the minimum and the product agree everywhere, so `square` cannot tell them apart.
- **The trapezoid was made smaller** than first staged: 60 and 30 pixels wide, it lost 1.0% of its
  area with its box drawn only to its bounds, on the limit, since the window's multisampled target
  still draws the part of the lost row its samples reach. At 40 and 20 pixels wide and 19.8 high,
  its top and bottom 0.1 short of a pixel's centre, it loses 2.0%.
- `canvas-ui-lines`: the trapezoid 0.0% off its area; the convex square 0 codes off the quad; a
  halved corner 0 codes off a quarter; the backdrop 0.249 of the triangles' diagonal; 0 px drawn by
  the refused shapes. The lines' numbers as in phase 1; the `canvas`, `ui` and `gate-docs` groups
  pass.
- **Falsified**, each alone: the box drawn to its bounds, the trapezoid 2.0% off (and the halved
  corner 40 codes off); the ramps' minimum, the halved corner 54 codes off; the convexity test
  removed, 254 px drawn where the concave shape is.

**Phase 3, the look.** The staged graph is drawn under `--ui-lines`' cases, in points, by

**Phase 5, review.** A thermo-nuclear review in the background at effort high, and with it, at the
user's asking, `/simplify`'s four (reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude), all at effort high,
applied once all five were back. None found a defect that drew wrong pixels.

- **Applied.**
  - `_push` takes a `UICorner` per vertex (position, UV, `border`) in place of five parallel arrays;
    the replicated border literal and the zero-UV statics are gone.
  - `_border_is_colour(mode)` says once which modes keep a colour in `border`: `_push` fades that
    alpha with the list's again, and the readback refuses the others by it, not by the modes'
    order.
  - A polyline is validated once (the width, then every point) and its hairline rule applied once;
    `ui_draw_line` is the polyline of two points; each segment is `_push_line`. A polyline with a
    point not finite is now refused whole, and a bad width logs once a call, not once a segment.
  - `ui_draw_convex` builds each edge's plane once (an inward unit normal and an offset, a missing
    edge's normal 0 and offset `UI_CONVEX_FAR`), and a NULL array logs its own message.
  - The shader's line and convex branches share one tail; the glyph's branch is untouched.
  - `docs/ui.md`'s field-by-field account of the vertex became a pointer to `UIVertex`, its one
    home, and `ui_vert.glsl`'s stale attribute comments the same pointer; `docs/shaders.md` drops
    the line restating the shader's comment on `aa`.
  - `apps/canvas`: `push_pixel_sheet` builds both pixel sheets, and `seams_rgb` is `probe_rgb`.
  - `gates.py`: `_hex_rgb`, `_off_rgb` and `_ink_cover` beside `_rgb_near`, used by the seams and
    lines arms; the line's area from `_canvas_area("capsule")`.
- **A defect claimed and falsified: a corner on an edge was refused.** The convexity test took the
  sign of each turn exactly, so a triangle given four corners, the fourth placed on an edge in
  floats, could turn the other way by rounding and be refused. The review's first proposal, a
  tolerance relative to the two edges' lengths, does not cover it: a thousandth of the way along
  an edge the turn is 1.9e-4 against a tolerance of about 6.3e-7, since rounding is in the
  positions, not the angle, and a short edge makes it look like a real one. The tolerance is 8
  float steps of the corners' size times the two edges' lengths, about 5.6e-3 there; a dart 0.01
  points deep between edges 20 points long turns by tenths and is refused. `--ui-lines` gains `straight`, that triangle; the arm wants
  its centroid drawn. **Falsified**: the tolerance at 0, the exact test, the triangle refused.
- **Skipped.** The glyph's edge written into `params[3]` after `_push_quad`: it predates the spec,
  and passing it through adds a parameter to every caller. One helper deciding the pixel past for
  a fill, a line and a convex shape: a fill's is a margin on its rect, which a custom program and
  an image go without, while a line's and a convex shape's quad is their geometry, so the three are
  not one rule. The glyph branch moved above `aa`, and the line and convex shapes' per-call
  `_pixel_points`: measured costs far below a frame.
- After the fixes, the `canvas`, `ui` and `gate-docs` groups pass with the same numbers;
  `--ui-seams`, `--ui-borders` and `apps/gametest`'s pause and main menus are 0 px from master's
  on a floor of 0, and `--ui-lines` 0 px from its picture before the fixes.
