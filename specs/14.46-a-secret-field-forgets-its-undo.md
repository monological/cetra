# cetra 14.46: a secret field forgets its undo

## Context

pcb 10.3's last review found that a secret field (`UI_FIELD_SECRET`, spec 14.45) keeps what it
was told to forget. `ui_field_set_text` zeroes the text's tail a shorter text leaves, but
`stb_textedit_initialize_state` only resets the undo store's counters: the store's characters --
everything Backspace or typing over a selection took out -- stay as they were until
`ui_field_free`. pcb's key dialog sets its field's text empty each time it closes and keeps the
field for the window's life, so the characters of a key the user corrected stay in pcb's memory
until it quits. Nothing shows them, writes them or sends them; a debugger or a crash dump would
find them.

Asked by the user: "do it now. cd and fix it in a new spec in cetra".

Branch and spec: `14.46-a-secret-field-forgets-its-undo`, off cetra's master (`6430c029`).

## The design

- **`ui_field_set_text` zeroes a secret field's stb state** -- its undo store among it -- before it
  starts it again, as `ui_field_free` zeroes it before freeing. A field that is not secret starts
  its state again as before: there is nothing in it to hide.
- **What a test reads**: `ui_field_undo_bytes` (`ui_field.h`, beside `ui_field_inspect`), how many
  bytes of stb's store of characters are not zero, used or not.
- Nothing drawn changes, so there is no look phase.

## Phases

0. **The spec**, this file, committed alone.
1. **The fix and the arm**: the wipe in `ui_field_set_text`; `ui_field_undo_bytes`; the arm
   `ui-field-secret` reading gametest's `--ui-probe field-secret` -- two characters taken out by
   Backspace kept to undo while the field is up, none once its text is set; falsified by the wipe
   taken out. `docs/ui.md`'s text field, the roadmap's line.

## As built

**Phase 1, the fix and the arm.**
- `ui_field_set_text` zeroes `f->state` through `_wipe` for a secret field (`f->shown`) before
  `stb_textedit_initialize_state`, beside the text's tail it already zeroed.
- `ui_field_undo_bytes` (`ui_field.h`) counts the bytes of `undostate.undo_char` that are not
  zero, the whole array, used or not.
- `--ui-probe field-secret`, after the press on the second star, goes to the end, presses
  Backspace twice and prints `undo bytes KEPT AFTER`: 7 (the four-byte character and `€`), then 0
  once the text is set empty. `ui-field-secret` wants `[7, 0]`. It, `ui-field-undo`,
  `ui-field-clipboard` and `gate-arm-docs` pass.
- Falsified: the wipe taken out; `ui-field-secret` failed on `undo bytes [7, 7]`. Put back.
- `docs/ui.md`'s text field and the roadmap's line and date say it.
