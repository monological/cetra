# Silent: the town round the street -- its edges and woods, the street's dressing, and a lake with a cabin

## Context

`apps/silent`'s street is a rough draft. It is one straight 90 m strip of ten box houses, seven lamps,
four poles, one car and two post-and-rail fences. Behind every house there is 40 m of flat empty dirt,
then an invisible collider wall. The 4-6 m gaps between houses open straight onto that dirt, and the
west end of the street is an invisible wall in the fog (renders in
`scratchpad/street/sheet_now.png`).

What the user wants:
- Creepy and Silent Hill-like, with the world built out first. Monsters and mechanics are not decided
  yet.
- Boundaries that both guide the player and block them.
- A lake with a log cabin that has a porch and a fireplace or stove inside.
- The PS2 look is no longer required, so new work may use real foliage, smooth geometry and cutout
  materials.

Direction agreed in conversation is option A:
- The far side of the street is raised on a concrete retaining wall.
- Our side gets fenced yards.
- Woods stand behind both sides.
- At the west end, a crossroads. Its two side arms are barricaded, and straight on the road breaks off
  into a fog-filled chasm that the game never explains.

The edges are built so a later mechanic can use them: barricades and gates that could open, and one way
in that is shut today, which becomes the road to the lake.

### Three specs

This is three specs, numbered after the work already claimed: 13.32 (captures) and 13.33 (audio
zones) are in flight in worktrees, and 13.34 is the loading screen 13.32's spec queues next, which
rewrites silent's `on_init` into steps. The silent phases branch after 13.32 and 13.33 merge; the
engine phase (conifers) shares no files with either and can start first.

1. **13.35, the town's edges.**
2. **13.36, the street's dressing.**
3. **13.37, the lake and the cabin.**

How each spec is run (CLAUDE.md planning workflow):
- Each gets its own branch off master, named for its spec file, through `EnterWorktree`.
- Each is copied into `specs/` and committed before any code.
- Each phase that changes the picture ends with a look:
  - stills opened in Preview;
  - the windowed run command, given from inside the worktree;
  - the user walks it.
- No gate arms or goldens for the app itself. Engine changes get their gate group, scoped with
  `--only`.

- **The far side (north):**
  - A retaining wall stands just behind the far sidewalk, at z = -6.2. Its top climbs from 1.6 m at
    the east end to 2.6 m at the west, so the terrace has a slope rather than one height.
  - The far-side houses move up onto the terrace and are reached by concrete stairs.
  - Their back fences run at z ~ -30. Past them the woods climb to about +14 m by the world edge at
    z ~ -80.
- **Our side (south):**
  - The street stays at y = 0.
  - Yards are enclosed by side fences with gates, and back fences at z = 34.
  - Past the back fences the ground FALLS gently toward the lake valley in the south-west. This is a
    deliberate change from "rising behind both", made because the lake is down there.
- **The crossroads:**
  - The cross street is centred at x = -52 and is 8 m wide, with sidewalks.
  - The north arm is barricaded at z ~ -18.
  - The south arm is barricaded at z ~ +22. That is the shut way in; 13.37 turns it into the lake road.
  - The road runs on west to a broken lip at x ~ -68. The chasm, about 50 m across, cuts the world
    from the north woods to a rock ridge above the lake valley.
- **The lake (13.37):**
  - South-west of the crossroads, reached down a winding road through pines.
  - The water stands at y = -12, below every other floor in the world. The basement floor is at -2.4.
- **What stays as it is:** the hill, the drive, the mansion and the dead trees.

## Shared ground: one height function for the world outside the street

`hill.c` already builds a 2 m faceted grid from `hill_height` and uses the same grid for its collider
(`hill.c:211-259`). That grid build moves into a new `land.c` and covers the whole world box.
`land_height(x,z)` combines these pieces:
- the flat street plate, whose cells are left out because kit ground boxes cover it;
- the terrace;
- the north woods slope;
- the south woods fall;
- the existing `hill_height` term for x > 45, with the drive still carved by `hill.c`;
- the chasm, whose cells are left out because its walls are built separately;
- in 13.37, the lake basin.

Rules carried over from `hill.c`:
- Flat stretches are boxes, never mesh-collider triangles. A long coplanar run trips Jolt's debug trace
  (CLAUDE.md, physics).
- Ground stays under 8 degrees wherever puddles should stand.

`trees_build` keeps `hill_height` working through `land_height`.

### Phase 1 (engine): conifers in tree_gen, and a needle-spray atlas

A new excurrent form in `TreeParams`:
- a straight trunk that wanders slightly, running to the top;
- whorls of branches whose length follows a profile up the height;
- branches that droop and then turn up at the tips;
- branchlets off those branches;
- flat needle-spray cards;
- a "snag" knob for dead and broken tops.

`veg_needle_spray_maps` in `vegetation_tex` draws the needle sprays. It is deterministic, and its
colours are set by parameters: dark blue-green, with some cells gone brown.

The existing recursive form must stay bit-identical.

Details from the design pass are folded in below under **Conifer design**.

Gate group: `conifer`, through a fixture scene in the render app (see **Phase 1 as built**). Look:
the `apps/tree` viewer with `--preset`.

## Conifer design

**The base is ez-tree's evergreen form**, which is in `../research/trees/ez-tree/src/lib/tree.js` and
is MIT. Our recursive form stays the default and must stay bit-identical.

What ez-tree's pine does (presets `pine_*.json`):
- **Structure:** one trunk and one level of about 100 branches. The trunk tapers to zero with no
  terminal fork (:343-346).
- **Branch spacing:** branches are spaced up the trunk by stratified sampling from `start` (0.29, the
  bare lower trunk) to the top. Each branch's azimuth comes from a SHUFFLED slot, so the long branches
  do not spiral round to one side (:468-519).
- **Length:** branch length is `length * (1 - position)`, a linear cone (:534-538).
- **Droop:** branches leave at 129 degrees, pointing below horizontal, and an upward pull divided by
  the radius bends thin tips back up (:378-399).
- **Needles:** 18 crossed double-quad cards per branch, with normals blended outward ("rounded
  normals", :697-703).
- **Where the realism comes from:** a photo of a spruce sprig on those cards
  (`textures/leaves/pine.png`, Git LFS).
- **LOD:** the skeleton holds every random draw, so each level of detail is a fresh meshing of the
  same skeleton. Each level keeps every Nth bark ring, uses fewer radial segments, keeps every Nth
  card, and scales the kept cards up by about 1/sqrt(kept). The far level uses single rather than
  crossed cards (:91-121, :279-310).

What we build in `tree_gen` (form `TREE_FORM_EXCURRENT`, a `TreeParams.form` enum that defaults to
recursive):
- **Taken from ez-tree:**
  - stratified placement with shuffled azimuth slots;
  - the length profile, generalised to `(1-u)^crown_shape` over the crown;
  - the drooping start angle with the radius-scaled upward pull. Our `droop` and `phototropism`
    terms already do this;
  - crossed spray cards, because the player sees trees from the side;
  - LOD by re-meshing the skeleton.
- **Added because a wall of woods in fog needs it:**
  - whorls, with `whorl_spacing` and `whorl_size`;
  - `crown_base` and `crown_width`;
  - dead lower stubs (`dead_lower`), browning branches (`dead_fraction`), and a broken dead top
    (`snag`);
  - branchlets in the branch plane carrying the cards.
- **Presets:** `tree_params_preset(TreeParams*, TreePreset, seed)` with BROADLEAF, SPRUCE, FIR and
  SNAG.
- **Card emitter:** `emit_leaf_card` takes fold and droop as arguments. The broadleaf call passes
  today's literals, so its output is float-identical.
- **Wind data:** the same as in the recursive form. Each branch gets its parent's phase and the root
  distance at its base, so wind phase and flex in UV1 stay continuous.
- **A wind-anchor bug found on the way:** wind lean is normalised by each mesh's own Y range, and the
  needle mesh starts at the crown base. So the bark and the needles lean by different amounts, and low
  cards slide off their branches in a gust. Both conifer meshes carry two unreferenced vertices at the
  skeleton's padded Y extent so the two ranges agree. The broadleaf form keeps its output unchanged.

Texture: a procedural `veg_needle_spray_maps`.
- It is deterministic, takes its own RNG and `NoisePerm`, and never touches `rand`.
- Its cells are live blue-green, browned and bare twig.
- If it does not read well enough next to ez-tree's photo, we switch to a CC0 photographed sprig.
  ez-tree's own photo is not shipped, because its source is not stated.

LOD:
- `mesh_build_card_lod_chain` appends thinned and enlarged card copies as index ranges in the one
  buffer.
- Bark uses `mesh_build_lod_chain`.
- `engine->lod_bias ~0.1` puts the switches at about 22, 45 and 90 m. Silent has no other LOD chains.

Cost:
- **Per tree:** at most 10k triangles and 1.2k cards; 8 prototypes.
- **Draw distance:** a `woods_update(eye)` cell cull refills the prototype groups within 70 m, or
  55 m by day, since the engine has no draw distance and the trees would otherwise draw out to the
  250 m far plane.
- **Batching:** bark and needles go on separate nodes under per-prototype groups, so each prototype's
  instances stay together and batch.
- **Target:** under 3 ms of GPU time, measured against `--no-woods`.

Gate group `conifer`, new:
- **recursive-identity:** the broadleaf and dead presets' digests are held to constants captured
  before any edit.
- **determinism:** two runs give the same digest, including a run that calls `srand` first.
- **shape:**
  - branch length decreases up the crown;
  - the whorl count is as set;
  - at most 10k triangles;
  - a snag has no cards above its break.
- **thin:** card counts and card area per level.
- **wind:** UV1 continuity, and equal Y extents for bark and needles.

The gate reads the render app's `--tree-probe` over `assets/scenes/conifer_fixture.cscn`, a
`trees` block placing each preset. The tree viewer (`apps/tree`) gains `--preset` and
`--irregularity`, for looking at a tree. (Amended in phase 1: the gate first read the viewer, and
the user moved it onto a fixture.)
