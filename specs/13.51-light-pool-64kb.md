# 13.51: the light index pool at 64 KB, and an overflow warning that repeats while it lasts

## Context

In silent's hall a hard horizontal line crosses the far door, and it moves when the player steps
forward or back. The cause is the clustered-light index pool overflowing. Every froxel's light list
lives in one uniform buffer of 16,384 one-byte slots, sized to the 16 KB GL 4.1 guarantees. The
slots are handed out froxel by froxel, nearest slice first, and once they run out every later froxel
gets an empty list. At the reported camera the view needs 18,270 slots. The pool runs dry at slice
13 (5.0 to 7.2 m), row 5, column 3, so the tile rows above 3/8 of the screen lose all their local
lights.

Spec 13.6 (`569ced13`) grew the pool to 16 KB and measured four views at 10.1k to 12.8k. Between
Oct 5 and Oct 8 the player's house gained six lights within a few metres of this hall, and the
overflow came back. Nothing reported it: the warning prints once a session, during load, when the
kitchen tubes have no range yet, so it blamed `tube_window` at radius 144.

A probe of this Mac (Apple M1 Max, GL 4.1 on Metal) reports `GL_MAX_UNIFORM_BLOCK_SIZE = 65536`.
GL guarantees only 16384. To our knowledge every current desktop driver allows 64 KB: NVIDIA,
AMD, Intel, Mesa (llvmpipe, virgl, radeonsi, iris) and Apple's. Only Apple's has been measured
here; the rest is unverified. So the pool is 64 KB, 65,536 slots, and a driver that allows less is
refused at startup by name, which is also how a wrong belief would show itself. That needs no texture slot, and the grid word's
20-bit offset field already holds 65,536. It is still a cap. Running out would take, on average,
more than 21 lights reaching every froxel in view.

The repo has made this call before: the punctual shadow pool's cap is "the driver's uniform limit,
not GL 4.1's guaranteed" minimum, with no fallback.

The overflow warning also changes. It now repeats every `LC_OVERFLOW_REPEAT` builds while the
overflow lasts, each time with the current demand and the current widest light, instead of once
per session.

## Design

**The pool is a constant.**
- `ubo.h`: `UBO_CLUSTER_INDICES_BLOCK_SIZE` is 65536.
- `include/lights_ubo.glsl`: `uvec4 lightIndices[4096]`.
- `light_cluster.h`: `LC_MAX_CLUSTER_INDICES` is its own literal 65536, so the
  `sizeof(GpuClusterIndexBlock)` assert against the UBO size stays a real drift check between two
  numbers.
- The capture key needs nothing: the build digest already covers a constant.

**One name for GL's guarantee.** `UBO_GUARANTEED_BLOCK_SIZE` (16384) replaces the literal in every
other block's "fits GL 4.1's guaranteed size" assert.

**A startup check.**
- `_setup_engine_glfw` reads `GL_MAX_UNIFORM_BLOCK_SIZE` through a `util.c` getter, beside the
  texture-unit getters, and logs it beside the sampler budget.
- Below `UBO_CLUSTER_INDICES_BLOCK_SIZE`, engine creation fails with a message that names the
  limit and the need. Otherwise every lit program would fail to link with a block-size error that
  says nothing of why.

**The warning repeats while it lasts.** One cooldown counter per kind (camera, capture) replaces
the once-per-session flag:
- An overflow at cooldown 0 warns and sets the cooldown to `LC_OVERFLOW_REPEAT`.
- Every other build counts it down.
- A lasting overflow is therefore re-reported with its current demand and widest light, and a view
  flickering at the edge is capped at one line per `LC_OVERFLOW_REPEAT` builds.
- The text adds demand against the pool: "needs N of 65536 slots".

**In passing.** `ubo_wire_program_block` binds before it validates, which contradicts its own
header. It now validates first.

- `./build.sh`. silent's startup log reports the driver's limit, and the hall shows no line.

  them. Any move is a finding to report, not something to re-bake.

  line), then the full suite once at the end. No GPU work while gates or goldens run.

  when this spec was revised.
- Nothing merges to master without the user's say-so.

## As built

### Phases 1-3: a driver-sized pool (`87876aa6`), since replaced
- The pool took `GL_MAX_UNIFORM_BLOCK_SIZE` at startup, at most 64 KB and at least 16 KB.
  `CETRA_UBO_LIMIT` capped it, and `create_shader` spliced its size into every shader.
- The startup log on this Mac read `driver allows 65536 bytes; light index pool 65536 slots`.
- At the reported hall camera, the first frame needs 21,477 slots (the kitchen tubes still have no
  range) and the steady state about 18,270. At 64 KB nothing overflows.
- Under the 16 KB lever, the one warning per episode still described the first frame and named
  `tube_window` at radius 144. The overflow starts in that frame and never fits again, so the
  episode never ends.
- **The 16 KB path was master's frame exactly**: 0 px against master at the same camera, with the
  cook off on both (`CETRA_NO_COOK=1`), against a 0 px run-to-run floor.
  - Through the cook, master's frame differed by 23% of pixels.
  - That frame restored its GI and probes from cook entries made by an earlier session, so it is
    not a baseline.

### Phase 4: the look
- At the reported camera, master's 16 KB frame has the line across the far door, and the 64 KB
  frame has none.

### Review of phases 1-3 (thermo-nuclear, one agent)
- **The fallback serves no driver the engine runs on.** The 16 KB path was reachable only through
  the lever that existed to reach it. A constant deletes the startup state, the splice, the lever,
  the capture-key fold and the runtime capacity. Adopted: the user dropped the fallback.
- **The episode warning did not fix the misattribution that motivated it.** A cooldown that repeats
  with current figures does. Adopted.
- **`LC_MAX_CLUSTER_INDICES` aliased to the UBO size** made the `sizeof` assert a tautology.
  Restored as two literals.
- **Three spellings of GL's 16 KB guarantee.** Now one name.
- **`ubo_wire_program_block` binds before it validates**, despite its header. Pre-existing; fixed
  in passing.
- **Comments** that named their callers or narrated silent's hall in a generic header. Rewritten.
- **The splice's `#line 2` on app sources with comments before `#version`.** Moot: no splice now.
