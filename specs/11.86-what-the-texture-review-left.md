# Spec 11.86 — what the texture review left

## Context

Spec 11.85 (block-compressed textures) merged at `19ef47d3` after an eight-agent review that
found five defects producing wrong pixels. It shipped with a list of things declined or not
reached, recorded in its own *Left alone, deliberately* section. This closes them while the
reasoning is still in someone's head.

**Two of the five are the reason this spec exists, and they are the same failure seen from
opposite ends.**

`clearcoat_fixture` is in **neither `goldens.py` nor `gates.py`**. 11.85 tagged the clearcoat
normal `TEXTURE_USE_NORMAL`, so it took BC5, and `pbr_frag` read its `.xyz` — a channel BC5 does
not store — so z decoded to **−1**, a coat normal pointing *into* the surface. Measured **27,364
px at PAE 226/255**, and a fully green 28-golden, 66-group suite said nothing. It is the only
material-extension fixture in that position: `sheen_fixture` and `specular_fixture`, its two
siblings from the same roadmap tier, both have goldens.

The **seven texture entry points were not collapsed**. Three of eight reviewers reached
`TextureDesc` independently. 11.85 deferred it as "a 27-site sweep touching `is_srgb` at every
one — too large to land on top of a review round", and named it the first thing a follow-up
should do.

**Exploration changed what that second item is.** It is not a 27-site rename. The eight-step
"make a pooled Texture from decoded pixels" body is written **three times** — the path loader
(`texture.c:936`), the memory loader (`texture.c:1069`), and inline in
`async_loader.c:686-724` — and the three have already drifted:

- **`TextureAlpha` reaches one of the three.** The path loader takes it as a parameter; the
  memory loader and the async worker both hardcode `channels == 4 && is_srgb`
  (`texture.c:1098`, `async_loader.c:57`). `async_loader.h:52-56` says out loud that a streamed
  decal "wants the override too".
- **The async path re-derives `is_srgb` by sniffing the format** — `result->internal_format ==
  GL_SRGB || == GL_SRGB_ALPHA` (`async_loader.c:706`) — rather than carrying the bool it was
  given and already stores on the request. `texture_gl_formats` ignores `is_srgb` below three
  channels, so the two disagree on a greyscale colour map. 11.85 found and fixed the downstream
  half of this inside `texture_box_halve` (`texture.c:285-292`); the divergent *source* is still
  there.
- The dilate buffer is a malloc'd copy in one and an in-place mutation in the other.

So the collapse is a **de-duplication that closes a documented gap**, and the call-site sweep
falls out of it. Two of the seven entry points also have **zero callers**:
`load_texture_from_memory` (none at all) and `load_texture_path_into_pool_used` (reached only
from `_ex` internally).

Three smaller items ride along, all from 11.85's own list.

## Phases

Branch `11.86-what-the-texture-review-left` off master. **Coverage lands before the refactor**,
which is the ordering the whole spec turns on: a golden baked *after* a sweep bakes in whatever
the sweep did, and AGENTS.md's own rule is to capture the before from the base commit.

### 1. The spec, alone

Copy this file to `specs/11.86-what-the-texture-review-left.md` and commit it before any code.

### 2. The clearcoat golden

One entry in `goldens.py`'s `RECIPES`, under the `# --- materials ---` heading at `:241`,
beside its two siblings:

```python
{"name": "clearcoat_fixture", "scene": "assets/clearcoat_fixture.gltf", "size": (1600, 900),
 "flags": ["-f", "30", "-W", "800", "-H", "450", "--no-auto-exposure", "-E", "1.0",
           "--cam-eye", "...", "--cam-target", "..."]},
```

**Default sky, not `-e <hdr>`.** The generator's docstring says the fixture is "meant to be
viewed under an HDR environment", and `goldens.py:220-222` refuses one. That refusal's stated
reason — "the HDRs live outside the repo" — is *overbroad*: `assets/abandoned_window/
abandoned_window_env.hdr` is committed. Taking the default sky anyway, because it matches
sheen/specular exactly, adds no IBL bake to the determinism surface, and the defect this golden
exists for measured PAE 226/255 under it.

**Pin the camera.** The fixture is `.gltf`-only — no camera, no lights, auto-framed. A framing
that drifts silently changes what is covered; `roughness_sweep` is the precedent.

**Falsify it before committing.** Revert the coat site to `texture(...).xyz * 2.0 - 1.0` and
confirm the golden goes red by ~27k px. A golden that has never been seen to fail is a file.

Fix the generator's stale docstring in the same commit: `gen_clearcoat_fixture.py:14` claims
"materials are flat factors (no textures)", false since `1b1f16be` added the weave map. It is a
comment, so the `.gltf` bytes are unchanged and `fixture-gen` stays green.

### 3. `--clearcoat-debug`

The `--purkinje-debug` / `--cs-debug` precedent: write the shading quantity out as bytes,
because no `--render-mode` exposes the coat normal — mode 1 is the *vertex* normal and mode 4 is
the tangent, and neither goes near `clearcoatNormal()`.

- Writes `Nc * 0.5 + 0.5` where `clearcoat > 0`, and **exact black where it is 0**. Black is
  what makes the uncoated sphere an in-frame control: an arm reading only a coated sphere passes
  on a build that writes the geometric normal everywhere.
- **No display encode**, and say so in the flag's own comment. `--cs-debug` records that a
  measured 0.6326 reads as 0.3564 if you sRGB-decode it; that trap has already cost this repo
  once.
- A flag rather than render mode 13: `--purkinje-debug`, `--cs-debug`, `--sky-debug` and
  `--probe-set-debug` are all flags, and the `RENDER_MODE_*` enum in `common.h` stops at 10
  while modes 10-12 are shader-side only.

The golden from phase 2 is the identity check: adding this must move **0 px** on the default
path.

### 4. The clearcoat gate group

`run_clearcoat_gate(workdir)` in `gates.py`, registered in `GATE_GROUPS` beside the other
material groups. Five arms, each falsified by a named mutation:

| arm | reads | falsified by |
|---|---|---|
| `clearcoat-normal` | coated sphere centre under `--clearcoat-debug`: blue channel high | forcing `rebuildZ` false → z collapses |
| `clearcoat-weave` | variance of R/G across the coated sphere exceeds a floor | dropping the coat map → constant normal, zero variance |
| `clearcoat-uncoated` | `car_paint_nocoat` reads **exact** black | writing `Ngeo` unconditionally |
| `clearcoat-storage` | `--texture-probe` says the coat map is `GL_COMPRESSED_RG_RGTC2` | untagging it — the frame stays *correct* and the saving silently goes |
| `clearcoat-off` | `--no-clearcoat` moves > 0 px | a dead flag, this suite's most repeated lesson |

`clearcoat-weave` is the one that covers the **bespoke import read** at `import.c:713-717` —
`aiTextureType_CLEARCOAT` **index 2**, which the index-0 mapping table cannot reach and which no
other fixture exercises. A silently-dropped coat map is invisible in every other arm.

`clearcoat-storage` is the one no pixel arm can replace: untagging the map makes the picture
*better* while deleting the feature's whole point.

Reuse `_texcomp_probe` (`gates.py:18536`), `_read_ppm`, `_linear_rgb`, `render`, `_run`. Docstring
needs the shallow anchor line + 2-space-indented arm names in exact run order, or `gate-arm-docs`
fails — `run_texcomp_gate:18561` is the model.

### 5. `TextureDesc`, the internal collapse

The half that pays. A `TextureDesc` carrying `is_srgb`, `TextureAlpha`, `TextureUse`, `wrap_s`,
`wrap_t`, and one internal `texture_create_pooled(pool, key, desc, pixels, w, h, channels)`.
All three bodies become one call to it.

What that buys, in order of value:

1. **`TextureAlpha` reaches the memory and async paths for the first time**, closing the trap
   `async_loader.h:52-56` and `texture.h:235-242` both document.
2. **The async path carries `is_srgb` instead of sniffing it.** `req->is_srgb` already exists
   (`async_loader.c:604`); it just never reaches the result.
3. `texture_upload_image`'s nine parameters collapse.
4. `wrap_s`/`wrap_t` stop being a post-hoc second call (`texture_apply_wrap` at `import.c:580`
   and `:630`).

**Bar: 0 px.** Goldens plus `--only texcomp,layers,decals,clearcoat` unchanged. This is a pure
refactor and anything that moves is a defect in it.

### 6. `TextureDesc`, the public boundary and the sweep

Seven entry points to two (`texture_load_file` / `texture_load_memory`, with an `owns_pixels`
field rather than a fourth `_owned` variant — the memory forms currently split on two orthogonal
booleans and spend four functions on two axes). Designated initialisers give the defaults, so a
site states only what it means.

**Delete the two dead ones**: `load_texture_from_memory` and `load_texture_path_into_pool_used`.

Then the sweep — `import.c` (4), `cscene_apply.c` (5), `forest.c` (9), `tree.c` (11).

One code-judo move inside it: **promote `texture_use_for_setter` out of `import.c:249` and beside
the setters in `material.c`.** `MATERIAL_PARAMS` rows already carry `set_tex`, so
`cscene_apply.c:782` — which today hardcodes `false` and defaults `COLOUR` for every `.cscn`
material texture slot — derives the right answer from the slot it already has. The rule stays
stated once. (Both texture-typed rows are `DATA` today, so this is a correctness *guarantee*, not
a fix; the sweep is what makes it hold for the next row added.)

`is_srgb` stays its own field and does **not** fold into `TextureUse`. `import.c:225-226` already
records why: reflectance is colour-ish and linear, a decal is colour with a real opacity alpha
loaded linear, and tree's sand albedo is deliberately baked non-sRGB so the stochastic transform
operates on stored codes. The two axes genuinely disagree.

**Bar: 0 px** again.

### 7. apps/tree's three procedural normals — the one deliberate behaviour change

`tree.c:87` (bark), `:102` (leaf), `:142` (sand) go through `load_texture_from_memory_owned` and
so default to `TEXTURE_USE_COLOUR`: no BC5 **and** no Z rebuild. 11.85 recorded this as "a saving
left rather than a defect".

Its own commit, because it is the only phase here that changes pixels. Measure VRAM through
`--texture-probe` (a numeric claim a frame cannot make) and read the frame as a bounded RMSE —
**`apps/tree` is not pixel-deterministic** (31,034 px run-to-run on the orbit path,
`tree.c:749`'s `glfwGetTime()`), so measure the floor at the framing first, per the standing rule.

### 8. The probe's line shape, and two false claims in 11.85's own text

- `texture_pool_probe` emits a **positional** line where every other probe in the tree emits
  `<prefix> <kind> k=v ...` and `gates.py` has a shared parser. `texture.h:228-231` says to
  convert it "the next time either side is touched" — this spec touches both. Convert it and
  drop `_texcomp_probe`'s bespoke parser.
- **`specs/11.85:191-193` contradicts `:203-206`.** The first says
  `--no-texture-compression` "reaches the pre-change frame **exactly**"; the second says it is
  "not a full return to the pre-11.85 engine" because the CPU mip chain and the Z rebuild are
  unconditional, and gives the numbers. The second is the corrected one; the first survived the
  review round. Fix it.
- `### Left alone, deliberately` appears **twice** (`:197` and `:247`). Merge them.

### 9. Closeout

Full `gates.py` + `goldens.py`. AGENTS.md's texture-model paragraph (the entry-point list and
the "one upload path" claim). Roadmap F1's as-built note.

## Said out loud

- **This is an M.** Phases 5-6 are the bulk, and they are a refactor with a provable bar rather
  than a design problem.
- **The golden bakes today's residual BC5 loss** (4,536 px, RMSE 0.41 codes) as correct. That is
  right: a golden's job is reproducibility, not correctness, and the arms in phase 4 are what
  bound the loss.
- **`moon_surface.c` is a fourth upload path** — raw `glGenTextures` + `glTexImage2D` +
  `glGenerateMipmap`, bypassing `texture.c` entirely. 11.85's "the one upload path" claim is true
  of the pool and not of the engine. Left alone here (its RGBA8 packs a tangent normal in RGB and
  an albedo in A, which no block format compresses), but the claim in AGENTS.md should be
  narrowed to say so rather than left reading wider than it is.
- **`is_srgb` is not part of the async dedup key** (`async_loader.h:127-131`), so two requests
  for one path that disagree both get the first submitter's colour space. Found while mapping
  this; not fixed here, because it wants its own measurement rather than a drive-by. Recorded.
- **Packed ORM is still the largest remaining VRAM gap** (raiden: 21.3 MB in one map, ~29 MB
  across all). Splitting it into three BC4s at import is a material-system change and stays out.

## As built

Nine phases planned, nine run. **65 gate groups and 29 goldens green.** Four things the plan
got wrong, and one measurement it did not know it needed.

### The deferred item was mis-sized, and the sweep was its smaller half

11.85 booked `TextureDesc` as "a 27-site sweep touching `is_srgb` at every one". The sweep was
mechanical. What actually paid was underneath it: the eight-step body from decoded pixels to a
pooled `Texture` was written out **three times** — the file loader, the memory loader, and inline
in `async_loader_process_pending`. 11.85 had shared the UPLOAD alone and left the other seven
steps in triplicate, which is why they had already drifted three ways:

- **`TextureAlpha` reached one producer of three.** Both other paths inferred it from `is_srgb`,
  and both said so in comments that had been asking for this for two specs.
- **The async path recovered `is_srgb` by sniffing the format it was about to pass.**
  `texture_gl_formats` ignores `is_srgb` below three channels — a 1-channel image is always
  `GL_RED` — so a greyscale albedo came back LINEAR on the streamed path and sRGB on the direct
  one, and the mip filter reads that flag. 11.85 found the downstream half inside
  `texture_box_halve`; this was the source.
- **The pool insert was the plain variant in two and the threadsafe one in the third**, and they
  are not the same function: the plain one appends to the array whatever the cache says.

Seven entry points became three. **Two of the seven had no callers at all.**

### `--clearcoat-debug` had to be a render MODE, and the plan said flag

The plan argued from naming — `--cs-debug`, `--sky-debug`, `--purkinje-debug` are all flags — and
ignored where the quantity is written. Those three are read in the POST chain and return early
from the tonemap. A coat normal is written in `pbr_frag`, and `postfx_run` takes the passthrough
blit only for a non-PBR `frame_mode`. So a flag read late would have arrived tonemapped, display
-encoded and dithered. It is render mode 13, with `--clearcoat-debug` as a named spelling set at
parse time so the two are honest last-wins.

### Two refusals, both against the plan's own text

**`texture_use_for_setter` was not promoted beside the material setters.** The plan wanted
`cscene_apply.c:782` to derive `use` from `slot->set_tex`. There are exactly two texture-typed
rows in `MATERIAL_PARAMS` and both are per-texel DATA, so the derivation would serve a constant —
speculative generality dressed as a code-judo move. A comment naming the condition under which it
stops being constant is the honest version.

**Ownership stayed a separate function rather than a `TextureDesc` field.** It is a fact about the
BUFFER where the desc describes the IMAGE, and one desc is meant to be reusable across calls.

### What it cost and what it saved

| | measured |
|---|---|
| clearcoat golden, falsified | **45,595 px** with the coat decode reverted |
| clearcoat arms | 5, each broken by exactly one mutation, no cross-talk |
| the collapse and the sweep | **0 px** on 29 goldens and 6 gate groups |
| `apps/tree` VRAM | **54.667 → 41.667 MB**, the three normals 17.333 → 4.333 exactly 4x |

**The tree pixel cost is framing-dependent and the commit quoted only the smaller half.** On the
`--player` framing it is 9,038 px of 480,000 at RMSE 0.081 of one 8-bit code. On the app's DEFAULT
orbit view, where the canopy fills the frame and the leaf normal is most of what is visible, it is
**37,442 px of 1,024,000 at 0.163 codes**. Both against a 0 px floor. Quote the second.

### The determinism claim this needed, and got

`apps/tree --player --headless` is **0 px** across two runs — AGENTS.md had recorded it as "the
cheapest uncovered determinism claim in the tree". It holds. And at 640x400 / 30 frames the ORBIT
path is 0 px too, which the same file's 31,034 px figure does not predict: that number is a
property of a framing and a frame count, not of the app.

### The orange leaf rims are not this spec's, and are worth a spec of their own

Raised while this was in flight: `apps/tree`'s backlit leaves carry a bright orange outline where
they sit against the dark sea, and none where they sit against the bright sky — which rules out
antialiasing, since an AA edge blends toward whatever is behind it. Measured:

- **Present on master, byte-identical to this branch's** in the crop, so nothing here caused it.
- **`--msaa 1` removes it entirely.**
- **`--render-mode 12` lights up along exactly those edges.**

That is spec 11.38's class — a varying EXTRAPOLATED where the pixel centre falls outside the
triangle while some MSAA samples are inside — and this app is where it surfaces because it runs
4x MSAA *and* TAA where `render` drops to one sample. `assets/varying_fixture.cscn` is the
instrument. Not fixed here: it is a shading defect with its own fixture and no relation to
texture storage.
