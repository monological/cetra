# Spec 13.18 -- skinned casters in cached point-light shadows

## Context
Spec 13.16 caches point-light shadows (silent's candles and hall bulb): each face is drawn once
and kept, and a caster whose NODE moves becomes a "mover", drawn every frame over a copy of the
face's still casters. A skinned mesh deforms without its node moving, so 13.16 left every
non-`DRAW_STILL` caster (skinned, wind-swayed, CDLOD-morphed) out of all three kept caster sets.
The result is that the cat casts no shadow from any candle or the bulb, which is where it spends its time.

Keeping deforming casters out of the STILL store is right: a face drawn once would freeze a pose.
Keeping them out of the movers overlay too is the gap. The fix treats a caster whose surface
moves as a mover on every frame it exists, which is what Unreal does: in UE4's cached movable-light
shadows and UE5's virtual shadow maps, skinned meshes invalidate every frame. The user chose to
add one gate arm, written only after the look is signed off.

## Design (all in `cetra/src/shadow.c`, one field in `shadow.h`)

1. **One test for "this caster moves"**: `kept_caster_moves(ss, item)` returns
   `!(item->flags & DRAW_STILL) || tile_node_moves(ss, item->node)`.
   - `caster_set_wants` drops its `caster_set_kept(set) && !(flags & DRAW_STILL)` line.
   - `caster_set_wants_node` becomes an item-level test:
     - `KEPT_STILL` wants the item if it does not move.
     - `KEPT_MOVERS` wants it if it does.
     - `KEPT` (whole faces) wants everything.
   - A deforming caster therefore never enters a store. It draws over the copy, and it draws in
     a face drawn whole: a block with no store, or a mover past the 32 cap.
2. **`tiles_note_changes` marks deforming casters every frame.**
   - An item counts as changed if it is not `DRAW_STILL`, or if its node transform differs from
     last frame. A deforming caster is registered as a mover by its node, and its "moved" frame
     is refreshed every frame, so it never expires.
   - Its box is `draw_item_bounds`, which gives the posed bone boxes plus the wind, morph and fur
     margins, transformed by the node's global matrix. The import box is the bind pose and would
     miss a curled cat or a swept tail.
   - It marks `TILE_BOX_DYNAMIC` only. It was never in a store, so it needs no `UNSTORE`.
   - Past the cap it marks `INVALIDATE`, as node movers already do.
   - `engine` is passed in for `render_cull_view(engine, scene, NULL)`, which carries the wind.
   - **No early return on a graph-epoch change.** The reset stays, but the loop still runs that
     frame. Without that, the frame the cat is attached draws it whole into faces nothing marks
     afterwards, and a sliver of a frozen cat would stay in any face its next frame's bounds miss.
3. **Faces stop being dynamic once nothing moves in them.**
   - **The problem:** today a face stays dynamic until some mover expires. The cat never expires,
     and nothing else in silent does either (the pendulum swings forever). So every candle face
     the cat ever walked through would be copied and redrawn every frame for the rest of the run.
     The cost would grow with the cat's walk.
   - **New per-block field:** `uint64_t touched` in `ShadowTileBlock` (`shadow.h:193`) records
     the faces a box marked THIS frame. It is zeroed at the top of `tiles_note_changes` and set
     in `tiles_mark_box`.
   - **The decay:** in `render_shadow_movers`, after a face is drawn (store copy plus movers),
     a face not in `touched` drops its `dynamic` bit. It now holds exactly what a whole draw
     would hold, and stays valid.
   - **Out-of-view faces:** a face skipped because it is out of the camera's view keeps its bit,
     so it is still redrawn before anything samples it. That is the case that would otherwise
     freeze a stale cat into a face.
   - **Node movers:** prev/current box marking is unchanged, and decay applies to them the same
     way.

Comments are updated where the mechanism lives: the `ShadowCasterSet` block, `tiles_note_changes`'
header (it says skinned meshes "mark nothing"), and `render_shadow_movers`' header.

## Risks
- **Draw-list walks:** each dynamic face walks the whole draw list in `draw_shadow_layer`, so the
  CPU cost is per face, not per triangle. Step 4 measures it rather than assuming.
- **Swaying foliage under a cached light** becomes a permanent mover too. No scene has that
  today. Past 32 such casters, their faces fall back to whole redraws every frame, which is
  correct but uncached. Noted in the spec.
  - *Correction (spec 13.26):* the cap never applied, since as built deforming casters take no
    mover slot (`ccff7d45`). The risk itself arrived with silent's drive: 110 swaying trees made
    408 faces a frame movers, drawn whole because sixteen bodied lights left no store, 176 ms of a
    227 ms frame. 13.26 answers it: a sway moves a face only under a wind that can move it, a
    material may keep its sway out of the kept faces, and stores have a pool of their own.
