# Spec 13.23 -- probe coverage

## Context

**The report.** By day the parlour door in silent's hall looked washed out, a pale pink-grey beside
the warm front door, though both are `MAT_WOOD`.

**The cause, measured.** The door hangs on its wall's centre line (`house.c`'s `door_in` puts the
leaf at `wf.at`), so its hall-side face sits 2.5 cm inside the wall. The hall's reflection probe box
stopped at the wall's inner face (`silent.c`'s `build_probes`), and its outward fade reached only
1.9 cm on that axis, because the fade is a fraction of each axis's half-size. So the face was outside
every box, and its reflection fell through to the global environment, the overcast day sky.
- **The engine behaved as designed.** `envVisible` dimmed that sky reflection to 5.4% on the door
  (6.8% on the front door, 6.5% on the panelling, 2.4% on the floor; read raw through a temporary
  debug mode). It is a luminance ratio, so it carries the sky's grey colour and its evenness, which a
  probe would have replaced with the dark warm panelling in that direction. At a grazing angle that
  grey layer reads as washed out. Unreal's reflection-capture mixing and sky occlusion are scalar too.
- **Widening the hall's box 5 cm** toward the parlour gave the door the front door's warm brown.

**Why it was missed.** The box and the door were placed in two files from two sets of constants,
the failure renders as a plausible frame, nothing warns when a surface falls outside every probe
(outdoors that is normal), and silent has no gates. 13.13's fade comment said each fade stopped
"halfway into the thinnest wall", which held only along each box's longest axis.

**Already on master** (`040ab37c`): silent's boxes reach the centre line of a wall shared with
another room and stay at the inner face of an outside wall; the fade stops a quarter into the
thinnest wall along the longest axis. The parlour door and the upstairs doors are fixed.

## Goal

1. **An engine debug view that makes this class of bug obvious** in any app: probe coverage.
2. **Every surface indoors in silent covered by a probe**, without any outside surface reflecting a
   room.

## Design

### The probe-coverage view (engine)
- `RENDER_MODE_PROBE_COVERAGE` (14): the albedo, display-encoded as the albedo view is, mixed toward
  magenta by `envShare`, the share of the pixel's ambient reflection no probe answers. In the GUI's
  Render Mode list, so any app with the debug panel has it; `--render-mode 14` on the render app.
- `envShare` is set where the reflection source is chosen in `pbr_frag`: 0 under a single probe,
  `1 - probes.a` under a set, 1 with no probe, 0 with no environment lobe at all.
- Outdoors every pixel is magenta, and right. Indoors any magenta is a miss.
- **Known limit:** it shows the sky fallback, not a WRONG probe. A surface inside another room's
  box reads as covered.
- Mode 13, the clearcoat normal, was reached by a literal with no enum member, so a new member would
  have collided with it. It is named, `RENDER_MODE_CLEARCOAT_NORMAL`, and `--clearcoat-debug` uses
  the name. The config snapshot and the GUI list both modes.

### silent's probe boxes: one rule
**A box runs out to the plane its doors and panes hang in:**
- a wall shared with another room: its centre line, where a leaf hangs;
- an outside wall: the room-side face of its pane, which is 6 mm of glass on the centre line
  (`kit_frame_pane`, `d +- 0.003`), so 3 mm short of the centre. That takes in the reveals, the
  inner face of the glass, and the wedge between the wall tops and the roof's underside;
- the front wall, for the hall: the front door's middle, `FRONT_DOOR_Z`, since that door hangs
  against the inner face rather than on the centre line. `layout.h` states it once, for `house.c`'s
  hinge and `silent.c`'s box.

**The fade drops to 3 mm**, half a pane, so the glass's street face is never the room's. The engine
floors a fade at 1e-3 of each half-size, about 5 mm on the great hall's axes, still under the
pane's 6 mm. Boxes now meet at the shared planes, so the fade no longer has a doorway to blend.

**What it must not do:** an outside face -- the front door's street face, a window's outer face, the
facade -- must not take a room's probe.

3. **A gate arm for the view**, on the render app and a FIXTURE, never silent: in mode 14 a patch
   inside a probe box is the albedo and a patch outside every box is magenta. The probe-set

   silent gets screenshots, not arms.
4. **Docs**: `AGENTS.md` (the silent row's probe rule, the Render Modes section),

   `docs/cli-reference.md`, and the spec's as-built notes.

## Not in this spec
- A per-channel `envVisible`. Rejected: the colour of light reaching a surface is not the colour of
  what it reflects, the per-channel division is fragile, outdoors would drift, and a fallback that
  looked right would have hidden this bug.
- 13.22 (the local exposure fringe) keeps its number.

## Phase 2 -- as built

- `kit.h` names half a pane's glass, `KIT_PANE_HALF` (3 mm), which `kit_frame_pane` extrudes by and the boxes stop at: the kitchen's front and east walls, the great hall's sides and back, the study's tower faces. The hall keeps `FRONT_DOOR_Z`; shared walls keep their centre lines. The fade is `KIT_PANE_HALF` over the longest half-size, floored by the engine at a thousandth of each (about 5 mm across the great hall).
- **Coverage view, by day:** no magenta indoors but the 8 mm gap round the front door, which looks outdoors. The great hall's lancets, their reveals and the wedge between the wall tops and the roof are covered; the kitchen window's inner face is covered; the study is unchanged.
- **Outside:** the front door's street face, the kitchen window's outer face and the lancets' outer faces stay magenta. Shaded, outside moves only in thin lines at the lancets' edges -- the reveals inside the wall, seen through the glass.
- **Before/after, pixels past 2%:** great hall west 0.8%, wall tops 1.1%, back 2.1%, kitchen window 0.7%, hall 0.4%, study 0.002%; outside 0.1-0.5%.
- **Found, and older than this spec:** from the yard, the house's west wall upstairs beside the tower, and a strip of roof above the lancets, read as covered. The study's box runs out to the tower's faces, past the house's own west wall, so that facade takes the study's probe. Outdoors everything should be magenta, so an outside framing is where this view does show a wrong probe. Unchanged by phase 2 (the shaded diff is empty there).

## Phase 2b -- the study in two boxes

Taken into this spec on finding it (phase 2's last bullet). The study was one box round the room and the tower bay together, the bay's x and z and its 9 m ceiling, so it reached past the house's west wall beside the tower, past the front wall east of it and up through the roof over the room, and that facade and that roof reflected the study.
- **Two probes now:** the room, from the house's west wall to the hall's line and the front wall to the great hall, up to its own ceiling (`CEIL2_Y`), captured from its middle; and the bay, the octagon's box up to `TOWER_CEIL_Y`, captured over the desk at its centre. Five probes of the set's eight. They overlap where the bay opens into the room, and blend there.
- **Coverage view:** the bay and the room covered; from the yard, from the street and from the tower's west side, the house's west wall beside the tower and the front wall east of it are magenta.
- **What a box cannot do, and stays:** the bay's diagonal faces (a box cannot follow a 45 degree wall, so their outside takes the bay's probe), and the tops of the pitched roof (a 10 cm slab at 50 degrees inside the great hall's box, which must reach the ridge for the hall's open roof, and over the band near the tower inside the bay's). Both are the limit of axis-aligned probes, not of this layout.
- **Before/after, pixels past 2%:** the bay 1.7%, the room 10.7% (its own capture now), the yard 2.1%, the street 0.1%, the tower's west side 0.9%.
