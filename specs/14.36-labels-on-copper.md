# 14.36 Labels on copper

pcb 7.5.4 writes on its board what each piece of copper is: a pad's number and its net inside it,
a trace's net along each segment, a via's net in it, each once zoomed in far enough to read. Three
things in the 2D canvas stood in its way. pcb worked round two of them; the third it cannot.

## What is wrong

1. **A text cannot be shown by another text's size.** Ball U18 is on `vccint`: its label is two
   lines, `U18` in the top half of the ball and `vccint` under it. Zoomed out, `vccint` is under
   `Canvas.text_min_px` and is not drawn, and `U18` stays in the top half, off centre beside ball
   U21, on no net, whose number alone sits in the middle. pcb wants the number centred while the
   net is too small to read, and moved up when the net appears, as KiCad does (each item decides
   per zoom, `ViewGetLOD`). But pcb places text once a layout, off the main thread, and the canvas
   drops a glyph in the vertex stage by its own em against one floor (`canvas_vert.glsl`): nothing
   lets a text show by another's size, nor hide once something reads. No workaround exists in pcb.
2. **A tag past the table is drawn as no tag.** `canvasColour` (`canvas_paint.glsl`) counts a shape
   tagged only where a tag is below `uTagCount`, one past the largest tag ever set. A shape whose
   non-zero tags lie past it is drawn untagged and never fades. That contradicts `canvas.h` ("Tag 0
   means none"), `canvas_tags_get` (a tag never set reads as state {0}) and `docs/canvas.md` ("An
   untagged shape never fades"). In pcb a via's hole and its mask openings, tagged with the via's
   object alone, stayed bright under the spotlight. pcb 7.5.4 writes the model's last tag once a
   layout to size the table.
3. **A tag's colour replaces every shape's colour, text included.** A white label carrying its
   pad's net tag turns the chosen net's purple and vanishes on the pad lit in it. pcb 7.5.4 gives
   each label a tag of its own and copies its copper's dim, hidden, raised and lit onto it at each
   retag: three tag ranges and some 65 lines that mirror the shader's own merge of two tags.
4. **A picked trace's ring crosses itself at a joint.** Seen on the `tags` sheet while this spec was
   built, and added to it at the user's word on 2026-11-14: where two ringed segments meet, a
   yellow tick of one segment's ring stands in the inner corner and the ring kinks at the outer
   bend. Each shape rings by its own distance, and a pixel just outside one segment, in the gap
   between it and its ring, still takes the other segment's ring. The roadmap has carried it since
   14.0 as "a pixel or two of error at a joint's inner corners ... invisible at 1x"; at 2x it shows.

## The design

**1. A text's zoom band: `CanvasText.shown_from` and `shown_until`**, each an em in units.

- `shown_from`: drawn only once a string of this em would read, its em on screen at least
  `text_min_px`; 0 = its own size, as every text is today.
- `shown_until`: drawn only until a string of this em would read; 0 = never hidden.
- Both are judged against the canvas's one floor, so what reads stays one setting, in framebuffer
  pixels, and a band moves with the display's scale as the floor does. A text under the floor by
  its own em is never drawn, whatever its band.
- pcb's use: a pad on a net writes its number centred with `shown_until` the net's em, and the two
  lines with `shown_from` the net's em. Zooming in, the centred number gives way to the pair in the
  same frame the net becomes readable.
- **Stored** as a dashed shape's pattern is: a glyph record is full (`p` its anchor, glyph and font,
  `f` its pen, em and angle), so a glyph with a band goes to the 64-byte store, its band in the
  record's fourth vector, under a flag of its own (bit 64 is free). A glyph without one stays the
  48-byte record it is.
- **Drawn**: the glyph branch of `canvas_vert.glsl` drops the glyph where `from` times the view's
  pixels per unit is under the floor, or `until` times it is at least the floor.

**2. A non-zero tag is a tag wherever it lies.** `canvasColour` sets `tagged` for a non-zero net or
object; the state is read only below `uTagCount`, the buffer past it never being uploaded, so a tag
past it reads as state {0}. `canvas.h`'s tag comment and `docs/canvas.md` say so.

**3. A layer keeping its colour: `CanvasLayer.keeps_colour`**, a SETTINGS store: a tag's colour
never replaces this layer's shapes' style colour. A uniform set per layer beside `uOpacity`
(`_draw_stores`, both programs) guards the colour override in `canvasColour`. Dim, hidden, raised,
lit and the view's fade still apply, and a tag's outline still rings a shape (never text). Per layer
rather than per text: pcb's labels are on layers of their own, and a layer setting is a plain store
that costs no record bit.

**4. A ring by the least distance: one pass more while a ring shows.** A pixel belongs to a ring
where the nearest of the layer's ringed shapes lies within the ring's band, `uRing.x` to `uRing.y`
outside it. Pass 0 already puts the largest ring coverage into alpha, and the shapes' own passes
spend the pixels they cover. A new pass, drawn after them and before the rings, takes each ringed
shape's quad again and multiplies the pixel's alpha by one less its coverage out to the ring's
inner edge, `uRing.x`, with the ring's own half-pixel ramp; so a pixel within the inner distance of
any ringed shape has no ring left to paint, and the rings then paint what remains as they do now.
It costs one pass a layer, only while a tag draws a ring.

**pcb then** (a spec of its own, after the bump): its labels carry their copper's own tags on layers
kept in their colour, and its three label tag ranges, the label tag functions, `tag_label`,
`tag_labels` and the table-sizing line go; a pad's number is centred until its net reads.

## Not in this spec

- **A halo behind text**, for white crossing a via's light-grey ring: not asked for.
- **Bands for shapes other than text.**
- **Texts outside their band culled on the CPU**: they are uploaded and dropped in the vertex
  stage, as text under the floor is today.

## Phases

0. **The spec**, committed alone; the roadmap's entry, started.
1. **A tag past the table**: `canvasColour`'s `tagged`. The `fade` sheet gains a box whose only tag
   lies past the table, under a fading view; `canvas-fade` checks it fades. Falsified: the old guard
   put back, the check fails.
2. **A layer keeping its colour**: `keeps_colour`, its uniform in both programs, the guard. The
   `tags` sheet gains a white text and a white box on a keeping layer under a tag coloured red and
   dimmed; `canvas-tags` checks them white, dimmed, never red. Falsified: the guard removed.
3. **A text's zoom band**: the fields, the record, the shader. A `band` sheet in `apps/canvas`: a
   plain string, one shown from a larger em and one shown until it, drawn at a zoom either side of
   where that em reads. A new arm, `canvas-text-band`: zoomed out, the plain and the until strings
   draw and the from string does not; zoomed in, the plain and the from strings draw and the until
   string does not; a banded glyph takes the 64-byte store and a plain one the 48-byte. Looked at.
   Falsified: the band ignored.
4. **A ring by the least distance**: the pass, in `_draw_layer` and `canvas_pass.glsl`. The `tags`
   sheet's ringed trace gains two probes: a pixel in the inner corner, in one segment's ring band
   and the other's gap, wanting the background; and one on the ring round the outer bend wanting
   yellow. `canvas-tags` checks them. Looked at, the trace's ring at 2x. Falsified: the pass skipped.
5. **Docs**: `docs/canvas.md` (tags past the table, a layer's colour, a text's band, the ring by
   the least distance), `canvas.h`, `docs/shaders.md` where it lists the glyph record, the roadmap
   (its line on a ring's error at a joint taken out).
6. **The reviews**: `/simplify`'s four and the thermo-nuclear review, background agents at effort
   high, applied once all are back. `--only canvas`. As built.

**Then**, with the user's say-so, master; pcb bumps `extern/cetra` from it and uses the three.

## As built

### Phase 1: a tag past the table

- **`canvasColour`** (`include/canvas_paint.glsl`) sets `tagged` for any net or object but 0; each
  tag's state is still read only below `uTagCount`, so one past it is state {0}: no colour, dim or
  light, and under a fading view faded as any tag not lit is. A view that does not fade draws as
  before.
- **The `fade` sheet** gains a red box at (3 mm, 2 mm) whose object tag, 1000, was never set and
  lies past the table of 3; `canvas-fade` wants seven points, the box faded with the far one, pale
  at half and gone under the mask.
- **Falsified**: `tagged` as it was, true only below the table, and `canvas-fade` fails with
  `past ff0000 (want ff8080), masked past ff0000 (want ffffff)`.

### Phase 2: a layer keeping its colour

- **`CanvasLayer.keeps_colour`**, under SETTINGS, false as a layer is added. `_draw_stores` sets
  `uKeepsColour` from it in both programs beside `uOpacity`; `canvasColour` takes a tag's colour
  only where it is 0. Dim, hidden, raised, lit, the fade and the outline are untouched.
- **The `tags` sheet** gains a layer keeping its colour, `tags kept`, with a white box at cell 9 and
  a white H above the rows, both under tag 9, coloured red and dimmed half. `canvas-tags` wants
  twelve points, the box 808080, and the H's ink grey (1771 pixels) with none red. Looked at: the
  box and the H grey, every other cell as before.
- **Falsified**: the guard made always true, and `canvas-tags` fails with the box `7f0000` and 1277
  red pixels in the H.
- The first commit of this phase failed: the mutation was written while the commit's hooks ran, and
  the hook saw a file change under it. The staged code was untouched; the file was restored from
  the index and committed again.

### Phase 3: a text's zoom band

- **`CanvasText.shown_from` and `shown_until`**, int64 ems in units after `mirrored`, so a text
  initialised by position draws as before. `canvas_edit_text` refuses either outside 0 to 2^30 as
  it does `size`, and a text with either set gets `CANVAS_FLAG_BANDED` (64) on each glyph, its band
  in the record's `dash[0..1]`.
- **The store**: a record carrying a vec4 after it, a dashed one or a banded glyph, goes to the
  64-byte store (`canvas_apply`); a plain glyph stays 48 bytes. `canvas_record.h` says what the
  vec4 holds for each.
- **Drawn**: `canvas_vert.glsl`'s glyph branch, after the floor on its own em, drops a banded glyph
  whose `shown_from` times the pixels a unit is under `uTextMinPx`, or whose `shown_until` is at or
  over it; 0 is no bound.
- **The band is an em smaller than the text's own**, not larger as the plan said: a pad's number is
  larger than its net, and a band over a text's own em would hide it before the band could
  matter. The plan's phase 3 corrected here.
- **The `band` sheet**: a red H plain, a green one shown from 0.05 mm and a blue one shown until it,
  a millimetre tall, at -1.5, 0 and 1.5 mm. **`canvas-text-band`** runs it at the gate's 800
  pixels, 20 mm across (the band 2 pixels, under the floor of 3) and 5 mm across (8 pixels), and
  counts each H's ink in its colour within 0.7 of its em: 376, 0 and 376 pixels, then 6054, 6054
  and 0. Sized for the gate's 400 by 300 points after two tries at the 1920 pixels a run with no
  size gets, where the band read on the near view and the far H's were too thin to count. Looked
  at, both views as the counts say.
- **Not checked by the arm**: that a plain glyph stays in the 48-byte store. A banded glyph
  reaching the other store would read no band and draw at both zooms, which the arm fails; a plain
  one in the 64-byte store would draw the same, and costs only bytes.
- **Falsified**: the band's test in the shader never true, and `canvas-text-band` fails, every H
  drawn at both zooms.
- Ran with it: `canvas-glyph-edge`, `canvas-text-width`, `canvas-tags` and `canvas-fade`, each
  passing.

### Phase 4: a ring by the least distance

- **Not the design's multiply, a min.** The plan had the new pass multiply alpha by one less each
  ringed shape's coverage out to the ring's inner edge. That would square every ring's own inner
  ramp, its own shape taking its share twice. Instead `CANVAS_PASS_NEAREST` (6) writes each ringed
  shape's inner ramp, `clamp(d - uRing.x + 0.5)`, into alpha by `GL_MIN`, over every pixel its quad
  reaches, its own body 0. Pass 0 already leaves the largest ring coverage any shape gives the
  pixel. The inner ramp rises with distance and the outer one falls, so the least inner ramp and
  the most outer one are both the nearest shape's, and the min of the two is the ring of the
  nearest ringed shape, exactly, with no ramp squared.
- **Drawn** between the shapes' fringe pass and the rings' (`_draw_layer`'s order 0, 1, 2, 3, 6,
  4), alpha alone, only while a tag draws a ring; fills take no part, and a ringed shape's quad
  reaches out to its ring in it as in passes 0 and 4. A polygon's edges discard their inner side,
  so its reflex corners keep their ring. `canvas_pass.glsl`'s list of passes says so.
- **Checked** in `canvas-tags`: the sheet prints the ringed trace's ends and joint, its half width
  and the ring's distances in framebuffer pixels, and the arm measures every pixel round it against
  the nearer of its two segments: those whose centre lies in the gap, 0.8 to 1.4 pixels out at the
  gate's 2x, dark; those well inside the band yellow. Before the pass, 15 of 214 gap pixels were
  lit, the tick; after, 0, and all 498 band pixels yellow.
- **Looked at**, the sheet at 1920 pixels: the kinks at the outer bend and the tick in the inner
  corner gone, one ring round the trace.
- **Falsified**: the pass writing 1, a min that takes nothing, and `canvas-tags` fails with the 15
  pixels lit again.
- `scripts/gates.py --only canvas` passes, every arm, after phases 1 to 4.
