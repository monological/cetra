# cetra 14.18: a hidden element

## Context

cetra has no way to hide an element. Every element stands in the tree, and everything in the tree
is laid out, drawn, eased and hit-tested. An app that wants an element out of sight while keeping it
sizes it to nothing (`UI_FIXED` 0 on both axes), and an element sized to nothing is still there:

- **It still takes a gap.** `_measure` (`ui.c:1113`) and `_arrange` (`ui.c:1208-1211`) count every
  child that is not `fill` among the gaps, so a row of three with the middle one sized to nothing is
  two gaps wide between the other two.
- **What is under it still lays out and draws.** Its children are arranged in a negative inner rect
  at their own sizes, and `_emit` clips nothing of its own (only a pane clips, `_emit_pane`), so a
  child's padding, words or chrome still show beside it.
- **It still asks for a scroll bar**, which spec 14.17 stopped drawing outside the element; an
  editor sized to nothing still sums every line's rows each frame to be told there is no room.
- **An editor sized to nothing re-wraps its whole text, twice.** `_place` (`ui_editor.c:1133-1140`)
  takes its columns from its text's width, held to at least 1; at 0 wide that is 1, which makes
  every wrapped line a guess again (`_rows_guessed`), and `_settle` wraps the text at one column, up
  to 192 KB a frame with a wake of 0 until done. Shown again, the columns change back and it all
  happens again at the real width. Meanwhile `ui_editor_columns` and `ui_editor_line_rows` answer
  for one column.
- **It still eases** (`_ease`, `ui.c:1883`, over the whole tree), so a pane's scroll easing when it
  was sized to nothing keeps asking for frames.

pcb sizes to nothing in four places, each with a workaround for one of the above:
- A pane holds a view of each kind it has shown, a code editor and a schematic, so each keeps its
  place while another is shown; the view not shown is sized to nothing (`panes.c`, `size_views`).
  The stray scroll thumb 14.17 fixed was this editor's.
- A code editor stands in a box over a bar; a hidden box's padding and its bar's height, padding
  and words are cleared by hand (`box_size`, `bar_show`), since phase 11 of pcb's 6.5 found a box
  sized to nothing still as wide as its padding and its bar's words.
- While one pane fills the window the other side of each split is sized to nothing and its gap
  taken away (`hide_under`).
- A compared pane's two sides touch, with no gap, because a gap would also stand beside every view
  sized to nothing.

Branch and spec: `14.18-a-hidden-element`, off cetra's master (`81ac8a7f`).

## The design

**`ui_set_hidden(el, hidden)`**, and `bool hidden` on `UIElement` under BY FUNCTION
(`// ui_set_hidden`). A setter, not a setting, because the library reacts at once: hiding lets go
of what the UI holds inside the element. Its contract in `ui.h`, beside `ui_remove` and `ui_move`:

> `el` and everything under it hidden, or shown again. Hidden, it takes no room and no gap in its
> parent, is neither laid out nor drawn nor eased, takes no press, wheel, hover or key, and holds no
> focus; it keeps everything else -- an editor's carets, scroll and wrapped rows, a field's text, a
> pane's scroll and rows -- until shown. Hiding lets go of the pressed element, a held bar and any
> screen's focus inside it, left on nothing, as `ui_remove` does. Refused by name: a screen's root,
> and a pane's virtual row.

What a hidden element is, place by place (the line numbers are master's at `81ac8a7f`):

- **Layout.** A hidden child is out of the flow as a `fill` one is, and further: it is not laid out
  at all.
  - `_measure` (`ui.c:1078`): not recursed into, not summed, not counted among the gaps.
  - `_arrange` (`ui.c:1153`): skipped in the `fill` loop, the cross-axis loop, the gap count, the
    SHARE and GROW sums and rounds (`_still_growing`), and the placement loop; the cursor does not
    move past it.
  - Its rect, and every rect under it, is left empty: 0 by 0 at the place in the flow it would
    start, so a host reading one sees nothing shown. Nothing else of theirs is touched.
  - The baseline: `_on_line` and `_baseline_of`'s first child in the flow skip it.
  - `_break_lines`, `_place_widgets` and `_settle_rows` do not go into it: a label keeps its
    lines, a field and an editor their place (`ops->place` is not called), and a pane of virtual
    rows builds and frees none.
- **Drawing.** `_emit` and `_emit_pane` skip it and everything under it.
- **Easing.** `_ease` does not go into it: its hover, focus and value weights and a pane's scroll
  stay where they were and ask for no frames. They ease on from there when it is shown.
- **The pointer.** `_deepest_at` (`ui.c:1917`) returns nothing in it, as for `disabled`, so no
  hover, press, wheel or bar lands there (`_hit`, `_pane_at`, `_scroller_at`); `_over` and
  `ui_pointer_over` (`ui.c:1974`, `2058`) do not count it.
- **Keys and the focus.** Nothing hidden holds the focus, so no key reaches it. To keep that so:
  - hiding runs `_forget` (`ui.c:1334`) on the element: the press, a held bar, the focus last seen
    and each screen's focus inside it let go;
  - `ui_set_focus` refuses, by name and logged, an element that is hidden or under a hidden one;
  - `ui_move` into a hidden element, or under one, lets go of what the element moved holds as
    hiding does;
  - `_gather` (Tab and the pad's navigation) skips it and everything under it.
- **Reveals.** `ui_scroll_into_view` of an element hidden or under a hidden one does nothing.
- **Refusals**, each logged, as `ui_remove` and `ui_move` refuse them: a screen's root (a screen
  is popped instead) and a pane's virtual row (the pane's to build and free).
- Showing does nothing but clear the flag: the next layout places it, and an editor laid out at the
  width it had keeps its columns and every wrapped row.

**Found, not in this spec.** `disabled` is read in five places but never set `UI_STATE_DISABLED`
(`el->state` is written only at `ui.c:2197` and `2487-2495`), so the `!= UI_STATE_DISABLED` checks
never fire; and neither `ui_set_focus` nor `_takes_keys` reads it, so a disabled field the app
focuses still takes keys. Its own spec, if wanted.

## Tests

A probe case in `apps/gametest/src/ui_editor_probe.c`, beside `remove` and `move`, read by an arm in
the `ui` group with `doc_flags`, its docstring in `run_ui_gate`'s list:

- **`hidden`** -> `ui-hidden`. Each flag 1 when it holds:
  - `gap`: a row of three panels 100 wide with a gap of 10, the middle one hidden: the third starts
    110 after the first, and the row fitting them measures 210.
  - `grow`: of two GROW children, one hidden, the other takes the whole row.
  - `empty`: the hidden panel's rect, and its child's, are 0 by 0.
  - `drawn`: a hidden editor of 200 lines and a hidden panel with a fill push no quad: the draw
    list is the one the screen draws without them.
  - `press`: a press and a wheel where the hidden editor stood before land on the panel behind it;
    the editor's scroll does not move.
  - `focus`: hiding the focused editor leaves the focus on nothing; a key typed then changes nothing
    in its text.
  - `held`: hiding the element holding the press, and the editor whose bar is held, lets go of both
    (`ui_system_held`).
  - `refocus`: `ui_set_focus` on the hidden editor, and on a field under a hidden panel, is refused
    and the focus stays as it was.
  - `kept`: an editor 300 wide on a text of long lines, scrolled to line 50 with two carets, hidden
    for five passes and shown: the same top line and carets, `ui_editor_columns` the same in every
    pass, and settled at once (`ui_editor_settled`), nothing wrapped again.
  - `sized`: the same editor sized 0 by 0 instead reports one column, which is why sizing to
    nothing is no hide.
  - `still`: a pane hidden while its scroll eases toward a target: `ui_build` reports nothing
    moving.
  - `rows`: a pane of virtual rows hidden for five passes builds and frees none (`row_builds`), and
    shown has the same rows built.
  - `shown`: shown again, each element stands where it stood before it was hidden.
  - `tab`: Tab from the element before a hidden focusable one goes to the one after it.
  - `move`: the focused editor moved under a hidden panel lets go of the focus.
  - `refused`: hiding a screen's root and a pane's virtual row is refused; neither is hidden.

## As built

### Phase 1: the hide

- **`ui_set_hidden`** in `ui.h` after `ui_move`, with `bool hidden` under BY FUNCTION. Its contract
  is the one above, with the empty rects said, and `ui_set_focus`'s and `ui_move`'s contracts name
  the hidden case. It refuses, by name, a screen's root, a pane's row and also an element taken
  out, as `ui_remove` does, since an element out of the tree has no screen to let go through.
- **The flow**: `_in_flow` (not `fill`, not hidden) is the one test, read by `_measure`'s sum,
  `_arrange`'s gap count and share loop, `_still_growing`, `_on_line` and `_baseline_of`'s first
  child. `_arrange` lays a hidden child out with `_arrange_hidden`, 0 long on each axis at the
  cursor (along) or the inner start (across), and everything under it the same; the fill loop
  skips a hidden backdrop.
- **Not visited**: `_measure`, `_break_lines`, `_settle_rows`, `_place_widgets`, `_emit` (and so
  `_emit_pane`'s children), `_ease`, `_deepest_at`, `_over` and `_gather` each return at once on a
  hidden element. `_nav` gathers through `_gather`, so the pad's moves skip it too.
- **The focus**: `_hidden_in(el)` (it or an ancestor hidden) refuses `ui_set_focus` and
  `ui_scroll_into_view`; hiding, and `ui_move` into a hidden element or under one, run `_forget`.
- **A departure, found by `rows`**: shown again, a pane of rows lost two rows and built them again.
  `ui_layout` settles rows before it lays out, from the pane's last rect, and a hidden pane's last
  rect is the empty one, so its view read as 0 tall. `_rows_settle` now keeps the rows a pane holds
  while the last layout left it no height; the layout after gives it one and the next settle uses
  it. A pane sized to nothing holding rows keeps them the same way, where it trimmed them to two.
- **`editor_theme`** split out of `editor_fixture`, so a scene builds editors on a screen of its own.
- **`hidden`**, 16 flags, each scene on a screen of its own (`hidden_scene`): `gap`, `grow`,
  `empty`, `shown`, `drawn` (the draw list's quad count and signature equal to the screen's with
  the two removed, and fewer quads than shown), `press`, `focus`, `refocus`, `held`, `move`, `tab`,
  `kept`, `sized`, `still`, `rows`, `refused`. `press` checks a wheel scrolls the pane round the
  hidden editor and a press neither presses nor focuses it.
- **Falsified**, each committed first:
  - the hidden child counted among `_arrange`'s gaps fails `grow`, not `gap`: the cursor still
    skips it, so the count only shrinks the room the growers share. Counted among `_measure`'s
    gaps as well, the row measures 220 and `gap` fails too;
  - `_forget` left out of hiding fails `focus` and `held`;
  - `_place_widgets` going into a hidden element fails `kept`: a hidden pass gives the editor its
    empty rect, and one column.
- **Gates**: `--only ui,gate-docs` passes, 109 arms.
- **The look**: `apps/canvas --editor cetra/src/shore_runup.h -x -f 10` rendered twice is
  byte-identical (noise floor 0), and byte-identical to master's.

### Phase 3: the reviews

A correctness pass and a thermo-nuclear review, in background agents at effort high, applied once
both were back (`74d9de40`). The review kept the nine early returns, each walk's one guard covering
all its callers as `disabled`'s already did, and the zeroed rects, which pcb reads (its schematic's
hit tests, a split's two sides).

- **The rows guard narrowed.** Phase 1's test, a pane left no height, could not tell a pane hidden
  last layout from one really 0 tall, which then kept changed rows (`ui_scroll_rows_changed`) and
  rows past a shrunk count for as long as it stayed so. `UIRows.hidden_last` is set by
  `_arrange_hidden` and cleared by the arrange of a pane's rows, and `_rows_settle` keeps what it
  holds only on that flag, in the one settle before the layout after it is shown; the settle after
  that layout frees and builds as ever. A pane 0 tall is as on master again.
- **Hover and focus looks at rest.** `_ease` froze them while hidden, so a button hidden by its own
  press faded out a hover and a focus it no longer had when shown. Hiding, and a move under a hidden
  element, put `t_hover` and `t_focus` at 0 through the subtree (`_hide_let_go`, `_rest_weights`);
  the scroll stays where it was, as designed. The spec's "they ease on from there" holds for the
  scroll alone.
- **`ui_move` refuses an element taken out**: two under a subtree removed mid-pass have no screen
  either, so the screen check passed them and the hidden case read a NULL screen.
- **One refusal**: `_child_screen` holds `ui_remove`'s and `ui_set_hidden`'s four refusals, logged
  under the caller's name.
- **Contracts**: `ui_scroll_into_view` names the hidden case; `ui_move` the element taken out;
  `ui_set_hidden` the looks at rest.
- **The probe by scene**: `hidden_flow`, `_drawn`, `_pointer`, `_focus` (focus, refocus, held,
  move), `_tab`, `_kept` (kept, sized), `_still` and `_rows` (rows, refused), with `scene_alone`,
  `bare_panel` and `fixed_editor`, which `editor_fixture` now builds on. `kept` and `rows` also
  check the element laid out empty while hidden, which a hide doing nothing would fail; `held`
  checks the button's looks at rest.
- **Docs**: `docs/ui.md`'s rows bullet says the behaviour, not the mechanism; the roadmap says pcb
  would hide its views, which it does once it moves to this.
- **Re-falsified**: without the rows guard, `rows` fails (4 rows in 4 builds, 6 builds shown);
  without the looks at rest, `held` fails.
- **Gates**: `--only ui,gate-docs` passes, 109 arms. **The look**: `apps/canvas --editor`
  byte-identical to master's.
- **Left as found**: a release action hiding the hovered element's panel still marks it hovered for
  that pass, which the next pass clears unseen; `g_presses == 0` in `held` passes without the
  feature, its other terms carrying the flag.
