# Spec 13.28 -- grain that holds still, and a CRT

## Context
The film grain crawls in diagonal lines. The cause is one line in `tonemap_frag.glsl:730`,
`hash21(gl_FragCoord.xy + grainSeed, ...)`. The frame number is added to BOTH x and y, so each
frame is the previous frame's noise shifted one pixel diagonally, and the whole field slides
across the screen. Real engines make every frame's grain independent. Unreal, for example,
jumps a grain texture to a random offset each frame. The Purkinje rod noise
(`include/purkinje.glsl:200`) is seeded the same way and has the same crawl.

The user also wants a CRT effect for silent: a consumer TV with a slot mask, soft scanlines, a
little composite colour bleed and a slight curve with rounded dark corners, all "noticeable
but subtle". The game's own UI (the door prompt, the menus) goes through the CRT; the Cetra
debug GUI and the FPS counter do not.

silent gets grain off and CRT on. Both remain engine features, off by default.

- The profile's "tonemap + finishing" row, as the cost baseline.
- The crawl measured on the current code, with the metric phase 1's gate arm will use:
  1. Render a flat region with grain, then subtract its grain-free twin to leave the noise
     field alone.
  2. Correlate frame N's field with frame N+1's shifted by (1,1).
  3. Expect about 1 today and about 0 once fixed.

## Phase 1 -- grain fix
1. **Read the source first.** Jarzynski and Olano, "Hash Functions for GPU Rendering" (JCGT
   2020), through pdf2md into `docs/papers`. Implement `pcg3d` from its text.
2. **An integer hash of (pixel x, pixel y, frame)** in `include/noise.glsl`, as
   `frameNoise(uvec2 pixel, uint frame)`. Every frame becomes independent noise, with nothing
   sliding. GLSL 330 has uint arithmetic.
3. **The grain calls it** with `uvec2(gl_FragCoord.xy)` and a new int uniform, the frame
   index. The luma weighting and the clamp stay as they are.
4. **Purkinje's rod noise calls it too**, on `uvec2(uv * 1024)`, so its basis stays independent
   of resolution. This moves `purkinje_night`, the one golden that evaluates it; the change is
   shown and re-baked. The float `grainSeed` uniform then has no reader and goes.
5. **The E10 rule.** AGENTS.md records "replacing the sin-fract form with an integer hash is
   REJECTED (roadmap E10)" and names grain, because "no consumer has a correct pattern to
   regress against". This crawl is that demonstrated artifact, which the row's own revive
   clause asks for. The change is scoped to grain and Purkinje. `hash21` and `hash13` and their
   other consumers (wind, foam, curl noise, tile offsets) are untouched. AGENTS.md's E10
   paragraph, its stale "zero of the 27 goldens", and the roadmap row get updated to say so.
6. **The dither is left alone.** It is static by design (spec 11.24), not crawling.
7. **Look:** silent with grain forced on, frames N and N+1 side by side plus a short
   `--screenshot-every` strip, for the user to confirm nothing crawls.

## Phase 2 -- the CRT, engine side
**Method.** Timothy Lottes' CRTS (2018, Unlicense; libretro `crt-lottes-fast.slang` and
Shadertoy MtSfRK). Before writing code, fetch its source into `docs/papers` and implement from
it.
- Rounded corners follow crt-geom's `corner()`, from its description only (crt-geom is GPL).
- The composite chroma bleed is YIQ with chroma band-limited wider than luma. That follows the
  NTSC bandwidth ratios: luma 4.2 MHz, I 1.3 MHz, Q 0.6 MHz.

**Placement:**
- When `crt_enabled`, `engine_present_frame` (`engine.c:2334`) passes an fb-sized RGBA16F
  intermediate as `postfx_run`'s `target_fbo` instead of 0.
- The debug tiles and the overlay hook (where the game UI draws) bind no framebuffer, so they
  land in it.
- The CRT then draws to framebuffer 0 between the overlay (2360) and ImGui (2364). So the game
  UI is CRT'd, while the debug GUI and the FPS counter stay sharp.
- The headless screenshot reads framebuffer 0, so it shows the CRT.
- With CRT off nothing changes: the target stays 0 and every golden stays 0 px.
- The intermediate must be exactly fb-sized, because `ui_draw.c:767` scales its scissor by
  `fb_width` and `fb_height`.

**Dither stays last (spec 11.24).**
- `ditherPattern` moves from `tonemap_frag.glsl` into `include/dither.glsl`.
- The tonemap skips it when CRT is on, and the CRT pass ends with it.
- It stays static, with no frame term.

**Passes** (`crt.c/h`, made on first use and latched off on failure, copying
`create_glare` / `glare_run` at `glare.c:264` and `postfx.c:3934`):
1. **`crt_resample_frag`.** The finished frame becomes a virtual-resolution signal:
   `crt_lines` tall (default 480), width from the aspect ratio.
   - Area-filtered from the full-resolution intermediate.
   - Decoded to linear with the tonemap's own `pow(2.2)` encode, not sRGB.
   - Stored RGBA16F.
   - Cheap, because it runs at about 854x480.
2. **`crt_frag`**, per output pixel:
   1. Lottes' `Warp` on the screen position, scaled by `crt_curvature`; black past the tube,
      with rounded corners.
   2. Two scanlines times a 4-tap horizontal filter: CRTS's windowed-cosine scanline, and its
      `exp2(blur*d^2)` horizontal filter.
   3. Luma and chroma taken through YIQ, with chroma blurred wider by `crt_bleed`.
   4. A darken-only slot mask in output-pixel units, its period scaled with output height.
   5. CRTS's exposure lift, so the picture keeps its mid-grey.
   6. Re-encode, then dither.

**Settings** (plain fields under PostFX's SETTINGS banner, all off by default):
- `crt_enabled`
- `crt_lines` (480)
- `crt_scanlines`, `crt_mask`, `crt_curvature`, `crt_bleed` (each 0..1)

The defaults are "subtle" values, set during the look. Scanlines and mask fade out when fewer
than about 2 output pixels fall on each virtual line, so a small window gets no moire.

`create_post_program` gets the two new programs (`program.c`), and `free_postfx` frees the
object.

**Look.** In the render app on a fixture, at the three window sizes, and in silent through a
temporary flag. Iterate on the picture with the user until it is signed off.

## As built

### Phase 0 -- baseline
- **Silent before**, master 9989f2db, frame 240: the hall (`--cam-eye -0.75,1.92,13.6
  --cam-target -0.75,1.5,10.0`), the kitchen (`4.3,1.65,13.4` -> `1.0,1.1,10.6`) and the street
  (`30,1.7,1.5` -> `60,2,0`). Each at framebuffers of 960x540, 1920x1080 and 3200x1800, which on
  this Retina display is `-W/-H` of 480x270, 960x540 and 1600x900. Grain on at 0.09 through the
  film look, as silent ships.
- **The crawl, measured.** `cornell_point` at 800x600 with `--grain 0.1`, frames 30 and 31, each
  minus its grain-free twin, leaves the noise at a standard deviation of 4.6 codes over 65% of the
  frame. Correlated at every shift in [-2, 2]^2, frame 31's field matches frame 30's at 0.994 one
  pixel diagonally across, (+1, -1) in image rows, and nowhere else above 0.06. Each frame is the
  last one moved one pixel along the diagonal.
- **The end of the chain's cost**, silent's hall at 1920x1080 (240 frames): "tonemap +
  finishing" 0.29 ms GPU / 0.12 CPU, "overlay" 0.00; frame 44.1 ms.
- **Read before any code:** Jarzynski and Olano 2020 for `pcg3d`, and Lottes' CRTS (2018) and
  crt-lottes (2014) for the CRT, each entered in `docs/papers/README.md`.

### Phase 1 -- grain
- **As planned:** `pcg3d` in `include/noise.glsl`, as section 6.1 of the paper writes it, and
  `frameNoise(cell, frame)` over it (the top 24 bits as a float). The grain calls it on the pixel
  and the frame; the rod noise calls it on its 1024-cell grid and the frame salted with
  `0x9E3779B9`, so the two do not match where a cell lands on a pixel. The float `grainSeed`
  uniform, and its `% 4096`, became an int `noiseFrame`.
- **The crawl, re-measured** with phase 0's metric: the largest correlation between consecutive
  frames at any shift fell from 0.994 to 0.005. The noise's spread is unchanged, 4.68 codes
  against 4.58. The sine hash's own spatial structure, up to 0.05 at small shifts, went with it.
- **`purkinje_night`** moved 87,259 px, at most 18 codes and 0.14 on average: the same frame
  with its rod noise drawn anew. Re-baked. No other golden runs grain or rod noise.

### Phase 2 and 3 -- the CRT, and silent
- **Built as planned**, two passes in `crt.c`, from CRTS and crt-lottes' slot mask. `--crt` and
  `--crt-*` on the render app. Measured at silent's default window (a 3200x1800 framebuffer),
  alternating runs: the CRT pass costs 0.61 and 0.70 ms of GPU, and the frame is 72.0 and
  71.9 ms with it against 73.1 and 69.8 ms without, inside the run-to-run spread.
- **silent: grain off, and the CRT OFF by default**, the user's call after seeing it on; `--crt`
  turns it on. The plan had it on with `--no-crt`.

### Review (thermo-nuclear), applied
- **The engine binds its own targets.** `postfx_picture_fbo` says where the frame is drawn (0, or
  the CRT's picture), the engine binds it explicitly for the debug tiles and the overlay, and
  `postfx_present_picture` shows it. The latched `crt_this_frame` is gone, and so is the overlay's
  reliance on an inherited binding.
- **`crt.c` knows nothing of PostFX.** It takes a `CrtLook` of the six settings plus the dither;
  the two targets are one `CrtTarget` type with one ensure, and its state save is `gl_pass_begin`,
  which now saves and disables the scissor too.
- **The shader owns its constants**: the luma and chroma blurs, the warp, the corner and the
  mask's line count, each said where it is. The composite bleed is the wide filter's colour with
  its luma replaced by the narrow filter's, which is what the YIQ round trip meant. The old
  matrices were three-decimal inverses (0.15% off) and lit near-black channels by up to 3 codes;
  every other pixel is unchanged (floor 0 px).
- **One display encode**, `include/display.glsl`, with its inverse for the resample.
- **The tonemap dithers only when it writes the window** (`target_fbo == 0`).
- Not taken: moving the composite blur into the signal pass. That would change the look.
- Goldens: 32 of 32 at 0 px.

### Phase 4 -- plumbing, gates, docs
Done after the merge, on the same branch name, at the user's request.
- **Config and GUI.** `postfx.crt.{enabled, lines, scanlines, mask, curvature, bleed}` rows, and a
  "CRT" group in Finishing after Dither. `config-coverage` 240 controls / 304 rows, and
  `config-perturb` moves all 445 values.
- **`grain`**, two arms, on the grain alone (a capture less its grain-free twin):
  - `grain-still`: |corr| between frames 30 and 31 at any shift in [-2, 2]^2 is 0.006, bound 0.05.
  - `grain-amount`: the field over the shader's scale has spread 0.2891 against 1/sqrt(12) =
    0.2887, bound 5%.
- **`crt`**, five arms, on `aerial_fixture`'s sky:
  - `crt-off`: every setting moved with the switch off is 0 px, through the snapshot.
  - `crt-lines`: period 4.00 px at h/4 lines, depth 0.90 thin against 0.005 fused.
  - `crt-mean`: **CHANGED from the plan.** The plan asked that the mean stay within a few per cent
    of CRT off. That is not the design: CRTS's tone curve holds mid-grey and compresses anything
    brighter ("won't get back peak brightness"), so the Cornell box's centre measured 0.92 and
    0.83. The arm instead predicts each flat 8x12 block from the plain frame through the slot mask
    and the curve. Measured 0.83% at worst over 3882 blocks, bound 2%, and the model stands 7.7%
    from the plain frame, so a CRT doing nothing fails.
  - `crt-curve`: corners 0 at full curvature and 99-150 flat, centre 1.0000.
  - `crt-dither-last`: a 21 px run dithered, 163 px undithered.
- **Falsified by hand**, each arm against a mutation of its own:
  - the old diagonal crawl: `grain-still` 0.996 at (1, -1);
  - the grain at 0.8 of its strength: `grain-amount` -19.9%;
  - scanlines forced fused: `crt-lines` depth 0.005;
  - the tone curve removed: `crt-mean` 37%;
  - the warp zeroed: `crt-curve`, corners lit;
  - the CRT's dither skipped: `crt-dither-last`, 163 px;
  - every frame through the CRT whatever the switch: `crt-off` 472,960 px, with `crt-mean`'s floor
    of flat blocks failing too.
- **numpy** is a suite dependency now, for the field arithmetic; `docs/verification.md` says so.
- **`frameNoise` runs on `include/pcg4d.glsl`**, the rain's and the fire's Jarzynski & Olano hash,
  rather than the `pcg3d` phase 1 added beside it: one integer hash in the tree, not two. The rod
  noise's pattern moved with it, `purkinje_night` by 87,417 px with its mean unchanged to 0.0008 of
  a code, and was re-baked.

  the shader inventory, the dither rule, the noise and E10 paragraphs, silent's row);
  `docs/shader-subsystems.md` (Film grain, The CRT); `docs/cli-reference.md` (the render app's
  `--crt*`, silent's `--crt`).
- Scoped gates: grain, crt, purkinje, dither, lut, flare, config, gate-docs, all passing.
