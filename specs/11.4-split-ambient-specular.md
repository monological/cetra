# 11.4 — Split ambient specular: exact specular occlusion by construction

## Context

**Why this exists.** Spec 11.3 built the bent-normal machinery (production, blur,
accumulation — all verified) and a correct cone-cone term, and then hit a wall
that no amount of term-tuning can move: the engine applies ALL screen-space
occlusion at tonemap, as one factor on a buffer where diffuse, specular and
direct light are already summed. Two consequences followed, both observed live
this session. First, the specular share must be *guessed* per pixel
(fresnel × metallic × smoothness), so the term can never multiply exactly what
it models. Second, the occlusion query's reflection vector comes from the
normal-mapped G-buffer normal while the bent normal is a half-res geometry-
scale field — the query varies at texture frequency, *incoherently* with the
specular already rendered, and the interference prints as mottling on smooth
metal (the artifact that stopped 11.3's default flip). Two fixes aimed at the
symptom (temporal window, wider blur) measured exactly zero change; the
diagnosis is placement, not noise.

**The principle** (what shipped engines satisfy, each by different means):
specular occlusion must multiply only the specular it models, using the same
reflection vector that produced that specular, at a point where both exist
together. Filament satisfies it with baked texture-frequency bent normals;
Activision by evaluating in the lighting pass. This engine can satisfy it
structurally: **write ambient specular to its own MRT attachment in the scene
pass, and let the post chain — where the AO/bent data exists same-frame —
occlude it exactly and fold it back.** When a bump swings R, the specular
sample and its occlusion query then swing together, and the mottling class
becomes impossible rather than tuned away.

**What it retires.** The entire tonemap spec-occ heuristic (4.2.1's accepted
residuals — rim shimmer, the aoMap diffuse/specular divergence — fall out of
the design), and 11.3's interim cone term at tonemap. The bent-normal
production from 11.3 is consumed unchanged.

**The open 11.3 decision this plan settles:** 11.3's branch currently has bent
as default with goldens un-rebaked. It lands OPT-IN first (default back to
legacy, reviews + as-built recording the frequency-mismatch finding, merge on
permission, zero goldens move) — 11.4 then builds on its machinery and is the
thing that finally changes the default look, once.

## Settled design

- **Two accumulators in pbr_frag, not a factoring.** Clearcoat and sheen
  multiply the *running* ambient then add their own specular lobes, so the
  split builds `ambDiffuse`/`ambSpec` from the start (dims applied to both,
  lobes added to ambSpec). The inline arm textually preserves the original
  parenthesization — the byte-identity gate. `SpecOut` at
  `layout(location = 7)` (the last GL-guaranteed slot); an
  `inlineAmbientSpec` uniform keeps specular energy in color for captures,
  the late/transparent pass, and OIT (their FBOs discard location 7 — safe
  for GL, fatal for energy). aoMap stays on both shares for now; the
  diffuse-only move (fixing 4.2.1's recorded divergence) is a follow-up.
- **Attachment 7 via the descriptor table**: `GBufferAttachment` gains an
  explicit `attachment` enum (the table assumed index==attachment; slots 5/6
  are OIT's, on a different FBO); creation, clears, draw-buffer gating,
  indexed blend-disable and capture exclusion then all follow from one row.
  Format **R11G11B10F** (~41.5 MB at 1080p ×4 MSAA + resolve, half of
  RGBA16F; no alpha needed; WS_SCENE_MAX already under its ceiling; bloom
  precedent) — FBO completeness verified first thing, one-row RGBA16F
  fallback. Raise the GL_MAX_DRAW_BUFFERS startup check to 8 (also fixes a
  pre-existing under-report vs OIT).
- **The composite: fog's blend idiom, not additive.** A naive additive
  `spec×SO` gets double-occluded by tonemap's whole-buffer AO factor. The
  composite pass instead outputs `vec4(spec·SO, aoDiffuseFactor)` and blends
  `(GL_ONE, GL_SRC_ALPHA)` → `spec·SO + scene·aoFactor`, one pass, no
  scratch. Tonemap's AO share collapses to 1 in split mode (the
  contact-shadow fold stays — direct light, independent). Deliberate,
  recorded consequences: fog inscatter and bloom stop being AO-darkened
  (physically better); metering sees the AO'd scene (goldens pin exposure —
  no golden impact).
- **Arm B ordering: GTAO moves ahead of TAA.** The normals resolve + GTAO
  sweep + AO accum/blur move above the TAA resolve; the composite lands
  after the AO blur, *before* TAA — so the reunited scene (diffuse +
  occluded specular) is TAA-stabilized together, preserving today's temporal
  behavior on smooth metal. The reorder is a structural no-op with SSGI off
  (GTAO reads only aux/normals/noise) and lands as its own 0-px phase. The
  composite point also sits before the SSR march (whose catcher reflections
  sample the HDR and must see model specular) and before fog/MB/DoF/
  metering/bloom — all get the corrected color for free.
- **SSGI gather: accept the small energy loss** (it samples the scene inside
  the GTAO pass, which now precedes the composite); the second-sampler sum
  is the recorded follow-up.
- **Modes: OFF/LEGACY/BENT/SPLIT through bring-up**, default LEGACY until
  approval (legacy = the byte-exact master anchor; bent = the A/B
  instrument). The cone-cone code extracts to `include/spec_occ.glsl` shared
  by the composite and debug view 7. LEGACY/BENT retirement is a post-flip
  follow-up.

## Phases

Every phase: built, committed, gates green, determinism ×2, 13-golden sweep,
raiden recipe. No merge without permission.

**Phase 0 — land 11.3 opt-in first** (on `bent-normal-spec-occ`): default
back to LEGACY, verify 0 px everywhere, background reviews, as-built
recording the frequency-mismatch finding and deferring approval/flip into
11.4, roadmap A5 → "delivered opt-in", merge on permission. 11.4 branches
only after.

**Phase 1 — spec + baselines** on `split-ambient-specular`: spec committed
first; golden sweep, gates output, raiden ×2, perf ×2 on master, and stills
of the four 11.3 artifact regimes (mottled metal close-up, catcher backdrop,
unoccluded ground, TAA flicker) under legacy AND bent for later A/B.

**Phase 2 — attachment 7 + shader split, default off.** The table row +
draw-buffer re-keying by slot; resolve after the SSS resolve; the
two-accumulator pbr_frag with inlineAmbientSpec wiring
(`!spec_this_frame || late_pass || oit || capturing`). Verify: goldens 0 px
(inline path byte-identical), R11G11B10F completeness, split mode renders
(visibly missing ambient spec — documented incomplete).

**Phase 3 — the Arm B reorder**, its own commit. Predicted 0 px on all
goldens; `--ssgi` A/B stills recorded as the one known small change.

**Phase 4 — the composite + tonemap SPLIT branch.** New
`spec_occ_composite_frag.glsl` (shared include), the fog-idiom blend, CLI/GUI
gain `split`, debug view 7 recomputes from the shared include. Verify: legacy
goldens 0 px; split determinism ×2; energy checks (OIT keeps transparent
spec, probe capture unchanged, AO-off split≈legacy conservation, catcher
reflections contain model spec); split-vs-bent A/B on the four regimes.

**Phase 5 — USER-APPROVAL CHECKPOINT.** Windowed sessions, 4-way combo live.
No default or golden change until approval. Tuning in the shared include.

**Phase 6 — default flip → SPLIT + golden re-bake** per a fresh
predicted-verdict table (~11 of 13 move; contact_debug/backface_dark
structurally immune; froxel_fog moves for a new reason — inscatter escaping
AO), measured AE/PAE + 1:1 inspection + per-commit justification; off/legacy
modes still 0 px vs master; churn recalibration from measured basis if
tripped; perf + memory after-numbers.

**Phase R — reviews + as-built + roadmap.** Follow-ups recorded: aoMap →
diffuse-only, SSGI gather energy, SSR occluded by the same cone,
LEGACY/BENT retirement, SSGI/SSR escaping AO under SPLIT.

## Risks

The AO-application-point move is the largest look change (fallback: keep AO
at tonemap and pre-divide, rejected as primary for crevice bloom inflation);
R11G11B10F MSAA renderability (checked first, one-row fallback); the
opaque/transparent seam (late-pass pixels keep inline spec and take the
composite's aoFactor — watch glass edges on the OIT fixture); reschedule
noise above 1 LSB is stop-and-investigate.

**Effort: L** (~1.5–2 weeks of gated sessions).
