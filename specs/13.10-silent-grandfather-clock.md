# Spec 13.10 -- a grandfather clock in silent's hall

## Context
The user wants a grandfather (longcase) clock in the hallway, seen as you step out of the kitchen. It should have:
- glass in the case door, so the pendulum shows swinging;
- an audible tick;
- a beautiful design and pattern;
- real wood textures, looked up.

Decisions already taken with the user:
- **Case:** a dark mahogany body (Poly Haven `lacquered_cherry_wood`) with rosewood panels (`rosewood_veneer1`) and white-maple stringing (`white_maple_veneer`).
- **Time:** the clock runs from a set start time (3:07) on the game clock, so headless runs repeat.
- **Sound:** tick only, no chimes.

This is app-only work in `apps/silent/`, with no engine change.
- Branch `13.10-silent-grandfather-clock`, spec `specs/13.10-silent-grandfather-clock.md`, in a worktree cut from local master. The number is 13.10 because 13.9 is the rain work on its own branch.
- Per the standing rule for demo apps: no gate arms, no goldens, and no review fan-outs. It is verified by renders shown to the user and a windowed run.

## Where it stands
- **Placement:**
  - The hall's WEST wall, directly opposite the kitchen doorway. The doorway is in the x = 0 wall at z 12.75..13.55; the west wall's face is at x = -1.45.
  - A `KitFrame` at (-1.45, FLOOR_Y, 13.15) with yaw pi/2 gives local +d = world +x (into the hall) and +a along the wall.
  - The face is 1.1 m from the door plane. The player's spawn looks straight at it after a quarter turn left.
- **Width limit:** at most 0.56 m (z 12.87..13.43), so it stays clear of the GI probe rows at x = -1.25, z = 12.6 and 13.6 (`silent.c:201-215`). A probe inside solid geometry darkens its surroundings.
- **Height limit:** the ceiling is at 2.90 m (2.6 m clear), so the finial tops stop at about 2.80.
- **The hall is unlit today** (`lights.c:36-66`). Only a sliver of the ceiling tube's spill reaches that wall, plus GI bounce, and the flashlight is off by default. A dim hall light is therefore part of the plan.
- **Audio:** silent is on the Game framework with an EntityManager but has no AudioSystem. The API (`cetra/src/game/audio.h`) gives:
  - held `Sound`s from WAV files, positioned with `audio_sound_set_position`;
  - re-triggered by `audio_sound_play`;
  - a listener updated from the camera after `on_pre_render`;
  - no device when headless, so renders are unaffected.
- **The API has no click sound.** Its only procedural sound is a 0.18 s sine beep, so the tick is a synthesized WAV.

## Design

### A. Assets, generated or fetched, all CC0 or OFL
- **Wood:** `tools/fetch_textures.py` gains `lacquered_cherry_wood`, `rosewood_veneer1` and `white_maple_veneer`, as three Poly Haven sets cut to 256 px.
- **Brass**, for the dial boss, capitals, rosettes, finials, weights and bob: a flat tarnished-brass reflectance, about (0.75, 0.62, 0.32), over the `Smear008` scan's roughness and relief (`surface_only`). This is how the stainless is made. Neither site has a brass scan.
- **Clock art** goes into the existing card picture, through `tools/make_cards.py` and `MAT_CARDS`. The dial is painted enamel and the rest is wood, so none of it is metal. The atlas grows to 2048 px. The new cards are:
  - **Painted break-arch dial**, 0.30 x 0.45 m:
    - a cream ground, aged and mottled;
    - Roman numerals set radially, with a minute track and Arabic five-minute numbers;
    - a subsidiary seconds ring and a date aperture;
    - painted roses in the four spandrels;
    - in the arch, a moon face in a starry sky over two gold-edged hemispheres;
    - a fictional maker's name.
    - The numerals are in IM Fell English (OFL), downloaded and cached, never committed.
    - Everything round the arch is the rosewood mask a hood door frames.
  - **Base marquetry panel:**
    - a rosewood field inside cross-banding, with maple stringing;
    - a central oval holding a sand-shaded maple shell patera;
    - quarter fans in the corners.
  - **Frieze:** blind fretwork on rosewood, under the cornice.
- `make_cards.py` imports `fetch` and `open_polyhaven` from `fetch_textures.py` for the veneer backgrounds (at 1k), instead of keeping its own copy of `fetch`.
- **Sound:** a new `tools/make_tick.py` writes `assets/audio/silent/tick.wav` and `tock.wav`, 48 kHz mono, about 150 ms each.
  - A 2-3 ms high-passed noise impact for the pallet striking.
  - Three damped high modes (about 3.1, 4.7 and 6.2 kHz, tau about 8 ms) for the escapement's ring.
  - Two damped low modes (about 180 and 420 Hz, tau about 30 ms) for the long wooden trunk resonating.
  - The tock has its modes about 8% lower and is slightly softer.
  - It is deterministic and CC0 by construction.

### B. The case, static, in the world kit (new `apps/silent/src/clock.c/.h`)
Georgian longcase proportions. Heights are above the floor.

| Part | Height | Build |
|---|---|---|
| Bracket feet, plinth | 0-0.50 | Stepped boxes for ogee feet and the skirting; the marquetry card on the front |
| Base moulding | 0.50-0.56 | Stepped `MAT_CASE` boxes |
| Waist, 0.44 wide | 0.56-1.50 | See below |
| Waist moulding | 1.50-1.56 | Stepped boxes |
| Hood, 0.52 wide | 1.56-2.12 | See below |
| Frieze, cornice | 2.12-2.24 | The fret card; stepped cornice boxes |
| Swan-neck pediment | 2.24-2.38 | See below |

- **Waist:**
  - A rosewood door frame round a GLASS pane (`MAT_GLASS_CLEAR`, a thin grimed box), with maple stringing boxes inset.
  - Turned quarter-columns at the front corners (`kit_frame_lathe`), with brass bases and capitals.
  - A brass keyhole.
  - A dark back board inside.
  - Two brass weights on chains (lathes, and pipes), which show through the glass.
- **Hood:**
  - A glazed door over the dial card, flanked by turned rosewood columns with brass capitals.
- **Swan-neck pediment:**
  - Two S-curves as rosewood `kit_frame_pipe` paths, ending in brass rosettes.
  - A centre plinth, with three brass ball-and-spire finials (lathes).
- **Collider:** one `KIT_COLLIDER_ONLY` box for the whole clock.
- **Grime:** `MAT_CASE`, `MAT_ROSEWOOD` and `MAT_BRASS` join `mats.c`'s grime table (about 0.4-0.5), so dust gathers in the case's crevices.
- **Hall light:** a dim, warm bare bulb hanging on a cord from the hall ceiling near the doorway, about (-0.75, 2.5, 13.7).
  - A point light with no shadows, like the street lamps, and a small emissive decoration bulb (a `GLOWS` row).
  - Its brightness is set at phase 2's look. It must be enough to read the clock through the door and keep the hall dim.
  - The GI capture at frame 2 then carries it into the hall's ambient too.

### C. The moving parts
- **Four parts:** the pendulum, the hour hand, the minute hand and the seconds hand.
- **Each is its own small Kit**, built around its pivot at the origin:
  - `kit_init(..., NULL, NULL)`, so it has no colliders;
  - `mats_register`, draw, then `kit_finish`, which gives a node.
  - `kit_finish` frees the unused material slots, so each part keeps only its one or two materials.
  - `clock.c` owns a helper that does that sequence.
- **Pendulum:**
  - A dark rod about 0.97 m long, from a pivot behind the dial at y about 1.62.
  - A lenticular brass bob, r 0.08, turned on its side.
  - This needs a lathe about an arbitrary axis. `kit.c`'s internal `lathe()` gains axis/u/w parameters, exposed as `kit_frame_lathe_on`, and `kit_frame_lathe` becomes its upright case.
  - Planar UVs project onto the plane perpendicular to the axis, not onto world XZ.
- **Hands:** flat black spade shapes built from `kit_tri_facing` fans, plus a small brass boss.
- **Motion:** in `on_pre_render`, from `game->time`, with a period of 2 s (a seconds pendulum).
  - The pendulum's node is `T(pivot) * R_x(theta)`, where `theta = 4 deg * sin(pi t)`.
  - The minute and hour hands turn continuously from 3:07:00.
  - The seconds hand STEPS once a beat, in time with the tick.
  - Each node's `original_transform` is written before the engine's transform walk, which latches the previous transforms, so motion vectors and TAA work.

### D. Sound
- **In `on_init`:**
  - `create_audio_system(engine->headless)`, then `game_set_audio_system`.
  - Load `tick.wav` and `tock.wav` with `audio_sound_from_file` on the SFX bus.
  - Position both once at the movement, behind the dial.
- **In `clock_update`:** `beat = floor(t + 0.5)` changes at each extreme of the swing, where the pendulum visibly stops. On each change it plays tick or tock alternately and steps the seconds hand.
- **`--mute`** sets the master bus to 0, matching gametest.
- Falloff is miniaudio's default inverse model. The volume is set by ear at the windowed check.

## As built

### 0. Setup
- The number moved from 13.9 to 13.10, because 13.9 is the rain work on its own branch.
- The work is in a worktree at `.claude/worktrees/13.10-silent-grandfather-clock`, cut from LOCAL master.
  - The worktree tool branches from `origin/master` by default, and origin was 29 commits behind: 13.7 and 13.8 are unpushed.
  - So the worktree was added by hand from master and entered with `EnterWorktree`'s path form.
- The texture download cache was copied in from the main checkout rather than re-fetched, so every existing set rebuilds from the same bytes.

### 1-3. Assets, the case and its light, the moving parts
These were built together and looked at together.
- **Woods:** the three Poly Haven veneers join `fetch_textures.py`. `MAT_CASE`, `MAT_ROSEWOOD` and `MAT_MAPLE` take them at roughness factors over 1, since old varnish is duller than a scan's fresh lacquer.
- **Brass:** `MAT_BRASS` is the smear scan under (0.72, 0.58, 0.3), metallic.
- **Clock art in the card picture:**
  - `make_cards.py` paints the break-arch dial, the marquetry base panel and the key-fret frieze.
  - It imports `fetch` and `open_polyhaven` from `fetch_textures.py` in place of its own copy.
  - It downloads IM Fell English (OFL) for the dial.
  - It emits the dial's two arbors into `cards.h` as ANCHORS (`CARD_CLOCK_DIAL_HANDS`, `CARD_CLOCK_DIAL_SECONDS`), since only the painting knows where its rings are.
  - The picture is 2048 x 1024; the cards fill a band, and a square was more than half empty.
- **The roughness map is a QUARTER the picture's size, and the normal map is 64 x 32. That is a fix, not a saving.**
  - A roughness map is a layer of the engine's material texture array, which brings every layer up to its largest.
  - At the picture's size it made all 27 layers 2048 x 1024: **288 MB**.
  - Now they are **18 MB** at 512 x 256. That is also below master's 108 MB, where 13.8's 1024-square card roughness was already lifting every layer.
- **The case** (`clock.c`):
  - bracket feet, a skirting, the marquetry card, and stepped mouldings;
  - a hollow waist with a glazed rosewood door, maple stringing, turned quarter-columns with brass caps, a keyhole, and two brass weights on doubled lines;
  - a hood with the dial card behind a glazed door, turned columns, a brass collet on the arbor, and the frieze card under a stepped cornice;
  - a swan-neck pediment: two cubic `kit_frame_pipe` curves at the front and back, a stepped board following them, brass rosettes, and three ball-and-spire finials;
  - one collider box.
- **`kit_frame_lathe_on`** turns a lathe about any axis, for the bob and the rosettes, which face the room.
  - `section_start` is now shared with the pipe.
  - Planar UVs project onto the plane across the axis. For the upright case that is the old world XZ exactly, so every existing lathe is unchanged.
- **The hall bulb:**
  - a flex, a black socket and frosted `MAT_BULB` glass (a decoration glow at 1500 nits);
  - a 30 cd warm point light with no shadows, range 7 m.
  - It reads the clock from the doorway and leaves the hall dim.
- **The moving parts** are four small kits, each finished into its own node round its pivot, and turned each frame from `game->time`:
  - the pendulum, a 0.97 m steel rod and a lenticular brass bob, at 0.07 rad = sin(pi t);
  - the hour and minute hands, flat black spade and lozenge plates, creeping from 3:07;
  - the seconds hand, stepping once a beat.
- **Looked at:** from the doorway; the hood close up; the swing at t = 2.5, 3.0 and 3.5 s, which catches both ends and the centre.
- **Vertices:** 317,498, up from 283,770.

### 4. Sound (built; waiting to be heard)
- **`tools/make_tick.py`** writes `assets/audio/silent/tick.wav` and `tock.wav`, 48 kHz mono, 150 ms each:
  - a high-passed noise click;
  - three damped steel modes, at 3.1, 4.7 and 6.2 kHz;
  - two damped case modes, at 180 and 420 Hz.
  - The tock is 8% lower and at 0.85 of the level.
  - `--plot` draws the waveform and spectrogram, which is how they were checked headless.
- **Silent has an AudioSystem now.**
  - Both sounds are positioned at the movement, behind the dial, at volume 0.5.
  - One plays on each change of `floor(t + 0.5)`, at the ends of the swing, alternating tick and tock. The first frame only records the beat, so nothing sounds at load.
  - `--mute` zeroes the master bus.
