# Spec 12.6 — A grotto to fall into

## Context

`apps/gametest` is a flat 50×50 plate with nothing around it. Walking off the edge is
**already possible today and entirely unhandled**: there is no kill plane, no respawn, no
world bound, and Jolt's `CharacterVirtual` has no y limit — you accelerate downward forever,
`grounded` reads 0 permanently, and nothing catches it. This spec does not create that
failure mode; it gives it a bottom.

The ask: a camera that follows the player, an ocean under the platform, a cave-like cliff
around it, turquoise water, and **swimming for both the player and the NPC**, with a new swim
clip. Neither character may sink.

Three decisions taken up front:

- **The world ships default-on; the camera ships behind `--follow-cam`.** Spec 12.4 gated its
  ramp fixture to protect the two menu goldens, then reversed it: *"trading the only
  demonstration of the feature for two unchanged reference images is the wrong way round."*
  The ocean is the same case. The camera is not — it repoints every headless capture, and the
  orbit-drag workflow should stay the default.
- **Surface swimming only.** You float, you cannot go under. Simplest to make read correctly
  at camera distance, and impossible to get stuck below.
- **The camera trails the player's facing**, using the smoothed `player_yaw` that already
  exists. No mouse control, and therefore **no camera-relative movement work**: W moves world
  +Z, the character turns to face +Z, the camera swings in behind. Self-consistent.

  **This was wrong and shipped differently — see §6.** It is left here as written because the
  reasoning reads as sound and is not, which is the useful part.

---

## What the exploration settled

**The swim clip is cheap.** `assets/gen_puppet_fixture.py` packs chunks in `CLIPS` order with
cumulative offsets, so appending an eighth clip appends chunks, accessors and bufferViews and
leaves every existing `byteOffset` and index untouched. Every consumer binds **by name**
through accessor indirection, so no existing clip's sampled values move. Cost: ~12 lines of
Python, one tuple, regenerate, commit `puppet.gltf` + `puppet.cscn`. **No `anim` or `ik` arm
moves.** The `fixture-gen` gate's contract is "the committed file is what this script emits
today", not "the file never changes".

**A fourth entry in the locomotion space would break a gate.** `gametest.c:1436` gates the
trace's three weight columns on `base->count == 3`, and `anim-trace-idle` asserts the idle
weight is 1.0. Swim must be a **separate space reached by crossfade** — which is also the
right semantics (swim is not "faster than run") and preserves the last of
`ANIMATOR_SPACE_MAX == 4`.

**Buoyancy has no engine support and needs none.** Jolt buoyancy is unbound; `gravity_factor`
is rigid-body-only and unreachable from a character; `character_controller_update`'s gravity
argument is world-wide. The seam is `character_controller_set_velocity`, at the exact line
that applies gravity today — `gametest.c:1325` for the player, `:1389` for the chaser.

**IK already releases when ungrounded** — `ik_weight` decays to exactly 0 and
`ik_update_targets` returns before casting a ray. But the foot ray filters on
`1u << OBJ_LAYER_STATIC` and nothing else, so **a seabed is indistinguishable from the floor**.
Gate `want` on an explicit swimming flag as well as grounded, or a shallow bed plants a
swimmer's feet on the bottom.

**Turquoise needs real seabed geometry.** The shader's `bed` term is the refraction resolve of
actual scene geometry and `path` comes from the depth buffer; `water->height_at` only drives
shoaling and foam. With nothing behind the surface every pixel takes the maximum path and the
sea becomes one flat colour — literally the defect spec 11.84 opens with.

**gametest has no sky and no IBL**, so `iblEnabled == 0`, the reflection term is zero and the
horizon goes black. Fixed by `create_sky_atmosphere()` + `create_ibl_resources()` + one
`sky_bake` — **no HDR asset needed**, the sky bakes the environment cubemap itself.

**`create_water()`'s defaults are a storm** — 11.5 m/s over 120 km fetch, ~2 m significant
height, on a 50 m world. `apps/tree` authors 6 m/s over 15 km instead.

## Design

### 1. The grotto

Keep the platform at ±25. **Do not shrink it** — the ramp's uphill end reaches x ≈ 23.94 and
the steps reach x = −20; anything smaller cuts the IK fixture. The `ik` gate casts rays at
x = 18 and x = −16, both comfortably inside.

New static geometry, all through the existing `create_box_node` + `create_entity` +
`entity_add_rigid_body(MOTION_STATIC, OBJ_LAYER_STATIC)` idiom in `build_ik_ground`:

| piece | placement | note |
|---|---|---|
| **cliff ring** | four walls at ±60, from seabed to y = +14 | encloses the basin; hides the horizon |
| **overhang** | a cantilevered ring at the top, leaning inward | reads as a cave mouth; rotated boxes, `entity_set_rotation_euler` **before** the body |
| **platform skirt** | four walls from the platform edge (±25) down to the seabed | what you fall past |
| **seabed** | a pale plane at y ≈ −11, sloping to ≈ −14 at the cliff | **load-bearing for the turquoise**, not decoration |

Starting constants, to tune by eye:

```
GROTTO_WATER_Y     -6.0     // still-water plane
GROTTO_SEABED_Y   -11.0     // near the platform
GROTTO_SEABED_FAR -14.0     // at the cliff
GROTTO_BASIN_HALF  60.0     // cliff ring, half extent
GROTTO_CLIFF_TOP   14.0
```

Two clearances these numbers are chosen for, both real hazards the exploration named:

- A floating character's feet sit at water level − `PLAYER_RIG_DROP` = −8. The foot ray starts
  at −7 and reaches −10; the seabed at −11 is **out of reach**, so it cannot plant even if the
  swimming gate were missed.
- `stick_to_floor_distance` is 0.5 m and active every step. A seabed 3 m below the feet is
  inert; a shallower one would pull a treading character down and report it grounded, which
  would fight the float clamp.

### 2. The ocean

```c
Water* water = create_water();
water->level  = GROTTO_WATER_Y;
water->extent = GROTTO_BASIN_HALF;          // bed domain, NOT a bound on the surface
water->height_at = grotto_bed_height;        // shoaling + foam
glm_vec3_copy((vec3){0.03f, 0.13f, 0.14f}, water->scatter_albedo);   // apps/tree's lagoon
scene->water = water;                        // scene owns and frees it
```

Absorption stays the clear-seawater default (gametest is 1 unit = 1 m, so no scaling).
`scatter_glow` stays zero — `apps/tree` tried a floor under it and reverted it. Sea state
authored calm (≈6 m/s, 15 km fetch) rather than the default storm.

The surface is a projected grid in NDC and effectively infinite; `level` is the whole
placement. The cliff ring is what gives it a visible edge, since the ground plane's vanishing
point sits **above the top of the frame** at this camera — there is no horizon in shot.

### 3. Sky and IBL

`create_sky_atmosphere()` + `create_ibl_resources()`, sun elevation set, then
`sky_bake_static_luts` and `sky_bake(sky, ibl, engine)`; on success assign `scene->sky`,
`scene->ibl`, `scene->render_skybox = true`. Copy `apps/tree:1254-1325`. `sky_bake_ex` hard-
refuses a NULL IBL, so both are required — but neither needs a file.

### 4. Swimming

One predicate and one branch, in `on_update` where gravity is applied today:

```c
static bool in_water(const vec3 p) { return p[1] < GROTTO_WATER_Y; }
```

Submerged, replace `vel[1] -= gravity * dt` with a critically-damped drive toward the surface:
buoyant term proportional to depth below `GROTTO_WATER_Y`, damping on `vel[1]`, and a clamp so
a long fall does not launch the character back out. Horizontal speed drops to a swim speed.
Applied at `gametest.c:1325` (player) and `:1389` (chaser) — **the chaser needs its own
handling**, since it never zeroes `vel[1]` when grounded and accumulates downward velocity.

`swimming` is also the gate on IK: `want = (grounded && !swimming) ? 1.0f : 0.0f` at
`gametest.c:1580`. One line, no gate consequence — every `ik` arm drives `--ik-probe` with
synthetic targets and its own world, never through `ik_update_targets`.

### 5. The swim clip

A new `swim()` in `gen_puppet_fixture.py` beside `gait()`, appended to `CLIPS`. Breaststroke
shape: arms sweeping about **X or Z** (`gait` uses Y, which is why this is a new function
rather than a new `gait(...)` call), legs in a frog kick, a gentle `Hips` bob. Looping, so it
must start and end on the identical quaternion — the generator asserts this.

Played as its own one-entry space, crossfaded on the water-entry and water-exit edges:
`animator_source_name` then reports `"swimming"` against `"locomotion"`, which is a readable
hook for a gate arm and leaves `base->count == 3` intact.

Both characters get it; the chaser's animator is a near-verbatim copy of the player's.

### 6. The follow camera

`--follow-cam`, a file static (parsed in `main`, read in `on_pre_render`). Written **from
`on_pre_render`** — the engine derives view and projection immediately after that hook, so a
pose written from `on_render` is a frame late. Follow `player_entity->position`, **not**
`player_rig->global_transform`, which the graph walk has not yet updated.

**What shipped: the arrow keys turn it and nothing else does.** The trailing form below was
built, failed twice, and was replaced. The record of why is worth more than the design was.

`player_yaw` follows the VELOCITY, and a camera chasing it can never be in front of you. Press
back: the character turns round, the camera swings in behind, and both directions read as
forward. There is no backward left to invert — which is why two sign changes each only moved
which direction felt broken. A third attempt gave the camera its own heading, eased toward the
facing while walking away from it; better, still automatic, still able to surprise. The fourth
stopped moving it automatically at all.

So `look_x`/`look_y` on the arrows and the right stick, `apps/forest`'s bindings and rates,
ticked on the sim clock so the turn is identical headless and windowed. The camera orbits the
player's position at `FOLLOW_CAM_DISTANCE` with the pitch clamped to forest's limits. Movement
under the flag **is** camera-relative after all — W into the screen, S back toward the lens —
because once the player can aim the camera anywhere, world-aligned input stops meaning
anything. That is precisely the work the decision section above cut as unnecessary.

The arrows are shared with menu navigation, which is safe rather than the collision the action
table warns about: `ui_up`/`ui_down` carry the `ui` flag, so a menu suppresses the camera
actions and the arrows navigate it. The hazard is two readers acting at once, which the
suppression switch prevents.

The original design, kept for the record:

```
eye    = player_pos - forward(player_yaw) * CAM_DISTANCE + up * CAM_HEIGHT
target = player_pos + up * CAM_LOOK_HEIGHT
```

Scale `apps/forest`'s constants by `PLAYER_SCALE` (2.0). Mutually exclusive with the drag
controller, following `apps/tree`'s precedent — skip `mouse_drag_update` while following. That
last part did survive.

## Phases

Branch `12.6-a-grotto-to-fall-into` off master. Each phase ends green on its own gate.

1. **Spec.** Copy this plan to `specs/12.6-a-grotto-to-fall-into.md`, commit alone.
2. **Sky + IBL.** Two constructors and a bake, nothing else. Green on: 33 goldens — `menu` and
   `menu_focus` **will move here** (the clear colour becomes a sky). Re-bake in this commit,
   isolated, so the movement is attributable to exactly one change.
3. **The grotto geometry.** Cliff, overhang, skirt, seabed. No water yet. Green on:
   `--only ik` (the fixture must be untouched) and the goldens, re-baked again.
4. **The ocean.** `scene->water`, the turquoise authoring, the bed callback. Green on: goldens
   re-baked; `--only ik,anim,ui,save,audio,gamepad` unchanged.
5. **Swimming.** The float clamp for both characters, the IK swimming gate, a `--trace-player`
   column for the swim state. Green on: `--only ik,anim,gamepad,save`.
6. **The swim clip.** `gen_puppet_fixture.py`, regenerate, commit both assets. Green on:
   `--only fixture-gen,anim,ik` and the `puppet` golden at 0 px.
7. **The follow camera.** `--follow-cam`, off by default. Green on: goldens **0 px** (the flag
   is off for bakes) and the full suite.
8. **Docs + the full suite.** `AGENTS.md`'s gametest row, `docs/cli-reference.md`, and
   `docs/verification.md` for what no arm sees.

**Golden re-bakes land in phases 2, 3 and 4 — one per commit, nothing riding along.** That is
12.4's rule and the reason its movement was attributable.

python3 scripts/goldens.py

By eye, which is the only instrument for most of this:

```bash
./out/bin/gametest                      # walk off the edge, land in water, swim
./out/bin/gametest --follow-cam         # the camera trails you over the edge
./out/bin/gametest --no-chaser          # the NPC's swim, isolated by removing it
```

**Two goldens cannot support single-measurement attribution.** `menu` has read 672 px three
times across unrelated code states and `menu_focus` 675 once, each vanishing on a re-run of
the same binary. Expected deltas here are 10³–10⁴ px, comfortably above that, but **re-run
before quoting any number.**

## Risks and what this does not do

- **Nothing in the tree solves water interaction**, so there is no abstraction to extend and
  no precedent to copy. `apps/tree` declares swimming an explicit non-goal; `apps/forest`
  never tests water. All of §4 is newly authored.
- **World-aligned input with a trailing camera** was accepted here as a known characteristic —
  "after turning, W still moves world +Z, which is now sideways on screen… not a defect." It
  was a defect, and naming it in advance is what made it easy to dismiss twice when reported.
  The reasoning covered the steady state of walking forward and never the case of reversing,
  where the camera chasing the facing removes backward entirely. Shipped camera-relative.
- **No kill plane below the water.** Surface-only swimming means the basin floor catches you;
  there is still no handling for leaving the grotto entirely, which the cliff ring prevents by
  geometry rather than by logic.
- **`build_ik_ground` has two callers** — `on_init` and `ik_probe_world`, which builds its own
  floor. The probe world does **not** get the grotto; its arms must keep their current numbers.
- **No 34th golden.** A new one falsifies prose in four places (`docs/verification.md:11` and
  `:589`, `AGENTS.md:159` and `:189`), which 12.4 records explicitly.
