# 10.7.2 — SSR flicker under TAA: inverse-luma accumulation + seed bound

## Context

At close range over the shadow-catcher floor, the SSR reflection of the
(now-correct) bright sheen rim flickers while TAA runs, and shows a frozen
noise pattern with TAA off. Diagnosis (measured at the user's camera,
frame-120-vs-122 AE, 1280x720):

- branch full config: 242,818 px churn; master: 128,850; **both builds with
  `--no-sheen`: 110,894 — byte-identical**, so 10.7/10.7.1 changed no
  temporal machinery. The correct sheen simply put much brighter, wider
  content in front of a pre-existing weakness.
- `--no-ssr` drops churn to 74,875: SSR is the churn engine. The dilated
  diff map localizes the instability to the catcher-floor reflection band
  plus a thin rim line.

Mechanism, from the pipeline scan: SSR traces with per-frame reseeded ray
jitter (seed advances only while TAA resolves — deliberate, 10.6-recorded).
The traced result — premultiplied `(radiance * weight, weight)`, radiance
already clamped at `WS_REFLECT_MAX` — goes through the SHARED plain-RGBA
temporal accumulator (`temporal_accum_frag.glsl`: per-channel 3x3
neighborhood clamp + plain `mix`), then a-trous, then a tent-upsample
composite. Per-frame jittered rays alternately hit and miss the bright rim,
the plain mix lets those bright swings through, the neighborhood clamp
cannot reject them (they are in the local min/max), and the a-trous smears
the residue into the moving streaks the user sees.

The codebase already names the cure: `taa_resolve_frag.glsl:103-106` and
`ssgi_accum_frag.glsl:61-66` both blend with **inverse-luminance (Karis)
weights** — `w = feedback / (1 + luma)` — and the shared accumulator's
header explicitly records that SSGI kept its own program for exactly this
("its YCoCg clamp and inverse-luma firefly blend are real signal
differences"). `run_temporal_accum` already accepts per-consumer programs
(TAA and SSGI pass their own). SSR joins that club.

## Design

### 1. SSR gets its own accumulator program with the inverse-luma blend

New `cetra/shaders/ssr_accum_frag.glsl`: a copy of
`temporal_accum_frag.glsl`'s structure (same uniforms/reset/reprojection
early-outs and the same per-channel RGBA neighborhood clamp) with ONE
change — the final blend becomes the house inverse-luma weighted form on
RGB (luma = Rec.709 dot on the premultiplied radiance; the 1.0 pivot is
diffuse white in working space, same reasoning as taa_resolve/ssgi_accum),
while ALPHA (coverage) keeps the plain `mix` — coverage is not radiance and
must not be luma-warped. Bright single-frame ray hits get downweighted, so
the accumulation converges to a stable mean instead of chasing them.

C side (`cetra/src/postfx.c`): create `fx->ssr_accum_program` beside
`ssgi_accum_program` (program.c factory + postfx create/destroy), and the
SSR call at `postfx.c:1682` passes it instead of
`fx->temporal_accum_program`. The shared accumulator shader and the
`run_temporal_accum` driver are untouched; the other five consumers cannot
be affected.

### 2. Bound the seed (the 10.6-recorded backport)

`postfx.c:1650`: `int ssr_seed = ssr_temporal_on ? fx->frame_index % 4096 : 0;`
with the PCSS rationale comment (float-hash conditioning over long
sessions — `render.c:699-709` is the pattern). This is what degrades live
sessions beyond what any short headless test shows.

### 3. Tidy-up rider (also 10.6-recorded): ssr_frag's inline IGN

**[SUPERSEDED BY MEASUREMENT — DO NOT REDO. See As built.]** The plan
called this migration a provable no-op; it is not. It was attempted,
measured at 31,800 px on a no-TAA render (the include's dot() form is not
bit-equal to the inline multiply-add and the hash's low bits steer rays),
and reverted. Both inline copies now carry the waiver in ssr_frag.glsl.

## Deliberately NOT in scope

- The shared accumulator, its clamp space, and the other five consumers.
- The composite (tent shader is source-shared with bloom — not touched).
- The TAA-off frozen-noise pattern: with no accumulator the seed freezes by
  design; a-trous handles it spatially. Unchanged behavior.
- YCoCg neighborhood clamping for SSR (SSGI-style): the recorded escalation
  lever IF the weighted blend's measured churn drop is insufficient —
  decided by numbers, not taken preemptively.
- `WS_REFLECT_MAX`, feedback constants, SSR march parameters.

## As built

The implementation evolved past the plan by measurement, in three steps; the
plan's "ONE change" framing and its "feedback constants not in scope" line
are both superseded by what follows. All numbers are frame-120-vs-122 AE at
the reporting close-up (1280x720, `--taa --headless-jitter`, pinned
exposure); the no-sheen baseline floor is 110,894 px and the starting churn
242,818 px.

1. **Inverse-luma blend alone: 238,299 px.** Barely moved it -- the swings
   are structured (whole neighborhoods re-aim with the jittered rays), not
   salt-and-pepper fireflies.
2. **+ the AO-class 0.97 at-rest window (`TEMPORAL_FEEDBACK_SSR`):
   177,995 px.** The shared accumulator's own comment names the law (a
   re-randomizing estimator's residual scales with 1 - feedback); SSR is
   exactly that class and had been running the 0.9 default.
3. **A velocity-GATED clamp (off at rest, on in motion): 107,619 px at
   rest -- and rejected interactively.** With the clamp disabled at rest,
   ghosts banked during a pan had nothing to flush them when motion stopped
   (user-visible sphere reflections stranded on the floor). Dead end,
   recorded so nobody walks it again.
4. **The shipped shape -- always-on clamp with motion-adaptive SLACK (box
   widened by its own extent at rest, zero slack in motion) + a moving
   window of 0.85: 123,826 px.** Converged history sits mid-box untouched
   (no boil); a stranded ghost lands outside even the widened box and is
   snapped back within a frame; moving pixels drop to a ~6-frame window
   because surface velocity reprojects reflections parallax-wrong. The SSR
   share of the churn fell ~90% (131.9k -> 12.9k above floor). User
   confirmed both behaviors interactively.

**The IGN rider was reverted** (see the annotated Design section): measured
31,800 px on a no-TAA render; the inline hash's float ordering is
load-bearing. Both inline copies in ssr_frag.glsl carry the waiver.

**Seed bounds:** ssr_seed takes the 10.6-recorded % 4096; the review round
extended the same bound to the three sibling unbounded-hash sites (contact
shadows' frameIndex, GTAO's slice-rotation frameIndex, grain's seed) --
identical values below frame 4096, so every golden and proof (all <= 122
frames) is unaffected by construction. Froxel inject's frameIndex was NOT
bounded: its usage couples to the fog history parity machinery and wants
its own look.

**Untouched-path proofs:** the accumulator runs only while TAA resolves, so
no-TAA renders are byte-identical by construction and measured so (sheen
fixture + golden 0 px; cornell_box 0 px). contact_fixture sits at a
pre-existing 3 px / 1 LSB vs the master base -- introduced by 10.7.1's
review commit restructuring sheen.glsl (compiler rescheduling of pbr_frag,
the recorded benign class), verified present with 10.7.2 stashed. Gates
pass with the catcher gate byte-identical (104,138 px / floor 101,316 --
that scene's reflections are dim, confirming the flicker was a
bright-content problem).

## Review outcomes

Five background reviews (thermo-nuclear + four /simplify angles). The
structure was endorsed unanimously (a third signal-specific accumulator
matches the codebase's recorded doctrine; the uniform/const feedback split
is principled). Applied:

- max() guards on both blend-weight lumas (TAA's YCoCg clamp can emit
  mildly negative RGB; the sibling blends carry the same guard).
- The stale-comment sweep: the SSR call site no longer claims to reuse the
  shared accumulator; run_temporal_accum's feedback comment names its two
  consumers; the shared shader's header names both siblings; the
  "enables the clamp" leftover from the rejected velocity-gate wording is
  gone; the smoothstep thresholds carry units and rationale; the header
  names all three signal policies; the second inline IGN copy carries the
  waiver; the init-block enumeration includes ssr_accum.
- The seed-bound family completion above.

Recorded, not done:

- Extracting the inverse-luma blend formula (now three copies: taa_resolve,
  ssgi_accum, ssr_accum) -- the rule of three is met, but the house policy
  (color.glsl's header) shares only exact-inverse pairs; a fourth consumer
  flips the verdict.
- Graduating slack-at-rest into the shared accumulator as a uniform: the
  same mechanism could serve GTAO's residual (TEMPORAL_FEEDBACK_AO's "the
  only way is a longer window" is now known to have a second option) --
  a measured change for whenever AO residual is next revisited.
- Reflection-parallax reprojection (reproject SSR history by hit-distance
  parallax instead of surface velocity) -- the UE/ReBLUR-class upgrade that
  would let the moving case keep a longer window. The honest next rung.
