# 10.7.2 — SSR flicker under TAA: inverse-luma accumulation + seed bound

## Context

At close range over the shadow-catcher floor, the SSR reflection of the
(now-correct) bright sheen rim flickers while TAA runs, and shows a frozen
noise pattern with TAA off. Diagnosis (measured at the user's camera,
frame-120-vs-122 AE, 1280x720):

- branch full config: 242,818 px churn; master: 128,850; **both builds with
  `--no-sheen`: 110,894 — byte-identical**, so 10.7/10.7.1 changed no
  temporal machinery. The correct sheen simply put much brighter, wider
  content in front of a pre-existing weakness.
- `--no-ssr` drops churn to 74,875: SSR is the churn engine. The dilated
  diff map localizes the instability to the catcher-floor reflection band
  plus a thin rim line.

Mechanism, from the pipeline scan: SSR traces with per-frame reseeded ray
jitter (seed advances only while TAA resolves — deliberate, 10.6-recorded).
The traced result — premultiplied `(radiance * weight, weight)`, radiance
already clamped at `WS_REFLECT_MAX` — goes through the SHARED plain-RGBA
temporal accumulator (`temporal_accum_frag.glsl`: per-channel 3x3
neighborhood clamp + plain `mix`), then a-trous, then a tent-upsample
composite. Per-frame jittered rays alternately hit and miss the bright rim,
the plain mix lets those bright swings through, the neighborhood clamp
cannot reject them (they are in the local min/max), and the a-trous smears
the residue into the moving streaks the user sees.

The codebase already names the cure: `taa_resolve_frag.glsl:103-106` and
`ssgi_accum_frag.glsl:61-66` both blend with **inverse-luminance (Karis)
weights** — `w = feedback / (1 + luma)` — and the shared accumulator's
header explicitly records that SSGI kept its own program for exactly this
("its YCoCg clamp and inverse-luma firefly blend are real signal
differences"). `run_temporal_accum` already accepts per-consumer programs
(TAA and SSGI pass their own). SSR joins that club.

## Design

### 1. SSR gets its own accumulator program with the inverse-luma blend

New `cetra/shaders/ssr_accum_frag.glsl`: a copy of
`temporal_accum_frag.glsl`'s structure (same uniforms/reset/reprojection
early-outs and the same per-channel RGBA neighborhood clamp) with ONE
change — the final blend becomes the house inverse-luma weighted form on
RGB (luma = Rec.709 dot on the premultiplied radiance; the 1.0 pivot is
diffuse white in working space, same reasoning as taa_resolve/ssgi_accum),
while ALPHA (coverage) keeps the plain `mix` — coverage is not radiance and
must not be luma-warped. Bright single-frame ray hits get downweighted, so
the accumulation converges to a stable mean instead of chasing them.

C side (`cetra/src/postfx.c`): create `fx->ssr_accum_program` beside
`ssgi_accum_program` (program.c factory + postfx create/destroy), and the
SSR call at `postfx.c:1682` passes it instead of
`fx->temporal_accum_program`. The shared accumulator shader and the
`run_temporal_accum` driver are untouched; the other five consumers cannot
be affected.

### 2. Bound the seed (the 10.6-recorded backport)

`postfx.c:1650`: `int ssr_seed = ssr_temporal_on ? fx->frame_index % 4096 : 0;`
with the PCSS rationale comment (float-hash conditioning over long
sessions — `render.c:699-709` is the pattern). This is what degrades live
sessions beyond what any short headless test shows.

### 3. Tidy-up rider (also 10.6-recorded): ssr_frag's inline IGN

Migrate the two inline IGN copies in `ssr_frag.glsl` (:128-143 hash pair,
:285-286) to `#include "noise.glsl"` where they compute the same function
— provable no-op. (The :128 pair computes TWO decorrelated values; keep the
second-channel construction local, express the base hash via `ign()`.)

## Deliberately NOT in scope

- The shared accumulator, its clamp space, and the other five consumers.
- The composite (tent shader is source-shared with bloom — not touched).
- The TAA-off frozen-noise pattern: with no accumulator the seed freezes by
  design; a-trous handles it spatially. Unchanged behavior.
- YCoCg neighborhood clamping for SSR (SSGI-style): the recorded escalation
  lever IF the weighted blend's measured churn drop is insufficient —
  decided by numbers, not taken preemptively.
- `WS_REFLECT_MAX`, feedback constants, SSR march parameters.

## Verification

1. **Golden-neutral by construction, then proven:** every committed golden
   renders headless without `--taa`, where `ssr_temporal_on` is false and
   the new accumulator never executes; the seed bound is also inside that
   gate (`% 4096` of a frozen 0 is 0). Re-verify: sheen_fixture golden,
   aerial_fixture golden, froxel_fog golden 0 px; the no-TAA determinism
   set (cornell_box, cornell_leak, contact, raiden) 0 px vs the current
   branch build.
2. **The churn harness from the diagnosis** (same camera, same
   frame-120-vs-122 metric): full config must drop substantially from
   242,818 px toward the 110,894 no-sheen floor; record before/after in
   the spec. Also re-measure master-content parity is unaffected.
3. **Gates:** full suite. The catcher churn gate (the only SSR-live gate)
   is EXPECTED to drop on both its shadowed and no-shadow-floor runs;
   record the new numbers. All positional gates byte-identical.
4. **Interactive:** the user's judgment at their close-up, TAA on.
5. Reviews (thermo-nuclear + /simplify) as background agents, wait for ALL.
