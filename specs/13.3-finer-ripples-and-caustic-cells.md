# Spec 13.3 — Finer ripples and caustic cells

## Context

13.2 shipped the refracted-grid caustics, and up close their cells read as squares. The lattice cell is 9.375 cm because it sits on the short band's texel (12 m / 128). The water surface has nothing finer than that texel either, so a finer lattice alone would only refract smooth interpolation.

Clearwater hides the facets with 1.8 cm cells. The fix is both halves, as 13.0's caustics row and 13.2's as-built already book it:
- **A ripple band.** A fourth spectral band in its own 512² texture, so the surface carries centimetre ripples.
- **A fine caustic level.** A second, finer lattice near the camera that traces through them.

A coarser grid is off the table. This spec only adds detail.

## Design

### Ripple band (spectral water only, like the short band it extends)

- **Size: 6 m at 512², giving a 1.171875 cm texel.**
  - That is exactly 1/8 of the short band's texel, so every short-band texel is a whole number of ripple texels, and the pattern stays commensurate with a 12 m combined period.
  - The Nyquist wavenumber is about 268 rad/m.
- **k window `[24, 260]`.** The short band keeps `[1.22, 24]` unchanged, so bands 0-2 are byte-identical and no energy is counted twice. The existing `short_fade = exp(-0.00016 k²)` decides how much energy survives up there. It stays as it is, because it is the spectrum's own small-wave cutoff.
- **Capillary dispersion.** `ω² = g·k·(1 + (k/370)²)`, applied to every band. It is about 0.2% at k = 24, so bands 0-2 move only by that much. Without it, ripples at k = 200 travel about 20% too slow.
- **Storage:** its own `RGBA16F` array (2 layers × ping-pong), mipped and REPEAT, plus its own 512×9 twiddle table. Sampled on free unit **4** (`WATER_RIPPLE_UNIT`), bound in `_water_bind_ocean`, since every program that includes `ocean.glsl` goes through it. `water_frag` goes to 13 of 16. There is no previous-frame copy (motion vectors stay bands 0 and 1) and no foam (bands 0 and 1).
- **FFT:** `_water_fft_transform` takes `(size, log, twiddle)` instead of the compile-time constants. `_water_build_twiddle` and `_water_build_spectrum` already take a size. The ripple band adds 1 + 18 = 19 passes, so 45 becomes 64. Its viewport is set per band, and the 128 viewport is restored before the foam pass.
- **Shading:**
  - `oceanShadingNormal` gains a footprint argument. The ripple slope is sampled at a LOD derived from the footprint, via a per-band-resolution version of `oceanCascadeLod`.
  - Its unresolved slope variance goes to roughness through `oceanRemovedMss`, the path the short band's view fade already uses. So distant water gets rougher glitter rather than aliasing.
  - `water_frag` passes the pixel footprint. The caustic land pass passes its cell, so the coarse lattice correctly sees the ripples filtered away.
- **Gerstner:** stays ripple-free, because `oceanShadingNormal` returns the mesh normal there, as it does today.

### Fine caustic level (both wave models)

- **Everything scaled by 1/4:**
  - cell 2.34375 cm, which is 2 ripple texels
  - G = 512, a 12 m lattice
  - target 1024² over the inner 10 m
  - snap 5 cells = 12 texels, the same invariant as the coarse level
- **Placement:** the fine window is centred ahead of the camera, like the coarse one, with the reach clamped to its own size.
- **Levels as data:**
  - `water_caustic_constants.glsl` becomes a coarse row plus `WATER_CAUSTIC_FINE_DIV 4`.
  - The vert shader's `GRID_N` and `TARGET_M` become uniforms.
  - `_water_run_caustics` loops over levels, reusing the one 513² land target per level in turn.
  - Both targets live in one `sampler2DArray` on unit 3, with no new unit.
- **Consumer:** `waterCaustics` samples both layers in uniform flow and computes `c = mix(coarse, fine, fineEdgeWeight)`. Both levels average 1, so the blend conserves energy.
- **Probe:** prints one line per level (`level=0`/`level=1`). `_water_caustic_probe` takes a level, and `water-caustic-energy` checks both levels on every leg.

- `cetra/src/water.c`, `cetra/src/water.h`: ripple config, textures, FFT parameterisation, unit define, caustic levels, probes
- `cetra/shaders/include/ocean.glsl`: ripple sampler/uniforms, per-band resolution LOD, `oceanShadingNormal(…, footprint)`
- `cetra/shaders/water_spectrum_frag.glsl` (or the CPU `wave_data`, wherever ω lives): capillary term
- `cetra/shaders/water_frag.glsl`: footprint to normal, ripple removed MSS, two-level caustic lookup
- `cetra/shaders/include/water_caustic_constants.glsl`, `water_caustic_vert.glsl`, `water_caustic_land_frag.glsl`
- `scripts/gates.py`: `water-fft-var` (four bands), `_water_caustic_probe(level)`, `water-caustic-energy` levels
- Docs as in Phase 4

**Reused:** `_water_build_spectrum`, `_water_build_twiddle`, `_water_make_field_array`, `_water_attach_colour`, `oceanCascadeLod`/`oceanRemovedMss`, `_water_pass_begin/_end`, and the whole caustic shader chain, which is scale-invariant.

## Risks

- **Cost:** about +19 FFT passes at 512² plus a second 262k-cell lattice. That could roughly double the 1.9 ms caustic cost. It is measured and reported, not reduced.
- **Visible 6 m tiling** of the ripple texture up close. Look for it in phase 2.
- **FFT gates** move slightly from capillary dispersion, and from new roughness on FFT water.
- **Every include of `ocean.glsl`** must bind unit 4, or it samples the cascade array through a 2D sampler. Binding in `_water_bind_ocean` is what covers all of them.
