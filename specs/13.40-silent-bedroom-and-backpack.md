# Spec 13.40: silent's bedroom, a backpack and a flashlight

## Context

The user asked for silent's home to gain a first goal.

**The request:**
- "build the bedroom with a bed, nightstands on both sides and a dresser on the wall across from the bed".
- A backpack lying on the bed, with a flashlight inside it.
- Tab opens a menu showing the backpack's contents on the left. Clicking an item shows its model rotating on the right.
- If the player goes down to the basement without the flashlight, they may open the door and go down, but are then told something like "it's too dark, I can't see, I need to find a flashlight".

**Today:**
- The flashlight is free from the start: F toggles it.
- No bed exists anywhere in the code.
- The only player-facing text is the door prompt (`prompt.c`).
- Tab is `release_cursor`.

**Decided with the user:**
- **The bedroom is the shut back bedroom across the hall from the basement door.**
  - Its door is in the hall's east wall, at z 17.6–18.4.
  - The room is 4.86 × 2.76 m clear, with walls, a floor and two dark-glazed windows already built, and nothing else.
  - The door literally to the basement door's right is the stair door, whose room is 3.4 × 1.6 m, too small.
- **At the foot of the cellar stair, without the flashlight, the player is held.** A thought line shows, and an invisible wall in the opening into the basement keeps them on the stair until they have the flashlight.
  - The bulb over the stair stays lit as spec 13.31 built it.
  - Past the foot, the basement is already dark apart from what the flashlight lights.

**Decided here:**
- Both models are built with silent's kit, from lathes, pipes and boxes, as its own nodes. The kit is what the house is made of, and it gives the grime and the scans.
- The rotating model is drawn by a small offscreen renderer of silent's own, inside the menu. The engine has no second view, and a model placed in the world would be fogged, darkened by the night exposure and clipped by walls.
- The world keeps running behind the menu; the player stands still.

## Phase 1: the bedroom

### Opening the room (`home.c`, `home.h`)

**The door**
- Replace `door_shut` on `HW_HALL_E_BEDROOM` with a hung door: `home_bedroom_door()`, on `home_bath_door`'s pattern.
- It hangs on its back jamb (18.4) with yaw π/2, so it swings into the room and stands open along +x at z ≈ 18.4.
- `DOOR_BEDROOM` is added to `silent.c`'s door enum and hanging (`silent.c:234`, `:775`).
- No sound link: the bedroom stays inside the `home` audio zone, as the bathroom does.

**The finishes**
- The two windows go from `MAT_DARK_GLASS` to `MAT_WINDOW_GLASS` in `GLAZING`: back wall index 2, east wall index 1.
- `finishes()` gains a `bedroom[]` side list, so the room gets a lining, skirting, crown, and the door's casing on its side. The lining is chosen by eye: paper or cream.
- `BEDROOM_AT_X/Z` sit beside `BATH_AT_*`.

**Comments:** the home plan (`home.c:20-40`, `home.h:13-21`) says the back bedroom is open and only the stair is shut.

### Furnishing it (`bedroom.c/h`, new, called from `home_build`)

- **A new file.** `home.c` is the shell and is already 1019 lines; `basement.c` set this precedent.
- **The bed**
  - A double, 1.4 × 2.0 m, its headboard against the east wall, centred z ≈ 18.03.
  - The headboard top stays under the east window's sill (0.85 m above the floor).
  - It has a wooden frame (headboard, low footboard, rails, legs), a mattress, a sheet, a quilt turned back, and two pillows.
- **The nightstands:** one each side against the east wall, at z ≈ 16.7–17.2 and 18.85–19.35, each a carcass with a drawer and a knob (`study.c`'s desk drawers as reference).
  - On the front one, a bedside lamp: a lathe base and a `MAT_LAMPSHADE` shade, with a warm cached point light (`floor_lamp`'s pattern, about 25 cd, range 5).
  - That lamp is how the room, and the bag, are seen without a flashlight.
  - On the other, small things: a book and a glass.
- **The dresser**
  - It stands on the hall wall, facing the bed.
  - It sits in the stretch the open door leaves clear, z 16.65–17.52: about 0.85 × 0.48 m and 1.05 m tall, with four drawers and knobs.
  - On it: a framed photo card from `make_cards.py`'s atlas if one fits, otherwise a plain frame.
- **A rug** beside the bed.
- **Colliders:** one `KIT_COLLIDER_ONLY` box per piece.

### Lighting
- A twelfth reflection-probe room for the bedroom, on the bathroom's pattern (`silent.c:486`).
  - Its box runs to the door's wall centre line and the panes' planes: x `HALL_X1`..`HOUSE_X1-KIT_PANE_HALF`, z `HOME_SPLIT_Z`..`HOUSE_BACK_Z-KIT_PANE_HALF`.
  - `PROBE_ROOM_COUNT` goes 11 → 12, against a cap of 16.
- The home's GI grid already covers the room.

### Materials (`mats.c/h`)
- Up to four new slots: `MAT_BEDDING` (pale cotton), `MAT_QUILT`, `MAT_CANVAS` (the bag) and `MAT_ANODISED` (the flashlight). That makes 94 of `KIT_MAX_MATERIALS` 96; raise the cap if a fifth is needed.
- New CC0 fabric scans are fetched through `apps/silent/tools/fetch_textures.py`, which records where each came from.

## Phase 2: the backpack, the flashlight, the HUD and the pickup

### The models (`backpack.c/h`, new)
Each is a kit of its own, built round its own origin (`clock.c`'s `part()` pattern).

**The backpack**
- A canvas daypack lying on its back on the quilt, near the foot of the bed.
- The body is a rounded box, from `kitchen.c`'s four-sided-lathe cushion.
- On it: a front pocket, a flap (`kit_frame_extrude`), shoulder straps and a haul loop (`kit_frame_pipe`), buckles, and a zip line.
- It is `capture_hidden`, so no capture keeps it once it is taken.

**The flashlight**
- A metal D-cell flashlight along its own axis (`kit_frame_lathe_on`): a tail cap, a ribbed barrel, a head flaring to the bezel, a reflector, a lens, and a rubber switch.
- It is `casts_nothing`.
- It is never in the world, since it is inside the bag. `kit_finish` attaches nodes to the root, so the node is taken off the root once built. It is kept only for the menu, and freed at shutdown.

### The pickup
- `door_reach_distance`'s test (distance plus view cone) is factored into one point test, so the bag is reached exactly as a door is.
- The interaction in `on_pre_render` (`silent.c:1020-1037`) takes the nearest of the doors and the bag. The prompt reads "E   Take the backpack".
- **Taking it:**
  - the node comes off the bed (`node_remove_child`);
  - the backpack holds the flashlight;
  - F works from then on;
  - the thought line reads "My backpack. There's a flashlight in it.";
  - for a few seconds the prompt line names "Tab   Backpack".
- **F before then** reads the thought "I don't have a flashlight.", and the light stays off.
- **`--flashlight`** (start with it on) implies the backpack has been taken.
- **New flag `--backpack`:** start with it, for testing.

### The HUD (`prompt.c/h` → `hud.c/h`)
- **Why it is restructured:** the engine has one overlay hook, `ui_attach` takes it, and `prompt_show(NULL)` calls `ui_pop_all`, which would close any second screen.
- **The new structure:** one `UISystem`.
  - A non-modal HUD screen stays on the stack.
  - It holds the door prompt and the THOUGHT line, a subtitle above the prompt that fades in and out over a few seconds.
  - Each plate paints only while it has words (`ui_set_draw` on the plate).
  - The backpack screen (Phase 3) is pushed over it.

### Look
- Frames of the bag on the bed, in the lamp's light.
- The flashlight model, through Phase 3's turntable once it exists.

## Phase 3: the backpack menu

### Input (`silent.c`'s action table)
- **New UI actions** (`ui = true`, so they read while the game's input is held):

  | Action | Binds |
  |---|---|
  | `backpack` | Tab, pad Back |
  | `menu_back` | Escape, pad B |
  | `menu_up`, `menu_down` | arrows, W/S, D-pad |
  | `menu_accept` | Enter, E, pad A |

- **`release_cursor` moves from Tab to Escape.** It is not a UI action, so it reads zero while the menu holds input. The `player.h` comment and the `--help` text follow.
- **`on_frame_input`:**
  - builds `UIInput` from the mouse (`input_mouse_pos` and the button edges) and those actions, then calls `ui_update`;
  - holds the game's input with `input_set_suppressed` while the menu or the loading screen is up (gametest's `on_frame_input` is the model).
- **Tab:**
  - opens the menu once the backpack has been taken, and closes it;
  - before then, it reads the thought "I'm not carrying anything.".
- **The cursor:** while the menu is open it is free, and closing it captures the cursor again if it was captured before. `player.c` gains one call for that; its `set_cursor_captured` is static today.

### The screen (`backpack.c` or `hud.c`)
- A modal screen that fades in fast, over a dim backdrop (`fill`) on the live world.
- **Left:** a column headed "BACKPACK", with one button per item: "Flashlight".
- **Right:**
  - empty until an item is clicked or accepted;
  - then the item's model turning slowly, its name, and a line about it ("A heavy metal flashlight. F turns it on and off.").
- **At the foot:** "Tab   Close".
- **New flag `--open-backpack ITEM`:** opens the menu at the start with ITEM selected, so a headless run can photograph it (with `--backpack`).

### The turntable (`item_view.c/h` and `apps/silent/shaders/item_view_*.glsl`, new)
- **Where it runs:** drawn from the preview element's `ui_set_draw` callback, which runs in the overlay before the UI's own draw list.
- **The target:**
  - its own FBO (RGBA8 colour plus depth, MSAA resolved), sized to the element's rect × the framebuffer's scale;
  - rebuilt when that changes;
  - shown with `ui_draw_textured_quad` through a `Texture` wrapping its colour id (the UI reads only `id`).
- **The drawing:**
  - the item node's meshes through their own VAOs, with a program from `create_program_from_source` over the embedded sources (`cetra_embed_shaders`, already wired for silent);
  - albedo texture × albedo × vertex colour (the grime), plus roughness and metallic;
  - a key, a fill and a rim light;
  - framed from the node's AABB and turned about its long axis's perpendicular;
  - a transparent background;
  - written display-encoded, since the UI shows it as it is.
- **GL state:** it saves and restores what it touches: the framebuffer, the viewport, depth, cull, blend, the program, the VAO and the texture bindings. Under `--crt` the overlay draws into PostFX's picture, which the framebuffer restore covers.
- **The spin** follows the sim clock's delta, so a headless capture is frame-exact.

- `--backpack --open-backpack flashlight` headless, at two frames, so the turn shows.
- The user walks it windowed: Tab, click, Escape.

## Phase 4: held at the foot of the cellar stair

- **`basement.c/h`** gains:
  - `basement_at_foot(const vec3 feet)`: the flight's foot, x < `CELLAR_FOOT_X` plus a little, z in the stairwell, feet below `BASEMENT_Y` + 0.3. The flight's constants are private to `basement.c`.
  - A barrier: an entity with a static box body filling the way into the basement through the stairwell's partition at the foot.
    - The opening is x `CELLAR_X0`..`CELLAR_FOOT_X`, z = `STAIRWELL_WALL_Z`, from `BASEMENT_Y` to the opening's head.
    - It is made at start unless the flashlight is already held.
    - It is taken away (`entity_remove_rigid_body`) when the flashlight is.
- **`silent.c`'s fixed step:** at the foot without the flashlight, the thought reads "It's too dark. I can't see a thing down there. I need to find a flashlight." It shows once per visit, and leaving the foot re-arms it.
- **New collider:** the barrier. The user walks it; no scripted pad walks.
