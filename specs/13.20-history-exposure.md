# Spec 13.20 -- histories that follow the exposure

## Context
In silent by day, stepping from the kitchen into the street settles to the new exposure as an
"expanding sphere". Near pixels change at once; distant, foggy pixels lag; the settled region grows
outward over about a second. The user reported it during 13.19, and it was booked there.

**Cause (confirmed by reading the code):** the engine renders pre-exposed, and no temporal history
is rescaled when the exposure changes.
- **The worst case is the froxel fog volume.** It stores its in-scatter as `S * preExposure`
  (`froxel_inject_frag.glsl:433`) and blends 90% history with no neighbourhood clamp (`:454-455`),
  so it carries the old exposure for tens of frames.
- **The fog's share of a pixel rises with distance.** That is why the lag reads as a sphere.
- **Six more histories hold pre-exposed radiance and are uncorrected:** TAA, TAAU, SSGI, SSR, the
  composited fog layer and SSS. Each clamps to the current 3x3 neighbourhood, which hides most of
  the error, but not at edges or in texture.
- **The meter reads the post-TAA, fogged frame,** so the lag also feeds back into the adaptation.
- **Already immune:** the cloud march, the GI volume and the probes store absolute radiance and
  expose at composite. AO, spec-occ and contact shadows are unitless.

**Fix:** Unreal's pre-exposure correction. A history remembers the exposure it was written at. On
read, its radiance (`.rgb` only, never coverage, transmittance or sigma) is multiplied by this
frame's pre-exposure over that one. The whole change of exposure then lands in one frame.

## Design
- **One statement of "the exposure the scene was shaded with":** `exposure_pre(const Exposure*)`
  in `exposure.c/h`. It is `exposure_multiplier()`, or 1 where that is not a positive number,
  which is the guard now written inline at `render.c:1440-1442`. `render.c` uses it and keeps its
  capture override to 1. `postfx_run` reads it once at its top into `fx->pre_exposure`. That is
  the same value, because the meter submits later in the same call (`postfx.c:3815`).
- **Each history records its own exposure, not a global "last frame":**
  - `PingPong` gains `float pre_exposure`, written whenever the history is written.
  - The fog volume keeps `froxel_prev_pre` beside `froxel_prev_view` (`postfx.c:2803-2805`).
  - A history skipped for a frame, a capture frame, or a second render call cannot then pair
    content with the wrong ratio.
- **`run_temporal_accum`** (`postfx.c:2422`) takes a `bool radiance`. It sets
  `uniform float historyScale`: `pre / pp->pre_exposure` for radiance, 1 otherwise, and 1 when the
  history is invalid or the switch is off. It covers four programs:
  - `taa_resolve_frag`, `ssgi_accum_frag` and `ssr_accum_frag` take `radiance = true`;
  - `temporal_accum_frag` takes `true` for the fog layer and SSS, and `false` for AO, spec-occ and
    contact shadows.
- **Each of those shaders, and `taau_resolve_frag`,** multiplies `history.rgb` by `historyScale`
  right after the fetch, BEFORE its neighbourhood clamp and its luma weights. Those then compare
  like with like.
- **`run_taau_resolve`** (`postfx.c:2463`) sets the same uniform from `taa_history`.
- **The fog volume:** `postfx_build_fog_volume` sets `historyScale` on the inject program, and
  `froxel_inject_frag` scales `history.rgb` before the 0.9 mix. The integrate pass, the composite,
  rain and fire all read the corrected volume with no change of their own.
- **A switch for A/B and the falsifier:** `PostFX.rescale_histories` (SETTINGS, default true), and
  the render flag `--no-history-rescale`. It is a diagnostic, not a GUI control, so it needs no
  snapshot row.
- **An instrument:** the render flag `--exposure-at <frame>:<multiplier>`. It joins
  `render_frame_update`'s schedule (`render.c:2658+`, the `--shadows-off-at` idiom) and writes
  `engine->exposure.multiplier`, the field `-E` sets (`render.c:3447`). With the meter off, that
  is an exact one-frame step.

## Phases
Each phase that changes the picture ends with a look. Update the spec and commit after each phase,
and announce each phase boundary.

1. **Instrument and baseline.**
   - Add `--exposure-at`.
   - On a foggy scene (`froxel_fog` golden's recipe), step the exposure 4x at frame N.
   - Capture frames N..N+30 (`--screenshot-every`) as a strip, together with a reference that had
     the new exposure from frame 0.
   - Render one configuration twice for the floor.
   - Show the strip in Preview: the lag, headless, before any fix.
2. **Fog volume, plus `exposure_pre` and the switch.** Then look 1:
   - the same strip with the fix and with `--no-history-rescale`;
   - the frame after the step against the reference;
   - the windowed run command for the user to walk silent `--day` out of the kitchen.
3. **The other six histories** through `run_temporal_accum` and TAAU. Then look 2:
   - the step strip with `--taa --headless-jitter --ssgi` on a glossy, foggy scene;
   - once more under `--render-scale 0.67` for TAAU;
   - the user walks silent again.
4. **Gates** (after sign-off). A new group, `exposure-history`. Each step arm compares the first
   frame at the new exposure against the always-new-exposure reference, and carries its own
   falsifier: `--no-history-rescale` must exceed a stated pixel count on the same frame.
   - `history-fog`: no TAA.
   - `history-taa`: TAA + SSGI + SSR + the fog layer.
   - `history-taau`: `--render-scale 0.67`.
   - `history-sss`: `skin_curvature_sss` with TAA.
   - `history-steady`: at a constant exposure, on against `--no-history-rescale` is 0 px. The
     correction must do nothing when nothing changes.
   - Falsify each arm by hand and record how in the docstring.
   - **Goldens:** pinned exposure means a ratio of exactly 1, but the new multiply may move fog
     codegen. Show any drift and re-bake if it is 1 code.
   - **Scoped runs:** `--only exposure-history,exposure,scale,local-exposure,gate-docs`, plus the
     TAA, SSR, SSGI and fog groups that `run_temporal_accum` reaches (names settled from
     `gates.py`).
5. **Docs.**
   - `AGENTS.md`: the Temporal accumulation pattern (radiance histories carry their exposure), and
     a line under the exposure and atmosphere entries.
   - `docs/shader-subsystems.md`: Tonemap / exposure and Atmosphere.
   - `docs/cli-reference.md`: the two flags.
   - The spec's as-built notes.

## Reused
- `run_temporal_accum`, the one entry point for four of the histories.
- `PingPong.valid` and the fog's adjacency stamp, to decide when a history is used at all.
- `render_frame_update`'s scheduled-flag idiom.
- `--headless-jitter`, for deterministic TAA.
- The gate helpers `compare`, `_read_ppm` and `cscn_copy`, and the self-falsifying arm pattern of
  `le-halo` and `glare-scene`.

## Risks
- **Clamps break exact equivalence.** `WS_MEDIA_MAX` and `WS_SCENE_MAX` apply at write time, so a
  history clamped at the old exposure, rescaled, is not what the new exposure would have stored.
  Pick the fixture's step away from both ceilings.
- **fp16 rounding.** The arms' tolerance must come from the measured floor and a test render, not
  from zero.
- **Codegen drift** on fog goldens from a multiply by a uniform that is exactly 1.
- **The meter's response may change** slightly now that it reads corrected histories. That is why
  `--only exposure` runs.
