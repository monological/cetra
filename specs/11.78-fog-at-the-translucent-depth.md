# Spec 11.78 — fog at the translucent depth

## Context

`docs/aaa-rendering-roadmap.md:3046` records an unbooked limitation: fog and aerial
perspective land on a translucent pixel at the depth of the wall BEHIND it. The atmosphere
composite reads one linear-Z per pixel (`froxel_composite_frag.glsl:52`, from the aux
G-buffer), and the late pass writes no aux — `render.c:1342` drops `glDrawBuffers` to
count 1 when the opaque scope closes, and nothing re-arms it. A smoke card 2 m from the eye
in front of a wall at 40 m receives 40 m of fog.

Water already solves this exact bug for itself by writing aux (`water_frag.glsl:1233`), and
its comment says so in as many words. Translucent stacks cannot take that route: aux is
single-layer, so writing the glass would un-fog the wall.

**The fix:** decompose the pixel in the atmosphere composite using the MBOIT moments — which
already store, per pixel, the translucent stack's total absorbance `b0` and its
absorbance-weighted mean warped depth `b1/b0` — and fog the translucent contribution at its
own depth, the background at the aux depth. All work happens in the post chain, where there
is no sampler-ledger pressure.

### Why not the in-shader route (investigated first, rejected on three verified facts)

1. `froxel_composite_frag.glsl:20-21` samples **two** `sampler3D` volumes (fog + aerial),
   separately owned, differently parameterised, non-mergeable without a resample pre-pass.
   `pbr_frag` is at 16/16 declarations and no tenancy escape applies to a 3D type. D0's only
   viable candidate (irradiance→atlas fold) frees ONE unit and is itself blocked on
   ownership: 11.70 made unit 14 a two-tenant texture owned by *opt-in* subsystems, and
   always-present IBL is exclusive with neither — a three-way arbitration concept, not a
   sampler swap. D0's own 0-px gate "cannot pass" per its correction 3.
2. **Both volumes are built AFTER the scene pass** (`engine.c:2674` `render()`, then `:2686`
   `engine_present_frame` → `sky_update_aerial` at `:1810` and `postfx_build_fog_volume` at
   `postfx.c:2755`). An in-shader read samples frame N−1, undefined on frame 0.
3. The composite applies `pixel × T + S` to the already-blended pixel (`GL_ONE, GL_SRC_ALPHA`,
   `postfx.c:2823`), so in-shader-fogged glass would be fogged again at the wall's depth —
   correct compositing needs the same moments decomposition this plan uses anyway. The
   in-shader route strictly contains this one.

## Mechanism

`froxel_composite_frag.glsl` currently outputs one `(S, T)` pair per pixel
(`FragColor = vec4(fogLayer.rgb + aerialLayer.rgb * preExposure * fogLayer.a,
fogLayer.a * aerialLayer.a)`, line 89) and the caller's blend applies it. The change:
evaluate that same fold at TWO depths and blend the pairs by translucent coverage.

```glsl
// existing: (S,T) at zOpaque from linDepthTex .z          — unchanged arithmetic
// new:      (S,T) at zTrans, where
//   b0   = texture(momentTex, uvAtlas + vec2(0.0, 0.5)).r  // upper half of atlas
//   b1   = texture(momentTex, uvAtlas).r                    // lower half, .r = b1
//   zW   = b1 / b0                                          // mean warped depth
//   zTrans = oitNearFar.x * pow(oitNearFar.y / oitNearFar.x, (zW + 1.0) * 0.5)
//   cover  = 1.0 - exp(-b0)                                 // == 1 - stack transmittance
// out    = mix(pairOpaque, pairTrans, cover)
```

Key facts, all verified against the code before writing this:

- The moment atlas (`engine->moment_atlas_texture`, `engine.c:2139-2151`) is render-res width
  × **2× height**, RGBA32F, NEAREST: b1..b4 lower half, b0 upper at `+vec2(0, 0.5)`
  (`mboit.glsl:131-135`, `MBOIT_ATLAS_B0_TAP`). Engine-owned, resolved during the scene pass
  by `engine_end_moment_pass`, persists — valid at post time.
- `b0` alone yields both coverage and depth; **no revealage read needed**. One new
  `sampler2D` in a post program that declares four. Zero ledger pressure.
- The warp interval is the camera's near/far (`render.c:500-502` uploads
  `{camera->near_clip, camera->far_clip}` as `oitNearFar`); the unwarp inverts
  `mboitWarpDepth` (`mboit.glsl:31-35`).
- `cover == 0` ⇒ `mix(a, b, 0) == a` exactly ⇒ **bit-identical to today wherever no
  translucent drew** (b0 is 0 there). Gated off entirely (`momentArmed == 0`) the shader takes
  today's expression verbatim.
- `mboitWarpDepth` clamps `viewZ` below `nearFar.x`, so `zW ∈ [-1,1]` and the unwarp is
  bounded; guard `b0 < ~1e-5` to avoid 0/0.

## Changes

**`cetra/shaders/froxel_composite_frag.glsl`** — add `uniform sampler2D momentTex`,
`uniform vec2 oitNearFar`, `uniform int momentArmed`; factor the existing two
`froxelSampleMedium` calls + fold into a small function of `viewZ`, call at `-linZ` (as today)
and at `zTrans`, mix by `cover`. When `momentArmed == 0`, single call — today's path, same
expression order.

**`cetra/src/postfx.h`** — `PostFXGBufferWrites` (at `:737`, already carries `oit_fbo` and
`oit_moment_weighted`) gains `GLuint oit_moment_atlas;` (0 = not this frame) and
`float oit_near, oit_far;`. The struct's comment already frames it as "the engine's
frame-start decisions, passed through rather than re-derived" — this is more of exactly that.
Passed through rather than re-derived from `projection` because `camera.near_clip` is
recomputed per frame by render.c (the config-snapshot lesson).

**`cetra/src/engine.c:1830`** — fill the new fields at the `postfx_run` call site:
`engine->moments_this_frame ? engine->moment_atlas_texture : 0`, camera near/far.

**`cetra/src/postfx.c`** — `postfx_run_atmosphere` (`:2733`): bind the atlas on the next free
unit after `aerialVolume`'s 3, upload `oitNearFar` + `momentArmed`; program setup at
`:948-952` gains the sampler unit assignment. Plumb the three fields from `writes`.

**`tools/gen_fog_glass_fixture.py` + `assets/fog_glass_fixture.cscn/.gltf`** — the instrument
nothing in the corpus is (verified: no fixture combines translucency with fog, DoF or AO —
`run_oit_gate` and `run_fog_volume_gate` both pass `--no-ssao`, the fog/DoF/AO goldens are
all-opaque, `absorption_fixture`'s ratio arms cancel AO out). Geometry: a distant emissive
backdrop; an OPAQUE pane at a near depth; beside it, byte-identical geometry as ALPHA_BLEND
with alpha 1.0 (enters OIT; `mboitAbsorbance` clamps a to 0.9999 so cover ≈ 1 and zTrans = the
pane's own depth); a third pane at alpha 0.5. Emissive backdrop so every read is exposure- and
lighting-independent (the `lut_fixture` reasoning). Fog via `--fog`. **60 frames, not 30** —
the froxel accumulator's documented settling (`fog_volume_fixture` lesson).

**`scripts/gates.py`** — a `fogdepth` group, three arms, falsifying mutations named in the
docstring:

| arm | asserts | falsified by |
|---|---|---|
| `fogdepth-twin` | the alpha-1.0 BLEND pane and the OPAQUE twin read equal fog (luma delta below a bar calibrated from both sides) | one line: `zTrans = -linZ` — the blend pane fogs at the backdrop's depth again; this arm READS THE DEFECT on today's renderer, which is the measurement that sets the bar |
| `fogdepth-partial` | the alpha-0.5 pane's fog lies strictly between the twin's and the bare backdrop's (monotone in coverage) | `cover = step(0.5, cover)` — collapses the ordering |
| `fogdepth-identity` | an all-opaque fog scene (`froxel_fog`'s own fixture) is 0 px with the feature armed vs `--no-oit` | `cover = 0.01` constant — contaminates opaque pixels |

Plus `gate-docs` registration.

**Docs** — rewrite the roadmap `:3046` paragraph against what was verified here: the stale
`render.c:975`, the wrong mechanism (draw-buffer list, not the depth mask), DoF being a
different buffer (`dof_coc_frag.glsl:10` reads resolved hardware depth — NOT fixed here, say
so), aerial not being a separate consumer, and the fuller consumer list (TAA, motion blur,
contact shadows, the denoisers). Record what this spec fixes (fog + aerial on OIT
translucents) and what it deliberately leaves: AO/TAA/motion-blur/DoF on glass (aux is
single-layer; largely inherent to forward rendering), transmissive surfaces (excluded from OIT
by construction — and mostly correct today, since light seen THROUGH glass genuinely travelled
from the wall; only the surface term is misfogged), particles (own pass, no moments), and the
`--no-oit` / `--no-oit-moments` paths (keep today's behaviour, gated). AGENTS.md gets the same
in its pipeline notes.

## Predictions, written before implementing

1. **All 27 goldens 0 px, no rebake.** No golden combines OIT geometry with fog or aerial
   (verified recipe by recipe; `oit_sphere_moments` has neither flag, the fog/aerial/water
   goldens are OIT-free). The composite change is gated to exact identity where b0 = 0.
   Caveat: the fog-bearing goldens (`froxel_fog`, aerial trio, `water_submerged`) recompile a
   changed shader — 11.28-style codegen movement at PAE 1/255 is the one acceptable deviation,
   anything more is a wrong diagnosis, stop.
2. `fogdepth-twin` on the UNFIXED renderer reads a large luma delta (the defect, quantified);
   with the fix, near zero. Both numbers go in the as-built.
3. `water`, `fog-volume`, `cloud-shadow`, `absorption`, `oit` groups green unchanged.
4. Perf: +2 texture taps and one mix in a fullscreen pass, only when fog/aerial runs — below
   the profiler's floor; not worth a measurement beyond noting it.
