# 10.8 — KHR_materials_specular conformance: f90, achromatic energy trade, clamp order

## Context

The specular fixture exposed three verified deviations from the
KHR_materials_specular spec (all confirmed against the spec text verbatim;
the fixture's `spec_dim` sphere shows the first one on screen — its grazing
rim renders at full strength instead of 10%):

1. **f90 is not scaled.** Spec: `dielectric_f90 = specular` (the factor
   scales the grazing response as well as f0). Cetra folds the factor into
   F0 only (`pbr_frag.glsl:998-1000`) and both Fresnel helpers hardcode
   f90 = 1, so a dim-specular material still reflects fully at grazing.
2. **The diffuse energy trade is per-channel.** Spec: the base is weighted
   by `(1 - weight * max_value(fr))` — an ACHROMATIC complement, chosen so
   a colored specular does not hue-shift the base. Cetra subtracts colored
   Fresnel per channel (`kD = 1 - F`), producing the teal-under-gold /
   olive-under-blue bodies on the fixture.
3. **Clamp order.** Spec clamps before the factor
   (`min(0.04*specColor, 1) * specular`); Cetra clamps after.

Recorded v1 history that CONSTRAINS the design (docs/rendering-roadmap.md
:543-557): a direct specular-term weight was tried and REVERTED because it
reassociated the base IBL float grouping and broke the cmp gate — the
clearcoat B3 compiler-reassociation lesson. Therefore every 10.8 change
must keep the default path's expression trees byte-identical, doing all
new math inside the existing extension sentinel
(`specularEnabled > 0 && specularFactor >= 0.0`) as guarded overwrites —
the pattern the iridescence branch already ships (pbr_frag.glsl:1229-1237
replaces F wholesale under its own condition without disturbing anyone
else).

Coverage facts (verified): only `assets/specular_fixture.gltf` carries the
extension — no gate, no golden, no other scene (raiden has no
KHR_materials_* at all; FBX imports cannot populate the key). So
guard-confined changes are 0-px everywhere by construction, and the proofs
make that measured.

## Design

All shader work in `pbr_frag.glsl`, inside one hoisted guarded block plus
guarded overwrites at the consumer sites. First step of implementation:
transcribe the spec's `dielectric_brdf` / `fresnel_mix` formulas verbatim
from the KHR_materials_specular README (re-fetch; do NOT trust a paraphrase
— the exact placement of the weight vs the f0/f90 folding must come from
the spec text, the same discipline as 10.7.1's lambdaSheen constants).

- **Hoist**: where F0 is re-parameterized (:998-1000), compute under the
  SAME guard: the corrected F0 (clamp before factor), a
  `vec3 specF90` (= specularFactor per spec; vec3(1) otherwise), and a
  flag/weight for the achromatic trade. Non-extension materials never
  enter the block: F0 keeps today's expression, f90 stays the implicit 1.
- **Analytic loop** (:1226 F, :1247-1253 kD): after the standard
  `F = fresnelSchlick(...)`, a guarded overwrite recomputes F with the
  f90 form (`F0 + (specF90 - F0) * pow5`); kD's guarded overwrite applies
  the spec's achromatic complement (`1 - weight * max_value(fr)`) in place
  of the per-channel subtraction. Ordering: BEFORE the thin-film branch
  (:1229-1237) so iridescence's wholesale F replacement keeps its current
  meaning.
- **Ambient block** (:1351 F, :1354 kD): same pair of guarded overwrites
  (roughness-Fresnel variant: the implicit f90 there is
  `max(1-roughness, F0)`; the spec form scales it by the factor —
  transcribe exactly).
- **IBL split-sum** (:1410): guarded overwrite
  `specular = prefilteredColor * (F * brdf.x + specF90 * brdf.y) * energyComp`
  — brdf.y is the f90=1 lobe, this is the omitted "IBL specular
  weight-dimming" the roadmap records. The default line stays untouched
  for non-extension materials. Fix the stale comment at :1407-1409 that
  claims "no change to this line is needed".
- **LTC area specular** (:1154 `F0*ltcAmp.x + (1-F0)*ltcAmp.y`) and
  **transmission Fresnel trade** (:1484-1486): same guarded f90/achromatic
  treatment — one-line overwrites each, included for consistency so every
  F0 consumer honors the extension the same way.
- **Energy compensation** (:1030) already reads the re-parameterized F0
  and needs no change (record the reasoning: Ess is the f90=1 single-
  scatter albedo; the factor-scaled lobe scales linearly through it).
- `fresnelSchlick`/`fresnelSchlickRoughness` signatures are NOT changed
  (avoids touching every caller's compiled tree); the f90 forms are
  spelled at the guarded sites.

## Not touched

The helpers' bodies, metals (:1001 overwrites F0 after the block, as
today), clearcoat/sheen internals, the fixture and its deliberately
boosted values (it is the A/B instrument), importer/upload plumbing
(already correct), `energyComp`.
