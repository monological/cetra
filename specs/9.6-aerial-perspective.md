# B9 — Aerial perspective

## Context

Roadmap Tier 1 #5 (`docs/aaa-rendering-roadmap.md:281-292`). Distant geometry should fade into the
atmosphere's *own* colour rather than a flat fog tint — the cue that sells outdoor scale. A small
3D volume marches Rayleigh/Mie against the sky's existing transmittance + multiscatter LUTs and is
sampled when compositing the scene.

### The premise needed correcting first

The AAA roadmap calls this *"Effort S — cheap once B1's 3D machinery exists"*. That answers the
wrong question. Aerial perspective was already deferred once, **user-approved**, and machinery cost
was never the reason — `specs/4.7-physically-based-sky.md:12-14`:

> **Aerial perspective is DEFERRED** (user-approved): invisible at studio/prop scene scales
> (<0.1% extinction over 100 m) … the follow-up shape (units→km app knob + transmittance-LUT term).

`docs/rendering-roadmap.md:433-437` repeats it and names the trigger: *"when a world-scale scene
needs it"*. **B1 did not remove that blocker.** Every asset here is a prop shot — the sky-lit
fixture this would naturally use puts the camera **6 m** from the subject
(`assets/contact_fixture.cscn:11`), where the effect is ~0.006% extinction: not subtle, absent.

**Decision (user, this session): build the world-scale fixture too.** So B9 is three things, and
honest effort is **M, not S**. The fixture is also what B2 (volumetric clouds, Tier 1 #7) will
need, so the cost is shared rather than sunk.

### Two roadmap claims that exploration falsified

- *"Owns foundations: none (reuses B1's create_texture_3d + layered volume draw)"* — half wrong.
  `create_texture_3d_float` (`texture.c:129-143`) is generic and reusable. **`draw_volume_slices`
  is not**: it hardcodes `fx->froxel_fbo`, `POSTFX_FROXEL_X/Y/Z`, the uniform name `"sliceIndex"`,
  and reports failure by setting `fx->fog_enabled = false` (`postfx.c:64-79`). A 32³ volume owned
  by the sky cannot use it.
- *"one extra tap in B1's composite"* — understates it. The composite's single
  `GL_ONE, GL_SRC_ALPHA` blend composes exactly **one** `(inscatter, transmittance)` pair per draw,
  and `postfx_run_fog` early-returns when `fog_enabled` is false (`postfx.c:1381`). Aerial is
  atmosphere, not fog; it must work with fog off.

## Design

### Ownership: sky builds, postfx samples

`sky.c/h` owns the aerial volume — it owns the atmosphere math, the constants, and the source LUTs;
PostFX knows nothing about the sky today and should keep it that way (`postfx.h` includes only
glew/stdbool/cglm/program.h). The handle crosses via a new **`sky_publish_to_postfx`**, following
`shadow_publish_to_postfx` / `reflection_probe_publish_to_postfx` verbatim: forward-declare
`struct PostFX` in `sky.h`, copy POD by value, pass GPU textures as raw `GLuint` handles PostFX
never owns, and signal absence with one canonical off state (a zero handle). Called from
`engine.c:1783-1787`, beside the existing two — `fx_scene->sky` is already in scope there.

**New cadence:** unlike every other sky LUT (baked once, or on sun move), this volume is
camera-dependent and must rebuild per frame. At 32³ that is negligible. `sky_update_aerial(sky,
view, projection, units_per_km)` is called from `engine_present_frame` immediately before the
publish, where the camera matrices are already available (they are passed to `postfx_run` at
`engine.c:1791`).

**The slice loop:** add `bake_lut_3d` beside `bake_lut_2d` (`sky.c:115-141`), same shape —
transient local FBO, scratch texture unit 6, caller uploads uniforms before the call — plus
`glFramebufferTextureLayer` per layer. This matches the house pattern: shadow cascades
(`shadow.c`), mask-array layers (`mask_array.c`), cube faces (`ibl.c`) and froxel slices
(`postfx.c`) each own their own layer loop. A fifth is consistent; unifying all five is a separate
refactor.

### The world-scale knob — mandatory, not optional

`include/atmosphere.glsl` is in **kilometres** (`Rg = 6360.0`, `Rt = 6460.0`); the scene is in
arbitrary units; **no conversion exists anywhere**. Without a declared mapping there is no correct
distance to march. Add `float world_units_per_km` to `SkyAtmosphere`, defaulting to **1000** (1
unit = 1 metre, the glTF convention), with `--world-scale <units-per-km>` to override. This is
deliberately honest: at the default, the existing 6 m fixtures show nothing, *correctly*, and the
new fixture is genuinely kilometres across.

Watch the C-side constant mirror at `sky.c:58-75` (`SKY_RG`/`SKY_RT`/`sky_extinction_at`), flagged
from both sides against `atmosphere.glsl:9-12`.

### The shader

New `cetra/shaders/aerial_lut_frag.glsl`, `#include "atmosphere.glsl"`. That include already gives
the medium (`atmosphereAt`), the geometry (`distanceToTop`, `hitsGround`) and — importantly —
`transmittanceLookup(sampler2D lut, …)`, which takes the LUT **as a parameter**, so no uniform-name
coupling. Three things it does *not* give and this shader needs:

- `rayleighPhase` / `miePhase` + `MIE_G`, currently local to `sky_view_frag.glsl:22-43`,
- `multiscatterAt`, local to `sky_view_frag.glsl:26-30` (and it reads a global uniform rather than
  taking a sampler — fix that when promoting),
- the march loop itself.

**Promote all three into `atmosphere.glsl`** rather than copying them, and have `sky_view_frag`
consume the promoted versions. That keeps the "ONE copy — the bake and its consumers must integrate
the same atmosphere" invariant the include's header states, and it is a strictly better outcome
than a second divergent copy. Verify `sky_view_frag` is unchanged afterwards (0 px on a sky scene).

Depth slicing reuses `include/froxel.glsl` unchanged — it was written for this
(`froxel.glsl:16-18`: *"a second volume at a different depth — the aerial-perspective LUT — can
reuse this mapping unchanged"*), and its three functions all take `slices` as an argument.

### Where it applies: one composite, two media

Aerial reads the same jittered aux depth as fog, after the TAA resolve — **so it has the identical
silhouette-flicker mechanism spec 9.5.1 just fixed**, and worse: at a km-scale silhouette the depth
flips between hundreds of metres and tens of kilometres, which is a large swing in aerial's output.
It needs the same stabilisation.

That rules out a separate aerial pass (it would need a second accumulator and a second blend), and
argues for combining both media in the existing composite, where the accumulator already sits:

- `froxel_composite_frag.glsl` mode 0 gains an aerial tap and combines the two `(S,T)` pairs
  analytically into the one pair the blend consumes: `T = T_f · T_a`, `S = S_f + S_a · T_f`. Exact
  for two media in series given an assumed order; fog is the nearer medium, so it is applied first
  and aerial attenuates its in-scatter. State that as the approximation it is — the physically
  unified answer is one medium in one volume, which is a future consolidation, not this item.
- The existing TAA accumulator then stabilises the **combined** layer for free. No second
  accumulator, no second blend, and mode 1 (the fold) is untouched.
- Gate restructure so either medium can run alone — replacing the current single early return:
  ```c
  const bool fog_on    = fx->fog_enabled && postfx_ensure_froxel_targets(fx);
  const bool aerial_on = fx->aerial_enabled && fx->aerial_volume != 0;
  if (!aux_written || (!fog_on && !aerial_on)) return;
  if (fog_on) { /* inject; integrate */ }
  ```
  Two named booleans and one guard — not scattered special-casing. The function is then genuinely
  the atmospheric-media pass, so rename it `postfx_run_atmosphere`.

**CLI:** `--aerial[=0]`, defaulting **on when the sky is active** and unavailable otherwise (no sky
⇒ no atmosphere to march), mirroring how `--sun-elevation` implies `--sky`.

### Sky-published fog ambient — last, and smaller than the roadmap implies

The roadmap bundles "sky-published fog colours" into B9. Exploration shows most of it **already
works**: the sun colour flows sky → `Light` → `shadow_publish_to_postfx` → `fog_light_color`
(`sky.c:325-344`, `shadow.c:769`). Only `fog_ambient` has no sky path — an app-set `vec3`
defaulting to `{0.05,0.05,0.05}` (`postfx.c:322`), written by exactly three sites and read once at
`postfx.c:1337`.

And once aerial exists, that flat ambient is largely what aerial computes properly, so publishing a
sky-derived value risks double-counting. Treat this as a **separate, optional final milestone**:
add an "app overrode it" sentinel, publish a CPU-derived ambient from the existing
`sky_sun_transmittance` march (`sky.c:77-107`), and only keep it if it measurably beats leaving the
default alone with aerial on.

## Verification

Per `CLAUDE.md`: complete `./build.sh` every time (never piped through `head`), pin exposure with
`--no-auto-exposure -E 1.0`, and measure the same-build noise floor before trusting any comparison.

1. **Fog golden 0 px, no regeneration** — `assets/froxel_fog_golden.png`. M3 restructures the fog
   composite's gate and renames the function; it must change no fog maths. This is the strongest
   regression gate and it runs at every milestone from M2 on.
2. **`sky_view_frag` 0 px after M1** — promotion is pure code motion; any pixel difference means
   the promoted math diverged.
3. **Aerial-off 0 px** — the feature is free when disabled.
4. **Headless determinism 0 px** — two identical runs, with and without `--headless-jitter`. The
   volume is camera-driven, not frame-index-driven, so it should be trivially deterministic.
5. **The actual feature, at 1:1** — ridges at increasing distance must desaturate toward the sky
   colour progressively, and the far ridge must approach the horizon colour. Inspect at 1:1, never
   a thumbnail (the 9.2/9.4 lesson). Sweep `--sun-elevation` to confirm the haze tracks the sun.
6. **Scale sanity** — `--world-scale` an order of magnitude either way must visibly strengthen or
   erase the effect. If it does not, the units→km mapping is wrong.
7. **Silhouette stability under TAA** — the reason aerial joins the existing accumulator. Measure
   consecutive-frame flicker on the fixture with `--taa --headless-jitter` the way spec 9.5.1 did,
   and confirm it is not a regression against aerial-off.
8. **Spot regression** — `./out/bin/spores -x -f 300` and a `tree` smoke build.

Caveat to re-test, not assume: `docs/rendering-roadmap.md:438` records that output was once not
frame-invariant for a static scene even with `--no-auto-exposure`. Recent fog work contradicts this
(the 9.5 golden reproduces at 0 px), so re-establish the noise floor on a sky scene first.

## As built

M0-M4 shipped as planned. Three things the plan got wrong, corrected here:

**The fog golden does not stay 0 px with aerial on.** The plan asserted aerial would be *exactly*
invisible at prop scale. It is not — it is about **1 LSB on 4% of the rock pixels**, plus a handful
seeing genuinely distant backdrop through gaps in the pile (`-auto-level` on the difference is
almost entirely black, with a few Rayleigh-blue dots). That is the feature working, not a
regression: at `--world-scale 100000000` the difference is 0 px, and at `--world-scale 1` it grows
to 266,046 px, so it tracks distance exactly as it should. The resolution is that each golden tests
one thing — `specs/9.5`'s capture command gained `--no-aerial`, keeping it a pure fog golden that is
byte-identical to the day it was captured. **`--fog --no-aerial` reproducing that golden at 0 px is
what proves the composite restructure changed no fog maths**, and it does.

**`draw_volume_slices` is not reusable and `include/froxel.glsl` is.** The roadmap claimed B9 "owns
no foundations" and reuses B1's layered volume draw. Half right: `create_texture_3d_float` and the
froxel depth mapping were reused verbatim, but `draw_volume_slices` hardcodes the fog FBO, the fog
volume's dimensions, the `sliceIndex` uniform name, and reports failure by clearing `fog_enabled`.
A sky-owned 32³ volume needs its own loop — `bake_lut_3d`, the fifth layered-pass loop in the tree.

**"One extra tap in the composite" understates it.** The composite's single
`GL_ONE, GL_SRC_ALPHA` blend carries exactly one `(inscatter, transmittance)` pair, and the fog pass
early-returned when fog was off. Aerial is atmosphere, not fog, so it had to work with fog off. The
result is a two-medium analytic combine plus a gate restructure and a rename to
`postfx_run_atmosphere` — which turned out better than a separate pass anyway, because aerial
inherits 9.5.1's temporal accumulator and needs it for the same reason fog did.

Also settled along the way: both volumes now integrate to a slice's **far face**, so the composite
indexes them with one expression rather than two off-by-half-a-slice rules; and `aerial_slices`
crosses the seam as published data rather than a mirrored constant, because postfx does not include
`sky.h`.

### M5: kept

The milestone was conditional on measurement, and it earned its place. The plan worried the sky
ambient would double-count aerial perspective; it does not. They are different media: aerial is the
atmosphere between camera and surface, `fog_ambient` is skylight scattered by the *local fog*, which
the fog volume models nowhere else. Its sun term already arrived via the light publish — the
ambient was the one part of fog lighting with no sky path, a hardcoded `{0.05, 0.05, 0.05}`.

`sky_zenith_radiance` computes the single-scattering integral straight up, reusing the same
constants and the same no-readback CPU march as `sky_sun_transmittance`. The haze picks up a blue
skylight cast instead of a neutral grey (RMSE 0.0066 on the fog fixture) and now tracks the sun.
`publish_fog_ambient` lets an app that authors the value keep it — `spores` does, and has no sky
anyway, so it is untouched.

This is the "sky-published fog colours" the roadmap attached to B9, and it is smaller than that
phrasing suggests: the colours were already published, only the ambient was not.

## Review follow-up

Four parallel audits. They found **three correctness bugs**, not just structure.

**Multiple scattering was 3× too dim.** `sky_multiscatter_frag` stores Psi *per unit sun
illuminance* and says so; `sky_view_frag` scales the accumulated total by `SUN_ILLUMINANCE`, which
covers single and multiple scattering together. `aerial_lut_frag` applied it to the single-scatter
term only. Distant surfaces therefore converged on a dimmer, bluer colour than the sky drawn behind
them — exactly the seam the file's own header claims sharing `atmosphere.glsl` prevents, and it was
baked into the first golden. The mechanism is instructive: the two loops applied the same scale in
*different places*, so promoting only the phase helpers could not catch it. Fixed by scaling once
at the end, matching sky-view exactly, and adding the fp16 clamp sky-view already had.

**Aerial was silently dead under `--no-ssao --no-fog`.** `postfx_wants_aux_gbuffer` decides whether
the aux G-buffer is written, and it never learned about aerial — which indexes its volume by that
same linear depth. Masked only because SSAO defaults on. Now keyed on the published volume.

**The GUI's Fog Ambient picker stopped working.** M5's publish overwrote `fog_ambient` every frame,
so the control snapped back. `publish_fog_ambient` was supposed to guard that but was dead
configuration — nothing ever cleared it, and the justification I wrote (spores) was false: spores
never creates a sky, so the publish no-ops there anyway. The picker now clears the flag on edit,
which gives the flag its real caller.

Performance and structure, all confirmed by measurement or by reading the cited code:

- `sky_zenith_radiance` ran **every frame** (two nested 40-step CPU marches) for a value that
  depends only on the sun, and whose only consumer is off by default. Now computed in
  `sky_update_sun_dir` — the single mutation point for `sun_dir` — and the publish is a copy.
- The atmosphere constants had reached **three** copies, two inside `sky.c`, against a file that
  names the one permitted C mirror. Worse, the new copy recomputed the two density exponentials
  that the function it called recomputed anyway. Now one named constant set plus `sky_medium_at`,
  the C analogue of `atmosphereAt`, with `atmosphere.glsl`'s guard note citing symbols rather than
  line numbers that had already rotted.
- `postfx_run_fog`'s `if (fog_on)` block was never re-indented (the hook only checks changed lines).
  It extracted wholesale into `postfx_build_fog_volume` — every local it used was dead outside it —
  taking the function from ~145 lines to ~55.
- `fogOn`/`aerialOn` uniforms deleted: a slice count of zero *is* the absent state, so the medium's
  on/off lives in the data. With `froxelSampleMedium` in `include/froxel.glsl`, the duplicated
  far-face slice arithmetic collapses to two call sites and the convention has one home.
- `PostFX::aerial_enabled` deleted. It made `engine.c` read PostFX state to gate *Sky* GPU work,
  inverting the publish direction everything else on those lines establishes. The toggle lives on
  `SkyAtmosphere` with the rest of the aerial state.
- The bake now latches on failure (it re-allocated, re-failed and re-logged at 60 Hz) and hoists
  the VAO bind out of its loop — an optimisation `draw_volume_slices` documents and my copy dropped,
  costing 31 redundant bind pairs per frame.
- `engine.c` passes the **unjittered** projection to the bake. Bit-identical today, but it stops
  handing a per-frame-churning input to something jitter-blind.

**Not done, deliberately.** `bake_aerial_volume` and `draw_volume_slices` really are near-identical
and want a shared `draw_fullscreen_quad_slices(...)` in `util.h` returning `false` on an incomplete
FBO; my in-code justification for a fifth layered loop was checked and does not hold — the other
three cited precedents differ structurally. Likewise the two GPU marches want a shared
`marchScattering` in `atmosphere.glsl`, which is the real fix for the class of bug above rather
than the instance. Both touch `sky_view_frag`, which carries its own 0-px gate; they are the right
next change, not a review patch. Recorded in the code comment where the decision lives.

**Also considered and rejected for now:** the volume's ray origin is pinned to `VIEW_ALTITUDE`, so
it is invariant to camera *translation* — only orientation, projection, sun and world scale move
it. That means the per-frame frustum rebuild pays for a dependence it does not have, and the volume
could instead be a sky-view-style LUT baked on sun move. Real, and worth doing; it is a redesign of
the feature, not a cleanup. The bake is ~1.6% of one 1080p fullscreen pass, so it is not urgent.

## Notes

- No merge to master without explicit per-instance permission.
- The fixture took three drafts. The first two put ridges at 400 m-2 km, where from a 400 m vantage
  a ridge's base sits well below the horizon and the nearest one swallows everything behind it.
  Only past ~20 km do the bases converge on the horizon and the crests stack — which is also where
  aerial perspective stops being marginal, so the geometric constraint and the subject agree.
