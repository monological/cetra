# Spec 13.17 -- a cat in silent, and the engine's first AI

## Context
The user asked for a cat in silent: black with yellow eyes (both configurable), with clips such as sitting, walking and curled up, and with sounds. It is shown in the study and also walks around the house. It jumps onto the hand railing and walks along it. It is the first entity that tests a simple AI.

Settled in conversation before this plan:
- **Order.** The AI is not built first. It is built with the cat, which is its first consumer: body, then sounds, then navigation, then the brain, then the rail.
- **Engine vs silent.** The engine gets only general pieces: a place graph with route finding, a BRAIN component, a head look-at, and an AUDIO_SOURCE that carries several sounds plus positional one-shots. Everything cat-shaped lives in silent. The user explicitly does not want a cat template in the engine.
- **Out of this spec, each a later spec:** entities in `.cscn` files, game-defined component types, non-humanoid ragdoll, a floor navmesh, and quadruped foot planting.

The user's choices for this plan:
- **The body is built by a headless Blender script** in the repo, after `tools/bake_fire_blender.py` (13.14). The committed `.glb` keeps the build itself Blender-free.
- **The rail is the gallery's:** level, 9 cm wide, 8.73 m long, top at y 4.07, over the drop into the great hall.
- **The temperament is an aloof watcher.** It mostly goes about its business, turns its head to watch you when you are near and in sight, moves off if you rush it, and now and then follows at a distance.
- **No gate arms**, for silent or for the new engine pieces. The look is checked by the user at each phase. Existing groups that the engine changes can reach are re-run, plus the goldens.

## What the house gives (measured from the source)
**Reachable rooms:** study, gallery, stair, great hall, hall, kitchen. Every connection is an OPEN hole:
- the study doorway at z 13.8, x -3.0..-2.1;
- the Tudor arch at z 13.8, x -1.45..-0.05;
- the kitchen doorway at x 0, z 12.75..13.55.

The front door is the only door leaf that moves, and the cat stays indoors. So **no door gating is needed**, which corrects what I told the user earlier.

**Floors and stair:** floors at 0.30 and 3.15. The stair runs x 3.90..4.91 with 15 risers of 0.19 and goings of 0.25. Its foot is at z 18.55 and its head lands at z 15.05; the pitch is 37.2 degrees.

**The gallery rail:**
- Top rail y 4.01..4.07, z 14.955..15.045, x -4.86..3.87.
- Its collider stands 4 cm above the wood, to 4.11.
- West half-post at (-4.84, 15.00), top 4.38.
- The east end meets the stair's head newel at (3.95, 15.00), top 4.38.

**A kinematic body never touches static geometry.** Jolt makes no kinematic-vs-static contacts. So the cat passes through walls and never falls: **the route is what keeps it legal**, and its height comes from the route.

**The player's capsule DOES collide with a kinematic body,** and is pushed by one moving into it.

**The probes capture once.** silent installs GI and four reflection probes at frame 2, and they capture once. A cat present during capture would be baked into every reflection for good. So **the cat's node is attached only once the probe set is `ready`**, or straight away on a run that installs no probe set.

**Glowing surfaces become lights.** silent turns emissive surfaces into area lights, so the eye material sets `emissive_light = 1` (decorative).

## Design

### A. The body: `apps/silent/tools/make_cat_blender.py`
**How it runs.** `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P apps/silent/tools/make_cat_blender.py -- [--clips a,b]`. It builds everything from nothing and saves `out/cat_blender/cat.blend` for inspection.

**Outputs:**
- `assets/models/cat.glb`.
- A GENERATED `apps/silent/src/cat_clips.h`, like `cards.h` and `gothic.h`, never edited by hand. It carries each clip's name, length, loop flag, stated travel, and events in seconds, plus model-space anchors (eye, head).

**A self-check reads the `.glb` back and asserts:**
- every rotated joint has a translation track (one without imports at the origin and collapses: `gen_puppet_fixture.py`'s lesson);
- the Hips bind rotation is the identity;
- each travelling clip's Hips displacement equals its stated travel;
- every in-place clip ends where it began.

**Proportions (a 4 kg domestic cat):**
- 0.46 m nose to tail base, plus a 0.30 m tail in 6 bones.
- 0.25 m at the withers.
- Eyes 0.018 across, about 15% over life so they read at render scale 0.5.
- Legs at least 0.02 thick and the tail 0.028 to 0.018, against TAA at one sample.

**Mesh.**
- A skin-modifier body plus head and muzzle primitives, voxel-remeshed and decimated to **at most 1,800 triangles in one skinned draw**, smooth-shaded: silent's PS2 era.
- Heat-map skin weights, at most 4 per vertex.
- A dirty-vertex-colour AO, which `pbr_frag` multiplies into the albedo, so the form reads under any fur colour.

**Skeleton: 39 bones.**
- `Hips` carries only travel (+Z) and yaw.
- All posture lives on a `Rump` child, so sitting or curling never moves Hips and never turns root motion on. No other bone name may contain "hip" or "pelvis".
- Then Spine1-2, Chest, Neck1-2, Head (Jaw, Ears, Eyes, Lids), four digitigrade legs, and Tail1-6.

**Materials.** Each is a separate glTF material, re-created in silent (see B):

| Material | Colour / settings |
|---|---|
| `cat_fur` | #262424, roughness 0.65, sheen (0.22, 0.22, 0.25). The sheen lobe is what makes a black cat read at night |
| `cat_eye` | #E8B923, roughness 0.08 |
| `cat_pupil` | near-black, a wide night oval |
| `cat_nose` / `cat_ear` | dark; they turn pink when the fur's luminance is above 0.15 |
| `cat_mouth` | pink |

**Clips.** All are authored at 30 fps. The script provides named poses, a gait generator with IK paw targets planted in world space and baked to FK, phase-lagged tail waves, and breathing. Gaits use a lateral-sequence footfall (LH, LF, RH, RF), with events `paw_lh/lf/rh/rf`.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| walk | 0.80 | loop | +0.44 Z (0.55 m/s), duty 0.62 | paws |

**Phase 1 builds a first subset:** idle, walk, the sit/lie/curl ladders, sleep, meow and blink. The rest arrive in the phase that needs them.

### B. Into the house: `apps/silent/src/cat.c/.h`
**Import through a library scene** (the engine design's option (b)). There are no engine changes, and so no golden risk:
1. `create_scene_from_model_path` on `cat.glb`, then drain the async loader, as gametest's `drain_async_loader` does (`gametest.c:2489-2501`).
2. `engine_add_scene(engine, library)` AFTER `game_set_scene`, so the library is never drawn and is freed after the entity manager (`engine.c:378-379`, `game.c:56-78`).
3. Take the `cat` node out with `node_remove_child`, and add it under silent's holder node.
4. Give each mesh a material silent creates. The draw list registers unowned materials itself (`draw_list.c:225-232`).
5. Use the skinned program, as gametest does with `node_set_programs` (`gametest.c:2737-2743`).
6. Make the animator from `library->skeletons[0]`.

Importing straight into an existing scene is deferred to its own spec. It touches the path every golden goes through: lights and cameras assigned outright, the texture directory reset, and per-file embedded texture keys. The cat exercises none of that.

**`create_cat(const CatDesc*)`** sets up:
- the fur and eye colours (sRGB, linearised);
- a kinematic box, half extents (0.07, 0.16, 0.20), on `OBJ_LAYER_KINEMATIC`. Its top at 0.32 is above the player's 0.25 step height, so the player cannot climb onto the cat;
- an ANIMATOR with `root_motion = true`;
- an `anim_graph` (section F).

Pose and body:
- The cat's yaw is `entity->rotation`. **`entity->position` is the source of truth**, because the game loop pushes it into the kinematic body every step (`physics.c:852-865`); `rigid_body_move_kinematic` would be overwritten.
- It starts curled on the **study chair cushion**: (-5.02, 3.655, 9.00), facing 0.52. That is the master's chair, backlit by the stained glass and lit by the desk candles 1.2 m away. Getting home takes a jump.

**Eyeshine** (my addition, not asked for; `--no-eyeshine`). A black cat's yellow eyes in a dark house are seen because they throw the flashlight back. The flashlight sits 0.28 m from the player's eye (`lights.c:303`). The eye emissive is computed from it each frame:

```
E      = on * 900 * smoothstep(cos21, cos9, cos theta) / d^2
facing = max(0, gaze . to_viewer)^6
nits   = min(600, 2.5 * E * facing)    eased over 0.08 s
```

It is zero with the lids closed, or with the line of sight blocked.

### C. Sounds: engine audio, plus `fetch_sounds.py`
**Engine, `cetra/src/game/audio.c/.h`:**
- **AUDIO_SOURCE carries up to 8 sounds, each at an entity-local offset.**
  - `entity_add_audio_source` and `entity_add_audio_source_at` APPEND.
  - New calls: `entity_audio_source_count`, `entity_get_audio_source(entity, index)`, `entity_remove_audio_source`.
  - Positions come from the entity's transform matrix.
  - The one existing caller, gametest's door tone at `gametest.c:3285`, behaves as before.
- **A pool of 32 positional voices:** `audio_play_voice(audio, prototype, &(AudioVoiceDesc){position, follow, volume, pitch})`.
  - Each voice is a `ma_sound_init_copy` of a decoded file Sound, on that Sound's bus, which means `Sound` gains a bus field.
  - A followed entity is held by ID through `find_entity_by_id`, never by raw pointer.
  - Voices are reaped on the main thread with `ma_sound_at_end` in `audio_system_update`.
  - When the pool is full, the oldest voice is stolen. That is required, because headless runs that pull no PCM never end a voice.
- **`audio_play_oneshot` is re-pointed at the pool as a 2D voice.** It has no callers, and it removes the tree's only ASYNC load, which is a determinism hazard for offline renders.
- **The listener stops lagging a frame under a camera rig:** `camera_rig_apply` before `game_pre_render` reads the camera (`game.c:295-303`). That is two lines.

**`fetch_sounds.py`:**
- **A ONESHOT recipe:** envelope-detect the events, cut each with a tail, fade it, high-pass it per job, and level the active part to RMS 0.1 with peaks capped at 0.9, as the loops are, so the C volumes are the only statement of loudness. Written as 16-bit WAV.
- **`--audition`** renders every candidate event for the user to pick.
- **A `purr` loop job.**
- **Candidates** (CC0 Freesound; the licences are rechecked when fetched):
  - meows: 110011, 412017, 479272, 528197, 455493, 66518
  - trill: 850104, 668825, 200336
  - purr: 553962, 463790, 656500, 575933, 732886
  - hiss, paws and landing: searched in phase 3.

**Wiring.** Everything is multiplied by `sounds_indoor_gain()`, and halved when the listener and the cat are on different storeys outside the great hall.

| File | Triggered by | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| cat_meow_1..3 | the `meow` layer event, never the same twice running | 0.45 |
| cat_trill | `trill` | 0.30 |
| cat_hiss | `hiss` | 0.40 |
| cat_paw_1..4 | paw events, round robin | 0.05 on bare floor, 0.04 on the rail, 0 on rugs, runners and the mat |
| cat_land_hard / soft | `land` | 0.25 / 0.12 |
| cat_purr | a loop on the cat's chest offset | 0.30 |

For comparison, silent's existing levels: fridge 0.15, clock 0.5, tubes 0.25, wind 0.6.

**When it purrs:** only while sitting, lying or asleep, and only when all of these hold:
- the listener is within 2 m, and the volume ramps up toward 0.7 m;
- the player is moving slower than 1 m/s;
- affinity is at or above 0.55, the aloof threshold.

### D. Navigation: `cetra/src/nav_graph.c/.h` (engine), and `apps/silent/src/cat_places.c/.h`
**Engine.**
- No GL, physics, clock or game type, like `camera_rig.h` and `anim_graph.h`. One line in the public header allowlist (`cetra/CMakeLists.txt:33-42`).
- **Nodes:** name, position, a clearance radius the author promises, and tag bits.
- **Links** are directed, or added as a pair. Each has an app-defined kind (below `NAV_KIND_MAX` 16; the descriptor carries a cost scale, a drive and a smoothing flag), a cost, an enabled flag, and a shape:
  - LINEAR;
  - STEPS: rise, going, first nosing, and a `hop` where 1 means the pitch line;
  - ARC: an apex above the higher end.
- **Two drives:** DISTANCE, advanced in metres, or PROGRESS, held until the app sets a 0..1 progress.
- **A\* route search** with ties broken by node index, under a query giving allowed kinds and cost overrides.
- **A FOLLOWER** that advances, replans from mid-link, and returns a sample: link, kind, progress, position after profile and smoothing, tangent, heading, waiting and arrived.
- **Corners** are rounded with a quadratic Bezier kept inside the node's radius. Turns sharper than 150 degrees pivot in place instead.
- **`nav_graph_check(probe)`** walks every link's PROFILE (not its chord) through an app ray probe, and reports blocked links by name.
- Size: about 230 lines of header and 650 of C.

**silent: the house's place graph**, from measured coordinates. Lit candles are never approached: there is no desk node, no table node, and the hearth stands are at least 1.5 m from any node.

**Links:**
- **WALK** between neighbours.
- **STAIR** from stair_head to stair_foot.
- **RAIL**, one way east: rail_w → rail_1 → rail_2 → rail_3 → rail_e. No U-turns on a 9 cm rail; every turn happens in the air.
- **JUMPS:** chair 0.505, counter 0.90, kitchen chair 0.516, gal_w→rail_w 0.92 (turned in flight), rail_e→newel_cap 0.31, newel_cap→stair_head 1.23 down, plus escapes off the rail at rail_1, rail_2 and rail_3.
- **`nav_graph_check`** runs at init against the static layer and logs any blocked link.

**Moving the body: `cat_step` in silent's `on_update`, every fixed step.**
- Drain `animator_take_root_motion` every step, unconditionally.
- **Walk, trot, run and rail:** advance the follower by the horizontal travel times the node scale. Height comes from the profile and heading from the follower.
- **Stairs:** advance by the stair clip's own clock, at 0.5 m per loop, so the paws stay on the treads through crossfades. Entry is always from a stop at stair_foot.
- **Jumps:** PROGRESS drive. `u` comes from the graph state's seconds between the clip's `takeoff` and `land` times (read from `cat_clips.h`). The anim rate is set so the authored air time matches the arc's natural flight time; for example, the 0.92 m rail jump plays at 0.78.
- **Facing:** set yaw from the tangent. Deflections over 100 degrees stop and play the turn clips.
- **Yielding to the player:** step sideways within the link's width. Below 0.46 m of clearance, stop. After 3 s, replan. The cat never moves into the player.

### E. Head look-at: `cetra/src/look_at.c/.h` (engine)
**The system.**
- A chain of up to 4 bones (the cat uses Neck2 0.4 and Head 0.6).
- Each bone's aim axis is derived from the bind pose: the model's +Z, expressed in that bone's bind frame.
- Yaw and pitch limits are measured in the chain parent's frame. There is a smoothing rate, an overall weight snapped to exactly 0 below 1e-4, and a `give_up` angle.
- `look_at_set_world` follows `ik_set_world`'s lag and refusal rules.
- The rotation is a shortest arc that keeps the clip's own roll, shared down the chain and carried rigidly through each bone's subtree, as IK's `rotate_below` does (`ik.c:386-401`).

**The seam.**
- A new `AnimationState` slot `look_at`, beside `ik` (`animation.h:210-211`), owned and freed by the state.
- Called in `animation_state_apply_pose` right after `ik_solve` (`animation.c:1386-1388`) and before pass 2 (`:1391`), inside the non-ragdoll branch.
- With the slot NULL, every existing pose is bit-identical.
- Size: about 380 lines.

**What the cat looks at:**
- the player's eye, once noticed;
- the pendulum bob, from a new `clock_bob(time, out)` accessor in `clock.c` (period 2 s, ±0.07 rad);
- the gutter drips past the kitchen window.

### F. The brain: `cetra/src/game/brain.c/.h` (engine, `COMPONENT_BRAIN`), and `apps/silent/src/cat_brain.c/.h`
**Engine.**
- `COMPONENT_BRAIN` is appended before `COMPONENT_MAX`. No save entry is needed: save keys components by name.
- A small utility AI: the game registers ACTIVITIES (name, score, begin/step/end, cooldown, fail cooldown, minimum seconds, user).
- **Re-scoring** happens on an interval, when an activity ends, or on `brain_interrupt`. A challenger must beat the current score by a hysteresis margin, or draw within a band from the brain's seeded xorshift32. A failed begin falls through to the next-best in the same tick.
- **Memory:** `brain_notice` / `brain_recall` hold up to 4 decaying memories, keyed by entity ID.
- **Perception:**
  - `brain_can_see`: `physics_world_raycast_ignore` from the eye, a hit counting when `hit.entity == target`. The hit normal is always zero and is never read.
  - `brain_in_view`.
- **Tick per fixed step**, at a new call in `game.c` between `on_update` (`:224-226`) and the particles (`:229`).
- **Teardown:** `free_brain` frees only its own memory. It never calls `end` and never touches siblings, other entities or the app's graph: components are freed after `on_shutdown`, in enum order.
- **No reads of per-frame edges.** An activity never reads `animator_finished`; it reads `anim_graph_state_name` / `_seconds`.
- Size: about 600 lines.

**silent, the aloof cat.** Its needs live in the app's struct:

| Need | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| energy | drains awake, recovers asleep; starts at 0.55, asleep at home |
| boredom | builds while stationary |
| alarm | set by stimuli, decays |
| affinity | rises slowly near a calm player, falls on a rush |
| novelty per spot | grows with time since the last visit |

**Perception at 5 Hz:** a line-of-sight ray to `player_eye()`, 8 m range, a 240-degree cone. A sprinting player is heard within 6 m.

**Activities:**

| Activity | Score and behaviour |
|---|---|
| SLEEP | 0.9·(1-E)^1.5, at home (or kitchen chair, stove mat, rug); through the posture ladder to curl |
| LOAF | 0.25 + 0.3·(1-E) at a novel spot (rug, gal_mid, study_bay_w, stove_mat, hearth, study_window); 20-40 s |
| EXPLORE | 0.2 + 0.5·boredom, to a novel spot weighted by distance; sits, sometimes grooms |
| WATCH_CLOCK | at hall_clock, 15-30 s, head on the pendulum |
| WINDOW | at kitchen_window, more with heavier rain, 20-45 s, head on the drips and the street |
| RAIL | gal_w → jump → rail east → hop to the newel → sit 10-25 s → jump down |
| GROOM, STRETCH | sub-behaviours: groom while sitting; stretch after waking or a long loaf |
| AVOID | personal space 1.3 m; retreat by graph to a node whose route does not pass within 1 m of the player; trot, or run if rushed |
| STARTLE | an interrupt: a player above 2.2 m/s within 2.5 m and closing, or touching the box; startle, then avoid, or hiss if cornered |
| FOLLOW | rare (sociability 0.3): when affinity is up and the player walks off, trail to 1.5-2 m for up to 40 s |

**Throughout:**
- A visible player within 7 m takes the gaze if close or fast; otherwise a glance every 4-8 s. Head ±70° yaw, ±40° pitch.
- A trill when it re-sees you after 45 s unseen.
- Meows are rare: every 60-150 s while sitting near you.
- Blinks every 8-20 s; a slow blink when you hold still and look at it.
- The knobs are one struct at the aloof values. There are no temperament presets: the user chose one temperament.

**Expected opening:**
- asleep on the chair for about 30 s;
- then it wakes, stretches, and goes down the gallery and stair to the clock (about 75 s);
- then the window, a loaf on the stove mat, the rail and newel at about 200 s;
- and home to sleep at about 300 s.

**The anim graph** uses 16/16 sources, 19/24 states, 14 params and 37 rows.
- Opposite transitions are paired into two-entry spaces, so there is no phase-sync trap.
- `loco` is walk/trot/run on a knob in m/s; `stair` is down/up on `slope`; `jump` is down/up; and there are posture ladders on `posture` with `tr_dir`.
- `startle` is the only FROM=NULL row, and it is guarded off the stair, the rail and mid-air.
- `jump`, `turning` and `stretch` triggers are fired only from states that take them.

## Phases
Each phase that changes the picture ends with the user looking:
- headless frames opened in Preview, with framings pinned by `--cam-eye`/`--cam-target`;

## Phase 1b, added after the first look: fur, whiskers and more triangles
The user, on seeing phase 1's body: "why is it so low poly? give it some hair dude. and some
whiskers." Raiden's hair was the reference offered. Raiden's hair is CARDS (a 1024² atlas of about
eight painted strips on a few dozen quads, with 11.20's strand map steering the anisotropic
highlight); cards suit long hair on a head and are a poor fit for a cat's short, dense coat, which
games draw as SHELLS. Agreed: shells for the coat, whiskers as strips, cards only for longer tufts
if the shells do not carry them, and the body's budget raised.

### The engine: fur shells (`PBR_FEAT_FUR`)
- **A fur coat is the skinned body drawn again as layers**, `gl_InstanceID` the layer, in the
  shading pass only. Layer 0 is the body exactly as before; layers 1..N stand out along the skinned
  normal, leaning along a comb, and each keeps only the cross-sections of the strands at its height.
  A skinned mesh is never batched (it carries no instance block), so `gl_InstanceID` is free.
- **Material fields** (SETTINGS, and `MATERIAL_PARAMS` rows under "Fur", so a `.cscn` and the
  editor reach them): `fur_layers` (0 = none), `fur_length` (m), `fur_density` (strands per metre
  of skin), `fur_thickness` (a strand's root radius as a fraction of its cell), `fur_root_shade`
  (albedo at the root), `fur_comb` (model-space direction the coat lies toward) and `fur_lie` (0
  straight out, 1 lying along the comb).
- **Per-vertex length** is the vertex colour's alpha, which an opaque material never reads: 0
  round the eyes, nose and mouth so the coat cannot grow over them, short on the face and paws.
- **The strands** are a Worley pattern on the BIND-pose position, so they ride the skin: the
  nearest of eight jittered feature points per cell, a per-strand length and tone, the radius
  thinning to the tip. No texture, so no sampler unit.
- **A new feature bit, `PBR_FEAT_FUR` = 256** (`PBR_FEAT_ALL` 511), set by the resolver on a
  material with `fur_layers > 0`. **The mask is spliced into the VERTEX stage too** (until now
  only pbr_frag), so the fur vertex code is compiled out of every existing variant: unconditional,
  it would re-lower every skinned program's position, which AGENTS.md records costing 130 px from
  an untaken branch and, against the depth prepass's LEQUAL, deleting surfaces.
- **A fur mesh sits out the depth prepass** (`item_is_prepassable`): its layer 0 comes from a
  program that differs from the prepass's by the fur code, the same hazard. It draws in the
  shading pass under LEQUAL against everything else's prepass depth, which is all it needs.
- **Shadows, the prepass and captures draw layer 0 only.** `submit_draw_run` takes a layer count;
  every caller but the shading pass passes 1.
- **The cull bound** grows by `fur_length` (`draw_item_bounds`), the vertex factor being at most 1.
- **Motion vectors** carry the shell offset under the previous pose too, or TAA would smear it.

### silent's cat
- **Budget:** 6,000 triangles for the body, 1,400 of them on the head; the shells multiply it
  by their count in the shading pass alone.
- **The coat:** about 14 layers, 9 mm, lying back and down, its length painted into the vertex
  alpha by region.
- **Whiskers:** six a side from the whisker pads, about 6.5 cm, tapering strips on the head, a
  double-sided pale material of their own.
- **For looking at it without silent:** `assets/scenes/cat.cscn` turns the coat on in the render

### Phase 1: the body
- **`make_cat_blender.py`** builds the cat from nothing in Blender 5.2: metaballs polygonised and
  collapsed, heat weights, IK-planted gaits, every clip a function of time keyed through
  `fcurve_ensure_for_datablock`, exported with every bone sampled.
- **Hips binds as the identity only as a VERTICAL bone**, and the self-check caught it: the glTF
  export re-expresses a bone's frame in its own axes rather than conjugating it, so the backward-
  pointing Hips first written came out rotated -90 degrees about X, its local +Z pointing down.
- **The face holds its share of the budget in two passes.** Blender's weighted collapse LOCKS a
  group at any strength rather than weighing it (factor 0.0001 and 1.0 gave the same 2,532 head
  triangles), so the body is collapsed with the head locked and then the head with the body locked.
- **The lower jaw is the body's own chin**, weighted wholly to `Jaw`, with the faces along the mouth
  line in the mouth material; a separate jaw wedge was tried first and dropped below a chin that
  stayed put, a second jaw rather than an open mouth.
- **The curl is the cat on its side with its spine flexed**: a roll of the pelvis and nose-down
  pitch down the chain, which the roll lays into a ring on the floor. A yaw-bent ring was tried
  first and twisted the body.
- **Heat weighting needed holding down.** It gave the groin and flank to the shins, which tore open
  into a lit triangle every stride. Each influence now fades past a reach per bone family, limbs
  fade toward the belly's midline, lower-limb bones move nothing above their joint, and the rules
  are applied again after a smoothing pass so it cannot carry an influence back. A hard cut was
  worse than the diffusion (66 edges past 3x, against 4). Materials are single-sided, so a fold
  stretched open shows nothing rather than its own lit inside. A faint patch remains at the haunch
  when sitting.
- **An engine bug, found and fixed (`f45255fa`).** Assimp gives every joint that weights nothing in
  a primitive a placeholder weight of 0 on vertex 0, and the importer let those take vertex 0's
  slots ahead of its real bones -- one unweighted vertex per primitive, patched from a neighbour.
  Zero weights now take no slot. The puppet never met it, every one of its bones weighting
  something.
- **`cat_sheet.py`** tiles the render app's frames of each clip from a pinned camera.

### Phase 1c: the look, against the user's reference
The user supplied a reference model (side, face, haunch) and judged each round. What it took:

- **The coat is an undercoat, not strands.** Sparse Worley strands read as hairs on skin; the
  pattern became overlapping strands with value-noise tufts (`fur.glsl`), the whiskers a dark
  `#3A3836` at a third the radius.
- **Blender will not polygonise a metaball finer than 5 mm**, so every "finer" grid before this
  was the same 5 mm and the face could not carry a 12 mm nose. The elements are built ten times
  life size and the mesh scaled back (`MB_SCALE`).
- **The face.** The snout read as a dog's because the whisker pads and chin stood as far forward
  as the nose; measured off the reference, the lip sits 8 mm behind the nose and the chin about
  1.5 cm. The nose leather is its own small dome mesh (a rounded shield, nostrils as strips) laid
  on the face by raycast; the philtrum and the inverted-Y lip line are strips projected from in
  front onto the edge `measure_face` finds where the pads overhang the chin, and the jaw is
  everything in front below that edge. The muzzle has its own decimation share (`in_face`).
  Strips widened on the surface zigzagged with its facets; projected from in front they hold.
- **The ears** are curved funnels with a fringe of fur, then smaller and set in on the skull so
  the base ring lands on the head; mounds raised under them read as headphones and went.
- **The legs are the reference's, measured.** The silhouette was segmented from the reference
  image (0.42 mm a pixel, its body this one's depth and length to 5%), and the sections taken
  from it less the coat: forearm 2.6-2.9 cm, metatarsus 2.3, the hind leg broad from the flank to
  the hock. The femur stands 30 degrees off vertical; at 45 the stifle sat under the belly and
  the leg read as a dog's.
- **The body below the neck is a ring mesh, and that is what fixed the walk.** Every metaball
  version creased when the legs moved -- a thigh folding into a fin, a sail of skin between leg
  and belly -- because a collapsed metaball surface has no structure at the joints and heat
  weighting gave the back of the thigh to the rump. The torso, the tail and the hind legs are one
  branched skin-modifier mesh over a stick figure whose points are the MIDDLES of the measured
  sections and whose rings are ovals sized to them; the forelegs are grown apart and joined by
  an exact boolean union, since as branches their first ring lay inside the chest's and the hull
  folded into ridges down the chest. The metaball head is cut at the neck and bridged on, its
  decimation share reaching the cut so the neck is not a faceted collar.
- **Weights are stated, not guessed.** Each stick-figure segment names its bones, eased into a
  half-and-half over 12 mm at each joint; a vertex plainly on one segment takes that segment's,
  and the ~1,000 where a leg meets the body (where nearness cannot tell a chest from an elbow)
  are filled in by averaging over the surface from the sure ones round them.
- **Tried and dropped, each for a named reason:** sweeps of ellipsoids along each bone (lumps);
  a thigh, a shin and a hamstring each swept on its own line (a second leg, which came apart in
  the walk); a ring mesh shrinkwrapped onto the metaball surface (sticks, and tears at the hips);
  every limb a separate tube fused by voxel remesh (it fixed the shoulder and ruined the hind
  legs the user had already accepted).
- **Still open, by the user's acceptance:** a soft step in the back line where the neck starts,
  and a few small specks the coat hides. Phase 1's sitting-haunch patch was not rechecked on the
  new body.
- Goldens and gates were not run during the look, at the user's instruction. With the look
  accepted: `--only pbr-variant` five of five, and all 31 goldens at 0 px, `puppet` (the one
