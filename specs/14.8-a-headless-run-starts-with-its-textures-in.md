# 14.8: a headless run starts with its textures in

## Context

macOS 27.2 moved ten goldens by one code. Nine were re-baked (821528c7, on master). The tenth,
`local_exposure`, cannot be re-baked reliably:
- **The bake refused it twice:** its two renders differed by 614,647 px.
- **The cause:** a headless run does not wait for its textures. The engine's loader decodes them on
  background threads, and the main thread uploads at most five a frame. The recipe takes frame 30
  with auto-exposure on. On a busy machine (today, a VM using two cores), frame 30 is a room half
  loaded, and the meter has spent 30 frames on a darker room.
- **It is not only this golden:** `taa-ortho`'s comment (`scripts/gates.py` ~14171) records results
  alternating between two values from the same timing, worked around by waiting 120 frames.

**Outcome:** a headless run waits once, before frame 1, until every texture its startup asked for
is in. Its frame N is then the same picture on a busy or an idle machine.
- **A window starts as fast as before**: it shows its first frame at once and lets textures arrive.
  This is the user's condition: the engine starts quickly.
- **A texture asked for after the run has started** still loads in the background, as now. So the one
  arm that tests a late texture, `idle-load`, keeps testing it.
- **Every app gets it**, since they all run through `engine_run` (`game_run` calls it,
  `cetra/src/game/game.c:366`, after `on_init` and the audio's loading wait). In practice it reaches
  the render app's scenes and a textured `--puppet` in gametest:
  - silent already drains at startup (`apps/silent/src/mats.c:741`, `library.c:30`);
  - forest, tree and sprites load their textures synchronously;
  - the rest load none.

## The change

In `engine_run` (`cetra/src/engine.c:3699`), before the loop and **before `last_frame_time` is
taken**, so frame 1's delta does not include the wait: when `engine->headless`, call
`engine_finish_texture_loads(engine)`, with a comment saying why.

- **Reused:** `engine_finish_texture_loads` (`engine.c:2315`) drains the loader one upload at a time
  and draws the loading screen between uploads, which draws nothing headless.
- **What opens on frame 1 as a result:** `async_loader_is_busy` is the gate for three things, which
  then all open on frame 1 instead of whenever the last texture landed:
  - GI and probe captures (`scene_capture_ready`, `cetra/src/render.c:2065`);
  - the mask-array build;
  - the height-map resolve.
- **Idle mode is left as it is:** the loader's wakes during the wait latch one request, so a headless
  idle run draws one frame after frame 1. Today it draws one for each wake. The `idle` group checks
  this.

### Phase 2: the instrument

**`layer_fixture` could not be it.** On master its frame 1 and frame 30 are already 0 px apart:
its seven maps come in through the scene file within the first frame, not through the per-frame
streaming. And no fixture in the corpus streams more than three images, few enough to decode
during start-up's shader compiles, so on an idle machine their first frame is whole by luck.

**So the arm has a fixture of its own**, `textures_in_fixture` (`gen_textures_in_fixture.py`):
- **sixteen tiles on a wall**, each with its own two-colour checker of its own hue, embedded in the
  glTF as data URIs, which the importer streams as it does a file
  (`load_texture_from_memory_async`);
- **otherwise still**: a fixed camera, one directional light casting nothing, exposure pinned;
- **its generator asserts** more images than three frames' uploads, and that no two are alike.

**`textures-in`** renders it at frame 1 and at frame 30 and wants 0 px.

**Measured on master:** 222,938 px. Frame 1 shows five tiles textured and eleven white: the log
names `*0` to `*4` loaded before the first frame's screenshot, and the rest after. The count does
not depend on load, since it is the per-frame cap and not the decode speed that holds them back.

`fixture-gen` (66 generators) and `gate-docs` pass with it.

### Phase 3: the change

**As planned** (dd92e379): `engine_run` calls `engine_finish_texture_loads` when headless, before
the clock is read.

**Measured:**
- **`textures-in`:** 0 px.
- **Falsified:** with the call disabled, 222,938 px again, the count master read.
- **`local_exposure` re-baked under the load that had refused it twice** (a VM at 240% of a core):

  - the new image is 3,181 px from the old, every one by one code, the same drift macOS 27.2 left
    on the other nine, so it is the fully loaded room;

- **The look:** its frame 1 without the wait has the plaster bare white, the blinds half drawn and
  the leaves pale; with it, the whole room is in from the first frame. Shown side by side in
  Preview.

### Phase 4: goldens and gates

**Goldens:** all 33 pass at 0 px, under the same VM load. No other golden was captured mid-load.

**Gates:** 15 of the 16 groups passed at once:
- `textures-in`, `local-exposure`, `layers`, `decals`, `texcomp`, `alphacov`, `mask`, `clearcoat`;
- `taa-ortho`, `gi-cook`, `cook`, `emissive`, `profiler`, `idle`, `gate-docs`.

**`ladder-a2c` failed, 0.58 of the truth against a bar of 0.6, and the change is the cause.**
- **With the wait disabled** it reads 0.89, as recorded in spec 11.101.
- **But 0.89 is a transient.** Without the wait the cards drew untextured for their first frames,
  and at frame 62 TAA's history was still fading from them:

  | frame | without the wait | with it |
  |---|---|---|
  | 62 | 0.89 | 0.58 |
  | 122 | 0.64 | 0.60 |
  | 302 | 0.59 | 0.59 |
  | 602 | 0.60 | 0.60 |

  At frame 602 the two builds read the same cards to the decimal (11.1 / 10.6 / 14.1).
- **One sample settles at 0.19** (4.3 / 4.0 / 3.1), against the 0.22 recorded.
- **So the finding holds in direction**: four samples keep about three times what one keeps. Its
  magnitude was the transient's.
- **The bar is 0.4, by the user's choice**: two thirds of the settled reading, the margin the old
  bar had, and twice one sample's. The arm's comments and its one-sample figure are corrected,
  with the reason.

**Timings that moved:**
