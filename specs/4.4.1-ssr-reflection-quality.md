# Tier 2 · 4.4.1 — SSR reflection quality (full-res tracing + temporal accumulation)

## Context

The glossy shadow-catcher floor casts a serrated, "venetian-blind" striped reflection. **Root cause
(confirmed with `--ssr-debug`): the SSR march runs at HALF resolution**, so the reflection's hard
hit/miss coverage edge is quantized to the half-res grid and tent-upsampled to full res — a jagged
edge.

A first attempt (a mip blur of the half-res buffer) was a **symptom-hide at the wrong altitude**: it
bought "not jagged" by paying "not sharp," over-softened a roughness-0.1 near-mirror floor, and leaked
SSR state into the shared fog/SSR composite (thermo + altitude reviews concurred). **Abandoned and
reverted.**

**The fix** (the correct modern approach): trace the reflection at **full resolution** (kills the
serration at its source, keeps it sharp) and layer **temporal accumulation** on top (reproject +
neighborhood-clamp + blend across frames — the machinery TAA/GTAO/SSGI already use) to stabilize and
denoise it, supersampling the already-TAA-jittered march into a clean result.

Prerequisite shipped on `master` (commit `77a4e11`): the **camera diagnostic** (`--cam-eye`/
`--cam-target`, "Print Camera" button, "Camera Info" HUD) for grabbing the grazing artifact pose.
**Artifact pose:**
`-W 3456 -H 2104 --fov 50.0 --cam-eye -0.759,0.192,1.885 --cam-target -0.756,-0.190,-0.001 --cam-up 0,1,0`.

### Recon facts (verified this session)

- **Half-res is set at three points:** buffer `ssr_fbo`/`ssr_texture` (`postfx.c:311`,
  `ssao_width×ssao_height` = width/2), Hi-Z pyramid base (`postfx.c:342-343`), march viewport +
  `hizWidth/Height` (`postfx.c:1224,1235-1236`). **Depth + HDR sources are already full-res**
  (`postfx.c:284,236`). Full-res = flip those three to `fx->width/height` (+ one Hi-Z mip level).
- **Cost:** ~4× marching fragments, **bounded to floor coverage** (the march early-outs after ~2
  fetches on non-reflective/sky pixels, `ssr_frag.glsl:81-102`). Measured in M1.
- **The march is ALREADY per-frame TAA-jittered** (fed `draw_projection`, sub-pixel Halton jittered
  under TAA, `render.c:612-620` → `postfx.c:1238-1239`) → temporal accumulation supersamples the
  jittered traces into a sharp edge. No new jitter needed.
- **Temporal machinery to reuse verbatim:** `run_temporal_accum(fx, prog, PingPong*, w, h, cur)`
  (`postfx.c:859`) binds current / velocity (`aux_texture`) / history, drives `reset` off `pp->valid`,
  ping-pongs on `frame_index & 1`. The shared `temporal_accum_frag.glsl` does a per-channel 3×3
  neighborhood clamp + `mix(current, history, FEEDBACK=0.9)` — **correct for the SSR buffer's
  premultiplied `(color*weight, weight)`**. `create_pingpong` (`postfx.c:54`) makes `ssr_history`
  (mirror `ssgi_history`). All accumulators gate on `taa_resolving = taa_enabled && aux_written`
  (`postfx.c:999`), with `else pp.valid = false`.
- **Two caveats, by design:**
  - **Jitter is disabled in headless** (`render.c:614`, for determinism) → temporal's supersampling
    shows **windowed only**. M1 (full-res) is deterministic + headless-verifiable; M2 (temporal) is
    verified interactively.
  - **Reprojection uses SURFACE motion** (`aux.xy`); a reflection's virtual image moves differently,
    so temporal can **ghost under fast camera motion**, bounded by the neighborhood clamp (the SSGI
    tradeoff). Static/slow (the artifact) is clean. No reflection-motion reprojection exists.
- **Composite:** currently the half-res buffer is tent-**up**sampled (`upsample_tent_program`,
  premultiplied-over into `hdr_fbo` before bloom, `postfx.c:1285-1295`). At full res there's nothing to
  upsample — composite 1:1 (full-res `texelSize`), which also keeps SSR off the shared tent's blur path.

## Design (sequenced: deterministic core first)

**M1 — Full-res SSR tracing.** Flip the three half-res sites to `fx->width/height` (+ one Hi-Z level);
composite 1:1 (full-res `texelSize`, no half-res tent upsample). Kill-switch `ssr_full_res` (postfx
field, default true; `--no-ssr-full-res`): **off = half-res = `cmp`-exact vs master** (byte gate;
buffers created at the flag's resolution). Measure the frame-time delta.

**M2 — Temporal accumulation.** Add `PingPong ssr_history` (RGBA16F, full-res, alloc next to
`ssr_texture`, free in the free block). After the march (`postfx.c:1266`), when `taa_resolving`, run
`run_temporal_accum(fx, fx->temporal_accum_program, &fx->ssr_history, fx->width, fx->height,
fx->ssr_texture)` (reuse the SHARED accumulator) and point the composite at the accumulated result;
else `ssr_history.valid = false`. Kill-switch `ssr_temporal` (default true; `--no-ssr-temporal`).

**M3 — (deferred) Stochastic glossy.** Roughness-importance-sampled ray jitter → physically-correct
glossy blur via accumulation. Near-mirror floor doesn't need it; future work.

## Milestone gates

- **M1:** `--no-ssr-full-res` → `cmp`-exact vs master; **on** → serrated edge gone at the artifact pose
  (headless A/B, deterministic), reflection sharp; off-floor RMSE ≈ 0; c64 + raiden clean; cost delta
  measured + acceptable.
- **M2:** TAA off / flag off → identical to M1 (single-frame full-res); TAA on, windowed → reflection
  stable + denoised (interactive verify via camera diagnostic); headless `--taa` (jitter off) → equals
  M1 frame-for-frame (accumulation of identical frames = identity).
- **R:** /thermo-nuclear + /simplify (background), dedup + apply + re-gate; roadmap shipped-shape;
  memory. **User merges on explicit request.**

## Budgets & risks

- **Full-res march cost** (~4× fragments, floor-bounded): the main risk — measured in M1; fallback is
  0.75× res, or keep half-res march + temporal reconstruction (windowed only).
- **Temporal reflection ghosting** under fast motion (surface-motion reprojection): accepted, clamp-
  bounded, documented; the artifact case is static/clean. Reflection-motion reprojection is future work.
- **Headless can't show temporal supersampling** (jitter off): M2 windowed-verified; M1 carries the
  deterministic gates.
- **Byte-identity:** `--no-ssr-full-res` `cmp`-exact vs master; `--no-ssr-temporal` (or TAA off) equals M1.

## After 4.4.1

Reflection-motion reprojection (the reflected virtual-hit's own screen motion) to kill temporal
ghosting under motion; stochastic roughness-jittered rays (M3) for glossy reflective materials; a
spatial à-trous denoise (like SSGI) for faster temporal convergence / disocclusion fill.
