# 14.24: dimming by light

## Context

pcb 7.3.1 (its spec `specs/7.3.1-the-spotlight.md`) puts a net or a part in a spotlight, as Altium's Ctrl+click highlights a net: what is lit keeps its colour and everything else on the canvas is drawn dimmer. The user tuned it with a spike in pcb's copy of cetra, and that spike is this spec's code.

The canvas's tag `dim` could not do it. It darkens toward black, so on the schematic's white paper a dimmed part stood out more than a lit one. A dim per tag also has no idea of room: the user wants the dimming to ease off within some points of whatever is lit, 0 for Altium's hard edge.

## The design

- **A tag can be lit** (`CanvasTagState.lit`): a light the view's dimming spares, and what is near it. A shape is lit when either of its tags is, so a pad on a lit net lights through its net however its part is tagged.
- **A view can dim** (`CanvasView.dim`, `dim_to`, `dim_room`): how far a tagged shape away from a light fades, 0 for none; toward which colour, the paper the view is drawn on; and over how many points round a light the fading eases off, 0 for a hard edge.
- **How it is drawn**, only while a view dims:
  - the layers are drawn once more, into a target of their own (`lit_fbo`, mipmapped), each tagged shape's coverage in green and a lit one's in red, by max (canvas pass 5);
  - the composite fades each pixel toward `dim_to` by `dim` times its tagged coverage, less as it nears a light: the largest of rings of taps round it within `dim_room`, each read at a level as wide as its spacing, so a light thinner than the taps' spacing still registers.
- **Untagged shapes never dim**, so the paper, the board and anything an app leaves untagged stay as they are.
- **A view that does not dim draws as before**, with no extra pass and the same composite.
- **The tag's own `dim` stays**, darkening toward black as now, for an app that wants one shape darkened.

## Phases

1. **The dimming:** the spike's code, its comments made the canvas's; `docs/canvas.md`; the roadmap's entry. Gates `--only canvas`.
2. **An arm:**
   - `canvas-dim`, from apps/canvas's tags sheet drawn with a view that dims;
   - a lit box is exactly as drawn;
   - an unlit tagged box is at its colour faded by the strength, within a code;
   - an untagged one is unchanged;
   - with room, an unlit box beside a lit one fades less than one far from it.
3. **The docs**, and the reviews. Then, with the user's say-so given already for this spec, master fast-forwards, and pcb's submodule is brought up to it.

## As built

### Phase 1: the dimming

The spike's code as pcb's look settled it:
- `CanvasTagState.lit`, in the tag's spare fourth texel;
- `CanvasView.dim`, `dim_to` and `dim_room`;
- pass 5 into `lit_fbo`, mipmapped;
- the composite's fade.

Added on the way:
- the lit target is freed when the canvas is, and when a resize fails;
- the pass is documented in `canvas_pass.glsl`'s list.

The docs: `docs/canvas.md` "Dimming by light" and the tag's list in its ten things; `docs/shaders.md`'s composite; the roadmap's entry.

`--only canvas`: all 35 arms pass. A view that does not dim draws no pass 5 and composites as before.

### Phase 2: the arm

- **apps/canvas's `dim` sheet** draws four red boxes: one lit, one a hair beside it tagged, one tagged far off, and one untagged.
- **`--dim STRENGTH,ROOM`** makes the view dim toward white.
- **`canvas-dim`** reads it at half strength:
  - with no room: lit `ff0000`, untagged `ff0000`, far `ff8080`, and near `ff8080`, the far one's;
  - with 20 pt of room: near `ff1414`.
