# Spec 13.30 -- static on silent's television

## Context
The living-room television in silent's home (`apps/silent/src/home.c`, the living room) shows a
flat blue-grey glow, "left on with nothing on it". The user wants realistic analog CRT static on
it. Spec 13.29 built the engine half: a material drawn in the late draw, past TAA, so a picture
that changes every frame is not averaged into grey (measured there: drawn before TAA, noise keeps
5 codes of its 31 and correlates +0.72 frame to frame). This spec is silent's own: its shader, its
screen, its light and its sound, with no engine change.

**What real static is**, and so what the shader draws:
- The receiver's own noise, amplified by the AGC to full scale. A new field every 1/59.94 s, so
  every field is unrelated to the one before; interlaced, each line holding the field that drew it.
- Band-limited along the line (the video bandwidth lets through about 440 values a line), so a
  speck is a short horizontal dash; independent between the ~480 lines.
- Monochrome, since the colour killer turns chroma off with no burst: a cool ~9300 K white.
- Gaussian noise clipped at black and white, then the tube's ~2.4 gamma: mostly dark, bright specks.
- A hum bar rolling up the picture, mains against the field rate, about once every 17 s.
- Rounded corners, a slight falloff to the edges, and scanlines only where a line covers about two
  pixels or more; where a pixel covers several lines, it averages them, so a far set reads finer
  and greyer.
- The light it throws barely flickers, since a field averages ~200,000 samples. What moves it is
  the AGC breathing.

## Workflow
- Branch `13.30-tv-static` off `13.29-shader-hooks`.
- This spec committed alone first. Commit after each phase with as-built notes.
- No gates (a demo app). The user looks at it from the sofa and close up, and picks the hiss by ear.

## Phase 1 -- the picture
- `apps/silent/shaders/tv_static.glsl`, embedded with `cetra_embed_shaders(silent shaders)` -- its
  first caller -- and built with `create_late_surface_program` over `late_surface.glsl`.
- A card over the glass face (`kit_frame_card_rect`, UV 0..1) with its own material,
  `MAT_TV_STATIC`, in the late draw.
- **The mean and the noise split.** `MAT_SCREEN`'s emissive carries the static's mean, set each
  frame, so SSR on the glossy floor, the probes and TAA's history see a lit screen; the late card
  adds `static - mean`, additively, and outside the rounded corners takes the mean away.
- `tv.c/h`: the field clock, the AGC level (one C function, read by the shader param, the glass
  and the light), and the mean from the clipped, gamma'd distribution.
- `tv_glow` takes the static's colour and follows the level.
- `--no-static`: the screen as it was.
- **Look:** from the sofa and close up, still and in motion.

## Phase 2 -- the hiss
- A CC0 Freesound TV-static recording, chosen by the user by ear, cut and looped by
  `tools/fetch_sounds.py` and credited like the others; positional at the set through
  `sounds.c`'s indoor gain. Silenced with `--mute` and `--no-static`.
