# Spec 13.30 -- static on silent's television

## Context
The living-room television in silent's home (`apps/silent/src/home.c`, the living room) shows a
flat blue-grey glow, "left on with nothing on it". The user wants realistic analog CRT static on
it. Spec 13.29 built the engine half: a material drawn in the late draw, past TAA, so a picture
that changes every frame is not averaged into grey (measured there: drawn before TAA, noise keeps
5 codes of its 31 and correlates +0.72 frame to frame). This spec is silent's own: its shader, its
screen, its light and its sound, with no engine change.

**What real static is**, and so what the shader draws:
- The receiver's own noise, amplified by the AGC to full scale. A new field every 1/59.94 s, so
  every field is unrelated to the one before; interlaced, each line holding the field that drew it.
- Band-limited along the line (the video bandwidth lets through about 440 values a line), so a
  speck is a short horizontal dash; independent between the ~480 lines.
- Monochrome, since the colour killer turns chroma off with no burst: a cool ~9300 K white.
- Gaussian noise clipped at black and white, then the tube's ~2.4 gamma: mostly dark, bright specks.
- A hum bar rolling up the picture, mains against the field rate, about once every 17 s.
- Rounded corners, a slight falloff to the edges, and scanlines only where a line covers about two
  pixels or more; where a pixel covers several lines, it averages them, so a far set reads finer
  and greyer.
- The light it throws barely flickers, since a field averages ~200,000 samples. What moves it is
  the AGC breathing.

## Workflow
- Branch `13.30-tv-static` off `13.29-shader-hooks`.
- This spec committed alone first. Commit after each phase with as-built notes.
- No gates (a demo app). The user looks at it from the sofa and close up, and picks the hiss by ear.

## Phase 1 -- the picture
- `apps/silent/shaders/tv_static.glsl`, embedded with `cetra_embed_shaders(silent shaders)` -- its
  first caller -- and built with `create_late_surface_program` over `late_surface.glsl`.
- A card over the glass face (`kit_frame_card_rect`, UV 0..1) with its own material,
  `MAT_TV_STATIC`, in the late draw.
- **The mean and the noise split.** `MAT_SCREEN`'s emissive carries the static's mean, set each
  frame, so SSR on the glossy floor, the probes and TAA's history see a lit screen; the late card
  adds `static - mean`, additively, and outside the rounded corners takes the mean away.
- `tv.c/h`: the field clock, the AGC level (one C function, read by the shader param, the glass
  and the light), and the mean from the clipped, gamma'd distribution.
- `tv_glow` takes the static's colour and follows the level.
- `--no-static`: the screen as it was.
- **Look:** from the sofa and close up, still and in motion.

## Phase 2 -- the hiss
- A CC0 Freesound TV-static recording, chosen by the user by ear, cut and looped by
  `tools/fetch_sounds.py` and credited like the others; positional at the set through
  `sounds.c`'s indoor gain. Silenced with `--mute` and `--no-static`.

## Review (after phases 1 and 2 were built and first looked at)
Two thermo-nuclear reviewers over `13.29-shader-hooks..13.30-tv-static`, one on silent's C side and
one on the shader and its contract with `tv.c`. The first look also found a bright spot in the
middle of the picture: `tv_glow`, the light standing in for the picture, reflected in its own
glossy glass -- there before this spec, on the plain glowing glass, and plainer over snow. Three
decisions were the user's, below; the rest restructures without changing the picture.

**The user's decisions:**
- **The hotspot is fixed in the ENGINE**, which this spec had said it would not touch: a light's
  `specular` becomes real. `Light.specular` was a `vec3` nothing read; it becomes one float, the
  share of the light that reaches the reflective lobes, 1 by default and physical. Clustered lights
  carry it in an array of 128 packed four to a `vec4` at the end of the light block (512 bytes,
  the block to 15,072 of GL 4.1's guaranteed 16,384 -- every per-light slot is already a cached
  light's), directionals in `sizeMisc.z`. `pbr_frag` scales a light's reflection as a capture
  removes every light's (`captureDiffuseOnly`): the Fresnel the diffuse gives up with it, the
  clearcoat and sheen lobes, a panel's LTC specular; water scales its sun glint. `tv_glow` takes 0:
  the glass's own emission is what SSR and the probes reflect, and the light's highlight was the
  picture reflected a second time.
- **The static follows the model the spec states**, in four places: the hum bar applied to the
  light after the gamma, so its depth means one thing on both sides; Gaussian noise (Box-Muller from
  two hash lanes) where three uniforms gave the brightest specks at a third of their rate; a raised
  cosine beam across a line, whose mean is 1 by construction, in place of a truncated Gaussian and
  its normaliser; and no cap on the footprint, so a set across the room is as grainy on a 1x window
  as on a 2x one.
- **The glass's emission takes the picture's shape**: a surface hook (spec 13.29) on the glass
  masks its emission to the rounded tube and its falloff, from the same GLSL the static uses, so
  the card adds nothing at its own edge -- where TAAU had softened the glass and not the card -- and
  reflections show a rounded screen.

**Restructured:**
- **One owner for the set**, `tv_build` in `tv.c`, as `clock_build` and `lights_build` own theirs:
  the cabinet, glass, card, knobs and ears, the glow light, the late program and the speaker.
  Deletes the light found again by name, the picture rectangle exported from `home.h`,
  `home_tv_speaker`, the flag through `home_build`, `mats.c`'s late program, and the order
  `silent.c` could state only in a comment.
- **The mean stated once.** With the hum after the gamma and the tube's shape applied to glass and
  card alike, the glass emits exactly the picture's expected light per pixel, and the mean C needs
  is the signal's alone; the numbers both sides use cross as params.
- **The field clock from C**: the field number and the hum's phase are uploaded, so the shader
  never reads `time`.
- **Params by lifetime**: one written each frame, the rest at build.
- **A late material casts nothing through the kit's shadow cells** (`casts_plainly` asks the pass).
- The card flush with the glass (the late canvas has no depth to fight); the hum's period as its
  formula; the hiss's audition list; comments to the house standard.

## As built

### Phases 1 and 2, as first built
- **The shader did not build at first**: it called a `pcg3d` no chunk defines. It takes three
  lanes of `pcg4d`, the engine's one integer hash.
- **The hiss is Freesound 765159 alone**, the user's pick; the fetcher lists no other candidate for
  it. `tv_static.flac` was made by `fetch_sounds.py`'s own steps for that one loop -- its decode,
  loop and FLAC -- from the audition's copy of the preview, so nothing else the fetcher writes was
  touched and nothing was downloaded again.
- **The snow is new every frame under TAA**: between two consecutive frames of a close-up, 51% of
  the screen's pixels moved by more than 2%.

### The review, as built
- **A light's specular share** (`9da0cf17`). The array went first at the END of the light block and
  read zero for every light: only the block's live prefix is uploaded, ending at the last packed
  light, so ten goldens moved by up to 1.17 million pixels. Ahead of the packed lights it is 32
  goldens at 0 px. The `light-specular` group's two arms each fail their falsifier: `spec-metal`
  with the share left out of the lobes (the metal box stays lit, 0.0232 against 0.0232), and
  `spec-diffuse` with the share dimming the whole light (the red wall goes to 0). A scene file's
  light takes it as `specular`.
- **The footprint keeps a cap, at 8 taps a side, and that departs from "uncap" as decided.** Past
  the cap the average of the taps taken is pulled toward the mean by as much as the whole
  footprint's would be, which is what the uncapping was for: the grain's contrast does not depend
  on how many pixels the window has. With no cap at all, a set one pixel across walks the whole
  picture's 211,000 samples in one fragment, and a gather costs by the length of its loops.
- **The hum bar's depth is 0.18 of the light**, where it was 0.08 of the signal; that is the same
  bar, measured after the gamma rather than before it.
- **The glass places a pixel in the picture by where it is**, from the picture's corner and its
  direction across, both world: its UVs are the smear's, in metres.

### The hotspot, which the review misdiagnosed
The decision to make a light's specular real rested on a diagnosis that was wrong: the bright spot
in the middle of the picture was not `tv_glow`'s highlight on the glass. Taking the light's
specular share to 0 moved the spot from 62.9 codes to 62.3, against 38.9 on the glass beside it.
With `--no-fog` the spot was gone, and from 30 cm to one side it moved off the middle of the glass
with the light: the fog scattering a point light that hung in the air 24 cm in front of the screen,
a ball of lit fog where a real screen lights the haze, faintly, from its whole face.

Two fixes were weighed and refused. A light's share of the fog, Unreal's volumetric scattering
intensity, would have taken the screen's light out of the haze altogether to hide that the light
was in the wrong place and the wrong shape. A spot on the glass would have worked only because the
fog scatters no spot after the flashlight.

**`tv_glow` is a panel** (`19eb1e7b`): the picture's size, 2 mm in front of the glass, shining into
the room. The glass, the cabinet's face and the wall behind the set are all behind it, so it lights
none of them, and the fog scatters no panel. The spot reads 31.9 codes against 31.9 beside it.
- **Its brightness is the picture's own**, its light averaged over its area: the signal's mean, the
  hum bar's average dip over the one cycle the picture's height is, the falloff's average over the
  rectangle, and what the rounded corners and half the soft edge leave. The closed form is within
  0.3% of a numerical integral of the shader's shape. So the room is lit by what the glass shows,
  which is about a third of the 3 cd the point light was given by eye; the room by the set is dimmer
  for it.
- **The light's specular share stays at 0**, the user's call once the diagnosis was corrected:
  the glass's own light is what SSR and the probes reflect, and the panel's highlight would be the
  screen reflected twice.
- Idle (`--no-static`), the panel is the glass's 4 nits of blue-grey.

**Booked:** the fog scatters no panel (its inject walks point lights and the first spot), so the
screen lights no haze at all; a missing effect, faint in a real room, rather than a wrong one.
