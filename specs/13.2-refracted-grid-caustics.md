# Spec 13.2 — Refracted-grid caustics

## Context

Our water caustics are a heuristic (`water_frag.glsl:330-345`, `:1021-1068`). They walk back from the bed along the UNrefracted sun by a fudge factor (`path * 0.18`), read the medium and short bands' horizontal-displacement Jacobian there, threshold and square it, and brighten the whole refracted bed colour. That has three consequences:
- **Wrong quantity.** It measures surface bunching, which is the whitecap signal, not optical focus.
- **Energy.** It only brightens, so light is created and the dark cells between lines never appear.
- **Wrong term.** It scales sky-lit and shadowed bed too, not just direct light.

It is also FFT-only. Spec 13.0 (line 108) already names the gap: "a crop shows ours missing the seabed focus the demo's projected texture reaches."

`../research/water/clearwater` does it properly (Evan Wallace's method):
1. A vertex grid over the surface.
2. Each vertex refracts the sun through the surface normal and intersects the floor.
3. The displaced grid is rasterised additively.
4. Intensity is source area over landed area, so flat water gives exactly 1 and the result is energy-conserving.
5. It multiplies only the direct-light term.

This spec replaces ours with that method. Decisions already made:
- **Apply in `water_frag`**, weighted by the key light's share of the bed's irradiance.
- **Fall back to a reference plane** when there is no baked bed, with clearwater's per-fragment registration shift.
- **Spec 13.2**, with the branch named after the spec file.

## Design

**Caustic window.**
- One world-space square of **48 m**, converted by `waterUnitsPerMetre`, centred ahead of the camera. It is snapped to its own cell lattice so the pattern never swims.
- **G = 512 cells, 9.375 cm each.** That is exactly 4 short-band tiles of 12 m at 128 texels, so grid vertices sit on short-band texels.
- **Target:** 1024² RGBA16F, CLAMP, mipped, covering the inner 40 m.
- The grid is shifted upstream by the flat-water refraction offset (clearwater's `uShift`) and padded 4 m per side.
- Bands don't share a period, so there is no periodic patch and no 9-instance wrap.

**Normal: one helper both sides use.** Add `oceanShadingNormal(meshN, displacedXZ, shortKeep)` to `ocean.glsl`. It folds in the short band's slope, which today lives only in `water_frag.glsl:582-592` as `WATER_SHORT_SLOPE_GAIN` and moves in as `OCEAN_SHORT_SLOPE_GAIN`. `water_frag` passes its view fade; the grid passes 1. It returns `meshN` unchanged on Gerstner, which has an analytic normal. Caustics therefore work on both wave models.

**Grid pass** (`water_caustic_vert/frag.glsl`, `_water_run_caustics` in `water.c`). It runs inside the existing `_water_pass_begin/_end` bracket after the spectral chain, and the inputs come from `_water_bind_ocean`.
- **Vertex shader:**
  1. Evaluate the surface at the grid point with `oceanEvaluateAt` (footprint = cell size), then apply `oceanShadingNormal`.
  2. `r = refract(-keyDir, N, 1/1.3335)`.
  3. **Floor.** On the baked bed (`bedTex`, inside its domain), iterate `t = (e.y - bedY(e.xz + r.xz·t)) / -r.y` 4 times. This contracts, since |∇bed|·tanθr ≤ 0.88. Otherwise intersect the reference plane.
  4. **`vSrc`** is the entry point projected along the incoming key onto the still plane, in metres relative to the window. That keeps `dFdx` precise.
- **Fragment shader:** `I = min(|det(dFdx(vSrc), dFdy(vSrc))| / texelArea_m², 40)`, blended ONE/ONE. Flat water gives exactly 1, and overlapping folds add. Then `glGenerateMipmap`.
- **Key light:** `water_key_light`, which may be the moon. The pass is skipped when caustics are off, there is no key, or the key is below the horizon. That keeps `water-night-caustic`'s dark leg at exactly 0 px.
- **Reference plane:** 3.0 m below the level (`WATER_CAUSTIC_PLANE_M`). That is the focal length of the short-band ripples this window exists for, (n/((n-1)·a·k²)) ≈ 2.5-5 m.
- **Sampler:** new `WATER_CAUSTIC_UNIT 3` (free), added to `water.h`'s collision asserts. `water_frag` goes to 12 of 16. Programs register in `program.c`/`engine.c` next to `water_probe`.

**Consumer in `water_frag`** (replaces the Jacobian block and `WATER_CAUSTIC_ON/FULL/GAIN`):
1. **Bed world position:** from `sceneDepthTex` at the final `refrUV`, via the existing `viewPosFromLinZ` path. No depth means c = 1.
2. **Registration:** walk the bed point along the flat-water refracted key ray to the traced floor height, using the baked bed or the plane, in 2 fixed-point steps. The shift is zero when the fragment is the baked bed itself.
3. **Lookup:** `c = textureGrad(...)` with derivatives ×2 (LOD bias +1).
4. **Fade c → 1** at the window edge, in the first 0.35 m of depth, and, on plane-fallback fragments only, over the existing 9-20 m deep fade.
5. **Key share:** `f = Ek/(Ek+Esky)`, where `Ek = sunRadiance·max(sunDir.y,0)·vis` and `vis` is the outermost-cascade shadow at the bed times `cloudSunForSlot(bedPos)`. `Esky` comes from the prefiltered environment's top mip.
6. **Apply:** `bed *= 1 + f·(c − 1)`. This darkens as well as brightens.

**Look decisions for you (A/B crops, not defaults):**
- **Dispersion:** 3 colour-mask passes at IOR 1.3315/1.3335/1.3365, at 3× raster cost. Ships single-pass unless you pick it.
- **A π in `Esky`:** the existing in-scatter omits it, and including it changes caustic strength.

## Phases

Every phase that changes the picture ends by looking at `water_fixture` (fft and gerstner) and `beach_fixture --water-bed dome`, with crops shown to you. Gates run with `--only water` / `--only beach` between phases; the full suite runs once at the end.

0. **Look at today, and measure the floor.** Crop today's caustics on `water_fixture --water-waves fft` and on the beach dome; these are the "before" crops 13.0 asks for. Render one config twice for the noise floor. Branch `13.2-refracted-grid-caustics`, and commit the spec alone first.
1. **`oceanShadingNormal` refactor only.** Expect 0 px on both goldens and the FFT fixture. Anything else is investigated before moving on.
2. **The grid pass, reference plane only.** Add `--water-caustic-debug 2` (the raw target, like clearwater's `?view=caus`) and `--water-caustic-probe` (reads the target back and prints mean/min/max/clamped% over the inner 80%). Add the new arm **`water-caustic-energy`**: calm water within 1 ± 1e-3 everywhere; Gerstner and FFT |mean − 1| < 0.02. Look at the raw pattern.
3. **The consumer.** Compute f, delete the old code, add plane registration, and add `--water-caustic-debug 1` (c on the bed). **A/B crops against Phase 0.** Keep `water-caustic`'s FFT leg, add a Gerstner leg with the threshold set from measurement, and check the `water-night-caustic` legs still hold.
4. **Baked-bed tracing** and bed-aware registration, with an A/B on the beach dome, and the energy arm's dome leg (< 0.05). Then the dispersion and π A/Bs, for your decision.
5. **Wrap-up.** Once you've approved how they look, re-bake the Gerstner goldens (`water_fixture`, `water_submerged`), which now show caustics. Time the pass (fall back to G = 384 if it's expensive). Update docs: `AGENTS.md` (the Water shader entry, the water sampler ledger), `docs/cli-reference.md` (new flags), `docs/verification.md` if the goldens moved, and 13.0's "already shipped" row. Run the full gates and goldens once.

## Risks

- **Cost:** about 0.5M tiny triangles with additive blending. Time it; fall back to G = 384 if needed.
- **The 40 clamp** leaks energy on steep seas; the energy arm measures how much.
- **The plane fallback** focuses as if every fragment were 3 m deep. It is correct in registration only; the deep fade limits how far that shows.
- **Gerstner goldens change**, deliberately.
- **Precision:** `vSrc` must stay window-relative.

## Verification

- **The numbers:** `water-caustic-energy` (calm exactly 1, seas mean ≈ 1), `water-caustic` on both models, and `water-night-caustic` (dark exactly 0 px, moonlit and day ≥ 8000).
- **The look, which no arm replaces:** debug modes 1 and 2 plus A/B crops against Phase 0 on `water_fixture` fft and gerstner and on `beach_fixture --water-bed dome`, shown to you at Phases 2, 3 and 4.
- **Commands:** `./build.sh`, `python3 scripts/gates.py --only water`, `--only beach` per phase, then the full `gates.py` and `goldens.py` once at the end.
