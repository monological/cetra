# Spec 11.107 — Nothing fails silently

## Context

The second of the three API specs planned in `specs/11.106-the-public-surface.md` (that file
carries the shared preamble, the measurements behind the series, and the outline of 11.108;
this one is written against master at 36e5b294, after 11.106 merged). 11.106 gave the engine a
public surface and one naming convention; the trapdoors it measured are still open, and each one
renders a plausible frame or dies where nothing is watching:

- Every app calls `engine_update_view` and `engine_update_projection` at the end of its
  pre-render hook (every app, plus `app.c:232-233` inside the drag controller and a deferral
  in `config_snapshot.c`). Forget them and the camera moves while the picture does not.
  Three apps only call them through the drag controller, which they skip whenever ImGui holds
  the pointer, so a window resized during a GUI drag keeps the old aspect until the next drag.
- `camera_set_position` / `camera_set_look_at` store (11.106 said so in the header, deferring
  the orbit re-derive here), so four sites re-derive `distance` / `theta` / `phi` by hand, two
  of them near-copies of `camera_sync_spherical_from_position` with different clamping.
- A mesh that was never uploaded passes the draw list: the filter at `draw_list.c:230` tests
  `vao == 0`, but `create_mesh` generates the VAO at `mesh.c:74`, so the test is unreachable and
  the mesh draws from empty buffers. Twelve app sites call `node_upload_meshes` to avoid that,
  and the importer relies on the app remembering (`apps/render/src/render.c:3581`).
- The draw list drops a mesh for six reasons across two lines (`draw_list.c:230,232`) and logs
  none of them. A triangle mesh on the `shape` program (`layout(lines) in`) is a
  `GL_INVALID_OPERATION` at draw that nothing checks. Nothing anywhere tests a draw mode against
  the program that will draw it.
- `mesh_upload` measures wind extremes and bone bounds but not the AABB, so 17 app sites call
  `mesh_compute_aabb`; 15 repeat what the generator already did. A triangle mesh with no
  normals uploads unlit; the importer omits `aiProcess_GenSmoothNormals` and dereferences
  `mNormals` unguarded at `import.c:898` and `:912` while every neighbouring stream is guarded.
- `scene_mark_materials_dirty` is documented as the required hook for a material attached
  mid-run and has zero callers; `materials_dirty` is set only by `create_scene` and
  `scene_set_root`, so such a material is never registered: no variant resolution, no mask
  array, no config row, no free.
- A scene with no light, no environment and zero ambient renders black without a word. The
  clear colour is one literal in a table row (`engine.c:109`), and `apps/sprites` builds an
  80-unit black quad to stand in for the setter it lacks (`sprites.c:249-267`, comment
  included). `engine_run` with a NULL render callback runs the whole frame and draws nothing,
  and so does `game_run` with no `on_render`. `create_scene` starts with no root, so eight apps
  and the importer make one, and `scene_set_root` leaks whatever it replaces.

Non-goals: settings sections, the deletion of plain-store setters, the 2D preset's growth, the
canvas controller, header overviews — all 11.108. No new gate group or golden: verification is
the existing suites, the captures, and a throwaway consumer program in the scratchpad for the
paths no in-tree app exercises.

## Design

### 2. The camera, derived by the engine

`engine_run` derives the view and projection matrices itself, once per frame, immediately after
the transform-walk block that contains the pre-render hook (`engine.c:2669-2681`) and before the
GI capture (`:2693`) and the shadow pass (`:2702`, which reads `engine->view_matrix` at
`shadow.c:1576-1578` and `camera->aspect_ratio` at `:1581` — so BOTH derivations must precede
it). The body is `engine_update_view` + `engine_update_projection` (`engine.c:1228-1254`)
verbatim, the 0x0-window guard included, as one static `_engine_derive_camera`. Unconditional
on the scene: the matrices describe the camera, not what it looks at. Nothing between the top
of the frame and that point reads either matrix or the aspect (checked: the origin shift reads
the position, the sky tick and the water sim read neither), and the load-time probe captures
substitute and restore both matrices themselves (`render.c:1974-1978,2110-2111`) under one
cascade, so the init-time calls the apps make today are dead too.

Deleted: the public pair (`engine.h:539-540`); `app.c:232-233`; `config_snapshot.c`'s
`camera_moved` deferral (`:92-105`, `_apply_camera_vec` `:399-406`, the three `CFG_ROW_FN`
rows `:611-613` become plain `CFG_ROW`, the discharge `:1671-1683`), which `run_config_gate`'s
own docstring already records as 0 px on every arm (`gates.py:16070-16073`; that prose is
rewritten, and `gate-docs` reads arm names, not prose); the app call sites
(`render.c:1920,2369,3829,3908`, `forest.c:2841-2842`, `player.c:132-133`,
`network.c:118-119`, `shapes.c:147-148,229-230`, `sprites.c:179-180`, pcb `main.c:272-273,
399-400`); the README template's `pre_render` (`README.md:261-265`). The resize callback
(`engine.c:1081`) calls the same static instead of the deleted public function: events are
polled at the END of the frame (`:2833`), and a click arriving in the same batch as a resize
ray-picks through `camera->aspect_ratio`, which would otherwise be a frame stale. Contract in
`engine.h` beside the hook typedefs: the frame renders the camera as it stands when
`pre_render` returns; a pose written later, from `render`, is a frame late.

**Orbit parameters follow the pose.** `camera_sync_spherical_from_position` (`camera.c:212-226`)
gains the `asinf` argument clamp `render.c:1912` had and the body lacked, and runs at the end
of `camera_set_position`, `camera_set_look_at` and (already) `create_camera`. It stays public,
for one caller with a reason: the drag controller re-derives at drag start (`app.c:62-77`),
because the GUI's orbit sliders write the three fields directly and a drag must continue from
the pose on screen, not from a slider. That block's inline copy of the derivation becomes a
call plus three copies into the controller's own `orbit_start_*` / `free_*` fields. The orbit
moves (`camera_orbit`, `camera_pan`, `camera_zoom_toward_target`, `camera_enforce_max_distance`)
write `position` directly from the parameters they own, so nothing recurses and nothing drifts:
manual orbit and auto-orbit compute a position from `theta` / `phi` / `distance` and the setter
re-derives the same three from it; a pan translates eye and target together and the derived
distance is unchanged; the FREE-look path computes from controller-local yaw / pitch / distance
and the camera's three now simply describe the pose it lands on. Nothing in the render path
reads any of them (checked: DoF autofocus recomputes from the pose at `engine.c:1863-1866`), so
no golden can move. The config restore writes the pose and the orbit rows by offset, never
through the setters, so the concern at `config_snapshot.c:1675-1680` stays met.

Deleted with it: the two syncs in `mouse_drag_on_key` (`app.c:299,308`), which existed because
the setters did not sync and now duplicate them; `apply_explicit_pose`'s hand copy
(`render.c:1908-1914`); `player.c:128` and its comment at `:120-121`, which says DoF reads
`distance` and is wrong; the `distance` and `height` seeds `render.c:3165`, `render.c:3895-3896`,
`gametest.c:403`, `spores.c:225`. `height` has no reader anywhere (left for 11.108's banner) and
the distance seeds are overwritten at drag start and ignored by auto-orbit; their one live
effect was the first arrow-key orbit step, which snapped to the seed instead of continuing from
the pose. **One seed survives**: `render.c:3164`'s `theta = 0.60f` is the render app's
interactive auto-orbit elevation — auto-orbit reads `theta` and never writes it (`app.c:125`),
and the framed pose sits at ~7 degrees while the orbit runs at 34. It moves after the pose is
set (`:3776`), where the sync no longer erases it, with its reason beside it. Headless never
arms auto-orbit, so nothing measured sees any of this.

### 3. Upload at attach; the AABB and the normals; the importer

`Mesh.gpu_vertex_count` (0 = nothing resident) replaces the unreachable `vao == 0` test;
`mesh_upload` sets it last. `node_add_mesh` (`scene.c:916-933`) uploads when
`vertex_count > 0 && gpu_vertex_count == 0`: the GL context is live by construction (the mesh
has a VAO), and the epoch bump it already does covers the list. A mesh uploaded before attach
(`forest.c:514`, `terrain_quadtree.c:160`) is not uploaded twice; an in-place edit after attach
still calls `mesh_upload` itself (pcb's `edge_update`, `main.c:73-84`, the corpus's one such
site). Every attach in the tree already follows the array fill, the draw mode and the material
(audited: shapes, network, sprites, tree, gametest, spores, forest, the quadtree, pcb). The
header states the one ordering rule that survives: build the LOD chain before attaching, since
it rewrites the index array.

**`mesh_upload` computes the AABB** first, before the wind and bone measurements it already
takes — nothing else writes `Mesh.aabb`, same function over the same vertices, 0 px. The
redundant app calls go (`shapes.c:289,327`, `sprites.c:262`, `network.c:183`,
`ground.c:340` with its comment, pcb `main.c:83,287,300,314`, `import.c:1758`); the eight
inside `shapes.c`'s commented-out blocks go with the blocks. `mesh_compute_aabb` stays public
and gains the NULL-vertices guard it lacks (`mesh.c:203`). The generators' own calls stay: a
generated mesh that is never uploaded (forest's Jolt collider, `forest.c:1871`) still deserves
a correct box.

**Normals.** New `mesh_compute_normals(Mesh*)` in `mesh.c`: area-weighted vertex normals over a
`MESH_TRIANGLES` mesh, indexed or not, a vertex no non-degenerate triangle touches gets +Y.
`mesh_upload` calls it when `draw_mode == MESH_TRIANGLES && !normals && vertex_count > 0` and
logs once per mesh. No in-tree triangle mesh lacks normals (every generator writes them,
`rock.c` accumulates its own with a radial fallback and keeps it), so 0 px.

**The importer.** `import_ai_scene`'s flags (`import.c:1886-1887`) gain
`aiProcess_GenSmoothNormals`, which assimp applies only to a mesh that has none and orders
before `CalcTangentSpace`. `process_ai_mesh` guards `mNormals` the way it guards every other
stream (`:898-900`, `:912`): the normals buffer is allocated only when the stream exists (today
`:844` allocates unconditionally and `:847` reads a NULL buffer as an allocation failure that
empties the mesh), no normals means `mesh->normals` stays NULL, the tangent branch is skipped,
and the upload computes them — so the two paths agree on what a model without normals gets,
and neither is a null deref. The importer calls `mesh_upload` after
`mesh_build_lod_chain` (`import.c:1761`), where its own comment already says "before any
upload"; `node_upload_meshes` is deleted with its twelve sites (`tree.c:309,334,415,467,493`,
`network.c:189`, `render.c:3581`, `gametest.c:76,392` — the first is the explicit upload inside
`create_box_node`, now the attach's — `spores.c:232`, `shapes.c:451`, `sprites.c:267`, pcb
`main.c:488`). Its second job, filling each node's XYZ gizmo VBO
(`_upload_xyz_buffers_to_gpu_for_node`, `scene.c:1041-1067`), moves into `create_node`, which
already generates that VAO and VBO with a live context; the per-node `validate_program` call in
it is dropped (it validated against a program that is NULL at creation).

### 4. The draw list refuses loudly; draw mode versus program; materials register

**Once per mesh, naming the node and the reason.** `Mesh.draw_refusal_logged`; the refuse
branch in `append_node` (`draw_list.c:225-233`) becomes one place that logs the first refusal
of a mesh — never uploaded, no material, no program, a program whose uniform setup failed
(`uniforms == NULL`, the state `program.c:504` leaves), or geometry the program cannot take —
then skips as before. The node's name is NULL on several nodes (forest's scatter, the quadtree
patches, sprites' root), so the line guards it the way `scene.c:1303` does. The list is built
from three places — the shadow pass (`shadow.c:1439`), the frame (`render.c:1305`) and a probe
capture (`render.c:1912`) — and the flag makes the first of them the one that speaks.
`draw_list.h:117`'s contract line is corrected to the field that is now true, and `:121-122`
loses its `materials_dirty` sentence with the marker below.

**Draw mode versus program, declared by nothing.** `setup_program_uniforms` (`program.c:493`),
where `instanced` and `sampler_count` are already resolved from the linked program, queries
`GL_GEOMETRY_INPUT_TYPE` into `ShaderProgram.geometry_input` when any attached shader is a
`GEOMETRY_SHADER` (0 otherwise: any primitive). `program_accepts_draw_mode(program, mode)` maps
`GL_LINES` input to the three line modes, `GL_TRIANGLES` to the three triangle modes,
`GL_POINTS` to points, and refuses everything else (`MeshDrawMode` has no adjacency members).
`shape` is the one built-in with a geometry stage (`shape_geo.glsl:2`, `layout(lines) in`);
pcb puts only line modes on it and the shapes app's shape-program meshes are all commented out,
so nothing in the corpus is refused. Every creation path attaches before it links, so the
shader list is populated when the query runs; `reload_program_from_paths` (`program.c:226-323`)
re-derives `instanced` on its own and takes the same query, though nothing calls it. The draw
list is the one checker, through the once-per-mesh log; `submit_draw_run` (`render.c:937-940`)
stays unguarded.

**Materials attached mid-run register themselves.** `Material.registered`, set by
`scene_add_material`, which then needs no linear scan (a material belongs to the first scene
that registers it — `free_scene` already frees every entry, so sharing one across scenes was
a double free before this). `append_node` registers an unregistered material with the scene
`draw_list_build` already receives: one branch per item. `scene_sync_materials` stays for the
first frame and a replaced root: it runs at `engine.c:2732`, before the SSS gate and the
mask-array build (`:2784`) read the registry, and with shadows off the frame's first list build
is `engine_render_scene`'s, after both. A material that arrives mid-run is registered at the
frame's first build — the shadow pass's when shadows are on, the frame's otherwise — and its
mask layers pack that frame or the next, where today it is registered never.
`scene_mark_materials_dirty` is deleted (definition `scene.c:270`, declaration and its
contract paragraph `scene.h:410-416`).

### 5. Clear colour, the lightless warning, the self-rendering loop, the default root

**Clear colour.** `Engine.clear_color`, a `vec3` defaulting to the 0.1 grey that
`_gbuffer_attachments` row 0 (`engine.c:109`) now reads; alpha stays 1. A `CFG_VEC3` row,
`"engine" / "clear_color"` — the first vec3 with owner `CFG_ENGINE`, which the generic
store already handles, and `config-perturb` moves a [0,1] value within the band. `apps/sprites`
drops the backdrop quad, its material and node (`sprites.c:249-267`) and sets black; the key
light stays (the motes tint by it). The capture MOVES: the quad was a black-albedo PBR surface
under a white key and white ambient, whose rough dielectric lobe is not zero, where the clear
is exactly zero. Measured and recorded in the ledger; sprites' other pixels are what the
capture is for.

**Lightless scene.** In `engine_render_scene`, after `scene_build_emissive_lights`
(`render.c:1328-1331`, so a scene lit by derived panels is not warned) and once per scene
(`Scene.lightless_warned`): `light_count == 0`, no `ibl`, no `sky`, and zero
`ambient_radiance` warns that unlit surfaces will render black (emissive still shows). The
count, not the emitted intensity: a zero-intensity light is the documented way a scene file
declines the default rig (`AGENTS.md:1353-1357`; `flare_fixture` and `oit_cards_fixture` author
one and nothing else, on purpose), and a scene that authored a light has answered the question.
The three apps that run lightless on purpose (network, shapes, pcb) do so under the 2D preset,
whose white ambient is exactly the condition that keeps them quiet.

**A loop that renders.** `engine_run` with `render == NULL` calls
`engine_render_scene(engine, current_scene)` (`engine.c:2798-2800`, keeping the
`current_scene != NULL` half, since the renderer logs every frame on a NULL scene);
`game_scene_render` (`game.c:270-278`) does the same with the scene the engine handed it when
`on_render` is NULL, instead of discarding both. Six apps whose render callback is
`engine_render_scene` plus a root-node guard pass NULL and lose the function (sprites, network,
tree, shapes, forest, pcb); spores keeps its transform probe, gametest its cull-face bracket,
render its bone overlay.

**A scene has a root.** `create_scene` creates one named "root" after the draw list, and a
`create_node` failure frees the scene and returns NULL the way the draw list's does;
`scene_set_root` frees the previous root and its subtree when handed a different one, and
marks materials dirty as before. The eight app sites that build a root (`forest.c:2327-2330`,
`tree.c:1256-1259`, `gametest.c:279-282`, `spores.c:157-160`, `network.c:164-167`,
`sprites.c:228-231`, `shapes.c:236-249`, pcb `main.c:402-409`) use `scene->root_node`; the
importer's direct write (`import.c:1933-1934`) goes through `scene_set_root` so the auto-root is
freed. This gives `create_scene` a precondition it did not have: `create_node` generates the
gizmo VAO and VBO, where the texture pool, the draw list and the shadow system (lazy at
`shadow.c:1549`) touch no GL. Every caller already holds an engine, and a Scene joins Mesh and
SceneNode among the things created under a live context; the header says so.

## Risks

- A camera moved from `render` is a frame late; the header says so and no app does it.
- The spherical fallback at `camera.c:220` (`dist < 0.001` → 1000) can fire transiently
  between a caller's `camera_set_position` and its `camera_set_look_at` when the new eye lands
  on the old target, and the second call corrects it; the engine's own moves write position
  directly and never pass through it.
- Deleting the distance seeds changes the first arrow-key orbit step before a drag in three
  apps: the camera continues from its pose rather than snapping to a seeded distance.
  Interactive only, nothing measured sees it, recorded here rather than preserved. The GUI's
  orbit sliders keep exactly their current reach: honoured by auto-orbit and the arrow keys,
  overwritten at drag start.
- `create_scene` now needs a live GL context. No caller in the tree lacks one; a consumer that
  built scenes before its engine could not have attached a mesh to them anyway.
- `node_add_mesh` uploading means a mesh attached before its arrays are filled uploads empty
  and needs an explicit `mesh_upload` after; no in-tree site does this (pcb's edges are
  calloc'd first and re-uploaded by `edge_update`).
- A driver reporting no `GL_GEOMETRY_INPUT_TYPE` leaves `geometry_input` 0 and the check
  inert, not wrong.
- The sprites capture moves where the quad was; the ledger records the count and the reason.
- `create_scene` allocating a node means one VAO and one VBO per scene that nothing draws
  unless `show_xyz` is on; 11.108's xyz item is where the per-node gizmo buffers may leave the
  node altogether.
- `scene_set_root` freeing the previous root frees a subtree an app may still hold a pointer
  into; no app replaces a root after building under it.

**Effort:** 4–5 days.
