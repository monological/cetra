# cetra 14.31: a baseline for a draw hook

## Context

pcb 7.4.2's board modal shows an impedance profile's line under its stackup: a picture of the line
and a graph of Z against frequency, each a panel painted by a draw hook, with a label under it. The
layout stands both midway between the stackup's box and the labels. The graph fills its panel, and
the frequencies along its foot are its own words, drawn with `ui_draw_text`, so it keeps a row for
them at the bottom of its panel.

That row is a line tall, from `ui_line_height`, and the words stand on a baseline an ascent below
its top. The rest of the line, below the baseline, holds no ink (the frequencies have no
descenders), so the graph reads about 3 points high in its room: 17 pixels from the box to its
title, 30 from its frequencies to the label under it.

A hook that draws words can ask how tall their line is, and not where in it they stand. The layout
knows: a label's baseline is its top padding and its face's ascent (`UIElement.baseline`, spec
14.13), and `ui_draw_text` sets a line an ascent below the top it is given, through
`ui_text_baseline` in the private `ui_text.h`. Nothing public says it.

Branch and spec: `14.31-a-baseline-for-a-draw-hook`, off cetra's master (`1ceee35b`).

## The design

One function in `ui.h`, beside `ui_line_height`:

```c
// How far below a line's top its baseline stands: its face's ascent at `size` points, where
// ui_draw_text sets a line it is given the top of. 0 with no face.
float ui_line_ascent(const Font* font, float size);
```

- **One source for the baseline.** `ui_text_baseline` becomes the top plus `ui_line_ascent`, so
  the UI's own text and a hook's answer cannot disagree. Its guard is kept as it is: a text with no
  face, or none of its size, stands on its top, as `ui_line_ascent`'s 0 makes it.
- **No line spacing.** `ui_line_height` takes one because a taller line stands its next line
  further down; the first line's baseline is an ascent below the top whatever the spacing, as
  `ui_draw_text` and the label's baseline both place it.
- **0 with no face**, where `ui_line_height` answers the size: a height of 0 would collapse a line,
  while an ascent of 0 stands the text on its top, which is what the UI draws for a text with no
  face now.
- The face's ascent is `Font.ascent`, which `text.c` reads at load; a pixel-snapped face's is
  rounded there (`docs/ui.md`, The editor's face), and this reads whichever the face holds.

## Tests

**`ui-line-ascent`**, a new arm in the `ui` group, from a new probe case `line-ascent` in
`apps/gametest/src/ui_text_probe.c`:
- `ui_line_ascent` at 14 and at 28 points against the face's own fields, `Font.ascent` times the
  size over `Font.base_size`: the arm's oracle is the face, not the function.
- A label at each size with no padding: its first glyph's baseline, read back from the glyph quad
  as `ui-label-padding` reads it, within half a pixel of its top plus `ui_line_ascent`.
- `ui_line_ascent(NULL, 14)` is 0.

**Falsified**: `ui_line_ascent` returning the line height, the arm fails on the face's oracle and
the glyph's baseline both; `ui_text_baseline` returning to its own arithmetic with an extra point,
the arm fails on the glyph's baseline alone, and `ui-label-padding` with it.
