# 13.12 Rain: glass, drips, pooling, wind

## Context

Spec 13.9 shipped rain and recorded what it did not build. The user picked seven of those
follow-ups:

1. drops running down window glass, for silent's kitchen window;
2. water dripping off roof edges and gutters;
3. splashes on water surfaces;
4. puddles that form where a surface is actually low, switchable;
5. a separate mist forward lobe (`mist_g`);
6. the rain's wind taken from the scene's wind;
7. hand-set porosity per material in silent.

What exploration found, and what shapes the design:

- **The kitchen window is dry today.** Rain falls straight and the pane sits under a 0.4 m
  eave. So drops on glass need wind driving the rain at the house, and items 1 and 6 ship
  together.
- **When the rain stops, the cover map snaps to vertical**, which would leave the pane covered
  the moment the rain ends.
- **Silent has no gutters, downpipes or wind.** Its textures carry only albedo, normal and
  roughness.
- **The full lit shader is at 16/16 samplers**, so drops on glass must be pure arithmetic.
- **Thin glass samples the refraction at its own pixel**, so drops need an explicit
  screen-space shift to bend the view.
- **A splash lands on the bed under water**, because water is not in the occlusion map.
- **`Wind` has no air speed in m/s.**
- **A height map is already bound** for any non-layered material that has one, parallax or
  not.
- **Gating `heightTex` on the rain bit would break `rain-ledger`.** Puddles from height maps
  therefore get their own variant bit.
- **New shader chunks must be included inside the existing rain `#if` block.** Includes are
  spliced once, in order.
- **Silent's roughness-derived porosity is wrong** for painted siding, trim and doors, the
  slate roof, the car paint and the lit neighbour windows.

User decisions:
- fetch real height maps;
- a gutter and downpipe on the player's house, and bare-edge drips on the neighbours;
- a gusty breeze in silent, blowing from the street toward the house;
- `mist_g` defaults to the streaks' 0.8.

## Rules that apply throughout

- **Rain-off variants stay textually identical**, so all 31 goldens stay at 0 px. Every
  shader change sits inside a rain or relief `#if`. Where an existing line changes, the old
  line stays verbatim in the `#else`.
- **Each knob lands with its plumbing in the phase that adds it:**
  - `rain.h` field and default in `rain_init_defaults`;
  - a `RAIN_KEYS` row in `cetra/src/cscene.c`;
  - a `CFG_RAIN` row in `config_snapshot.c`, under the same key;
  - a Rain-panel control in `gui.c`;
  - a render-app flag where useful;
  - a line in `docs/cli-reference.md`.
- **Visual phases: look before gates.** Pinned headless screenshots of silent go to the user
  first; arms are written after the look is signed off. Silent gets screenshots only, no arms.
- **One commit per phase at least**, plus a spec as-built note per phase.
- **Gate runs:** `--only rainfall` plus the groups the phase can reach. The whole suite once,
  at the end.

## Phase 1 -- wind, the remembered direction, the mist lobe

- **`Wind.air_speed`** (`cetra/src/wind.h/c`): m/s, SETTINGS, default 0.
  - `.cscn` `wind.airSpeed` in `cscene.c`'s wind block (~624) and `cscene_apply.c`'s wind
    apply (~428).
  - `wind_gust(const Wind*, float t)`: a CPU twin of `wind.glsl:83`'s envelope.
- **Rain follows the scene wind.** New SETTINGS `Rain.follow_scene_wind` (default true).
  - ENGINE-OWNED `Rain.wind_mean` and `Rain.wind_now`, resolved each frame beside
    `rain_update` (`engine.c:3025`):
    - with a scene Wind whose `air_speed > 0`: `normalize(direction.xz) * air_speed`, and that
      times `wind_gust(t)` for `wind_now`;
    - otherwise `Rain.wind` for both.
  - `rain_fall_direction` and the cover map use `wind_mean`; the cover grid must not crawl.
    The streaks' `rainWind` uses `wind_now`.
- **ENGINE-OWNED `Rain.travel`**: the last falling direction, held while `rain_active`.
  - The cover map (`shadow.c` `shadow_render_rain_layer`) and splashes read it rather than
    `rain_fall_direction`, so a sheltered pane stays sheltered after the rain stops.
  - A NULL Rain stays straight down.
- **`Rain.mist_forward_g`** (default 0.8). `rain_publish_to_postfx` publishes it in place of
  `streak_forward_g`.
  - Rewrite the comment at `rain_render.c:218` that argued for one lobe.
  - The render probe prints both values.
  - Key `mistForwardG`.
- **Silent** (`apps/silent/src/silent.c`): a scene Wind blowing +z toward the house, about
  2.5 m/s with gusts.
- **Look:** slanted, gusting streaks; the lower window and the facades wetting.
- **Arms:**
  - `rain-wind`: travel tilts by atan(air speed / fall speed); `wind_now` gusts while the cover
    map stays put frame to frame.
  - `rain-mist-g`: the published g equals the knob, and the streak frames are unchanged.

### As built (commit `4aa07b13`; its two arms landed in `17055dfd`)

- **The cover reads the gust cycle's MEAN, not the air speed.** `wind_gust_mean` beside
  `wind_gust`: the envelope is `mix(1 - a, 1, s^3)` over `s = (1 + sin)/2`, whose mean is
  `1 - a + a * 5/16`. Reading `air_speed` alone would have tilted the cover by the gusts' peak,
  which the rain spends most of its time below.
- **Silent's wind:** 2.5 m/s at the peak, gusting at 0.6 rad/s by half, toward the house (+z).
  `--no-wind` turns it off.
- **The probe's `air` row** prints the air now, its mean, the travel and both lobes, which is
  what `rain-wind` reads. That arm also holds that a rain told not to follow falls along its own
  wind: straight down, at the fixture's zero.
- **`rain-mist-g` reads the misty frame, not the streaks.** Its first draft compared streak frames
  and moved 0%: with streaks on, the medium starts at the outermost streak box, 36 m out and past
  the whole fixture.

## Phase 4 -- drops on glass

- **New file `cetra/shaders/include/rain_glass.glsl`**, included inside the rain block after
  `rain_surface.glsl`. It declares no sampler.
  - `RainGlass rainGlassDrops(inout N, inout roughness, Ng, P, V, fragCoord)` returns a
    screen-space shift and its size in pixels.
  - It is called right after `rainWetSurface` (`pbr_frag.glsl:1563`), so Fresnel, opacity and
    lighting all see the beaded normal.
- **Gate**, all uniforms, so control flow stays uniform and `fwidth` stays legal:
  `uRainBeads > 0`, `rainWetness > 0`, and not the depth pass.
- **New `Material.rain_beads`**, a `MATERIAL_PARAMS` row, key `rainBeads`, default -1:
  derive 1 when `transmission > 0`, else 0. Uploaded beside `uPorosity` in `render.c`.
- **The pane frame:** the struck side is `Nf`, and down is gravity projected onto the plane.
- **Exposure:**
  - Looked up on BOTH sides, `P ± Nf * RAIN_NORMAL_OFFSET`, and the larger wins. A thin slab
    seen from inside still shows the drops on its outside face.
  - Weighted by how squarely the rain strikes the pane.
  - `rainWetSurface` returns its own exposure as an out parameter, so one lookup is shared.
- **Beads:**
  - Two layers of jittered cells, 16 mm and 7 mm.
  - Present while `hash < fill * wetness * reach`, so they evaporate one by one as the
    surface dries.
  - A life cycle on an ENGINE-OWNED `Rain.bead_clock`, which advances at the ripple
    activity. Its wrap is a constant in `rain_constants.glsl`.
- **Running drops:**
  - 6 cm lanes, each with a hashed speed and a stick-slip motion, wobbling and leaving a
    trail.
  - They need slope: none on horizontal glass.
  - They stop with the rain and wipe the beads in their path.
- **Optics:**
  - Paraboloid caps tilt the normal.
  - Roughness drops toward 0.05 inside a drop, so the water clears the smear.
  - Unresolved beads add roughness variance, standing in for specular AA, which ran earlier.
  - Each drop is a lens: the shift is the image at infinity through a deflected view, scaled
    by the SETTINGS knob `Rain.glass_lens` (1 = physical).
- **The transmission sample** (`pbr_frag.glsl:~2484`): under the rain `#if`, sample at
  `refrUV + shift` with the mip raised to the shift's footprint. The current line stays
  verbatim in the `#else`.
- **New uniforms in `rain_bind_surface`:** `rainTravel`, `rainBeadClock`, `rainGlassLens`.
- **Fix:** `wet_surface.glsl`'s hue power darkens a wet pane's tint. Pass
  `wet * (1 - transmission)` there, in rain variants only.
- **Opaque glass:** set `rain_beads` 1 on silent's `MAT_DARK_GLASS` and `MAT_WINDOW_LIT`. They
  get the normal and roughness only, since they have no refraction to shift.
- **Cost:** about 400 ALU and 21 taps per beaded glass fragment. Every other rain fragment pays
  one uniform compare.
- **Fixture:** `gen_rain_fixture.py` emits `rain_glass_fixture.gltf` and a variant `.cscn`
  with three thin panes: in the open, under the roof, and at the roof edge.
- **Look:** silent from the spawn, through the kitchen window at night; then pinned at the
  window; and the car glass from the street. Sign off first.
- **Arms:**
  - `rain-glass`: the open pane moves; the covered pane moves 0 px.
  - `rain-glass-inner`: the edge pane's sheltered face shows the drops.
  - `rain-glass-lens`: lens 1 against lens 0 moves the image through thin glass.
  - `rain-glass-dry`: `dryFor` 20 s keeps beads; 5000 s gives 0 px.
  - Determinism.

### As built

- **The cover is asked on the STRUCK side, not on both.** The plan had both sides looked up
  and the larger kept. The struck side is the face looking against the rain's travel. A
  fragment on that face reuses the cover `rainWetSurface` already read; one on the other face
  (a pane seen from indoors) asks for it 12 cm out on the struck side. What it is for is
  glass the rain falls ON -- a glass roof, a skylight, a sunroof -- which shelters its own
  underside: asked there, it would never bead as seen from below.
- **A wall pane does not shelter its own lee in the cover map, and two reasons given for the
  lookup on the way were wrong.** The first said a pane shelters the point just behind it; the
  second, that a window's room side is under the house's roof. Falsifying `rain-glass-inner`
  disproved both. With the lookup put back on the seen side, a vertical pane still beaded from
  inside, twice:
  - The rain meets a vertical pane nearly edge on. The map's slope-scaled offset (factor 2)
    stores it about 0.66 m deeper than it is, and the point 12 cm behind it is only 0.45 m
    deeper along the rain, so it reads as open sky.
  - Rain blowing in under an eave reaches the room side of a window just as it reaches the
    street side, below a line about 0.43 m under the eave per 12 cm in.
  A flat pane is stored about 5 cm deep under this rain and shelters the point under it. The
  arm therefore uses a flat glass canopy seen from below: 21.5% of its underside moves with the
  struck-side lookup and 0 px of 200,900 with the lookup on the seen side.
- **`Rain.glass_drop_size`** (key `glassDropSize`, 0.1..20, default 1) scales the beads' size
  and spacing together. At physical size a 16 mm bead is a pixel or two from across a room, so
  silent's kitchen window read as clean glass. Silent sets 2.5. A slope is a ratio of lengths, so
  the scale leaves every tilt and every bend as it was.
- **A dark rim, from total internal reflection.** Light from behind a bead meets the bead's
  surface past water's critical angle (48.6 degrees, slope 1.134) near the rim and is reflected
  back, so the transmission sample is darkened by `blocked` there. This is what outlines each
  bead against a lit street, and it is what made the drops readable in the first look.
- **The lens is not faded where the beads are unresolved, and the tilt is.** A field of beads
  finer than a pixel still bends the view by different amounts inside one pixel, and that spread
  is what raises the transmission's mip: a wet window seen from across a room is a diffuser. The
  tilt, by contrast, averages to roughness there.
- **Zero-weight steps are skipped rather than run with a zero weight.** A renormalised normal
  and the square root of a square are not the values they started as, so a pane the rain cannot
  reach ran a few bits off the bare glass. `rain-glass` holds the covered pane at 0 px.
- **Running drops do not wipe the beads in their path.** Where a drop's head or trail covers
  more of a point than the beads do, it is what shows; elsewhere the beads stay. They run on
  the frame's `time` rather than the bead clock, and stop with the rain because their fill is
  the ripple activity.
- **Silent:** `rain_beads` 1 on `MAT_DARK_GLASS` and `MAT_WINDOW_LIT`, as planned.
- **The fixture** is `rain_glass_fixture.gltf` plus `.cscn`, from `gen_rain_fixture.py`. It
  carries its own material list, so `rain_fixture.gltf` is byte-identical to before.
  - Three 6 mm panes facing +z, all on `rain_glass`: transmission 1, thickness 0, IOR 1.5.
  - The open pane at z 4 and the covered one at z -4 under the roof's middle, both vertical,
    and a flat 2 m canopy at 2.4 m in the open between the roof and the camera.
  - **The cover map's resolution is coarse under an eave.** The map is 1024 texels over 96 m,
    9.4 cm each, and rain drifting 0.28 m per metre of fall turns that into about a third of a
    metre of vertical blur on a wet line under an eave. A vertical pane placed 0.3 m inside the
    roof's edge put an arm's band 0.1 m from that line, and it moved 4 px it should not have.
  - **Seen against even sky, a bead's lens shows nothing.** Looking level through a pane at a
    uniform background read 1.4%: only the dark rims showed.
- **`rain_settle_dry` takes the scene's `Wind`**, so a stopped rain in a scene that follows its
  wind keeps the direction that wind gave it. The `.cscn` installs the wind before the rain.

## Phase 5 -- drips, and silent's gutter

- **API in `rain.h`:**
  - `RainDripLine {from, to, rate, ground}`; a point source has `from == to`.
  - `RAIN_DRIP_MAX` (32) in `rain_constants.glsl`.
  - BY FUNCTION: `rain_set_drip_lines` copies and clamps, and warns past the cap.
  - SETTINGS: `drip_count` (slots, default 512).
  - `rain_draws(rain)` is true while it is falling, or while it is active and has lines and
    slots.
  - `rain_settle_dry(rain, seconds)` is "stopped this long ago", for tests and scenes.
- **Gating:** `engine.c:2289` and `rain_render_drops` use `rain_draws`. When the rain is not
  falling, only drips draw. Drips skip the cover term.
- **CPU, per frame, in `rain_render.c`:**
  - Slots are shared out by `rate x max(len, 0.25)`, largest remainder first, at least one per
    line.
  - The fall time at each end is closed form: free fall capped at the terminal speed of a
    4.5 mm drop.
  - The period is `max(1.5 s, fall time + splash life + 0.25)`.
  - The fire chance comes from the rate times the flow. The flow is the rate over the
    reference while it rains, and half the wetness after.
  - Landing height is `max(ground, rainWaterLevel)`.
- **Uniforms:** `uniform vec4 dripFrom/dripTo/dripTime[RAIN_DRIP_MAX]` plus `dripLines`. The
  `.w` components carry the cumulative slot end and the landing height.
- **`rain_vert.glsl`:**
  - A third instance range after the splash droplets: `1 + RAIN_SPLASH_DROPLETS` instances per
    slot. `main()` becomes a three-way bounded test.
  - `dripInstance` finds its line by linear search over the slot ends.
  - Its cycle: a bead forming (growing with the cube root of time, drawn as a dot), then
    falling (vertical, drifted by `rainWind`, drawn as a streak), then the crown via
    `crownDroplet`.
  - `standsFor` is 1.
- **`.cscn`:** `rain.drips: [{from, to, rate, ground}]`, plus `dripCount` and `dryFor`.
  - The config snapshot carries `dripCount` but not the lines, which are authored like probes.
- **Silent (`apps/silent/src/house.c`, `houses.c`):**
  - **A gutter on the player's house** along the front eave, using kit geometry: a box
    channel, plus a `kit_frame_pipe` downpipe at one end.
  - **Its drip lines:**
    - two leaking joints, point sources at rate about 3;
    - a sagging overflow, a 0.3 m line at rate about 15, placed so it falls past the kitchen
      window;
    - a downpipe spout near the ground, at rate about 40, which reads as a stream.
  - **Neighbours:** front-eave and porch-roof edges as lines at rate 1. The total stays under
    the cap.
- **Look:** drips past the window from inside; the spout and the eaves from the street. Sign
  off first.
- **Arms:**
  - `rain-drips`: a line on a variant moves the column under it; the sky box and the open
    ground move 0 px.
  - `rain-drips-dry`: `dryFor` 20 s still drips; 5000 s gives 0 px.
  - `rain-drips-water`: a ground below the water level lands on the water.
  - `rain-drips-determinism`.
  - The fixture's default scene stays drip-free, so the existing exact-1 arms stay clean.

### As built (commits `86afa509` and `282b0720`)

- **`RAIN_DRIP_MAX` is 48, not 32.** Silent's street needs up to 34 lines: ten neighbours, each
  an eave split round its porch plus the porch edge, and four lines on the house. The three
  vec4 arrays then take 576 of the 1024 components GL guarantees the vertex stage.
- **Slots are shared by rate times cycle, not rate times length.** A line's rate is already for
  its whole length, so the plan's weighting counted length twice: the neighbours' eaves took
  most of the slots and capped the kitchen overflow at a third of its rate. Weighted by the
  drops it keeps in the air, every slot of every line drips with the same chance.
  `rain_drip_period` is the one statement of the cycle, read by the sharing and the renderer.
- **The fall is linear drag, not free fall capped at the terminal speed.** It approaches the
  terminal speed smoothly and has a closed-form position. The time to fall a height is solved by
  Newton, the same way on the CPU and in the vertex stage. Drips honour `fall_scale` as the
  streaks do.
- **Drips fall straight down; the plan drifted them with the wind.** Silent's breeze blows toward
  the house, and a gutter's drop carried with the open air for its 1.1 s fall crossed the wall
  under it into the kitchen. The air in a wall's lee is broken anyway.
- **`Rain.drip_brightness`** (key `dripBrightness`, silent 16). One drop smeared over the
  shutter covers about 3% of the pixels it crosses; a streak stands for hundreds of drops and a
  drip for one, so the two need separate scales.
- **Drops take light from area panels** (`282b0720`). `dropLit` skipped them, so the drips past
  silent's kitchen window took no light from its tubes. A panel lights a drop as a small source:
  luminance times area, foreshortened, over the distance squared, floored at the area. It is
  unshadowed like every light the drops take, so it reaches drops behind the walls round its
  window too.
- **The look took a debug pass.** At night the drips are as bright as the fog behind them, and
  the first screenshots showed nothing anyone could point at. Drawing them solid red and 6 px
  wide, uncommitted, showed the overflow column falling where it should. The user asked for the
  overflow to go from 15 drops a second to 40, and for the tubes to light the drops.
- **Silent:**
  - The gutter is a box channel hung on the fascia along the whole front eave, with a downpipe
    swan-necked back to the wall at x 4.75 and down to a shoe.
  - Leaks: two joints at 3 drops a second (one over the porch, one past the front room's
    window), the overflow over the kitchen window (0.3 m at 40), and the shoe at 40.
  - The neighbours' bare eaves drip at 0.6 a second a metre, split round the porch roof, which
    drips from its own edge at 1 a metre.
  - `Drips` collects the lines while the street is built and hands them to the rain, and adds
    them without drawing from the street's RNG, so every seed builds the same street as before.

## Phase 6 -- puddles from low points (switchable)

- **Knob `Rain.puddle_relief`**, 0..1, default 0, which is today's noise exactly. Key
  `puddleRelief`; render flag `--rain-relief <f>`.
- **Bit `PBR_FEAT_RELIEF 128`**, with `PBR_FEAT_ALL` going to 255.
  - The resolver (`render.c ~798`) sets it per material when the scene has the rain bit,
    `puddle_relief > 0`, a `height_tex` exists, and the material is not layered.
  - `heightTex`'s declaration gate (`pbr_frag.glsl:149`) becomes `PARALLAX || LAYERS || RELIEF`.
    Only relief variants pay the sampler.
- **In the rain block:**
  - `relief = puddle_relief * RAIN_RELIEF_RANGE * (height(uv) - coarsest-mip mean)`, at the
    post-parallax uv. The mean keeps the average coverage the same.
  - `rainWetSurface` takes `relief`: `depth = level - noise - relief`.
  - Without the bit `relief` is the literal 0.0.
  - The noise still places the basins; the height map shapes their edges and fills the lows
    first.
  - Check that resolved height maps carry mips; if not, use 0.5.
- **Layered materials** keep noise only. Their unit 4 holds the virtual-texture page.
- **Silent:**
  - `apps/silent/tools/fetch_textures.py` fetches displacement as `<stem>_height.png` for the
    asphalt, the sidewalk and the yard: 256², single channel, white = raised.
  - `mats.c` loads them into `height_tex`.
  - Silent turns relief on, with a flag to turn it off.
- **Fixture:** a twin, `rain_relief_fixture`, from `gen_rain_fixture.py`. Its UV'd grounds
  carry a trench-and-plateau height map and a white albedo.
- **Look:** the road and the pavement in silent with relief on and off. Sign off first.
- **Arms:**
  - `rain-relief`: in roughness view, the trench centres puddle and the plateaus mostly do not;
    at relief 0 the two agree.
  - `rain-relief-off`: on `rain_fixture`, relief 1 against 0 is 0 px, and no variant carries
    bit 128.
  - `rain-relief-ledger`: the relief variant declares the rain variant's samplers plus one.

### As built (commits `35cdae00` and `9bfdc5b7`)

- **The height sweep had been broken since spec 12.8, and is fixed in its own commit.** The
  engine pairs a material with `<albedo stem>_height.png` and turns POM on, but it handed the
  loader the candidate's full path. The loader resolves against the pool's directory and falls
  back to stripping leading segments, which can never reach `../textures`. So no height map in
  the tree resolved, and `parallax_fixture` has rendered without POM since the assets split by
  kind. The loader now gets the path past the pool's own directory. Goldens stay 31 at 0 px.
- **Silent's maps are `_disp.png`, not the plan's `_height.png`.** The jars' contents share the
  grass's albedo, so the sweep gave them the grass's height map and turned on POM in the
  kitchen. A `_disp` file is loaded by `mats.c` and never found by the sweep.
- **Mips are there.** The pool builds every texture's chain on the CPU. The arm would have seen
  a missing chain anyway: the coarsest mip would equal the texel, and the relief would be 0.
- **Coverage is close to the same, not the same.** On the fixture the noise's own share is
  about 61% at relief 0 and about 55% at relief 1: 99% of the trenches and 12% of the
  plateaus. The comment in `pbr_frag` says the map lowers as much ground as it raises, which is
  what the mean buys, and claims no more.
- **The fixture** is the yard with its two ground halves replaced by one UV'd plane carrying a
  band map: a trench across the middle half of every 4 m repeat along z. Symmetric about the
  repeat's centre, so a V flip in the importer cannot move a band. Seen from above in the
  roughness view, at puddle scale 0.5 m so every band holds many puddles. The arms pass
  `--no-parallax`, since the sweep arms POM for the plane and POM declares the same sampler.
- **Silent's look:** the red A/B (relief at 0 and 1, puddles painted red uncommitted) shows the
  pavement's joints filling and the asphalt's and the yard's puddle edges breaking up and
  speckling. The relief is 1 pending the user's verdict on that speckle.
- **Falsified:** dropping `relief` from the depth fails `rain-relief` (62.4% against 59.6%), and
  a resolver that does not ask for a height map fails `rain-relief-off` (`pbr-192` on the
  plain fixture). The ledger's mutation has not run. Two things were learned getting there:
  - **A declaration with no read anywhere is not counted.** Declaring `heightTex` in every rain
    variant left `pbr-64` at 12. AGENTS.md's "declarations, not uses" was measured with the
    reads still present and folded away, and holds only in that form.
  - **A new sampler cannot be the mutation.** It takes the full mask to 17 and the uber-shader
    fails to link, which fails every arm. Reading `sheenTex` from the relief branch takes
    `pbr-192` to 14 and leaves the full mask at 16.

### Review (after Phase 6)

Two reviews ran in the background over the whole branch: a strict structural audit in four
areas, and a four-angle cleanup pass (reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude). What changed:

- **The drip CPU half is one function.** `rain_drip_schedule` fills a `RainDripSchedule` -- each
  line's slots, landing height, cycle and chance, and the drips' terminal speed. The four public
  helpers are now static: `rain_drip_flow`, `rain_drip_period`, `rain_drip_slots` and
  `rain_drip_fall_time`. The renderer only packs it.
- **Two fixes, which change behaviour as the spec meant it:**
  - On flooded ground the slots were shared by the dry cycle while the chance used the wet one.
    Both now use the same landing height, so every slot drips with the same chance there as well.
  - A rain at `fallScale` 0 drips nothing. It used to hand the vertex stage a zero terminal speed,
    and the fall time came out NaN.
- **The vertex stage:**
  - A `Drop` carries its own brightness and whether it asks the cover, so `main` no longer asks
    which range an instance came from.
  - The splashes and the drips share one slot cycle, `slotCycle`.
  - `dripSpeed` is the one statement of the speed under drag.
  - Each line is three uniforms: its two ends, and one vec4 of cycle, chance, slot end and
    landing height.
  - "No water" is a single level far below the world, not a flag beside a level.
- **The lit surface:**
  - The relief is `rainWetSurface`'s own business. The height read, its constant and its uniform
    are inside the relief `#if` in `rain_surface.glsl`, which includes `pbr_features.glsl` as the
    convention says.
  - `rain_glass.glsl` is included beside `rain_surface.glsl` in the one rain block. It no longer
    stands down in the moment and A2C prepasses, so a beaded BLEND material computes its opacity
    from the same normal in every pass, which `pbr_frag` requires.
  - The transmission sample through the drops is `rainGlassTransmit`.
  - The bead and run layers are skipped where nothing can reach the glass, and the lens is
    skipped on glass that transmits nothing. Both are bit-identical.
  - The bead hash is `rain_ripples.glsl`'s `rainCellHash`. It is integer-only, so water stays
    0 px.
- **`Material.rain_beads`** is a labelled int: `auto`, `off` or `on`. As a float, any positive
  value acted as 1.
- **The relief bit** is decided in `_material_pbr_features`, beside the layered refusal it shares.
- **`rain_settle_dry`** calls `rain_update` rather than re-deriving the drying. It is
  bit-identical.
- **The import fix's path inverse** is `path_under_base` in `util.c`, beside the join it inverts.
- **`parse_drips`** hands the rain an array as long as its count.
- **Silent:**
  - `Kit` owns the drip lines. `Drips`, its parameter on six functions and the two header
    includes are gone.
  - The gutter derives its reach, fascia, downpipe and leaks from the front wall, its openings,
    the porch and the eave.
  - `house_gable_roof` returns its eave height.
  - One `RAIN` table in `mats.c` states the porosity, the relief and the beads per surface. The
    jars and bottles say `off`, since they are indoors.
  - The street view moves 4 px of about 2 million, from the drip speed's rounding.
- **The gates and the generator:**
  - `variant` takes a camera and a rain update, and `shot` renders one.
  - `quad_moved`, `apart` and `_inscribed_rect` replace six near-copies.
  - The splash-on-water arm is the splash arm on the flooded twin.
  - The drip fall-time and gust-mean twins integrate numerically, rather than repeating the C's
    closed forms.
  - The puddle threshold is read from the shader constants.
  - The glass twin carries its own camera, and its canopy is named one.
  - The generator gives each piece a geometry, which deletes the `relief=` special case and the
    relief glTF's orphan meshes. The other two glTFs regenerated byte-identical.
- **Verified:**
  - `rainfall` 34 of 34.
  - `pbr-variant`, `water`, `config`, `fixture-gen`, `gate-docs`, `import` and `layers` all pass.
  - Goldens 31 of 31 at 0 px.

Left for the user, since each changes what the spec chose or what the frame looks like:
- **Removing `follow_scene_wind`:** a wind stating no air speed already says the same.
- **Uploading the fall time:** sending it per line, rather than solving it in the vertex stage, is
  exact only on level lines.
- **A `-1` sentinel for `parallax_scale`:** it would make an authored 0 survive the height sweep,
  and delete both the `_disp` naming and `--no-parallax` in the relief arms.
- **A GLASS variant bit:** today every rain variant carries the bead code behind a runtime test,
  and AGENTS.md prices that in occupancy. Unmeasured.
- **The glass cap and slope rework:** returning raw slope and cover would move the rim.
- **Running drops on flat glass:** they still draw, standing still, though the plan said none
  would.
- **Panels in the fog:** the fog's rain medium takes no light from area panels where the drawn
  drops now do.
