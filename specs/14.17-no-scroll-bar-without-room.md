# cetra 14.17: no scroll bar without room for one

## Context

pcb shows a stray scroll thumb: a small rounded dot at the top of the gap between two panes, left of
a pane showing the schematic. The user found it looking at pcb 6.5's window.

**Why it happens.** A pcb pane keeps a view of each kind it has shown, the one it shows filling it
and the rest sized to nothing, so each keeps its place while another is shown. A pane showing the
schematic therefore holds a code editor sized 0 by 0. The editor still has a scroll bar:
- `_scroll` (`ui_editor.c`) reports the editor's view as `text.h / row` rows, 0 for an editor with no
  height, and its text's rows as the total, so `total > view` and it says there is a bar.
- `ui_bar_place` (`ui.c:1667`) refuses a bar only while everything shows. For a rect 0 high it makes
  a track `rect.h - 2 * UI_BAR_INSET` high, -4 points; the thumb's height, `track.h * view / total`,
  is 0, and the floor meant to keep a thumb grabbable, `UI_BAR_MIN < track.h ? UI_BAR_MIN :
  track.h`, takes the track's own height, -4, so it does not lift it. The thumb is 6 by 0 points.
- `_emit` draws it with `ui_draw_rounded` at a radius of half its width, which draws a dot.
- Its place: `rect.x + rect.w - UI_BAR_W - UI_BAR_INSET`, 8 points left of the editor, which for a
  pane in a row is the gap between it and the pane before; at the window's left edge it is off
  screen, which is why a pane on the left never shows it. At `rect.y + UI_BAR_INSET`, the top.

Any element that scrolls and is laid out with no room has the same dot: an editor or a scrolling
pane sized to nothing, or narrower than its bar. pcb 6.5's boxes did not cause it: the hidden editor
was sized to nothing before them too.

Not in this spec: a way to hide an element from the layout altogether. pcb sizes a view to nothing
to keep its state, and with no bar a view sized to nothing draws nothing.

Branch and spec: `14.17-no-scroll-bar-without-room`, off cetra's master (`877135fe`).

## The design

One test in `ui_bar_place`, the one placer of a bar, which the draw (`_bar`, for a pane and an
editor) and the press on a bar (`ui.c:2353`) both read:

- **No bar when the track has no room**: false when the track would be no taller than nothing
  (`rect.h <= 2 * UI_BAR_INSET`) or the element is narrower than a bar and its insets
  (`rect.w < UI_BAR_W + 2 * UI_BAR_INSET`). Then nothing is drawn and no press lands on a bar.
- The floor stays as it is for a track with room: shorter than `UI_BAR_MIN`, the thumb fills it.
- Its comment says the new refusal beside the old: "False while everything shows, or while the
  element has no room for a bar."

## Tests

A probe case in `apps/gametest/src/ui_editor_probe.c`, read by an arm in the `ui` group with
`doc_flags`, its docstring in `gates.py`'s list, built as `editor-whole-lines` is (`editor_fixture`,
`editor_pass`, quads read back with `ui_draw_list_quad`):

- **`editor-bar-room`** -> `ui-editor-bar-room`. A text of 200 lines, more than any of the editors
  shows:
  - `none`: an editor sized 0 by 0 beside one sized 300 by 150 draws no quad left of the first's
    rect, nor any of the bar's width anywhere outside the second;
  - `narrow`: an editor 4 points wide and 150 high draws no bar;
  - `room`: the 300 by 150 editor draws its thumb, `UI_BAR_W` wide, inside its rect;
  - `press`: a press where the hidden editor's thumb was drawn takes no bar: the scroll of neither
    editor moves.

## As built

### Phase 1: the fix

- **`ui_bar_place`** refuses a bar after its old test, when `rect.h <= 2 * UI_BAR_INSET` or
  `rect.w < UI_BAR_W + 2 * UI_BAR_INSET`. Its comment in `ui_internal.h` says the new refusal
  beside the old, and so does `_bar`'s in `ui.c`.
- **`editor-bar-room`**, four flags, with `bars_outside` (the fills a bar wide outside a rect).
  Two departures from the plan:
  - **The two editors stand in a row**, the screen's root set to `UI_ROW`, the one sized to nothing
    first, as pcb's panes are. The root centres a column's children, so in a column the second
    editor's quads stood left of the first's rect and `none` could not tell them from a bar.
  - **`press` is on the editor 4 points wide**, below where its thumb would stand. A press can
    never land on an editor 0 by 0, since nothing is under the pointer there, so a press at its
    dot passed without the fix as well. The narrow editor's old track overlapped its own rect, and
    a press there paged it.
- **Falsified**: with the test taken out (the fix committed first), `none` finds one quad left of
  the hidden editor and one bar outside, `narrow` one bar outside, and `press` scrolls the narrow
  editor to line 10. `room` holds. Three flags fail, not the two the plan named.
- **Gates**: `--only ui,gate-docs` passes, 108 arms.
- **The look**: `apps/canvas --editor cetra/src/shore_runup.h -x -f 10` rendered twice is
  byte-identical (noise floor 0), and byte-identical to master's render. Seeing the dot gone in pcb
  needs pcb built against this branch, which is pcb's side, done when `extern/cetra` moves.
