# Spec 13.41 -- silent: the lake road, the lake and the log cabin

## Context
- **Planned but never built.** Spec 13.35 planned silent's town in three parts and only built the
  first, the town's edges. The last part, numbered 13.37 there, was a lake with a log cabin. It
  exists only as a section of `specs/13.35-silent-town-edges.md`; no code was ever written.
- **What the user wants:**
  - the crossroads' barricaded south arm opens onto a road winding down through the pines;
  - a dark, calm lake about 12 m below the street;
  - on its shore, a log cabin with a porch and a fire still burning inside, as if someone just
    left.
- **The spec number is 13.41.** 13.39 and 13.40 are taken by worktrees; 13.36, the street's
  dressing, is still unbuilt and is not part of this.
- **What the code says now** (found while planning). The 13.35 draft's assumptions are corrected:
  1. **The engine's water is an infinite plane.** It would flood the chasm at -12. It would also
     darken every surface below -12, anywhere. So the water needs bounds.
  2. **Nothing walls in the south-west** except the barricade.
  3. **The world stops at z 85, and the chasm runs the whole length.** There is no room for a lake
     valley.
  4. **The drive's spline and carve are hill-only.**
  5. **The haze ends at y -2.4.**
  6. **Seven fog boxes are in use against a cap of 8.** A third hole needs ten.
  7. **`door_hang` ignores the hinge's height.** So the mansion's front door probably hangs 8 m
     below its doorway.
  8. **13.33 already removed the "below 0.6 m means underground" sound test**, so the draft's reason
     to wait is gone.
- **The fire is the engine's hearth flipbook (user's choice).** It is filmed flames: nearly free,
  so it burns all the time and the cabin is always captured lit. It is CC BY 3.0, and its credit
  is already in `assets/textures/fire_hearth.json`.

## Phase 4 -- the lake: water, shore, dock, boats
- **The water (`lake.c`, `create_water`):**
  - Gerstner, calm: amplitude about 0.015, wavelength 3, steepness 0.3.
  - Dark: high `absorption`, low `scatter_albedo`, `scatter_glow` 0.
  - `height_at` NULL, as gametest's basin does.
  - `level` -12.
  - Bounds x -124..-68, z 97..141. Startup prints the lowest ground along the bounds and asserts it
    is above -11.8.
- **On only in the valley.** `lake_update(eye)` switches `enabled` with some hysteresis: on at
  x < -40 and z > 66, off at z < 60 or x > -36. It is forced on under `--no-fog`.
  - Rain already splashes and rings on it.
  - It reflects only the fogged sky, not the cabin.
- **The wading ring:** about 47 boxes on the 0.6 m contour, from y -12.9 to -10.4. It leaves a gap
  for the dock.
- **The dock:**
  - four steps down from the pad;
  - a deck at -11.45, out to x -85;
  - posts and planks in `MAT_PORCH`/`MAT_FENCE_BOARD`;
  - invisible bodies along its sides and end, down to the bed.
- **Wading:** `water_ripple_drop` at the player's feet while they are under the level and
  moving.
- **The shore:**
  - **Reeds:** cutout card clumps in the shallows, away from the dock and the beach in front of the
    cabin. A new `tools/make_reeds.py` draws the atlas, as `make_chainlink.py` does, for
    `MAT_REEDS`.
  - **Boulders:** woods.c's `boulder`, made public.
  - **Drowned trees:** three or four dead trees standing in the shallows (`trees_grow_still`).
- **Two rowboats:**
  - built from one parametric hull: station by girth quads, thwarts and oars, in lake.c;
  - one upturned on the beach, one moored at the dock's end, still on the calm water.
- **Look:** stills of the shore from the track's foot, the dock looking out, and wading. The user
  walks it.

## Phase 5 -- the cabin (`cabin.c`)
- **The kit.** A kit of its own beside the street's, its origin at the pad, built in local
  coordinates.
  - Its node gets a draw distance of the fog's reach plus 15 m.
  - It goes into silent.c's `kits[]`, and `candles_light` is called for it.
- **Structure.** 5 x 6 m, on a 0.45 m stone footing, floor at -10.35, axis-aligned.
- **The walls:**
  - a `kit_frame_wall` chinking core (`MAT_CREAM`), cut at the openings by `kit_clear_spans`;
  - round logs laid along it in courses: `MAT_LOG` lathes with `MAT_PORCH` end grain;
  - saddle-notched corners read as alternating courses running 0.25 m past each corner.
- **The roof:** rusted corrugated tin, swept from a wave profile with `kit_frame_run`, in a new
  `MAT_TIN`. Drip lines run along its eaves; 7 of the 48 are free.
- **The chimney:** fieldstone (`MAT_FOUNDATION`) on the east gable, so the fire shows through the
  open door from the dock. Its pattern is house.c's stack.
- **The porch** faces the water:
  - posts, home.c's `railing` pattern, deck boards;
  - a rocking chair: `kitchen_chair`'s legs on `kit_arc_band` rockers;
  - a woodpile: stacked log pipes with end grain.
- **The door:** a new `door_leaf_battened` (board and batten, a thumb latch) in `door.c`, through the
  fixed `door_hang`.
- **Windows:** two grimy panes (`kit_frame_pane`, `MAT_WINDOW_GLASS`).
- **Inside:**
  - **The fireplace:** a stone fireplace with a `MAT_SOOT` firebox (`hearth.c`'s `firebox` pattern),
    an iron grate and logs, and the hearth flipbook burning on them (`fire_set_flipbook`,
    `assets/textures/fire_hearth.json`). Its light is an area panel at the firebox mouth, cached
    with no body (6 cells), its brightness driven by the fire, as `fire_fixture.cscn` lights it
    (check it).
  - **A cold cast-iron cook stove:** `basement.c`'s `furnace` pattern, its pipe into the chimney.
  - **A table laid for one:** a plate, a mug, cutlery and a half-eaten meal (`mansion.c`'s `setting`
    pattern), with a chair.
  - **A cot** with a blanket.
  - **Shelves** of tins and jars (`kitchen_preserves`).
  - **An oil lamp, lit:** a lathe font, a `MAT_GLASS_CLEAR` chimney and a `kit_wick`. That makes
    `candles_light`'s flame and a cached point light with a body (48 cells).
  - **A survey map of the lake** pinned to the wall: a new card appended in `make_cards.py`, which
    regenerates `cards.h`.
- **Lighting:**
  - a GI volume over the cabin (`create_gi_volume_spaced`, 1 m, classify on, about 170 probes),
    installed at frame 2 with the others;
  - one reflection-probe box (`PROBE_ROOM_COUNT` 11 to 12);
  - a fog hole, making 10 boxes.
  - The fire burns from load, so the volume always sweeps it lit.
- **Audio:** `ROOM_CABIN` and its boxes in `sounds.c`, a link to the world through its door
  (`SoundsDoor`, `swung[]`).
- **Look:**
  - stills of the cabin from the dock and from the track, the porch, and the interior by firelight;
  - night and day;
  - the user walks in and out.

## As built

### Phase 1 (`aa7b22be`)
- **The fog cap is 16** in the scene, PostFX and the inject shader. The scene-file parser's own cap
  stays 8.
- **`Water.bounds` reaches every consumer through one rule.** "A zero rectangle bounds nothing"
  is said once for each language:
  - `cetra/src/water_bounds.h` (`water_bounds_cover`) has no GL, because `rain.c` has none;
  - `include/water_bounds.glsl` (`waterBoundsCover`) serves the shaders.
- **What the bounds now gate:**
  - **`waterDepthBelow`** takes a point rather than a height. A lit surface outside the bounds is in
    air, and the water's caustic bed is likewise.
  - **`water_frag`** discards outside, after the shoreline's own discard.
  - **The underwater medium:** `cameraSubmerged` and the froxel medium's arming need the eye
    inside.
  - **The rain:** splashes and drips land on the plane only inside. `rain_drip_schedule` takes the
    bounds, NULL for none.
- **The arms are named for what they hold.**
  - `water-bounds-whole`: bounds round the whole world leave the frame at 0 px.
  - `water-bounds-cut`: bounds off the water give the frame with no water. Bounds ending down
    the frame's middle give the unbounded frame on their side and the no-water frame on the
    other, both at 0 px.
  - They run on the downwell fixture, whose lit floor shows the light under the level.
  - The first run held ±1000 m as "the whole world" and failed by 800 px. The projected grid runs
    out past a kilometre to the horizon, so the arms use ±1e20.
- **The doors hang at their hinge's height.** `door_hang` moves the opening's top and bottom up
  by the hinge's y.
  - The home's doors are unchanged, since their hinge stands at 0.
  - The mansion's front door had hung 8 m under its doorway since 13.25 moved the mansion to y 8.
    Its doorway stood empty and its body was buried in the hill.
  - Stills of the doorway before and after: `door_sheet.png` in the session scratchpad. It now
    stands shut in the doorway and opens with E, so the mansion is entered by opening its door.
- **Gates:**
  - `fog-volume`, `water` and `water-night` all pass.
  - Goldens 32 of 32 at 0 px.
