# 11.11 The fog volume's depth range, and the blocks at a sharp beam

## The symptom

Raising the spores flashlight to a torch-scale 20000 cd (spec 11.10) made hard
blocky facets visible along the beam near its source. Turning fog off removes them.
They reproduce headlessly with a completely static camera at:

```
--cam-eye 14.467,12.566,-14.330 --cam-target 0,4,0 --fov 51.6
```

## What it is

**Two conditions have to hold at once**, which is why attacking either alone does
nothing and why this took so many wrong turns.

1. **The beam's silhouette is traced at the froxel grid's XY density.** The spot's
   18->20 degree band is under a tenth of a unit a couple of units from the lamp,
   against cells a third of a unit across, so the volume stores a step. Trilinear
   reconstruction can only put a one-cell ramp under that step.
2. **The in-scatter sits far above the tonemapper's white point.** The clip discards
   those one-cell ramps and leaves the bare step structure.

The softening is real; it just gets clipped off.

**Proven by isolation, not by inference.** Forcing the composite to
`vec4(fogLayer.rgb * 0.02, 0.0)` -- alpha 0 kills the scene under
`GL_ONE/GL_SRC_ALPHA`, and the scale keeps the tonemap from clipping -- renders the
fog in-scatter alone at the same camera. It is a smooth gradient. **The blocks are
not in the fog data.**

## The evidence

| test | result | reading |
|---|---|---|
| static camera | reproduces | not temporal disocclusion |
| `WS_MEDIA_MAX` 500 -> 60000 | **0 px changed** | never clipped in the volume; clipped at display |
| bloom off | unchanged | not bloom |
| spot shadows off | 632k px changed elsewhere, blocky region **pixel-identical** | not the binary shadow tap |
| `fog_near` 0.1 -> 2.0 | 74% of frame changed, blocks **identical** | not slice placement |
| `fog_far` 40 -> 80 | unchanged | not the volume's far face |
| `fog_sun_boost` 3.5 -> 1.0 | softer, still stepped | condition 2 alone is not enough |
| cone widened at the light (6->30 deg) | **gone** | both conditions at once: feature grows past a cell, peak drops |
| cone widened per-cell in the shader | unchanged | condition 1 alone is not enough |
| grid 160x90 -> 320x180 | **blocks halve** | step size is exactly one cell |

The two positive controls are the load-bearing ones. Widen the feature: gone. Halve
the cell: halved.

## What shipped

### The volume owns its depth range

Every froxel pass recovered `nearZ` from the projection matrix -- the CAMERA's near
plane. The slice mapping is exponential, so equal depth RATIOS get equal slices: at a
0.1 near against a 40 far, half of 64 slices land in the first 2 units of air and a
room 20 units out gets about seven. UE and Frostbite both treat the volume's depth
range as a property of the fog; `fog_far` was already authored here and its partner
did not exist.

`include/froxel.glsl` already made this argument, but only at the far end.

| field | meaning | default |
|---|---|---|
| `fog_near` | volume front face | `0` = derive `fog_far/50` |
| `fog_depth_dist` | slice bias on top of the exponential (UE's `DepthDistributionScale`) | `1.0` |
| `fog_temporal_blend` | history weight, was a literal `0.9` | `0.9` |
| `froxel_grid_x/y/z` | grid dims, off the `#define`s onto the struct | 320 / 180 / 64 |

`fog_near = 0` meaning "derive" mirrors `light->range`. A dimension change reallocates
and drops the adjacency stamp: a differently-sized history reprojects to neighbours,
not to the cell.

### The grid default: 160x90 -> 320x180

8-pixel tiles at 2560x1440, the density UE's volumetric fog defaults to
(`r.VolumetricFog.GridPixelSize` 8). The old grid was half that. This addresses
condition 1 and **halves** the step size; it does not remove the blocks, because the
beam still clips.

### Two defects found while threading it through

- `froxel_composite_frag` passed ONE `nearZ` to both media, but `aerial_lut_frag`
  bakes the aerial volume against the camera's. Fog now takes `fogNear` and its
  distribution; aerial keeps `camNearZ` and a pure exponential. Sharing it read the
  wrong slices silently -- as haze at the wrong distance, not as an error.
- `froxel_integrate_frag` walked its column by a constant per-step ratio, which only
  holds for a pure exponential. It asks the mapping for each depth now, so a
  distribution bias cannot silently mis-integrate.

### Exposure

Panel: Volume Near/Far, Depth Distribution, Temporal Blend, Grid X/Y/Z beside the
existing density and phase controls. `.cscn` gains `near`, `far` and
`depthDistribution` under `post.fog`, on the route `density` already takes with the
same CLI-wins guard. Grid dims stay out of `.cscn` -- a quality setting like MSAA, not
a property of the scene.

## Status

Condition 1 is addressed. **Condition 2 is untouched and the blocks are halved, not
gone.** Removing them means the beam not clipping at the tonemap, or a
smoother-than-linear reconstruction when the composite samples the volume. The panel
now carries live Grid X/Y and Sun Boost, so the two conditions can be bracketed
interactively rather than a build per value.

`scripts/gates.py` all green; the `fog` scale gate moved 3 px -> 6 px, both 1 LSB.
Fog-lit goldens have NOT been diffed or re-baked and must be before this merges.

## Rejected

- **Widening the spot cone.** Removes the blocks and was the first thing that worked,
  but it changes the authored light to hide a sampling limit.
- **`WS_MEDIA_MAX` as an overflow guard.** `view.glsl` does say these constants must
  be overflow and firefly guards rather than brightness controls, and +9 stops is
  arguably mis-set -- but raising it 120x changed exactly 0 px here, so it is not this
  bug and was reverted rather than shipped on a plausible-sounding argument.
- **Filtering the cone against the cell's angular footprint.** Correct in principle
  and implemented (widening symmetrically about the band midpoint, floored at the
  angle the cell subtends from the lamp). It fixes condition 1 only, and measured no
  visible change. Reverted along with its `froxelCellRadius` helper.
