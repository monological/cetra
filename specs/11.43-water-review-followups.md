# 11.43 — Water review follow-ups

## Context

Seven review agents audited 11.42's branch (three structural, four `/simplify` angles). Their
findings were triaged into four tiers. **Tier 1 and most of Tier 3 landed on the 11.42
branch**; this spec is the remainder.

One item drove the sequencing: 11.42 flipped `water_fixture`'s ramp so the camera sees ground
rather than the underside of a zero-thickness quad. That moved the waterline from screen row
0.508 to 0.453 and mis-aimed every hand-written box in `run_water_gate` — and two of the
affected arms went on **passing for the wrong reason**, which is worse than failing. The ramp
flip is therefore **temporarily reverted** on the 11.42 branch so the review fixes could be
validated against a green suite. Re-flipping it is the first job here.

### Already done on the 11.42 branch (do not redo)

- The `sunShadowSlot` gate defect, the `(1-kept²)` variance law, `spectral_frames`/
  `foam_frames` reset on re-seed, the silent foam program, the `numShadowLights` dead write,
  the "Fetch (km)" label, four false comments, the generator's winding assert.
- `_water_fft_transform` — the impulse probe now runs the sea's own stage schedule.
- `bind_outermost_cascades_to_program` in `shadow.c` — water and the catcher share it, and
  the catcher gained the `msmEnabled`/`tsmEnabled` it had never uploaded.
- `_water_bind_cascades` — the three-site cascade bind.
- `gates.py` renames: `hr`/`sr`, two bare `ratio`, `r`→`proc`, and the whole
  `WATER_PERSIST_*` family out of `water-shore-foam`'s `WATER_FOAM_*` namespace.

### The finding that came out of Phase 2, which was not on anyone's list

`water_fixture.cscn` authored `environment.sun_elevation` / `sun_azimuth` **flat**, and
`parse_env` reads them one level down under `"sun"`. So the fixture's stated 26° sun has
never been in effect — every water arm and both water goldens were calibrated against the
sky's own default of **35°**, and a `--sun-elevation 26` render moves **85% of the frame**.
`WATER_GLITTER_SUN` carried the same shape, so the glitter arm's whole premise — "the arm
puts the sun ahead and low" — was false and it had been measuring the fixture's own sun.

Fixed at the class rather than the instance: `parse_env` now reports an unknown key, the
way `parse_water` does. The generator was corrected to the working nested form **at the
angle actually in effect (35°)**, not at the one it claimed, so the file becomes truthful
at 0 px rather than re-tuning twenty-nine arms as a side effect of a key-name fix. Whether
the fixture *should* be at 26° is now a visible choice rather than an invisible bug.
Only `water_fixture.cscn` was affected; the four other scenes with a sun use the nested form.

## Phase 1 — derive the gate boxes, then re-flip the ramp — **DONE**

As built, and the order was the point: the derivations were verified to reproduce the
existing literals **exactly** on the pre-flip ramp (`(0.30, 0.47, 0.70, 0.555)` and
`(0.44, 0.42, 0.56, 0.46)` both to four decimals) before the geometry moved, so the flip was
the only variable afterwards.

- `_water_ramp_edges` reads the wedge's corners out of the fixture's own **glTF buffer**, so
  the boxes follow the mesh rather than a transcription of where it used to be.
  `_water_ramp_band` states a box as two HEIGHTS on the ramp — the waterline is height 0 by
  definition — and projects them through `_cscn_camera`.
- The flip moved the waterline from row **0.5079 to 0.4530**, as predicted, and both derived
  boxes followed it without being touched. `water-dry` still reads dry land (2.07 → 1.98).
- **`water-under-fog`** was the arm the flip genuinely broke: its three boxes ran near-to-far
  *down the screen*, and turning the wedge round made further-down-the-frame mean nearer, so
  a falling R/B became a rising one (0.8312 / 0.9074 / 0.9821). Now stated as fractions along
  the ramp and **sorted by distance from the eye**, which is orientation-free:
  1.1448 / 0.9692 / 0.7910 = 1.45x.
- **`water-shore-soft`** fell to 1,121 px against a 1,500 floor. Not a weakened effect — the
  sharpest-fall ratio improved, 0.846x → 0.756x — but the waterline moved from z 0.18 to
  z −1.38, half again as far from the eye, so the same sliver covers fewer pixels. Floor
  lowered to 700 with that reason recorded.
- Both water goldens re-baked. All 24 goldens 0 px, all 29 water arms pass.

### Still transcribed, and two of them are sky

Found while placing the above, not fixed here, because neither can produce a false pass in
the direction its arm asserts — but both weaken the claim they make:

- `WATER_FOAM_OPEN_BOX` spans rows 0.10–0.24 against a horizon at 0.2254, so it is **71%
  sky**. It asserts foam is 0 there, and "the sky has no foam" is most of what it checks.
- `WATER_SHOAL_OPEN_BOX` spans 0.17–0.26, **63% sky**. It asserts open water is unchanged
  with and without the bed; sky is trivially unchanged, so the invariance it reports is
  mostly the sky's.

## Phase 1 (original plan) — derive the gate boxes, then re-flip the ramp

**The problem is transcription, and the harness already solved it once.** `_cscn_camera`
(`gates.py:37`) exists because "a gate that predicts where geometry lands on screen needs the
exact camera the render used… the failure is silent — the gate keeps passing while measuring
a different scene than it predicts." The water gate is the only group that hand-writes all
its boxes anyway.

1. Add a `_water_band(y0_world, y1_world, x0f, x1f)` helper over `_projector` + `_cscn_camera`,
   and derive `WATER_SHORE_WINDOW`, `WATER_DRY_BOX` and `WATER_FOAM_SHOALED_BOX` from the
   wedge's world coordinates instead of literals. `_water_glitter_box` and
   `_water_persist_box` (Phase 2) are the worked examples — both derive from the projector,
   and the glitter one is already flip-proof by construction.
2. Re-flip the ramp: `LOW` at `NEAR_Z`, `HIGH` at `FAR_Z`, winding `[0,1,2, 0,2,3]`. The
   normal already follows from `wedge_indices`.
3. Re-run `--only water`, then re-bake `water_fixture` and `water_submerged`.

**Bar:** the three arms above pass *and* their printed numbers are explicable from the new
geometry. A green run whose reason nobody has established does not count — that is the state
this phase exists to leave behind.

**Also fix `water-under-fog` here.** It reads the submerged ramp fading with distance and the
flip inverts its depth ordering (measured R/B 0.8312/0.9074/0.9821, rising). Its boxes need
re-placing against the new geometry, or the arm needs to state a direction the new fixture
actually has. Do not relax the bar.

## Phase 2 — the two arms that measure the wrong place — **DONE**

Both were 11.42's own, and both were green while measuring something other than their claim.

- **`water-glitter`** — the sun override was dead (above), the box was 32% sky and sat off
  the streak. Now: the sun actually moves (elevation 22, which puts the specular point at
  `eye_y / tan(el)` = 3.0 units, clear of the ramp's near edge at z 2.2 **under both ramp
  orientations**), the box is derived from the specular point and the horizon, and the bar
  is a ratio rather than `lum_on > lum_off`, which a 1e-9 lobe satisfies.
  **26,334 px / 1.52x → 258,246 px / 13.90x.**
- **`water-foam-persist`** — the box was 17% sky, so the reported 6,687 → 7,544 = 1.13x was
  partly a count of bright sky. Rather than notch the box around the ramp — whose dry wedge
  sits at rows 0.43–0.61 one way round and 0.29–0.34 the other, leaving nothing to measure —
  the arm **drowns it**: `level` 1.1 clears the ramp's 0.9 crest, so every row below the
  horizon is open water and the arm is indifferent to the flip. Its own camera keeps 2.1
  units of freeboard over a 20 m/s sea. Added an absolute floor (a ratio has no scale:
  `1 → 3` is 3.00x) and a second 90-frame render, since nothing asserted that config is
  deterministic. **6,687 → 7,544 = 1.13x becomes 2,667 → 4,921 = 1.85x, on water.**

## Phase 3 — the rest of Tier 3 — **DONE, minus one dropped item**

- **`_water_seed_cascades`** — `_water_reseed` and `_water_ensure_spectra` carried the same
  body and now share it; `_water_build_twiddle` is out of the nullable out-param. Verified
  inert: every gate number identical, 24 goldens 0 px.
- **`_water_fft_probe`** — every line leads with its kind, and a decline names its cause
  (`gerstner` / `unseeded` / `nowater` / `alloc`) instead of a bare `available=0`.
  `water-fft-var` reads the probe's own `cascades=` field rather than counting rows.
- **Generator round-trip** — `parse_water` now reports an unknown key, and the arm checks
  three key sets against each other (what it reads, what its `known[]` tolerates, what the
  fixture authors), none of them transcribed into Python. Generalised to a `fixture-gen`
  group over all 34 `gen_*.py` — run as copies in a scratch tree, so no generator had to
  learn an output-directory convention. **All 34 reproduce their committed assets: 51 text
  files byte-identical.** 3.2 s, no GPU.
- **`gate-docs`** — a group's documented arm list is now asserted to be the list it runs, in
  run order, statically, for every group that declares one. Found water at 25 documented /
  29 run with 15 out of order, and `absorption` with one undocumented. Both fixed.
- **DROPPED: the `_Arms` scope restructuring.** The case for it was a count — 658 lines of
  body, 192 locals — and a size metric is not a finding in this tree. The two defects that
  came from that scope are already fixed by 11.42's renames; the rest was prevention argued
  from the wrong evidence.

## Phase 4 — deferred, each needing a judgement this spec should not make alone

Recorded so they are choices rather than oversights.

| item | why deferred |
|---|---|
| `WATER_SHORT_SLOPE_GAIN` in the handover | Strictly it belongs there, but the gain is 0.42, so 82% of the short band goes to roughness even in the fully resolved near field. **Measured: flattens `water-shoal` from 0.54x to 0.96x.** Needs an A/B and a decision on what the gain *means*. |
| `WATER_GLITTER_MAX` / `MIN_VAR` | The clamp is the **operating point**, not a guard: `MIN_VAR` floors the variance so calm near water clips at 400 and renders the clamp as a plateau. Feeding the lobe `alphaAuthored² + removedMss` deletes `MIN_VAR` and makes `MAX` real — but it changes how the sun looks. |
| Smith–Beckmann `G` | `D` is a Gaussian slope distribution and `G` is Schlick-GGX — different microfacet families, which is what the frozen `WATER_GLITTER_SMITH_K` is treating the symptom of. |
| `scene_key_directional` | `particle_renderer.c:145-151` still has the first-directional scan `0b6fd7f` fixed for water. Correct fix, but outside this diff. |
| Value noise: `noise.glsl`, camera-relative, wind-aligned drift | The `sin`-hash in absolute world coords loses cell distinction at `apps/tree` scale and is driver-dependent, which this tree's cross-build bar cannot carry. `ocean.glsl:257-278` argues the same case for cascade lookups. |
| Glitter early-out; CSM kernel 5×5 → 3×3 | ~65M redundant texture fetches/frame at 2800×1800. The early-out is free; the kernel is a look call. |
| `_water_reseed` atomicity | A failure removes the sea and retries every frame; a mid-loop failure splices two spectra. Stage all three cascades before swapping any handle, and make failure non-fatal to the frame. |
| `.cscn` keys for `glitter`/`foam_history`/`foam_decay`; numeric validation | The fixture can no longer author every parsed key, and the five sea-state numbers get no reject-and-warn where `waves` does. |
| `water_frag` `main()` decomposition; `ocean_bands.glsl` split; unit-ledger `_Static_assert`s | Cohesion wins, no behaviour change, no urgency. |
