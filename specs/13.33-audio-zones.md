# Spec 13.33: audio zones

## Context

The engine's sound has no walls. Every positioned sound falls off with straight-line distance and nothing else, so silent fakes walls by hand in `apps/silent/src/sounds.c`, and has ended up with three versions of one idea that disagree:

- **`inside`:** how far into either house the listener is, eased. It gives `sounds_indoor_gain`: 0.3 on the street, 1 indoors.
- **`below`:** how far under the yard the eye is, eased. It gives `sounds_overhead_gain`: 0.4 for the house's sounds heard from the basement.
- **`sounds_gain_at`:** a height test. Its 2 m threshold and great-hall exception are judged from the listener only, so it is asymmetric:
  - the drip heard from the hall gets 0.5, stepped;
  - the fridge heard from the basement gets 0.4, eased;
  - a cat in the study heard from the great hall gets 1, but from the study the great hall gets 0.5.

Each sound's module multiplies these in by hand (`hearing * overhead` at three call sites in `silent.c`, plus the fridge, the drip, the cat and the rain).

The 13.31 review left this for an engine feature, and the user chose the engine version over an app-side tidy.

**What this builds: audio zones in the game layer's `AudioSystem`.**

- A zone is a named set of boxes.
- A **link** joins two zones (or a zone and the world outside every zone) with a `through`: the gain a sound keeps crossing that boundary.
- Each frame the engine finds which zone the listener is in and which zone every positioned sound is in.
- It gives each sound the best path's gain between the two (the largest product of `through` along any chain of links).
- It eases that gain over a fraction of a second, and multiplies it under the app's own volume.
- The app sets volumes once and never multiplies a wall in again.

**A graph and not a nested tree, which is a change from what was first described.**

- A tree gets storeys wrong. With the mansion's upstairs as a child of the mansion, outdoor sound reaches upstairs through two boundaries (0.5 × 0.3) instead of one, so the rain and the wind would halve upstairs, which they do not today.
- With links:
  - upstairs ↔ outdoors is 0.3;
  - upstairs ↔ downstairs is 0.5;
  - the basement ↔ outdoors is 0.4 × 0.3, through the house.
- This is Wwise's rooms-and-transmission model in miniature.
- Unreal's Audio Volumes (one interior/exterior value per volume) cannot express the storey case either.

**Doors open links** (the user's choice). A link's `through` can be set at any time, and silent drives the doors' links from `Door.travel`:
- opening the basement door lets more of the drip up into the hall;
- opening a front door lets more wind and rain into the house.

**Out of scope:**
- **Muffling.** miniaudio has a low-pass node, but the game layer exposes no filter; it would be a later spec. Until then silent keeps its muffled wind layer as the stand-in.
- **`.cscn` zones.** The render app plays no sound.
- **Reverb.**

## Engine (`cetra/src/game/`)

### `audio_zones.c/h` (new): pure, no miniaudio, no clock
Following `residency.c`'s precedent, the graph and the membership test are testable without a device.

```c
#define AUDIO_ZONE_WORLD 0u      // outside every zone
#define AUDIO_ZONE_MAX   16      // the world included
#define AUDIO_ZONE_BOXES 8
#define AUDIO_ZONE_LINKS 32
typedef uint32_t AudioZone;
typedef uint32_t AudioZoneLink;
typedef struct AudioZoneDesc { const char* name; const AABB* boxes; int box_count; } AudioZoneDesc;
```

`AudioZones` holds:
- the zones (name, boxes copied in);
- the links (a, b, through);
- `float heard[MAX][MAX]`, the best-path gain between every pair: 1 on the diagonal, 0 where nothing joins.

The best path is recomputed by Floyd–Warshall over the max-product semiring whenever a link is added or changes. At 16 zones that is 4096 multiplies.

**Membership:** a point belongs to the **last-added** zone whose boxes contain it, so an inner zone is added after the room around it and carves itself out of it. Outside every zone is `AUDIO_ZONE_WORLD`. It uses `AABB` from `mesh.h`, which is already public.

### `audio.c/h`: the public API on `AudioSystem`

```c
AudioZone     audio_zone_add(AudioSystem*, const AudioZoneDesc*);   // AUDIO_ZONE_WORLD on refusal, logged by name
AudioZoneLink audio_zone_link(AudioSystem*, AudioZone a, AudioZone b, float through);
void          audio_zone_link_set(AudioSystem*, AudioZoneLink, float through); // a door's swing
float         audio_zone_heard(const AudioSystem*, AudioZone z); // the eased gain a sound in z reaches the listener at
```

- **`audio_system_update` takes `dt`**, which its two callers have: `game_pre_render` (`game.c:317`) passes `sim_clock.delta`; the gametest probe passes its window's duration.
- **Each frame:**
  1. Find the listener's zone.
  2. Read each held, positioned `Sound`'s position back with `ma_sound_get_position` (only those with spatialization on), and each live voice's after `place_voice`, and find its zone.
  3. Ease its factor toward `heard[listener][zone]` with `1 - exp(-dt / AUDIO_ZONE_FADE)` (0.4 s, silent's `INSIDE_SECONDS`).
  4. Write it with `ma_node_set_output_bus_volume(&sound, 0, factor)`.
- **Why the output-bus volume:** it is a separate stage from `ma_sound_set_volume` (`miniaudio.h:72868`), so the app's volume and the zone's never overwrite each other, and nothing needs caching.
- **Seeding:** a sound or voice is seeded at its target the first frame it is placed, so a new voice never fades in from 1.
- **Unaffected:**
  - 2D sounds (tone, noise or music never positioned) keep factor 1.
  - With no zones added, every factor is 1, so gametest and every existing arm are unchanged.
- **`heard[z]` for the query** is eased per zone the same way, toward `heard[listener][z]`. It is for an app shaping 2D ambience; silent's wind and rain use it.
- **New fields:**
  - `Sound` gains `float zone_factor; bool zone_seeded`.
  - `AudioVoice` gains the same.
  - `AudioSystem` embeds an `AudioZones` and the eased `heard[]`.

### Gate arms: `audio` group, through gametest's `--audio-probe` (`run_audio_probe`, `gametest.c:4327+`)
New probe cases print `audio <case> <label> rms %.6f %.6f`, using the existing `probe_measure` and `_audio_probe_run`.

- **`audio-zone`:**
  - a tone in zone B, the listener in zone A, link A–B 0.25: the RMS over the same placement with no zones is 0.25 (±0.01);
  - a 2D tone in the same run is unchanged.
- **`audio-zone-path`:** links A–B 0.5, B–C 0.5, A–C 0.1. A tone in C heard from A comes out at 0.25, the best path, not 0.1 or 0.025.
- **`audio-zone-fade`:**
  - the listener steps from A into B; consecutive windows rise monotonically and are within 2% of the target after 2 s;
  - a voice started in B after the step starts at its target in its first window;
  - `audio_zone_link_set` moves the level the same way.

Each arm is falsified by hand after the arms are committed:
- the factor not applied;
- the path taken as the direct link;
- easing removed, and the voice left unseeded.

## Silent (`apps/silent/src/`)

### The zones, registered in `sounds_start` (`sounds.c`, which takes the audio system)
Boxes are built from the named constants in `layout.h`, `home.h` and `mansion_at`:

| Zone | Boxes | Link | through |
|---|---|---|---|
| `home` | the house's footprint from its floor framing to the roof | home ↔ world | 0.3 shut |
| `basement` | `CELLAR_*` from `BASEMENT_Y` to `SUBFLOOR_Y0`, plus the stairwell above the floor behind the basement door. Added after `home`. | basement ↔ home | 0.4 shut |
| `mansion` | its footprint and the tower | mansion ↔ world | 0.3 shut |
| `mansion_up` | the upper front band and the tower bay above `FLOOR2_Y`, not the gallery | up ↔ world; up ↔ mansion | 0.3; 0.5 |

- **The basement door is the basement's boundary.** That is why the stairwell above the floor is the basement's.
- **The great hall's gallery is part of `mansion`.** That is what the old great-hall exception was trying to say.

### Doors drive links, each frame in pre-render
`through = lerp(shut, open, door.travel)`:

| Door | Link | Shut → open |
|---|---|---|
| Home front door (`DOOR_HOME`) | home ↔ world | 0.3 → 0.6 |
| Mansion front door | mansion ↔ world | 0.3 → 0.6 |
| Basement door | basement ↔ home | 0.4 → 0.8 |

- The open values are starting points, set by ear.
- The bath door is left out: the bathroom is not a zone, and nothing in it makes sound.

### Ambience: the 2D beds and the wind laid over the listener
Shaped by the app from one engine number, `o = audio_zone_heard(audio, AUDIO_ZONE_WORLD)` (eased: 1 outdoors, 0.3 in either house, 0.12 in the basement):

- `indoors = clamp01((1 - o) / (1 - WALL_THROUGH))`: 0 outside, 1 in any house.
- `deeper = clamp01(o / WALL_THROUGH)`: 1 in a house's rooms, the basement's 0.4 under them.
- `WALL_THROUGH` is the 0.3 both houses register.

| Bed | New | Today |
|---|---|---|
| Wind, outside layer | `WIND_VOLUME * (1 - indoors)` | unchanged |
| Wind, muffled layer | `WIND_VOLUME * indoors * deeper` | the basement's 0.25 becomes 0.4, the one deliberate level change |
| Rain beds | `rain_bed_update`'s `hearing` = `deeper` | the same 0.4 below ground |

The wind stays positioned over the listener, so its own zone is always the listener's and its factor is 1. The rain beds are noise, so they are 2D and unzoned.

### Level changes, stated rather than found
| Change | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| House sounds heard from the basement, and the reverse (the drip from the hall) | inconsistent | 0.4 both ways, eased |
| Storeys in the mansion | asymmetric | 0.5 both ways |
| The other house from inside one | 1 | 0.09 (on top of 115 m of falloff) |
| Wind in the basement | 0.25 | 0.4 of the muffled layer |
| Any door, as it opens | — | its link's open value |

## As built

### Phase 1: the engine
- **`audio_zones.c/h`** as planned: up to 15 zones of up to 8 boxes and 32 links, the best path between every pair by Floyd–Warshall over max-product, recomputed only when a link's value actually changes. Membership goes through `mesh.h`'s `aabb_dist_sq` (0 inside), not a second containment test. Failure returns `AUDIO_ZONE_WORLD` for a zone and `AUDIO_ZONE_NO_LINK` for a link, each logged by name.
- **A sound is zoned once it is PLACED**, by `audio_sound_set_position` (an `AUDIO_SOURCE` places its sound the same way) or as a voice, not merely because miniaudio spatializes it: a sound from a file is spatialized from creation, at the world origin, and zoning that would attenuate an unplaced sound by wherever the origin falls.
- **Seeded at placement, not at the next update.** A voice and a newly placed sound take their zone's gain before they start, since on a real device the mixer would otherwise play their first milliseconds whole before the frame's update reached them. `placed` doubles as "seeded", so `Sound` carries no second flag.
- **`audio_system_update(…, dt)`**: `game_pre_render` passes the sim clock's delta, as the animators take; the probe passes its window's length.
- **Arms**: `audio-zone` 0.2500 through a link of 0.25 and a 2D tone at 1.0000; `audio-zone-path` 0.2500 (the way round, not the direct 0.1); `audio-zone-fade` 0.2500 from the other zone, then 0.549, 0.730, 0.839, 0.905, 0.946, 0.970, 0.985, 0.994, 0.999, 1.003 a window at a time (0.545 predicted for the first), the opened link 1.0002, a voice 0.2481 in its first window. The six older arms are unchanged; `gate-docs` and `gamepad` pass.
- **Falsified**: without the closure, `audio-zone-path` reads 0.1000; a voice seeded whole reads 0.9924; with the gain never applied every zone arm reads 1.000; with the easing removed the first window is already 1.008.
