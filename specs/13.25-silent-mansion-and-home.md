## The move: a kit with an origin
The whole existing house, shell intact (tower, great hall, gallery, stair, study, hall, the
kitchen's footprint), moves as ONE block. Cutting it apart would break five couplings: the shared
z = 13.8 wall, the full-depth outside walls, one roof with its ridge at x = 0, the tower in the
front row, and the only stair.

**The kit gets an origin.**
- `Kit.origin` (a vec3, translation only) is applied at the lowest level: vertex emission, box
  colliders, drip lines and wicks.
- Planar UVs and grime are computed BEFORE the offset, so every texture and every smudge lands as
  it does today.
- So `house.c`, `tower.c`, `interior.c`, `hearth.c` and `study.c` build in house-local
  coordinates, unchanged.

**Two kits.**
- **The mansion kit** at `MANSION_ORIGIN` (in `layout.h`), at the end of the street and up the
  hill. Its front faces -z, toward the road's arrival.
- **The street-and-home kit** at origin 0.
- Two kits also give two meshes per material, so each building culls on its own.

**One helper, `mansion_at(local)`,** for everything placed outside the kit:
- the study lamp;
- the GI volume's corner and the five probe boxes;
- the cat's places and gaze points;
- the sound boxes;
- the fog hole;
- `house_clearance` (queried at world minus origin).

`layout.h` stays the Gothic house's plan. The front row's kitchen and hall constants are shared
by both buildings in local coordinates, so `kitchen.c`, the tubes and `clock.c` keep their
numbers for the home at origin 0.

**What the whole-world code needs:**
- `house_outside_distance` and the great hall's other-storey box become per building (the
  minimum over buildings).
- The prompt takes a LIST of doors: the home's and the mansion's.
- The world bounds are rebuilt as an outline round the street plus the hill. The east wall at
  x = +44 opens where the road leaves.
- The fog becomes "a rect minus holes", 2 holes in at most 7 boxes, within the cap of 8. The holes
  run full height, since the mansion sits on the hill.

## The hill, the road and the trees
**The hill.**
- A faceted heightfield in the kit (PS2 look, flat-shaded like the rest of silent), from a
  height function: a bump plus noise.
- The ROAD is carved into it: heights follow the road's own profile, at a grade of about 6% so
  its flats puddle (puddles form below 8 degrees).
- Material: `MAT_DIRT` (the grass_ground scan, with relief).
- Collider: a new `kit_mesh_collider` built from the same triangles (`SHAPE_MESH`, static).
- Runs of exactly coplanar triangles hit Jolt's debug-build trace (AGENTS.md, physics), so the
  noise stays on and the flat start uses boxes.

**The road.**
- A Catmull-Rom ribbon about 5 m wide, in `MAT_ASPHALT`, cracked and with gravel shoulders. It
  leaves the street at x = 45 and winds about 120-150 m up to the mansion's gate.
- It is walkable in about 90 s, or 45 s hurrying.

**The trees.**
- Bare trees from the engine's `tree_gen` (`cetra/procedural/tree_gen.h`, as forest uses it)
  with `show_leaves = 0`. Twisted: high droop and curve noise, low phototropism.
- Six to eight models, about 150 instances, along the road and round the grounds.
- Placed from their own RNG stream, so the neighbours do not change.
- Their own nodes outside the kit, since a wind material is skipped by kit shadow cells. They
  sway in the scene's gusting Wind.
- Bark is a CC0 scan added to `tools/fetch_textures.py`.

**The creepy set.**
- **Iron gates on stone piers** at the foot of the drive, one leaf hanging open. The piers use
  the rubble base material.
- **A rusted wrought-iron fence** round the grounds, with gaps (`kit_frame_pipe` bars and spear
  finials).
- **A small graveyard beside the road:** headstones, a few leaning crosses and a low rubble wall.
- **Two dead lamp posts** (the street lamp's kit) on the drive. One is dark. One flickers: a point
  light with the street lamp's IES profile and a flicker like the failing tube's.

## As built

### Phases 1 and 2 -- the move, the hill, the drive and what stands on it
**The move.** As planned, a second kit at `MANSION_X/Y/Z` (115, 8, 40). Its materials are
shared with the first: `kit_init_beside` copies the slots.
- **That sharing cost a crash on the first run.** `kit_finish` freed every material its kit
  never used. With two kits, a material neither house used (the home's new ones, before phase 4)
  was freed twice.
- **The fix:** a kit that shares its materials frees none at its finish. `kit_free_unused`
  frees, once both are finished, each material no kit used.

**What the first runs found.**
- **The mansion's GI grid** had its top at `GI_TOP` without the origin's height, so its seven
  layers were squeezed into 1.4 m. The clearance print caught it: 242 probes inside walls.
- **The opening fade never lifted, and the screen stayed black.** silent held the screen black
  until `gi_world_pending` cleared, and the mansion's volume stays pending by design: it sweeps
  only once its candles hold tile blocks, which is when the drive brings them near. The fade
  now waits for the volume the eye is in (`gi_world_ready_in`).

**An engine fix the hill exposed (13.24's rule outside every box).** A point outside every volume
took the nearest swept one's clamped edge, as a lone volume always had.
- **The symptom:** on a hillside a hundred metres from either house, black lines lay straight
  across the ground along the planes of a volume's box. Every probe there failed the visibility
  test, and the weights underflowed to an exact zero. A zero irradiance read as black, and also
  zeroed `envVisible`, which took the environment's specular with it.
- **How it was found:** the albedo view had no lines, and `--no-gi` had none.
- **The fix:**
  - A volume's answer fades to the environment's over `GI_REACH` (three) of its own cells past
    its box.
  - A sum of weights that reaches zero is no answer, rather than a black one.
- **Goldens:** all 32 at 0 px.

**Seeing it.** `--no-fog` (new) drops the fog volumes and the haze, so the layout can be seen from
above at all.
- **The hill:** a 2 m faceted grid over x 45..150, z -46..85, rising from the street to the
  grounds at y 8, with the drive carved through it. The drive is a Catmull-Rom ribbon through
  eight points, with a switchback.
- **The trees:** 110 dead trees from six `tree_gen` models, most of them hugging the drive, and
  none within 14 m of the gate. The gate is where the house is first seen whole, and the first
  stills had it hidden behind branches.
- **The rest:** the gates on their piers, the fence with its gaps, the graveyard's wall and
  stones, and the two dead lamps up the drive.
