# Spec 13.25 -- the mansion at the end of the street, and a real house (silent)

## Context
**What the user wants.**
- The Gothic house doesn't fit a kitchen with a fridge and tube lights. Keep the kitchen, the
  grandfather clock and the hallway as the PLAYER'S house, and make that a real house in the
  style of P.T. (the user's three reference screenshots: a long hallway of cream plaster, white
  crown moulding and baseboards, dark glossy boards, dark framed paintings, a lantern sconce and
  a hanging lantern, double-hung windows, panelled doors, a console with a radio, a plant, an
  ashtray, keys and a framed photo).
- Move the rest of the Gothic house to the END OF THE STREET, up a winding road through creepy
  bare trees, with:
  - iron gates on stone piers,
  - a rusted wrought-iron fence,
  - a small graveyard,
  - dead street lamps, one of them flickering.
- In the mansion, the kitchen becomes a Gothic DINING ROOM.
- The CAT lives in the mansion.
- The player's house is walkable: hall, kitchen, living room and bathroom; its other doors are
  shut.

**What it stands on.** 13.24 (lighting data that streams) is what lets two buildings about 100 m
apart each have their own GI volume, nine reflection probes between them, and more candles than
the shadow pool holds. This branch is cut from 13.24's tip, which awaits the user's permission to
merge to master.

**No gates, goldens or fixtures for silent.** 13.25 is checked by screenshots and by the user
walking it. The mansion keeps the stair, where 13.22's fringe reproduces.

## The move: a kit with an origin
The whole existing house, shell intact (tower, great hall, gallery, stair, study, hall, the
kitchen's footprint), moves as ONE block. Cutting it apart would break five couplings: the shared
z = 13.8 wall, the full-depth outside walls, one roof with its ridge at x = 0, the tower in the
front row, and the only stair.

**The kit gets an origin.**
- `Kit.origin` (a vec3, translation only) is applied at the lowest level: vertex emission, box
  colliders, drip lines and wicks.
- Planar UVs and grime are computed BEFORE the offset, so every texture and every smudge lands as
  it does today.
- So `house.c`, `tower.c`, `interior.c`, `hearth.c` and `study.c` build in house-local
  coordinates, unchanged.

**Two kits.**
- **The mansion kit** at `MANSION_ORIGIN` (in `layout.h`), at the end of the street and up the
  hill. Its front faces -z, toward the road's arrival.
- **The street-and-home kit** at origin 0.
- Two kits also give two meshes per material, so each building culls on its own.

**One helper, `mansion_at(local)`,** for everything placed outside the kit:
- the study lamp;
- the GI volume's corner and the five probe boxes;
- the cat's places and gaze points;
- the sound boxes;
- the fog hole;
- `house_clearance` (queried at world minus origin).

`layout.h` stays the Gothic house's plan. The front row's kitchen and hall constants are shared
by both buildings in local coordinates, so `kitchen.c`, the tubes and `clock.c` keep their
numbers for the home at origin 0.

**What the whole-world code needs:**
- `house_outside_distance` and the great hall's other-storey box become per building (the
  minimum over buildings).
- The prompt takes a LIST of doors: the home's and the mansion's.
- The world bounds are rebuilt as an outline round the street plus the hill. The east wall at
  x = +44 opens where the road leaves.
- The fog becomes "a rect minus holes", 2 holes in at most 7 boxes, within the cap of 8. The holes
  run full height, since the mansion sits on the hill.

## The hill, the road and the trees
**The hill.**
- A faceted heightfield in the kit (PS2 look, flat-shaded like the rest of silent), from a
  height function: a bump plus noise.
- The ROAD is carved into it: heights follow the road's own profile, at a grade of about 6% so
  its flats puddle (puddles form below 8 degrees).
- Material: `MAT_DIRT` (the grass_ground scan, with relief).
- Collider: a new `kit_mesh_collider` built from the same triangles (`SHAPE_MESH`, static).
- Runs of exactly coplanar triangles hit Jolt's debug-build trace (AGENTS.md, physics), so the
  noise stays on and the flat start uses boxes.

**The road.**
- A Catmull-Rom ribbon about 5 m wide, in `MAT_ASPHALT`, cracked and with gravel shoulders. It
  leaves the street at x = 45 and winds about 120-150 m up to the mansion's gate.
- It is walkable in about 90 s, or 45 s hurrying.

**The trees.**
- Bare trees from the engine's `tree_gen` (`cetra/procedural/tree_gen.h`, as forest uses it)
  with `show_leaves = 0`. Twisted: high droop and curve noise, low phototropism.
- Six to eight models, about 150 instances, along the road and round the grounds.
- Placed from their own RNG stream, so the neighbours do not change.
- Their own nodes outside the kit, since a wind material is skipped by kit shadow cells. They
  sway in the scene's gusting Wind.
- Bark is a CC0 scan added to `tools/fetch_textures.py`.

**The creepy set.**
- **Iron gates on stone piers** at the foot of the drive, one leaf hanging open. The piers use
  the rubble base material.
- **A rusted wrought-iron fence** round the grounds, with gaps (`kit_frame_pipe` bars and spear
  finials).
- **A small graveyard beside the road:** headstones, a few leaning crosses and a low rubble wall.
- **Two dead lamp posts** (the street lamp's kit) on the drive. One is dark. One flickers: a point
  light with the street lamp's IES profile and a flicker like the failing tube's.

## The mansion's front row
- **The kitchen footprint becomes a Gothic DINING ROOM.** It has a refectory table set for a meal
  long abandoned (plates, goblets, dead flowers), high-backed chairs, an iron candelabra
  (candles: flames plus cached lights), a sideboard with pewter, and portraits (cards from
  `tools/make_gothic.py`).
- **The kitchen window becomes a leaded Gothic window** (`MAT_LEADED`, pointed head) with a
  WINDOW SEAT.
- **The hall stays Gothic** (it already is). A suit of armour stands where the clock stood, and
  the hall is lit by a candle sconce rather than the bare bulb.
- **Probes:** the kitchen box becomes the dining room's.
- **The gutter and downpipe stay,** for their drips.
- **The cat:**
  - Its places move by `mansion_at`. The ten kitchen and hall places become dining-room places
    (table, chair, window seat, sideboard) and hall places (by the armour, the front of the hall).
  - `watch_clock` becomes `watch_flame`, a gaze at the candelabra's flicker. `window` moves to
    the window seat.
  - The sleep and loaf lists are updated.
  - The rail, the newel and the stair are unchanged. Home stays the study chair.
  - `cat_places_check` reports the blocked links.

## The player's house (the home), built new on the original lot
**The exterior.** A two-storey American clapboard house with:
- double-hung windows with white trim and shutters;
- a shingled gable roof and a brick chimney;
- a covered front porch with square posts, a railing and concrete steps;
- gutters and downpipes as drip lines;
- dark upstairs windows, one faintly lit.

There is no tower. The kitchen window keeps its place and its rain beads, under the porch roof as
now, so 13.12's wet window survives.

**The rooms (ground floor).**
- **Kitchen:** unchanged (`kitchen.c`, the tubes, the fridge sounds, the table's candle).
- **Hall, as P.T.:**
  - A straight corridor from the front door back to a double-hung window, with a cased opening
    part way along.
  - Cream plaster walls, white crown moulding, baseboards and door casings.
  - A dark, glossy hardwood floor, which reflects through its probe and SSR.
  - Dark framed paintings and a lantern wall sconce with frosted glass. A hanging pendant
    lantern replaces the bare bulb.
  - A console with a radio, a potted plant (a small `tree_gen` with leaves), an ashtray, keys and
    a framed photo.
  - The CLOCK where it stands, opposite the kitchen door.
- **Living room**, front-west: sofa, armchair, CRT television, coffee table, floor lamp, rug.
- **Bathroom**, at the back: tub, sink and mirror, toilet, tiled floor, a single bulb.

**Doors.**
- Four-panel leaves, a new leaf in `door.c`.
- The front door swings. The stair, the back bedroom and the basement are shut.

**Lighting data (13.24's world lists).**
- **GI.** The home's grid comes from its bounds (`create_gi_volume_spaced`), with classification
  switching off any probe in a wall. The mansion keeps silent's hand-tuned 11x7x11 grid, moved by
  the origin, with its clearance print unchanged.
- **Probes.** Four for the home: kitchen, hall, living room and bathroom. Five for the mansion:
  great hall, study, tower bay, entrance hall and dining room. Nine in the world. The mansion's
  are captured as you come up the drive.
- **Candles.** Distance decides which cast: the mansion's when you are there, the home's when you
  are home. The mansion keeps its bodied lights within the sixteen the tiles hold, or a capture
  there would wait on one that never gets its block.
- **The cat** is `capture_hidden`, so it may be attached at once.

**Pictures and textures.**
- The paintings and the photo are generated by `tools/make_cards.py`. They are original, not
  P.T.'s.
- New CC0 scans: plaster, hardwood, shingles, bathroom tile and rusted iron.
