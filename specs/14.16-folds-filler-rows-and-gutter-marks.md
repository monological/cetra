# cetra 14.16: folds, filler rows and gutter marks

## Context

pcb 6.5 (pcb's `specs/6.5-comparing-versions.md`) shows a past version of a file beside the file
now, in one pane of two editors: the old text on the left, read only, removed lines red; the file
now on the right, added lines green, whole lines coloured (14.15's `whole_lines`), the two scrolled
together (14.15's `ui_editor_top_line` and `ui_editor_scroll_line`). The user wants it to look as
GitHub's split diff does, and four things there are missing:

1. **Unchanged code folded away**: a long run of lines the two versions share shows as one row
   ("@@ -74,7 +74,7 @@" on GitHub), opened to show them. The editor shows every line.
2. **Marks in the gutter**: a `-` or `+` beside each changed line, and its number on a stronger red
   or green. The gutter draws only numbers.
3. **Rows lined up**: blank filler rows on the side with fewer, so a line and its counterpart stand
   on the same row. The editor's rows are its text's alone (14.15 left them out: they touch the row
   model throughout); pcb lines the sides up by their top lines and they drift below.
4. **The changed words** within a changed line, on a stronger red or green. This needs nothing new:
   a layer's ranges may overlap and differ in style (`text_doc_set_ranges` sorts them and merges
   nothing), and `_draw` lays the whole-line fills under the banded backgrounds, so pcb puts each
   changed word as a plain range on the layer that holds its line. No fifth layer.

Items 1 to 3 are this spec. Each is the editor's general mechanism, not a diff: a host folds what
it likes, pads any line, and marks lines through range styles.

Not in this spec:
- **Nested folds, and folds made by the person** (`zf`, `zd`, `zE`, `foldmethod`): the host makes
  the folds. The vim mode opens and closes them.
- **One border round both editors**: pcb's, with `ui_set_style` on the two editors and its own
  border on the pane.
- **Turning wrapping off.**

Branch and spec: `14.16-folds-filler-rows-and-gutter-marks`, off cetra's master (`df9a3d9f`).

## The design

### The row model

Every walk of the editor's rows asks one thing of a line: how many rows it takes. Today that is its
wrapped count (`_rows_of`, the `tops` prefix sums, `_row_down`, `_row_up`, `_line_at_row`,
`_scroll`, `ui_editor_offset_at`, `ui_editor_point_of`, `_rows_down`, `_draw`). This spec makes it
the rows the line SHOWS:
- a closed fold's first line: 1, its summary row, whatever its own fillers (below);
- any other line in a closed fold: 0;
- any other line: its filler rows, then its wrapped rows.

One function gives it, and every walk goes through it: `_row_down` and `_row_up` step over lines
showing no rows; `tops` sums it; a change to the folds or fillers sets `tops_good` back to the first
line it touches. Within a line the shown rows are its fillers, then its text rows: `top_row`, and
the `rows` of `ui_editor_top_line` and `ui_editor_scroll_line`, count from the first filler. A line
inside a closed fold is never wrapped for drawing; the settle budget may skip it.

Folds and fillers belong to the editor in a text, `UIEditorPlace`, not to the text: two editors on
one text fold it apart, as two vim windows do, and an editor parked on a text keeps them. They move
with their lines through every change, in `_shift_lines`, as the rows do.

### 1. Folds

```c
// A run of lines the editor shows as one row while closed. `label` is that row's text, copied;
// NULL for "N lines".
typedef struct UIEditorFold {
    size_t first, last;
    bool open;
    const char* label;
} UIEditorFold;
// The folds of the text the editor shows, replaced whole. They may not overlap or nest: a set that
// does is refused and logged, and the folds stay as they were.
void ui_editor_set_folds(UIElement* el, const UIEditorFold* folds, size_t count);
// The folds as they stand, in order of their lines, open or closed; valid until they or the text
// change.
size_t ui_editor_folds(const UIElement* el, const UIEditorFold** out);
// The fold holding `line` opened or closed; false for none.
bool ui_editor_fold_open(UIElement* el, size_t line, bool open);
```

- **Drawn**: a closed fold's row has `UITheme.editor_fold` behind it from the gutter's left to the
  editor's right, and its label from the text's left edge in the line numbers' colour; no number.
  The lines after it keep their numbers (line 104 after a fold of 74 to 103).
- **Through edits**: a line inserted inside a fold joins it; a fold whose lines are all removed
  goes; one whose first or last line is removed shrinks.
- **Carets** are vim's: a caret may stand on any line of a closed fold and is drawn on its row, at
  its start. A move by rows or lines (Up and Down, `j`, `k`, `gj`, `gk`, a count of them) takes a
  closed fold as one row. What vim's default `'foldopen'` names opens the fold a caret lands in:
  `block,hor,mark,percent,quickfix,search,tag,undo` -- a search or an undo landing in it, a move
  along the line in it. Typing or pasting at a caret in a closed fold opens it first. A click on a
  fold's row opens it and puts the caret on its first line.
- **An operator on a closed fold's row takes all its lines**, linewise, as vim's does: `dd` there
  deletes the fold.
- **The vim mode**: `zo`, `zO`, `zc`, `za`, `zR`, `zM`, each as vim 9.2 does them over manual
  folds, under the oracle (the vim side's folds made by `:{first},{last}fold`, the editor's by
  `ui_editor_set_folds`). `zj` and `zk` too if the oracle holds them cheaply.
- **The standard mode**: commands `open-fold`, `close-fold` and `toggle-fold` in
  `ui_editor_commands`, for a host to bind. None is bound by default.
- `ui_editor_reveal` and `ui_editor_scroll_place` of an offset in a closed fold show its row and
  leave it closed; `ui_editor_scroll_line` to a line in one goes to its row.

### 2. Filler rows

```c
// Blank rows above a line, which no caret stands on.
typedef struct UIEditorFiller {
    size_t line;
    uint32_t rows;
} UIEditorFiller;
// The fillers of the text the editor shows, replaced whole; a line named twice takes the last.
void ui_editor_set_fillers(UIElement* el, const UIEditorFiller* fillers, size_t count);
// The rows `line` wraps to at the editor's columns, fillers and folds aside: wrapped now when it
// was a guess. 1 before the editor is laid out.
size_t ui_editor_line_rows(UIElement* el, size_t line);
```

- **Drawn**: `UITheme.editor_filler` behind each filler row, from the text's left edge to the
  editor's right; no number, no text, no range.
- **Where**: above their line. Fillers above a closed fold's first line stand above its row;
  fillers above its other lines are not shown. There are none below the last line: nothing follows
  for them to line up.
- **Through edits**: a filler stays above its line, and goes with it.
- **Carets**: a move by rows skips filler rows; a click on one puts the caret at the start of the
  line it stands above.
- `ui_editor_line_rows` is for a host padding two editors to each other: it changes when the
  editor's columns do, which a host polls with `ui_editor_columns`.

### 3. Gutter marks

Two fields in `UIEditorStyle` (`ui.h`), so a host marks lines through the ranges it already keeps:

```c
vec4 gutter;   // behind the gutter on every row of each line the range reaches; alpha 0 = none
uint32_t sign; // drawn in the gutter's gap on the first row of each line it reaches; 0 = none
```

- **Reaches** as `whole_lines` does: the line's bytes with its line break overlap the range.
- **`gutter`** fills from the gutter's left to the text's left edge, under the number.
- **`sign`** is drawn in the first cell of the gap after the number (`GUTTER_GAP` is 2), in the
  editor's plain text colour. The gutter keeps its width.
- Not on fold or filler rows.
- **Zero is the default**: a zeroed style draws what it does now, so `ui-theme-identity` and every
  golden are untouched.

### The theme

`UITheme` gains `editor_fold` (behind a closed fold's row; alpha 0 = the selection colour at a
third) and `editor_filler` (behind a filler row; alpha 0 = the editor's text colour at 4%).

## Tests

Probe cases in `apps/gametest/src/ui_editor_probe.c`, each read by an arm in the `ui` group with
`doc_flags`, its docstring in `gates.py`'s list, built as `editor-whole-lines` is
(`editor_fixture`, `editor_pass`, quads and glyphs read back from the draw list):

- **`editor-folds`** -> `ui-editor-folds`. A text of 60 lines, some wrapping, a closed fold over
  10 to 39 with a label, an open one over 45 to 50.
  - `rows`: the closed fold is one row with the fold colour and its label; the next row is line 40,
    numbered 41; the open fold draws its lines.
  - `walk`: a row move down from line 9 lands on the fold, the next on line 40; a wheel of 1 row
    from line 9 at the top puts the fold at the top; `ui_editor_offset_at` on its row gives line
    10's start; `ui_editor_point_of` an offset inside it gives its row.
  - `open`: a click on its row opens it, the caret on line 10; `ui_editor_fold_open` closes it
    again; `ui_editor_folds` reads both.
  - `edits`: a line inserted inside it grows it by one; its lines all deleted, it goes; typing at a
    caret on it opens it.
  - `parked`: an editor shown another text and back has its folds as they were; a second editor on
    the text has none.
  - `refused`: overlapping folds are refused and the folds stay.
- **`editor-fillers`** -> `ui-editor-fillers`. Two fillers above a line, one above a closed fold's
  first line, one above a line inside it.
  - `rows`: two filler rows with the filler colour and no number, then the line; the fold's filler
    shows above it, the inner one not.
  - `walk`: a row move from the line above lands on the line, not a filler; `ui_editor_top_line`'s
    rows count the fillers.
  - `click`: a click on a filler puts the caret at the start of its line.
  - `line-rows`: `ui_editor_line_rows` of a wrapping line matches its rows drawn.
- **`editor-gutter`** -> `ui-editor-gutter`. A style with a gutter fill and `+` on one line's range,
  another with `-` only.
  - `fill`: one gutter quad per row of the marked line, gutter-wide, under the number;
  - `sign`: the `+` glyph in the gap on the first row only, the `-` on its line;
  - `zero`: a zeroed style draws neither.
- **The vim oracle**: sequences of `zo`, `zO`, `zc`, `za`, `zR`, `zM`, and `j`, `k`, `dd`, `/` and
  `u` over closed folds, each with a prelude of manual folds, held to vim 9.2 with 0 differences.

## Phases

0. **Spec**: this plan into `specs/` on the branch, committed alone; a task list.
1. **Folds in the row model**: the place's folds, the three calls, the shown-rows function through
   every walk, the fold row drawn, the click; through edits and parking; the `editor-folds` case and
   arm. Build with no warnings; `scripts/gates.py --only ui,gate-docs`, nothing else rendering.
   **Falsified**: closed folds drawn as open lines, `rows` and `walk` fail. **Look**: `apps/canvas
   --editor` with a fold (a test-bed flag for one), and without, against its noise floor, rendered
   twice first. As built, committed.
2. **Folds in the vim mode**: `zo` to `zM`, moves and operators over closed folds, `'foldopen'`'s
   defaults; the standard mode's three commands; the oracle sequences. Gates as in 1.
   **Falsified**: `j` stepping into a closed fold's lines, the oracle fails. As built, committed.
3. **Filler rows**: the place's fillers, `ui_editor_set_fillers`, `ui_editor_line_rows`, the
   shown-rows function's fillers, the filler row drawn, the click; the `editor-fillers` case and
   arm. Gates as in 1. **Falsified**: fillers left out of the count, `walk` fails. **Look** as in 1.
   As built, committed.
4. **Gutter marks**: the two style fields, drawn; the theme's two colours; the `editor-gutter` case
   and arm; `ui-theme-identity`. Gates as in 1. **Falsified**: the sign drawn on every row, `sign`
   fails. **Look**: `apps/canvas --editor` unchanged by default. As built, committed.
5. **Docs**: `docs/ui.md`'s editor section (folds, fillers, the gutter's two fields, the theme's two
   colours); `docs/eda-roadmap.md`: a 14.16 bullet under "Not among the nine" for pcb's diff, item
   6's "left for later" without folding and gutter markers, the smaller specs' line to 14.16, and
   14.15's bullet without its sentence on filler rows. Committed.
