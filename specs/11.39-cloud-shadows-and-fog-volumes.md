# Spec 11.39 — D2: cloud shadows and local fog volumes

## Context

Roadmap D2, both halves, agreed. Two gaps in the shipped atmosphere that share one integration
point, `froxel_inject_frag.glsl`:

- **Cloud shadows.** B2 shipped volumetric clouds and explicitly deferred their shadows
  (`specs/11.0-volumetric-clouds.md:251-258`, a bare naming with no design), so the sky has
  weather and the ground does not know. Clouds reach lit surfaces today *only* through the env
  bake, as ambient, on the slider-release cadence. The roadmap calls this "the highest
  look-per-line item left in the atmosphere stack".
- **Local fog volumes.** B1's froxel volume carries one global medium, so a smoky room, a dust
  shaft or a mist pocket cannot be authored — only the whole world's fog can change.

**Scope agreed:** both halves. **Cloud shadows ride the existing `--clouds` flag** rather than
their own — a cloud that casts no shadow is wrong rather than un-featured — with
`--no-cloud-shadows` as the bisect lever.

**Explicitly out of scope:** sampling the cloud shadow in `pbr_frag` for direct sun occlusion.
That half needs a `pbr_frag` sampler and is hard-blocked on D0 (`roadmap:848-852`). The roadmap's
own instruction is to do the froxel half first and *measure whether the surface half is still
worth a unit* — so this spec must end with that measurement.

State: `master` at `de171a9`, clean, **12 commits ahead of `origin/master`, not pushed**.

## What exploration established, because it reshapes the work

**1. The temporal accumulator will ghost this, and that is the main risk.** The froxel volume has
its own accumulator, ungated on TAA, with `fog_temporal_blend = 0.9` (`postfx.c:648`) — 10% new
per frame, ~29 frames to 95%. Reprojection is **camera-only** (`froxel_inject_frag.glsl:296-316`)
and there is **no history rejection**: line 313 is an unconditional
`mix(result, history, temporalBlend)`. The 2D accumulator clamps history to a 3×3 neighbourhood
for exactly this reason; the 3D one cannot afford 26 fetches × 921,600 cells.

Static media are free — that is the recorded argument for water at `:205-208`. **Wind-driven
cloud shadows are not static**, so they will trail a visible half-second smear. This is a
pre-existing latent defect that D2 is the first feature to expose, and it needs its own phase.

**2. The cloud deck is camera-anchored in XZ.** `cloud_march` sets `ro = vec3(0.0, obsAlt, 0.0)`
(`clouds.glsl:122`) and is fed only an altitude, so camera translation produces no cloud parallax
— documented at `clouds.glsl:13-22` as a deliberate compromise. A world-locked shadow therefore
cannot agree with the deck overhead. Decided below.

**3. Everything else is precedented.** `cloudDensity` (`clouds.glsl:63`) is a pure function of
position and samplers — no screen state, no history, no camera — and `sky_env_clouds_frag.glsl:34`
already calls the march from a non-screen pass, so a second consumer is proven. World-space `P`
is already in hand at `froxel_inject_frag.glsl:166`. Sampler units 0 and 4-15 are free in the
inject program, ~700 of 1024 uniform components are free, and UBO binding 5 is free.

## Phase 1 — the cloud shadow map

**A `Sky`-owned low-res 2D transmittance texture, built once per frame.** Owner is `Sky`, not
PostFX: Sky owns the noise, the sun and the wind clock. `sky_update_aerial` + `sky_publish_to_postfx`
(`engine.c:1757-1759`, `sky.c:331-339`) is the exact precedent — a sky-owned GL pass whose result
is handed to postfx one line later. It runs long before froxel injection (`postfx.c:2386`).

**Build:** one fullscreen pass over a ~256² `GL_R16F` texture. Each texel marches the cloud shell
(`CLOUD_BOTTOM_KM 1.5` → `CLOUD_TOP_KM 4.0`) along `sky->sun_dir`, accumulating optical depth from
`cloudDensity` and storing transmittance. Call `cloudDensity` directly rather than `cloud_march` —
no phase, no ambient, no LUT taps needed for a shadow.

**Parameterisation.** The layer is a horizontal shell, so transmittance along the sun ray depends
only on where that ray crosses the shell: a 2D map indexed by the crossing point is *exact* for
any receiver below it. Lookup for a froxel at `P` is `P.xz` sheared by the sun direction over
`(shellY - P.y)` — a couple of ops, no matrix, and correct at every altitude below the deck.

**The anchoring decision, and it is a real one.** The shadow map is **world-anchored**, the deck
stays camera-anchored, and they will not agree.
- World-anchored is required for the deliverable: a dapple that *slides across the terrain as you
  walk* is unmistakably wrong, and camera-anchoring would do exactly that.
- The deck's own anchoring is a pre-existing documented compromise, and the mismatch is only
  observable by cross-referencing a cloud overhead against its own shadow underfoot, which nobody
  does precisely.
- **File the fix rather than doing it here:** feeding the camera's world XZ into the march's sample
  position un-anchors the deck and makes both consistent — a small change that moves the cloud
  goldens and belongs in its own spec.

`sky->world_units_per_km` (`sky.h:128`) converts the km-space shell into world units;
`sky_aerial_far_units` (`sky.c:16-18`) is the existing pattern. **Sun convention:** `sky->sun_dir`
points *toward* the sun while `fog_light_dir` is travel direction — two conventions are live, so
the new code states which it uses at every boundary.

**Consumption** in `froxel_inject_frag.glsl`: multiply the directional light's contribution at line
217 by the sampled transmittance. One new sampler (unit 4), one new matrix-free lookup.

**Off-gate discipline.** Clouds off must allocate nothing and diff 0 px — the shipped pattern for
that is *two programs*, not a uniform branch (`sky.c:621-629`). Either follow it or prove the
`cloudShadowEnabled == 0` path inert at 0 px.

## Phase 2 — a history clamp for the froxel accumulator

**Phase 1 looks wrong without this**, and it is worth having on its own merits.

A **one-tap range clamp**: bound the history sample against the current cell's own σ and S before
the blend, rather than against a neighbourhood. Essentially free — no extra fetches — and it
bounds the ghost to the frames where the medium is genuinely changing, which is exactly the
wind-driven case. The 26-fetch neighbourhood clamp the 2D accumulator uses is unaffordable here;
this is the affordable substitute.

Also worth fixing while here: **nothing invalidates the history when fog parameters change.**
Dragging the density slider feeds the accumulator with no reset. `fx->froxel_prev_frame = -1` is
the existing one-frame-reset idiom (`postfx.c:1015`).

Measure the ghost before and after — a frame-to-frame delta on a wind-moving cloud shadow, the
same shape as `run_cloud_churn_gate` (`gates.py:1511`).

## Phase 3 — local fog volumes

**Scene-owned boxes, published to PostFX.** The water publish idiom (`water.c:349-382`) is the
pattern: a subsystem writes flat POD onto PostFX each frame and PostFX never learns about the
subsystem. But **water is not a template for the medium itself** — it is a world-Y half-space that
*replaces* air rather than composing with it, and exits early (`froxel_inject_frag.glsl:196-212`).

**Authoring:** a **top-level `fogVolumes[]`** block in `.cscn`, not `post.fog.volumes`. The rule is
recorded at `cscene.c:373-378`: fog is a field on PostFX, but a volume is a scene-graph citizen with
a transform, so it belongs beside `water` and `wind`. Copy `lights[]`'s cap-and-warn array parsing
(`cscene.h:222`).

**Delivery:** a plain uniform array for N ≤ 8 to start — 8 AABB tests × 921,600 cells is cheap
beside the 9 cascade taps and 128 clustered-light iterations already there. Bin into the cluster
grid only if profiling says so; the froxel grid already shares that grid's depth slicing, so
`clusterLightListUv` is the upgrade path.

**Combination, and this is the part with a real trap.** The integrate pass treats `cell.rgb` as the
medium's *source function*, not as pre-scaled radiance (`froxel_integrate_frag.glsl:66-68`). So:
- a box that only changes **density** is a one-liner at `froxel_inject_frag.glsl:186`:
  `sigma += boxDensity`;
- a box with its own **tint or albedo** must combine as a σ-weighted average,
  `S = (σ_air·S_air + σ_box·S_box) / (σ_air + σ_box)`. Adding radiances double-counts.

**Arming, and the lesson this repeats.** A box must **not** set `fog_enabled` — that is spec
11.33's defect #1, a permanently latched flag. Add a term to the pass gate at `postfx.c:2371-2372`:
`(fog_enabled || water_medium != 0 || local_fog_count > 0)`, with count-zero as the single off
state.

`upload_fog_uniforms` (`postfx.c:2147-2197`) is the single edit site for fog uniforms, by its own
stated contract.

## Phase 4 — fixtures, gates, and the D0 measurement

**Cloud shadows** need a fixture where the dapple is visible and deterministic. `cloud_fixture`
exists; a fog-plus-clouds variant gives a froxel volume for the shadow to land in. The arm asserts
the ground under a cloud is *darker than* the ground beside it — an in-frame ratio, not a stored
image.

**Local fog volumes** need a box over a known backdrop: the arm reads inside-box against
outside-box, and a second arm asserts the count-zero path is **0 px** against `--no-fog`.

**The measurement the roadmap asks for:** with cloud shadows live in the froxel volume, judge
whether the `pbr_frag` surface half is still worth a sampler unit and record the answer. That is
D2's stated exit condition, and it decides whether D0 gets scheduled.

## Verification

Baseline: **151/151 gate arms, 23/23 goldens 0 px** at `de171a9`.

- **Determinism is a hard gate here.** Every cloud golden claims byte-determinism ×2, and the wind
  clock rides `engine->render_delta`, which is fixed-step headless. Any wall-clock or
  non-frame-index randomness in the new pass breaks all of it.
- **Goldens at risk:** `cloud_fixture`, `cloud_fixture_lowsun`, `aerial_fixture`, `froxel_fog`.
  Cloud shadows riding `--clouds` means only frames that asked for clouds can move; budget the
  re-bake, and confirm the non-cloud goldens are 0 px rather than assuming it.
- **The off-gate:** `--no-cloud-shadows` and count-zero fog volumes must each reach 0 px.
- Measure the noise floor first; `--no-auto-exposure -E 1.0` for anything cross-build; no other
  GPU work alongside the suite; `prepass-crossover` is the known-flaky timing arm.
