# Spec 13.45: fog cells without history take several samples

## Context

When the camera turns in silent, the volumetric fog shows a hard-edged vertical band at the
leading screen edge. It looks different from the fog beside it and catches up once the camera
stops. The user's screenshots show it on the right when turning right and on the left when
turning left.

**Cause, read in the code (`cetra/shaders/froxel_inject_frag.glsl`):**
- The fog is a 160×90×64 grid of cells covering exactly the camera's view (`postfx.h`
  `POSTFX_FROXEL_*`), with no margin past the screen.
- Each frame a cell is sampled once, at a jittered point: `halton(frameIndex+1)`, the same offset
  for every cell, moving the sample ±½ cell sideways and across the whole depth slice. Its shadow
  tests are taken at that one point; the cached-light tile tap and the rain-cover tap are binary.
- The cell then blends 90% against the previous frame's volume, reprojected through the previous
  camera (`:439-467`). A cell whose reprojection falls outside the previous volume gets no
  history and keeps that single raw sample.
- On a turn, the columns newly entering the view therefore show one jittered sample, where their
  neighbours show a 10–20-frame average. The difference is quantised to the grid's 160 columns,
  which makes a straight vertical edge.

**The fix follows Unreal and Flax** (Flax's `MissedHistorySamplesCount`, 1–8, preset values 4 and
8, documented as reducing noise when panning or on camera cuts at a variable cost): a cell with
no history takes N samples and averages them.

**Rejected: a guard band** (a fog volume wider than the screen). A cell finds its point lights
through the screen's light grid, which ends at the screen edge, so cells past it would build up
history with no point lights in it.

**Intended outcome:**
- The band at the leading edge of a turn is gone, or no longer visible.
- Every still frame, and every cell that has history, is unchanged to the pixel.
- The extra cost appears only on frames where the camera moved, and scales with the cells that
  missed.

## The design: a second pass that discards every cell with history

The fix runs as a second draw over the 64 slices, straight after the inject draw and before the
integrate draw. It is the same source file, compiled again with `#define FROXEL_HISTORY_MISS`, and
every cell that has history is discarded. That gives three things a loop inside the inject shader
cannot:

- **Byte identity by construction.** The inject program never contains the miss loop, and a
  discarded cell is never written. AGENTS.md records 130 px of change on this driver from an `if`
  whose new branch was never taken.
- **No register pressure on ordinary frames.** The heavy shader is unchanged.
- **An exact count of the cells that missed.** A `GL_SAMPLES_PASSED` query around the pass counts
  the fragments that survive the discard, which gives the gate its non-vacuity check.

**The shader** (one file, so "where every medium meets" stays true):
- **`froxelJitter(int index)`:** the Halton point.
- **`froxelMediumAt(jitter, nearZ, out submerged)`:** today's per-sample body, word for word: the
  jittered point, the media, the water early-out, local fog, cloud, rain, the directional and
  spot lights, the cluster walk keeping `clusterLightListUv(TexCoords, -viewPos.z)`, and the
  σ/rain fold. Returns the clamped (S, σ).
- **`froxelHistoryAt(nearZ, out prevUvw)`:** the one statement of the reprojection rule (the
  `centreP` reprojection and the `[0,1]^3` test). Both passes call it, so they cannot disagree about
  which cells have history.
- **The default `main`:** today's statements, through those functions.
- **The miss `main`:**
  - Discard a cell that has history, or one whose first sample is underwater.
  - Otherwise average `missSamples` samples taken at the N most recent Halton points
    (`frameIndex+1`, `frameIndex`, … wrapped at the start), with equal weights.
  - Those are the points the neighbours' averages weigh most, so the cell agrees with its
    neighbours. The next frame takes a new point.

**The C side:**
- `postfx.c`:
  - The miss program is created optional, following `ssr_fold_wet_program`'s precedent.
  - A helper `upload_inject_uniforms` is shared by both passes, so their uniforms cannot drift.
  - The pass runs only when `temporal && N > 1 && the camera moved`. The move test is just a cost
    skip: identity never depends on it.
  - N is clamped where it is used, not where it is stored.
  - `create_froxel_miss_program` in `program.c` uses the existing `shader_source_with_defines`.
- `PostFX.fog_history_miss_samples` (SETTINGS, default 4; 1 is today's behaviour), with
  `POSTFX_FOG_MISS_SAMPLES_MAX` 8:
  - a config-snapshot row `postfx.fog.history_miss_samples`;
  - a GUI slider beside Temporal Blend;
  - `--fog-history-miss-samples N` on the render app and on silent, for the A/B.
- `PostFX.fog_miss_probe` and `--fog-miss-probe` on the render app: print
  `fog-miss-probe frame= ran= cells=` from the query.
- `RENDER_CAM_AT_MAX` goes from 16 to 128: an array size only. The cost runs need turns longer
  than the profiler's window.

## Phase 1: the instrument, and the arm red on today's code (draws nothing new)

**The gates.py turning capture:**
- Render app, 640×400: `--fog --no-aerial --no-auto-exposure -E 1.0 --no-dither --no-bloom`
  `--no-vignette --no-ssao`, no TAA.
- 60 still frames, then 15 `--cam-at` entries turning the target about a level eye by 1.72° a
  frame (silent's 1.8 rad/s at 60 Hz).
- The distance to the target is kept constant, since the fog's near plane follows it.
- The reference is the same 75 frames posed at the final pose throughout.

**The columns, worked out in Python** from the two cameras, with the grid size read from `postfx.h`:
- L: the columns missed on the captured frame;
- S: the columns swept since the turn began;
- C: the clean columns, at least |S| + T + 2 columns from the leading edge.

**The metric.** D(c) is the mean signed luma difference of column c against the reference.
- band = mean |D| over L
- floor = mean |D| over C

**Arm `fog-miss-band`:** band / max(floor, 0.25) ≤ `FOGMISS_RATIO_MAX`. It must be red on today's
code.

**The fixture.** Measure band and floor on two candidates and pick the clearer:
- `contact_fixture` at a level pose (its shadows are ESM-soft);
- `cornell_point` with the lamp cached (`shadow_cache`) and `--fog --fog-density 0.3`: the
  tiles-fog setup, whose binary tile tap is what silent's candles do.

**Silent's before-capture, reported only:**
- `--player-at` on the street at night, `--pad-script` with `rx=1` for 30 frames, `--no-flicker`.
- Compare frame 330 against the converged frame at the same pose.
- Show the user crops of today's band.

## Phase 3: the fix

- The miss variant's `main`, the C pass, the move skip, the query and probe, the field, the config
  row, the slider, and both apps' flags.
- **Arms**, all in a new `fog-miss` group:
  - **`fog-miss-band`, its second half:** the default's band ≤ `FOGMISS_REDUCE_MAX` × the band at
    N=1; and the band at N=1 ≥ `FOGMISS_PRESENT_MIN` × floor, so a band exists to remove.
  - **`fog-miss-confined`:** the turning frame at the default against N=1. The C columns differ by
    0 px, so no cell with history was touched. The S columns differ by more than
    `FOGMISS_SWEPT_MIN`, so the fix did something.
  - **`fog-miss-count`:** the probe reports `ran=0` on frames 1–59. On frames 60–74 it reports
    `ran=1`, with `cells` equal to |L|×90×64 within one column. That proves the cells really
    missed, and that the shader's validity rule matches the geometry.
  - **`fog-miss-static`:** the `froxel_fog` golden's recipe at the default against N=1, 0 px, with
    `ran=0` throughout. That covers frame 0 (`temporal == 0`) and a still camera.
- Thresholds are set from the measurements on the fixed build, with margin, and recorded with
  their numbers.
- The goldens (0 px expected everywhere), then gates:
  `--only fog-miss,fog-volume,fogdepth,cloud-shadow,night-floor,overcast,water,rainfall,shadow-tiles,scale,exposure,config,gate-docs,lighting-stream`.
  The `exposure` group matters because its teleport now goes through the miss pass.
- **Cost, an A/B of N=1 against 4,** since the profiler's "atmosphere" scope covers the whole fog
  chain:
  - silent `--profiler` over a sustained turn;
  - the render app turning for 120 frames;
  - the render app with a cut every frame (the variable-cost worst case);
  - still runs, which should read equal.
  - Also silent's `--startup-ms`, since the miss variant is another compile of the heaviest post
    shader.

**The user looks (not the last step):**
- Before and after crops of the headless turn, and of silent.
- Then the user runs silent windowed from the worktree, at night on the street, turns with the
  mouse or arrow keys, and flips G → Volumetric Fog → History Miss Samples between 1, 4 and 8.
  Warn them the first launch recaptures GI.
- The default N, or the design, changes here if the look calls for it.

## Risks

- **The Phase 2 refactor is the one place identity is not guaranteed.** It is measured against
  the full set of branch references, not only the goldens.
- **Cut frames cost up to N× the inject work for that one frame.** Measure it. A cap belongs to a
  later spec.
- **Turns faster than the gate's could miss through depth** (near slice 0). That is cheap and
  correct, and `fog-miss-count`'s exact count would expose it in the gate.
- **The trailing-edge spread margin (T+2 columns) is an argument until it is measured.** It is
  measured before `fog-miss-confined` is trusted.
- **In silent, the TAA fog-layer accumulator may already hide part of the band**, so the windowed
  improvement may be smaller than the headless one. The user's look decides.

## As built

### Phase 1: the instrument, and the arm red

**What drives the band, measured in silent.** Headless, on the street at night (`--player-at
-0.8,4,200`), turning right at full stick for 15 frames. Each run is compared against a run that
made the same turn 100 frames earlier and then held still, so the two last frames share the pose
and the frame number. The figures are the mean signed luma difference per fog column, in codes.

| Run | Last 5 columns (missed) | Clean columns (floor) |
|---|---|---|
| as it ships (TAA on) | 0.62 | 0.05 |
| `--no-taa` | 0.60 | 0.01 |
| `--no-taa --no-rain` | 0.03 | 0.00 |

**The rain is what the band is made of.** In the fog cell, its medium takes:
- a binary cover tap (`rainExposure`);
- a steep `smoothstep` handover between 0.75× and 1× `rainNear`, near the eye in silent, whose
  streak radius is 2.5 m.

A cell's one sample lands anywhere across its depth slice, so those two vary widely within a cell.
With the rain off, nothing else in the street varies enough to show. There is no time dependence
in the term (`rainDropPhase` is a phase function), so averaging samples in space within one frame
is the right remedy.

**The fixture.** Both planned candidates showed no band at a level turn:
- cached `cornell_point` with fog: 0.17 codes against 0.02;
- `rain_fixture` with the lamp away from the edge: 0.04 against 0.13.

`rain_fixture` with the lamp's lit rain entering at the leading edge as the turn ends shows it
clearly: **1.40 codes against a floor of 0.015**.
- The pose: eye (0, 2.4, 11), yaw −41° to −15.2°, 1.72° a frame.
- The previous frame's missed columns still carry −0.5, the next frame's blend keeping 90% of
  their single sample. That is why the band on screen is several frames wide.

**Built:**
- **`fog-miss` group**, its first arm `fog-miss-band`: the missed columns' mean |D| against the
  clean columns' floor (never below 0.25 codes), at most `FOGMISS_RATIO_MAX`, which is
  provisional at 2.0.
  - The missed columns come in closed form from the two cameras.
  - The grid width is read from `postfx.h` and the FOV from the fixture's own camera.
  - The fixture and pose are `FOGMISS_*`.
- **Red on today's code:** 5 columns, 1.400 codes against 0.015, **5.60× the floor**.
- **`RENDER_CAM_AT_MAX`** is 128, and `docs/cli-reference.md` says so.

### Phase 2: the refactor, with nothing changed

**The references**, rendered from the Phase 1 build twice, are 0 px between the two runs, so any
pixel that moves is real:
- the `froxel_fog` golden's recipe;
- `water_fixture` submerged;
- `fog_volume_fixture`;
- `aerial_fixture` with clouds and fog;
- cached `cornell_point` with fog at 0.3;
- `rain_fixture` with fog, with and without jittered TAA;
- the `fog-miss` turn and its held twin.

**The shader**, `froxel_inject_frag.glsl`:
- **`froxelJitter(index)`:** the Halton point.
- **`froxelMediumAt(jitter, nearZ, out submerged)`:** the per-sample body, moved in order. The
  water branch returns its value and sets `submerged` in place of writing and returning.
- **`froxelHistoryAt(nearZ, out prevUvw)`:** the centre's reprojection and the `[0,1]^3` test.
- **`main`:** the jitter, one medium sample, the water exit, and the blend through those three.
- The comments moved with the code they describe.

**Measured:**
- Every reference is **0 px** against the refactored build.
- All 32 goldens pass at 0 px.
- `fog-volume`, `fogdepth`, `cloud-shadow`, `rainfall` and `shadow-tiles` pass, 76 arms in all.
- `fog-miss-band` fails as before, to the third decimal (1.400 codes, 5.60×).
- No fallback or re-bake was needed: on this driver the code moving into functions changed
  nothing.
