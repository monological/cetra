# Spec 13.56: the dock lamp, an FFT lake and a motorboat

Branch and spec `13.56-silent-motorboat`, in the main checkout (no worktree: a fresh one takes too
long to build), branched from master at 8e063eb5.

## Context

Since 13.41 the lake has a dock, a rowboat moored off its end and another upturned on the bank.
- Both boats are hulls lofted from kit quads in house paint.
- The moored one is merged into the lake's static kit.
- The water is a calm Gerstner plane.
- Nothing lights the dock. The only lights by the water are the cabin's fire and its table lamp.

The user wants:
- **A lamp at the boat**: brass that goes up and hangs down, glass and brass like the hall's
  lantern but round rather than square, a warm light, lighting the boat.
- **FFT water** on the lake.
- **A far more realistic boat**, textured, with **a working outboard on its stern**:
  - E at the boat gets in;
  - E starts the motor, and the player drives it round the lake;
  - in the middle of the lake the motor cuts out and has to be started again;
  - starting it is a pull cord: a marker slides along a bar, and E pressed while the marker is in a
    small zone is a good pull; three good pulls start it.
- **The boat is the user's download**, `~/Downloads/wooden_boat.glb`: "Wooden Boat" by o0ozexo0o
  on Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0.
  - A 3.4 m rowboat, weathered white outside and red inside, with ribs, three thwarts and a small
    square transom.
  - 1,880 triangles, a 1024 colour map, a 512 metal/roughness map and a 1024 normal map.
  - Its units are centimetres, z up, the bow toward -y.
- **The outboard is modelled in a Blender script**, as silent's other models are, after the user's
  reference photo:
  - an old two-tone tiller outboard with a rounded cowl;
  - a pull-start handle on its front, a clamp bracket on the transom, a long leg;
  - a cavitation plate, a gearcase with a skeg and a three-bladed propeller.

Decided without the user (each is one constant or a few lines to change):
- **The motor cuts out once a trip**: the first time the boat reaches the spot circled "here?" on
  the cabin's lake map, near the middle, after each time it leaves the dock.
- **A missed pull does not count and does not reset**: three good pulls in all.
- **The bank's upturned boat is the new hull too**, with no motor.
- **The player gets out only alongside the dock.** Nowhere else is shallow enough to step out.
- **The lamp is lit always**, like the cabin's fire and its lamp: someone has just left.

## Behaviour

**The dock lamp.**
- A brass post stands on the dock's end beside the mooring, rises about 2.4 m and bends over in a
  shepherd's crook.
- From its hook a short chain hangs a round lantern over the moored boat:
  - a brass base, and a domed brass cap with a finial and a ring;
  - a clear glass cylinder between them, held by brass straps;
  - inside, a clear bulb with a glowing filament.
- Its light is a warm cached point light, shaped by the brass. It is bright enough to show the boat
  and the dock's end under the night's pinned exposure, and it scatters in the fog.
- The glass beads in the rain.

**The lake.**
- The FFT wave model, authored calm: a light wind and no swell.
- Small wind waves and ripples in the shading; the surface itself hardly moves.
- Rain, wading and the touch ripples carry on as before.

**The boats.**
- The moored boat and the upturned one are the downloaded hull, sized in metres.
- The moored one carries the outboard on its transom and oars along its thwarts, and is roped to the
  tall post.
- No water shows inside a floating hull. The engine's water gets dry shapes for that.

**Getting in.**
- From the dock's end, with the boat in reach: "E   Get in the boat".
- The view eases down onto the stern thwart, beside the tiller, facing the bow.
- The rope is cast off.

**In the boat, with the motor off.**
- The player looks round freely. The boat carries the view: its turn, its heave and its roll.
- Movement keys do nothing, and the boat drifts to a stop.
- The prompt is "E   Start the motor". Beside the dock it also shows "RMB   Get out" (B on a pad).

**Starting.** E opens the pull cord.
- The view turns to the motor.
- A bar shows under the middle of the screen: a marker running back and forth, a small zone lit on
  it, and three marks for the pulls.
- E pulls: the start handle snaps out from the cowl and back, the view jolts, and the cord rips.
  - In the zone: a good pull. A mark fills, the motor coughs, and the zone moves somewhere else.
  - Outside the zone: a weak pull, with no cough. Nothing is lost.
- The third good pull catches: the motor sputters into its idle, the bar goes, and the view turns
  back to the bow.
- RMB or B gives up the start.

**Driving.**
- **Controls:**
  - W, or the stick forward, opens the throttle.
  - S slows the boat, then backs it.
  - A and D, or the stick sideways, swing the tiller.
  - E stops the motor.
- **Handling:**
  - The boat gathers speed slowly, to a little over 3 m/s.
  - It turns better the faster it goes, and slides a little in a turn.
  - It coasts when the throttle closes.
  - The bow lifts with speed, and the hull leaves a wake.
- **The motor:** it turns on its bracket with the tiller, and its propeller spins. Its sound rises
  and falls in pitch with the throttle.
- **Collisions:** the shallows and the dock stop the boat. The hull bumps, then slides along them.

**The cut-out.** The first time each trip that the boat reaches the circled spot:
- the motor stumbles, sputters and dies, and the boat glides to a stop;
- the player thinks "No... not out here.";
- the prompt is "E   Start the motor" again, and the start is the same pull cord.

**Getting out.** Alongside the dock and nearly stopped, RMB or B:
- the motor stops, if it is running;
- the boat is brought round onto its mooring and roped;
- the player stands on the dock beside it.

**Saves.**
- A save remembers:
  - where the boat is and which way it points;
  - whether the player is in it;
  - whether the motor has cut out this trip.
- A load puts the boat there with its motor off, with the player in it if they were.

## Design

### The engine

**Dry shapes on the water** (`water.h`, `water_bounds.h`, `include/water_bounds.glsl`).
- **What a shape is:** `Water.dry[WATER_DRY_MAX]` and `Water.dry_count`. Each `WaterDry` is a centre
  in xz, a yaw, a half length, a half beam and a power: a superellipse in its own frame where the
  water is not.
- **Zero shapes is today's water**, to the bit.
- **One rule in each language**, placed where the bounds' rule is now. A point is in the water's
  domain when it is inside the bounds and outside every dry shape.
  - `water_under` and `waterUnder` ask that domain.
  - Everything that asks the bounds today asks it too:
    - `water_frag` discards outside it;
    - the downwell (`water_light.glsl`, so `pbr_frag` too);
    - the shore's wet band (`shore.glsl`);
    - the fog volume's water test;
    - the rain's splash lift and drip landing;
    - the eye's submerged tests.
- **Uploaded beside the bounds**, wherever they are. Each program pays a count plus two vec4s a
  shape. `pbr_frag`'s uniform total is checked against the driver's limit.
- **The render app** gets `--water-dry cx,cz,yaw,hl,hb[,power]`.
- **Arms in the `water` group**, both on the downwell fixture, whose lit floor shows the light
  under the level:
  - `water-dry-none`: a shape of zero size leaves the frame at 0 px.
  - `water-dry-cut`: a shape over half the frame gives the no-water frame there and the plain frame
    elsewhere, both at 0 px.

**Re-pitching a held sound** (`audio.h`, `audio.c`).
- `audio_sound_set_pitch(sound, pitch)` sets the playback rate, as a voice's pitch already does.
- An arm in the `audio` group: a tone held at pitch 2 renders at twice its frequency.

### silent

**The FFT lake** (`lake.c`).
- `wave_model = WATER_WAVES_FFT`, with a calm `sea`:
  - the swell's scale is 0, and the depth is the bed's;
  - the wind sea's speed and fetch are tuned by stills.
- `--lake-waves gerstner|fft` is the A/B, and the cost is read with `--profiler` at the dock.

**The round lantern** (`fixtures.c`).
- `fixture_lantern_round(kit, f, a, y, d, r, h)`, the square lantern's sibling. It shares the
  square one's crown, finial and bulb.
- Its glass is a thin-walled lathe cylinder.
- A new `MAT_LAMP_GLASS`: the clear glass with rain beads, for the outdoors.

**The dock lamp** (`lake.c`).
- The post is a brass pipe on a lathed foot: up, then over in a Bezier crook.
- `lights.c`'s `chain` becomes public as `fixtures_chain`.
- A body stands on the deck for the post.
- The lamp does not move, so it lives in the lake's kit.
- Its light is a cached point light named `dock_lamp`, warm (about 1.0, 0.70, 0.40), its intensity
  tuned by stills.
- Its filament is a material of its own, so the haunting's red filament leaves it alone.

**The boat model** (`tools/make_boat_blender.py`, new).
- **The source** is copied to `assets/models/wooden_boat.glb`, as `army_flashlight.glb` was.
- **The hull** is scaled to metres and turned into model space, the bow toward +z.
- **The outboard** is modelled on the transom's middle, in parts that move:
  - `motor` pivots on the swivel bracket, for the steering;
  - `prop` turns about the propshaft;
  - `cord`, the start handle, moves along the cord.
- **Its paint** is two-tone: a pale grey cowl with a dark band and a faded stripe, and a dark grey
  leg.
  - The scans come from Poly Haven through `polyhaven.py`.
  - They are worn and grimed by occlusion and pointiness, and baked into the motor's own atlas by
    Cycles, as the birdcage is.
- **The oars** are their own object, so the upturned boat goes without them.
- **Anchors:**
  - `seat`, the seated eye;
  - `bow_eye`, the rope's end;
  - `stern_wash`, where the wake leaves the hull;
  - `cord_pull`, where a pulled handle reaches.
- **It writes:**
  - `assets/models/boat.glb`, crediting the hull's author in its copyright;
  - `apps/silent/src/boat_shape.h`, a GENERATED header with:
    - the dry superellipse, fitted inside the hull's outer waterline;
    - the draft;
    - the hull's plan half-length and half-beam, for collisions;
    - the mooring's numbers;
  - with `--preview`, a sheet of stills.
- **The user sees the look before anything uses it.**

**The boat in the world** (`boat.c/h`, new).
- **Loading:** brought in through `library_take` as its own node, `capture_hidden`, so the cached
  lights and the captures treat it as a mover.
- **The upturned copy** is a second import, lying where the old one lay, with its old body.
- **Each step** it holds the pose: position, heading, speed, slip, yaw rate, throttle, tiller and
  rpm.
- **The motion:**
  - Thrust is throttle times rpm, against linear and quadratic drag, with reverse at 40%.
  - The yaw rate eases toward the tiller times (a little plus the speed).
  - Slip is damped hard.
  - Heave, pitch and roll are a few slow sines, about 2 cm and a degree, plus a bow lift with speed.
- **Collisions are analytic:**
  - three circles along the keel, tested against `lake_shore_distance` past the wading ring and
    against the dock's rectangle;
  - on contact they slide, lose the speed along the normal, and knock.
- **Each frame** it draws the step-interpolated pose:
  - it places the node, and sets the motor's yaw, the prop's spin and the cord;
  - it sets the water's dry shape;
  - it drops wake ripples at the stern while moving (`water_wake`);
  - it pitches the motor's sound loop by the rpm.
- **The rope** to the post is a small kit node of its own, shown only while the boat is moored.

**Riding** (`player.c`).
- `Player.riding` holds the move, the crouch and the footsteps, but not the look. (`holding` holds
  the look as well.)
- While riding, the controller is disabled. Its position follows the seat each step, so a save
  finds the player there.
- `player_pre_render` takes its anchor from the boat's seat, and the rig's yaw turns with the boat.
- `player_teleport(p, feet, yaw)` stands the player somewhere, for getting out.

**The interaction** (`silent.c`).
- The boat joins the reach dispatcher, offering "E   Get in the boat" while it is moored.
- While riding, the boat owns E and RMB through its own step, as inspect does. The reach list stays
  empty.
- Its prompt joins the HUD's chain.

**The pull cord** (`pull_cord.c/h`, new).
- Its state: the marker's phase, the zone and the good pulls.
- It draws through one out-of-flow element on the HUD's screen (`hud_set_gauge`), like the save
  mark: a dark plate, a pale track, the zone in amber, the marker and three marks.
- The marker sweeps the bar in about 0.9 s each way, and the zone is about a tenth of the bar.
- It is non-modal, so `interact` still reads.

**The cut-out.**
- The circled spot is the lake map's `(-6, 2)` from the lake's centre, checked against
  `make_cards.py`'s `px()`.
- It is armed when the boat leaves the dock, and fires within 4 m of the spot.

**Sounds** (`fetch_sounds.py`). CC0 Freesound recordings, auditioned for the user to choose by ear:
- a running outboard loop, re-pitched by the rpm;
- the cord's pull;
- a cough;
- the catch into idle;
- the stumble and death;
- the stop;
- a hull knock;
- water slapping the hull, louder with speed.

**Saves** (`save_game.c`).
- A `boat` section holds x, z, heading, riding and cut.
- A load calls `boat_restore`, with the motor off.

**Flags.**
- `--in-boat` starts seated at the mooring.
- `--boat-at X,Z[,YAW]` starts seated out on the water.
- `--boat-running` starts with the motor running.
- `--pull-cord` opens the start.
- `--lake-waves` picks the wave model.
- `--trace-boat` traces the boat's state.

Every start flag goes in `starts_elsewhere`, and the seated starts are applied in `start_play`.

## Phases

1. **The FFT lake.** Sea state, the A/B flag, stills from the dock and over the water by night and
   day, and the cost. The user looks.
2. **The dock lamp.** The round lantern, the crook post, the light and the glass. Night stills of
   the dock's end and the boat under it. The user looks.
3. **The boat model.** `make_boat_blender.py` and its preview sheet. The user looks before it goes
   in.
4. **The boats in the world, and dry water.**
   - The engine's dry shapes and their arms.
   - The two boats placed, and the rope.
   - Stills from the dock, and looking down into the hull.
5. **Riding.**
   - Getting in and out, the boat's motion and collisions, the camera and the wake.
   - A key starts the motor for now.
   - The user drives it.
6. **The motor.**
   - The pull cord, the sounds and the pitch, the cut-out and its thought.
   - The sounds are auditioned first.
   - The user drives it.
7. **Saves, flags, docs, reviews.**
   - AGENTS.md: silent's row; the Water inventory line for dry shapes; the audio line for pitch.
   - `docs/cli-reference.md`.
   - Scoped gates: `--only water,audio`, and the goldens once.
