# cetra 14.44: a pinch and a label cut short

## Context

pcb 10.2's datasheet pane, run by the user (pcb's spec, phase 12), wants two things cetra does
not have:

- **The trackpad's pinch.** The pane zoomed on Ctrl and the wheel, which macOS takes for its own
  zoom where that is on, so on a Mac the pinch is the zoom and cetra hands an app none. GLFW 3.4,
  cetra's window library, has no callback for the gesture on any system.
- **A text held to a few lines.** The pane says where a datasheet it lacks is published, an
  address of any length. A wrapping label breaks it into lines -- cetra breaks a word longer than
  its line -- but a very long one fills the pane, and nothing in cetra shortens a text.

pcb's AGENTS.md says that where cetra's UI cannot do something, pcb stops and asks rather than
working round it. Asked, the user chose both in cetra, in one spec, landed and pinned before pcb
uses either.

Branch and spec: `14.44-a-pinch-and-a-label-cut-short`, off cetra's master (`65fc4b01`).

## The design

### The pinch

```c
// InputState, beside the wheel:
// The trackpad's pinch since the previous frame ended, on the wheel's schedule: the part of
// itself what is pinched grows by, + as the fingers part, so a scale is multiplied by 1 + pinch.
// A pinch while the debug GUI has the pointer is dropped, as a scroll is.
double pinch;

// UIInput, beside the wheel:
float pinch; // the pinch this frame, as InputState's
```

- **The engine's input, and the UI's.** An app reads `engine->input.pinch` in its update hook, or
  `UIInput.pinch` after `ui_input_read`. The UI's own elements do nothing with it.
- **macOS, without a fork.** GLFW is an upstream submodule, pinned, and has no handler for
  AppKit's magnify event; it is not forked for this. cetra watches the event itself: one
  Objective-C file, `pinch_macos.m`, puts a local monitor on the app's magnify events
  (`NSEventMaskMagnify`) for the engine's window (`glfwGetCocoaWindow`) and adds each event's
  `magnification`, AppKit's own change in scale, to the frame's sum. The monitor hands the event
  on. The event comes in a poll, so it wakes an idle loop as a scroll does.
- **Other systems read 0.** Windows hands a precision touchpad's pinch to a window that asks for
  no pointer input as Ctrl and the wheel already. Wayland's pointer gestures and X11's XInput 2.4
  each want a hook as the Mac's has. Neither can be looked at from the machine this is built
  on, so neither is in this spec.
- **The script.** `pinch=<f>` in `--input-script` is a frame's pinch, through the call the
  monitor makes.
- **`apps/canvas`** zooms the view under the pointer by `1 + pinch` about the pointer, as it
  does by the wheel.

### A label cut short

`max_rows`, which a multi-line field has, on a wrapping label:

```c
// The most rows a multi-line field grows to before it scrolls, or a wrapping label shows: where
// the label's text runs on past them, its last line ends in "…". 0 = no most.
int max_rows;
```

- **The last line is cut so the mark fits**: broken at the label's width less the mark's, its
  trailing spaces left off, the mark drawn after it and counted in the line's width, so a
  centred last line is centred with its mark.
- **The mark is U+2026** in the label's face, which an atlas holds where the face has it;
  three full stops in a face that has none, which would draw the missing-glyph box for it.
- **The height is the lines shown.** A label of `max_rows` 3 over a text of nine lines measures
  three line heights.
- **One row is a text shortened to its width**: a wrapping label of `max_rows` 1.
- **Kept as the lines are**: the same width, face, size, tracking and most break nothing again.
- A label that does not wrap, and any other element, does not read it, as `wrap` is not read
  off a control.

## Not in this spec

The pinch on Windows and on Linux. AppKit's other gestures: rotate, swipe, the smart zoom of
two taps. The pointer's shape over a link or a text, which pcb's pane also lacks. A label that
takes a press, pcb pressing its address through its own pointer. A text cut in its middle, or
at its start.

## As built

### Phase 1: the pinch

- **As designed**, with the watch an object of its own: `PinchWatch`, `create_pinch_watch` and
  `free_pinch_watch` in `pinch.h`, handed the window and a function to tell. The Objective-C
  file so names nothing of the engine's, whose header brings GL's, and `pinch.c` is the other
  systems' "none to make". The engine makes one for a window on a screen and frees it before
  the window; a headless run has its script's pinch alone.
- **A script replaces the hand**, as it replaces the mouse: what the watch hears while one plays
  is dropped (`_engine_pinch_heard`).
- **The build**, which took three tries, the two that failed now said beside the one line in
  `cetra/CMakeLists.txt`: `enable_language(OBJC)` there made every `.m` of the build `OBJC`,
  GLFW's five with it, in directories with no rules for the language, and the generate step
  failed; `LANGUAGE C` on the file made CMake pass `-x c`, and Cocoa's headers do not compile
  as C. Listed and nothing more, CMake gives a `.m` to the C compiler and clang reads the
  language off the extension, which is how GLFW's own are built.
- **The commit hook's cppcheck** reads a `.m` as C and stops at its first message send; `.m`
  is left out of that hook.
- **`canvas-pinch`**, in the canvas group, plays a script at `apps/canvas` under its probe:
  pinches of 0.25, -0.2 and 0.5, the last with the pointer elsewhere, with a frame of none
  between. Each multiplies the zoom of the view under the pointer by 1 + itself, from where
  the last left it; the unit under the pointer is the same before and after; the frame of
  none says none. Its first run failed on the probe and not the pinch: the zoom, 1.7e-5 points
  a unit, was printed to nine places, four digits of it; it is printed to fifteen digits.
- **Falsified**: the pinch not cleared with the wheel at a frame's end. The frame of none
  pinched again, and the arm failed on the pinches it read and on a fourth said.
- **Not looked at by me: a hand's pinch.** The script goes through the engine's entry and not
  through AppKit's monitor, which only a hand on a trackpad reaches. The user's to pinch, in
  `out/bin/canvas` and then in pcb.
- **Docs**: `docs/architecture.md`'s Input, `docs/ui.md`'s input, `docs/cli-reference.md`'s
  script, `docs/modules.md`'s rows, `docs/building.md`'s compiler, `apps/canvas/README.md`.
- Run: `scripts/gates.py --arms canvas-pinch,cam-drag-zoom,gate-arm-docs`, the camera group
  whole for the script's wheel, whose reading the pinch's shares.

### Phase 2: the label

- **As designed.** `UILines` keeps its most and, where the text runs on, that it is cut and
  the byte its last line ends at; `ui_lines_break` takes the most, and what keeps the lines
  reads it with the width. The line cut is fitted again at whichever glyph leaves the mark its
  room, not at a space (`_fit_end`): a line is the text's last word or a long one's first
  part, and a word kept whole could leave it empty. `ui_lines_end` gives a cut line its cut,
  and `ui_draw_text_lines` draws the mark after it, its width part of the line's.
- **The mark** is asked of the label's face each time (`_cut_mark`): U+2026, or three full
  stops where the atlas holds none.
- **A field's rows** pass no most; its `max_rows` stays how far it grows.
- **`ui-label-rows`**, in the UI group, reads gametest's `--ui-probe label-rows`, a label
  across 280 points:
  - a paragraph of four lines held to three, and to one, and an address of one word held to
    two: that many lines, that many line heights, cut; every glyph inside the width; the last
    line's glyphs its text's and the mark's; the line with its mark inside the width and with
    the glyph cut away put back past it;
  - the paragraph held to 99, and a text of two lines held to three: whole, no mark;
  - laid out twice more, broken once; at another most, broken again.

  The probe's first draft put back the character after the line's trimmed end, which after a
  space is the space, and read a line with room to spare; it puts back the glyph the cut
  stands at.
- **Falsified**, two mutations in one build, each caught by its own key: no room left for the
  mark, and the three cut steps drew to 286.12, 284.62 and 288.62 of 280; the most left out of
  what keeps the lines, and the label was broken once in three layouts and showed three lines
  where two were asked.
- **The UI group whole passes**, the lines' breaking being every element's with text.
- **Looked at**: `apps/canvas --ui --ui-text ... --ui-rows 3`, a flag added to look, the
  panel's label in three lines ending `dog and keeps on r…`.
- **Docs**: `docs/ui.md`'s Layout gains the label's most rows, and beside it what pcb found
  wrapping an address: a wrapping label measures as its longest word, along a row every
  parent keeps that measure up to the window, and a parent sized as a share stops it.
  `docs/cli-reference.md` and `apps/canvas/README.md` say `--ui-rows`.
- Run: `scripts/gates.py --only ui,gate-docs`.

### Phase 4: the reviews

Seven reviews ran as background agents at effort high: `/simplify`'s four (reuse,
simplification, efficiency, altitude), the thermo-nuclear review, and correctness of the pinch
and of the label. All were back before anything was applied.

- **Applied, to the pinch:**
  - **A frame's changes compound; the design summed them** (altitude, and the pinch's
    correctness, each on its own). A change is a scale's, so two in a frame are
    `(1 + a)(1 + b) - 1`. Summed, a slow frame's many small ones zoom further than the hand
    did, and enough closing ones pass -1, a scale of nothing. A change of -1 or below is no
    pinch, and the script refuses one. A line of the script may hold four `pinch=`, each a
    change of its frame, which is what lets an arm hold the rule.
  - **The watch is freed before the debug GUI**, whose state what it hears asks for; it was
    freed after, safe only because nothing polled between the two.
  - **A pool** round the monitor's making and its removing, both called outside GLFW's own;
    the comment says the block keeps the window it names.
  - **The `.m` is in the build's digest**, whose list had only `.c`, `.h` and `.cpp`.
  - The script's numbers read by one `_script_number`; `pinch.h`'s opening held to its
    contract; the canvas app's probe line a `say_pinch` apart from the zoom.
- **Applied, to the label:**
  - **The mark's room and its place come from one sum** (thermo-nuclear, with simplification,
    reuse and efficiency on its parts): `_cut_mark` gives the mark and its reach, the tracking
    before it and its width, which the break leaves and the draw fills. The two had each
    worked it out, 180 lines apart, and differed for a line of no text. The atlas is asked
    with `font_get_glyph` in place of a walk of one glyph, and the draw is one arm.
  - **A label held to a most measures as its mark** (altitude): what it can shorten to, where
    its longest word, an address the whole of one, held every parent that fits or grows round
    it to that width and left the label nothing to shorten for (`ui_text_cut_width`,
    `_intrinsic`). The pairing of `wrap` and `max_rows` is this spec's, so nothing before it
    measures otherwise. `docs/ui.md` says the rule -- an axis that measures no content, a
    FIXED one or a share, stops a measure going up -- where it said pcb's recipe.
  - **A tail of white space is no cut** (the label's correctness): `abc\n` held to one line was
    drawn `abc…` with nothing hidden. The lines stop unmarked where only spaces and line ends
    are left (`_runs_on`), and a last line leaves a tail of both kinds off (`ui_lines_end`).
  - **A text with no face is still held to its most**, cut where its line broke; the guard had
    let the lines run on past it.
  - The room clamped where a tracking below the mark's width would have made it more than the
    width; the rule of who has a most in one place, `_most_rows`, beside `_wraps`.
- **Applied, to the arms and the probe:** `ui-label-rows` holds where the mark stands, its
  last line's rightmost glyph after its text, where it had counted glyphs and would have
  passed a mark drawn over the line's start; a text of exactly its most, and one with a tail of
  white space, whole; and the measure with no width. `canvas-pinch` holds three closings by a
  half in one frame as one of -0.875. The probe measures by `ui_text_span_width` on the text it
  has, steps past the cut by `text_utf8_next`, and shares `drawn_right` with the two probes
  that had the loop.
- **Skipped:**
  - The mark and its width kept with the lines (efficiency): a lookup and three glyphs a cut
    label a frame, worth it for hundreds of them.
  - `_fit_end` merged into `ui_text_break` (reuse): two flags in the shared loop, which the
    thermo-nuclear and the simplification reviews each said to leave.
  - A sentinel, `starts[count]`, in place of `cut` and `cut_end` (the thermo-nuclear review's
    candidate): a field's one row writes its lines by hand, and the pair is right zeroed.
  - The mark in a styled run's face and colour where the cut falls in one: the label's, as
    designed.
  - The watch made for a headless run too (thermo-nuclear): altitude found the gate right, a
    hidden window never an event's, as the clipboard is the engine's own there.
  - A pinch's phase: one cancelled keeps the zoom it had made.
- **Falsified**, three mutations in one build, each caught by its own key: the changes summed,
  and the fourth pinch read -1.5 with the zoom unmoved; the measure left as the longest word,
  and the label with no width was 1114.988 points wide to its mark's 12.372; every text at its
  most cut, and the tail's line was marked.
- Run: `scripts/gates.py --only ui,gate-docs`, and `--arms ui-label-rows,canvas-pinch,
  cam-drag-zoom,gate-arm-docs` with the canvas group's arms that draw the UI (`canvas-under-ui`,
  `canvas-ui-borders`, `-seams`, `-lines`, `-table`, `-table-pane`, `-pictures`, `-over`,
  `canvas-panes`), every label's draw having changed.
