# Spec 11.75 — depth-aware AO upsample, and the chain's last 8 bits

## Context

GTAO runs at half render res into a `GL_RGBA8` target and every consumer magnifies it with
plain hardware bilinear — `tonemap_frag.glsl:16` says so itself: *"upsampled by its linear
filter."* On `cornell_rooms` that produces the two artifacts the user reported and I measured:

- **A doughnut.** The AO term reads **0.974 directly at the occluder's contact and 0.936 a
  hundred pixels out** — occlusion inverted at the one place it should be deepest, because
  bilinear blends AO texels across the silhouette. The ring's diameter scales with `ao.radius`
  (verified at 0.4 vs 1.2).
- **Contour bands** across the floor: the AO term spans 0.92–1.00, ~20 codes in 8 bits.

This is the standard half-res-AO problem and the standard fix is the one every shipping engine
uses: a **joint-bilateral (depth-aware) upsample**, plus enough storage precision that the
temporal accumulation isn't re-quantized every frame. Neither GL 4.1 nor the sampler ledger
blocks it — both consumers already bind `auxTex` (full-res linear view-Z, NEAREST, RGBA32F)
with free units to spare, and the AO buffer is the **only half-res post buffer still stored at
8 bits** while its own history ping-pong beside it is already RGBA16F for exactly the banding
reason (`postfx.c:421-425`).

**Coverage fact that shapes the plan:** there is NO ao gate group — the chain's only executable
assertion in the suite is golden pixels. All 27 goldens render GTAO+split on; 25 will move,
2 are structurally immune. Spec 11.4 is the precedent for exactly this shape of change
(predicted-verdict table, A/B inspection, one revertable rebake commit).

**Three design corrections found during planning, all load-bearing:**

1. **The banding arm cannot read a debug view.** Any single 8-bit screenshot re-quantizes at
   display, so a 16F gradient and an 8-bit-stored one produce the same plateau statistics.
   The arm must recover sub-LSB precision from lit frames: AO-on/AO-off **ratio**, averaged
   along iso-AO contours (shading varies along a contour and decorrelates display rounding;
   the AO staircase is common-mode and survives the mean).
2. **The half-res texel-centre UV lands on an exact full-res texel boundary** ((i+0.5)/half_w
   × 2·half_w = 2i+1.0), where NEAREST rounding is implementation-defined. Consumer depth taps
   use `texelFetch(auxT, ti*2, 0)` — explicit and deterministic; the residual one-texel
   ambiguity vs GTAO's own read is inside the depth tolerance on surfaces and bounded by the
   nearest-depth fallback at cliffs.
3. **No new fixture needed.** `gen_ao_fixture.py` already authors a sphere resting on a ground
   quad — its docstring says the sphere exists for "the sphere-on-ground GTAO/contact
   interaction". The contact arm uses it; the banding arm uses cornell_rooms' floor where the
   0.92–1.00 span was measured. (cornell_rooms has no free-standing box — the diagnosed
   contact is the partition base.)

Branch `11.75-depth-aware-ao-upsample`; spec copied to
`specs/11.75-depth-aware-ao-upsample.md` and committed first, alone.

## Phase A — joint-bilateral upsample at both consumers

**New `cetra/shaders/include/ao_upsample.glsl`** (auto-picked-up by the GLOB; sampler-as-
parameter per `probe_specular.glsl`'s precedent):

```
vec4 aoFetchBilateral(sampler2D aoT, sampler2D auxT, vec2 uv, vec2 aoRes, float zRef)
```

- Sky/aux-missing guard first: `zRef >= -1e-4` → plain `texture(aoT, uv)` (GTAO's own sky
  constant, `gtao_frag.glsl:92`; also covers the tonemap's aux-unbound case, preserving
  current behaviour byte-for-byte at sky).
- 2×2 footprint: `st = uv*aoRes - 0.5; base = floor(st); f = st - base`; taps at
  `ti = clamp(ivec2(base)+ivec2(dx,dy), 0, aoRes-1)` — explicit clamp, texelFetch ignores
  CLAMP_TO_EDGE.
- Per tap: `zi = texelFetch(auxT, ti*2, 0).z` (the depth the AO texel represents);
  `w = wBilinear * exp(-dz*dz)` with `tol = max(0.05*|zRef|, 0.02)` — the blur's own
  constants verbatim (`ssao_blur_frag.glsl:33,42-43`). Sky taps (zi==0) self-reject.
- AO value via `texelFetch(aoT, ti, 0)`. Accumulate the **full vec4** so `.gba` (bent normal,
  affine-encoded) shares the weights; one `normalize` downstream recovers it.
- Fallback: track min-|zi−zRef| tap; `wsum < 1e-4` → return it (nearest-depth; no divide-by-
  ~0 at silhouettes). ss_scale=2's 1:1 case degenerates to identity modulo ulps — note in
  the header.

**`include/spec_occ.glsl`**: `specOccSplitAt(uv, invFocal)` → `(uv, invFocal, vec4 aoSample)`;
delete its internal `texture(aoTex, uv)` at :67 — the includer supplies the sample, so each
consumer performs exactly ONE bilateral fetch per pixel. Two includers only, both updated here.
**`ssao_blur_frag.glsl` is not touched** — it is also the contact-shadow denoiser, pinned by
the contact group and the `contact_debug` golden.

**`spec_occ_composite_frag.glsl`** (the live path — split is the default and tonemap's AO
multiply stands down under it, `postfx.c:3599`): add `uniform vec2 aoRes`, include the new
file, fetch `zRef = texture(auxTex, TexCoords).z` (aux guaranteed present past the aoActive
gate), one `aoFetchBilateral`, pass the vec4 into `specOccSplitAt`.

**`tonemap_frag.glsl`**: same uniform + include; route `aoVisibility()` (:316) through it.
**Debug views show the FILTERED value** — what the frame received, not what the buffer stores
— so view 7 and the live composite cannot drift and one revert of the include flips every
consumer at once. Stated as a choice in the spec; nothing in the suite reads `--ssao-debug`.

**`postfx.c`**: upload `aoRes = (half_width, half_height)` per draw at both dispatch sites
(the :986-989 house rule — never seeded). Fix the stale comment at :3570 (`ao_result_tex` is
always `ssao_texture[1]` since the SVGF reorder). No profiler scope renamed (`gpu-rows`
asserts "gtao sweep" by name).

## Phase B — storage: GL_RGBA8 → GL_RGBA16F

`postfx.c:407-411`: one format literal, and the rationale comment at :404-406 rewritten — it
currently argues FOR 8 bits. 16F stops re-quantizing the RGBA16F temporal accumulation to 8
bits at the blur every frame, and removes the ~20-code banding. Nothing else moves:
`gl_transfer_format` already handles RGBA16F (hdr_fbo), the float clear is format-agnostic,
and SSGI's RGBA16F attachment-1 on `ssao_fbo[0]` becomes homogeneous rather than mixed.
Memory +2.88 MB at a 1600×900 render. `VIS_EDGE_SOFTNESS 0.25` stays (it compensated 8-bit
bent-normal contouring among other things; revisiting it is not this spec).

## Phase C — the first `ao` gate group

`run_ao_gate` in gates.py, registered after `contact`; docstring lists the arms exactly
(gate-arm-docs enforces name and order). Thresholds as fractions of frame height so 1x/2x
framebuffers both pass.

- **ao-contact** — the doughnut instrument, on `ao_fixture.cscn`'s sphere-on-ground. Two
  renders differing only in `--no-ssao` (plus `--no-shadows --no-ssr`, pinned exposure);
  per-pixel ratio = the applied AO factor. Three floor bands walking outward from the
  projected contact point (geometry derived from the generator's authored camera, not
  hardcoded): assert contact ≤ ring − margin AND bands non-decreasing outward. The defect
  reads 0.974 vs 0.936 — an inversion this fails loudly.
  **Falsified by:** reverting the include's depth weight to pure bilinear (one line).
- **ao-banding** — on cornell_rooms' open floor. Same on/off ratio; per-row mean over ~100+
  floor columns = a 1-D AO profile at ~1/(255·√N) effective precision. Assert max flat-run
  length below a bound (eps derived from a measured identical-config noise floor).
  **Falsified by:** reverting Phase B to GL_RGBA8 — ~20 plateaus return.
- **ao-deterministic** — the contact frame ×2 at AE == 0 (no --taa → accumulators
  force-invalidated, GTAO frame-static).

Both falsifying mutations run by hand, recorded in the spec, reverted.

## Phase D — golden rebake, the 11.4 method

1. Predicted-verdict table for all 27: **25 move** (GTAO+split live in every recipe; expect
   small PAE, silhouette-concentrated diffs plus a broad sub-code dequantization field),
   **2 structurally immune** (`contact_debug` — early-return debug view reading only csTex;
   `backface_dark` — black frame). The rebake's own twice-render/refuse-on-drift mechanics
   confirm the immune pair by skipping them byte-identical.
2. **A/B crops to the user BEFORE baking** (house memory: look changes get crops early):
   cornell_rooms (partition base + floor band + the known-open stair-step region),
   ao_fixture's sphere contact, cornell_box.
3. Measure the known-open cornell_rooms stair-step (specs/11.70, filed shadow-related,

4. One revertable `goldens.py --rebake` commit, same build type as the stored bakes.

## Perf protocol

Light scene at high res, A/B/A/B interleaved, time-adjacent samples only (the profiler cannot
price sub-ms scopes on heavy frames and drifts between sessions): `dir_shadow_fixture.cscn`,
1600×1000, master binary stashed in the scratchpad vs branch, ≥3 pairs. Watch "spec occ
composite", "ao denoise", "gtao sweep", frame. Budget: ≪ 0.5 ms — 4 aux + 4 AO texelFetches
replacing one bilinear fetch, at render res, composite only (split-path tonemap pays nothing).

# CORRECTION, after Phase A was built and measured

Everything above is the plan as approved. Phase A's own measurement falsified its central
claim, so this section is the record of what is actually true. The original text stays because
what was believed is part of what happened.

## The claim that was wrong

**"The doughnut is bilinear blending AO texels across the silhouette, and a joint-bilateral
upsample fixes it."** It is not, and it does not.

Phase A shipped exactly as designed and the contact profile is **identical to four decimals**
before and after. The arithmetic that should have been done before any of this was planned: a
2x2 footprint on a half-res buffer reaches about **4 full-res pixels**. The ring spans
**74-137**. The two scales were never the same order, and nothing in the diagnosis ever
compared them.

The disproof was already in the first measurement -- the profile that produced "0.974 at the
contact against 0.936 a hundred pixels out" also showed the structure spanning ~560 px, which
is the kernel's reach and not a filter footprint. A second signal was ignored the same day:
`edge_filter: false` moved 1% of the frame and left the contact profile byte-identical, which
already said that nothing at AO resolution decides that profile.

**What the ring is instead.** `gtao_frag.glsl:142` computes
`screenRadius = 0.5 * p00 * radius / -linZ`, clamped at `MAX_SCREEN_RADIUS` 0.08 UV -- about
137 framebuffer pixels at the diagnosis framing, which is exactly where the measured ring
structure ends. It scales with `ao.radius` because it IS the kernel. And it is systematic
rather than sampled: 300 accumulated TAA frames leave the profile's shape intact (the
0.961 -> 0.930 dip survives), so the temporal accumulator cannot average it away. The standing
hypothesis, untested and belonging to the follow-up rather than to this spec, is estimator
error -- `SLICES = 2` (gtao_frag.glsl:48) with `HEIGHT_BIAS` 0.04 plus the `zGrain` slope
compensation and a `THICKNESS` 0.25 slab, which is a coarse enough horizon estimate to have
systematic distance-shaped error across its own reach.

## What Phase A does do, and why it stays

Cross-silhouette bleed at the 2-4 px scale is real, was unaddressed, and is now gone. It moves
**3,592 px of 7.3M** on `cornell_rooms`, all of it in thin bands at silhouettes -- the exact
reach of the footprint, which is the measurement confirming both that it works and that it
could never have been the ring. With AO off it is **0 px**, every changed line being dead
there.

The `specOccSplitAt` signature change earns its place independently: the AO fetch is now four
taps rather than one, and the old shape had the composite paying for it twice per pixel.

## What changed in the remaining plan

**Phase C's `ao-contact` arm is deleted.** It was broken in both directions at once: it
asserted a non-inverted contact profile that the shipping build does not produce, so it would
have gone red on correct code; and its named falsifying mutation does not move the value it
reads, so it could not have gone red on broken code either. Replaced by **`ao-silhouette`**,
which reads what Phase A actually changed -- a band just outside an occluder's silhouette
against a control band further out, asserting no foreign AO crossed the depth edge, falsified
by reverting the include's depth weight to pure bilinear.

**Phase B's banding premise is now explicitly on trial rather than assumed.** The 0.92-1.00
span is measured; that RGBA8 storage is what quantizes it is not. There is a second candidate
parent in the tree already: aux is RGBA32F precisely because "fp16 view-Z staircases at scene
scale (banded GTAO on large grounds)". `ao-banding`'s mutation -- revert the format -- is
therefore the test of Phase B's own justification, and the reading gets recorded whichever way
it falls. Phase B keeps two other legs regardless: it stops re-quantizing the RGBA16F temporal
accumulation to 8 bits at the blur every frame, and every other half-res post buffer is 16F.

**Phase D's prediction softens** from "25 move" to "up to 25": Phase A alone touches only
silhouette bands, so the rebake's skip-if-byte-identical is the measurement rather than the
prediction being it.

## The follow-up this spec names and does not do

The user's actual doughnut. Its first step is the discriminator that should have preceded all
of this: force the AO chain to full resolution -- one allocation change, diagnostic only -- and
see whether the ring survives. If it does, resolution is exonerated entirely and the
investigation is the kernel; if it vanishes, the half-res INPUTS to the horizon march (the
NEAREST depth snap, `zGrain`) rejoin the suspects. Either way it changes the look of every
scene with AO on and wants its own spec, its own crops and its own golden decision.

## The process failure, which is the transferable part

The diagnosis matched a symptom CLASS -- bright rim at a depth discontinuity is the classic
bilateral-upsample halo -- and stopped there. No scale check, though the data to do it was
already printed.

Then it was laundered. The brief handed to the three planning agents opened with "The defect
(user-visible, diagnosed, measured)" and told them to treat it as ground truth. They did
exactly that, and did it well: the Plan agent returned three corrections, every one of them an
implementation detail (texelFetch versus UV arithmetic, an unmeasurable banding arm, the
fixture choice). None questioned the premise, because the premise had been presented as
settled. **A hypothesis described to a subagent as verified will come back optimized, not
tested** -- the agents can only audit what they are told is open.

The cheap thing that would have caught it: before planning, ask what SIZE the proposed cause
can produce, and compare it to the size of the artifact.
