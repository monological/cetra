# 11.42 — Water: sun glitter, persistent foam, and a spectrum that knows itself

## Context

Cetra's water subsystem (specs 11.32–11.36) was ported from
`siliconjungle/inkwell-webgpu-water`, closely enough that the sea state, the cascade
table, the fade windows and the PRNG are the same numbers. Comparing it against that repo
and against `owenyuwono/poseidon` (an independent take on the same gasgiant/Horvath
lineage) found the port ahead of both on mesh, filtering, refraction, motion vectors and
optics — and behind both on three things, two of which 11.32 planned and never delivered:

1. **There is no sun on the water.** `water_frag.glsl` declares `sunDir` (`:58`) and uses
   it in exactly one place, the caustic crossing point (`:358`). The reflected share is a
   single split-sum cubemap lookup, and `sky_env_frag.glsl:6` says outright *"NO sun
   disc"*. So under the procedural sky the sea reflects a sky with no sun in it: no
   glitter path, no sparkle, no specular streak. 11.32 planned the Cox-Munk port
   (`:205`); Phase 3's as-built never mentions it again.
2. **Foam is instantaneous.** `water_frag.glsl:225-227` computes foam from this frame's
   Jacobian and discards it. Poseidon keeps a per-texel turbulence that snaps down on a
   fold and recovers slowly (`maps.js:47-49`); inkwell backtraces a foam field through the
   local velocity with separate birth and decay rates (`:397-407`). Whitecaps here appear
   and vanish with the crest instead of being left behind by it.
3. **The spectral sea state is not data.** `WATER_SEA_DEPTH`/`WIND_SPEED`/`FETCH`/
   `WIND_ANGLE`/`PEAK_ENHANCEMENT`/`SWELL` are `#define`s (`water.c:51-56`), and the
   `water{}` block carries none of them. `windDirection` is authorable but **dead on the
   FFT path** (`ocean.glsl:436` reads `waterWindDir` only in the Gerstner branch). Since
   11.35 made FFT the default in `apps/tree` there is no way to author a calmer or
   differently-oriented spectral sea.

This spec also closes the two D3 open items ranked *above* those, because both are the
surface being wrong rather than plain:

4. **The waterline is answered twice at different scopes.** The normal flip
   (`water_frag.glsl:252`) is per-PIXEL; the optical-path branch (`:269`) is per-FRAME;
   and `cameraSubmerged` is one bool from `cam_world[1] < water->level` against the STILL
   level (`water.c:945`). A crest closing over a camera still above the still level gets
   its normal flipped toward the eye and is then charged its path against the depth buffer
   *behind* the surface, which is air there.
5. **The projector aims at the still plane, not the displaceable slab.** Each lattice ray
   meets the flat plane, so a wave can lift geometry into view from XZ the lattice never
   sampled; and `above = waterCamPos.y >= waterLevel` (`ocean.glsl:104`) picks one side of
   the eye plane per frame when a straddling camera needs both.

**The unifying idea.** Items 3, 5 and the far-field roughness fix all want the same
missing number: what the seeded spectrum's variance actually *is*. Accumulating height
variance and slope variance inside `_water_build_spectrum` yields the displacement bound
the slab offset needs, the mean-square slope the roughness endpoint needs, *and* the
Cox-Munk lobe's slope variances — which is strictly better than the reference's
hardcoded clean-sea constants. It also gives a derivation for `OCEAN_BOUND_LONG_VAR` /
`OCEAN_BOUND_MED_VAR` (`ocean.glsl:245-246`), hardcoded estimates of exactly that
quantity. So the statistics come first and three later phases consume them.

**Outcome:** a sea with a sun in it, whitecaps that linger, an authorable sea state, a far
field whose roughness comes from the spectrum rather than a constant, a waterline decided
per pixel, and the first instrument in this subsystem that can falsify the FFT itself.

## Decisions taken

- Every feature ships **ON by default with its own `--no-` lever**, matching
  `--no-water-caustics` / `--no-water-lod` / `--no-water-coverage`. Both water goldens
  re-bake; `apps/tree` and `apps/forest` frames move.
- Phases land in dependency order, each independently committable with its own bar.
- **Out of scope, named so they read as choices:** the nearshore shallow-water solver, a
  plunging breaker patch, spray particles (deferred with still-valid reasons at
  11.32:289-292); Johanson's full two-plane projector; extending the CPU wave query
  (`water_waves.c:155-157`) to the FFT path.

## Phase 0 — the waterline is one question, answered once

- `water_frag.glsl`: hoist `bool seenFromBelow = (cameraSubmerged == 1) || !gl_FrontFacing;`
  above the flip at `:252` and use it for **both** the flip and the optical-path branch at
  `:269`.
- `ocean.glsl:99-143` (`oceanProjectedPosition`): the `above` split at `:104` picks one
  side per frame. When the eye is inside the displaceable slab the surface is visible on
  both sides of the horizon row, so span the full `[-OCEAN_OVERSCAN, OCEAN_OVERSCAN]`
  there; outside the slab keep today's one-sided span exactly. Use the Gerstner analytic
  half-thickness for now; Phase 4 supplies the spectral one.
- **Bar:** `water_fixture` and `water_submerged` 0 px — at amplitude 0.06 no crest ever
  shows its underside, so `!gl_FrontFacing` is never true and **the fixture cannot see
  this**, which is the point and another instance of D3 item 3. New `water-waterline` arm
  on a shallow high-amplitude framing where it can. All other goldens 0 px.

## Phase 1 — the sea state becomes data

- Promote the six `#define`s (`water.c:51-56`) to `Water` fields, defaults identical, so a
  build with no scene change is byte-identical.
- Plumbing per key, following the precedence the tree already has (**CLI sentinel →
  `.cscn` `has_*` → `create_water()` default**): field + `has_` in `CSceneWater`
  (`cscene.h:163-205`), a `get_float` line in `parse_water` (`cscene.c:421-434`), an
  `if (w->has_x)` line in `apply_cscene_water` (`cscene_apply.c:338-367`), field + default
  in `create_water` (`water.c:293`), a GUI row (`gui.c`). Add the keys to
  `assets/water_fixture.cscn` (`:45-73`), which authors every parsed key today.
- **Re-seed on change**, not a uniform: these are baked into `cascade_initial`. Route
  through the existing latch — clear `spectra_ready` so `_water_ensure_spectra`
  (`water.c:631`) rebuilds. Free at load; a GUI slider would hitch, noted rather than
  solved (poseidon does it async).
- Make `windDirection` mean something on the FFT path: it becomes the seeding wind angle
  instead of being silently ignored.
- **Bar:** all goldens 0 px with defaults unchanged; a `.cscn` arm varying wind speed
  proves the surface moved (`_water_cscn_variant`, `gates.py:3648`).

## Phase 2 — the spectrum's own statistics, plus an instrument that checks them

- In the seeding loop (`water.c:166-250`) accumulate per cascade `Σ E[|h0|²]` (height
  variance) and `Σ k²E[|h0|²]` (slope variance). Two accumulators, no new loop.
- **Do not trust the derivation.** The synthesis is an unnormalized inverse DFT and
  `h0 = (ga + i·gb)·A` rather than the textbook `(1/√2)(ξ_r + i·ξ_i)√S`, so the
  normalisation is exactly what D3 item 1 says "has to be traced". Add `--water-fft-probe`
  in the shape `--water-probe` already uses (field `render_args.h:89`, help
  `render.c:157`, `strcmp` arm `render.c:695`, print block after `render.c:3041`, stdout,
  distinct line prefix, does not exit): read back the transformed height channel with
  `glGetTexImage` (precedent `postfx.c:3289`) and print measured RMS and max against the
  predicted values.
- Cross-check `OCEAN_BOUND_LONG_VAR` 0.080 / `OCEAN_BOUND_MED_VAR` 0.030
  (`ocean.glsl:245-246`) — hardcoded estimates of the same quantity. If the derivation is
  right they land near those; if not, that is a finding about the crest-sharpening term,
  not about this phase.
- **Bar:** predicted vs measured within a stated tolerance, printed; 0 px everywhere,
  since nothing consumes the numbers yet.

## Phase 3 — an FFT that can be falsified

`water-fft-det` (two runs diff 0 px) and `water-fft-live` (differs from Gerstner) would
both pass on a wrong-but-deterministic transform — a swapped twiddle sign, an off-by-one
butterfly partner, a missed fftshift. That is the failure mode 11.39 shipped with the
cloud shadow map, and D3 item 3 already says this corpus has a structural blind spot.
Poseidon (`fft.js:114-153`) has this test; Cetra does not.

- Extend `--water-fft-probe` with an impulse mode: seed a scratch cascade with a centred
  DC impulse, run the existing 14 stages, assert a constant field; then an impulse at
  `(N/2+1, N/2)`, assert `cos(2πx/N), sin(2πx/N)`. Print max error.
- **Bar:** new `water-fft-correct` arm asserting both `< 1e-3`, written as a stdout arm
  mirroring `water-cpu` (`gates.py:4079-4101`) with its own `_water_*` runner in the
  helper block at `gates.py:3603-3751`. No pixels, no scene.

## Phase 4 — the projector aims at the slab

- Offset the intersection plane toward the eye by the displacement bound: Gerstner is
  analytic (`amplitude × Σ 0.44^i`, i<4 = **1.7188**); spectral takes Phase 2's measured
  height variance at a stated sigma multiple, labelled as the padded estimate it is.
  Zero extra lattice rows — it moves the plane, it does not add geometry. Completes
  Phase 0's slab test with a real half-thickness.
- Record honestly that this **reduces rather than removes** the artifact when the eye is
  inside the slab: the horizon-row clamp still cannot reach a crest projecting above the
  flat-plane horizon. The complete answer is Johanson's two-plane construction, not
  attempted here.
- **Bar:** the Phase 0 shallow-framing arm shows the near-field wedge closing;
  `water_fixture` movement quantified and explained.

## Phase 5 — the horizon's roughness comes from the spectrum

- `WATER_ROUGH_RIPPLED = 0.115` (`water_frag.glsl:146`) caps the far field however much
  slope was filtered away. Both Cetra and inkwell use that constant; Cetra is the only one
  of the three that knows how much it dropped (`s.filtered`) *and* what the total was
  (Phase 2), so it is the only one that can derive the endpoint.
- Convert the removed variance fraction to a GGX roughness against the band's mean-square
  slope instead of lerping toward a literal.
- **Bar:** `water-farfield` (`gates.py:4228-4272`) re-read — it already measures far-box
  spread, near-box invariance and authored-roughness sensitivity, and its
  `near_delta == 0` clause is the guard that this did not leak forward into the near
  field. Goldens re-baked with the delta quantified.

## Phase 6 — Cox-Munk sun glitter

Split in two so the risky half lands on a verified base.

**6a — one source of truth for the sun, 0 px.** `#include "lights_ubo.glsl"` in
`water_frag.glsl` (UBO wiring is automatic at link, `program.c:306`/`:420`) and take
direction from `dirLights[0].dirShadow.xyz` and the CSM slot from `.w`. Delete the
`sunDir`/`sunAvailable` uniforms and the hand-rolled light scan at `water.c:920-942`.
**Bar:** `water-caustic` unchanged, all goldens 0 px.

**6b — the lobe.** Port inkwell's facet-slope BRDF (`webgpu-water-engine.ts:888-906`):
wind-aligned along/across mean-square slopes, Smith visibility, dielectric Fresnel on
`dot(V,H)`. Wind comes from Phase 1; **the slope variances come from Phase 2**, not from
Cox-Munk's clean-sea constants.

- **Radiance needs no new C plumbing.** `dirLights[k].colorIntensity.xyz` is
  `color × intensity` premultiplied (`light_cluster.c:178-183`), and
  `sky_apply_sun_to_light` (`sky.c:667-686`) has already folded atmospheric transmittance
  into `light->color` — so that one vec3 *is* sun radiance and transmittance together, in
  scene radiance, un-pre-exposed.
- **Multiply the lobe by `preExposure`** — already in scope (`view.glsl` via
  `water_frag.glsl:77`). Water applies it per-term because half its terms arrive
  pre-exposed, so this cannot ride a composite conversion the way `pbr_frag`'s `Lo` does.
  Clamp the BRDF factor dimensionlessly (`pbr_frag.glsl:310-319`'s `BRDF_MAX` reasoning),
  not the product.
- **Expect to need a tuning constant, and do not chase it as a bug.** `view.glsl:10-20`
  names the sky as the working-space contract's one unresolved exception: the procedural
  sun is 10 lux against a real ~1e5, and `SUN_ILLUMINANCE = 3.0` / `sunIntensity = 20.0`
  are on that arbitrary scale. Migrating the sky to lux is spec 10.2 phase 5, not this.
- **CSM is reusable — do not duplicate `pbr_frag`.** `#define CSM_OUTERMOST_PCF` before
  `#include "csm.glsl"` gives `csmOutermostOcclusion(worldPos, slot)`
  (`csm.glsl:101-159`), the same scene-fit outermost-cascade lookup the shadow catcher and
  particle motes use. It is camera-independent with no per-fragment cascade selection,
  which is the right lookup for a surface reaching the horizon. The per-fragment PCSS path
  is inline in `pbr_frag.glsl:605-775` and is ~170 lines that must not be copied.
  C side mirrors the catcher (`render.c:1318-1349`): `shadow_upload_cascade_uniforms`,
  `numShadowLights`, `shadowBias`, `shadowTexelSize`, own array bind.
- **Bind the cascade array on unit 14 or 15, NOT 10.** `SHADOW_MAP_TEXTURE_UNIT` is 10 and
  water already binds `cascadePrev1` (a `sampler2D`) there; two sampler *types* on one
  unit in one program is `INVALID_OPERATION` at draw. Water does its own
  `uniform_set_int(u, "shadowMaps", …)`, so this is entirely local and touches no
  `shadow.c`.
- **Cloud shadow composes against the occlusion**, catcher-style
  (`catcher_frag.glsl:61-62`): `occ = 1.0 - (1.0 - occ) * cloudSunAt(WorldPos)`, gated on
  `dirShadow.w == cloudShadowLight`.
- **Three comments go stale and must be updated in the same change:**
  `water_frag.glsl:380-384` and `water.c:939-941` both assert "water has no analytic sun
  lobe"; `water.h:54-58`'s "the water program binds neither" is the invariant
  `sampler2DArray shadowMaps` breaks.
- **Bar:** new `water-glitter` arm (`_water_box_luma` / `_water_roughness` on a sun-facing
  box, on vs off); `--no-water-glitter` reaches the pre-change frame at 0 px.

## Phase 7 — foam that lingers

- New `cetra/shaders/water_foam_frag.glsl` + `create_water_foam_program()`, a one-liner:
  `return create_post_program("water_foam", water_foam_frag_shader_str);`
  (`program.c:560-572` is the model — that helper handles MRT fine), declared beside
  `program.h:89-96`, registered with the 4-line stanza at `engine.c:1459-1472`. A new
  `.glsl` needs no reconfigure (`CONFIGURE_DEPENDS`); the new `water.h` fields do.
- Reads this frame's per-band Jacobian and last frame's turbulence, writes
  `min(J, prev + dt·decay/max(J,0.5))` (poseidon `maps.js:47-49`).
- **One pass writing one RGB 128² target**, three bands in three channels, sampled three
  times at three UVs — **not** three textures. That is what keeps it to **+1 sampler
  declaration** and **+1 draw** rather than +3 and +3. The submission budget is the real
  cost: 11.32 measured the 45-draw FFT chain at **+0.24 ms GPU against +1.19 ms CPU**.
- Build with `_water_make_field` (`water.c:580` — RGBA16F/LINEAR/REPEAT, and REPEAT is
  what a world-tiling field needs) and the FBO block at `water.c:675-698`; ping-pong pair
  plus a first-frame seed of 1.0 (un-foamed) following the `spectral_frames` /
  `cascade_prev` pattern at `water.c:756-770` and the `prev_ready` guard at `water.c:1031`.
  Add the textures to `free_water`'s bulk-delete idiom (`water.c:329`). Draw on the
  existing `fft_vao`. **GL 4.1 has no texture barrier**, so obey the same read/write
  separation the FFT ping-pong does. It inherits the `"water"` profiler scope; the
  spectral sim deliberately has none of its own (`water.c:848-857`).
- **Determinism survives:** `water.c:914` feeds `uDeltaTime` from `engine->render_delta`,
  which `engine.h:269-286` documents as the fixed-step clock headless. Frame N is state N,
  so `water-fft-det` still reads 0 px.
- **Breakup noise lands here and costs nothing:** inkwell's `valueNoise` (`:168-177`) is
  pure ALU, not the baked tiling texture 11.32:204 assumed. It modulates foam brightness
  and coverage only *after* the physical selection — it never chooses the crest.
- **Bar:** new `water-foam-persist` arm using `_water_foam_px` (`gates.py:3694`) — foam
  pixel count on frame N vs N+k after a crest has passed; `--no-water-foam-history`
  reaches the instantaneous frame.

## Sampler ledger, tracked across the spec

`water_frag` declares **14 of 16** today — 5 in the file plus 9 from `ocean.glsl`
(6 cascade targets, 2 prev, `bedTex`). Phases 0–5 cost **zero**, which is why they run
first. Phase 6 adds `shadowMaps` → 15 (verified: `csm.glsl` declares exactly one sampler,
`msm.glsl` and `lights_ubo.glsl` declare none). Phase 7 adds the foam target → **16/16**.
After this spec `water_frag` is where `pbr_frag` has been since 4.10, and nothing further
gets a texture here without an alias.
