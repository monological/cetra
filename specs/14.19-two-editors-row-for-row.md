# cetra 14.19: two editors row for row

## Context

pcb 6.5's phase 13 lines a compared pane's two sides up row for row, as GitHub's split diff does:
the version's text on the left and now's on the right, each run of unchanged lines folded alike on
both (14.16's folds), and in each change the shorter of a pair of lines padded with filler rows
(14.16's fillers) so the pair stands level. pcb scrolls the two together each frame. A review of
pcb's phase 12 found three things cetra does not give for that, each checked against the code:

- **No scroll by rows from the text's start.** pcb holds the sides together through
  `ui_editor_top_line` and `ui_editor_scroll_line`: a line and how far into it. A line's rows count
  from its first filler (`_shown`, `ui_editor.c:376-381`), so a line with fillers above it on one
  side and none on the other's counterpart maps to the other side a row off for each filler; and
  `_scroll_line` holds `rows` to the line's own rows and steps a row on past them, dropping the
  part (`ui_editor.c:1330-1339`), so a pair of unequal lines loses the leader's place inside the
  longer. Once every pair stands level the two sides show the same rows in the same order, and the
  plain way to hold them together is to copy the top's count of rows from the text's start. The
  editor keeps those sums already (`_top_of` and `tops`, `ui_editor.c:430-442`), neither read nor
  set in public.
- **No equal columns for two texts of different lengths.** The gutter is `_digits(line_count) +
  GUTTER_GAP` cells wide (`_place`, `ui_editor.c:1130`, and the draw, `ui_editor.c:3189`), from
  each editor's own text. A version of 999 lines beside now's 1000 differs by a column, so an
  unchanged long line wraps differently on the two sides, no filler covers it, and every row below
  drifts apart.
- **No filler under the last line.** `ui_editor_set_fillers` leaves out, and logs, fillers past the
  text's lines (`ui_editor.c:1505-1512`). A change at the end of a text with no last line ending has
  no line after it, so the shorter side's fillers for the other's extra lines have nowhere to
  stand.

Branch and spec: `14.19-two-editors-row-for-row`, off cetra's master (`8bf14eef`).

## The design

In `ui_editor.h`, beside `ui_editor_top_line` and `ui_editor_scroll_line`, and the folds and filler
rows block:

- **`double ui_editor_top_row(const UIElement* el)`**: the view's top as rows from the text's start,
  every row the editor shows above it counted -- fillers, a closed fold's one row, each line's
  wrapped rows, guessed for a line not wrapped yet -- and the part of the top row scrolled past:
  1041.25 is a quarter of the way down the 1042nd row. 0 for anything not an editor showing a text.
  A double, as a text's rows pass a float's whole numbers long before its lines run out.
- **`void ui_editor_scroll_row(UIElement* el, double rows)`**: the view put with its top `rows`
  rows from the text's start, held to the text's rows as a wheel's scroll is; before the editor is
  laid out on its text, taken at its next layout, as `ui_editor_scroll_line` is (a `Pending` of its
  own kind). The line holding the row is found by a binary search of the sums `_top_of` keeps, the
  row within it and the part set as `_scroll_line` sets them. A row inside a closed fold's lines is
  the fold's row; a filler row is a row like any other.
- **`void ui_editor_set_gutter_digits(UIElement* el, int digits)`**: the line numbers' gutter as wide
  as `digits` digits at least, so two editors on texts of different lengths keep the same columns;
  0, the default, as many as its own text's line count has. The editor's, not the text's, as its
  look is. `_place` and the draw take the larger of it and `_digits(line_count)`. Changed, the
  columns may change, and the rows wrapped at the old ones are guesses again, as a change of width
  makes them.
- **A filler under the last line**: `UIEditorFiller.line` may be the text's line count, the rows
  standing under its last line. They are drawn after it as filler rows are, counted in the text's
  rows (so in `ui_editor_top_row` and the scroll's end), and no caret stands on them: a move by rows
  stops at the last line, and a click on them lands at the text's end. Fillers past the line count
  are still left out and logged. Through every change they stay under the last line, whatever the
  line count becomes.

## Tests

Probe cases in `apps/gametest/src/ui_editor_probe.c`, each read by an arm in the `ui` group with
`doc_flags` and its docstring in `run_ui_gate`'s list, built as `editor-bar-room` is
(`editor_fixture`, `editor_pass`, quads read back with `ui_draw_list_quad`):

- **`editor-top-row`** -> `ui-editor-top-row`. A text of 200 lines, some wrapping at 300 points, with
  3 filler rows above line 40 and lines 60 to 90 folded closed:
  - `start`: the top row is 0 at the text's start.
  - `count`: put at line 50, half a row in (`ui_editor_scroll_line`), the top row is the rows above
    line 50 -- the wrapped rows of the lines above, the fillers, the fold's one row -- plus 0.5.
  - `trip`: `ui_editor_scroll_row` then `ui_editor_top_row` gives the same rows back for a row on a
    filler, on the fold's row, on a wrapped line's second row, and with a part.
  - `held`: past the text's rows it stops at the last row as a wheel does; below 0, at 0.
  - `pending`: asked before the editor is laid out on its text, it is taken at the next layout.
  - `level`: two editors 300 wide side by side, one with a line wrapping to 3 rows and the other
    with 2 fillers above the line after its counterpart, put at the same top row, draw the
    counterpart lines after the pair at the same height.
- **`editor-gutter-digits`** -> `ui-editor-gutter-digits`:
  - `columns`: a text of 9 lines with 4 digits asked has the columns of a text of 1000 lines in the
    same width.
  - `numbers`: its numbers stand right-aligned in the wider gutter.
  - `own`: 0 gives back the text's own; fewer digits than the text's own change nothing.
  - `wrapped`: changing it makes the rows guesses again, settled by the budget.
- **`editor-filler-end`** -> `ui-editor-filler-end`. A text of 20 lines with no last line ending:
  - `under`: 2 filler rows on line 20 draw 2 filler fills after the last line, with no number.
  - `rows`: the text's rows count them, and the view scrolls to show them.
  - `caret`: `G` stands on the last line; a click under it lands at the text's end.
  - `moved`: the last line taken out, the fillers stand under the new last line.
  - `past`: a filler on line 21 is left out and logged.

## As built

### Phase 1: the rows, the gutter and the end

- **`ui_editor_top_row`** reads `_top_row`, which `_scroll` (the bar) now reads too: `_top_of` of
  the top line, plus its row and part.
- **`ui_editor_scroll_row`** pends a `PENDING_ROW` (a `double` in `Pending`'s union) and takes it
  through `_scroll_row`, which holds the rows to 0 and the last row and finds the line with the
  existing `_line_at_row`, the binary search of the tops. The bar's `_scroll_to` now goes through
  `_scroll_row` as well, with `_last_row` the two share.
- **`ui_editor_set_gutter_digits`** sets `UIEditor.gutter_digits`, the editor's and so kept across
  texts; `_gutter_digits` (the larger of it and `_digits` of the line count) is read by `_place` and
  the draw. A change of columns makes the rows guesses through `_place`'s existing test.
- **A filler under the last line** is a filler on the line count, sorted last. It is not a line of
  its own, which every reader holding a line below the count would have had to learn; its rows are
  counted after the own rows of the last line that shows (`_under`, read by `_shown`), so the sums,
  the scroll's end and `ui_editor_top_row` count them with no change of their own. `_in_under` says
  whether a row is one of them, and holds there: `_caret_row` stops before them, `_row_offset` and
  `_at_column` give the text's end, and the press does not open a closed fold from them. The draw
  draws them after the last line that shows, a closed fold's row only from its own row now, and
  `ui_editor_set_fillers` keeps one on the count, sums the rows again from the last line that shows
  for it, and leaves out and logs one past. `_shift_fillers` needed nothing: `_moved` takes the old
  count to the new one through any change.
- **Found by the gates**: `_under` first counted a line a closed fold hides, whose rows are none, so
  `_row_offset` on such a line's own row (Home and End there, `ui-editor-folds`' `hidden`) gave the
  text's end. A line a fold hides has no rows under the text (`03dac9d4`).
- **Three cases**, after the hidden element's in `ui_editor_probe.c`, so `level` stands its two
  editors in a row on a screen of their own (`scene_alone`):
  - **`editor-top-row`**, 6 flags. `count` puts line 100, not 50, half a row in: below the fold too,
    so its one row is counted; the rows above are summed by hand from `ui_editor_line_rows`
    (`rows_above`). `trip` also reads the fold's row back as the fold's first line.
  - **`editor-gutter-digits`**, 4 flags; `wrapped` on a text of 4000 long lines, more than a frame's
    budget, so the guesses are seen before they settle.
  - **`editor-filler-end`**, 5 flags, in the vim mode for `G`; `rows` in the editor made 120 tall,
    since at 300 it showed every row and the wheel had nothing to scroll.
- **Falsified**, each committed first:
  - the fillers left out of `_top_row`: `count`, `trip` and `held` fail;
  - the draw's digits `_digits` of the line count: `numbers` fails (line 1's number 6 points left);
  - a filler on the line count left out: `under`, `rows` and `moved` fail.
- **Gates**: `--only ui,gate-docs` passes, 112 arms.
- **The look**: `apps/canvas --editor cetra/src/shore_runup.h -x -f 10` rendered twice is
  byte-identical (noise floor 0), and byte-identical to master's.
